TIBIC
With the development of the economy and technological progress, the government not only promoted the development of the bio-medical industry after the 1980s, but also focused on promoting the health care industry.In 1993, the Industrial Research Institute combined information technology with medical care to establish a medical information evaluation network, laying the foundation for the national medical information network.
Driven by urbanization, late marriage, late childbirth and low childbirth have officially transformed Taiwan into an elderly society in 1993. In order to alleviate the pressure of elderly care in China, the Institute of Technology and Research has established the "Taiwan Long-distance Home Care Industry Alliance" since 2004, and has developed various home care systems and sensing equipment through long-term accumulated communication energy. In 2006, it cooperated with Mackay Memorial Hospital and Yuanxiong Construction to launch the first large-scale community "Long-distance Health Care Demonstration Plan" in the country to accelerate the development of the community's long-term care system.Through technology linking enterprises, hospitals and care fields, multiple alliances have been established one after another. In 2020, we have used cross-domain technology to create a "Silver Hair Treasure Basin" and established the "Healthy and Happy Health and Smart Medical Care Alliance" to provide integrated solutions to prevent premature aging, disability and dementia of the elderly. We cooperate with Taiwan's largest care organization, Rongjia and Rongyuan, and work together to invest in smart long-term care services.In 2022, we will work with the National Health Center to focus on drug and vaccines and biotechnical drug manufacturing, nucleic acid drugs, regenerative medicine, health data models, etc., to drive the development of the precise health industry.
Over the past 30 years, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has continuously developed advanced care technology with innovative technology, including the development of "intelligent sensing post-its" in 2016; the development of "PECOLA Age Companion Robot" in 2019, and jointly created the world's first "handheld fundus camera" with AI analysis functions with Jinhong Technology; the launch of "Chronic Wound Intelligent Care" and "Full Body Gait Test System" in 2020; the "WiFi Radar Physiological Sensing Technology" published in 2021, etc., to continuously protect the health of the whole people with technological power.
1993
Completed the "National Medical Information Network Evaluation Plan of the Department of Health" and conducted a detailed evaluation of the National Medical Information Network Hsinchu District trial plan, laying the foundation for the pioneer of the national medical information network.
2004
The "Taiwan Long-distance Home Care Industry Alliance" was established, and it was converted into the "Taiwan Long-distance Care Service Industry Association" in 2007, with a total of 100 members including production, research and medical care.
2006
The three parties of Industrial Research Institute, Mackay Memorial Hospital and Yuanxiong Construction jointly launched the first large-scale community "Long-distance health care demonstration plan" in the country, combining bio-medical testing, online communication, and health care services to build a community-based long-term health care system development.
2007
With network service security and service-oriented architecture technology, it is applied to home health care services, transfers technology and assists ZTE Preservation, Kaibo Technology and Maya Information Company.
2007
Promote the "Silver Clan Companion Robot R&D Alliance" and develop the first Silver Clan Robot Service System.
2008
Working with Royal Philips to cooperate with a new generation of interactive aids, the cooperation projects include: assessment of the needs of disabled people, concern for urban-rural gap issues and future home care potential.
2009
Assist Huabao Communications, Taiwan Xinguang Preservation and Huizhi.com to establish the "Home Companion Robot R&D Alliance for Middle-aged and Senior Citizens" to promote domestic manufacturers to deploy the global smart robot market and technology as soon as possible.
2010
Assisted Jinhong Company in the development of the prototype of the "Digital Handheld Fundus Camera", it is the first ophthalmic imaging diagnostic instrument developed and manufactured in Taiwan, and obtained a marketing license in July 2012.
2014
Completed the development of the "Home-type Somatic Sports Guided Health Management Service System", cooperated with two domestic furniture indicator sports centers, and established a new business office for spin-in Zhongqiang Optoelectronics Group Medical Network.
2016
The technology authorized the "Medical Clinical Service Process Decision-making Inference" technology, which led to the investment and establishment of "Tianshu Health Information Company".
2016
The "Intelligent Sensing Post-it" is a sensor platform with a flexible substrate and the first "Hunting Smart Insole" in China.
2018
Establishing the only health risk analysis technology based on Taiwan’s local database has led to Cathay Group Life Insurance Company’s first domestic group insurance spillover policy.
2019
Created the "PECOLA Age Companion Robot" and won the CES 2020 Smart Home Innovation Award.
2019
Together with Jinhong Technology, we created the world's first "handheld fundus camera" with AI analysis functions. In 2022, we won the Edison Award and the World's Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Award for "Eye Medical Imaging Assisted Diagnosis Technology".
2020
The lightweight "Smart Care for Chronic Wounds" device developed combines sensing technologies such as color, heat sense, 3D to accurately present the wound condition and won the world's top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards.
2020
Create a "Silver-haired Cornucopia" smart care verification test field, providing integrated solutions to prevent premature aging, disability and dementia in the elderly.For example, the "full-body gait testing system" can track the elderly's gait and posture changes in early screening.
2021
The first LBS solution in China that combines indoor positioning services and physiological activity detection.
2022
Cooperate with the National Health Center to focus on top ten research and development issues such as drug and vaccines and biotechnical drug manufacturing, nucleic acid drugs, regenerative medicine, and health data models.
