Taiwan's PCB industry has been developing for more than 40 years, and has the world's largest PCB industry chain. It is the world's largest exporter of PCB circuit boards. It has won the world's leading position for 11 consecutive years in 2022, with a market share of 32.8%. It is the third largest industry in Taiwan after semiconductors and displays.In the early stages of industrial development, the Industrial Research Institute provided a good product and equipment testing environment for operators. In 1982, it invested in the research and development of PCB independent materials and technology-transformed industries, laying the foundation for the PCB giant industry.
In order to drive the development of the PCB industry, the Industrial Research Institute signed a transfer contract for the "Printed Circuit Board Automation Production Technology" with Nanya Plastics Company in 1985. In 1988, it cooperated with Changxing Chemical Company to develop "alkali liquid imaging dry film photoresist" to assist the PCB factory in reducing costs; it also cooperated with Dadong Resin Company to develop dry film photoresist to promote the development of domestic PCB to multi-layer high-density development.In 2005, the "multi-engine electronic component inkjet fabric process platform" was developed, and cooperated with the largest PCB factory in China to develop the production process of mobile phone PCB wires with printing technology.
Recently, the PCB industry has faced challenges of aging of the working population, lack of experience and testing quality. The Industrial Research Institute helped operators develop smart manufacturing. In 2016, it cooperated with the Taiwan Circuit Board Association to establish the "PCB Equipment Communication Agreement Alliance" to accelerate the intelligent promotion of PCB industry equipment, issue equipment communication agreements, promote industrial standards, and apply them to more than 100 domestic factories. In 2018, it connected the leading industrial computers Advantech Technology, PCB operators and automation manufacturers to form the "PCB A-Team Smart Manufacturing Alliance" to create Taiwan's "Industry 4.0" demonstration case and establish international standards.In 2021, it pioneered three technologies: integrated venture communication standards, cause analysis, and AI artificial intelligence image repainting to create a "circuit board industry smart manufacturing service application platform" to assist in the digital upgrade of the PCB industry.

Develop flexible PCBs.
1988
We cooperated with Changxing Chemical Company to develop "alkali liquid imaging dry film photoresist" to assist PCB factories in obtaining low-cost dry film photoresist and rapid and effective technical services; we also cooperated with Dadong Resin Company to develop dry film photoresist to promote the development of domestic PCB to multi-layer high-density development.
2005
The world's first "optical electronic component manufacturing technology with multiple architectures" was developed, and the "inkjet implantation system platform" was completed, replacing the traditional yellow light process, and jointly developed with the largest PCB factory in China to replace the production process of mobile phone PCB wires with printing technology.
2016
Published technologies such as "High-deep aspect ratio, ultra-fine wire printing technology without masks", "added fine wire manufacturing technology", and "PCB equipment communication software" to assist Taiwan's printed circuit board industry from automated manufacturing to smart manufacturing.
2016
We cooperated with the Taiwan Circuit Board Association to establish the "PCB Equipment Communication Agreement Alliance" to accelerate the intelligent promotion of PCB industry equipment, issue equipment communication agreements, promote industrial standards, and apply them to more than 100 factories in China.
2018
Connecting the leading industrial computer industry computers, Advantech, PCB operators and automation manufacturers, it forms the "PCB A-Team Smart Manufacturing Alliance" to create Taiwan's "Industry 4.0" demonstration case and establish international standards.
2019
Establish "full addition micro circuits and laser-induced metallization platform technology" to promote circuit production to achieve the goal of simplifying, greening, subtleizing and three-dimensional circuits.
2021
The Taiwan Circuit Board Association established the "Smart Automation System Integration Alliance (iASIA)" to jointly formulate the circuit board industry information public version model (ImPCB) to allow the information of different brands of equipment and machines to be interconnected, which will help the equipment intelligent upgrade.
2021
The first to create three technologies: integrated venture communication standards, cause analysis, and AI artificial intelligence image repainting to create a "circuit board industry smart manufacturing service application platform" to assist in the digital upgrade of the PCB industry.
