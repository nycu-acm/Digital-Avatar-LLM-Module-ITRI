The State Administration of Telecommunications of the Ministry of Transport opened its operations in 1997, and mobile phones gradually entered people's lives.As early as 1992, the Industrial Research Institute had invested in the research and development of mobile communication technology, starting from the research and development of CDMA spread frequency wireless telephone technology, and from 2G to 5G, it has been side by side with the industry.
In 2001, the RF IC of GSM dual-frequency transceiver was completed, and the key technology of 2G mobile communication was established. Taiwanese manufacturers began to invest in mobile phone production and manufacturing, and my country's communication industry also shone brightly, with output value maintaining nearly 20% growth for five consecutive years. In 2004, WCDMA Common IP core technology and 3G chips were completed, elevating my country's mobile chip and system industry from 2G/2.5G to a highly challenging 3G field. In 2008, the research and development of 4G WiMAX mobile communication chips was invested in, and the first IEEE 802.16e MIMO base frequency chip and the world's first CMOS power amplifier (PA) with Watt level and linearity were provided to provide the industry's complete 4G WiMAX mobile communication solution. In 2019, the first wave of 5G small base station products were launched simultaneously with the world to create "5G". The base station ecosystem helps manufacturers to layout the "white-branded" business opportunities for network equipment in advance; in 2020, the first domestic 5G O-RAN independent small base station was successfully developed, and the technology was transferred to multiple servers and network operators, and has successfully entered the three major Japanese network equipment suppliers.
In order to exert the spirit of telecommunications liberalization, as early as 2005, the Institute of Technology and Research successfully connected 14 telecommunications operators and invested in mobile phone door portable services to solve the problem that people need to change door numbers when changing telecommunications companies, and save the cost of reprinting various communication documents, so that technical services and environmental protection and energy conservation can complement each other.Taiwan is the first country in Asia to implement a portable door number.After its successful launch, it also attracted countries such as Singapore and Malaysia to come to Taiwan to learn from the scriptures.
1992
Develop CDMA spread-frequency wireless telephone technology to assist in the development of the 2G mobile communication market.
1999
Complete VoIP network phones and gateways to save corporate international and long-distance calls.
2001
Complete the RF IC of GSM dual-frequency transceiver and establish key technical energy for 2G mobile communication.
2002
Complete the system integration of the General Wireless Packet Switching Service (GPRS) base station and core network technology to provide better data communication services.
2004
Complete the WCDMA RF IC and establish 3G radio frequency key components. Complete the core WCDMA Common IP technology, promote VIA and BenQ Dentsu to accelerate the development of WCDMA products, push my country's mobile phone industry from 2G to 3G, and greatly increase the added value of chip and system products.
2005
Assist the State Administration of Telecommunications to complete the portable service of the Taiwan Door Number, making Taiwan the first country in Asia to implement portable portable services.
2008
The first IEEE 802.16e MIMO basic frequency chip independently developed in China.
2009
With the WiMAX 802.16e/16m specification as the goal, it can also be used in LTE/LTE-A systems. It has successfully developed the world's first CMOS power amplifier with Watt level and linearity, which can be used in communication chips such as mobile phones, network cards, PMDs, small laptops, etc.
2019
The first wave of 5G small base station products will be launched simultaneously with the world.Develop solutions that comply with the 3GPP standards and are compatible with the O-RAN standard 5G base station software technology and hardware integration, so that Taiwan can plan ahead of schedule the "white-branded" business opportunities for future network equipment.
2020
Develop Taiwan’s first domestic 5G SA (Stand alone) independent small base station, transforming technology into multiple server and network operators, improving the autonomy of domestic 5G telecom equipment development, and successfully entering the three major Japanese network equipment suppliers.
