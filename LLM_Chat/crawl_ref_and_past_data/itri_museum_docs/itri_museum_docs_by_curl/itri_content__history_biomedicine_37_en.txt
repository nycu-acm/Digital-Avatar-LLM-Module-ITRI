In the 1990s, the government vigorously supported the biotechnology pharmaceutical industry. As early as the 1970s, the Institute of Industrial Research focused on the development of medical chemical process technology, and developed raw materials with a wide range of uses, so it was able to use long-term deep-rooted chemical technology to help develop the pharmaceutical industry.In the pharmaceutical manufacturing and production part, in 1990, the "cardiovascular drug process technology" was developed, and in 1993, the "complete process technology of antibiotic intermediate 7-ADCA" was developed, assisting Taiwan Pharmaceuticals in establishing key process technology; in 1992, the "release control test factory" was opened and the "Traditional Medicine GMP test factory" was established in 2002, and the "Traditional Medicine GMP test factory" was established to shorten the commercialization process of new drugs.
In terms of R&D, new drug technology has been continuously developed to transfer manufacturers. For example, in 2000, the "local drug microliposome technology" was developed and the technology has been transferred to Ruian (now Jianqiao Xinyuan Pharmaceutical Biotechnology); in 2004, the technology has been transferred to "B liver Chinese herbal new drug BMEC-101" for Sanhuang Biotechnology; in 2005, the technology has been transferred to the first "anti-asthma drug BMEC-1217B" in China, which has been licensed by the US FDA clinical trial (IND) to Hongya Biotechnology, and cooperates with manufacturers to accelerate product commercialization.
From cardiovascular, hepatitis, cancer, influenza, immune diseases to brain medication, the Institute of Technology assisted the development of the pharmaceutical industry with innovative technologies and derived a number of new startups, such as: Jianyu Biotechnology, Berks Biotechnology, Jusheng Biotechnology, etc., injecting live water into Taiwan's Chinese and Western medicine industry.In recent years, we have helped the pharmaceutical industry move forward through platform methods, and in 2018, we cooperated with the German Merck Pharmaceutical Factory from pharmaceutical research and development to talent cultivation to drive my country's new precision medicine development capabilities; in 2019, we have formed an alliance with the International Jiaoshen Group Jansen Pharmaceutical Factory to successfully link with the international drug development technology with dual-acting immunotherapy drug development technology; in 2021, we use patented eye drops to develop eye drops for the treatment of wet macular lesions and convert them to the technology of Xinli biotechnology, and have been derived for the development of new functional contact lenses and transform them into Jingshuo Optics; in 2022, we use antibody engineering technology to develop "anti-TIGIT antibody new drug" that can kill cancer cells.From research and development, manufacturing, production to new drug development, Taiwan companies have led to break through the international pharmaceutical industry.
1973
Engaged in the development of medical chemical process technology, and research focuses include extraction of traditional Chinese medicine ingredients, synthesis of animal drugs, and synthesis of key intermediates.
1992
The first "release control test factory" in the country was officially launched, and the research and development of drug release control dosage forms was carried out in different fields such as polymer chemistry, pharmacy and dosage form design, analytical and inspection technology, chemical process amplification and animal testing.
1993
Develop the complete process technology of antibiotic intermediate 7-ADCA and facilitate the industry's investment in the establishment of Junxiang Chemical Company, which is the fourth manufacturer in the world that can produce this new drug.
1995
The first GMP certified release control preparation factory with a cleanliness of 10,000 in the country will be built to help domestic manufacturers shorten the commercialization process of new drugs.
1996
Develop process technology for taxol (Taxol), a raw material for anti-cancer drug.
1997
The groundbreaking ceremony of the GMP test factory of raw materials.
2000
The development of local drug microliposome technology was transferred to Ruian Pharmaceutical Factory, and obtained a drug certificate in 2009 and officially launched on the market.
2002
Using Shanfangfeng Traditional Chinese Medicine, after synthetic screening, it has anti-cancer effects, and the results are transferred to Jingde Pharmaceutical Company of Hexin Group.
2002
Completed the first multi-functional "Traditional Medicine GMP Test Factory" in China that meets international standards.
2004
The database of the DNA identification sequence of Chinese herbal medicines was transferred to the technology, Yushuntian Biotechnology Company and China Pharmaceutical Industry Company, and assisted Hong Kong Xianglan Pharmaceutical Company in the identification of the basis of important medicinal materials.
2004
BMEC101, a new Chinese herbal medicine anti-hepatitis B, is a technology-transferred Sanhuang Company.
2005
The research and development of anti-avian influenza drugs has made major breakthroughs, with the ability to make the technical capabilities of "shikiic acid" and "sialic acid" in our homemade key raw materials, and has successfully completed anti-influenza drugs and cooperated with Ruilesha technically.
2005
The technology has been converted to "anti-asthma drug BMEC-1217B" for Hongya Biotechnology, which is the first new drug developed in China for the prevention and treatment of asthma, and is also the first anti-asthma drug sent to the US FDA to apply for a drug clinical trial license.
2007
Shikimic acid (Shikimic acid) rapid extraction process technology is used to provide trial production and mass production of raw materials for influenza drugs.
2008
The first "Blood Brain Barrier (BBB) ​​drug transmission technology in China was introduced to the to-BBB branch in Taiwan to develop drugs for central nervous diseases.
2011
The research and development results of new anti-enteritis drugs and technology platform (DLS-01) and new anti-rheumatoid arthritis drugs and technology platform (DLS-03) are transferred to Wright Advanced Biomedical Company.
2011
Combined with the academic and research energy of NTU, Peking University and other academic research, the first anti-cancer target drug ITRI-260, developed independently developed by the Chinese, and transferred the technology to Ancheng Biotechnology Company.
2013
The "collagen scaffold" technology has been developed to transfer Xuanjie Biotechnology Company, which is a new drug used in autoimmune diseases such as "lupus erythematosus", "multiple sclerosis", and "type 1 diabetes".
2014
It jointly established Jusheng Biomedical Co., Ltd. with Handa Venture Capital, Jingde Pharmaceuticals and Innovative Technology Transfer.
2014
"Key Technologies for Chinese Herbal Medicine in Hepatitis and Anti-inflammatory Plants" was derived from Jianyu Biotechnology Company, which is the first natural drug derivative startup in China.
2016
The company was established to develop new plant medicines for the treatment of tumors and chronic liver diseases.
2018
Cooperation with the German Merck Pharmaceutical Factory includes developing and introducing advanced processes, cooperating with forward-looking pharmaceutical technologies and cultivating domestic advanced biotechnical pharmaceutical talents.
2018
At that time, Vice President Chen Jianren visited the "Process Training Laboratory" of the Institute of Technology and Merck, and was accompanied by Lin Qiwan, director of the Institute of Biomedical Medicine, and Merck.
2019
Alliance with the Jansen Pharmaceutical Factory of Jiaoshen Group successfully links with the development technology of dual-activity immunotherapy drugs.
2021
Using patented eye drops platform technology, the first national eye drop for fundus lesions was developed to replace existing eye injection therapy.
2021
Develop a new plant drug for non-steroid psychomycosis treatment PTB323X, which can effectively improve psychomycosis
2022
Developing "new anti-TIGIT antibody drugs" can effectively activate T and NK cells, which can increase the effect of toxic cancer cells by more than several times.
