Locomotives are the main transportation tool for Chinese people, with the highest density in Asia and are known as the kingdom of motorcycles. However, Taiwan did not have relevant technical support before the 1950s, and mainly sold imported complete motorcycles. After the 1960s, due to the gradual rise of industry and commerce, employment opportunities increased, and locomotives gradually replaced bicycles and became the mainstream transportation tool.Although Taiwan can build a body, it has no domestic engine available. Driven by government policies and market demand, the Industrial Research Institute began to invest in the research and development of the locomotive industry. In the 1980s, it made the first "jet engine Sukda MIRL80i" in Taiwan that could be launched almost immediately from start to finish. Its jet system is more advanced than foreign Sukda.
After the 1990s, the Industrial Research Institute not only continued to improve locomotive engines and components, but also assisted domestic manufacturers in building locomotive production lines, and jointly invested in research and development with major domestic locomotive factories, battery factories and motor factories.From the engine of the second-trip and four-trip locomotive to the completion of the development of the first V-shaped multi-cylinder 250cc large-displacement locomotive engine in China, to support the domestic locomotive industry to gain a foothold in the international market, Taiwan’s satellite factory can now supply 80% of the world’s locomotive parts.
In the 2000s, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has been successfully investing in the research and development of electric locomotives, and has successively published "New Generation Concept Locomotive RoboScooter", "High-speed charging and dischargeable aluminum batteries", "High-energy and high-safety resin solid-state batteries", "National-produced heavy-duty electric locomotive drive subsystem public version solution", etc., moving from engines to electric car motors, batteries and systems development, and connecting operators to form an electric locomotive alliance to complete the upstream, midstream and downstream industrial chains of electric vehicles, and pushing the domestic electric vehicle industry to the international level.
1988
The jet engine speed can reach the MIRL80i "Rattlesnake", using jet oil supply and water-cooled engines.
1988
The Institute of Industrial Research Institute's locomotive technology appeared on the cover photo of the monthly motorcycle of "Popular Knight".
1988
"Popular Knight" locomotive monthly report that the jet engine developed by the Institute of Technology Research can reach the speed of the MIRL80i "Rattlesnake", using jet oil supply and water-cooled engines.
1991
We have invested in the research and development of electric locomotive technology, and have cooperated with domestic Guangyang, Sanyang, Taiwan Yamaha, Tailing, Zhanye, Yongfeng Locomotive Factory, Tongyi, Yuasa, Guanglong Battery Factory, and Shidian and Taiwan All Motor Factory to develop the prototype EC1 vehicle, and the vehicle performance can meet the short-distance transportation needs.
1993
Launched the ZES2000 electric locomotive prototype.
1993
Development of electric locomotive drivers and motors.
1993
Research and develop a two-trip locomotive.
1995
Assist Sanyang Locomotive Company in setting up a fully automatic domestically produced elastic joint production line, mainly producing 125 cc locomotive cylinder blocks.
1996
It jointly signed a state-owned patent authorization contract for locomotives with Sanyang and Guangyang, two locomotive manufacturers, and pioneered the precedent for the patent authorization of domestic locomotive operators to use the patent, which was also the largest production-research cooperation action at that time.In the future, we will join forces with locomotives and motor operators to form an electric locomotive technology alliance.
1996
Completed the first four-trip water-cooled 125cc Sokda locomotive engine in China, and transferred technology to Liantong Industrial Company.
1996
It signed a contract with Siemens to develop EMS (Engine Management System) hard and software technology for electronic control of four-trip locomotive engines, and implemented it for promotion and use in the domestic industry.
2000
Completed the development of the first V-shaped multi-cylinder 250cc large-displacement locomotive engine in China, and transferred technology to Guangyang Company, which led to an investment of 1.5 billion yuan in mass production of Venox 250 locomotives, successfully entering the European locomotive market.
2001
Tutoring Taiwan Yuasa Battery Company to carry out the development of process technology related to high-performance lead-acid batteries for motor vehicles and develop lead-acid batteries for motor vehicles that meet market demand.
2008
Joining Sanyang and MIT Media Lab to create a new generation of concept motorcycle RoboScooter, which has the characteristics of environmental protection, energy saving, lightweight, foldable, and easy parking to meet the future urban energy conservation and carbon reduction needs.
2009
Promote the establishment of the "Electric Locomotive Industry Promotion Alliance", combining 33 alliance members including: locomotive factory Sanyang, Guangyang, Yamaha; lithium battery factory Nengyuan, Bixiang and Xinpu, etc., to form a complete upstream, midstream and downstream industrial chain of electric vehicles.
2019
It signed a technical contract for electric locomotive energy management system with KYMCO of Guangyang Group, and cooperated with 36 patented technologies including "self-learning dynamic power estimate" technology to develop a new generation of AI concept electric locomotives.
2021
The "nationally produced heavy-duty electric locomotive driving subsystem public version solution" is developed to give electric locomotives high power, high horsepower, high endurance and high environmental protection characteristics, and to connect series chips, power subsystems to all-vehicle products and other domestic supply chains.
