TIBIC
In the 1980s, the domestic information and communication industry began to flourish, while foreign countries have begun to combine information and communication across the domain to develop innovative technologies in various fields. One of the trends is to combine technology with life, and to develop "bio chips" that can complete a large amount of information in a short time and analyze a large number of genes or proteins in the organism.In the 1990s, the government vigorously promoted biotechnology policies, and the Institute of Technology responded and started research and development of biochips that were more advanced in technology.
In 2000, we successively published biological chips such as fever chips and gene chips, and continuously improved the related technologies of biological chip production, including substrate preparation, surface treatment, micro-electromechanical processing and production, including 41 patented technologies such as fever chips in 2001, and used a new biochip manufacturing technology platform to significantly reduce the cost of biological chips by 90%; in 2002, we formed a new startup company Hualian Biotechnology to enter the global biochip foundry market; in 2003, we completed the first high-density "hepatic gene microarray chip" developed by the Chinese people.
In 2006, in order to promote the future clinical application and industrial development of biochips, the Institute of Technology and Research shouldered the expectations of the industry and established the "Biochip Clinical Application Alliance" in conjunction with manufacturers such as Hualian Biotechnology, Shenlong Biotechnology, YonghShang Technology, Weimao Biotechnology, Titanium Technology and Kaiwu Technology. Through the heterogeneous alliance, it will vertically integrate the biochip manufacturing and R&D service industry and create new business opportunities in Taiwan's biotechnology and medical industry.In 2016, a new startup company, Hansheng Medical, was established to develop microarray biochip scanners, and combined with international and Taiwanese homemade biochip manufacturers, it entered the global high-density biochip scanner market.
1998
Combining biotechnology with Taiwan’s most competitive industries, semiconductor and petrochemical industries, has established two research groups for biochips and biopolymers.
2001
41 patent technologies including fever chips were published, and relevant domestic manufacturers were invited to form a "Biochip R&D Alliance" to centrally authorize a number of important and complementary patents in the form of a "patent combination".
2002
Completed and transferred relevant process technologies such as biofilm substrate preparation, surface treatment, micro-electromechanical processing and production, and promoted the establishment of the "Hualian Biotechnology" startup company.
2002
Developed printing process and component technology, and has been developed and applied to mass production of biological wafers.
2003
We jointly implemented the "Gene Chip Design Key Technology Development Plan" with Hualian Biotechnology and tested the first high-density "Liver 2k Microarray" developed by Chinese people.
2006
Combined with six other companies including Hualian, YonghSBC, Weimao, Shenlong, Titanium and Kaiwu, we will develop the test technology, gene chip production technology, gene chip automatic hybridization technology and gene chip scanner technology.
2006
Combined with domestic manufacturers, including Hualian Biotechnology, Shenlong Biotechnology, YonghShang Technology, Weimao Biotechnology, Titanium Technology and Kaiwu Technology, it officially announced the establishment of the "Biochip Clinical Application Alliance", through the heterogeneous alliance to vertically integrate the biochip manufacturing and R&D service industry.
2016
A new startup company, Hansheng Medical, was developed to develop microarray biochip scanners.
