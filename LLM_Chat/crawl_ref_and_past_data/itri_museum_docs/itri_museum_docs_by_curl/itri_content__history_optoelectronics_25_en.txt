Although Taiwan was known as the king of computers in the 1980s, it was often ridiculed by international industry as a "headless industry" due to its lack of disk read and write heads and printer inkjet head technologies.In 1984, the Industrial Research Institute joined hands with Datong, Jinbao, Shengbao, Far East Electronics, Sanguang Weida and other home operators to form a hard drive R&D alliance, and established Taiwan's self-made disk drive capabilities from scratch, and invested in the research and development of optoelectronic peripheral equipment.
In 1993, it completed the 3.5-inch magneto-optical disc machine and CD-ROM prototype; in 1995, it completed the optical reading head of CD-ROM optical disc machine. In the same year, it converted the CD-ROM Chipset technology to the relevant design department of UMC CD-ROM. The department later formed MediaTek, which once became the world's largest optical disc IC supplier; in 1996, it completed the thermal bubble inkjet printing head and DVD optical reading and writing head; in 1998, it changed to the 8x speed DVD optical reading head to domestic manufacturers, assisting the country in establishing independent key technologies and getting rid of the headless industrial dilemma.In addition, the Institute of Technology Research and Development is also actively creating a good development environment for domestic manufacturers. In 1998, the World "DVD Alliance" (DVD Forum) included the Institute of Technology Research and Development as a member of the standard setting team. Taiwan's optoelectronics industry has been taking off and becoming the kingdom of optical disc machines. One out of every 10 optical disc machines is from Taiwan.
With the development of high-speed network, cloud and portable disc technology, the once-prosperous optical disc machine manufacturers are also facing the challenges of transformation.In 2013, the Industrial Research Institute combined the application of optoelectronics and biomedical sciences to transform the optical disc machine that was originally used to read materials into an imaging diagnosis medical device, becoming a weapon of personalized preventive medicine and an innovative driving force for my country's optical disc machine industry development.
1984
It has formed a "hard drive R&D alliance" with Datong, Jinbao, Shengbao, Far East Electronics, and Sanguang Weida.
1989
The second generation laser printer has been developed to enable my country to have the ability to develop and integrate the entire printer.
1991
The R&D was completed by five and one-quarter inch and only writing a disc machine once, laying the foundation for Chinese people to develop disc machines themselves.
1993
Completed the 3.5-inch magneto-optical disc machine and readable disc machine prototype, laying the foundation for the Chinese people to develop disc machines on their own.
1995
Complete the CD-ROM optical disc machine optical reading head.Technology-to-CD-ROM chipset technology is UMC's first product.Later, UMC derived the disc machine-related design department to establish MediaTek, which later became the world's largest disc machine IC supplier.
1996
The development of hot bubble inkjet printing heads and DVD optical reading and writing heads has helped Taiwan get rid of the dilemma of "headless industry".
1997
The development of DVD-ROM disc machine is the first product to enter the disc machine market in Taiwan, and it has signed a technical license with Taiwan Panasonic Electrical Appliance Company.
1998
Selected as a core member of the world's "DVD Alliance".
1999
The DVD verification laboratory was established, which is the first A-level DVD verification laboratory in Asia outside Japan.
2000
Complete the first domestic DVD-ROM/DVD Player disc machine, master the sources of important components, and reduce dependence on international manufacturers.
2001
The first optical head and optical machine module of the Blu-ray disc machine in China was developed to complete the system integration, no longer be subject to Japanese manufacturers, and to establish a completely independent Blu-ray HD-DVD disc machine system.
2002
In combination with 27 manufacturers of the "Taiwan Prospect Light Storage R&D Alliance", we jointly published a proposal to complete the "New Specifications of Blu-ray HD-DVD" as a reference for my country's development of HD-DVD.
2006
A complete Class-A DVD/HD DVD verification laboratory can be built, and can perform 6 product verification and testing services, making it one of the 10 HD DVD Class A verification laboratories around the world.
2013
The development of blue-ray scanning microscope technology has created new business opportunities for my country's optical disc machine industry to enter the field of biomedical science.
