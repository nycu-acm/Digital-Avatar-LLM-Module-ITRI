Textile manufacturing
Taiwan's artificial fiber industry developed rapidly under the support of policy. In 1974, Taiwan's artificial fiber industry ranked fourth in the world, but all from spinning machines to spinning oil agents are imported.In 1975, the Industrial Research Institute invested in human fiber spinning research, set up a spinning machine room, and integrated multiple fiber factories to carry out spinning oil agent research and development. In addition to improving product quality, it also promoted the import substitution of textile oil agents.In 1993, Taiwan's first fully automatic spinning nozzle inspection and cleaning system and high-speed textile technology were developed. It was synchronized with Japan's technology and converted into four human fiber factories including Shin Kong, Far East, ZTE, and Dongyun, greatly improving textile production technology.
After the industrial migration in the 1990s, in 2005, the WTO completely abolished textile quotas, and Taiwan's textile industry encountered more intense international competitive pressure. In 2006, the Institute of Technology and Research called on textile manufacturers in South Taiwan to promote the "Southern High-Tech Textile R&D Alliance", combined with industry, official, academic and research resources, to drive rapid industrial transformation and promote the development of new products including nano-anti-anti-bacterial yarns and environmentally friendly yarns.In 2008, using high-value specialty products and functional fiber technology, we assisted Changhua Shetou Sheets to upgrade settlements, formed a production, sales and verification R&D alliance system, and jointly developed high-value functional socks.
Since 2010, under the trend of low birth rate, small amounts of diversity and short-term delivery, the textile industry urgently needs digital transformation.The Institute of Technology and Research assisted many textile factories such as Dongfeng Fiber to introduce smart manufacturing technologies such as the Internet of Things, production of big data, and AVG unmanned transport vehicles. In 2019, it assisted Hongyuan Industrial Co., Ltd. to complete the world's first "Preparant garment round T unmanned automation assembly line", which can complete rapid proofing in 3 days, integrate the sewing process, and save more than 70% of the labor.
1975
Completed the construction of the spinning machine room and established technical exchanges with major manufacturers in the artificial fiber industry, including Hualong Company, Yuhe Company, Xinguang Company, Far East Company, etc.
1991
Integrating domestic fiber factories and special chemical plants, including Far East, Shin Kong, ZTE, Wenhao, etc., investing in the research and development of spinning oil agents and conducting research and development, which is of great benefit to product quality.
1993
The successful development of high-speed textile technology of 6,000 meters per minute is twice as fast as the traditional spinning speed of 3,000 meters per minute. The technology has transformed into four human fiber factories including Xinguang, Far East, ZTE, and Dongyun, which has a revolutionary impact on my country's textile industry production technology.
1993
The first fully automatic spinning nozzle inspection and cleaning system in China has been issued.The inspection speed is fast and the accuracy is high, which improves the automation level of the textile industry.
1995
Guidance of Steady Polymer Chemical Company to set up factories to mass-produce spinning oil agents, break through the long-term dependence on foreign imports, and make the domestic textile industry develop towards rapid and high added value.
2006
The "South Taiwan Textile R&D Alliance of the Republic of China" was established to establish an innovative R&D platform to drive rapid industrial transformation.
2008
Promote the settlement of Changhua's sock weaving industry, and integrate sock weaving brands, dyeing, pathways, special chemicals, and inspection companies to form a production, sales and verification R&D alliance system to develop high-value functional socks.
2016
iSmartweaR Smart Sensing Clothes has won the world's top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards.
2019
Assist Hongyuan Industrial’s first ready-to-wear round T unmanned automation assembly line, which can quickly proofread in 3 days, saving more than 70% of manpower.
