While the development of personal computers, the Industrial Research Institute is also actively investing in the research and development of computer peripheral products, hoping that the information industry's product line will be connected together, and the upstream and downstream integration will be closer, and the initial goal will be to disk drives and printers. The professional talents required for the development of these two products are all over the optical, electronic, mechanical, materials and chemical fields, and the technical threshold is high.Taking disk drives as an example, as long as there is an error in the storage and access process of computer storage equipment, the files that users have worked hard to build will be destroyed.Therefore, the error tolerance rate of disk drives is very low, only one in one hundred million.
In 1988, a 3.5-inch 100MB hard disk drive and laser printer were developed. In the process of developing hard drive machines, the Industrial Research Institute also established the capabilities of hard drive component manufacturers.The key to driving the head movement on this 100MB hard drive includes the voice coil motor, from materials, parts to design, which is completely made by Taiwan.
In terms of printers, in 1984, the Industrial Research Institute completed the first 24-pin dot matrix impact printer prototype sample, and in 1989, the second generation of laser printer was developed, which enabled my country to develop and integrate the entire printer technology.In 1992, a four-in-one laser printer was developed in combination with printing, fax, scanning, photocopy and other functions.In 1996, the color thermal transfer printer was completed and mastered a number of key technologies, including paper feeding system design, color positioning technology, heat writing head control technology, driver and decoder design, halftone technology and color reproduction, etc., which will help the popularization of domestic multimedia products applications and office automation.
