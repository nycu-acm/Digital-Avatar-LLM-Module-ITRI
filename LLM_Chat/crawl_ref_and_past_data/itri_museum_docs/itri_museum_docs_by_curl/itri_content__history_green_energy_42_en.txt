According to the "Taiwan 2050 Net Zero Emissions Path", by 2050, renewable energy will need to account for 60 to 70% of the total power generation, and diversified development of renewable energy is imperative.The Taiwan Strait is one of the world's best and strong wind farms. In addition, Taiwan is located in the Pacific volcanic belt and has wind and geothermal advantages, which is conducive to the development of wind and geothermal renewable energy.
The Institute of Technology began to invest in wind power generation technology as early as the 1980s. In 1985, it completed the development and development of wind power units and set up a wind power station in Hukou. At that time, the single-unit power generation capacity could reach 20,150kW, which was a world-leading group and laid the foundation for local wind power technology. Since 2000, my country has vigorously promoted renewable energy such as solar energy, wind power, geothermal, biogas, biomass energy, geothermal, marine energy, etc., and the device capacity will grow significantly in the future. In the future, Taiwan's offshore wind power operation and maintenance market will see 10 billion yuan.The Industrial Research Institute uses high-temperature superconductors as generator rotors. Through digital operation and maintenance technology, the weight and cost of wind turbine maintenance can be greatly reduced, and the reliability will also be improved accordingly. It is a forward-looking technology that is very suitable for the development of offshore wind turbines for local wind power development.
In terms of geothermal development, in 1973, the government promoted a geothermal census due to the global oil crisis. At that time, the Institute of Technology and Research was the only R&D institution in Taiwan that had one-stop technology for geothermal power plants.In 2012, a 50 kW enhanced dual circulation power generation demonstration system was established in Qingshui Geothermal Park, which was the first geothermal power plant to be operated and tested in Taiwan. It was later commercially transferred in 2018; the "Geothermal Expert Assisted Decision-making System" developed in 2015 accurately assesses the potential area for developing geothermal heat. Since 2020, the government and private institutions have actively invested. As of the end of 2022, more than 4 MW of the device capacity in Taiwan has been in operation. In the future, it will continue to cooperate with the central/local New Taipei City Government, Taiwan Power, CNPC, etc. to carry out geothermal development to promote the development of geothermal power generation.
1986
Yuhukou Wind Power Station has successively completed the development and development of 4KW, 40KW and 150KW wind turbine units.
2012
A 50 kW enhanced dual circulation power generation demonstration system was established in Qingshui Geothermal Park, which was the first geothermal power plant to be operated and tested in Taiwan, and was commercially transferred in 2018.
2015
Develop a "geothermal expert assisted decision-making system", accurately evaluate and develop geothermal potential areas, and develop "acid-resistant composite coating material technology" to overcome acidity problems.
2015
We cooperated with Taiwan Electric to promote the "Green Island Geothermal Generator Experimental Project" and adopted the "Organic Rankine (ORC) dual circulation geothermal generator set" developed by the Industrial Research Institute.
2015
The cooperation with New Taipei City to carry out the largest geothermal development project in Taiwan’s “Kinshan Sulfur Ziping Geothermal Demonstration Zone” is a milestone in the history of geothermal development in Taiwan.
2021
It signed a "Offshore Wind Power Industry and Technology Cooperation and Information Exchange MOU" with the UK Offshore Renewable Energy Integration Development Center to start cooperation in the wind power industry in Taiwan and Britain.
2021
Signed a letter of intent for deepening underground exploration technology and information exchange and cooperation in Taiwan's geothermal oil zone with China National Petroleum Corporation to jointly promote the development of geothermal heat.
