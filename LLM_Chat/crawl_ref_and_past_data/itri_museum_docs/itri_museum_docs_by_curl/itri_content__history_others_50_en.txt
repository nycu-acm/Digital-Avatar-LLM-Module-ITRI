Cultivation Center
Technological innovation is one of the most important driving forces for national economic growth. In order to nurture new technology industries, the Institute of Technology and Research promoted the open laboratory and entrepreneurship cultivation center in 1994, transforming the Institute of Technology and Research from a Sutra Library with rich knowledge treasures into a knowledge treasure house without walls, connecting scientific and technological research and development and industries, and providing an environment for nurturing high-tech industries.
In 1996, it established the first entrepreneurial and cultivation center in Taiwan. The following year, it obtained the NBIA membership of the American Enterprise Cultivation Association, and is committed to building Taiwan to become the world's leading entrepreneurship and cultivation base for new technology. In 2006, it was awarded the "Best Breeding Center of the Year" award by the American Enterprise Cultivation Association, setting a precedent for the association's 20 years since its founding. In 2013, the Innovation Cultivation Center and Open Laboratory were transformed into TVC's new technology accelerator; in 2020, it won the New Cultivation Award of the 17th National Innovation Award.
The Institute of Industrial Research and Research continues to build new startup companies with high scientific and technological content in Taiwan, including Nangang IC Design and Development Center, Zhongchuang Park of the Ministry of Economics, Nanchuang Park of the Ministry of Economics, Shalun Green Energy Technology Demonstration Field, Hsinchu Entrepreneurship Cultivation Center-Light Restoration Hospital Area, etc., and has cultivated thousands of companies, including information and communications, materials and chemicals, biomedical and medical materials, electronics and optoelectronics, machinery and systems fields, green energy and sustainability, etc.
1996
"Open Laboratory/Entrepreneurship Cultivation Center" was officially established and is the first cultivation center in Taiwan.
1997
"Open Laboratory/Entrepreneurship Cultivation Center" has obtained membership of the NBIA (National Business Incubation Association), and is the first Chinese nurturing center to obtain a membership of the association.
2003
Establish the Nangang IC Design and Development Center to cultivate and develop new IC design companies for key technologies, creative products, and small and medium-sized enterprises in the fields of information, communications and electronics.
2004
In order to promote balanced development of the north and south regions and assist southern enterprises and traditional industries in upgrading and transformation, we will promote the establishment of the "South Taiwan Innovation Park".
2005
He was awarded the exclusive title of "AABI Award" by the Asian Association of Business Incubation.
2006
It won the "Best Cultivation Center of the Year" award from the American Enterprise Cultivation Association, setting a precedent for the NBIA to win awards by Asian institutions in the 20 years since its founding.
2013
The Entrepreneurship Cultivation Center has transformed into a TVC technology innovation accelerator.
2014
The China-Taiwan Innovation Park has been opened to assist central enterprises in successfully transforming and upgrading, becoming a high-value industry or successfully opening up new businesses.
2014
Zhubei Biomedical Cultivation Center was officially opened.
2020
The Innovation Cultivation Center and Open Laboratory are committed to the construction of a domestic innovative ecosystem. The results of tutoring the growth of the new innovation industry have been highly recognized and were awarded the 17th National Innovation Award.
2021
Hsinchu Entrepreneurship and Development Center-Luantity Hospital District is opened.
