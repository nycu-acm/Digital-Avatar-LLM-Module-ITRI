Memory
With the success of the RCA project, in order to implement the industrialization of technology, the Industrial Research Institute of Technology established "Lianhua Electronics Co., Ltd." in 1980 to transfer the four-inch wafer technology and R&D team. It was the first integrated circuit company in Taiwan, and the first company to enter the Hsinchu Science Park. In 1985, it became the first listed semiconductor company in Taiwan.After that, technological protectionism emerged internationally, making it difficult for Taiwan to transform from overseas technology. In 1984, the Institute of Technology Research and Research began the "VLSI Project" and invested in research and development on its own. At the invitation of Sun Yun-chi, then President of the Executive Yuan, and others, in 1985, Dr. Zhang Zhongmou returned to Taiwan to serve as the Director of the Institute of Technology Research and Development and developed six-inch wafer technology.
In 1986, the VLSI experimental factory was officially opened. In order to make the six-inch integrated circuit experimental factory have benefited, it cooperated with Philips, the Netherlands.In 1987, the "Taiwan Integrated Circuit Manufacturing Company" was established, which transferred 98 people in factories, equipment, technology and personnel, and pioneered the global integrated circuit foundry and manufacturing operation model.IC design companies only need to entrust manufacturing the designed products, and do not need to invest in setting up huge wafer fabs, which makes Taiwan’s IC design companies live one by one like mushrooms after a rain.In 1989, the "Taiwan Optical Mask Company" was established to provide domestic integrated circuit design companies with optical mask production services, reduce the production cost of integrated circuit products, shorten the profit time and improve market competitiveness.
Through the development of branches and leaves, since the 1990s, Taiwan's semiconductor industry chain has gradually been completed. To this day, Taiwan's semiconductor industry is world-famous. The Institute of Technology and Research continues to build the next generation of semiconductors. In 2010, it built the first "Three-dimensional Volume Volume Circuit (3DIC)" R&D laboratory in Asia with a complete 12-inch 3DIC core process-silicon substrate perforation (TSV).In 2021, we will work with Arm, the world's leading semiconductor chip core core silicon chip manufacturer, to jointly build a new IC design platform to enhance the market share of Taiwan's IC design industry in the global market.In 2021, the "Southern Rainforest Plan" was announced to jointly invest in the development of compound semiconductors and automotive power electronics in production, government, academic and research to create a semiconductor rainforest for the South Taiwan industry.
1980
The first professional four-inch wafer manufacturing company in the country, "Lianhua Electronics Company", was derived and established, setting a precedent for the transfer of research institutions to establish private enterprises.
1981
The CIC181 and CIC481 melody generator integrated circuit products were successfully developed, and the technology was transferred to UMC.This research and development breaks through the limitations of single tones and has melody generator assembly circuits with combined accompaniment and multiple instrumental sound effects.
1982
Create a leading IC music card in China, which can produce different musical melodies.Later, IC music cards were introduced into the industry and became a popular product in the early days of China Unicom.
1983
Execute the "VLSI Project".
1984
The super-large integrated circuit (VLSI) experimental factory is under construction.
1986
The super-large integrated circuit experimental factory was officially opened.
1986
In order to make the experimental factory effective, we signed a contract with Philips, the Netherlands.
1987
The "Taiwan Integrated Circuit Manufacturing Company" was derived and established to create the first global integrated circuit foundry and manufacturing operation model.
1989
A "Taiwan Photocoat Company" was established to provide photocoat production services.
2010
The "Three-dimensional Volume Volume Circuit (3DIC)" R&D Laboratory was established, and it is the first laboratory in Asia with a complete 12-inch 3DIC core process-Silicon Via (TSV).
2021
It has jointly established a new IC design platform with Arm, a major semiconductor chip core, to increase the market share of Taiwan's IC design industry in the global market.
2021
Announced the launch of the "Southern Rainforest Project" to work with industry, government, academic and research to invest in the development of compound semiconductors and automotive power electronics to create a semiconductor rainforest for the South Taiwan industry.
