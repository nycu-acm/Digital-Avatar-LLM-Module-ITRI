Taiwan's "PC Kingdom" is well-known worldwide. From e-sports brands, industrial computers, laptop OEMs, laptop metal case, laptop battery modules, etc., all of them are gifted students in the world's top class. In 1983, microcomputers were leaping towards personal computers. The Industrial Research Institute and Taiwan Factory developed the first IBM PC/XT compatible personal computer PC-100 equipped with independent research and development of ERSO BIOS software, successfully opening up the road to export. In 1990, 46 manufacturers established a notebook computer industry alliance to establish industrial standards and division of labor, and laid the reputation of the computer kingdom.
In addition, in order to allow computers to process Chinese information, the Institute of Technology began to develop Chinese text recognition technology since 1983. In 1989, it published a smart online handwritten Chinese recognition system, allowing people to communicate with computers through electronic pens and Chinese. It is a simple and convenient communication weapon. In 1995, the Chinese recognition system was developed, so that computers could understand handwritten Chinese. The recognition rate was as high as 94-96%. It was used in taxation, form processing, credit card applications, etc., and was also used in libraries, bringing a major breakthrough in the CD-ROM of the collection to the Central Library.
1979
Develop Chinese No. 2 computer and actually apply it to the Telecom Training Institute of the Ministry of Transportation to perform Chinese display and data editing work.
1980
Develop microcomputer technology and successfully develop a series of CMC microcomputers, laying the foundation for the development of personal computers in the future, opening a new page for the domestic computer industry.
1981
Admitted by the commission of China Petroleum Corporation, we have completed the development of my country's first computer-controlled tanker.
1982
Complete the "Product Development Procedure Specification" Yangtze River Thousand Miles Map, and design multiple review levels and related precautions in it to improve R&D quality, and lay the standard process for domestic computer system product development.
1983
The development of a personal computer (PC-100) compatible with IBM PC/XT is the first legal and self-developed personal computer in China. It is equipped with its own ERSO BIOS to establish the booming development of the personal computer information industry.
1985
For the second time, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has successfully developed the IBM PC/AT-compatible computer PC-400 with three companies, including Datong, Quannao, and Jingye, so that my country's information industry can continue to grow.
1989
Publishing a smart online handwritten Chinese identification system allows people to communicate with computers through electronic pens and Chinese, is a simple and convenient communication tool.
1995
Develop a Chinese identification system so that computers can understand handwritten Chinese, with a recognition rate of up to 94-96%, and is used in taxation, form processing, credit card applications, etc.
1997
Develop business card computers, condense the host of personal computers into a business card size, laying the foundation for Taiwan's development of smart handheld devices.
