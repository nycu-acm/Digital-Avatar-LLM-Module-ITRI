Measuring
Based on the cornerstone of metrology standards, the Industrial Research Institute has developed industrial measurement technology and testing and verification services to establish a global product quality for our country.With a slight slight loss in measurement technology, the product quality will be greatly different. Precision technology industries such as semiconductors, energy, medicine, aviation, etc., all use measurements to ensure that the production of goods meets international standards and regulations and has the most advanced market competitiveness.
The National Weights and Measures Laboratory of Maintenance and Operations of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, which also controls my country's Weights and Measures Standards, develops forward-looking measurement technology, and provides testing and verification services to assist the industry in improving its manufacturing capabilities.For example, in 1991, the photoelectric three-dimensional object surface measurement system was completed, and high-efficiency photoelectric detection and measurement technology was established to improve the level of domestic automation. The Optoelectronic Semiconductor Measuring Laboratory was established in 1994. It is the first LED measurement laboratory in China to pass the CNLA Laboratory Certification System (CNLA).It is committed to the analysis, research and testing of photoelectric semiconductor materials and component characteristics. It has been recognized by the National Voluntary Laboratory Certification System (NVLAP) in the United States, providing testing services for solid-state lighting products. In 1998, it was approved by the Federal Food and Drug Administration (FDA) and recognized as a third-party review agency of the Premarket Notification (510(k)) for the second-grade medical equipment.It is the first 510(k) medical equipment review agency in Asia, allowing 11 medical materials in China to be marketed in the United States and become a bridge for cooperation in medical equipment management between China and the United States.In 2000, a 3 GHz spectrum analyzer for dynamic signals that can be used in the measurement of communication products was developed for the first time in China.In 2004, the first national transportation solar light testing system was completed. In 2007, it became the first unit to develop "density standard liquid" in China. The solar photoelectric testing laboratory in 2009 passed the TAF certification and provides domestic PV industry testing services. In 2010, we cooperated with the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) to complete the world's first "non-stable frequency dual-optical comb ranging system".In addition to being the optical frequency measurement in its own laser spectroscopy, it can also be used in optical atomic clocks, respiratory molecular component analysis (can be used for diagnosing diseases), fiber communication, laser radar (LIDAR, used for directional and ranging), etc. In 2012, the LED lighting test laboratory obtained IECEE CBTL qualification, becoming the first international LED lighting test laboratory in China that meets international standards IECEE, providing domestic LED operators with more instant and local international qualified testing.In response to the miniaturization of semiconductor processes, the "New Generation Solution Nanoparticle Monitoring System SuperSizer" was developed in 2015, which can measure particles at a minimum of five nanometers, allowing the semiconductor industry to see smaller particles "Hawkeye" and improve process difficulties. In 2017, it was derived from the establishment of "Zhaosheng Nano" to check the yield of advanced processes.In 2017, "Yiliu Micro Testing Company" was established to use microfluidic chip system technology to solve the limitation that liquid samples cannot be detected under vacuum by electron microscope.In 2022, it will pass the TAF certification and become a greenhouse gas verification agency, providing Chinese operators with carbon inspection related services that meet international standards.In 2022, semiconductor 2nm GAA process X-ray measurement technology and online wafer-level high-deep and aspect ratio TSV detection technology will be developed to provide important solutions for the yield of advanced semiconductor processes.
1995
Complete the "optical three-dimensional scanning system", and can perform continuous scanning of workpieces, with a scanning speed of more than 5,000 points per second, with an accuracy of up to 0.05mm, and a 360-degree full-form measurement.
2000
The development of the "Dynamic Signal 3GHz Spectrum Analyzer" is the first domestically developed and successfully used in the measurement of spectrum, power, or EMI, EMC tests of communication products.
2004
Completed the first "Travel Number Sunlight Test System" in the country, providing various high-brightness LED Traffic Number Light Module Test System, and excluded cases of insufficient signal due to excessive sun reflected light.
2005
The "Measuring Technology Company" was established, based on instrument calibration and supplemented by instrument component sales, and is an instrument manufacturer and service provider.
2007
The launch of "Liquid Density Reference Substance and Electronic Density Meter Verification Technology" is the first unit to develop "density standard liquid" in China.
2010
We have cooperated with the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST) to complete the world's first "non-stable frequency dual-optical comb ranging system".
2014
The "In-Line Compact Thermal Analyzer (ICTA)" was developed to measure the complete thermal structure of LED packaging components within 3 minutes. It was the most efficient thermal structure analyzer in the world at that time and won the 2014 Global Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Award.
2015
The "New Generation Solution Nanoparticle Monitoring System SuperSizer" was developed to measure the distribution of particles in chemical solutions using gas suspended particle measurement technology, allowing the semiconductor industry to see smaller particles "hawkeye" and improve process dilemma.
2017
The "Yiliu Micro Testing Company" was derived and used microfluidic chip system technology to solve the limitation that liquid samples cannot be detected under vacuum by electron microscope.
2017
"Zhaosheng Nano" was derived and established, using the world's first aerosol particle size measurement technology, applied to the online monitoring of micronom particles in solution to solve the confusion of micro bubbles in solution.
