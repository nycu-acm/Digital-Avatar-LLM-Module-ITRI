In the 1990s, Taiwan became a kingdom of notebook computers, and mobile phones began to flourish. Lithium batteries met the needs of lightweight and short 3C products such as laptops and mobile phones, and had a promising development prospect. However, at that time, both lithium battery materials and production technology were in the hands of the United States and Japan.In 1993, the Industrial Research Institute invested in the research and development of lithium battery positive electrode materials and electrolytes. The research and development results have been gradually transferred to multiple battery factories, which has promoted talent investment in the industry, and has given rise to the first wave of Taiwan's lithium battery investment.
After 2000, the Institute of Technology and Research broke through the patent shackles of major manufacturers, moved towards technological independence, and continued to develop high-stable and high-capacity lithium battery positive electrode materials.In addition, the nano-refining technology has also been used to develop "STOBA ® high-safe lithium battery material" to overcome the high heat and explosion problems caused by short circuits of high-capacity lithium batteries, transfer technology to domestic and foreign manufacturers, and enter the international brand lithium battery supply chain to drive the development of Taiwan's battery industry.In 2008, it established Dazhen Energy with Taiwan's major battery manufacturer Tongzhen Company. It is the first professional company in Taiwan's power battery energy management system. It will later develop into Taiwan's largest supplier of two-wheeled motor vehicle battery modules.
Since 2010, the battery research and development of the Institute of Technology has flourished, including: "Chemical SEI modified long-endurance electric vehicle lithium battery", which improves battery life and life; the "high-speed charging and discharge aluminum battery" technology that can be recharged in one minute has driven the transformation and upgrading of my country's battery industry; "high-energy and high-safety resin solid-state batteries" replace flammable electrolytes with high ion conductive resin (NAEPE) materials to improve the safety of lithium batteries; make good use of out-of-service batteries and develop "RAIBA dynamic recombination and self-regulation battery array system", which promotes sustainable use of resources and helps the industry grasp the next generation of green energy business opportunities.
1993
Develop synthetic formulas of positive electrode materials and electrolytes to give birth to Taiwan's lithium battery industry.
1996
Completed secondary lithium batteries with lithium cobalt oxide (LiCoO2) as electrode material, and promoted Delta JetNet, Pacific Optoelectronics, Huaxin Lihua, Xiexi Electric and other companies in the following year, and established a production line of lithium-ion batteries with an annual output of 80 million.

2000
Develop lithium manganese square battery technology with a higher technical level and transfer it to Liwei Energy Company. The era of mass production of Taiwan's lithium battery has officially begun, which is of contemporary significance.
2001
Break through the US Bellcore's patent for polymer batteries and develop a new generation of polymer lithium batteries independently developed in China.
2003
Develop high-stability and high-capacity lithium battery positive electrode materials, use nanosurface modification technology to overcome the high heat release characteristics of high-capacity lithium battery positive electrode materials, and increase the standby time of color mobile phones.
2008
With power lithium battery technology and related patents, we cooperated with Tongzhen Company to establish Dazhen Energy Company, becoming the first professional company in Taiwan to have power battery energy management systems.In the future, it will become Taiwan's largest supplier of light electric vehicles, electric locomotives and electric bicycle battery modules.
2009
"High-safety STOBA lithium battery" won the world's top 100 science and technology research and development awards, which can prevent high heat and explosion caused by battery short circuits and greatly improve the safety of lithium batteries.
2016
It jointly developed the "high-speed charging and discharge aluminum battery" with Stanford University, which is the world's first aluminum battery that can be charged and discharged stably. It only takes one minute to charge and discharge, and can be recharged and discharged for more than 100,000 times. It won the 2016 Global Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Awards and the 2017 American Edison Award.
2017
The development of "Chemical SEI modified long-endurance electric vehicle lithium battery" can double the battery life and extend the life to ten years.
2019
"RAIBA Dynamic Recombination and Self-Adjustment Battery Array System" integrates energy storage systems with new and old battery modules, reduces the ineffective energy of the battery system and extends the system's cycle life. It won the 2019 Global Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Awards and has cooperated with Zhimao Electronics, Huacheng Motors, and gas stations to transform into charging stations.
2020
Developed "high-energy and high-safety resin solid-state batteries" to solve the safety problems of high-energy lithium batteries and significantly increase the energy density of the battery module. It won the 2020 Global Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Awards, and has been transferred to Taiwan's CNPC, Formosa Plastics, and Yiliang Ges Technology.
