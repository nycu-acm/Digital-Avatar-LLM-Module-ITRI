The birth of the Institute of Technology
In 1971, the Diaoyutai incident occurred and the Republic of China withdrew from the United Nations; in 1972, Taiwan and Japan cut off diplomatic relations; in 1973, a global oil crisis broke out, and oil prices rose continuously.The setbacks in diplomacy and the insulting inflation immediately put the domestic economy into a depression.Sun Yun-chi, then Minister of Economics, felt deeply that Taiwan "if it doesn't do it, it won't be able to catch up!" and decided to merge three units, including the United Industrial Research Institute, the United Mining Research Institute and the Metal Industry Research Institute to form a research institute, and operate it as a consortium legal person, so that it can get rid of the restrictions on employment, treatment and equipment procurement of public units, and be more flexible in employment, salary and management, and is no longer a government unit.

Semiconductor Industry
The source of childhood memory electronic watches, singing music cards, and even the world-famous semiconductor industry is a breakfast party half a century ago.In 1974, seven people including Sun Yun-hui, then Minister of Economic Affairs, Gao Yushu, Minister of Transportation, Wang Zhaozhen, Director of the Institute of Technology, Kang Baohuang, Director of the Institute of Telecommunications, Fei Hua, Secretary-General of the Executive Yuan, Fang Xianqi, Director of the State Administration of Telecommunications, and Pan Wenyuan, Director of the Research Office of the American Radio Company, and a breakfast meeting at the Xiaoxin Soymilk Store became the starting point of Taiwan's semiconductor industry. Pan Wen-yuan then went into seclusion at the Yuanshan Hotel and wrote the "Draft of Integrated Circuit Plan".Hu Dinghua, then deputy director of the Electronic Industry Research and Development Center of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, became the first member of the integrated circuit project. In 1976, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research signed a license contract for the transfer of integrated circuit technology with the US RCA company to introduce semiconductor technology.

Communications Industry
Taiwan's "PC Kingdom" is well-known worldwide. From e-sports brands, industrial computers, laptop OEMs, laptop metal case, laptop battery modules, etc., all of them are gifted students in the world's top class. All of this originated from the pressure of the government to ban video game machines in the early 1980s. Under the pressure of the government to ban video game machines, Taiwan's electronics industry turned to counterfeit production of Apple II personal computers in order to survive, which also caused a lawsuit from the United States.In order to avoid accusations from Apple computers, the Industrial Research Institute, together with five manufacturers including Acer, China Computer, Jingye, Xinli, and Chengzhou, jointly developed a personal computer (PC-100) compatible with IBM PC/XT. It is the first legal and self-developed personal computer in China, laying the foundation for the booming development of the domestic personal computer information industry.

Materials and chemical industry and nanometers
In the 1990s, Taiwan became a kingdom of notebook computers, and mobile phones began to flourish. Lithium batteries met the needs of lightweight and short 3C products such as laptops and mobile phones, and had a promising development prospect. However, at that time, both lithium battery materials and production technology were in the hands of the United States and Japan.In 1993, the Industrial Research Institute invested in the research and development of lithium battery positive electrode materials and electrolytes. The research and development results have been gradually transferred to multiple battery factories, which has promoted talent investment in the industry, and has given rise to the first wave of Taiwan's lithium battery investment.

Vehicle industry
Locomotives are the main transportation tool for Chinese people, with the highest density in Asia and are known as the kingdom of motorcycles. However, Taiwan did not have relevant technical support before the 1950s, and mainly sold imported complete motorcycles. After the 1960s, due to the gradual rise of industry and commerce, employment opportunities increased, and locomotives gradually replaced bicycles and became the mainstream transportation tool. Although Taiwan can build a body, it has no domestic engine available.Driven by government policies and market demand, the Industrial Research Institute began to invest in the research and development of the locomotive industry. In the 1980s, it made the first "jet engine Sukda MIRL80i" in Taiwan that could be launched almost immediately from start to finish. Its jet system is more advanced than foreign Sukda.

Textile Industry
The textile industry is an important contributor to Taiwan's post-war economic development. In the 1970s, textile and garment exports accounted for nearly 40% of Taiwan's total exports, ranking among the world's four largest textile exporters, along with Hong Kong, Italy and South Korea. However, in the face of the pursuit of backward countries, wages rose and environmental issues emerged, and the textile industry was moving towards the development of high-value-added products.The Industrial Research Institute and the Industry work together to cooperate with the textile manufacturer Hualong Company to develop the "Fine Danny PET Silk Process", which greatly improves the productivity of polyester fibers and paves the way for the future research and development of microfibers.

Optoelectronics Industry
While the development of personal computers, the Industrial Research Institute is also actively investing in the research and development of computer peripheral products, hoping that the information industry's product line will be connected together, and the upstream and downstream integration will be closer, and the initial goal will be to disk drives and printers. The professional talents required for the development of these two products are all over the optical, electronic, mechanical, materials and chemical fields, and the technical threshold is high. Taking disk drives as an example, as long as there is an error in the storage and access process of computer storage equipment, the files that users have worked hard to build will be destroyed.Therefore, the error tolerance rate of disk drives is very low, only one in one hundred million.

Printed circuit board industry
Taiwan's PCB industry has been developing for more than 40 years, and has the world's largest PCB industry chain. It is the world's largest exporter of PCB circuit boards. It has won the world's leading position for 11 consecutive years in 2022, with a market share of 32.8%. It is the third largest industry in Taiwan after semiconductors and displays.In the early stages of industrial development, the Industrial Research Institute provided a good product and equipment testing environment for operators. In 1982, it invested in the research and development of PCB independent materials and technology-transformed industries, laying the foundation for the PCB giant industry.

Machinery Industry
Tool machines are the foundation of the development of the machinery industry. In the 1970s, Taiwan changed from labor-intensive to capital-tech-intensive, and developed metal tools and heavy industries.In order to enhance the domestic manufacturing foundation, the Industrial Research Institute built a precision tool machine demonstration factory in 1977, laying the foundation for Taiwan's precision tool machine and automation. In the same year, it signed a technical cooperation contract with Brown Sharp Manufacturing Company in the United States to jointly develop various tool machines with enterprises to assist the industry's transformation.In the 1990s, the technology turned to linear cutting and discharge processing controller technology to many domestic manufacturers, increasing the added value of products and making Taiwan a world-class wire cutting machine manufacturer.

Biomedical industry
Infectious diseases are an important international issue today. In 2003, Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SARS) broke out. In accordance with the instructions of senior government officials, the Industrial Research Institute urgently completed the first full-function anti-SARS hospital in the National Songshan Hospital of the Guojun. It built 120 negative pressure isolation wards in just two weeks. At the same time, it assisted in the design, supervision and acceptance of more than 600 negative pressure isolation wards across Taiwan and built a medical protection network. In the same year, it published the "SARS Patient Droplet Trap" to reduce the chance of infection by frontline medical staff and hospitalized patients.

Green Energy Industry
As global warming and climate change affect, uneven floods and droughts will become normal. The earth's water resources are limited, and all countries are thinking about water treatment measures.The Institute of Technology has been deeply engaged in water treatment technology for more than 30 years and has been actually used in more than 20 industries such as semiconductors, panels, textiles, dyeing and finishing, food, and petrochemicals. Hundreds of manufacturers are the basis for Taiwan to build a circular economy system. Relevant water treatment technology has entered international markets such as Singapore semiconductor operators, Indonesian shoe factories, and Malaysian chemical plants.
