Sharon Smart Green Energy Science City
Environmental Protection Convention
In 2017, the government launched the "Foresight Basic Plan". One of the projects was green energy construction. In order to achieve Taiwan's energy transformation goals in 2025 and to build a green international supply chain, the Sharon Smart Green Energy Science City was launched in 2020. It is the first science city in China that combines nature, humanities and science and technology.The "Green Energy Technology Demonstration Field" built by the Institute of Technology has 26 partners, with an entry rate of up to 92%, jointly creating greater value for Taiwan's green energy technology.
The Institute of Technology integrates renewable energy such as solar energy, wind power generation, biomass energy, geothermal power generation and hydrogen energy into the demonstration field, and provides a scientific and technological settlement with one-stop services such as green energy research and development technology, industrial testing, mass production, verification and media cooperation at home and abroad.The Institute of Technology has also used green energy technology to create an energy-saving display house that simulates the common form of translucent residential buildings in South Taiwan. It not only consumes zero energy, but also shows the future appearance of green energy buildings combining AI and 5G.In addition, the Industrial Research Institute has built the first "Subtropical Green Energy Building Technology R&D Test Platform" in the subtropical field, which is a full-size and rotatable building energy-saving experimental field that simulates different houses, and provides various units in need for green building technology development, simulation software and product verification.
In 2021, the Institute of Technology Research and Research developed the "Virtual Power Plant" (VPP) verification platform, using the Hsinchu ZTE campus as the dispatching center, connecting the Tainan Liujia campus and Shalun Green Energy Technology Demonstration Field, etc., to conduct on-site verification, improve the operation flexibility of the power grid, assist in the integration of renewable energy grids, and promote the goal of net zero emissions.The Institute of Technology has created the "Green Energy Technology Demonstration Field" as an important settlement, linking domestic and foreign green energy supply chains to jointly create green business opportunities.
