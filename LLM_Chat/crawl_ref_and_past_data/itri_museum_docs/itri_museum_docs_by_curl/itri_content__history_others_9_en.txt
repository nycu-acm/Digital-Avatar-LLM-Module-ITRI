Micro-electromechanical and electrical components are tiny components that combine electronic and mechanical components. Through a series of micro-processing processes, electronic processing and mechanical action are integrated to form a micro system below micron.The popular Nintendo game console Wii, gyroscopes in mobile phones, piezoelectric components in printers, dynamic control and stability systems in cars, micro projectors, etc., are widely used.
The Institute of Technology and Research has invested in the field of smart sensing around 2000 to build Taiwan's first micro-mechanical and electromechanical production line. In 2001, the technology transfer scope includes process technology, equipment knowledge-how and factory design, etc. It is the first case of whole-plant technology transfer in this field in Taiwan.In 2003, the "Micromechanical System Technology User Alliance" was established to provide relevant equipment and technology platforms to assist the industry in accelerating the development of advanced process product technologies.
Since then, the field of smart sensing of the Institute of Industrial Research has become more diverse. In 2008, Taiwan's first micro-electromechanical microphone was developed, and in 2010, soft pressure sensors were cooperated with Global Cement to help the production enter the electronics industry; in 2013, the three-axis accelerometer and six-axis inertial sensing components were developed, and the technology was transformed into the Spin-in analogy to the IC manufacturer Richtek Company, urging it to establish a new sensing business unit.Recent research and development achievements include gas sensors, low-voltage drive microphone sensing components, the first domestic PM2.5 air product sensor, the first domestic vibration sensor, single-photon sensor, thermal image sensor, etc., which promote the application of wine measuring chips, air product monitoring systems, light-driving chips, smart manufacturing equipment prediagnosis systems, thermal image protection systems, etc., benefiting the industry and enhancing the welfare of the people.
2001
It signed a contract with Huaxin Lihua Company and transferred the first micro-mechanical and electrical production line in Taiwan created by our institute, setting a first case of technology transfer in Taiwan's micro-mechanical and electrical industry.
2003
Establish the "Micro-electromechanical System Technology User Alliance" to provide relevant equipment and technology platforms to assist the industry in accelerating the development of advanced process product technologies.
2008
It signed a MEMS monitor process service contract with American business Pixtronix to assist in DMS and AP production services, and invested 50% of the shares.Later, Pixtronix was acquired by Qualcomm.
2008
Successfully developed my country's first CMOS/MEMS microphone, which can be integrated with the amplifier circuit.
2010
Cooperate with Huanji to expand soft pressure sensors, work together to expand soft electronic business opportunities, and assist the transformation of Huanji in traditional industries into the soft electronic high-value-added technology industry.
2013
Develop innovative inertial sensing technology and design three-axis accelerometers and six-axis inertial sensing elements.The technology has been transformed into analogy with the IC manufacturer Richtek, which has led to the establishment of a new sensing component emerging business office.
2016
The MEMS gas sensor was developed, and related technologies derived "alcohol sensing chips" with only 0.75 micron cubic meters, which can be placed in watches, mobile phones, and wearable devices.
2016
With "low drive voltage MEMS microphone sensing element technology", Fudi Audio Technology was established.
2017
Develop the first domestically produced and independent PM2.5 air quality sensor and invest in the public construction of domestic air quality monitoring.
2018
Develop the first domestic vibration sensor for the application of spindle/bearing life prediction, and introduce it to Far East, Fade, Quanbao and other companies for β-site testing.
2020
Using a self-developed single-photon sensor, a multi-channel light transmission (LiDAR) technology chip is completed.At present, it has cooperated with optical machine manufacturers to develop Guangda sensing products, imported into the field of unmanned transport vehicles, and assisted Taiwanese manufacturers to quickly enter the Guangda application market.
2021
Developed "vibration sensing and equipment prediagnosis system" to provide premonitor diagnosis solutions for the smart manufacturing industry.
2022
The "Smart Thermal Imaging Protection System" was developed with AI artificial intelligence and thermal imaging detection technology, and zero-contact monitoring of physiological signals, and won the CES Innovation Award.
