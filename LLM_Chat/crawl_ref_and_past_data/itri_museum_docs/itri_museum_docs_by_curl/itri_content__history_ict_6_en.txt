With the establishment of academic networks, Taiwan entered the Internet era in 1990. From early telephone dialing and accessing the Internet, to cable data machines, ADSL Internet access, and wireless Internet access, the Institute of Technology and Research has always played a key role.As early as 1986, the Industrial Research Institute developed regional network technology on its own, and started a niche application of factory network control. In the 1990s, it continued to invest in the development of Netcom technology equipment and technology transfer to manufacturers, accelerating the entry of Taiwan Netcom products overseas and achieving the world's first brilliant results.
The Ethernet network drives the development of regional networks. The Institute of Technology and Research has developed 10Mbit/s network hubs and Ether switches, which has promoted Taiwan to become the largest supplier of regional network switches in the world. In the late 1990s, the Internet network has grown explosively.The Institute of Technology and Research has developed broadband user terminal equipment and cable data machine technology and ADSL broadband data prototype machine system to assist the data machine industry in capturing more than 80% of the global market share; the development of 802.11b wireless regional network cards has entered the era of Wi-Fi, laying the foundation for Taiwan to become the "WLAN kingdom".
In view of the company's emphasis on network security, in 1996, 23 manufacturers organized a virtual private network (IP-VPN) R&D alliance with broadband encryption functions, and established an IP-VPN interoperability testing laboratory to assist manufacturers to pass international certification tests, enhance product added value, and welcome the rising network security equipment market.
In 2005, we cooperated with China Telecom to promote the fiber home delivery industry, and applied the "Ether passive fiber network" technology to solve the problem of insufficient access bandwidth;in 2009, we developed the fiber optic communication (FTTx) management system, and transferred to manufacturers such as Dongxun, Tailian Telecom, Tailin, Heqin, and Zhengwen to assist my country in achieving the goal of 4.2 million fiber home delivery in 2010.
1986
The personal computer network (EP-NET) has been developed and can connect 64 IBM/PCs or their compatible PCs, and can send 1 million bits of data per second.
1989
Complete IEEE802.4 Token-Bus/MAP regional network technology and open up the application market for factory network automatic control.
1993
The frame relay communication interface connecting regional networks is accelerated to accelerate the entry of Taiwan's network products overseas through the US test.
1994
The network hub and ether switch with a completion rate of 10Mbit/s have prompted Taiwan to become the largest supplier of regional network switches in the world.
1996
Complete key components of smart network services, and organize IP-VPN virtual private network industry alliance to assist manufacturers in systematic large-scale software development.
1996
Develop broadband user terminal equipment and cable data machine technology to assist Taiwanese traditional data machine industry in upgrading and transformation, and set a global market share of more than 80%.
1996
Develop 802.11b wireless regional network card, expand Taiwan's regional network industry map, and enter the era of Wi-Fi Internet access.
1998
Turn on the broadband transmission era of telephone lines and complete the prototype system of ADSL terminal and local data machine.
2000
Complete the development of the All IP telecommunications industry and other network integration system technologies.
2003
The development of the first PMD IC required for the application of 10Gbps optical transceiver in China is an important milestone in the development of domestic optical communication IC design.
2009
The Fiber Optic Communications (FTTx) management system developed has 12 manufacturers including Dongxun, Tailian Telecom, Tailin, Heqin, and Zhengwen, assisting manufacturers in laying out optical communication product lines, with a domestic market share of more than 80%, and assisting my country in achieving the goal of 4.2 million optical fibers in 2010.
