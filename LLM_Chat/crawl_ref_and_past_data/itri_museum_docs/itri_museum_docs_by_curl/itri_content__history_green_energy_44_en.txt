Hydrogen is the simplest molecule, with only water after burning, and is regarded as the ultimate clean energy source.Under the global net zero emission trend, the government announced the "Taiwan net zero emission path in 2050" in March 2022, with a target of 9% to 12% of hydrogen power generation by 2050. In response to the government's energy transformation strategy, the Industrial Research Institute also released the "Taiwan 2050 Hydrogen Application Development Technology Blueprint" in June of the same year, proposing the development strategy of Taiwan's hydrogen energy use technology in the next 30 years from the three major hydrogen energy applications, including power generation, vehicles and industry, and creating new business opportunities for hydrogen energy.
Hydrogen can mainly generate electricity through fuel cells. As early as 1987, the Institute of Technology invested in fuel cell research. In 2002, the "Taiwan Fuel Cell Partner Alliance" was established to integrate the upstream, middle and downstream of fuel cells, improve the technical capabilities of the industry, and lay the foundation for my country's hydrogen energy application; in 2006, the key component of hydrogen fuel cells, "bipolar plate technology", turned the technology of the key component of hydrogen fuel cells into the industry, sowing seeds for the domestic hydrogen energy industry and attracting more manufacturers to invest in fuel cell production; in 2015, the hydrogen energy station located in the Lokka Campus passed the international certification, becoming the first fuel cell testing and certification laboratory in Asia to pass UL evaluation;in 2022, a new startup company with metal plate as the core is established to actively promote batteries in the field of hydrogen energy application.
The Institute of Technology has also published a number of technologies including hydrogen production, storage and application, including low-cost and high-efficiency "high-efficiency hydrogen filtration purification modules", which can purify semiconductor and petrochemical processes for recycling and reuse; the "renewable energy electrolytic hydrogen production system" uses the self-developed catalyst/membrane technology to produce hydrogen to achieve multiple benefits of high efficiency, low cost and zero pollution; the "carrier hydrogen storage cylinder" produced with carbon fiber composites and automatic winding technology is lighter and safer to be used in hydrogen energy vehicles;the "hydrogen fuel cell system" uses industrial hydrogen power generation, cooperates with manufacturers such as Hydrogen Power and Fanxuan Technology to create its own technology energy, establish Taiwan's hydrogen energy industry chain, and achieve the last mile of net zero emissions by 2050.
1987
Received the commission of Taiwan Power to conduct a "feasibility study on the application of fuel cell power plants in Taiwan".
2006
The "bipolar plate technology" technology, the most critical component of fuel cells used in hydrogen energy, was transferred to Shengying Company, becoming the first domestic fuel cell supplier with mass production capabilities.
2015
The hydrogen energy station located in the Lokka campus has passed international certification and has become the first fuel cell testing and certification laboratory in Asia to pass UL evaluation.
2022
The development of the "metal plate fuel cell stack" can effectively improve the power density and life of the battery and can be used as hydrogen power generation such as transportation vehicles and drones.
2022
The "fixed-type solid-state oxide fuel cell" is developed to generate electricity using industrial waste hydrogen, and convert carbon and hydrogen fuel energy into electricity output through electrochemical reactions.
2022
Research and develop "Hydrogen storage cylinders for vehicles" that are highly safe, lightweight and corrosion-resistant.
2022
The "renewable energy electrolytic hydrogen production system" is developed to use solar photoelectricity and wind power to obtain hydrogen.
2022
The "high-efficiency hydrogen filtration purification module" is developed to purify, recycle and recycle the residual hydrogen in semiconductor, petrochemical and other industries.
2022
Release the "Technical Blueprint for the Development of Hydrogen Application in Taiwan 2050".
