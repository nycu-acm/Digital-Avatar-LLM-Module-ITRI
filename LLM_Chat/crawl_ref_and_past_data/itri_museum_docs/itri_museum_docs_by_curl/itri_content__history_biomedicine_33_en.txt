TIBIC
Infectious diseases are an important international issue today. In 2003, Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SARS) broke out. In accordance with the instructions of senior government officials, the Industrial and Research Institute urgently completed the first full-function anti-SARS hospital in the Guojun Songshan Hospital. It built 120 negative pressure isolation wards in just two weeks. At the same time, it assisted in the design, supervision and acceptance of more than 600 negative pressure isolation wards across Taiwan and built a medical protection network. In the same year, the "SARS Patient Droplet Trap" was published to reduce the chance of infection by frontline medical staff and hospitalized patients.
The global epidemic in 2019 was spreading, and the Institute of Technology and Research also used scientific and technological energy to assist in building a science and technology epidemic prevention network to protect the health of the people from four stages: prevention, diagnosis, treatment, and post-epidemic.During the prevention stage, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research participated in the national mask team, assisting mask equipment manufacturers to expand production capacity in just one week, use domestic raw materials, develop meltblown polypropylene materials in the middle layer of medical masks, actively break through the restrictions on foreign technology monopoly, and create an opportunity for Taiwan's self-made mask supply chain; in order to screen patients with fever, "thermal imaging body temperature abnormality detection technology", combined with AI artificial intelligence applied to access control, it was introduced and applied to hundreds of institutions such as the Ministry of Economic Affairs, Penghu Hospital, and TSMC to assist in the improvement of the epidemic prevention mechanism.
During the disease detection and diagnosis stage, in 2020, the Institute of Technology and Research and Development developed the "iPMx molecular rapid test system", which can complete nucleic acid testing in 40 minutes, and will be sold nationwide in Japan in 2022; in 2020, the first positive pressure testing booth in Taiwan was developed to create a safer testing environment for front-line medical personnel. When the epidemic suddenly increased the following year, it worked with manufacturers to build nearly 200 in Taiwan in two months; in the treatment stage, in order to reduce the mortality rate of infection, the Institute of Technology and Research completed the first medical-grade respirator prototype manufactured in Taiwan in just 17 days, and in 2021, the technology will be transformed into a master's degree and improve the epidemic prevention front.
Entering the post-epidemic era, vaccines are an important material for epidemic prevention. The Institute of Industrial and Research assists Jilongmix Biotechnology to build a GMP nucleic acid manufacturing plant. It is expected to provide 12.8 million doses of adjuvants each year in the future to meet the needs of domestic vaccine factories.As the lockdown is gradually unblocked at home and abroad, the Industrial Research Institute has pioneered the "COVID-19 Online Video Quick Screen Digital Certification Service" platform to assist the public in quickly and instantly obtaining fast screening certificates.During the epidemic, the Institute of Technology and Research has shown the spirit of "Taiwan Helps, ITRI Helps" and provided substantial support to society.
