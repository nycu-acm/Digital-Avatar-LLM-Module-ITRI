Mass energy is the most abundant natural resource in the world. Its greatest value lies in its recycling, taking into account both economic and sustainable development. It is a renewable energy worth developing.In 1986, the Industrial Research Institute and Taiwan Sugar Company jointly built the first biogas power generation demonstration factory in China. In the same year, it also developed the "aerobic fermentation technology of fruit and vegetable waste" to generate biogas by fermenting waste fruit and vegetable. In 2013, it developed the "nearly carbon-free cellulose biomass butanol" technology, and the next year it was derived to establish Dingtang Energy New Company to promote the export of Taiwan's biomass energy technology.In 2017, the era of Taiwan's pig farming industry moving towards a circular economy has arrived. With the support of the Committee of Agriculture, the Institute of Technology established the "Biogas Power Generation Promotion Office". With the "bioma energy and agricultural green electricity co-structuring technology", it achieved the historical goal of creating 2.5 million pigs in Taiwan to invest in biogas reuse (power generation) in 2018, and took the lead in the Asian pig farming industry. In 2019, the total power generation capacity reached 4.7 MW, reducing carbon emissions by nearly 50,000 tons per year.
In 2018, we cooperated with the Dutch Energy Research Center (ECN), a subsidiary of the Dutch Applied Science Research Organization (TNO), to develop catalyst gasification technology to convert biomass waste into clean biomass fuels; in 2019, the Institute of Technology and Research and Technology worked with Bufeng Group to build the first domestically produced "wet anaerobic fermentation technology" biogas power generation demonstration model factory in Taiwan in Mailiao, Yunlin, to demonstrate the scale of 2,000 pigs in the model farm, with an annual electricity output of 240,000 kWh, which not only solves the environmental problems caused by pig excretion, but can also transform into valuable biomass energy.
In the same year, the Industrial Research Institute also developed the "lignin depolymerization and sugar production technology"."Depolymerization" is to hydrolyze solid cellulose into glucose, while hemicellulose hydrolysis can produce xylose and other rare sugars. The produced sugars become important raw materials for biomass fuels and chemicals. Agricultural waste, such as oil-palm fruits that have been squeezed into high-value products such as xylitol, amino acids, or ethanol fuels, through chemical or biological processes. This technology has been transformed into Malaysian palm oil operators to establish a sustainable raw material supply chain for energy and chemicals, which will create larger new biomass energy business opportunities.
1986
"Anaerobic fermentation technology for fruit and vegetable waste" can solve many environmental protection problems of fruit and vegetable waste, and generate a large amount of biogas for use.
1986
The first biogas power generation demonstration factory in China was built in cooperation with the Animal Products Research Institute of Taiwan Sugar Company.
1991
"Solid-state derivative fuel RDF-5 Manufacturing Plant" successfully transforms waste paper slag, organic sludge, rice stalk and other waste into derivative fuel.
2013
"Nearly carbon-free cellulose biomass butanol" technology is sublicensed and established Dingtang Energy New Company, leading Taiwan to become an exporter of biomass energy technology.
2018
Cooperating with the Dutch Energy Research Center (ECN), a subsidiary of the Dutch Applied Science Research Organization (TNO), we will develop catalyst gasification technology to convert biomass waste into clean biomass fuels, enhance my country's energy independence, provide stable electricity, and reduce carbon dioxide and waste.
2019
The "lignin depolymerization and sugar production technology" is developed, and the sugars produced can become important raw materials for biomass fuels and chemicals, and the technology is transferred to Quanyu Biotechnology from Malaysia.
2019
Working with Bufeng Group, we will build the first domestically produced "wet anaerobic fermentation technology" biogas power generation demonstration model factory in Taiwan, in Mailiao Township, Yunlin County.
