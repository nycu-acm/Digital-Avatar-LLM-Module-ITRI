Textile manufacturing
The dyeing and finishing process of the textile industry, the water resources and sewage treatment consumed account for a large proportion of the cost. The "Zero Emissions of Hazardous Chemicals" (ZDHC) formed by international sports brands has set strict standards for "Zero Emissions of Hazardous Chemicals" to drive supply chain investment to innovative research and development that saves water and reduces waste.
The Institute of Industrial Research has assisted the textile industry in transformation and upgrading for many years. In 2001, it integrated the wastewater treatment technology in the hospital and applied it to the dyeing and finishing industry.In 2018, the world's first "supercritical fluid dyeing and functional synchronization technology" was created to "no water" to replace water with carbon dioxide for dyeing and finishing, and simultaneously imparting functions such as moisture absorption and sweating. After use, carbon dioxide can also be recycled through a gas valve to reuse, zero wastewater and meets the net zero emission trend.It has been introduced into the textile industry and is the driving force of the circular economy of the textile industry.
In addition, in order to solve the problem of environmental pollution caused by high-pollution chemical dye production, the Industrial Research Institute also developed microbial dyeing technology in 2021 to modify the microbial metabolism path through genetic engineering, supplemented by fermentation technology to produce dyes that meet commercial needs. Currently, 3 primary colors have been produced and dozens of colors have been mixed to provide high-quality multi-color dyes with an industrially friendly environment, leading the industry to a new era of net zero perpetuity.
2001
Integrate waste treatment and chemical environmental technology to assist in the integration and application of wastewater treatment technology in the dyeing and finishing industry.
2018
The Industrial Research Institute and the textile manufacturer Ruhong cooperated to develop the "supercritical fluid dyeing and functionalization synchronization technology" that "no water use", filling the industrial gap from the material and technical ends, and opening a smooth road to the blue ocean market for Taiwan's textile industry.
2021
"Microbial dye development and fiber application" is transformed with commonly used safe microorganisms, and uses fermentation and separation and purification processes to produce several natural dyes for cotton and nylon, which are low-carbon and environmentally friendly processes.
