Textile manufacturing
The textile industry is an important contributor to Taiwan's post-war economic development. In the 1970s, textile and garment exports accounted for nearly 40% of Taiwan's total exports, ranking among the world's four largest textile exporters, along with Hong Kong, Italy and South Korea. However, in the face of the pursuit of backward countries, wages rose and environmental issues emerged, and the textile industry was moving towards the development of high-value-added products.The Industrial Research Institute and the Industry work together to cooperate with the textile manufacturer Hualong Company to develop the "Fine Danny PET Silk Process", which greatly improves the productivity of polyester fibers and paves the way for the future research and development of microfibers.
In 1990, the Institute of Technology and Research invested in the research and development of microfibers, which have the characteristics of waterproof, moisture permeability, breathability, and thermal insulation, and can make various detailed clothing and artificial leather.Sanfang Chemical, which started with synthetic leather, is facing shrinking its shoemaking industry and seeking transformation. It cooperates with the Industrial Research Institute to develop 0.15 Danny Microfiber and establishes the first microfiber artificial leather factory in Taiwan, making Sanfang the world's top three artificial leather suppliers.
After the successful microfiber, the Institute of Technology continued to innovate in mass production technology and new fibers, and developed "photochrome-distorted fibers", "fast polymerization catalyst technology", "new polyester composite yarn functional fabrics", "hollow microporous polyester fibers", and "positive quadrilateral nylon 66 industrial fibers". In 2002, it promoted the establishment of the "New Fiber New Yarn New Scarf Strategy Alliance", and developed various functional fibers and rear yarns and cloths from the upstream with different chemical technologies, spinning processes and weaving methods, integrating the upstream, middle and downstream of the textile industry, and developing higher value-added products, laying the foundation for my country's functional fabrics to future market share of 70% and winning Taiwan's reputation as "Textile Silicon Valley".In 2011, it successfully developed chronic wound negative pressure treatment fiber materials, which entered the field of biomedical materials.
When the COVID-19 outbreak occurred in 2020, the Institute of Technology and Research used domestic raw materials to develop meltblown polypropylene materials in the middle layer of medical masks, actively breaking through the restrictions on foreign technology monopoly, and creating an opportunity for Taiwan's self-made mask supply chain.
1979
Cooperation with Hualong Company has promoted the successful industrial production of Sedanny UDY process technology and completed a commercial trial production of 8,000 metric tons.
1990
With Sanfang Chemical, we have developed microfiber technologies with waterproof, moisture permeability, breathability and thermal insulation.
1992
The research and development of "photochromic distortion fiber" manufacturing technology has better results than the market, and its technology ranks as an international leading position, and authorized Xinyan Company.
1996
Developing "petrochemical intermediate process technology" can increase the productivity of polyester fiber by 15~20%, convert technology into four companies including Dongyun, ZTE, Xinguang, Hualong, etc., and complete commercial scale trial production.
1997
Cooperating with Daruko Fiber, we will develop new polyester composite yarn functional fabrics with environmentally friendly characteristics such as moisture absorption and sweat recyclability, and provide downstream garment operators with new products to develop.
2000
The development of "hollow microporous polyester fiber" can quickly absorb and eliminate sweat, making the human body cool and dry.
2002
Promote the establishment of the "New Fiber, New Yarn, New Cloth Strategies Alliance", with 22 units including Xinguang, South Asia, ZTE, etc., integrating the upstream, middle and downstream of the textile industry to develop higher value-added products.
2002
Promote the establishment of the "Aromatic Nylon Multifunctional Application Textile R&D Alliance", with Jiyouji Jisheng, Huaran, Yuyuan, Hualan, Huachen and other manufacturers joining; and establish the "Flame-retardant Polyester Application Textile Development R&D Alliance", with Jiyouji Zuyang, Xinguang, Sanyong, Lili, Qingquan and other manufacturers joining.
2003
Cooperate with Tainan Textile to apply nano technology to develop special polyester fibers and assist the industry in developing special functional products.
2005
Cooperate with New Synthetic Fiber, breaking through traditional round fibers, and developing "Oriented Four-corner Nylon 66 Industrial Fiber and Textile Application Technology", which is suitable for use in automotive airbag bag fabrics, boarding boxes, bag materials and other fields.
2011
The research and development of chronic wound negative pressure treatment fiber materials is the first time in China to successfully develop hydrophilic TPU fiber melt spinning technology and make preforms of dressings.Combined with the antibacterial formula containing silver, it has excellent antibacterial power.
