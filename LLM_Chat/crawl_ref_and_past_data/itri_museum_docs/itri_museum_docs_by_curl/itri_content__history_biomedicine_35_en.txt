In order to repair damaged or aging organs and tissues to prolong life, regenerative medicine that utilizes cell regeneration capabilities has become a field that the biomedical community is rushing to invest in research and development.In the 1950s, with the successful experiments on cell replication technology and scientists discovered stem cells, regenerative medicine had a specific research direction. However, it was not until the introduction of bone marrow transplant technology in China that the door to regenerative medicine research was opened in the 1980s.In 1999, the Institute of Technology focused on the research and development of biomedical engineering technology, and started to develop regenerative medicine from materials and biological sciences.
In the 2000s, the Institute of Technology Research and Research learned from abroad, starting from stem cells, bone material repair, cell culture, etc., and overcame all obstacles in the field of regenerative medicine in Taiwan.In 2002, it jointly invested in the research and development of "two-phase material soft and hard joint repair technology", and transferred technology to Exacttech, the fifth largest manufacturer of artificial joints in the United States, in 2008, and set a record for technology transfer in the field of biomedical medicine in Taiwan by exclusive authorization, setting a record for the highest technology transfer in the field of biomedical medicine in this era. In 2005, Taiwan's first cell production laboratory was established, becoming a key promoter of cell therapy.In 2017, the "Abandoned Cell Generator Reactor" significantly improved the cell production and quality; in 2019, the world's first "bionic multi-protrusion magnetic bead preparation technology" was developed for cancer treatment, and in 2021 it was upgraded to "cell production automation system" to build the first automated cell factory in Taiwan, which can be widely used in cell therapy, cell tissue regeneration and repair, etc.; in 2021, "Promote tissue integration Bionic 3D printing technology" can quickly repair tissues and transform technology into biotechnology; in 2022, the "cell tissue layer biomedical material integration system" was developed to accelerate the development of cell therapy regenerative medicine in my country.The Institute of Technology is committed to building Taiwan’s regenerative medicine technology energy and assisting the industry to stand on the international stage.
2001
Human embryonic stem cell samples were obtained from Australia/Singapore ESI for cooperative research and development to establish the first human embryonic stem cell in China.
2002
Join hands with Bosheng Biomedicine and NTU Hospital to invest in the research and development of "two-phase material soft and hard bone joint repair technology".
2003
From Tulane University in the United States, it has introduced rapid proliferation stem cell isolation and proliferation technology, established relevant technologies for adult stem cell GLP/GMP laboratory, and completed the first domestic medical cell mass production laboratory that complies with GMP specifications.
2008
It cooperated with NTU Hospital to develop "Two-phase Materials Soft and Hard Bone Joint Repair Technology" with short surgical time, small wounds and quick recovery. It also transferred technology to Exacttech, the fifth largest manufacturer of artificial joints in the United States, to Taiwan, the newly created Meijing Technology Company, setting a record for the highest technology transfer in the field of biomedical medicine in this era.
2010
We jointly developed a polymer substrate suitable for the adhesion and growth of umbilical cord interstitial stem cells with Altrika Company. We used Altrika's polymer synthesis, screening and analysis technology to be used for mass production of umbilical cord interstitial stem cells 3D culture.
2011
In cooperation with the Dutch AAE company, collagen matrix materials with eye tissue repair function are processed into biological lenses with optical performance through precision optical grinding technology.
2015
The first Chinese bionic epidermis tissue "EPiTRI" has successfully established an innovative mechanism for cosmetic testing.
2017
"Abandoned cell mass reactor" has become the best tool for stem cell medical treatment.
2017
"Adhesive Cell Ranch" cultivates cells of high quality, and has developed a 3D structure to cultivate the maximum number of cells in a limited space.
2019
"Bionic polypropilation magnetic bead preparation technology" won the world's top 100 science and technology research and development awards and the 2020 Edison Award.
2020
EPiTRI's full-skin tissue printing technology signed a Taiwan-Zhejiang four-party contract, connecting the EU market to win multiple awards.
2021
The first "promote the organization's integration of bionic 3D printing technology", integrating the advantages of 3D printing, mechanical structure, biocompatibility, etc., can quickly repair bones and tendon ligament soft tissues, and won the world's top 100 scientific and technological research and development awards.
2021
Published "Cell Production Automation System", which can produce "attached stem cells and somatic cells" as biological agents or biological drugs.It has a wide range of applications, including cell therapy, cell tissue regeneration and repair, etc.
2022
The "Cell Tissue Layer Biomedical Material Integration System" is combined with the self-built cell bank and biomedical materials of the Institute of Technology and Research, which can turn the cell mixture into a "jelly-like" cell tissue layer, which is aseptic and safe to use.In the future, it can be invested in cell therapy and regenerative medicine.
