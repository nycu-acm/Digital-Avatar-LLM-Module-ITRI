98% of Taiwan's energy relies on imports, and the development of renewable energy is imperative. The Industrial Research Institute began to invest in the technological development of renewable energy as early as the 1980s. It successfully developed amorphous silicon solar cells in 1985 and developed a solar medium-temperature collector in 1986, which was of great help to the improvement of domestic solar medium-temperature collector technology and application, and established the first domestic solar house demonstration solar energy system to operate.
In order to popularize solar energy applications, in 2008, the Institute of Technology and Research Institute applied solar energy technology to people's livelihood fields such as bus shelters and convenience stores. In 2012, it developed the "microstructure lateral solar collector technology" and installed it in building glass, which can transmit light and generate electricity. In 2016, the government listed the green energy industry as one of the "5+2" industrial innovation plans, vigorously promoting green energy power generation. The Institute of Technology and Research Institute actively invested in solar cell research and development, including jointly developed with Formosa Plastics Company in 2020. "Dye-sensitized batteries are used in low-light fields", expanding the application of indoor light sources, and creating the world's first trial production line with an annual production capacity of 100,000 pieces in the Shalun Green Energy Technology Demonstration Field; the "Tunneling Oxide Passivation Contact (TOPCon) Solar Cell" in 2021 and the "perovskite Solar Cell" in 2022 both have high photoelectric conversion efficiency, and the latter has a better chance to break through the limit of the photoelectric conversion rate of silicon crystal solar cells and is extremely competitive in market.
Policies promote the increase in solar energy installations year by year. A large number of solar panels that have been eliminated can only be broken or reduced in order to cause environmental problems.In 2022, the Industrial Research Institute jointly developed the "high-efficiency and easy-disassembled solar photoelectric module" with the joint Regeneration and Sanfang Chemical, which can recycle silicon wafers, glass covers and high-priced precious metal materials, so that solar panels can be properly handled from the source to the outage, and essentially achieve a sustainable environment. This technology has also been awarded the first IEC international certificate from TÜV Rheinland, Germany, to accelerate the easy-disassembled solar modules to enter the international market.
1985
The development of amorphous silicon solar cells has been successfully carried out, and the government has promoted the solar water-heating system to promote the vigorous development of relevant domestic industries.
1986
Establish the first demonstration house for solar energy system in China to operate and use solar photoelectric equipment to achieve energy-saving effects.
1986
The development of domestic solar medium-temperature heat collectors will be of great help to improve the technology and application of domestic solar medium-temperature heat collectors.
1993
Develop solar solid adsorption refrigeration technology.
2008
Apply solar technology to bus shelters.
2011
"Green Energy Antenna Technology" combines thin high-gain antennas and solar panel power storage technology developed by the Institute of Technology.
2012
"Microstructure lateral solar collector technology" saves the cost of a large number of silicon crystal cells and considers the high light transmittance of people, and is used in building glass, building materials and solar power plants.
2013
Assist the FamilyMart Convenience Store in China's first green energy national highway convenience store. The overall design can save 20% of electricity, which is equivalent to reducing carbon emissions by about 74.4 metric tons per year.
2020
The research and development of "high-efficiency and easy-to-disassemble solar photoelectric modules" will break through the pain points that are difficult to recycle in traditional solar panels and allow the materials of various components of solar panels to be effectively separated.
2020
The "Dye-sensitized Battery Application in Smart Home Technology" jointly developed with Formosa Plastics Company has created the world's first trial production line with an annual production capacity of 100,000 tablets in the Shalun Green Energy Technology Demonstration Field.
2021
Develop the first domestic new weather-resistant and corrosion-resistant nylon engineering plastic frame material, which can extend the service life of solar modules in coastal fields.
2021
The "Tunneling Oxide Passivation Contact (TOPCon) solar cell" has an open circuit voltage greater than 700mV and an efficiency of 23.5%, making it one of the most competitive solar cells at present.
2022
The research and development of "high-efficiency and easy-to-disassemble solar photoelectric modules" has solved the technical and economic problems of recycling waste solar panels in the past, and obtained the German Rhine International certification.
2022
Perovskite solar cells have the opportunity to break through the limit of the photoelectric conversion rate of silicon crystal solar cells and are extremely competitive in the market.
