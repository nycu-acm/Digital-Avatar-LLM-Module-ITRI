3D printing
Since 2000, 3D printing (layer manufacturing) technology has grown rapidly and has begun to be used in mold manufacturing, biotechnology and medical materials, and aerospace fields.In order to assist in the high-value industry, the Industrial Research Institute has developed 3D printing technology. In 2012, it organized the "Laser Laminar Manufacturing Industry Group" to introduce the first domestic laser 3D printing equipment, and built the first domestic metal laser 3D manufacturing laboratory. Combined with more than 30 manufacturers of domestic and foreign equipment, materials, applications, etc., it promoted the development of key technologies for metal 3D printing, and applied to customized medical materials, including teeth, artificial stents, artificial bones, artificial joints and surgical instruments, providing support for smart medical care.
In 2013, we began to invest in technological research and development, and successively completed the development of AM100, AM250 and the largest 500X500X500 cubic centimeter equipment in China. In 2014, we won the first prize in the Singapore International 3D Printing Competition with our own design and manufactured orchid hairpins, winning the first prize, opening up Taiwan's popularity in the field of 3D printing.In the same year, in order to expand international energy, we cooperated with the Netherlands TNO, which has invested more than 15 years of experience in 3D printing research, and formed a "3D Printing International Alliance". In 2015, we established the "3D Printing International R&D Platform" to launch the first large-scale FDM 3D printer designed with industrial robot arms in China, which can print large-sized workpieces. Through the platform, we work with Taiwan and Europe 3D printing manufacturers to accelerate technological research and development and jointly enter the international 3D printing market.
In the aerospace field, in 2017, the first large-area laser metal lamination manufacturing equipment was developed in China. In response to the needs of large-scale and diverse mass production of metal lamination manufacturing products, Taiwanese manufacturers will strive for aerospace business opportunities.We cooperate with Hanxiang Company and Jinsheng Company to invest in the manufacturing of laser metal layering for aviation and space components, and its related specialty products research and development and production simulation and evaluation, assisting manufacturers to break through the limits of traditional processing and move towards high-performance aerospace components research and development.
In the field of biomedical science, we will promote the 3D printing and printing technology of biomedical science, assist the domestic biomedical industry in establishing special customized biomedical materials rapid manufacturing and production equipment, and aim at the field of high-value-added medical materials. In 2018, the "3D printing medical materials intelligent manufacturing demonstration field" was established in the Kaohsiung Park of the Southern Science Park, providing one-stop services from design, trial production to commercial production, meeting the trial production needs of various customized products such as hospitals and medical materials companies, and promoting South Taiwan to become a major center for the metal medical equipment industry.In 2019, we cooperated with Kaohsiung Rongzheng and Allianz Corporation to build the first domestic 3D printing medical material experience diagnosis line, selecting jaw remedies for patients with oral cancer of the head and neck as a demonstration, and assisting the medical materials and surrounding metal industries in the Southern Science Park to enter the emerging 3D printing supply chain and open up precision medical business opportunities.
