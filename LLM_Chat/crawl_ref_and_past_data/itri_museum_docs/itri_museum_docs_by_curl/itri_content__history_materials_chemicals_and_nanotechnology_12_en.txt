Nano is one billionth of a meter. When the material structure is small to the nano level, its physical properties will undergo significant changes, which can lead to new applications, from electronic information to people's livelihood chemical industry, will be improved by nanotechnology.In cooperation with the government's promotion of the "Nano National Science and Technology Project", the Institute of Technology has promoted nanotechnology from technology to application and assisted in the transformation and upgrading of the industry.
In 2001, we entered the field of electronic information and successfully built 4-inch automotive nano-carbon tube field emission displays, nano-carbon tube field-effect transistors (CNT FETs), 20-inch nano-carbon tube field emission backlight plates, etc.; in terms of people's livelihood industry, we developed nano-photothermal powders to make warm heating clothes, which greatly improves the photothermal efficiency, and reduces costs, opening up new growth momentum for Taiwan's textile industry; and developed water-based glass anti-fouling transparent coating technology, which is anti-dirty and stain-resistant, and is used in Taiwan Railway, high-speed railway, and Beijie vehicles, saving a lot of cleaning costs.
In 2002, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research spread the benefits of nanotechnology industry through the establishment of an industrial alliance.Including the "65-nanometer R&D Alliance" of the semiconductor industry, the "Nanometer Carbon Ball R&D Alliance" of the materials industry, the "Industrial Nanotechnology Application Promotion Association", the "Nanometer Biomedical R&D Alliance" of the biomedical industry, etc.; it also created the "Nanometer Mark" to help consumers understand real nano products. To this day, there are more and more new products that transform existing materials through nano technology, and nanotechnology is everywhere, entering your and my life.
2001
The first four-inch automotive nano-carbon tube field emission display (CNT FED) in the country has been developed and has a low driving voltage, high luminous efficiency and low cost.
2002
The "Nanium Carbon Ball R&D Alliance" was established, and the participating manufacturers include Hon Hai Precision, Formosa Plastics, Dongyuan Nano, Zhongru, Taiwan Rubber, Yongfeng Yu, etc. Nanocapsule has special structure and optical, electrical and magnetic properties, and can be used for heat dissipation, mechanical lubrication, etc.
2002
The 65nm Research Alliance was organized, and the founding members include TSMC, Nissan Chemical Industry Co., Ltd., Changxing Chemical, Yongphotochemistry, Synchronous Radiation Center, etc., to promote the technological integration of materials in the next generation of semiconductor industry materials.
2002
The first P-type and N-type nanocarbon tube field-effect transistor (CNT-FET) was developed in China, which is 100% compatible with the existing IC production lines, and a large-area nanocarbon tube positioning technology was developed to complete the production of nanocarbon tube circuits.
2003
The "Industrial Nano Technology Application Promotion Association" was established to promote the application of traditional industrial nano powders, including 100 organizations including Formosa Plastics, South Asia, Changxing, and Changchun.
2003
An international-level nano-cooperative laboratory is built for cutting-edge nano research and development, development of industry-university-research application platforms, and implement rapid verification and testing of domestic nano technology industrialization.
2004
Developed the world's largest "20-inch nano-carbon tube field emission backlight plate" as a backlight source for large-size LCD displays.
2004
Establish the Taiwan Nano Technology Industry Development Association.
2005
The "Nanomic Medical R&D Alliance" was established. The alliance combines biotechnology, medical care, and pharmaceutical factories to jointly develop nanobiological medical industry technology in terms of technology, market, regulations, capital and international cooperation.
2006
The "nanogold catalyst" has been developed and used in stoves such as anti-virus masks and water heaters, which can be used to reduce the harm of carbon monoxide.
2006
The world's first "nanometer" system to help consumers understand real nano products.
2007
Developed "water-based glass anti-fouling transparent coating technology", and promoted the products to anti-fouling treatment of cars such as Taiwan Railway, high-speed rail, and Taipei MRT, which can save a lot of cleaning costs every year.
2007
"Heat-resistant anti-glare light-controlled diaphragm" won the Geneva and Pittsburgh Inventory Exhibition Awards.
2015
"Nanofiber Filter Membrane" won the world's top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards. It can be used for recycling of recycled water, softening of hard water, and even desalination of seawater, and has led to the new startup Xin Changfeng.
2015
Developing nanophotothermal powder to make warm heating clothes, opening up new momentum for Taiwan's textile industry.
