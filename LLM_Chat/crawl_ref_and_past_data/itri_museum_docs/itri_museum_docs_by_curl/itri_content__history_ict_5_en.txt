Another climax of the development of personal computers is laptop computers. Because of their small size and light weight, they require more refined production and design capabilities. In order to assist Taiwanese computer manufacturers to break through this threshold as soon as possible, in 1990, the Industrial Research Institute and 46 manufacturers established the "Laptop Computer Alliance" to establish a standard division of labor in the industry. Laptop computers were developed within one year after the establishment of the alliance, and shine in the International Computer Exhibition, allowing the international community to see Taiwan's manufacturing capabilities.Although the alliance was disbanded and companies began to develop on their own, the alliance's talent diffusion benefits still indirectly spread the technology and are the sowers in the development of Taiwan's laptop computers.
In addition, the Industrial Research Institute also made contributions to establishing the laptop supply chain. In 1997, it developed the filling technology and packaging technology of micro-heat pipes, assisted Chaozhong Company in establishing the first fully automated micro-heat pipe production line in China, and assisted the company in the simulation analysis and design of laptop computer heat dissipation modules, becoming the largest supplier of laptop computer heat dissipation modules in China, with a supply volume exceeding 25% of domestic demand.In 2008, the patent cooperation model was used to sell relevant patents to Acer, allowing it to successfully counter-claim HP Technology in the United States. The two parties reached a settlement, laying the foundation for Acer's development in the US market and successfully deploying the global market.From alliances, components, to Zhicai patents, the Industrial Research Institute has always been with the industry, making Taiwan’s laptop computer market share among the top.In 2009, the Taipei Computer Association launched the "Pattern Communication Industry Patent Mutual Alliance". Chairman Wang Zhentang said that the purpose of the alliance is to rely on the resources of the Heavy Industry Research Institute to help Taiwanese communication manufacturers respond early when facing the suppression of international competitors with patent issues.
