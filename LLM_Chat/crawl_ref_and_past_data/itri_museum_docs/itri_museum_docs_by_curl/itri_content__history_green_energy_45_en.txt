Human beings have developed a linear economy for a long time, resulting in the rapid consumption of natural resources. The circular economy attaches importance to resource recycling, which is more in line with the trend of sustainable development. The cross-ministerial government of my country will promote the circular economy and avoid pollution and waste from the source.The Institute of Technology has invested in the research and development of biomass cycle technology very early. All bamboo, lemon peel, bean dregs, oyster shells, etc. are transformed into innovative products with high added value through agricultural and industrial technology. Waste is turned into gold and becomes a "slag economy".
In 1999, the Institute of Technology and the Forestry Bureau jointly established Taiwan's first mass-produced carbon kiln with "thermal cracking technology", which revitalized and promoted the development of Taiwan's bamboo charcoal industry.In 2012, the "lignin epoxy resin technology" which uses agricultural waste biomass as the source of material solved the health and environmental protection problems caused by bisphenol A in epoxy resin, becoming the first biomassed epoxy resin material in Taiwan.In 2017, we cooperated with Yongda Food to extract high-value materials such as small molecule pectin and limonene from the world's largest fruit waste residue from Rutaceae, using the green extraction process, and then established a "steady-state mass production and fermentation" technology with patented bacteria. The transformation of phlegm storage residue into a livestock replacement for high-quality feed that can reduce the potential of exhaust (methane) by nearly 20%.In 2018, we will comprehensively promote agricultural and industrial cooperation and develop a leading international "low-energy-environmentally friendly mass-produced biochar" facility. Agricultural waste has begun to have huge opportunities to utilize. In 2022, the "bacterial-carrying biocarbon" materials will be released, becoming a soil carbon sequestration material with potential potential in the next generation.
In order to slow down the earth warming effect caused by carbon dioxide, the Industrial Research Institute began to develop carbon capture technology in 2011. In cooperation with Taiwan's mud company, it established Taiwan's first "Calcium Loop Capture Carbon Dioxide Pilot Test Plant" in 2013. In order to create economic benefits and reduce costs, the "Low Carbon Generation Green Methanol Production" in 2018 synthesizes carbon dioxide and hydrogen into high-value chemicals, and launches "Flue Gas CO" in 2020
