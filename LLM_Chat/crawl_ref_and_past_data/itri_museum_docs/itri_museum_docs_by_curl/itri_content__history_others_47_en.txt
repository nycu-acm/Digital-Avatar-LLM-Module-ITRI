In the 1960s, as the global economy entered a high growth period, the volume of commodity distribution grew significantly, and the logistics industry emerged.In the 1990s, commercial activities gradually combined with information and communication technology, and the Industrial Research Institute also used technical energy to assist Taiwan's logistics industry in transformation and upgrading.In 1994, the "Electronic Label Assisted Picking System" was completed, including Sanshang Department Store, Rolling Stone Records, Jinshitang, Lai Guan and other companies, opening a new era of electronic business.
At that time, Taiwan's logistics industry was booming, and cold chain logistics technology has also become a must-fight place for military strategists.The Institute of Industrial Research and Research actively invested in the research and development of cold chain technology, assisting domestic operators in establishing "automatic warehouse for low temperature logistics", "multi-quality temperature and cold storage low temperature logistics conveying system with no refrigeration power transportation", "multi-temperature co-distribution operation system" and "full temperature strata preservation system", etc. In 2018, it was derived and established the first fourth-party logistics new creation - fresh-speed cold chain technology in the country, providing Taiwan's diversified and customized cold chain solutions.
The era of AI artificial intelligence is approaching. In 2019, the Institute of Technology and Research has combined AI and logistics, and cooperated with Yahoo, Hsinchu Logistics and Hanlu Technology to create Asia's first high-density smart warehousing, allowing the traditional logistics industry to pick with manpower to quickly upgrade; in addition, it cooperated with Hsinchu Logistics to launch self-driving logistics services from warehouses to warehouses in 2021, which is the first in Taiwan to drive on the mixed vehicle flow road of automobiles and locomotives, and promote Taiwan's logistics industry to move towards smart logistics.
1994
Completed the first vacuum insulation high-efficiency refrigerator in China.
1994
Completed the "Electronic Tag Assisted Picking System", including Jiemeng, Sanshang Department Store, Rolling Stone Records, Jinshitang, Guiguan, Mizhi Mail Order and other operators successively introduced them.
2001
Completed the "multi-product temperature-freeze-free power-carrying cold-temperature logistics and conveying system" and the automated low-temperature storage system.
2001
Assisting Darong Company in establishing Linkou International Automated Low Temperature Logistics Center, it is the first automatic warehouse for low temperature logistics at 2,000 storage spaces in the country after 300,000 operations.
2003
Establish the "Fresh Preservation Service Industry Alliance for Full Temperature and Strata Preservation Services Industry Alliance" and prepare to establish demonstration systems such as chain stores full temperature strata co-distribution system, production and sales integrated automated full temperature strata logistics center, and e-commerce full temperature strata home delivery system.
2018
Established the first fourth-party logistics startup in the country, "Xiansu Cold Chain Technology Co., Ltd.", to create a B2C cold chain logistics service sharing platform.
2019
We cooperated with Yahoo to create Asia's first "AI high-density dynamic storage and sorting decision-making system", integrating Hsinchu Logistics and Hanling, and creating maximum storage space efficiency.We won the Edison Gold Award in 2021.
2020
Integrate fleet, cooling, temperature monitoring and AI Routing technologies to create a cold chain capacity sharing platform, and has introduced full-day logistics, Green Forest, Lairfu and other operators.
2021
Cooperating with Hsinchu Logistics, we have pioneered self-driving delivery from warehouses to warehouses across Taiwan, and traveled in the busiest urban area of ​​Hsinchu with mixed traffic.
2021
The company used the "cold chain intelligent automation technology" technology to transfer to Quanji Dalin Cold Chain Co., Ltd. to promote its construction of the "Smart Fresh Fresh Logistics Park" to generate smart storage of fresh cold chains for Taiwan's first country.
