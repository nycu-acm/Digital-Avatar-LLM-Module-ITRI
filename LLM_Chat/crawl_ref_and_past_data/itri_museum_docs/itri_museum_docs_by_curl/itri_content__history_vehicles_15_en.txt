In the 1950s, Taiwan began to develop the automobile industry, but it was still unable to master the core technology of automobiles - engines, so it was unable to develop a complete automobile industry chain.It was not until 1991 that the Industrial Research Institute began to invest in the development of the automobile industry, starting with the development of the "engine" known as the heart of automobiles, and led engineers to the British Lotus Company to learn core technologies.In 1995, the Industrial Research Institute and Yulong, Sanyang, China Automobile, the Ministry of Economics and others jointly established Taiwan's first engine company, "AsRock Machinery". In 1998, it mass-produced a 1.2L shared engine and loaded it on China Wiley Commercial Vehicle.Since then, it has developed and produced a variety of engines for use by Taiwan's independent automobile brand Nazhijie.
With the development of technology, in the 2000s, the first generation of hybrid system prototype (T1) was published in 2005 and was equipped with public display in domestic cars. In 2007, the "Innovative Light Electric Vehicle LEV (Light Electric Vehicle)" was published, and in 2011, the 50kW electric power system prototype and "Smart Electric Commercial Concept Vehicle" was published, which promoted the Taiwan industry to move from oil vehicles to electric vehicles, and developed related technologies, such as battery management technology, power control technology, power conversion technology, and charging pile technology. In 2017, the startup company "Qier Xing Green Energy" was established. It is now one of the three major charging pile suppliers in Taiwan and the only company in China that can supply charging piles for two, four and multiple wheels of transportation vehicles.
At the same time, since 2000, it has fully launched the research and development of vehicle intelligent technology, such as on-board aircraft and communication technology, vehicle image recognition technology, vehicle line control technology, etc. In 2007, a number of vehicle image assisted driving module technologies were transferred to domestic manufacturers for mass production. In 2015, it began to integrate the aforementioned vehicle intelligent technology and develop autonomous driving technology. In 2019, it won the first self-driving license plate in the country, "Test 0001", which made Taiwan's first self-driving car officially landing in the open field.After 2020, we will continue to cooperate with the industry, from electric vehicle perception subsystem, second-speed transmission module, vehicle line transmission controller, silicon carbide power motor drive controller, etc. to improve electric vehicle and self-driving vehicle technology; starting from 2021, we will gradually launch self-driving buses and logistics commercial vehicles in the urban open roads with mixed traffic of automobiles and locomotives, conduct commercial transfer testing, and verify step by step from parts and complete vehicles to field, assisting my country's electric vehicle industry in developing independent technology and mastering electric vehicle business opportunities.
1989
Implement the "Shared Engine Plan" to develop the automotive industry by manufacturing automotive engines, establish independent technology, and develop the automotive industry.
1991
Innovation Company assists in planning and establishing an engine manufacturing company.
1993
It signed a technology transfer contract for my country's first automobile engine development plan with Lotus Limited, cooperated with Yulong, China and Sanyang Automobile Factory, and sent engineers to Lotus Company for training.
1994
At the start ceremony of the 16-dual prototype of the shared engine, Director Cai Xinyuan introduced the 16-dual prototype of the shared engine.(From left to right: Jiang Bingkun, Minister of Economics, Director Cai Xinyuan of the Institute of Mechanical Industry, President Shi Qintai and General Manager of China Automobile Lin Xinyi).
1995
ASRock Machinery Industry Co., Ltd. was jointly established by Yulong, China, Sanyang and the Ministry of Economics, becoming the first professional engine research and development design and manufacturing company in Taiwan.
1998
ASRock mass-produced 1.2L shared engine, and the China Willie commercial vehicle equipped with the first Chinese-made engine was officially launched.
1999
Developed a modular engine of 1800~2200cc, cooperated with Mitsubishi, Japan, and later adopted by Nazhijie Automobile.
2007
The "Innovative Light Electric Vehicle LEV (Light Electric Vehicle)" is developed, integrating vehicle, battery and information technology, and its energy consumption is only one-third of that of traditional cars.
2012
Develop key component technologies for charging control modules and connectors, and establish common standards and design verification specifications to drive the charging industry chain of connectors, chargers, and operation service manufacturers.
2013
It has signed an electric vehicle motor inverter development contract with Nippon Electric Corporation (NIDEC), which has the highest market share in small and medium-sized motors in the world, creating opportunities for the industry to enter the international supply chain.
2014
Integrate Guangyang Industry, Kangshu Technology and Lianjun Optoelectronics to establish the "Extended-distance Multi-function Electric Vehicle REX Integrated Development and R&D Alliance" to integrate upstream and downstream operators of key vehicle components in China.
2016
The first domestic "Advanced Crossroad Anti-collision Warning System" was built on the roads of Hsinchu County and City, which reduced the incidence rate and won the 2019 Edison Award.
2016
It assists Yulon Power with charging module technology to build charging columns, with a market share of 80%, and provides charging of a variety of electric vehicles such as Nazhijie Electric Vehicle, BMW Electric Vehicle, Tesla and Leaf.
2017
The company was established in Erxing Green Energy Company to develop electric vehicle charging module solutions.
2018
The "Automatic Driving Perception Subsystem Industry Partner Program" was established to attract 10 operators including Yaxun Technology, Dingtian International, Pengcheng Technology, Optoelectronics, Xinsheng Electronics, Yingyeda, Chewang Electronics, Huadian Network, Mingtai Technology and Yuanshi Telecom.
2019
The Ministry of Transportation issued the first self-driving car test license plate in Taiwan with the number "Test 0001" to Hsinchu City and the Institute of Technology, becoming the first self-driving car in Taiwan to be on the road in an open field.
2019
Cooperate with Chewang Electronics to develop the first self-driving electric bus in China.
2020
We have cooperated with Taiwan's Large Electric Power Research and Test Center and Vehicle Industry Association, and jointly established the "Taiwan Electric Vehicle Power Supplement Industry Technology Promotion Alliance" with nearly 50 industrial and research institutions including Yudian Energy, Tuolian Technology, Delta Electronics, Huacheng Motor, Feihong Technology, Qierxing Green Energy to establish the "Taiwan Electric Vehicle Power Supplement Industry Technology Promotion Alliance" to promote the unification of the interface of public charging stations and accelerate the development of Taiwan's smart transportation.
2021
Co-developed a "second-speed transmission module" with local companies to improve the battery life of electric vehicles.
2021
Indirect services for self-driving electric minibus in Zhubei city and high-speed rail are launched, and self-driving bus commercial transfer testing is carried out for Taiwan's first open roads with mixed traffic in urban areas.
2022
Cooperate with Kangshu Technology, Tota Mechanical and Electrical, and Daxin Green Energy to develop a six-in-one "Silicon Carbide Powered Motor Driver" key power system for the next generation of electric vehicles.
2022
Develop the first two-in-one vehicle line transmission controller in Taiwan to replace the original oil vehicle engine controller.
