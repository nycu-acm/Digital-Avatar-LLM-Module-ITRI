TIBIC
Medical equipment that integrates materials, machinery, electronics and biomedical medicine is an indispensable key industry in the field of biomedical medicine. Since the 1940s, Taiwan has manufactured primary medical equipment, such as gauze, bandages and other surgical dressings; in the 1970s, it mainly used infection control products such as masks and surgical medicine. However, because medical equipment involves human safety, technology is more complex than that of materials and machinery, and the regulations are rigorous, there are not many small and medium-sized enterprises in China that are capable of producing.
In order to enable Taiwan's medical materials industry to master core technologies and switch from agency to manufacturing, in 1988, the Institute of Technology conducted feasibility studies on the medical materials industry, conducted preliminary research from monitors, ultrasonics, and rehabilitation equipment in 1990, and joined the development of biomedical materials in 1992. It was subsequently recognized by the US FDA as a medical material 510(k) review agency in 1998. It was the only representative of the Asian region among the 13 510(k) review agencies around the world at that time, and played an important bridge for cooperation in medical equipment management between China and the United States.
After 1999, the Industrial Research Institute gradually assisted the industry in developing emerging biotechnical materials, such as "non-sterilized orthopedic medical materials products", "percutaneous patches", and "electronic nose technology", and developed a niche market with high-value medical materials; in 2009, it pioneered the rapid trial production service center of medical equipment in Taiwan; in 2011, it promoted Spin-in to establish a biotechnical medical business department with "microblood analyzer technology"; in 2012, it developed the "HA joint injection" technology to transfer Yongxin Pharmaceuticals, the largest pharmaceutical manufacturer in China, and promoted it to invest in advanced medical materials from pharmaceuticals across the border; in 2022, it published the world's leading new non-additive crosslinking agent implantable biotechnical materials "Composite Medical Materials Temperature Hydroglue".
In order to promote the industrialization of medical equipment, the Institute of Industrial Research has derived a number of new startups, including the establishment of Jingtian Biotechnology in 2008; the establishment of the orthopedic startup company Taiwan Minimally Invasive Medical Materials with YBT Technology in 2010; the establishment of Taiwan's minimally Invasive Medical Materials in 2013; the establishment of Taiwan's new biotechnical materials in 2013, etc., continuously cultivating a new force for Taiwan's medical materials industry, promoting industrial manufacturing and exporting, and driving Taiwan's economic growth.
