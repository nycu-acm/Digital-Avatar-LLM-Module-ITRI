Taiwan's Semiconductor Origins

Memory
The source of childhood memory electronic watches, singing music cards, and even the world-famous semiconductor industry is a breakfast party half a century ago.In 1974, seven people including Sun Yun-hui, then Minister of Economic Affairs, Gao Yushu, Minister of Transportation, Wang Zhaozhen, Director of the Institute of Technology, Kang Baohuang, Director of the Institute of Telecommunications, Fei Hua, Secretary-General of the Executive Yuan, Fang Xianqi, Director of the State Administration of Telecommunications, and Pan Wenyuan, Director of the Research Office of the American Radio Company, and a breakfast meeting at the Xiaoxin Soymilk Store became the starting point of Taiwan's semiconductor industry.Pan Wen-yuan then went into seclusion at the Yuanshan Hotel and wrote the "Draft of Integrated Circuit Plan". Hu Dinghua, then deputy director of the Electronic Industry Research and Development Center of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, became the first member of the integrated circuit project.In 1976, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research signed a license contract for the transfer of integrated circuit technology with the US RCA company to introduce semiconductor technology.
The first batch of RCA training personnel were 19, with Yang Dingyuan as the general leader and the New Jersey regional leader. The team members included Xie Jinming, Cai Mingjie, Lin Xude, Wang Guozhao and others, mainly focusing on design; Shi Qintai was the Ohio regional leader, leading Zeng Fancheng, Liu Yingda, Ni Qiliang, Cao Xingcheng, Chen Biwan, Dai Baotong, Qiu Luohuo and others to learn the process; Zhang Qingju was the California regional leader, and the members included Xie Kailiang, Wan Xueyun and others to learn the test technology;Xu Jian took Lin Heng, Huang Xianxiong and others to Florida to study equipment. After returning to Taiwan, he devoted his knowledge to the demonstration factory and became the pioneer of Taiwan's semiconductor industry.In 1977, the integrated circuit demonstration factory was completed and the first integrated circuit manufacturing production line in China was launched. The yield rate reached 70% in the sixth month of operation, far higher than the RCA Company, a parent factory of technology transfer. The technical results exceeded expectations. In 1978, the first batch of domestically produced electronic meter integrated circuits were completed, making Taiwan become the world's third largest electronic meter exporter and lay a solid foundation for my country's semiconductor industry.
1976
It signed a "Integrated Circuit Technology Transfer License Contract" with the US RCA, successfully introducing three-inch wafers and developing semiconductor process technology.
1976
Send personnel to the United States for training.
1976
The first commercial IC CIC001 design in the country was completed. It was successfully designed by Xie Jinming in RCA. After returning to China, it made a timer and handed over to customers for use, opening a new page of Taiwan IC design.
1977
The first "Integrated Circuit Demonstration Factory" in the country was completed, and the production line launch ceremony was presided over by then Minister of Economic Affairs Sun Yun-chi, symbolizing Taiwan's official entry into the field of integrated circuit manufacturing and production.
1978
Complete the first batch of domestically made electronic meter integrated circuits, and open up the history of my country's manufacturing integrated circuit products.
