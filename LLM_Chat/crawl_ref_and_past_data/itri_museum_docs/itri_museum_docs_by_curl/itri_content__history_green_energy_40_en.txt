As global warming and climate change affect, uneven floods and droughts will become normal. The earth's water resources are limited, and all countries are thinking about water treatment measures.The Institute of Technology has been deeply engaged in water treatment technology for more than 30 years and has been actually used in more than 20 industries such as semiconductors, panels, textiles, dyeing and finishing, food, and petrochemicals. Hundreds of manufacturers are the basis for Taiwan to build a circular economy system. Relevant water treatment technology has entered international markets such as Singapore semiconductor operators, Indonesian shoe factories, and Malaysian chemical plants.
As early as 1985, the Industrial Research Institute invested in industrial wastewater treatment and built the first "gas-averse fluidized bed treatment tank" in 1991. It covers a small area and has less sludge, which can effectively treat industrial wastewater; it gradually evolves to assisting high-tech industrial process wastewater treatment, and sets the first application precedent for the "Fluidized Bed Crystal Tank Technology" in 1996. Yu Hanlei Technology Company established the world's first fluorine-containing wastewater treatment plant; in recent years, it has developed from wastewater treatment to wastewater recycling and reuse. In 2021, it developed "LCD glass nanopore material", which can convert electroplating wastewater into clean recycled water, with a heavy metal removal rate of 99.9%, and has been used in the wastewater treatment system of Jixiang Electroplating Plant in Zhangbin Industrial Zone.
The Institute of Industrial Research has also applied water treatment technology to public welfare. For example, after the storm disaster, residents in the disaster area often face the problem of lack of clean drinking water. The Institute of Industrial Research uses industrial water filtration technology to meet the needs of the disaster area to create water purification equipment. After Typhoon Morak in 2009 and Typhoon Sudil in 2015, it advanced into the disaster area to purify the water for the victims as soon as possible, so that the people can have clean water to drink and bath.
1985
Completed the development of "mobile solar pure water machine", and provided the mobility power of the RO reverse osmosis pure water system with solar photoelectric templates to supply high-quality pure water.
1988
Developed "Upstream Air-Adventilated Sludge Bed Wastewater Treatment Technology (UASB)" to assist the Public Sales Bureau winery and Asian Chemical Corporation in designing and monitoring the completion and activation of UASB equipment.
1991
Cooperating with Wenhao Polymer Corporation, we have completed the first "air-averse fluidized bed treatment tank" in China, which covers a small area and has less sludge, and can effectively treat industrial wastewater.
1996
Developed the "Fluidized Bed Crystal Tank Technology", set a precedent for the application of fluidized crystal tanks, and established the world's first fluorine-containing wastewater treatment plant at Hanlei Technology.
2009
After Typhoon Morak, he cooperated with Kangnaxiang to complete the "mobile emergency water purification system", purifying the contaminated water source, meeting the drinking water quality standards, and solving the water problems in the disaster area.
2014
Work with the operators to enter the Singapore plant of Micron Technology, a major semiconductor manufacturer, to carry out water treatment projects.
2015
Typhoon Sudil passed through, roads in the Wulai Mountains were interrupted, and the disaster area was short of water and power outage. The "Qwater Emergency Water Purification Equipment" of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research urgently entered the disaster area and provided clean drinking water for local people to use every day.
2017
For the first time, we cooperated with a water company to use "BioNET" technology to improve the water quality of Donggang Creek to meet the drinking water source water quality standards, and alleviate the water limit pressure in Kaohsiung.
2018
The development of the "portable UVC LED Flowing Water Module" uses LED as the light source, which can save more than 50% of energy, and has won the world's top 100 science and technology research and development awards in the year.
2020
Develop intelligent water treatment technology, introduce technologies such as the Internet of Things, artificial intelligence and big data, and jointly establish Huanyan IoT with Advantech.
2021
In cooperation with Qimei Industrial, the "EDR Water Resource Center" of the recycled water system was built in the Rende factory. It is the first set of Taiwan's petrochemical industry and the largest EDR industrial wastewater system in Taiwan.
2021
The research and development of "LCD glass nano-pore material" can convert electroplating wastewater into clean recycled water, meet the environmental protection standards for discharged water, and has been applied to the wastewater treatment system of Jixiang Electroplating Factory in Zhangbin Industrial Zone.
2021
The research and development of "isothermal and high-efficiency water and gas separation technology" is the world's first unique technology that can use air to create clean water sources and has won the world's top 100 science and technology research and development awards.
