TIBIC
The biomedical industry is one of the six core strategic industries of the government. The Institute of Industrial Research has created the TIBIC cross-domain integrated experimental field of the biomedical industry (Taiwan Integrated Biomedical Industrial Center), which was announced in 2020. It will use three verification laboratories (human-machine interactive laboratory, usability laboratory, smart surgical robot laboratory) and four simulated clinical fields (simulated tomography room, simulated general ward, simulated operating room, simulated intensive care unit), combined with innovative technology, and lead the establishment of an international cross-field platform for medical and technology, so that medical material R&D industry can directly communicate with doctors through this platform to stimulate more technological innovation cooperation.
This is Taiwan’s first cross-domain integrated experimental field for smart medical care, attracting the internationally renowned biomedical practitioner AstraZeneca (AZ), the first to invest in the establishment of Asia’s first “future medical laboratory”; in 2021, we will work with Hsinchu National Taiwan University Branch and Zitong and medical materials companies to improve the capacity of epidemic prevention care, build a “physiological signal real-time monitoring integration platform” to assist manufacturers in introducing more than 10 clinical fields; in 2022, we will sign a letter of intent for cooperation with Hon Hai and Taiwan Medical Optoelectronics, and will conduct clinical testing at Tucheng Hospital in New Taipei City, bringing more welfare to the people.
2020
TIBIC's cross-domain integration experimental field of the biomedical industry has been launched.
2021
Work with Hsinchu National Taiwan University Branch, Zitong and medical materials industry players to improve the capacity of epidemic prevention care, and build a "Physiological Signal Integrated Integrated Platform" to protect life with technology.
2022
Work with Hengxi Enzhu Nursing Home to build a PECOLA AI perceptual care system.
2022
It signed a letter of intent for cooperation "Smart Medical Equipment Development and Smart Medical Field Promotion" with Hon Hai and Taiwan Medical Optoelectronics, and will conduct clinical testing at Tucheng Hospital in New Taipei City to accelerate the launch of the product.
