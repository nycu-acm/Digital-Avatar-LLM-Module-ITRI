It has been 63 years since the development of lasers by 2023, and the industry is widely used, but Taiwan's laser industry has faced many challenges in the past, such as the lack of independent products, insufficient independent capabilities for key components, lack of system integration capabilities, and insufficient professional talents.In order to solve the industrial dilemma, the Institute of Industrial Research has begun to promote Taiwan's laser industry's technological independence since 2007 and develop the research and development of laser sources and key components and equipment.In 2012, the first domestic metal material laser lamination manufacturing equipment was introduced, and Taiwan's metal 3D printing technology was launched. First, it focused on customized medical materials, including teeth, artificial stents, artificial bones, artificial joints and surgical instruments, to solve the problem that the complex curved surfaces of traditional metal medical materials cannot be mass-produced due to customization.In 2013, the implementation of the Laser Optics Valley Project was expanded, and the "Southern Laser Optics Valley Cultivation and Test Production Factory" was established in the Lokka Campus, and the first laser application test production factory and metal laser lamellar manufacturing laboratory were established, which led to the development of the "Southern Taiwan Laser Optics Valley Cluster".
Since 2014, a number of key laser technologies have been developed, such as Naser laser source, picosecond laser source, Pinaser laser source, blue light laser source, high-power laser cutting/welding/heat treatment processing head, the first large-scale metal accumulation manufacturing equipment in China and various laser processing optical path modules, etc., and the laser source and key module technology have been transferred to the industry, such as promoting the establishment of Taiwan's first domestic laser source company-Bomeng Technology, and guiding Dongtai to become the first domestic laser metal 3D printing equipment factory in China.In the past, there were only 4 laser source key component manufacturers in Taiwan. After the promotion and guidance of the Laser Optics Valley project, by 2021, there have been more than 15 laser source key component and product manufacturers, and 250 metal, optoelectronics, electronics, displays, machinery, molds, automobile and locomotive parts, medical materials, cultural and creative industries and other industries introduced laser applications, which has facilitated the establishment of 15 innovative companies. Through laser lectures, seminars and forums, we will promote application training and technology sharing, and gradually improve the domestic laser technology level and talent training.
Taiwan's optoelectronic semiconductor industry is booming and ranks as the world's leading position, but Taiwan lacks a verification platform for fine advanced laser processes. In 2021, it will work with the Taiwan Machinery Industry Association and the German Chuangpu Group (TRUMPF) to launch the "Taiwan Advanced Laser Application Service Center for Semiconductor and Electronics Industry", introduce the only high-end pulse ultra-fast laser source in Asia, build the first domestic semiconductor and electronics industry process rapid service center, provide fast process verification services for domestic equipment manufacturers, accelerate the development speed of domestic semiconductor equipment, and enhance the competitiveness of Taiwanese equipment manufacturers.
In terms of high-energy laser applications, we have promoted Taiwanese metal equipment manufacturers to introduce laser cutting technology, and guided equipment manufacturers to launch laser flat cutting machines, laser pipe fitting cutting machines, etc. In 2019, we assisted Taiwanese heat exchanger manufacturers in establishing the first laser full welding process production line in Asia.In 2022, we will further promote the application of direct-drive diode laser sources (DDL), complete the development of relevant laser sources and processing application technologies, assist domestic manufacturers in establishing intelligent laser welding process technology and equipment capabilities, and promote domestic leading operators such as lithium-ion battery modules/factory metal pipe fittings/turn fasteners to build domestic laser manufacturing production lines, carry out production and manufacturing technology upgrades and advanced product development, and introduce them to terminal markets such as electric buses, semiconductors, and automobiles to promote the increase in the proportion of domestic production, accelerate the formation of the domestic laser market, and promote the manufacturing industry to enter the era of optical manufacturing.
2007
It has led to the establishment of Taiwan's first "Laser Micro Process Equipment Technology R&D Alliance".The alliance members include Junhao Precision Industry, Fanxuan System Technology, Leike, etc.
2011
We jointly signed a R&D cooperation agreement with the University of Missouri in the United States to cooperate with the application of femtosecond laser technology in material processing and manufacturing processes, and extend the development of biotechnical medical products in the fields of teeth, bones, etc.
2012
In conjunction with Dongtai Precision Machinery, Yashu, Dongjie and other companies, we established the "Laser Optics Valley Industry Association" to promote the emerging laser industry by promoting the high-power processing applications, micro-machining applications, laminar manufacturing applications, laser source applications and fine detection applications.
2013
Published the first domestically produced Nanosecond fiber laser from the Institute of Technology.
2013
The first "Southern Laser Optics Valley Cultivation and Test Production Factory" was launched, driving the development of the "Southern Taiwan Laser Optics Valley Cluster".
2014
"Naisie fiber laser technology" has transferred Bomeng Technology, which has led to the company's establishment of a new laser source business unit and launched 10W and 20W pulsed fiber laser sources to achieve independent laser source technology in my country.
2015
It published Taiwan's first 3D fiber laser fully automatic pipe cutting machine, so that the entire machine and key components can be completely self-made.
2016
Establish the first large-area laser metal layer manufacturing equipment in China.
2016
Published a domestically produced nanosecond high-energy short-pulse laser source.
2018
The "Energy-saving Micron Laser Cleaner" of a self-made low-power, high-pulse fiber laser source not only saves electricity costs, but also has a size of a suitcase, with a wide range of application and materials, and greatly improves the operating precision.
2019
We call on 13 important domestic independent laser key components and laser source manufacturers to jointly establish the "Domestic Independent Laser Source Industry Alliance" to expand the application of domestic laser industry.
2020
Eksha, a high-end ophthalmic laser manufacturer, developed the "Minimal Invasive Full Femtosecond Laser" technology and signed a license with the Institute of Technology to "OCM Technology Application in Ocular Tissue Virtual Slicing Technology Module", setting the world's first case of synchronous viewing of corneal cross-section images during the surgical process, opening up a new "visual" world in ophthalmic precision medicine.
2021
Assist domestic laser source and key component manufacturers to invest in important epitaxial, packaging, and optical fiber active passive component products, and export them to Japan and the United States.
2021
Assist mechanical equipment industry operators to introduce laser technology, such as Tailifu, Yinglin, Hehe, Qinghong+㵢jie, Xindian/Lianda, etc., optimize and improve laser process technology, enhance product added value and promote investment of more than 1 billion yuan.
2021
The "Taipei Laser Industry Joint Working Group" was established with the Lithuanian Laser Industry Association, and continued to promote cooperation in laser technology application between the two countries, and introduced Lithuanian ultra-fast laser sources for application development, and established an Asian laser resilience supply chain.
2021
It has jointly established the "Taiwan Advanced Laser Application Service Center for Semiconductor and Electronics Industry" with the Mechanical Association and Germany Chuangpu Group (TRUMPF) to launch the "Taiwan Advanced Laser Application Service Center for Semiconductor and Electronics Industry" to introduce the only high-order pulse laser equipment in Asia, including four units including picosecond ultraviolet laser source, picosecond green laser source, femtosecond green laser source, femtosecond infrared laser source, etc. It is the world's highest-level application service center outside Germany, and accelerates the development speed of domestic semiconductor equipment.
2022
Develop "high-energy laser welding technology" to solve the problems faced by the traditional welding industry and drive the domestic production of equipment.
