Since 1985, AT&T in the United States has proposed patent claims to global competitors. The Institute of Technology and Research has been approved by the Ministry of Economic Affairs to complete interactive authorization with AT&T in 1993, in addition to significantly reducing the manufacturer's premium, it also ensures that my country's IC industry can continue to develop.
The announcement of the Basic Law of Science and Technology of my country was implemented in 1999, giving the Industrial Research Institute the responsibility of independently and actively applying patents. Since then, it has begun to actively build patent layout and optimize patent quality, assisting various industries to resist foreign patent owners and maintain the competitive position of the industry.Since then, many classic cases have been created and have become legends, including:
In 2001, Japan's Sharp Company, South Korea's Samsung Company and LG Company filed patent lawsuits against Chinese panel manufacturers. In 2003, the Industrial Research Institute formed a TTLA alliance with 7 domestic panel manufacturers with more than 200 patents, successfully assisting the panel industry in surviving the survival crisis, and still occupies a place in the world.
In 2008, through patent transfer, it assisted Acer and HP in successfully reaching an interactive license. At that time, Acer was already the world's top four computer brands, and protecting Acer was to protect my country's computer industry.In 2017, it once again assisted Songteng Company in successfully countering the case of the small shrimps in the United States against the big whale through patent transfers, etc.
In order to expand the benefits of the patent portfolio, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research jointly organized an IP Bank patent pool with industry, academia and research in 2020, and built a high-quality patent library for the industry with themes such as 5G, electric vehicles and net zero carbon emissions.In 2022, we will further establish intelligent partnerships with companies such as Lightbao Technology and deepen industrial cooperation.
In addition, in 2020, the Institute of Technology and Research, together with the Small and Medium Enterprise Credit Insurance Fund and 26 banks, jointly promoted patent financing evaluation and guarantee projects and technology added financing and value-preservation projects, linking technology and capital markets. So far, it has assisted nearly 50 companies in applying, and the cumulative financing amount has exceeded NT$200 million, allowing Taiwanese companies to realize the financial value of patents and technology.
So far, the Institute of Technology has been selected as the "Top 100 Global Innovation Institutions" for the 7th consecutive year, and comprehensively evaluated a number of indicators such as technological uniqueness, influence, globalization and success footprint. It is not only the institution with the most awards in Taiwan, but also won the first prize winners of the Asia-Pacific R&D institutions.The Institute of Technology and Research will continue to move forward in the future and continue to work hard for Taiwan’s high-quality intellectual and financial environment and solid intellectual power support.
