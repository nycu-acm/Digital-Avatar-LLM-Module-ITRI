Drone
Drones have a wide range of applications, and all walks of life can save time and effort with the assistance of drones. They are an important vehicle for industrial, disaster relief and logistics applications.The Industrial Research Institute is aware of the commercial drone market and is committed to improving the endurance and load capacity of drones. In 2016, it completed the world's first "UAV fleet management system" operating across continents, through 4G LTE allows operators to remotely control Taiwan’s drone fleet in the United States; in 2018, it developed a "Telecommunication Control UAV Fleet Solution" to transform the drone into the core system of air police vehicles and improve security effectiveness; cooperate with Taiwan’s high-speed rail to accurately grasp the bridge structure and damage status and protect the safety of public transportation; the "High Load and High-Range Drone" developed in 2019 has been used for pesticide spraying in Tongluo, Hangju and New Taipei Tea Garden in Miaoli County, and has also been introduced into smart agricultural applications, assisting Pingtung Fangliao Farmers Association in assessing mango transportation, and direct transportation from the orchard to the packaging yard to improve operational efficiency. It has won many international awards such as CES 2019 Innovation Award and 2020 Dubai International Drone Competition Multi-rotor Group Championship (Drone X Challenge 2020).
Drones are paired with 5G communication, and can also provide competitive events that integrate virtual and real.In 2020, the Institute of Technology and Research and Research, together with Renbao Computer, Taiwan Drone Industry Alliance and other units, held the "5G MR Competitive Drone Competition". It is the first competitive drone competition in the world that combines digital wireless image transmission and adopts virtual and real obstacle tracks for the first time, thereby promoting the complete industrial chain from operation, supply, equipment manufacturing to service, and allowing Taiwan's drone technology to enter the international market.
The Industrial Research Institute has also launched the "fuel cell drone", which has excellent long-distance characteristics and has successfully completed a series of cross-sea and alpine rescue verifications, making it the first drone in China that has passed the special physical test for cross-sea, mountain and long-distance flights of more than 80 kilometers.In 2022, the Industrial Research Institute and 10 industrial and research institutions including China Optoelectronics Intelligent Robots, ZTE Electric, and Xinpu Technology will form the "Fuel Cell and Drone Integration Alliance" to strengthen the technical strength of domestic fuel cells and Long-term Way UAVs and enhance the competitiveness of the industry.
2016
Completed the world's first "UAV fleet management system" to operate across continents, breaking through the limitation of the market-based aircraft models with a view distance of less than 7 kilometers, and can be used in aerial photography, safety patrols, and earth and rock flow monitoring.
2018
The "UAV Bridge Inspection System" has been developed, which has 4G/5G control live broadcast, scheduled waypoint inspection and smart image analysis, which can quickly complete bridge inspection operations and greatly save operating time.
2018
"Telecommunication UAV fleet control solution" solves the pain points of short flight distance and poor endurance of drones.Winner of the 2018 Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Awards in the World.
2019
"High load and high-endurance drone" is introduced into smart agricultural applications to assist Pingtung Fangliao Farmers Association in assessing mango delivery.
2019
"High load and high-endurance drone" combines a lightweight oil-electric hybrid system to create an eight-axis multi-rotor multi-function drone.
2020
Develop the world's first 5G drone e-sports system, leading the world to hold the first "5G MR competitive drone" competition combining digital wireless image transmission and using virtual and real obstacle courses.
2021
The "fuel cell drone" developed has passed the special physical inspection of the Civil Aviation Administration and is the first drone in China that crosses sea, mountains and long-distance flights exceeding 80 kilometers.
2021
Working with industry players to develop hybrid reality (MR) technology, we will hold the world's first "5G MR Luminous LED Drone Racing Expert Challenge" in Kaohsiung Yawan
2022
The smart exit station drone is integrated with the cold chain logistics and warehousing system, and the drone takes off and lands through a platform with an area of ​​about 1 meter square and a height of about 2 meters, which can achieve the effect of separation of man-machine when delivering and picking up parts.
2022
Establish the "Fuel Cell and Drone Integration Alliance" to accelerate the development of the long-term drone industry chain from fuel cell materials, system integration, data communication, drone applications, etc.
2022
Long-term flight is a major challenge to drone technology.Fuel cell drone technology is expected to triple the energy density of lithium batteries to meet the future commercial application needs of long-term flights.
