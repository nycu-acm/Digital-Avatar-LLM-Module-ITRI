Artificial intelligence and huge amount of information
In 2016, AlphaGo defeated the world Go champion and wrote an important milestone in the development of AI artificial intelligence.As early as the 1990s, the Institute of Technology and Research conducted research and development with the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) in cooperation with AI-related research, laying the technical foundation for my country's AI industry.
From data collection to the establishment of AI algorithm models, to huge data analysis to enable AI to be implemented, since 2000, the Institute of Technology has actively invested in the research and development of AI and huge data applications, including the establishment of a "smart police and security video monitoring system" with huge video data to improve the efficiency of crime investigation and promote public safety; develop huge data algorithms to assist momo shopping network and Taiwan Big Brothers to increase the viewing rate and analyze consumption behavior.
After 2010, the Institute of Technology Research and Research has also established powerful AI applications such as the "ITRI IoT PaaS Big Data Service Platform" and the "Artificial Intelligence Co-creation Platform (AIdea)" in the platform model.The AI ​​vertical industrial applications developed by the Institute of Technology are blooming everywhere: for example, "AI financial robots" in the financial field, "eye medical imaging assisted diagnosis technology" in the medical field, "beCert smart pre-certification service" to assist farmers and fishermen, and "AI high-density dynamic sorting decision-making system" to assist logistics and warehousing smart upgrades, etc.
With the rapid evolution of AI and huge data technology, the Industrial Research Institute continues to plan for the future of the industry, and includes AI in the intelligent enabling technology of the Industrial Research Institute's "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" as an important backing for the development of various application fields.At the same time, combining artificial intelligence and semiconductor technology, we joined hands with 147 operators to establish the Taiwan Artificial Intelligence Chip Alliance (AITA) in 2019 to promote the development of next-generation smart chips to high computing and low energy consumption.
After 2020, we will target the energy industry and launch a "virtual power plant (VPP)" to improve energy efficiency, using AI to achieve flexible scheduling and smart electricity use;for the next generation of vehicle and electricity technology, we will develop "advanced self-driving system" and have been tested in multiple points in Taiwan;in 2022, we will launch the "MACSiMUM" AI machine learning platform, which will provide solutions for 70% of Taiwan's small and medium-sized enterprises that have not yet imported AI, and help Taiwan's artificial intelligence and huge data industries to enhance their competitiveness.
1999
We signed a technical cooperation contract with the Computer Science Laboratory (LCS) and the Artificial Intelligence Laboratory (AI) of the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) to jointly develop smart human-machine interface technologies such as "On-voice conversation system" and "Image and Video Retrieval".
2008
"Research and development and service construction of smart police and security video monitoring systems" assists the Criminal Bureau's Science and Technology Crime Prevention and Control Center to quickly and effectively analyze huge amounts of video data of public security incidents.
2015
Develop huge data algorithms to assist momo shopping network (Fubon Media Technology) and Taiwan Big Brothers to increase the viewing rate and analyze consumption behavior.
2016
Develop the "ITRI IoT PaaS Big Data Service Platform" to assist the industry in collecting big data from machine or device sensors and processing and analyzing it.
2017
Organize a deep learning development team, cooperate with the national highway electronic toll collector "Far East Electricity Collection", and in 2020, it will lead to the first AI startup company of the Industrial Research Institute.
2017
The "Artificial Intelligent Building Energy Saving System Platform (BESTAI)" with a friendly interface and analyzing the use of building energy has won the world's top 100 science and technology research and development awards.
2018
Enable the "Artificial Intelligence Co-creation Platform (AIdea)" to gather the topic database, data database, and talent database to strongly assist the industry in developing AI applications.
2019
Develop "AI Financial Management Robot".
2019
With Jinhong Technology, it has created the world's first "handheld fundus camera" with AI analysis functions. In 2022, it won the Edison Award and the World's Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Award for "Eye Medical Imaging Assisted Diagnosis Technology".
2019
We cooperated with Yahoo, Hsinchu Logistics and Hanlu to create Asia's first "AI high-density dynamic sorting decision-making system", and won the Edison Gold Award in 2021.
2019
Cooperate with Chewang Electronics to develop the first self-driving electric bus in China.
2019
Established the Taiwan Artificial Intelligent Chip Alliance (AITA).
2021
The first to create three technologies: integrated venture communication standards, cause analysis, and AI artificial intelligence image repainting to create a "circuit board industry smart manufacturing service application platform" to assist in the digital upgrade of the PCB industry.
2021
Develop a virtual power plant that integrates diverse resources" (VPP) verification platform, using network communication technology to integrate and allocate energy scattered everywhere.
2021
Developed the "vibration sensing and equipment prediagnosis system" to provide the smart manufacturing industry's omen diagnosis solution, and assisted more than 15 tool and component operators in importing it.
2021
"beCert Smart Pre-Certification Service" assists agriculture and fishery to win the first international certification in Taiwan.
2022
Build an AI machine learning platform of "MACSiMUM" to enable enterprises to easily step into the threshold of smart IT technology.
2022
The "RGB-D AI Robot" is developed to quickly determine the stable picking point when facing new style items without re-teaching.
2022
The world's first "IV AI Fish Tank" was introduced into the National Museum of Marine Science and Technology.
