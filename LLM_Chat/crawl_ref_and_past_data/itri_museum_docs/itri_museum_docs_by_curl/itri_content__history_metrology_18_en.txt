Measuring
Measuring standards are the basis of various fields such as global international trade, medical care, public safety, environmental protection, industrial innovation, scientific and technological development, and people's livelihood economy.In 1987, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research was commissioned by the Ministry of Economic Affairs to implement the "National Weights and Measures Laboratory" which is the highest domestic measurement standard, to check domestic weights and Measures standards, establish national measurement standards, and in 1995, it was awarded the ISO 9001 international quality assurance verification.The Institute of Technology has established 15 laboratories in measurement fields, including length, mass, strength, pressure, vacuum, electricity, electromagnetic, microwave, optical radiation, vibration, sound volume, temperature, humidity, chemistry, and flow. To 2022, it has expanded to 116 standard measurement systems to meet the needs of domestic first-level instrument traceability correction.
In 2002, it assisted my country in breaking through diplomatic barriers, joining the International Conference on Weights and Measurement (CGPM), signing the mutual recognition agreement (CIPM MRA), and logging into the database of the International Bureau of Weights and Measurement (BIPM) to make the correction reports issued by my country accepted by countries and organizations, and protecting the rights and interests of domestic industries in the international market.In 2018, the International Bureau of Weights and Measures planned to redefine kilograms with Planck's constant. The Institute of Technology introduced the quality standards of silicon crystal spheres produced by the German Federal Institute of Physics Technology in the same year to accurately realize the kilograms defined by Planck's constant, ensuring Taiwan's highest measurement standard and independent traceability.
1983
The Institute of Technology and Research has joined the American National Standards Association.
1987
A calibration laboratory for weights and measures instruments was established, and it was approved by the Ministry of Economic Affairs as my country's "National Standard Laboratory".In May of the same year, the industry was officially provided with services.
1991
Signed a "China-Filipino Measuring Standard Mutual Recognition Agreement" with the South African Institute of Science and Industry Technology.
1991
Work with the Finnish Institute of Geoscience to establish a length standard of 0~432 meters.
1992
He participated in the Asia-Pacific Metrology Programme meeting as an observer and became a formal member without objection by representatives of 18 member states.
1995
Purchase a platinum-iridium kilogram prodder from the International Bureau of Weights and Measures.
1995
The Measuring Center of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research won the ISO9001 international product guarantee verification.
1995
Complete the "Iodine-stable frequency red light helium-neon laser 633nm recommended radiation" as the original standard of length, and establish the independent traceability of my country's length standards.
1996
The National Flow Laboratory has been completed and put into use to solve the problem of traceability correction of large flow meters used in various types of oil products, natural gas, tap water, etc., and strictly control the consumer rights of the whole people.
1998
It was approved by the US FDA and is the first 510(k) medical equipment review agency in Asia.
2002
Officially join the International Conference on Weights and Measures.
2003
Photoelectric semiconductor measurement laboratory.The photoelectric semiconductor measurement laboratory is the first national-level light emitting diode (LED) measurement laboratory in China that has passed the CNLA certification system of the Republic of China.
2005
Density standard reference substances are CNLA certified.
2006
It has been certified by the National Certification Foundation as the first nanometer measurement laboratory in the country.
2009
The solar photoelectric testing laboratory has obtained IECEE international certification.
2011
It has obtained the National Voluntary Laboratory (NVLAP) certification and has the laboratory qualifications for LED lumen maintenance (LM-80) testing and Energy Star testing. It can provide local instant, internationally recognized professional LM-80 lumen testing and verification services for domestic LED packaging, and provide global credibility testing reports.
2012
The LED lighting testing laboratory has obtained CBTL certification for lamp safety regulations and LED modules, becoming the first LED lighting testing laboratory in China to obtain CBTL international standard certification.
2018
The quality standards of silicon crystal spheres produced by the German Federal Institute of Physical Technology were introduced, and a new international unit system (SI) measurement standard was established.
2020
It was approved by the US FDA again, and the medical materials review projects increased to 114, and it is still the only 510(k) medical equipment review agency in Asia.
