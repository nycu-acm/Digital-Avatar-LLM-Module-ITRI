Car information communication
With the popularization of information communication technology, the in-vehicle information communication system began to develop after 2000, upgrading the automotive system that originally only had the ability to play music and view vehicle information into an in-vehicle information communication system with communication and multimedia entertainment.During this period, the Institute of Technology also used the technology to enter the technology research and development of communication energy. In 2005, the technology was transferred to vehicle electronic information device technology to Huanlong Electric Company, and in 2009, it developed the first set of domestic vehicle-specific short-distance communication technology (WAVE/DSRC). With the support of the EU, it hosted the global "Galileo Satellite Creative Competition" in 2011. With the main axis of WAVE/DSRC technology, it upgraded my country's satellite navigation and vehicle-mounted communication applications to the world stage.
In 2012, it jointly developed DSRC vehicle-mounted aircraft with Japanese automobile supplier DENSO and sold it globally; and cooperated with three major foreign industries including Taiwan Fleet, Weijie Technology, and Yuanshi Telecom, etc., including three major industries including the leading taxi industry, including Taiwan Fleet, Weijie Technology, and Yuanshi Telecom, to build the "In-Vehicle Interactive Digital Media Platform (TOLiFE)" and installed more than 13,000 vehicles to create new services and value for Volkswagen on the move.
2005
Technical transfer vehicle electronic information device technology is Huanlong Electric Company, and assisted Huanlong Electric in manufacturing the first domestic vehicle information system that complies with the automotive regulations.It is mass-produced and equipped with European PEUGEOT logo cars, and was equipped with domestic car manufacturers in 2006.
2009
Develop the first set of domestic vehicle-specific short-distance communication technology (WAVE/DSRC), conduct testing with the internationally renowned unit PATH, and connect with major international car manufacturers.Joined with car manufacturer supplier Visteon to join the Vehicle Collision Prevention Index Alliance (CAMP) to assist domestic manufacturers in connecting with international companies.
2011
The Taiwan "2011 Galileo Satellite Creative Competition" was held, and the number of global competition registrations and submissions ranked first in the world.
2012
We jointly developed DSRC with DENSO, a first-class international automotive supplier, and produced DSRC vehicle-mounted aircraft for global marketing.
2014
TOLiFE's in-vehicle interactive digital media platform held a press conference.
2014
We cooperated with three major industries including Taiwan's Large Fleet, Weijie Technology, and Yuanshi Telecom to build the "In-Vehicle Interactive Digital Media Platform (TOLiFE)" and installed it in 13,000 taxis across Taiwan, providing innovative application services such as interactive audio and video entertainment, interactive games, safe ride messages, driver information, and questionnaire surveys.
2018
Smart vehicle-mounted broadband service platform helps commercial fleets such as passenger vehicles to import one-stop networked services.
