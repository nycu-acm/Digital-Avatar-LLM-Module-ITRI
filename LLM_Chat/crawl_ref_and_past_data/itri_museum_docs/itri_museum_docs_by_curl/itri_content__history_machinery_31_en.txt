Service-based robots began to flourish internationally in 2000, and Taiwanese businessmen have also kept up with the international trend in this wave. Acer, Hon Hai, Asus, etc. have successively invested in the research and development of service-based robots. The Institute of Technology and Research also developed guided and safeguarded robots in 2006 and actually operated at the Kaohsiung Science and Technology Museum, so that the robots can be integrated into people's lives and become human helpers.In 2008, the Industrial Research Institute developed the world's first Smart Blower series of smart cleaning robots, which have intelligent functions such as ultra-silence, sensing and identification. It not only fully layouts the patents of cleaning robots, but also transforms into Japanese parts manufacturer NIDEC, successfully entering the international chain.
In 2011, the patents related to cleaning robots were authorized to Delicheng, Songteng, Linghai, Wanrun, NIDEC and other manufacturers.In 2017, Songteng Industrial was accused of patent infringement by iRobot. The Institute of Technology and Research has compiled more than 100 pieces of relevant patent materials as backing for Songteng and filed a counterclaim against iRobot, which has enabled iRobot to withdraw the lawsuit, winning a key patent war for Taiwan's cleaning robot industry, and also shows the Institute of Technology and Research's vision in the layout of cleaning robot patents.
In addition to cleaning robots for people's livelihood, the Industrial Research Institute also developed exoskeleton action assistive robots in 2016 to benefit disabled patients and move again. In the same year, it established the startup "Fubao Technology", which has obtained TFDA and CE marketing licenses and is marketed in 20 countries around the world.Taiwan is about to enter an extremely elderly society. In 2018, the Institute of Technology Research and Research developed the "Smart Vision System Robot" combined with smart vision, sensing and deep learning functions. It can be used as home companionship, care and industrial use, as well as the "PECOLA Age Companionship Robot", which can accompany people of different ages and make the robot a good helper for family care.In 2021, the "Humanlike Double-Arm Robot" has two seven-axis and two arms, which can perform a variety of tasks in a similar way; in 2022, the Industrial Research Institute developed "AMR Unmanned Intelligent Logistics Technology", which cooperated with unified supermarkets to focus on food delivery services, and has been operated in the commercial office building of Kaohsiung Software Park. In addition to providing new zero-contact and low-carbon delivery services, it can also save labor costs for delivery personnel to solve the problem of epidemic prevention and aging labor population in the post-epidemic era.
2006
The research and development of guided and safeguard robots with vision and positioning and navigation capabilities is the first robot in Taiwan to be actually displayed and operated at the Kaohsiung Science and Technology Museum.
2007
The Industrial Research Institute and Shinguang Preservation successfully developed the preservation robot "SeQ-1".
2008
Developed the world's first Smart Blower series of smart cleaning robots, with intelligent functions such as ultra-silence, large suction, sensing and identification.
2009
The service robot "Roppie" has been developed, integrating technologies such as stereo vision, feature recognition, and lightweight arm modules. It is a robot with multi-functional and multi-system.
2011
Completely deployed the patents of smart cleaning robots, and authorized a total of 5 manufacturers such as Delicheng, Songteng, Linghai, Wanrun, and NIDEC.
2012
Smart cleaning robot impeller technology has been transferred to the Japanese component manufacturer NIDEC, which has successfully entered the international supply chain.
2016
The development of exoskeleton action assisted robots has allowed the disabled to ignite the hope of walking again. They won the world's top 100 science and technology research and development awards.In the same year, the new startup company Fubao Technology was established.
2016
The action-assisted robot "FREE Walk" will benefit disabled patients and walk again.
2018
He won the CES Innovation Award for "Smart Vision System Robot".
2020
He won the CES Innovation Award for "PECOLA Age Companion Robot".
2021
We have joined hands with eight manufacturers including Zhongqiang Optoelectronics and Delta Electric to establish an independent mobile robot alliance to promote the formulation of common standards in the AMR industry.
2021
The "human-like double-arm robot" developed has two seven-axis and two-arm arms, which can perform various tasks in a similar way to humans, grasp irregular objects such as Baute bottles.
2022
Research and develop "AMR unmanned intelligent logistics technology" and cooperate with Uni-Cheng to promote independent mobile robot direct door delivery service.
