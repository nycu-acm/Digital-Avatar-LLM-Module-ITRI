Environmental Protection Convention
my country's diplomatic situation is special and has been repeatedly suppressed, so it is unable to participate in the United Nations organization as an official capacity. International environmental protection issues such as ozone layer destruction and climate change have been increasingly valued since the 1980s and have led various international environmental protection conventions in the United Nations. If my country is excluded, it will have a huge impact on Taiwan's foreign trade and industry entering the international supply chain.Since the 1990s, with the assistance of Dr. Mostafa Tolba, former CEO of the United Nations Environment Programme (UNEP), the Commission has invited my country to organize a delegation to participate in the "United Nations Convention on Outline of Climate Change" and the Conference of the Parties to the "United Nations Convention on Outline of Climate Change" and the "Montello Protocol" in the name of the observer of the non-governmental organizations (NGOs) of the Institute of Technology (ITRI), to demonstrate its importance to global environmental issues through diversified actions, and call on the international community to pay attention to Taiwan's contribution and recognition, grasp changes in the situation of international consultation and reduction control, as a reference for domestic policy measures, strengthen the foundation of climate governance and other relevant laws, and express my country's determination to actively participate in the international environmental protection convention.

1995
