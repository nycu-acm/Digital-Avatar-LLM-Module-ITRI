Communication materials
Low-Temperature Cofired Ceramics (LTCC) is a ceramic material that can be embedded in conductive lines or passive component circuits, sintered into substrates, and combined with active components such as wafers to form a specific and micro functional module.LTCC is small in size and fast in heat dissipation, with excellent high-frequency and high-speed transmission characteristics, and is widely used in mobile phones, Wi-Fi, Bluetooth, power amplifiers and automotive electronics.
The Institute of Technology has invested in the research and development of LTCC technology for more than 30 years. In 2000, it cooperated with Japan's Panasonic Life (MKE) to develop the world's smallest Bluetooth LTCC module at that time, providing complete wireless system functions, with the advantages of miniaturization of components and low-cost. This technology has been transferred to many LTCC manufacturers to drive the LTCC industry and greatly improve the self-made rate of RF key element components.In addition, in 2009, LTCC technology was used to assist Gongde Electronics, the global consumer electronic fuse leader, to jointly develop high-performance ceramic chip fuse components to improve the reliability and safety of electronic products.
Taking advantage of LTCC's advantages in high-frequency transmission, the Industrial Research Institute will work with LTCC major manufacturer DuPont Micro Circuit and Component Materials Company in 2022 to introduce its LTCC products into 5G high-frequency millimeter wave communication applications, which not only opens the door to 5G high-frequency communication for DuPont Materials, but also greatly reduces the threshold for manufacturers to layout 5G millimeter wave communication and welcomes the vast market with independent 5G O-RAN technology.
2000
Through international cooperation, the prototype of the world's smallest size NS-LTCC non-shrinkage low-temperature co-fired ceramic Bluetooth module was completed at that time, and the world's smallest Bluetooth module (Bluetooth Module) was launched, which is thin and short, with high reliability and stable RF characteristics.
2009
Assisting Gongde Electronics Company in the development of "LTCC embedded micro-hole structure materials and processes", developing high-voltage-resistant high-performance ceramic wafer fuse components, replacing consumer and automotive electronic products fuse components, and improving the reliability and safety of electronic products.
2022
Assisting DuPont Micro Circuit and Component Materials Company to introduce its product "9KC GreenTape™ LTCC" into 5G high-frequency millimeter wave communication application, opening up the small base station, RU radio frequency unit, and user terminal equipment (CPE) market for Taiwan's communication industry, and at the same time assisting the industrialization of 5G O-RAN of Taiwan factories, winning the 2022 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Award.
