Mass transport tools have always been an important way for the people of Taiwan to travel north. Since the 1980s, the Institute of Technology has upgraded Taiwan's mass transport tools with technology, and assisted Taiwan Railway in computerizing and networking of complex ticketing systems through information and communication technology, which was the beginning of the computerization of domestic mass service systems. In 1997, advanced materials were used to assist Taiwan Railway in laying ground fabrics to prevent rails from sinking.After the high-speed rail is opened, it continues to use communication technology, materials, chemicals, machinery and equipment to promote communication without obstacles during operation, anti-fouling and operation safety of high-speed rail. For example, in 2007, water-based glass anti-fouling transparent coating technology is successfully developed, which is used for anti-fouling treatment of carriages such as Taiwan Railway, high-speed rail, and Taipei MRT.
In 2019, we will cooperate with the high-speed rail to develop the first "bogie walk test equipment" in Taiwan to strengthen the operation safety of high-speed rail and comprehensively promote the localization of the rail industry; in 2021, we will integrate technological energy such as machinery, communications, materials, and electronics to develop alternative components to improve track maintenance capacity and reduce maintenance costs; and develop track measurement and material analysis to improve track testing and verification energy; carry out subsystem integration, circuit board and machine factory maintenance equipment, etc., and strive to localize the dual-iron vehicle components and machine factory equipment.
In response to the domestic rail industry needs, the Railway Bureau's "Research Plan for the Independent Technology Improvement Plan for Light Rail Vehicle Door Systems" will be undertaken in 2021. The goal is to develop a light rail door system that complies with international safety standards, and propose a "double-fan external electric sliding plug door" product development plan, including door mechanism, door body structure, door controller and system integration. The entire design process must comply with the RAMS system safety assurance certification requirements and be supervised and implemented by an independent third-party fair unit.In order to establish a complete supply of energy such as local design, manufacturing, production, maintenance, and maintenance of domestic railway doors, and drive the upgrading of related industrial chains and improve the localization of domestic railway systems. In the future, manufacturers can be further supported to develop towards a global market.
1984
Completed the computer reservation and ticketing system commissioned by Taiwan Railway, and was the beginning of the computerization of domestic mass service systems.
1991
Complete application systems such as automatic ticketing reservations in Taiwan Railway, automatic monitoring of Linkou winery, and automatic winemaking in Taipei winery, and promote the computerization and networking of large-scale system projects.
1997
We cooperated with Taiwan Railway to carry out a ground fabric demonstration project of about 200 meters long at 56 kilometers south of the Neili section to prevent the rails from sinking.
2007
Develop water-based glass anti-fouling transparent coating technology for anti-fouling treatment of cars such as Taiwan Railway, high-speed rail, Taipei MRT, etc., which can save a lot of cleaning costs.
2010
Completed the world's first WiMAX high-speed rail wireless network system, and built 94 WiMAX base stations along the high-speed rail from Taipei to Zuoying, effectively providing WiFi access for train passengers.
2012
Leading the global development and completion of the high-speed rail WiMAX broadband network application service system.
2015
We cooperated with Ericsson on the Wireless backhaul and the Small cell network technology of the high-speed rail mobile network, and showed an internet bandwidth of more than 50Mbps at a speed of 300km/h.
2018
The high-speed rail commissioned the Industrial Research Institute to study the "train wireless axle box temperature monitoring system" to increase the axle temperature monitoring information, which can reduce the operation delay time caused by temperature sensor failure.
2019
Cooperate with the high-speed rail to develop the first "bogie walking test equipment" in Taiwan, strengthen the operation safety of high-speed rail, and comprehensively promote the localization of the rail industry.
2021
We will join hands with Taiwan Railway, Hi-Tech University and Beijing University of Science and Technology to sign a letter of intent for cooperation to provide services such as parts and equipment development, testing, and maintenance, as well as related talent training to promote the localization of the track industry.
2021
Undertake the Railway Bureau’s light rail door development plan, implement localization, and drive the upgrading of related industrial chains.
