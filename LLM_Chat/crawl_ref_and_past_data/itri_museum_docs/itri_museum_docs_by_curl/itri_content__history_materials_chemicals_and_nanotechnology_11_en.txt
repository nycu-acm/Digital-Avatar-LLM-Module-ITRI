The density of carbon fiber is one fifth of that of steel, but its strength is 5 times that of steel. It is resistant to electromagnetic, fatigue and corrosion, and has excellent mechanical performance.It has been used in aerospace and sports products since the 1980s.With its mastery of carbon fiber technology, the Institute of Technology has assisted enterprises in improving the manufacturing level of carbon fiber golf clubs and tennis rackets, and has also driven the bicycle industry to transform and greatly enhance the added value of the product.
Since 1985, we have cooperated with Giant Machinery to develop carbon fiber bicycle frames. In 1988, the first batch of carbon fiber bicycle frames were shipped. The test durability was 10 times higher than that of foreign carbon fiber frames. It also helped Giant sponsored teams achieve excellent results in the 2004 Tour de France, greatly increasing the sales of giant carbon fiber vehicles, and also transforming Giant Machinery from OEM to a brand. "Giant" successfully entered the global high-priced bicycle market, becoming the best example of material innovation driving industrial upgrading.
The Industrial Research Institute has developed the "Composite SMD Reinforced Concrete Structure Technology" with carbon fiber to strengthen concrete structures as a reinforcement for reinforced concrete structures.Taiwan encountered a 921 earthquake in 1999. The Institute of Technology also used this technology to assist in the reconstruction of buildings in disaster areas. Later, it entered the aerospace field and cooperated with the American Spirit to develop a new generation of aerospace carbon fibers, successfully developing high-modular carbon fiber precursor materials. Its rigidity meets the international aerospace carbon fiber standards, which will help my country's composite materials industry to transform into the aerospace high-tech market.In response to the environmental trend, a 100% recycling "thermoplastic carbon fiber composite" was developed in 2018. Technology-transferred manufacturers made light and sturdy suitcases, which will be sold internationally, which will drive the business opportunities of Taiwan's circular economy.
1984
Assisting the weaving machine factory Yijin Machinery to make carbon fiber composite pulleys and replace traditional aluminum alloy pulleys, resulting in an increase in the speed of the loom and a 50% increase in productivity. This is the key to Taiwan's shuttleless loom replacing Japan and dominating the Southeast Asian market.
1985
Apply composite materials to bicycles, tennis rackets and golf clubs.
1985
Cooperate with Huge Machinery to develop carbon fiber bicycle frames.
1986
Cooperate with the industry to develop single-direction prepreg material manufacturing technology for hot melt adhesives to improve the manufacturing level of tennis rackets and golf clubs in my country.
1997
Using carbon fiber, the "Composite SMD Reinforced Concrete Structure Technology" was developed, which can be used for defect reinforcement, earthquake-proof reinforcement, fire reinforcement and sea sand house reinforcement of reinforced concrete (RC) structures.In 1999, he assisted in the reconstruction of buildings in 921 disaster areas.
2010
We cooperated with Spirit, the United States, to carry out the development project of the new generation of aerospace carbon fiber, and successfully developed high-modular and high-rigid carbon fiber precursor materials, reaching international standards, and can be used in high-tech products such as aerospace.
2018
Developed "thermoplastic carbon fiber composite" and "water-phase solvent-free environmentally friendly formula" to make a 100% recyclable and remade light and tough suitcase.
