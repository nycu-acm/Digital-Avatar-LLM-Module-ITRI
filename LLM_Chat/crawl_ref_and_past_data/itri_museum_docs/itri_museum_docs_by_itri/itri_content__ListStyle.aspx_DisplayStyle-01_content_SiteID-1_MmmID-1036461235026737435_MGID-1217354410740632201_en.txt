Service Description
The chip layout design service team of the Institute of Technology Research has accumulated rich experience in IC design process, and uses professional knowledge and EDA tools to provide service projects such as Automatic Placement and Routing, Static Timing Analysis, Formal Verification, Physical Verification and other services.
