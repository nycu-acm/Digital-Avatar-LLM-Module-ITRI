The Ministry of Economics' Intelligent Operational Results have been recognized internationally. The Institute of Industrial Research has won the top 100 Innovation Institutions Award for its patent influence for five times

Date: 2021/02/24
Under the active investment and leadership of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research has won the "2021 Global Top 100 Innovation Institutions Award" for the fifth time for four consecutive years. It is the research institution with the most awards in Asia, and the number of awards is also the first among Taiwanese institutions.
The Ministry of Economics has won international recognition for its comprehensive intelligent power management strategy and international patent layout!Clarivate released the report of the top 100 innovative institutions in the world today.With the active investment and leadership of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research has won the fifth award for four consecutive years. It is the research institution with the most awards in Asia, and the number of awards is also the first among Taiwan institutions.In order to accelerate Taiwan's industrial innovation and upgrading, the Ministry of Economic Affairs not only continues to use science and technology projects to encourage legal person research institutions to develop key technologies in the market demand, but also promotes and implements global patent layout and intelligent finance combination business strategies. The Institute of Industrial Research continues to win the Clarivate Security Top 100 Innovation Institution Award, and is as famous as internationally renowned companies Apple, Microsoft, Intel, Qualcomm, Samsung, as well as R&D institutions Fraunhofer, University of California, etc., showing that the R&D resources and influential patent layout of the Ministry of Economic Affairs have achieved remarkable results, bringing glory to Taiwan.
Patents are an important weapon for new startups to build on the market. Smart property has profound strategic significance for national development.The Ministry of Economic Affairs spares no effort to assist legal entities and industries, formulate internationally competitive R&D directions, and actively invest resources to promote patent layout, and build a patent shield for the industry to enter the international market. Taking the Institute of Industrial Research as an example, about 80% of patents are R&D results supported by the Ministry of Economic Science and Technology Project, promoting the Institute of Industrial Research to become the most award-winning research institution in Asia.From 2017 to 2019, a total of 4,638 patents have been accumulated, and up to 3,552 authorized enterprise patent applications have been authorized, showing the outstanding achievements of the Ministry of Economic Affairs in promoting the creation of the value of intelligent property rights of legal persons.In the future, the Ministry of Economic Affairs will continue to use the R&D ecosystem thinking model to formulate intelligent property management strategies that are in line with the international level, and use the strategic thinking of "focusing on quality rather than chasing quantity", continue to promote the patent layout mechanism before R&D, and build patents that are close to market demand. It will also continue to assist the industry in strengthening patent quality, creating key core patent portfolios and optimizing the integrated application of R&D technologies. At the same time, it encourages systematic and diversified innovative practices, and uses intelligent finance to help new startups improve their competitiveness and seize global business opportunities.
The Clarivate Security Ball's top 100 innovative institutions report has long used patent data analysis to evaluate the institutions' innovation energy, market layout strategies and the influence of R&D technology. This year it has entered its tenth year and is of indicator significance in the field of global R&D innovation.Fan Yongyin, general manager of Clarivian Taiwan, said: "I am very pleased that the Institute of Technology has been selected for four consecutive years and the fifth time. The Institute of Technology has continued to be among the top 100 first-class teams in the world. In recent years, it has begun to show results in the active strengthening of international layout. In today's rapidly changing world, top innovation institutions are more important social resources."
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research, said that the fifth consecutive year that won the Top 100 Innovation Institution Award in the World for the fifth time, not only demonstrates the success of the Institute of Industrial Research, focusing on cross-field forward-looking research and development, output and using diversified high-value patents, but also shows that the Institute of Industrial Research has reached the international first-class level of intelligence and finance, and the breadth and depth of cross-domain research and releases have global influence.The Institute of Industrial Research continues to take cross-field integration and meeting industrial needs as its R&D goal, and develops and implements the Intellectual Finance Strategy with international influence. By the end of 2020, the Institute of Industrial Research has accumulated 17,022 validly certified patents, of which the proportion of invention patents reached 98%, and the number of foreign patent applications is nearly twice that of domestic applications.
Liu Wenxiong further stated that the Institute of Technology Research and Research continues to promote and implement the patent analysis layout mechanism, and will take industrial technology to assist industrial innovation and transformation and upgrading as the main axis. Under the "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" structure of the Institute of Technology Research and Research, it will develop three application areas: "smart life", "healthy and happy life" and "sustainable environment", as well as explore intelligent enabling technologies that support the development of the three application areas, further assist domestic enterprises in improving competitiveness and creating value; at the same time, it will actively promote the link between the capital market and the technology market, and has joined hands with the Small and Medium Enterprises Credit Guarantee Fund and 26 key financial banks in Taiwan to launch the "Technology Added Financing Guarantee Project", hoping to connect the strong funds of the Taiwan capital market with potential scientific research results, transform from manufacturing priority to wisdom priority, create smart value, and bring the greatest benefits to Taiwan's economy and industry.
With the strategy of "exploring creativity and accurately laying out high-value patents", the Institute of Industrial Research and Research focuses on core technologies with international competitiveness and future market demand, actively deploys patents in important countries around the world. Through research and development, a large number of high-quality patents produced by rich research and development, and an excellent talent pool with rich practical experience in supporting manufacturers, it has become the best backing for Taiwan's industry to enter the international market and transform and upgrade.

It is the research institution with the most awards in Asia, and the number of awards is also the first among Taiwanese institutions.
