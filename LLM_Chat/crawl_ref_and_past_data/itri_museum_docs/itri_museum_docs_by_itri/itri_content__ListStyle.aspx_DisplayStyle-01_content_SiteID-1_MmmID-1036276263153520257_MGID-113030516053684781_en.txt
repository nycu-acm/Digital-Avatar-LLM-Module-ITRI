The Ministry of Economics has achieved fruitful scientific research and innovation achievements, and the Institute of Industrial Research won the Top 100 Innovation Institutions Award for the World for 800 times

Date: 2024/03/05
With the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research has won the "Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award" for the eighth time for seven consecutive years, setting a record for the most awards from R&D institutions in the Asia-Pacific region.
Clarivate released the 2024 "Top 100 Global Innovation Institutions" report.With the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research has won the Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award for the eighth time for seven consecutive years. This year, only three research institutions in the world were selected, and the Institute of Industrial Research is one of them, setting a record for the most awards won by R&D institutions in the Asia-Pacific region.The patent energy of the Institute of Technology has performed outstandingly in five major indicators, including technical uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity, and is comparable to internationally renowned companies ASML, GE, Johnson & Johnson, Siemens, etc., with remarkable innovation results.
At the critical moment of the US-China technological competition, net zero emission wave and the transformation of industrial structure, the Ministry of Economic Affairs has joined hands with legal persons, industry and academic circles to actively promote forward-looking, critical and cross-field industrial technology research and development, promote industry-university-research cooperation, lay the foundation for the next generation of scientific and technological development, and continuously strengthen industrial competitiveness to respond to future challenges.With its market-oriented international patent layout and R&D strategy, the Institute of Industrial Research has strengthened its patent physique year by year and strengthened its industrial intelligence energy. About 85% of the patents are R&D results of subsidies for the Ministry of Economics' science and technology projects. In 2023, 723 science and technology were transferred (including new ventures), and 16,137 valid patents have been accumulated, demonstrating the outstanding results of the Ministry of Economics in promoting legal persons to create intellectual and financial value.
Clarivate Security Ball’s top 100 innovative institutions report has been actively analyzing the innovation energy of global enterprises or institutions through patent data for a long time, and selecting them based on scientific patent indicators to commend top institutions’ persistence in innovation and their excellent creativity.Gordon Samson, president of Clarivian Intelligent Property Rights Group, said: "Congratulations to the 8th and seven consecutive years of selected 100 innovative institutions in the world. It is not easy to be selected into the top 100 innovative institutions in the world, because maintaining advantages in the innovation ecosystem is more difficult than before. This year, only three R&D units in the world are on the list, and the top 100 innovative institutions have shown great influence and achievements on the international stage."
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research, said that it has won the Top 100 Innovation Institution Award for the World for the 8th for seven consecutive years. This not only shows that the Institute of Industrial Research focuses on cross-field forward-looking research and development and successfully uses diversified high-value patent strategies, but also shows that the Institute of Industrial Research has a stable and correct direction, has reached the international first-class level and has global influence.Faced with the opportunities and challenges of the new era, the Institute of Technology focuses on market-oriented research and development and plans the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint", and provides innovative and market-oriented solutions to the industry with four major application areas such as smart life, healthy and happy life, sustainable environment, and resilient society, as well as intelligent enabling technologies that promote the development of the application field.In addition, through innovative application models such as innovative innovation of new ventures, patents and technical intangible asset financing, and Intellectual Property Innovation Corporation (IPIC), we can innovate value and sustainable management with the industry.At present, 57 small and medium-sized and new start-up enterprises in China have assisted 57 domestic small and medium-sized enterprises to apply for intangible asset financing, and 28 companies have successfully obtained intangible asset financing of more than 500 million yuan through patent evaluation financing or technology added financing, which has caused countless investment benefits, and actively used smart property links to the technology market and the capital market.
The Institute of Technology has deployed scientific and technological research and development strategies ahead of schedule, and based on the development trends of net zero emissions, super-aged society, and generative AI, etc., it will take stock of and develop the corresponding patent energy, establish a high-quality patent pool, and propose solutions through innovative technology and services to help the industry accelerate transformation and strengthen international competitiveness. The Institute of Technology is willing to share the achievements of its scientific and technological research and development and intelligent operation, and looks forward to helping more Taiwanese manufacturers stand on the international stage.
