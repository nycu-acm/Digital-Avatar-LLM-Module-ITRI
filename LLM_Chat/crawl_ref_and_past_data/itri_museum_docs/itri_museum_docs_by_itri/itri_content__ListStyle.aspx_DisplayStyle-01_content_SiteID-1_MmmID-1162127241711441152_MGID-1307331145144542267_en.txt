Technology introduction
Solid amine.
Low energy consumption, good stability, highly selective adsorption and carbon capture materials.It can be regenerated at <100℃ to solve the problem of high energy consumption and insufficient stability of traditional chemical absorption methods.
Application scope
Solid amine adsorbent is synthesized with modified amine-based piezoelectric silicon dioxide.
Adsorbent materials have high adsorption capacity (1-2 mmol CO2/g adsorbent) and high stability characteristics, with a lifespan of more than half a year, easy to fill and no doubt about pipe corrosion.It can be widely used in flue gas and semiconductor thermal decomposition furnace flue gas carbon capture.
Carbon reduction benefits
In response to the net zero emission target of 2050, solid amine adsorbents can be developed to capture carbon for boilers and VOC exhausts at low concentrations of CO2 (<10%) in small and medium-sized boilers (two tons or above); taking the real-time verification of small and medium-sized boilers (two tons or above) as an example, a single module can capture 50-100 tons of carbon per year, with substantial carbon reduction benefits; it can be applied to direct air capture in the future, with negative carbon potential.
