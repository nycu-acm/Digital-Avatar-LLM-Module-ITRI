Technology introduction
Deep decolorization technology for polyester fabrics.
206 well-known textile brands around the world have announced their goal of manufacturing textiles with 100% fully recycled polyester fibers by 2030, but currently recycled polyester accounts for only 14%, and the material source comes from Bote bottles, which is far less than the needs of the textile industry. Therefore, using textiles as a source of recycled polyester has become a future development trend.
Features and innovation
Due to the high content of impurities such as dyes and polyester fabrics in waste variegated bottles and polyester fabrics, the quality and reuse of chemical recycling products are seriously affected. The Industrial Research Institute can effectively improve the removal and purification effects of dyes and heterogeneous monomers through integrated process technology development such as catalysts, depolymerization and purification, and can obtain high-purity and low-color BHET recycled raw materials.
Application and benefits
The Industrial Research Institute has developed the "Polyester Fabric Decoloration Technology" for the dispersed dyes used in the current largest use of polyester fabrics. It uses environmentally friendly water-based decolorization accelerator to achieve deep decolorization of polyester fabrics to recover high-quality colorless polyester raw materials, solve the problems of poor decolorization rate of domestic polyester fibers, the residual dyes that are easy to crack and cannot spin, and recycling can only be made into dark textiles or downgraded for use, effectively improving the reusability of textile recycling polyester and realizing the polyester fiber circulation.
