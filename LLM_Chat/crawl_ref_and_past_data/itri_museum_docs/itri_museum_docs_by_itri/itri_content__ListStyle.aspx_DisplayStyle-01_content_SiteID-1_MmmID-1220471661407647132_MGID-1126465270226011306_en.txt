Soft hybrid electronic non-skin contact EMG sensing smart pants and smart knee pads.
Technology introduction
The world's first multi-channel soft non-skin contact EMG smart pants system, adopts soft coupled physiological sensing technology, which can accurately sense muscle electrical signals through the thickness of 1.5mm fabric under dynamics.
4-channel smart pants movement system integrates the muscle strength signal calculation across muscle groups and analyzes the output value, balance and action history of each muscle group.
Application and benefits
Non-skin contact muscle strength sensing smart pants and smart knee pads provide the convenience of sensory wearable sensing, greatly improve the experience and help coaches and students effectively improve communication efficiency, movement accuracy and training benefits.
In the future, it can be applied to scientific sports training, fitness and long-term care fields.
