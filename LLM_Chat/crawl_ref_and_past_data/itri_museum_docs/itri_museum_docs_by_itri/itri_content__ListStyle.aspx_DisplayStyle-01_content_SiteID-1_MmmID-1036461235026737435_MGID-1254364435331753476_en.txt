Service Description

In order to ensure the safety and compliance of domestic explosion-proof electrical equipment and mechanical equipment and equipment, and to reduce the occurrence of occupational disasters such as fire, explosion and labor work hazards, the Institute of Industrial Research took the lead in establishing relevant inspection and verification technologies, and assisted the competent authorities (formerly Labor Committee, now the Ministry of Labor) in planning and promoting the type verification system for explosion-proof electrical equipment and mechanical equipment.It is also the first recognized type verification agency in my country, providing third-party verification tests with high credibility and technical capabilities in the industry.In response to the implementation of explosion-proof safety, we also participate in the relevant safety research and promotion of the competent authority, as well as equipment after-market inspection and other projects.In recent years, we have promoted education and training services for explosion-proof safety personnel, assisting the industry and government units in improving their explosion-proof safety knowledge and reducing the risks of operating in areas with dangerous explosive environments.
Services
• Type verification and inspection of mechanical equipment such as explosion-proof electrical equipment, power stackers, grinders, grinding wheels and other mechanical equipment.
• Education and training of explosion-proof safety personnel.
• Research on Explosion Protection and Machinery Safety Policy
• Aftermarket inspection of machinery and equipment
Explosion-proof electrical equipment and mechanical equipment type verification and testing
The Green Energy Institute of the Institute of Technology has a testing laboratory certified by the National Certification Foundation (TAF) of the Consortium Legal Person, and a product verification agency that complies with CNS 17025 (ISO/IEC 17025), and is recognized by the Ministry of Labor as a type verification agency for mechanical equipment and equipment.The certificate of certification of type verification of machinery and equipment issued by the Institute of Technology can be used as a certificate of compliance with the safety standards specified by the products complying with Article 7 of the Occupational Safety and Health Law, and can also be used as supporting documents stipulated in Article 4, Item 1, Paragraph 1, Article 1 of the Safety Information Measures for the Application and Login of Machinery and Equipment Equipment.The verification and testing items are based on the safety standards of machinery and equipment. Education and training of explosion-proof safety personnel
The Green Energy Institute of the Institute of Technology and Research cooperates with domestic and foreign professional institutions to establish personnel education and training courses and build the first professional training classroom in China.Provide education and training on explosion-proof safety principles, domestic regulations, equipment protection technology, hazardous area division, equipment installation and selection, inspection and maintenance, etc. The course is also linked to the International Explosion Protection Verification System (IECEx) through foreign professional institutions, which will help to obtain the IECEx personnel competence certification (CoPC), and can be recognized as a qualification for the overseas large-scale petrochemical and chemical industry.
