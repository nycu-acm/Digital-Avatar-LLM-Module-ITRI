The Ministry of Economic Affairs supports the Industrial Research Institute to build the first MIT "cell automation production system" and lay out the cell therapy industry to promote the transformation and upgrading of the local supply chain

Date: 2021/10/29
The Institute of Industrial Research announced today (29th) that with the support of the Science and Technology Project of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the innovative "cell automation production system" has been developed. It is not only the most integrated system module in the world, but also the first domestic automation system that combines complete functions such as cell culture, quality detection and harvesting succession.
Among the top ten causes of death in Chinese people in 2021, the terrifying cancer has ranked first for 39 consecutive years. Cell treatment has become one of the treatment options for many patients. Since cells need to be cultured or processed in vitro and become "special forces" before they can be put into the human body for treatment or prevention of diseases, the demand for cell culture continues to grow.MarketsandMarkets reported that the global cell culture market size will reach nearly NT$3 trillion in 2025. The Ministry of Economics Technology Department has deployed innovative cell regeneration technologies in response to this huge industry and medical needs, and used a special project to support the Industrial Research Institute to develop an innovative "cell automation production system" to complete the world's most complete system module. It is also the first domestic automation system that combines complete functions such as cell culture, quality detection and harvesting succession. It has created MIT technology, with high cell survival rate and 10 times production capacity, which will help the transformation of the domestic biotech industry and further seize the cell culture market.
Since 2017, the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs has continued to support the Industrial Research Institute in establishing a "cell production automation system" with a special science and technology project, which can produce "adhesive stem cells and somatic cells" as biological agents or biological drugs. It has a wide range of applications, including cell therapy, cell tissue regeneration and repair, and will first focus on cardiovascular diseases and chronic wounds in diabetes as the entry point in the early stage, and gradually expand the scope of application.It has obtained patents from Taiwan and the United States, hoping to drive the domestic cell culture industry, transform into industrial production, and promote the progress of the local biotechnology industry.
Shen Xinxin, deputy director of the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials Research Institute, said that in the face of huge business opportunities, the control of quality control, safety and effectiveness of cell production will be the key to victory.Since cell production is mainly based on human operation and the booming development of domestic clinical trials, the market urgently needs more production investment, and the "cell production GMP process" with high yield and stable quality is the best solution.
The "cell production automation system" developed by the Institute of Technology has three major characteristics. The first is production automation.It is equipped with automatic environmental control, intelligent monitoring, robotic arm replacement fluid replacement and other equipment.It only requires 3 to 40% of the original human labor to produce the traditional 10 times of the output, which can reduce the defective rate and the cell survival rate is above 95%. The second is closed manufacturing equipment, which can reduce the risk of cell contamination and ensure quality. The third is the creation of domestic technology, and can customize and match different modules to create exclusive equipment according to the diverse needs of the operator. It can also assist in the planning of factory buildings and guide factory construction and output.In the future, it can enter the "Contract Development and Manufacturing Organization (CDMO)" and provide the most complete "cell factory" for the global industrial integration modules.

The Institute of Industrial Research and Research will exhibit eight intelligent and green-gold-based innovative technologies such as "cell production automation system" based on precision medicine this year.
