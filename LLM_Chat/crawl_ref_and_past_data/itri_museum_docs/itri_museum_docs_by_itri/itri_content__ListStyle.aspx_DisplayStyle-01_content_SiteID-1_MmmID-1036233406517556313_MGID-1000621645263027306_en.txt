About Service System Technology Center
The Service System Technology Center focuses on three major application areas: smart medical care and care, smart logistics and supply chain, and cultural and sports technology, integrates ITRI R&D energy, connects cross-field operators to carry out business and service models (Service Design), plays the role of solution operators as "technical original factory", and creates a high-value solution (System-thinking Solution) to shape the industrial ecosystem, drives the startups and cultivates emerging services.
Task:
Verify cross-field professional core technologies through service/commercial design, system solutions and field to promote industrial innovation and cultivate new industries.
Based on customer and market demand, we will strengthen the cross-domain integration advantages of soft and hard integration, and develop solutions and smart service systems of "soft + hard + smart service".
Use business verification to lead R&D strategies to drive cross-domain cooperation and innovation.
