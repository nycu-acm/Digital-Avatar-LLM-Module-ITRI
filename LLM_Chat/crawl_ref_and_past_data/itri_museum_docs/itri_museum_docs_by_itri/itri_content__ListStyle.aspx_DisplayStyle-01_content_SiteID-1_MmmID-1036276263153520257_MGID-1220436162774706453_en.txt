Taiwan’s innovative R&D strength has shined internationally again! The R&D 100 Awards, known as the Oscars in the R&D industry, recently released the list of winners.With the support of the Science and Technology Project of the Industrial Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics and the Digital Development Department, the Institute of Technology won eight major awards, which is only slightly less than the Los Alamos National Laboratory, which is only three times the R&D funding of the Institute of Technology, surpasses the MIT Lincoln Laboratory, Siemens Medical Equipment Institute, NASA Research Center, Dow Chemical, DuPont, 3M and other international leading institutions and major manufacturers. In addition, the number of awards won in "net zero energy-saving technology" and "biotechnology and medicine" also set a record high in the annual order. It shows that Taiwan's scientific research strength is deep and solid, so that it can shine on the international stage. Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology, said that this time, the Institute of Technology has a total of 100 R&D Awards won eight awards, all of which showed the value of innovation. The awards selected can be divided into two categories, including net zero energy saving and biomedical technology. There are five net zero energy saving technologies, such as: "VOC-3R Near Full Recycling - PI Film Green Process Innovation" is to practice the concept of green product recycling, help manufacturers to recycle and purify working solvents by themselves, and reuse waste. "Path-oriented AI control parameter optimization and precision manufacturing technology" improves the processing accuracy of tool machines and equipment up to 5 microns, helping manufacturers seize processing markets such as advanced semiconductor fields. "High-efficiency dual-mode atomic layer coating system" is the world's first dual-mode atomic layer coating. Break through the patent barrier of major foreign manufacturers to meet the needs of semiconductor 3D high-deep aspect ratio pore coating, multi-component control, and atomic layer precision coating. The "Sustainable Energy System" repolymerizes and organizes multiple low-voltage decentralized energy through the integration of software and hardware systems to provide electricity elastically. "O-RAN private network energy-saving management technology" is 5G smart energy-saving technology, which can be used for diverse uses such as telecommunications, smart factories, and smart transportation. Secondly, the biomedical industry, as one of the core driving the growth of Taiwan's next generation industry, won three awards this year, with the most impressive results in history. "Smart RF Thermal Ablation System" can accurately ablate tumors, reduce surgical risks and facilitate wound recovery, and is a doctor and patient The blessing of the author. "Novel target glaucoma drugs" and "supramolecular composite technology applied to wet macular lesions eye drops" The former improves the efficiency of ocular aquamia discharge, while the latter treats macular without injection, which fully demonstrates the innovative value of the Institute of Technology and Research and Development to solve problems and the industrialization results. Lin Quanneng, deputy director of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, said that the research and development results supported by the Ministry of Economic Affairs have won the top 100 scientific and technological research and development in the world for 16 consecutive years, with a cumulative amount of 84 awards, enhancing the visibility of Taiwan's scientific research strength worldwide. In addition, three biomedical technology awards have won the fifth consecutive year. This is the fifth consecutive year that the biomedical achievements have won the world's top 100 scientific and technological research and development awards, indicating that Taiwan's biomedical research and development strength has reached international standards. Huang Yaping, Director of Digital Industry Department of Digital Development, said that "O-RAN Energy Saving Private Network Management Technology" is a domestic energy-saving network solution jointly created by the Institute of Technology and Research and Technology of the Industrial Research Institute and Pegatron Technology. It has AI-assisted energy-saving algorithms and domestic energy-saving base stations to predict changes in network usage demand. When the demand decreases, it will be guided immediately through the energy-saving control interface. Terminate the adjacent base station, so that the idle base station can enter the power saving mode to achieve network energy saving effect. At present, we have cooperated with Pegatron United Technology to build an energy-saving dedicated network of eight O-RAN base stations at the Xindian factory. Through energy saving regulation of the base station, the average saving of more than 50% of the base station network energy consumption is saved. In addition, this solution is integrating testing and verification with domestic telecommunications operator Far Transmission Telecom, and cooperates with the construction of a telecommunications energy-saving test network in the South Taiwan Innovation Park field of the Ministry of Economic Affairs. It includes seven stations for verification in the outdoor/indoor base station, which can save more than 20% of the energy consumption. It is expected that the energy saving algorithm and the energy saving technology of the base station will be continuously optimized, and by establishing the first domestic telecommunications in China Demonstration application of energy-saving networks, achieving a model example of improving network energy conservation with communication technology.
