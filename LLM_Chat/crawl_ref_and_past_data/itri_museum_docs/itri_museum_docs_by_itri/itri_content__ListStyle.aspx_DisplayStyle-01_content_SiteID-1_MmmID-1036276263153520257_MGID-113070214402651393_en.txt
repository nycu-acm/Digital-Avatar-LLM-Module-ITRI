Six gold medal technology debuts at the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, innovative research and development to open up Taiwan's new semiconductor, 5G and biomedical markets

Date: 2024/07/02
The Industrial Research Elite Award was officially announced on the 2nd, and 6 gold medals were awarded, demonstrating the Industrial Research Institute's leading position in the next generation of technology deployment, closely following market demand, and echoing the global industrial trends.From left to right, there are Yang Weida, the head of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering, Xu Shixuan, the head of the Institute of Electrical and Optical Engineering, Xu Renyuan, the head of the Institute of Materials and Communications of the Institute of Technology, Liu Wenxiong, the head of the Institute of Biomedical Engineering, Wang Yuqi, the head of the Institute of Biomedical Engineering, Huang Mengqi, the head of the Institute of Machinery Engineering, and Qiu Bizhen, the deputy head of the Institute of Materials and Communications of the Institute of Technology.
On this day (2nd), the Institute of Industrial Research announced the Industrial Research Elite Award, known as the "Industrial Research Institute Oscar Award", and awarded six annual gold medal innovative technologies. With two fruitful industrialization achievements and four forward-looking technologies, it fully demonstrates the Industrial Research Institute's leading position in the deployment of next-generation technology, closely follows market demand, and echoes the global industrial trends.
The six gold award technologies of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research cover breakthrough technologies in the fields of semiconductors, 5G and regenerative medicine.In terms of semiconductors, the "density doubled composite material probe card" significantly improves the accuracy and efficiency of wafer testing, allowing the probe to not only be more refined, cost-saving and faster assemble, but also meets the testing needs of IC components and Micro LEDs, and wins an outstanding research bonus.In addition, the "Advanced MRAM chip technology and verification platform" provides one-stop verification of magnetic component chips from design, manufacturing to testing. It has carried out the most forward-looking MRAM chip development with domestic and foreign industry, academia and research institutions, and has also won the Outstanding Research Award; in the 5G field, the core materials, independent software and network management systems of the Institute of Technology have all won the gold award.Among them, "Copper Foil Substrate and Carrier Materials and Verification Technology" has successfully assisted domestic operators in introducing advanced materials, promoted cooperation between Taiwan's copper foil substrate industry and major raw material manufacturers such as Europe, America and Japan, and won the Outstanding Research Award."5G O-RAN Private Network Energy Saving Management System Technology" assists the operator in quickly deploying dedicated networks, investing in niche applications such as smart factories, smart hospitals, drones, and smart warehousing, and also won the Outstanding Research Award.This year, "Promoting the Industrialization of Millimeter Wave Technology with Soft Band Hard Cross-border Transnational", one of the technologies that won the Industrialization Contribution Gold Award, provides the world's first 5G millimeter wave solution, promotes cooperation with the US and Japanese communication giants, and opens up new markets of 5G millimeter wave; in the field of regenerative medicine, the above, midstream and downstream one-stop supply chains create "core technology in the international cell therapy industry", from the source screening, amplification and cultivation of allogeneic stem cells to clinical application, injecting new impetus for rapid development into the domestic cell therapy industry, and also won the Gold Award for Industrialization Contribution.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research, said that the Institute of Industrial Research has been deeply engaged in the industry for more than half a century and knows that technological innovation is the core force in promoting industrial development.Faced with the critical moment of rapid global industry transformation, the Institute of Technology is committed to innovation and pursues a balance between government scientific research and enterprise cooperation.This year's "Industrialization Contribution Award" and "Outstanding Research Award" are the best witnesses to the spread of government scientific research projects and cooperation with enterprises to drive industrial development.These six gold medal technologies cover the fields of semiconductor, 5G and regenerative medicine, which not only marks the outstanding achievements of the Institute of Technology in the next generation of technology deployment, but also echoes the trends of global industrial changes such as the AI ​​boom, the continuous growth of the semiconductor industry and the cellular medical market.The Institute of Technology Research has drawn up a 2035 technical strategy and blueprint, and will continue to uphold the spirit of "industry upgrading, scientific and technological innovation, and serving the society", develop cross-domain integration solutions, work hand in hand with the industry to jointly enhance competitiveness and resilience, and strengthen Taiwan's technology towards a sustainable future.
Binary technologies that won the Gold Medal of Industrialization Contribution Award:
1. "Connecting with the core technology of the international cell therapy industry", a new blessing for precise clinical treatment
According to a survey by Japanese Business Global Information (GII), the global cell therapy market is expected to reach US$37.5 billion by 2030, with an annual compound growth rate of 14%.Therefore, building a faster and more effective cell therapy production supply chain has become the core driving force for market growth."Cell Therapy Industry Core Technology" develops the "key for cell function" upstream, establishes an accurate and standardized screening mechanism for allogeneic stem cells, uses precise single-molecule identification technology to screen high-quality cells in real time, and finds the most suitable cell type for 40 indications.In terms of mass production process, Taiwan’s first “attached cell automation production system” was created, integrating system modules and robotic arms, etc., which can be elastically optimized according to the needs of the cell process, and the survival rate of cultured stem cells reaches more than 95%.In addition, the first commercial "allomestromal stem cell (MSCs) raw material library" in China has been established, and the freezing technology is adopted at any time. It has been stored for the treatment of myocardium, difficult-to-heal wounds and fractures, and can store tens of thousands of MSC bone stem cells to provide a rapid treatment plan.Currently, the technology has been transferred to domestic manufacturers such as Boyi Biotechnology.
2. "Promote the industrialization of millimeter wave technology through soft belt and hard cross-border" to assist Netcom factory product upgrade
With the blooming of diverse applications of AI and information communication technology, it has promoted the explosive growth of data transmission.In order to meet the needs of wireless network applications, the new generation of 5G and 6G communication technologies are developing rapidly. Major telecommunications companies and equipment manufacturers are looking forward to more efficiently using high-frequency millimeter waves to improve data transmission capacity and satellite coverage.The Industrial Research Institute promotes the "transnationalization of millimeter wave technology with soft-band and hard" and assists the international development of the industry with two major strategies. First, it creates independent software and chip module technology for 5G millimeter wave O-RAN base station system, assists at least 5 ICT base station/terminal system operators, provides the world's first 5G millimeter wave solution, and has successively entered European and Indian telecommunications companies for application concept verification shipments.Second, we will work with the communication giants of the United States and Japan to strengthen cooperation and connect with Taiwan’s industrial chain, jointly explore advanced markets, and assist Taiwan in international 5G millimeter wave competition through diversified industrialization strategies to grasp future application business opportunities.
Four technical achievements that won the gold medal in the outstanding research award:
1. "5G O-RAN private network energy-saving management system technology", complete 5G private network ecosystem
In the 5G era, network equipment and design are moving towards an open architecture. In order to fill the gap in my country's private network management, the Industrial Research Institute has developed the first "5G O-RAN private network energy-saving management system technology" in China. It has three characteristics: First, high construction efficiency: provide a one-stop management system, which can simulate the deployment of management base stations and private networks with one click; Second, high energy-saving benefits: In cooperation with network traffic monitoring and traffic redirection technology, intelligent distribution allows idle base stations to rest and improve energy management; Third, high elastic application: Smart modularization is like an app that can apply different private network applications. Whether it is factories, hospitals, transportation, disaster relief, etc., it can be quickly met. At least eight manufacturers, such as Heshuo, Mingtai, Renbao and other major manufacturers, have helped to introduce private network solutions to upgrade and transform, and at the same time, it connects industry, academic research, and cultivates the soft power of telecommunications talents, hoping to promote my country's industry to move towards the international level.
2. "Density doubled composite material probe card" leads the new era of wafer testing
After the wafer manufacturing is completed, functional testing is required using a probe card to remove defective products.Currently, the width and gap of the probe needle on the market can only reach 25/25 microns, and cannot meet the testing needs of ICs and Micro LEDs below 10/10 microns.The "density multiplication composite material probe card" developed by the Institute of Technology has three technological innovations: First, ultra-fine: using glass and metal composite materials to achieve a needle width of less than 10 microns, breaking through the limitations of traditional metal probes; Second, super cost saving: using laser patterned 3D ceramic circuits to replace multi-layer ceramic circuits, which are cheap and require only one-tenth of the traditional probes, and are highly elastic, and the line width can be reduced from 30 microns to 10 microns; Third, ultra-fast assembly: Micro-electromechanical modular joints can assemble more than 100 probes at a time, without manual needle installation, and is the world's first micro-electromechanical probe equipped with lens for CIS wafer testing.This technology is jointly developed in conjunction with the Industrial Research Institute, international material manufacturers and domestic probe card factories, successfully meeting the testing needs of ICs below 10/10 microns and Micro LEDs.
3. "Copper Foil Substrate and Carrier Board Materials and Verification Technology" assists Taiwan's 5G mmWave Substrate Material Technology Breakthrough
Millimeter wave communication has the advantages of speed and bandwidth, but the loss of high-frequency transmission signal is extremely impacted. Due to the characteristics of high-frequency band radio waves, material design needs to move towards lower losses.The Institute of Technology has laid out millimeter wave substrate material technology and patents, including low-loss resin, copper foil and copper foil substrate formula and process technology, and simultaneously established the "Copper foil substrate and carrier plate material and verification technology" platform, which has three major characteristics: 1. Substrate materials with low loss and high copper foil connection characteristics to improve the stability and reliability of signals under high frequency transmission; 2. Exclusive low-loss resin has good solubility in organic solvents, complies with the existing copper foil substrate process, and improves processing convenience; 3. Verification platform technology can help domestic and foreign material factories to conduct raw material evaluation and verification, and has assisted domestic operators in introducing international high-end upstream raw materials, and promoted cooperation between Taiwan's copper foil substrate industry and European, American and Japanese raw material manufacturers.
4. "Advanced MRAM chip technology and verification platform" to create independent research and development capabilities of domestic innovative memory
With the emergence of the automotive market, AI, high-efficiency computing chips and quantum computer fields in the future, the rapid processing of huge data has led to a surge in demand for high-efficiency memory.The Institute of Technology has developed magneto-resistive Random Access Memory (MRAM) technology, which has low energy consumption, fast reading and writing speed, no memory loss due to power outage, and has established a one-stop "Advanced MRAM chip technology and verification platform" to complete wafer design, manufacturing to test, to assist manufacturers in the introduction and mass production of new generation memory.In 2022, the platform has joined hands with leading semiconductor manufacturers to complete the world's fastest 0.4 nm-second write and ultra-high reliability SOT MRAM array chip, and cooperated with the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA) to develop ultra-low power magnetic memory chips, and the results have been recognized internationally.
