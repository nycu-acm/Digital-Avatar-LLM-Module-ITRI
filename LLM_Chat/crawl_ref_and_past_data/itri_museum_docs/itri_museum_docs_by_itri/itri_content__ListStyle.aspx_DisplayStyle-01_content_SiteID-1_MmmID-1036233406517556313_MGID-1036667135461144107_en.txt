About the Central Branch
The Central Branch of the Institute of Technology has entered the Zhongtai Innovation Park of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, combined with the China-Taiwan industry, academia and research resources to build an innovative R&D driving platform to develop forward-looking innovative technologies to drive the optimization of the central industrial structure. There are two main axis of R&D:
Science and Technology Agriculture and Industry System Integration Platform: Promote the transformation of net zero carbon emissions with industry-supported agriculture, connecting the "technology use end" of agriculture and the "technology supply end" of industry. The key technologies are net zero carbon emission R&D and application such as recycling of biomass materials, development of smart agricultural machinery and Internet of Things technology, and integration of agricultural green energy technology.
Sports Technology Cross-domain Integration Platform: Develop the sports technology industry ecosystem, promote the sports industry to reach a new value across the region, establish the integration of sports data, artificial intelligence, sensing and information communication technology, and lead international innovations. Related research and development include the integration of visual image recognition technology, communication positioning technology and added value of motion data.
