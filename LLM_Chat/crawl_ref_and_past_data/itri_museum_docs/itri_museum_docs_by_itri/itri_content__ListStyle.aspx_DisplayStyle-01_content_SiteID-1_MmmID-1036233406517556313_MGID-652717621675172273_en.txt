About Smart Machinery Technology Center
The Institute of Technology established the Machinery Machinery Technology Center in January 2015, hoping to combine the energy of the central precision machinery industry to develop high-end equipment and smart manufacturing technology, and promote the central region to become a new settlement of the machinery industry combining R&D, innovation and manufacturing high-value, so that the industrial settlement can be in line with the international level and enter new business opportunities in high-end emerging industries.
In line with the government's "Smart Machinery Innovation Industry" policy, it integrates cross-field smart machinery energy, promotes the industrialization of new technologies, and connects the central local industries. Since August 2016, the "Mechanical Machinery Technology Center" has been officially renamed "Smart Machinery Technology Center".
The Smart Machinery Technology Center will adapt to the government's strategic goal of promoting precision machinery as a smart machinery in response to the upgrading and transformation of the international manufacturing industry and the development of Industry 4.0. In addition to continuing to invest in the development of domestic controllers of tool machines and system application development, accelerating the energy construction of advanced tool machine controllers, system application software and processing verification demonstration systems in my country, optimize the industrial structure and enhance the added value of the industry.It will also develop on the core technology of the tool machine, combine smart robots and industrial 4.0 China NetEase Entity (CPS), Internet of Things, big data and other technologies, and develop to build digital manufacturing, smart factories and other systematic applications, and expand its application to related industrial machinery and processing and manufacturing fields, in order to closely grasp the industry's pulse, and connect related R&D energy in the mechanical field and other related R&D energy to create Taiwan's industrial value.
The R&D team of the Smart Machinery Technology Center has moved into the service industry of Taichung Industrial Zone and Nantou Zhongchuang Park to develop key smart machinery technologies such as advanced smart tool machines and controllers, cloud virtual tool machine design and analysis platform, high-end value-added application software, and remote service platforms.Through strengthening the connection with industry, academia and internationally, the Machine Tool Technology Center has made the Total Solution for domestic controllers and tool machines from design, manufacturing to application; to the core technologies of advanced tool machines and 3S (system/software/service; System/Software/Service) required for the development of smart machinery, allowing my country's tool machine industry to be upgraded from smart stand-alone to factory system output, and has developed integrated application technology in aerospace, green energy, biomedical, and automobile locomotives, and constructed a demonstration operation verification system to accelerate the integration of the tool machine industry settlement with the international level, enter the business opportunities of advanced emerging industries, and at the same time, collaborate with local energy to jointly promote the implementation of Taichung's goal as the "Smart Machinery Capital".
