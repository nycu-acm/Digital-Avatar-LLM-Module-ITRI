Cai Huiliang Academician
Deputy Director Cai Huiliang is currently the Strategy Director of Tai Optoelectronics. He led Tai Optoelectronics Materials Company to focus on transformation and enter the research and development of halogen-free materials, establish high technical barriers, and strengthen customer service, actively expand the market, optimize performance management quality and self-inspection system, and increase the shipment ratio of high-frequency and high-temperature substrates.In 2013, Tai Optoelectronics successfully developed the next-generation ultra-low dielectric Mid Tg environmentally friendly substrate, etc., and has become the world's largest environmentally friendly substrate manufacturer, and has obtained high-end application quality certification and mass production by many terminal manufacturers around the world, continuing to lead the way.
