Liu Deyin Academician
Academician Liu Deyin is the chairman of Taiwan Metal Circuit Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (TSMC), leading the board of directors, making final decisions for the company, and communicating with the government, industry and other countries on behalf of TSMC.Previously, Chairman Liu served as the senior deputy general manager of TSMC's advanced technology career, senior deputy general manager of operations, executive deputy general manager and co-operation director, general manager and co-executive director.Under the leadership of Chairman Liu, TSMC has won many international recognitions. In 2019, it was selected including the 500 largest companies in the world in Fortune, the top 100 best sustainable companies in the world in Corporate Knights, and the Dow Jones Sustainable World Index.
Chairman Liu graduated from the Department of Electrical Engineering of National Taiwan University and obtained a master's degree in electrical and computer information from the University of California, Berkeley.After graduation, Chairman Liu joined Intel Corporation and AT&T Bell Laboratory in the United States, and engaged in the research and development of microprocessor process technology and optical communication systems.In 1993, Chairman Liu joined TSMC and invested in the construction of Taiwan's first 8-inch wafer factory. He then presided over the construction of TSMC's first 12-inch wafer factory, creating an important business for the company to achieve super-large wafer factory and establishing TSMC's international status in semiconductor wafer manufacturing.Chairman Liu has also led TSMC Fabs to break through technical bottlenecks many times, greatly improving the yield of advanced processes, and building competitive advantages through the promotion of process technology.From 40 nanometers, 28 nanometers, 20 nanometers to below 16 nanometers, it has led the world's professional wafer manufacturing service industry, continues the growth momentum of TSMC's development, releases the innovative energy of the IC design industry, and brings diverse and rich terminal devices and application services.
Chairman Liu adheres to the company's core values.He and his business team established the TSMC Corporate Social Responsibility Executive Committee in 2019 and served as the chairman of the committee. He and a number of senior executives in different fields have formulated the company's five major strategies and guidelines for future corporate social responsibility, namely green manufacturing, establishing a responsible supply chain, creating a diversified and inclusive workplace, talent cultivation, and vulnerable care; at the same time, he encourages employees to participate and strengthen the depth and breadth of corporate social responsibility in TSMC's culture.
From the economy, society to the environment, Chairman Liu led TSMC to continue to forefront and strive forward. On the road to pursuing sustainable common ground, he has become a positive force to enhance society and bring good changes and impacts.
