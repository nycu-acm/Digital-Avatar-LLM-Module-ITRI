The Ministry of Economics Technology Department promotes the Taiwan-US Semiconductor R&D Alliance to sign MOU cross-border AI chip industry cooperation
The Ministry of Economics Technology Department supports the cooperation between the Institute of Industrial Research and the representative semiconductor R&D alliance between Taiwan and the United States. Under the witness of Zhang Peiren, Vice President of the Institute of Electrical and Optical Systems of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, Wu Zhiyi (the person sitting in the front row) and Professor Subramanian S. Iyer (the bottom left of the screen) of the University of California, Los Angeles, signed the "Memorandum of Cooperation for Advanced Packaging" to seize new business opportunities for AI artificial intelligence chips.
AI chips are an important core of AIoT (artificial intelligence Internet of Things) applications, like the brain, and are the technological focus of competition among countries.The Ministry of Economic Affairs' Technology Department promoted semiconductor and system operators to develop AI chips and vertical integration applications many years ago. Now it supports the representative semiconductor research and development alliance between the Institute of Technology and Research and Technology and Taiwan and the United States - Taiwan Artificial Intelligence Chip Alliance, and the UCLA Center for Heterogeneous Integration and Performance Scaling (CHIPS) and signed the "Memorandum of Cooperation on Heterogeneous Integration Advanced Packaging" (MOU) today (14th), hoping to strengthen the research and development and complementarity of Taiwan-US semiconductor technology with the advantages of Taiwan's AIoT and the development experience of high-efficiency computing in the United States, further deepen the supply chain cooperation between Taiwan and the United States, and successfully seize new business opportunities for AI artificial intelligence chips.
Lin Xianyi, a science and technology expert at the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, said that AI on Chip is the policy focus of the President's "Six Core Strategic Industries" and the Executive Yuan's "Taiwan AI Action Plan". Under this goal, the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs actively integrates semiconductor, system operators and global innovation institutions to develop AI chips and vertical integration applications.In 2010, it established the "AI on Chip Taiwan Alliance (AITA) in 2010, hoping to connect domestic IC design, manufacturing, packaging and testing and terminal device system application manufacturers, shorten product launch time and accelerate industrial transformation and upgrading. Since Taiwan has a complete semiconductor industry chain and AIoT advantages, the United States has an irreplaceable position in high-efficiency computing and has long had a close cooperative relationship. In order to deepen the cooperation between the Taiwan-US Industrial Technology Alliance and assist the domestic industry in developing highly competitive AI chips, the Technology Department supports the Industrial Research Institute, ATIA and UCLA CHIPS to work together to quickly grasp the international system specification trends from the fields of design, manufacturing, and packaging. In combination with Taiwan's advanced semiconductor manufacturing, it invests in the development of high-efficiency computing AI chips, and then creates the next generation of artificial intelligence innovative technologies and new services under the complementarity of both parties, and becomes a more reliable partner.
