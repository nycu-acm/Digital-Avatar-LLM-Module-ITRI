Academician Lin Genghua
Dr. Lin Genghua is an academician of the Central Academy of Research. He worked at IBM in his early years and is committed to the research and development of the field of optoelectronics.He has made significant contributions to the inception and pioneering of microwave surface acoustic waves, integrated optics, thin film acoustic and optical research. Dr. Lin returned to China to serve in 1992 and invested in my country's optoelectronics industry. He served as deputy director and director of the Optoelectronics Industry Research Institute of the Industrial Technology Research Institute.During his tenure, he worked hard to cultivate key domestic optoelectronics technology technologies and establish an infrastructure for the optoelectronics industry, and promoted the vigorous development of Taiwan's optoelectronics industry.He has successively won awards such as Academician of the Central Academy of Sciences, IEEE Fellow and Academician of the American Optical Society, and was the winner of the first Pan Wenyuan Award. Play a pioneering role in the leapfrog development of my country's optoelectronics industry.
