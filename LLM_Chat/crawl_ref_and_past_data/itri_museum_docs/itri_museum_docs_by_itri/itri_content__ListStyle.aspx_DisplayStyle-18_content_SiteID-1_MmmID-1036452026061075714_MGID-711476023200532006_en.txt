Cover Story Features
Make the machine smarter and quickly copy every perfection
Written by/Lai Wanjing Photography/Cai Shihao
The processing and manufacturing industry is an art, but no matter how exquisite the skills are, there is still a chance that flaws will appear between humans and machines. If you want to interpret every perfect work in a superb way, you will inevitably have to sacrifice your effectiveness.How to break the bottleneck?The Institute of Technology has developed the "process optimization software SpeedPro" across the country, becoming the smartest "head" of processing machines, allowing perfection to continue to reappear.
Process technology in the processing industry is changing with each passing day, but there is still a problem that cannot be managed by manual operations.For example, aerospace components require high safety and reliability. Manufacturers often need to directly process the whole piece of embryo material. Most workpieces have more than 90% cut and removal, and the parts are complex in shape and long processing time. For example, in order to meet the requirements of thin-shaped composite surface structural parts, most aerospace component manufacturers must purchase high-priced five-axis processing machines. The equipment costs are high and the engineering design of experienced technicians is still required to improve production efficiency as much as possible, but it is still difficult to escape the fate of high proofing costs, slow production capacity, and low yield.
Cross-border cooperation, a great leap forward in process optimization
Since 2011, the research team of the Institute of Technology has gone to the University of British Columbia in Canada to open the development of "digital processing".Luo Zuoliang, head of the Smart Manufacturing Technology Group of the Smart Machinery Technology Center of the Institute of Technology Research Institute, said that the main purpose of the research is to simulate the processing process. The R&D process not only requires many professional talents to invest, measure various machines, and establish a database.After running-in and communicating with international teams, we have slowly cultivated tacit understanding and finally jointly developed the "process optimization software SpeedPro."
The Institute of Technology and the University of British Columbia have worked together to provide delicate division of labor. After countless experiments, collecting cutting application data of various materials and tools, and establishing a huge database, the Institute of Technology and Technology conceived an optimized process software."After returning to Taiwan, we further thought about how to lower the threshold for operators to use. After all, no matter how easy-to-use process optimization software is, it must be convenient to popularize it to help the industry improve." Luo Zuoliang said that the best way is to write a huge database into a program and install it directly in a tool machine for customers to use it.
Drive the machine and get the product in place at once
SpeedPro introduces cutting path parameter diagnosis and optimization services, and is equipped with machine cutting energy measurement and analysis. It not only has stable cutting force, but also can accurately control the speed and depth, extend the tool life, but also makes the machine spindle load more stable."In the past, the processing process that needed to rely on the experience of senior engineers and masters was often unable to predict the limits of the machine due to limited capabilities, and we had to constantly find answers in failure. SpeedPro is to help people control the machine, master the variables in the production process, quantify every detail and improve product yield."
At present, auxiliary manufacturing software commonly used in the processing and manufacturing industry often only considers geometric accuracy and ignores the impact of cutting mechanics on processing quality. It usually depends on the experience of engineers. Constant attempts and errors can only find the available feed rate parameters. In addition, a processing path can only set a fixed feed rate, and the continuous changing feed rate cannot be set according to the geometric characteristics of the path and the contact conditions between the tool and the workpiece; in addition, if the feed rate is to be changed in the process, the user needs to obtain the original CAM program, and cannot adjust it on the processing machine immediately, which consumes time and manpower.
SpeedPro combines cutting dynamics and optimization methods to calculate the optimal feed rate at every point on the machining path, which can avoid damage to tools, workpieces and even machines due to feed rate setting errors. SpeedPro can provide an online engineering diagnostic mechanism, and can perform process diagnosis and optimization operations on the on-site machine. This is also an important technology that can greatly improve production efficiency.
Luo Zuoliang pointed out that all processed products are the most time-consuming and cost-effective.The team has done actual tests. It takes more than 20 hours to produce aerospace parts manually operated by the machine. SpeedPro uses digital and measurement technology to complete products with excellent accuracy in six hours, which is equivalent to improving four times the efficiency.
The optimization effects are also different for different industries and products.For example, if a product that has spent 10 years to accumulate experience, importing SpeedPro optimization process at this time can usually improve the efficiency by 10 to 20%; the other product development has only one or two years, and SpeedPro assists in the future to make progress so that it can even be calculated using "fold".
SpeedPro optimization process software can calculate the different needs of processed objects through simple settings and operations, and quickly and in large quantities of products with uniform quality.
Know the limits of machines and reach the highest level
Luo Zuoliang said, "The manufacturing industry often faces the dilemma of taking one month to proofread and making the first finished product for another month. After going to the production line, due to poor process efficiency, it may take two or three years to slowly correct it. SpeedPro's advantage is that it reaches the highest level at one time, and completes the proofing in the shortest time, and can seize business opportunities with high efficiency."
SpeedPro can also help the industry control product yields.Just as fast words are sloppy when writing, and slow words are neat, the words written should be for exhibition purposes or only for recording events, and can vary according to requirements.The same is true, the parameters can be adjusted during processing, depending on whether the customer needs to produce quickly or emphasizes the ultimate quality. They can all adjust the optimal production time according to the needs, and then control the consistent quality of the product.
In addition, SpeedPro is also suitable for any processing process. Through software calculations, the machine limit will be used to improve efficiency and the processing time will be shortened.Luo Zuoliang shares the direction of the team's current efforts: "In the future, the processing optimization process software can be put into the drawing files and materials like 3D printing technology, and the machine will calculate the process, including the conversion of the drawing files into processing paths, the processing paths and then automatically optimize them. These can be completed directly in the tool machine and then smoothly produced. In the process, no longer rely on manpower, and can reduce costs and increase corporate competitiveness."
Luo Zuoliang revealed that the team is building a platform that in addition to SpeedPro optimized process software, it will continue to develop several software with different functions, and in the future it can be used by the "pull-down menu" method for customers to use.Currently, manufacturers are often given trial use of "cooperation and sharing", so that more valuable data can be collected and SpeedPro software can be more powerful.
Improving cutting manufacturing technology has always been the focus of industrial development for countries around the world. Only by strengthening the competitiveness of manufacturing can we strengthen the country's economic development strength.SpeedPro optimized process software provides a shortcut to the rapid integration of the industry and assists in the upgrading of the industry. I believe that in the near future, Taiwan's processing and manufacturing industry will stand tall and stand at a new height.
Luo Zuoliang (second from right) and his team have been constantly investing in the field of processing products, and have tried to write the database into a program and directly install it in the tool machine to improve manufacturing efficiency.
