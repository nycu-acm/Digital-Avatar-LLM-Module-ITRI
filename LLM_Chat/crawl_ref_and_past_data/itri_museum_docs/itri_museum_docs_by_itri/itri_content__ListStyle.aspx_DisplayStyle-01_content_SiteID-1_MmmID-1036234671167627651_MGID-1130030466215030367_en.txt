Academician Ye Changjing
Academician Ye Changjing is the chairman and CEO of the Swiss new biotechnology company Onward Therapeutics, and serves as directors of two French biotechnology companies invested in by his company. He also serves as vice chairman of the Taiwan Bioindustry Development Association.Academician Ye has deep experience in the planning of new drug development projects, project management, and operation of international cooperation alliances, and is a leader in promoting innovation in Taiwan's biotechnology industry.
Academician Ye uses the original "NRDO" (No Research, Development Only) and Networked Pharma strategies to create a new new drug development model. The former refers to not doing basic research, but introducing potential new drug candidates, and developing them through authorized introduction (license-in).The latter refers to not creating a new drug development model for laboratories and factories, and working closely with the outsourced manufacturing industry (CMO) and the outsourced research industry (CRO), and from preclinical trials to human clinical trial stage. The key core capabilities of NRDO lie in the acumen and judgment of topic selection.For potential new drugs, process development, mass production, design animal tests and execution of multi-national and multi-center clinical trials, and maximize R&D resources.
Academician Ye graduated from the Department of Biology at Fu Ren University, and later went to the United States to obtain a doctorate degree in the Department of Immunology at South Carolina Medical University.He has served as a tenured researcher at the British Center for Biological Research and the French National Institute of Health and Medicine (INSERM). Later, he moved to the American biotechnology industry and joined well-known biotechnology companies such as T Cell Sciences, CytoMed, LeukoSite, and Millennium, serving as chief scientist and deputy general manager of product development.She has successfully integrated the research team many times, and her experience in international cooperation in developing new drugs includes small molecule drugs, genetic recombinant proteins, and single antibodies.
In 2003, Zhiqing Biotechnology Pharmaceutical Co., Ltd. was founded from animal testing and completed the second phase clinical trial of the new pancreatic cancer drug Onivyde (Annide, PEP02, MM-398) in just 8 years. It successfully authorized the United States Merrimack, setting a record for the foreign authorization amount of Taiwan's new drugs. It is also the first cancer drug in Taiwan to be approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA), setting a new milestone in the development of new drugs in my country.Onivyde has been approved by more than 40 countries in the Americas, Europe and Asia, and has been recognized as a standard therapy in major markets such as the United States and Europe. Another radiation lifting agent for the treatment of soft tissue sarcoma, Hensify (PEP503, NBTXR3), has also obtained a European Marketing License (CE Mark).
Academician Ye believes that the "NRDO" development model is very suitable for small and medium-sized biotech industries in Taiwan with small capital and low risk pressure.She applied it specifically to Zhiqing Biotechnology. This successful model has a far-reaching impact on the development of domestic biotechnology and pharmaceuticals, and has also opened up a new path for Taiwan's new biotechnology companies.
