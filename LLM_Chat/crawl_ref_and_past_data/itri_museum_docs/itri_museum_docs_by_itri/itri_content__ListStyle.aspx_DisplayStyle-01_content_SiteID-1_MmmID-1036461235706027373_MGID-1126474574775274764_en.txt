The Executive Yuan approved the "Taiwan Display Technology and Application Action Plan" in May 2020. In order to implement the policy implementation, the Ministry of Economic Development and Development Agency officially established the "Ministry of Economic Development and Development Agency's Smart Display Industry Cross-domain Cooperation Alliance" in 2021. The two presidents of the alliance are Dr. Ke Furen, Chairman of the Taiwan Display Industry Federation (TDUA), and Dr. Wu Zhiyi, Vice President and Senior Technical Expert of the Institute of Technology Research. At the same time, the alliance promotion team was established and four major SIGs (Special Interest) Group), through the integration of the "Smart Display Industry Cross-domain Cooperation Alliance" and the integration of industry, the company and research energy and resources, plans to promote cross-domain cooperation between domestic display components, panels, system integration and application services, in response to the demand for smart display applications in smart medical care, smart retail, smart mobile and smart culture fields for the development of 5G infrastructure, develop and integrate smart display application systems and system solutions, and introduce domestic and foreign application field trials to set an international model and expand industrial exports, accelerate industrial transformation and value improvement, improve my country's smart display application system ecosystem, and strengthen the sustainable development of my country's smart display industry supply chain.
