Technology introduction
Chessboard format AGV car delivery system.
Multi-carrier robot operating systems have been widely used in automated material processing systems. However, for systems that operate at the same time, how to build a back-end pipe system to resolve conflicts (dead knots) between vehicles is the primary problem.On the other hand, domestic light-load trucks with two-way operation functions have also been developed, and key module technologies including single-chip controllers, magnetic sensing modules and magnetic tracking drive control have been implemented in actual cases.
Features and innovation
Back-end instant management system: Through wireless transmission, the location of each truck is instantly returned to the back-end management system, and the management system will immediately coordinate the planning of different tasks such as travel path, speed, and docking time according to the conditions at the time.
Light load AGV: The whole machine system and key components are domestically made, with two-way movement and in-place rotation functions, suitable for operation in small spaces.
Application and benefits
Automotive, medical materials and other businesses – automated material handling.
Automation equipment operators – Integration of electric drag panel magnetic guide, low-bed AGV, and drive magnetic guide modules.
