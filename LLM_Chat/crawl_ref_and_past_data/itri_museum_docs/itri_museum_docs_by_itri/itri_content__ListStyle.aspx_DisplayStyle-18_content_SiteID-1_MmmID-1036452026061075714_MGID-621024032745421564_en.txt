Online instant thermal analyzer benefits the global LED industry
Chen Yufeng
"Online Instant Thermal Analyzer" assists LED manufacturers in improving production line yields in terms of speed, cost and accuracy, which is extremely important for the development of the LED industry.
The "Online Instant Thermal Energy Analyzer" technology developed by the Institute of Technology has been a year-long development, stands out in the fierce competition and won the "R&D 100 Awards of the World". The breakthrough of this technology is that it can use a higher efficiency and lower cost method to measure and analyze thermal resistance on the production line, greatly improving output efficiency and product yield.
The selection of the top 100 Science and Technology R&D 100 Awards, known as the Oscar of the technology industry, was announced, and the technology of the Institute of Technology has shined again.Among them, the "online instant thermal energy analyzer" developed by the Industrial Research Institute has been recognized in the field of imaging technology.It has cooperated with LED operators to actually improve production efficiency.
The development of "In-Line Compact Thermal Analyzer (ICTA)" integrates thermal, electrical, control and signal analysis technologies across fields. Important technologies include innovative TSP and thermal signal synchronization control technology, thermal structure analysis & In-line thermal detection, integrated measurement and automation control system through PXI interface, etc. It took about one year to complete. It has broken through international technical bottlenecks in key patent layout, and has applied for 4 cases and 6 patents in total.
The top 100 technologies are honored and new technologies are recognized
The top 100 global science and technology research and development awards held since 1963 have entered the 52nd year.The importance of this award lies in the fact that it has become an important indicator for identifying new technologies in the market. Technology that wins awards and affirmations can often establish a historical position in the history of scientific and technological development. For example, fax machines (1975), LCD screens (1980), Kodak Photo CDs (1991), Nicoderm smoking cessation patches (1992), Taxol anti-cancer drugs (1993), laboratory chips (1996) and high-quality TVs (1998), etc., have all been selected as the top 100 science and technology awards of that year. These technologies have had a significant impact on human life and have even become an indispensable part of life.
Regarding the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, President Xu Juemin pointed out that with the strong support of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has been able to develop rapid thermal energy measurement technology, and can combine LED automation machines to measure instantly on the production line, assisting LED manufacturers in improving production line yields in speed, cost and accuracy.This technology is extremely important for the development of the LED industry.
Liu Junting, director of the Institute of Electronics and Optoelectronics of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that light-emitting diodes (LEDs) are the main lighting sources in the future.According to the IHS and IEK report of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, the LED lighting market size will reach US$30.5 billion in 2014 and will grow to US$51.6 billion by 2018. The LED lighting market is extremely large, but there are still blind spots that need to be broken through, that is, the thermal resistance problem of the material interfaces of various layers of LEDs is still one of the important issues in the development of LEDs.In fact, as long as you can master the LED thermal resistance data, you can estimate the product's life, efficiency and optical quality.
Technology transformation results are domestically produced for the first time
However, although we know the crux of the problem, due to the complicated and time-consuming steps of measuring LED thermal resistance, and the measurement instruments often cost millions of NTDs, we can generally only measure in the laboratory during the product design stage, and cannot provide more comprehensive and accurate tests. The major breakthrough of the ICRC technology is that using this instrument can conduct real-time measurements on the production line, which can effectively improve production efficiency and product yield.
Liu Junting emphasized, "Heat dissipation performance has always been an important factor affecting the life and light quality of LEDs. The Industrial Research Institute has developed an analyzer that can measure LED thermal resistance in real time on the production line, which will greatly reduce the defect rate of LED lighting products and make consumers feel more at ease when purchasing products. This is conducive to the further popularization of LED lighting." In addition to being used in LED lighting production lines, this instrument can also be built on LED panels, mobile phones, personal computers, electric vehicle power modules, central processing units (CPUs), etc. that require thermal resistance measurement and analysis. In a flash, the advantages and disadvantages of the components can be analyzed.
It is particularly worth mentioning that this technology was transferred to Wangsi Technology last year, and the product is expected to be mass-produced and launched by the end of this year.Guo Yuanming, general manager of Wangsi Technology, said that the "online instant thermal energy analyzer" that the company transferred the technology of the Industrial Research Institute and is about to be mass-produced will be the first set of instruments developed and mass-produced by Taiwan for the first time. He also emphasized that "compared with the instruments of major foreign manufacturers, our instruments have the advantages of high measurement efficiency and low cost testing, and the product market competitiveness is optimistic." Wangsi Technology is a global professional manufacturer of wafer testing probe cards and LED testing related equipment, and has always focused on providing measurement technology for micro-contact micro-contact micro-engine industry.
Effectively improve production efficiency and significantly reduce costs
The high measurement efficiency mentioned above refers to the use of this instrument to measure the thermal structure of LED packaging components. Each LED only takes up to 3 minutes to measure the complete thermal structure of LED packaging components. This is currently the most efficient thermal structure analyzer in the world. Wang Jianping, deputy director of the Institute of Electrical and Optical Engineering, emphasized, "The past measurement took about ten minutes, and the use of this instrument can greatly shorten the test time."
More importantly, the "online instant thermal energy analyzer" developed by the Institute of Technology has been successfully integrated with LED automation photoelectric classification machines to form the world's first automated LED thermal resistance testing equipment. "Under the combination of the two, the measurement speed of this set of equipment can reach 12,000 components per hour, which is equivalent to only 0.3 seconds for each component measurement, which is much faster than the traditional measurement of 6 components in the laboratory in one hour, which is much faster than the speed of 2,000 times," said Wang Jianping.
The traditional steps for measuring the thermal resistance of LEDs are very complicated and time-consuming, so based on production efficiency considerations, most operators only conduct measurements in the laboratory during the product design stage.The thermal energy analyzer developed by the Industrial Research Institute breaks through this blind spot, not only can the thermal structure of the components be analyzed layer by layer, but also can be measured in real time on the production line.The main method used is to analyze the solid crystal layer - this is the key to LED heat dissipation capabilities. Through this method, the heat dissipation characteristics of LED products can be thoroughly analyzed.
In addition, in terms of low-cost competitive advantages, almost all similar instruments that can be seen on the market are international manufacturers, with prices ranging from about NT$3 million to 4 million, while the prices of domestic instruments are expected to be one-third to one-quarter of those of imported instruments. This will undoubtedly greatly reduce the equipment investment burden of LED operators, and can also be more popularized to be built on the production line to achieve an increase in yield.Wang Jianping said that in the LED packaging factory, about 203 thermal energy analyzers need to be built on one production line.
Strengthen consumer confidence and enhance LED lighting market acceptance
The "Online Instant Thermal Energy Analyzer" jointly developed by the Institute of Technology and Wangsi Technology is the world's first analyzer that combines transient thermal structure analysis and online thermal resistance measurement functions. It can meet the needs of R&D and mass production verification at the same time, and provides production lines for real-time monitoring, screening of defective products, process optimization, and improve yield, greatly reducing the defective rate of LED lighting products.
At present, there are about 100 billion LEDs produced every year around the world. Through this breakthrough in measurement technology, it can not only improve efficiency and yield, but also improve the degree of automated production. It is of great significance to LED lighting and panel applications, especially from previous sampling detection to large-scale measurements using this instrument. The chance of consumers buying bad products is greatly reduced, which is more conducive to promoting the market acceptance of LED lighting fixtures.
Driven by the policy of the Institute of Technology and Research, the "online instant thermal energy analyzer" research, this time, with key software and module technologies, combined with the complete patent layout, integrated domestic LED equipment manufacturers, and successfully developed an online instant thermal energy test system "online instant thermal energy analyzer" with high efficiency and low cost competitive advantages, which can provide domestic manufacturers with complete technical solutions.
This breakthrough in measurement technology will accelerate the mass production speed of the LED industry and improve product quality, once again demonstrating the innovative spirit of driving the industry through scientific and technological research and development, and working with the industry to make the world a better place for the industrial research institute.
