Technology introduction
Waste foam disposable polyol is used to make a sofa.
The existing physical recycling technology of thermosetting waste PU foam can only be remade by extrusion/bonding for factory scraps to be used as a supporting material to reduce the level, and the sales volume is limited. The related recycling technology cannot meet the demand of the brand automobile/locomotive and furniture industry for PU recycling materials sources.The Institute of Technology has developed PU depolymerization and characteristic control technology, which can dispolymerize waste foam into polyol slurry, and then foamed foam microstructure is similar to the new material, and can be directly introduced into the original foaming process for original foam application.
Features and innovation
Based on the characteristics of waste foam material source, our team can successfully produce regenerated polyols with similar crosslinking characteristics to new polyols through the design of depolymerization agent formula and process control. The content of waste foam can reach 30%, and there are no by-products in the depolymerization process. The regenerated polyols can be directly introduced into the original foam foaming process and can be completely reused for PU soft foaming applications.
Application and benefits
Chemical depolymerization technology of waste foam can be widely used in various foam materials such as general use and high rebound, and can be used to perform depolymerization and subsequent formulation design and control according to the application characteristics of mattresses and cushions.Linking brands and building a domestic polyurethane cycle industry chain will strengthen Taiwan's technical energy and competitiveness in circular polyurethane.
