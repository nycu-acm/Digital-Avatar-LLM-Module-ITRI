Technology introduction
In the efficacy trial of homologous mice, M1+anti-CD47 CAR (M1-CAR) mRNA-LNP (MC3-t2) can effectively inhibit A20 tumor metastasis to the liver and reduce tumor volume (Tumor Burden).
This technology can be applied to the treatment of solid tumors (such as liver cancer, lung cancer, etc.).It is characterized by the use of lipid nanoparticles (LNPs) to form mRNA-LNP nucleic acid drugs with tumor antigen (TAA) specific use, combined with mRNA that promotes the activation of macrophages M1 type, to form mRNA-LNP nucleic acid drugs.This innovative strategy can directly generate chimeric receptor CARs with tumor antigen (TAA) specificity in vivo (In Vivo) and activate into M1 chimeric antigen receptor macrophages (CAR-M), thereby producing anticancer effects.
M1 CAR-M mRNA-LNP drugs can be used as an off-the-shelf immune cell therapy preparation, with low treatment costs and can be administered multiple times to make long-term treatment possible.Using macrophages with natural properties that can penetrate the tumor parenchyma combined with mRNA-LNP technology provides a high-efficiency, scalable and clinically accessible new generation immunotherapy for solid tumors.
Features and innovation
Technical Features
M1-mRNA: Effectively induce macrophages to polarize to M1 type and enhance their anti-tumor ability.
CAR-mRNA: enables macrophages to express chimeric antigen receptors (CARs), improve tumor cell recognition ability, and enhance their tumor suppression efficacy.
The above two mRNAs are encapsulated in lipid nanoparticles (LNPs), protecting mRNA from degradation and enhancing their efficacy in ingestion by macrophages.
Activated tumor microenvironment regulation: After CAR-macrophages penetrate into the tumor microenvironment, they actively participate in tumor cell phagocytosis, antigen presentation and release of proinflammatory cytokines, and promote strong immune activation of solid tumors.
Technical Advantages
Macrophages play a key role in cancer immunity, including tumor infiltration, phagocytosis, antigen presentation and regulation of adaptive immunity to achieve tumor clearance.
Dual mechanism: combining macrophage activation (M1 type polarization) with tumor-targeted CAR expression to produce a more comprehensive anti-tumor immune response.
In Vivo Delivery (In Vivo): No in vitro cellular engineering, reducing the complexity of production and clinical treatment.
Regulating the tumor microenvironment: Activating macrophages promotes immune activation and enhances solid tumor suppression efficacy.
Application and benefits
Solve the difficulties in the treatment of solid tumors in clinical immune cell therapy.
Reduce the cost of immune cell therapy in clinical cancer treatment and provide high-efficiency clinical solid cancer treatment strategies.
