Wu Minqiu Academician
Academician Wu Minqiu is the founder, chairman and CEO of Wanghong Electronics. He led Wanghong to become a world-class non-volatile memory leader. Not only is the world's largest read memory, but the NOR type flash memory is also the world's number one.Wanghong is the first semiconductor company in Taiwan to promote its own brand "products" and "technology", and is also one of the few leading IC integrated components manufacturers in Taiwan with IC design, manufacturing, packaging and testing and marketing capabilities.On average, 1 out of every 3.6 people worldwide uses Wanghong's chips, and each new car has Wanghong's products.
Academician Wu led the global implementation of comprehensive computerization of semiconductor wafer factories 32 years ago, and also pioneered the creation of the "Independent Research and Development Engineering Data Analysis System" (sNOVA) in semiconductor wafer factories combining statistical knowledge, which quickly discovered abnormal quality problems with AI and big data technology to ensure the highest quality products provided by customers.Many Taiwanese wafer factories have learned to introduce them to accelerate the production efficiency and vigorous development of the electronics industry.
In order to assist the domestic high-tech industry with more sufficient resources for product and technology research and development, Academician Wu promoted the establishment of the "third-class stock" with high-tech industry as the investment target.In 1995, Wanghong Electronics became the first benchmark company to be listed in Taiwan with the third-class stock, which in turn helped to significantly increase the proportion of overseas funds investing in Taiwan. In 1996, Wanghong Electronics also led Wanghong Electronics to become the first Taiwanese high-tech company listed on NASDAQ in the United States.
Under the leadership of Academician Wu, Wanghong Electronics' R&D strength has been highly recognized internationally. In 2011, the US Patent Board ranked 18th in the world in terms of overall patent strength, and was the first among Taiwanese companies;the scientific strength of making good use of scientific patents to build patents is ranked 11th in the world. Faced with the international patent war, Wanghong fought back with high-quality patents and received more than US$100 million in compensation, once again proving Wanghong's strong R&D and intellectual financial strength.
Academician Wu has been deeply engaged in the industry and supports scientific education for a longer period of time. In 2001, he established the "Wanghong Education Foundation" and promoted the "Wanghong Golden Silicon Award" and the "Wanghong Science Award" for more than 20 consecutive years. More than 35,000 people participated in the total, and the scholarship awarded exceeded 160 million yuan.In 2019, Wanghong donated 420 million yuan to build the "Success Innovation Center - Wanghong Pavilion" and also created the "Minxu Intelligent Computing Academy" with a donation of 1 billion yuan in 10 years, promoting an innovative education system for smart computing and cross-domain talent cultivation, which has made outstanding contributions to enhancing Taiwan's international competitiveness and cultivating young scientific and technological talents.
From industrial innovation to talent cultivation, Academician Wu led Wanghong Electronics to continue to forefront and strive forward. While pursuing innovation, he actively implemented the concept of green enterprises and giving back to society, and continued to play a key role in Taiwan's global semiconductor industry chain.
