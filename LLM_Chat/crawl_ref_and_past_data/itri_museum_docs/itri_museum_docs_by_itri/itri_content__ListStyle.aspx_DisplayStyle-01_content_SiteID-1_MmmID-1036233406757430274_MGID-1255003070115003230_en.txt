The Institute of Technology and Mitsubishi Electric will focus on "net zero emissions" as the primary cooperation, conduct research and development exchanges on key technologies, and hope to assist the industry in building a green ecosystem and jointly move towards the net zero emission target in 2050. From the front row, the picture starts from the left, Director of the Obstetrics International Institute of the Institute of Technology Research Institute, Lin Jiarong, Director of the Institute of Technology Research Institute, Hu Zhusheng, Deputy Director of the Institute of Technology Research Institute, Liu Wenxiong, Director of the Institute of Technology Research Institute, Okatoshi Okatoshi, Chief Executive Officer and Development Headquarters of Mitsubishi Electric, Taiwan’s Chairman and General Manager Toshio Kawahiro, Japan’s Mitsubishi Electric Technology Director Jun Osamu, and Japan’s Mitsubishi Electric Senior Manager Takashi Kawakami.
On April 16, the Institute of Technology and Research signed a cooperation agreement with Mitsubishi Electric Japan. This is the first time that Mitsubishi Electric has carried out comprehensive and multi-field cooperation with overseas research institutions.It is also a model of cooperation in which a few researchers arranged for joint research and corporate R&D teams in the Institute of Technology and Research, setting an important milestone for the relationship between the two sides.The Industrial Research Institute and Mitsubishi Electric will focus on "net zero emissions" as their primary cooperation focus, focusing on key technologies such as carbon capture, utilization and storage, decentralized energy management systems, and low-carbon hydrocarbon production technology and application for research and development and exchanges, hoping to assist the industry in building a green ecosystem and jointly move towards the goal of net zero emissions by 2050.
The Institute of Technology and Mitsubishi Electric has been in close contact since 2013, and has interacted and communicated in the fields of smart machinery, smart life, biomedicine, renewable energy, etc. Driven by the net zero emission target of 2050, the Institute of Technology and Mitsubishi Electric jointly focused on technical research and development, innovative cooperation, and in-depth talent exchange in the field of net zero emissions, including joint investment in technology development of carbon capture, utilization and storage, as well as focusing on the research and development of decentralized energy management systems, and carried out large-scale cross-domain power dispatch verification at the Shalun Campus of the Institute of Technology and Technology to accelerate the realization of energy transformation goals.Looking ahead, we plan to focus on hydrogen application research and development. The two sides will jointly promote the market application of hydrogen energy and low-carbon technologies to achieve the goal of energy transformation and carbon emission reduction, and open up new paths for the green transformation of Taiwan's energy industry.At the same time, the two sides will further deepen cooperation, expand innovative technology cooperation in multiple fields such as AI digital transformation and smart life, and join forces to usher in a bright and sustainable future.
