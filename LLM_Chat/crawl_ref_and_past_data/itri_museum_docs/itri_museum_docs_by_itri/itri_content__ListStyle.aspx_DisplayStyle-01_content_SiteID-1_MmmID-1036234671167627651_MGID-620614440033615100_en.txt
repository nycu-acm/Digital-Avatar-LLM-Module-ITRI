Academician profile
Li Bingjie Academician
Chairman Li has been engaged in the development of MOCVD (organometal gas phase epitaxial) technology since 1985. He is a pioneer in Taiwan's investment in this technology. He has won the Executive Yuan Outstanding Science and Technology Contribution Award and was listed in the 15th edition of the World Who's Who.Under its leadership, the team successfully demonstrated Taiwan's first LD and LED of red light aluminum gallium phosphide (AlGaInP), making Taiwan's optoelectronic semiconductor materials and various epitaxial technologies from scratch, making Taiwan a major center for the production of light-emitting diodes in MOCVD technology, and also making Taiwan a world optoelectronic semiconductor component manufacturing center, which has made great contribution to industrial development.
