Scientific research strength Blooming International, three innovative technologies of the Industrial Research Institute won the 2022 Top 100 Global Science and Technology R&D Awards
Share to Facebook
Share to Twitter
Share to my Line
Contact Us
Date: 2022/10/05
The Ministry of Economic Affairs held the "Scientific Research Strength Blooming International 2022 R&D 100 Awards Awards Awards Press Conference" today (5th), sharing ten major achievements in the world in terms of scientific and technological research and development awards, the second largest number of awards in the world and the first in Asia.
Taiwan’s innovative R&D strength has shined internationally again!The R&D 100 Awards, known as the Oscars in the R&D industry, recently released the list of winners.With the support of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology has won three technologies in total with the "9KC GreenTape™ LTCC 5G mmWave Communication Technology" jointly developed by DuPont Micro Circuit Materials (Micro Circuit Materials (MCM) company! This year, the Institute of Technology has won the top 100 scientific and technological research and development awards in the world for 15 consecutive years. It has also ranked awards with well-known institutions such as MIT Lincoln Laboratory, Oak Ridge National Laboratory and Florida State University National High Magnetic Field Laboratory. It is truly the light of Taiwan!
Lin Quanneng, Deputy Minister of Economic Affairs, pointed out that Taiwan's strong R&D momentum is world-renowned. The top 100 science and technology research and development awards in the world are one of the most representative awards in the international research and development industry. This year, Taiwan’s innovative technology not only includes 12 awards in one go, but also ranked second in the world and first in Asia, surpassing Europe and Japan.Among them, the four legal entities supported by the Science and Technology Project of the Ministry of Economic Affairs - the Institute of Technology, Metal Center, Capital Policy Association, and Textile Institute, won 10 top 100 global science and technology research and development awards, with outstanding results.Not only that, the R&D results supported by the Ministry of Economic Affairs’ science and technology project are the core driving force for Taiwan’s industrial transformation to leap forward.The Technology Department's science and technology project has won 75 top 100 global science and technology research and development awards for 15 consecutive years.More than 90% of the technology-transferred manufacturers have even incubated many new startups. This is to truly use innovative technology to solve industrial problems and drive industrial benefits and value.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research and Research, said that Taiwan’s R&D energy can be said to be blooming everywhere this year and is highly competitive internationally.According to statistics, there are 165 technologies shortlisted for the world's top 100 science and technology research and development awards this year, and Taiwan has been shortlisted for 22, accounting for 13.3% of the world, making it the largest number of shortlisted except the United States.In addition, the award-winning technologies have huge industrial or social benefits.Taking the three technologies won by the Institute of Technology Research and Research as an example, the "high-precision array micro-micro LED full-color display module" with ultra-wide color gamut has entered terminal demand from the R&D stage, driving Taiwan's industrial transformation and upgrading.Another Industrial Research Institute assisted DuPont Microcircuit and Component Materials Company to develop its high-temperature, low-depletion low-temperature cofired ceramics (LTCC) application and development, which is an important support for Taiwanese manufacturers to enter the Open Radio Access Network (O-RAN). "AI-assisted Diagnosis and Detection Technology for Fundus Lesions" can quickly screen out and mark lesions, greatly reducing the burden on doctors.
Liu Wenxiong pointed out that the Institute of Technology has participated in the world's top 100 science and technology research and development awards for the first time in 2008, and has won awards for 15 consecutive years, accumulating 50 awards, of which more than 90% have been invested in the industry.I also thank the Ministry of Economic Affairs for its long-term support for the development of innovative technologies through scientific and technological projects, giving the Institute's innovative capabilities the Institute has the opportunity to leap to the international level and at the same time enhancing Taiwan's industrial competitiveness.The Institute of Technology will continue to work hard to be market demand-oriented, develop solutions, create new markets, and add fuel to Taiwan's industrial transformation and upgrading.
The award-winning technologies supported by the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs are as follows:
High precision array micro Micro LED full color display module
From 3C products and automotive curved displays, international manufacturers are all aiming at the unlimited potential of Micro LED!The Industrial Research Institute uses technology to assist Taiwanese panel operators in seizing Micro LED business opportunities.The "High Precision Array Micro Micro LED Full Color Display Module" developed by the Institute of Technology not only has high resolution, high brightness, high integration, low power consumption and other characteristics, but also has great commercial value in terms of experience intensity, applicable devices, environment and battery life.It also drives Taiwan's Micro LED industry in the form of an alliance, and joins hands with manufacturers such as Chuang Technology and Zuozhen to enter emerging markets such as in-vehicle display and next-generation XR glasses, successfully assisting Taiwan's Micro LED industry in the international stage. The total amount of cooperation or technology conversion has exceeded hundreds of millions.
AI-assisted diagnosis and detection technology for fundus lesions
Factors such as low birth rate, aging and the COVID-19 epidemic have accelerated the popularization of global smart medical applications.Through more than 50 ophthalmic specialists, the Institute of Technology and Research can help mark more than 100,000 fundus images. The trained AI-assisted diagnosis system can quickly screen out and mark lesions, greatly reducing the burden on doctors, and increasing the confidence of non-ophthalmologists in AI interpretation results; it can also integrate and match existing medical equipment such as fundus cameras, and cooperate with public welfare and medical institutions to conduct fundus screening services in mountains, offshore islands, rural areas and other places to achieve the purpose of early detection and early treatment, which will greatly help the health of Chinese people, and protect the health of rural areas with technology.
9KC GreenTape™ LTCC in 5G mmWave Communication Technology
With the rapid development and popularization of global wireless communications, the 5G millimeter wave band has become an important key to the development of next-generation networks.The Institute of Technology and Technology has cooperated with DuPont microcircuits and component materials to jointly build the low-temperature cofired ceramics (LTCC) of DuPont microcircuits and component materials to achieve low loss, high stability and high heat dissipation through the design and other technologies of the Institute of Technology and Technology millimeter wave circuit, and successfully creates 5G millimeter wave communication components with great commercial value, further driving domestic manufacturers to introduce 5G terminal applications, becoming a powerful tool for Taiwanese operators in the field of 5G O-RAN, and gaining a leading position in the global high-frequency communication market.
The "High Precision Array Micro Micro LED Full Color Display Module" developed by the Institute of Technology won the 2022 Top 100 Science and Technology Research Awards. It has four major characteristics: high analysis, high brightness, high integration, and low power consumption. It can meet the high immersion, thinness, high battery life and comfortable operating characteristics required by the metaverse world.
The "AI-assisted Diagnosis and Detection Technology for Fundus Lesions" developed by the Institute of Technology won the 2022 Top 100 Science and Technology Research Awards. The AI ​​diagnostic assisted analysis system combining ophthalmologists' professional and artificial intelligence technology can quickly screen out and mark lesions, greatly reducing the burden on doctors.
The "9KC GreenTape™ LTCC developed by the Institute of Technology won the 2022 Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Awards for 5G mmWave Communication Technology". It will successfully create communication components for 5G mmWave for high-temperature, low-depletion, low-temperature cofired Ceramics (LTCC) to successfully create communication components for 5G mmWave, helping Taiwanese factories expand the field of open wireless communication research and development.
