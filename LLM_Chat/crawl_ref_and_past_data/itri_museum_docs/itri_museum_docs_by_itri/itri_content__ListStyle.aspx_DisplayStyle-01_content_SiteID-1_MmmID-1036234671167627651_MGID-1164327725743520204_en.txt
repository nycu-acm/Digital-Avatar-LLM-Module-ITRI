Hai Yingjun Academician
Academician Hai Yingjun is the chairman of Delta Electronics Industry Co., Ltd. In 1999, he transferred from the financial industry to the technology industry and joined Delta for more than 20 years. With his financial and financial expertise, he established a more complete and sound management and operation model for Delta, sorted out his investment business, led Delta to focus on the power electronics industry, and successfully extended its core technology to related fields such as energy conservation and green energy.Under the leadership of Chairman Hai Yingjun, Delta has carried forward the business mission of "Environmental Protection, Energy Saving, Love the Earth" and became a leading manufacturer providing new generation green energy technology and energy management solutions. It has achieved great results in its operation and has led more companies to jointly promote environmental sustainability through actions.
Academician Hai graduated from the Department of Sociology at National Taiwan University and obtained a master's degree in international enterprise management from the University of Texas, Dallas, USA. He has held important positions at well-known international financial institutions such as Citibank, JPMorgan Chase Group, and Lehman Brothers.He has served as the general manager of GE Capital's Taiwan. He entered Delta in 1999 and served as vice chairman and executive director in 2004. In 2012, he was elected by the board of directors as chairman. He is responsible for examining the company's long-term strategic development direction and actively strengthening corporate governance and board operation functions.He is the first Taiwanese corporate leader to win the "Asian Business Leader Best Talent Management Award" twice.
In recent years, Academician Hai has spared no effort to promote the issue of net zero emissions. In 2015, Delta joined the We Mean Business initiative to align with the international sustainable trend; passed the scientific carbon reduction target in 2017; joined RE100 (100% Renewable energy) in 2021, promising that Delta's global stronghold will use 100% recycled electricity by 2030, hoping to be the leader of Taiwanese companies and promote sustainable environmental development.Academician Hai also serves as the chairman of the Taiwan Climate Alliance, and works with technology giants such as AUO Optoelectronics, TSMC, Taiwan Microsoft, Optoelectronics, Acer, PetroChina, and Asus to use their influence to assist Taiwan's industrial links to the international community and contribute to the sustainable environment.
