The Institute of Technology and Research Works with four major medical systems, using AI innovative technology to assist healthy Taiwan, and build MedBobi 2.0 smart decision-making medical system, accelerating the upgrading of the smart medical industry

Date: 2025/07/21
The Institute of Technology and Research announced that it will work with Yadong Hospital, Sun Yat-sen Medical University Hospital, Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital and Chimei Hospital to jointly launch the "MedBobi 2.0 Integrated Platform" to create a new model for the implementation of smart medical AI and a diffusible business ecosystem.From the left are Cai Mingzhe, director of the General Hospital of Sun Yat-sen Medical University, Qiu Guanming, director of the Yadong Memorial Hospital, Zhuang Yaoyu, deputy general manager of the Institute of Technology and Biomedical Medicine, Chen Jinshun, director of Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital, and Chen Xiuqin, deputy director of the Qimei Hospital.
In response to the challenge of tight medical and labor, the Institute of Technology announced today (21st) that it will work with Yadong Hospital, Sun Yat-sen Medical University Hospital, Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital and Chimei Hospital to jointly launch the "MedBobi 2.0 Integrated Platform" to create Taiwan's first smart medical co-creation platform with "clinical corpus (referring to commonly used communication terms in clinical practice) and AI model training" as the core.By introducing multimodal generative AI and Chinese voice recognition technology, the integrated platform will launch an upgraded version of "MedBobi 2.0" with intelligent decision-making capabilities, assisting medical staff from administrative support to clinical judgment, not only shortening the operating time from 30 minutes to 3 minutes, but also helping to reduce the turnover rate of medical staff by about 50% to 70%, creating a new model for the implementation of smart medical AI and a diffusible business ecosystem.
Zhuang Yaoyu, deputy president of the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials Research and director of the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials, said that according to the report of the research agency Research Nester, the global healthcare artificial intelligence market size in 2024 is approximately US$49.71 billion, with an annual compound growth rate of more than 45.3%, and will exceed US$6.4 trillion by 2037. In order to assist the medical industry in the wave of AI, the Industrial Research Institute developed and launched the "MedBobi Medical and Care Voice" in 2024, which has been successfully introduced into clinical hospitals and has received widespread praise.This year, we will evolve again on the basis of existing ones and upgrade the launch of "MedBobi 2.0", using an open platform to integrate clinical voice information from four smart medical institutions to build a smart medical assistant system that is closer to on-site needs.This system not only effectively reduces the documents and administrative burdens of medical staff, but also assists in providing precise decision-making and comprehensively improves the quality of care.This platform will be open to the use of clinical medical institutions and long-term care institutions across Taiwan, showing the strong momentum of the Industrial Research Institute and the medical community to promote industrial transformation and promote technological diffusion, and implement the policy direction of supporting medical care with science and technology and strengthening care resilience. It specifically responds to President Lai Ching-te's "Healthy Taiwan" policy to create a friendly medical environment and reduce the burden of medical care, so that the care site is more powerful, and the health of the whole people becomes the cornerstone of national progress.
Qiu Guanming, director of Yadong Memorial Hospital, said that the hospital continues to be committed to smart medical innovation. This time, we will work with the Institute of Technology to jointly promote the MedBobi 2.0 integration platform. We hope to strengthen the front-line medical decision-making support capabilities through the integration of clinical voice and AI models, accelerate the implementation of AI technology, improve the quality of patient care, and realize the vision of smart medical care.Cai Mingzhe, director of the General Hospital of Sun Yat-sen Medical University, pointed out that the integration of clinical pronunciation and semantic data will help improve the efficiency of medical decision-making. In the future, we hope to accelerate the introduction of clinical processes and create a more immediate and accurate smart care model. We look forward to jointly opening a new chapter in the implementation of AI medical care.
Chen Jinshun, director of Kaohsiung Veterans General Hospital, said that promoting AI into clinical services and becoming an intelligent assistant for medical personnel is our goal to improve medical services and innovation.We are looking forward to cooperating with the Institute of Technology and Research to create a smarter and higher-quality medical field through the MedBobi 2.0 platform to provide patients with warmer and considerate care services.Chen Xiuqin, deputy director of the Qimei Hospital project, said that as the largest medical system in Yunnan, Qimei Hospital is committed to promoting the practice of smart medical care.We are honored to participate in the cooperation of "MedBobi 2.0 Integrated Platform". Through the clinical application of smart medical assistant systems, we not only reduce the burden of medical care, but also improve the quality of patient care, demonstrate the actual benefits of AI in clinical practice, and open a new milestone in smart medical care.
The "MedBobi 2.0 Integrated Platform" takes actual clinical needs as the core, introduces AI technology from the Institute of Technology and Research, and combines the high-quality voice information provided by four clinical hospitals to cover medical terms, tone and clinical situations, thereby effectively improving AI's understanding and response ability to medical language."MedBobi 2.0" can integrate functions such as voice recognition, perceptual reasoning and instant response, assist medical staff in making accurate judgments quickly, reduce the burden of documents and communication, further optimize work processes, and improve overall care quality.The Institute of Technology hopes to promote the use of smart medical technology from single-point verification to large-scale application through cross-hospital sharing of clinical corpus and continuous optimization models, create a replicable and diffusible innovative service model, build a more complete and advanced and expanding smart medical and nursing ecosystem, accelerate the systematic development of Taiwan's smart medical industry, and assist Taiwanese factories in looking at the international market.
Faced with opportunities and challenges in the new era, the Institute of Technology continues to focus on new market values ​​and new needs, formulated the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" as the research and development direction, focusing on the research and development directions of four major application areas: "smart life", "healthy and happy life", "sustainable environment" and "resilient society", and develops "smart enabling technology" to promote application fields.In the field of "healthy and happy life", market demand-oriented development solutions are guided by partners, such as introducing innovative technologies into the front-line field applications, driving the transformation and upgrading of Taiwan's medical technology industry, so as to seek the welfare of human society and lead the industrial society to a better future.
