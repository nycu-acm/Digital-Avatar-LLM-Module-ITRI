Technology introduction
Smart reading meter verification system - gas meter.
Under the "Constructing Public Internet of Things for People's Livelihood" in the government's "Foresight Infrastructure Design Painting", it is mentioned that sensor layout must reach a certain level in order to build the base architecture of the Internet of Things, and the people's livelihood table is the most common and widely distributed sensor.
Features and innovation
In order to meet the needs of national development, the Institute of Technology has established a "smart meter reading verification system" for people's livelihood meters that are highly accurate and compatible, such as water meters, electricity meters, and gas meters. According to international standards, a system has been established to standardize it. It has a complete basic perception layer for smart cities and is built to meet my country's smart meter reading electronics, safety regulations, metrology and detection energy and specifications.
Application and benefits
To ensure fair market transactions, maintain public safety, and promote the scientific and technological development of related industries.
