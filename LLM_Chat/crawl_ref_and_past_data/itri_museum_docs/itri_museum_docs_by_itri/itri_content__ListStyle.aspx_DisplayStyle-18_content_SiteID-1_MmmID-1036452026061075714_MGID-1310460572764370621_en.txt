Research Institute of Technology advances Japan Drone and deepens Taiwan-Japan supply chain cooperation
Compiled/Lai Wanjing
Attack the Japanese high-growth market of drones
The Institute of Technology and Research participated in the Japan Drone Taiwan Pavilion, displaying six major technologies including long-term fuel cell drones, logistics drones, and AI drone fleet systems. It is expected to start industrial cooperation between Taiwan and Japan.
In response to the wave of geopolitics and global supply chain restructuring, "non-red supply chain" has become the focus of international industrial layout.Observing the rapid growth and independent policy orientation of Japan's drone market, the Taiwan Excellent UAV Overseas Business Opportunity Alliance recently led the "Taiwan Excellent UAV Overseas Business Opportunity Alliance" with the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs to create a Taiwan Pavilion in the "Japan Drone 2025" held by Makuhara Chiba, Japan.
The Institute of Technology has exhibited six innovative technologies for independently developed drone development, locked in high-value application markets such as inspections, logistics and transportation, actively linked Taiwan's supply chain and international buyers, and demonstrated its determination and strength to enter the Japanese market.On the first day of the launch, including Taipei Economic and Cultural Representative Office Ambassador Li Yiyang, Japanese government officials and many industry, officials and academics, visited the Taiwan Pavilion and expressed high interest in the many innovative technologies displayed by the Institute of Technology Research.
During the exhibition, the Institute of Technology also formed a group to visit the Japan UAS Industry Development Association (JUIDA), as well as 10 furniture representative companies including the Japan UAS Industry Development Association (JUIDA), Japan's leading drone service development company, Japan's leading electric vertical take-off and landing (eVTOL) and logistics drone development company, to expand cooperation opportunities such as technology authorization, joint R&D mechanisms and application links, and deepen the Taiwan-Japan drone independent supply chain partnership.
Master the technological dominance and seize the business opportunities of Japan's market
According to a report by the Japan Impress Comprehensive Research Institute, the Japanese drone market is expected to reach US$3.84 billion in 2025, exceed US$6.34 billion in 2028, with an average annual growth rate of 18.6%, especially in the surge in demand for logistics and distribution and infrastructure inspection, reaching US$700 million by 2024. Especially recently, driven by the Japanese government's promotion of independent supply chain policies and the reconstruction of "non-red supply chains", a drone budget of more than 100 billion yen has been compiled, indicating that it attaches great importance to and is optimistic about the potential for industrial development.With the support of the Industrial Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics, the Institute of Industrial Research is focusing on industrial needs and committed to promoting the independent and high-value application development of key drones. This time, six innovative achievements are exhibited, covering the application of the entire machine and key modules.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that the Institute of Technology Research, accurately responds to industrial needs, assists the overseas layout of the "Taiwan Excellent UAV Overseas Business Opportunity Alliance", masters key technologies for drones and expands international business opportunities. For example, it has introduced Parrot, the world's second largest drone manufacturer, and ACSL, Japan's largest drone manufacturer, to establish a supply chain in Taiwan, and at the same time promotes the Japanese UAV Alliance to build a non-red supply chain in Taiwan.This exhibition is the second time to participate in the most influential Japanese drone exhibition in Asia after participating in the largest German drone exhibition in Europe in February this year.As Japan actively promotes the independent supply chain of drones, this exhibition is not only an important opportunity to demonstrate Taiwan's technological strength, but also looks forward to opening a new wave of cooperation in Taiwan and Japan's industries.
Core technology debuts, focusing on high-value applications and independent modules
The six drone technologies exhibited by the Institute of Technology cover 3 whole aircraft applications and 3 key modules. They focus on features such as "high battery life", "smart group flight", and "strength performance", and are optimized for design according to different application situations.
In terms of the application of the whole machine, we will focus on high-value application needs such as inspection, logistics and special tasks.First of all, the "Long-time Fuel Cell UAV" is equipped with hydrogen fuel cells, breaking through the battery life limit of lithium batteries and achieving 181-minute flight time (load 5 kg). Compared with most of the large manufacturers currently using lithium batteries, the battery life performance is three times that of the mainstream international commercial drones. It can perform tasks such as mountain search and rescue, long-distance patrol and emergency medical transportation, and adopt a modular design to quickly configure task requirements."Logistics UAV" is specially designed for the delivery needs of remote areas. It integrates high-efficiency motors and modular payload design, has stable flight and strong endurance, and can flexibly respond to various types of material transportation. It has completed a meal logistics demonstration with Japanese drone company Tompla in Kobe Industrial Zone.The "AI UAV Fleet System" combines smart waypoint automatic generation, real-time image transmission and AI fishing cluster identification technology to support multi-machine collaborative operations. It has been applied to ocean-going smart fish search, bridge inspection and smart storage indoor inspection. The fishing search efficiency has been increased by more than three times, effectively replacing high-cost and high-risk helicopter fishing operations.
In terms of key modules, the Industrial Research Institute strengthens the performance of the whole machine and supports multiple field applications."Power module: Motor and electronic speed regulator" adopts innovative motor and blade design, combined with sensorless control technology and air cooling and cooling system. Compared with general power modules on the market, the thrust can be increased up to 10%, extending flight time and increasing load capacity.The "Redundant Flight Controller" has a dual flight control system, just like the second brain in a drone flight mission, which can greatly improve stability and fault tolerance in extreme climates and long-term missions.For miniaturized applications, the "micro flight control board" integrates advanced algorithms and fast response control, and is suitable for high-mobile scenarios such as indoor warehousing, agricultural inspections, and post-disaster search and rescue.
The Industrial Research Institute also joined hands with domestic operators to form a visiting group to extend the exhibition and exchange results, visit many representative furniture companies, such as manufacturers that develop and manufacture commercial drones, and drone software and hardware companies focusing on the development of drone solutions and drone automation platforms, actively negotiate specific cooperation plans, promote the deepening of independent supply chain cooperation between Taiwan and Japan drones, and jointly build a resilient and autonomous Asia-Pacific drone industry network.
