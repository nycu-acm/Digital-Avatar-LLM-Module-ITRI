Service Description
"Artificial Intelligent Chip Design Laboratory" provides system architecture planning and key technologies such as AI architecture design, AI compiler research and development, and AI software and hardware integration. It also designs simulation services and AI chip design services, assisting domestic manufacturers to shorten the manufacturer's design verification process, accelerate the time of market launch of AI chips (the average AI chip development cycle is about 1.8 to 2.5 years), strengthens the industry's AI technology energy, and accelerates the implementation of Taiwan's AI industry and the formation of the industrial chain.
