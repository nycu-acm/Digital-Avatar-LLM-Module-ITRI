Technology introduction
Smart charging and power scheduling technology.
According to ReportLinker research, global charging piles will grow from 2.354 million to 14.62 million from 2022 to 2027.
Features and innovation
In order to assist in the popularization of charging piles, the Industrial Research Institute has developed technologies such as "non-uniform charging" and "charging column leakage remote support".After the car is connected to the charger, it reads the battery information of each car, analyzes the data and the amount of battery capacity of each car, and makes maneuverable current allocation to avoid power off, which can also achieve optimal charging.
Application and benefits
At the same time, for large charging stations, the Industrial Research Institute further developed "electric energy dispatch technology", which can be customized to meet the needs of large charging stations.At present, we have cooperated with domestic charging operators, automobile leaders, and large electromechanical manufacturers to establish new startups, providing one-stop services for charging pile installation, smart charging management and fixed-guarantee maintenance.
