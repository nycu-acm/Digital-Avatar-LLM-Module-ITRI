Technology introduction
Multimodal olfactory imaging diagnostic system provides tomography of olfactory mucosal epidermal tissue.
Patients with current olfactory loss or injury mainly use "olfactory test tubes, odor cards and questionnaire scales" to make subjective diagnosis, and cannot provide objective quantitative data; if only nasal endoscopy is used, it is impossible to effectively identify the status of olfactory and respiratory cells, so it is impossible to provide sufficient diagnostic information.The Institute of Technology Research has pioneered a multimodal olfactory imaging diagnosis system. The endoscopy combines OCT (optical homomodulation tomography) to provide tomography of olfactory mucosal epidermal tissue, which is guided into the nasal cavity through the ENT endoscopy. It uses a soft OCT probe with an outer diameter of only 0.8mm, which can easily enter the upper nose clip, providing clinicians with direct observation of the state of olfactory mucosal cells.
There are also clinical studies that early Parkinson's disease patients, infectious diseases, brain trauma and a series of neurodegenerative diseases all have symptoms of olfactory failure or loss. Having a tool to objectively evaluate the olfactory system will help more diverse clinical studies.
