Academician profile
Academician Lin Shuhong
Chairman Lin founded Changchun Company by starting a business with R&D. In the 1950s, he developed bakery powder and urea glue, providing raw materials for plastic processing and the combo industry, which prompted my country's combo industry to rapidly expand the international market in just a few years and contribute a lot to the country's foreign exchange.Continuously investing in the field of thermosetting plastics with high technical obstacles, and even providing high-purity epoxy resins, semiconductors and panel developers and diluents for use in the semiconductor industry are all indispensable key raw materials in Taiwan's two-triple industry process.Chairman Lin attaches importance to R&D and encourages employees to move towards innovative and forward-looking/future R&D.To implement the unique business philosophy of accelerating the commercialization of R&D products and obtaining IP, Changchun Petrochemical Group has become a leading R&D manufacturer in Taiwan's petrochemical industry under the leadership of Chairman Lin. Chairman Lin is a model operator in leading my country's petrochemical industry to a high-value benchmark enterprise.
