Technology introduction
Precision reducer.
The demand for robotic arms continues to increase with the rapid development of industrial automation, and Harmonic Drive has also become one of the key components of the development of domestic robotic arms industry.The Institute of Technology continues to develop application products with harmonic reducers as the core, such as joint modules and small rotating platforms.
Features and innovation
Integrate innovative dentition type, flexible wheel rigid control, virtual and real integrated system (Cyber​​-Physical System; CPS) online tolerance distribution control and microparticle surface treatment to achieve low transmission error and high operating life, and break through the gap in the domestic automation industry chain.
Application and benefits
This technology has developed a test platform that is in line with the use situation and has passed many industry-wide verifications, such as six-axis robots, semiconductor transfer arms and mobile aids.In response to industry needs, a new startup company was established in 2019.
