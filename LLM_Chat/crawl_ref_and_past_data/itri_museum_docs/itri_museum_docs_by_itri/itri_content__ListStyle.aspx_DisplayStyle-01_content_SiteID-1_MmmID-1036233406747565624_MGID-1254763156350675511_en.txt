The RIN Chiefs' Annual Meeting is hosted by the Institute of Technology Research and Technology. During the meeting, the representatives of the heads of the world's top applied research institutions will be exchanged for cooperation and business opportunities such as scientific and technological cooperation, talent exchange and regional links, and will enhance Taiwan's visibility.
The world's largest applied research organization (RTOs International Network; RIN) Chief Executives' Annual Meeting was held in Taiwan on March 26 and 27, 2024. Associate Su Mengzong of the Institute of Technology Research has served as the chairman of RIN since 2023. This is the first time that RIN has been held in Asia since its establishment.Heads of top international R&D institutions from 12 countries including Finland, France, Germany, Japan, Canada gathered in Taiwan to highlight Taiwan’s profound strength in innovative R&D and gained international recognition.
RIN is one of the world's largest and most important international application R&D institutions linking platforms.This year, RIN Chiefs' Annual Meeting has moved to Asia for the first time and was selected to be held in Taiwan, highlighting Taiwan's scientific and technological strength and R&D energy have been highly recognized internationally. This is an important milestone for Taiwan.At the RIN Chiefs' Annual Meeting on March 26, the discussion focused on global issues, including how to respond to arduous challenges such as climate change, population aging and labor shortage. After the meeting, we jointly advocated that scientific research institutions from all countries work together to establish close partnerships, complement each other's strengths, and accelerate the construction of a "resilient society" through innovative technology and sharing resources, and jointly create a better future world.On March 27, with the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, a total of 16 international cooperation achievements were screened for domestic R&D legal entities, and 15 R&D legal entities in Taiwan were invited to exchange face-to-face with the heads of various international R&D institutions to deepen international cooperation.Su Xieli said that at the meeting on the 27th, he focused on global innovation and R&D strategies and business issues and proposed the "SUPER" concept, where S represents Sustainability, aiming to jointly plan the sustainable development path of international research institutions; U represents Uniqueness, emphasizing the establishment of a clear positioning and characteristics of the research institutions themselves; P represents talent (People), emphasizing cultivating cross-border and cross-domain innovative research talents needed in the contemporary era; E represents entrepreneurial spirit (Entrepreneurship), focusing on research institutions introducing entrepreneurial spirit to maintain vitality and resilience; Finally, R represents resilience, aiming to assist societies in various countries and countries to establish resilience, and hopes to work with domestic and foreign legal partners to develop innovative technologies and solutions to meet different challenges around the world.
EARTO (European Association of Research and Technology Organizations) is the most important association of research and development organizations in Europe.The association represents applied research institutions from 32 European countries and 350 different fields.EARTO's member institutions have a total of more than 150,000 engineering and technical R&D personnel, and cooperate with more than 100,000 companies every year.
RIN (RTOs International Network) is an organization established in 2019 under the jurisdiction of EARTO and is also the largest international application R&D institution link platform in Europe. The Institute of Industrial Research and Research joined EARTO in 2003 and assisted in the creation of RIN.Including: National Institute of Research, Canada (NRC), Japan Institute of Integrated Industrial Technology (AIST), Fraunhofer, Germany, the French Atomic Energy and Alternative Source Committee (CEA), Finnish Institute of Technology (VTT), the Netherlands Organization for Applied Science (TNO), and Tecnalia, Spain.In 2023, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research was awarded the chairman of RIN with its R&D energy and successfully won the right to host the 2024 RIN Chief Annual Meeting.
