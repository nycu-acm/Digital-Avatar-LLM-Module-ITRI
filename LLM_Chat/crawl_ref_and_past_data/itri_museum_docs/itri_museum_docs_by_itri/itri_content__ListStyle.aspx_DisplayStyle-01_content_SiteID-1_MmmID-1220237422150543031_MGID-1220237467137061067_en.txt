Technology introduction
Infrastructure maintenance and operation of high-load commercial drones.
Taiwan has an island-type climate, and power transmission and transformation equipment located in coastal areas and industrial areas is prone to adhesion of particles such as salt, smoke, waste gas, etc., causing insulation damage to the surface of the high-voltage electric tower hindrance, affecting the power supply quality, and may even indirectly lead to power shortage.To solve this problem, in the past, operators often hired helicopters or manpower climbing to use manual cleaning of obstacles, but this work also faced the problem of shortage of work due to the trend of population aging and the dangerous, hard and dirty working environment.
The Institute of Technology has developed the "Infrastructure Maintenance and Operation High Load Commercial UAV", and provides a close-range servo water anchor function and safety solution within two meters for the needs of precision air operations such as towers and fan blade maintenance. It will cooperate with the operators and conduct on-site verification to co-create new business models in response to the business opportunities of new energy infrastructure in the next 20 years.
Application and benefits
One of the high-paying long-range drones was won the multi-rotor group championship in the 2020 Middle East Drone International Competition (DXC2020 Final Prize), and is the only selected R&D institution in Asia.
