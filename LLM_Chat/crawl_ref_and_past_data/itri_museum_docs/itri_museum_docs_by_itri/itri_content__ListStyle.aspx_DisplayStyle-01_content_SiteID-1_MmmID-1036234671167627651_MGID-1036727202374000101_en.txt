Jiao Youjun Academician
Academician Jiao Youjun is the chairman of Huabang Electronics and also serves as a director of Xintang Technology. For more than 30 years, he has actively promoted the technological development of the semiconductor industry and various product innovations, and has been committed to improving the memory and logic ICs with its own development capabilities. He has successfully led Huabang Electronics to break through the red ocean and gained a unique position in the global semiconductor industry.At the same time, New Tang Technology is committed to developing analog/mixed signals, microcontrollers and computer cloud-related IC products, which are an indispensable part of the semiconductor production process.
In 1987, Chairman Jiao took on the important task of Chairman Huabang Electronics. In the early stage of Huabang Electronics, he introduced technology from Toshiba Semiconductor in Japan and cooperated with the Industrial Research Institute for product and technology development. Starting from the chips of wired telephones, he successively developed PC peripheral application chips, consumer electronic chips and other products, gradually becoming Taiwan's leading integrated component manufacturer, and serving as the pioneer of the Taiwan semiconductor industry in its infancy.
Chairman Jiao is brave enough to break through the framework and be good at agile innovation. In the era of small profit competition, he led Huabang Electronics and New Tang Technology to strengthen core competitiveness and implement and create value, and is the only DRAM manufacturer in Taiwan with independent development of process technology capabilities.In 2007, Huabang Electronics launched the Quad SPI sequential flash memory, breaking the high cost of traditional sequential flash memory and bringing epoch-making innovation to the industry.It is now the fifth largest DRAM and the largest SPI NOR Flash supplier in the world, and one-third of the world's electronic products have HuabangDi's memory.
In addition to business operations, Chairman Jiao has been concerned about Taiwan's motor and electronics industry competitiveness for a long time.He has served as the chairman of the Taiwan Electrical and Electronics Industry Association and consultant of the Industrial Research Institute.He focuses on forward-looking innovation and research and development technology, advocates the concept of industrial environmental risk assessment, and actively promotes the expansion of the international market of Taiwan's motor electronic products, and is an important driving force for the development of my country's motor electronics industry.
