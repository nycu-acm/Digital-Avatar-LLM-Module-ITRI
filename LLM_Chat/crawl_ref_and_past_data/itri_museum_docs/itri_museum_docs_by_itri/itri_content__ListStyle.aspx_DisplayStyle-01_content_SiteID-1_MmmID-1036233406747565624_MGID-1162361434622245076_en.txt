In order to accelerate the cooperation between the Indian new venture team and Taiwan's industrial and new ventures, under the witness of Huberty (top left), Deputy Director of the Small and Medium Enterprises Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs (top left), and Rishikesh Swaminathan (top right), CEO of the Industrial Service Center of the Industrial Research Institute Liu Jiaming (bottom left), and CEO of the Invest India (bottom right), signed a memorandum of cooperation (MOU) to build a new venture exchange platform for India and Taiwan and promote exchanges and cooperation between the two sides' new venture ecosystems.
New Southbound cooperation will bring good news again!Invest India has jointly created a Taiwan-India innovative exchange platform with Taiwan for the first time.Under the witness of Huberty, Deputy Director of the Small and Medium Enterprises Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, and Rishikesh Swaminathan, Deputy Executive Director of the Indian Taipei Association (ITA), the Institute of Technology and Invest India signed a Memorandum of Cooperation (MOU) on October 26, 2011 to promote exchanges and cooperation between the two sides' new innovation ecosystems and build the India-Taiwan Startup Connect Hub. Taiwanese new entrepreneurs can enter the Indian market with 1.35 billion customers through this platform, and at the same time connect with the Indian new innovation energy that has risen rapidly in recent years, drive industrial innovation transformation, and incubate more potential enterprises.
According to statistics from the research company Preqin cited by international financial media Bloomberg, in July this year, India received a global investment of US$7.9 billion, which not only surpassed China for the first time in 2013, but the amount was nearly twice as high.At the same time, India also successfully created 14 new unicorns in the first half of this year, all showing India's abundant new energy.Invest India said: "This MOU will become an opportunity for India-Taiwanese-Taiwanese-New Industry Cooperation, and it is also an important first step in India and Taiwan becoming a partnership in all industries, and strengthening the partnership between "Taiwan Hardware and Indian Software" and improving the cooperation benefits. India is a country that cultivates unicorn companies and actively promotes new industries, with more than 80,000 new startups, while Taiwan is a global leader in innovation and competitiveness. We really want Taiwan's startups to develop this market with 1.35 billion customers. Supply chain resilience is a buzzword under the new normal, with India having 64 million micro and small and medium-sized enterprises, which we are sure will provide excellent supply chain resilience for Taiwanese startups to face the ever-changing global market. "
Bilateral trade between Taiwan and India has grown rapidly in recent years.In the past two years, the TAcc+ international training courses sponsored by the Small and Medium Enterprises Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs have registered in India.More than 65 new startups have been registered. For two consecutive years, Indian teams have been selected for this international training course and have received the opportunity to tutor and come to Taiwan.By handling international training courses, the Taiwan New Innovation Team has communicated and cooperated with the local Indian Institute of Technology, Indian Software Association, and Bangalore’s accelerator and cultivation institutions in advance, and gradually established a tacit understanding of cooperation and exchanges between Taiwan and India New Innovation.Liu Jiaming, CEO of the Industrial Service Center of the Industrial Research Institute, said that the Industrial Research Institute actively promotes the "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" to systematically create forward-looking innovative industrial technology, and on this basis, through technological innovation and R&D results, add value to new products and services to spread industrial benefits.At the same time, the Industrial Research Institute continues to gather all-round resources such as technology, talents, funds, market analysis and professional guidance to maximize the success rate of innovation.Through the signing of the MOU with the Invest India, this time, it will open a fast communication channel for both sides to create new innovations, allowing Taiwan’s new venture team to accelerate integration and expand the Indian market, and at the same time, it will also make Taiwan an important partner for the Indian new venture team to enter the Asian market.
