Technology introduction
Smart customer service robot.
Smart customer service robots use artificial intelligence Q&A and dialogue technology to integrate deep learning and natural language processing, and develop and apply it to corporate customer service. In addition to improving service efficiency and maintaining customer service quality, they can also create a new customer service experience.
Features and innovation
In the past, corporate customer service mainly focused on real customer service, because of the large customer service traffic and insufficient manual efficiency, and the high cost of real customer service, frequent customer service specialists and lack of experience in new employees, resulting in poor customer service experience and user problems are always repeated, so customer service robots are urgently needed to improve the current situation.According to Gartner's forecast, with the combination of technology and artificial intelligence, by 2020, corporate organizations will successfully contact 85% of their customers through chatbots.
By introducing smart customer service robots through artificial intelligence Q&A and dialogue technology, using multiple robots to run at the same time and providing different services, we can solve the problems currently encountered in the industry.After the customer service robot handles 80% of the problems, only a small number of customer problems that cannot be solved by the customer service robot will be left, and then handed over to real customer service.
This technology is based on deep transfer learning technology as the core, including question-Answering technology, Nature language Understanding, Dialogue Management technology, breaking through the traditional knowledge base to establish rules, using artificial intelligence technology to train and analyze, and establish question-and-answer and dialogue models, which can accurately identify user problems and intentions, achieve strengthening marketing and enhancing the industrial competitiveness of customer service.
The smart customer service robot of the Institute of Technology Research Institute has been actually used in various industries such as finance, logistics, retail and commerce.In addition to quickly importing customer service services in different fields to improve service efficiency and maintain customer service quality, it can also reduce construction costs, helping operators quickly enter different fields and increase market share.
Application and benefits
It covers application fields with a large number of customer service Q&A needs, such as e-commerce customer service departments, financial customer service, government 1999, etc.
