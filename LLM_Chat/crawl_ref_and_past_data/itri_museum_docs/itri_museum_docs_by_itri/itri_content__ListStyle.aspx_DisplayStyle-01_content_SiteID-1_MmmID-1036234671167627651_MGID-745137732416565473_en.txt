Academician Lu Mingguang
Academician Lu Mingguang is the chairman and CEO of Sino-US Silicon Crystal and Pengcheng Technology. He has served as Texas Instruments, Guangbao Group, Sino-US Silicon Crystal and founded Pengcheng Technology. He has led more than ten mergers and acquisitions in his career, re-transformed the company, successfully turned losses into profits, and achieved great success in corporate management. He has been known as the "M&A King".
During his tenure as Chairman Lu, Yu Guangbao Group, he successfully rectified Xuli Rubber, a subsidiary of Guangbao. A year and a half later, Xuli Rubber became the most profitable rubber factory in the country.Later, he was ordered to rectify the group businesses covering rubber, connectors, semiconductors and electronic components, which can often turn the world around in a short period of time, turn losses into profits, and even sell them to foreign companies successfully.
Because of his outstanding business capabilities, Chairman Lu was invited to serve as the chairman of Sino-US Silicon Crystal and participated in the operation since Guangbao Group retired. During this period, he launched six cross-border mergers and acquisitions, and his turnover increased by more than 8 times within ten years. China-US Silicon Crystal grew from a local enterprise that originally had only 3-inch semiconductor factories to a third largest semiconductor silicon wafer factory in the world covering Taiwan, China, the United States, Japan and Europe, with an 8-inch monthly production capacity of 1 million pieces and a 12-inch monthly production capacity of 750,000 pieces.
Chairman Lu has served in the semiconductor industry for more than 40 years, from foreign companies to local companies, from corporate managers to company leaders, witnessing the booming development of Taiwan's electronics industry and participating in Taiwan's economic miracle. He attaches importance to talents and enjoys surplus with his employees. At the same time, he has built R&D strength, and R&D funds account for 3% to 6% of revenue for a long time. He also led Sino-US Silicon Crystal and Pengcheng Technology to both ranked among the top 5% of the "Company Governance Evaluation" on the Stock Exchange.
