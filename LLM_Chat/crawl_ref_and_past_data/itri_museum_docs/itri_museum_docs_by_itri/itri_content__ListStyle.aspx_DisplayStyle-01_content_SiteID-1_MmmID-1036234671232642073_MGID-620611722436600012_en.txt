102 Annual "Academician of the Institute of Technology" Conference
Group photo of the Second Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (102 years)
The academician meeting of the Institute of Technology Research was held and chaired by Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of Zhirong Group, in Taipei Science and Technology Building, and discussed the two major themes of "Taiwan's industrial structure transformation: How to go from followers to innovators" and "Outlook of Taiwan's entrepreneurship and venture capital environment". The participants included: Zhang Zhongmou, founder and chairman of Taiwan's integrated circuit manufacturing Co., Ltd., Hu Dinghua, chairman of Jianbang Venture Capital Co., Ltd., Zheng Chonghua, founder and honorary chairman of Delta Electronics, Lin Shuhong, chairman of Changchun Petrochemical, Li Bingjie, chairman of Jingyuan Optoelectronics Technology Co., Ltd., Lin Baili, chairman of Guangda Group, Lu Zhiyuan, general manager of Wanghong Electronics Co., Ltd./founder and chairman of Xinquan Technology Co., Ltd., Chen Xingshi, president of Ronggang Group/chairman of Ronggang Materials Technology Co., Ltd., and Lin Benjian, deputy general manager of research and development of Taiwan integrated circuit manufacturing Co., Ltd.
Meeting conclusion focus
Promote the transformation of Taiwan's industrial structure from followers to innovators.
The government and the Industrial Research Institute should strengthen the establishment of new industries, derivative companies, and promote industrial upgrading as the goal, focus on future forward-looking needs, lead resource reset with a clear vision, and lay out the global market.
Establish a cross-field cooperation platform to cooperate with key domestic and foreign partners to explore target markets.
Taiwan should establish its own R&D integration and industrial integration model with an open mind, integrate the industry and academic circles, and invest in the next generation of industrial technology and market development to create new industries with high added value.
Improve the venture capital environment in Taiwan and make Taiwan a "high value-added innovation and entrepreneurship center."
