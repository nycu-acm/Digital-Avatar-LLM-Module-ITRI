Chen Xingshi Academician
Ronggang Materials Technology Company, founded by President Chen Xingshi, is the only professional special alloy manufacturing company in Taiwan that has complete production processes from steelmaking, forging, rolling, heat treatment to finishing. It has a wide range of products and uses tool machines, power plants and even medical and national defense industries.It is also one of the top ten special steel suppliers in the world. Just two years after Rong Gang obtained the certification of the American Qiyi Company, President Chen won the "Fastest Entry of the Qiyi Excellent Supplier Award". Through the certification of the Qiyi Company, Rong Gang successfully entered the supply chain of the international aerospace field, making its revenue exceed 10 billion yuan. He has successively passed the certification of customers of Qiyi Company (GE), Germany's Siemens (SIEMENS), Japan's Mitsubishi (MITSUBISHI), and the United States Boeing (BOEING), leading Rong Gang to enter the energy and biomedical fields and become the leader of Taiwan's special steel and special alloys.
President Chen promotes supply chain organization with the One Stop Shopping service concept, structures the connection of complete high-end materials manufacturing, logistics, precision processing and special application alliances. At the same time, he has excellent results in promoting the application of special alloy materials and the internationalization of brands. He has won many recognitions such as the National Quality Award and the outstanding new supplier of American strange power systems. He has led Ronggang Group to globalization and contributes outstandingly to promoting Taiwan's overall economic growth and the industrial development of the materials field.
