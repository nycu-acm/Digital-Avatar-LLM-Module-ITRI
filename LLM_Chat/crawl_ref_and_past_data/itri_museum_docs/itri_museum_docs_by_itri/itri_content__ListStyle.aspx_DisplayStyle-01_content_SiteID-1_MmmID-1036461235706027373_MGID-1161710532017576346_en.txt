Introduction
The Hi-CHIP Alliance founding conference on August 30, 2023.
The alliance was formerly the Advanced Stacking Systems and Application R&D Alliance (Ad-STAC).In order to help the industry master the key to advanced semiconductor processes and high chip integration capabilities, the Industrial Research Institute and the Industry jointly established the Heterogeneous Integration and Chiplet System Package Alliance (Hi-CHIP) on August 30, 2023, which is the first to create a complete platform for integrating domestic and foreign semiconductor industry into domestic and foreign semiconductor industry from packaging design, testing and verification to small-scale production services. With the localized supply chain and the international integration service model, heterogeneous integration technology leads the semiconductor industry to the next peak of growth and seizes international supply chain business opportunities.
Many years ago, the Institute of Technology has invested in the research and development of related process technology and material equipment upgrades, strengthened the development of heterogeneous integration technology, and has achieved outstanding results in the development of fan-out wafer-level packaging (Fan-Out Wafer Level Packaging (FOWLP), 2.5 and 3D wafers, heterogeneous integrated carrier board architecture (EIC) and programmable packaging architecture (Programmable Package). Now we have joined hands with domestic and foreign semiconductor manufacturers to establish a Hi-CHIP alliance to form an advanced packaging process production line, and use packaging Shuttle Service to provide AIoT system application platform and one-stop service.
Currently, the alliance members include heavyweight semiconductor industry players such as Huan Electric, Ruifeng Semiconductor, Xinxing Electronics, Kulicke & Soffa, EVG, DuPont, Brewer Science, Xinyun Enterprise, etc., and hope to reach 40 members in the future, connect the upstream and downstream to create customized acceptance models and core process capabilities, and master new impetus for semiconductors to move forward in the future.
Alliance Mission
Establish a common platform technology to accelerate product design and joint development.
Participate in relevant foreign organizations to grasp the world's development trends.
Implement localization of the supply chain and promote new industrial operation models.
Assist domestic equipment and material manufacturers in cross-border cooperation and technology upgrades.
