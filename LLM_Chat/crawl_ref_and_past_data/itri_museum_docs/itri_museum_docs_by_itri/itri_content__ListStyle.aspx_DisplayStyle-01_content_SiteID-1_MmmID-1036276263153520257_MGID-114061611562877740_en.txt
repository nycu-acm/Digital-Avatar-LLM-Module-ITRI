Date: 2025/06/16
The Institute of Technology held the "2025 Global Science and Technology Industry Competition Trends and Challenges Series Seminar", focusing on three major themes: semiconductor strategic positioning, electronic components business opportunities and quantum technology supply chain layout.
At the critical moment of rapid global technological transformation and supply chain restructuring, the Institute of Industrial Research recently held the "2025 Global Science and Technology Industry Competition Trends and Challenges Series Seminar", attracting nearly 500 academic and research experts and operators to participate.The seminar focused on three major themes: semiconductor strategic positioning, electronic components business opportunities and quantum technology supply chain layout, and analyzed the opportunities and challenges of Taiwan under global industrial changes.The Institute of Technology Research pointed out that in the face of changes in tariff policies, the heating of geopolitics and the intensified technological competition, Taiwan must strengthen its industrial resilience, actively plan forward-looking technology, and grasp the next wave of global development dominance.
AI applications are accelerating their implementation, and chip and hardware requirements are fully launched
In 2025, CES and COMPUTEX will be themed "Dive In" and "AI NEXT" respectively, indicating that AI artificial intelligence technology is fully integrated into life applications and drives chip and terminal device innovation.At the same time, generative AI technology lowers the threshold for computing power, and open source and lightweight models allow smartphones, PCs and other devices to be equipped with AI, driving the demand for Taiwan’s IC design industry to rise. The Institute of Technology and Research estimates that Taiwan’s IC design industry output value will grow by 13.9% in 2025 compared with the previous year.The Institute of Technology Research pointed out that the popularity of edge AI not only consolidates the advantages of Taiwan chip industry, but also creates more innovative applications and services, allowing opportunities for chips and hardware to truly blossom everywhere.
CPO and advanced packaging help heat up the high-speed computing market
In response to the rapid expansion of AI model scale and the high-speed computing needs of data centers, co-packaging optics (CPO) technology has become the focus of the industry.This technology integrates the optical engine with the wafer package, significantly reducing transmission losses.The Institute of Technology estimates that with the introduction of silicon photon exchanges into mass production, the CPO market size will grow by more than 4 times by 2029 compared with 2024, reaching US$47.5 million.Corresponding to heterogeneous integration needs, advanced packaging technologies such as 2.5D/3D packaging and TSV will accelerate the application, and the global advanced packaging market will also grow steadily with an average annual average of 10.8%.It is estimated that the global advanced packaging market will reach US$67.19 billion in 2029. Taiwan has the advantages of integrating wafer process, packaging and silicon photons, and has the opportunity to become the core base for CPO's global technology implementation in the future.
Strong growth of semiconductors, policy variables become challenges
AI applications have pushed up the demand for terminal upgrades and computing, driving semiconductor technology and the market to reach a new peak.The Institute of Technology estimates that the global semiconductor market will reach US$700.9 billion in 2025, with an annual growth of 11.2%; in Taiwan, AI-related applications will boost the utilization rate of IC manufacturing and packaging and testing capacity, and the annual output value is estimated to reach NT$6.331.3 billion, with an annual growth of 19.1%, showing strong momentum.However, in the face of rising geopoliticals and pressures for global supply chain restructuring, policy risks cannot be ignored.After taking office, US President Trump has promoted a new round of tariff policies and related measures, which has brought high uncertainty to the international trade situation and supply chain stability.At the same time, the global semiconductor industry is accelerating its regionalization and multi-point allocation development, and countries continue to strengthen their local manufacturing capabilities through policies.The Institute of Technology and Research recommends that Taiwanese semiconductor industry should pay close attention to the impact of policy changes and adjust its response strategies in a timely manner.
Display and sensing technology innovation drives new application scenarios
Empowered by AI, the market potential of smart glasses is promising.The Institute of Technology pointed out that LEDoS (LED on Silicon) with the advantages of high brightness and miniaturization is most suitable for the display needs of smart glasses, and is estimated to have a market penetration rate of 57.4% in 2029. If the conditions for replacing mobile phones in the future are met, the market size is expected to reach more than US$40 billion compared with the current mobile phone panels.It is recommended that Taiwan should make good use of the advantages of Micro LED and semiconductor process, lay out LEDoS technology in advance, and strive for opportunities to upgrade smart displays.
At the same time, MEMS sensors have a stable growth momentum due to the diversified application scenarios.High-value-added applications such as automotive electronics, AI Sensing, and smart medical care have driven up market demand. The Institute of Technology estimates that the annual output value of Taiwan sensors will reach NT$223.2 billion in 2025, an annual increase of about 2.5%.Especially in high value-added fields such as automotive electronics, smart medical care and AI Sensing, MEMS sensors have become key components due to their small size, low power consumption and high sensitivity.In the future, with the trend of integration and modular design, a new niche will be created for Taiwan’s sensor industry.
Component demand has increased steadily and must be cautious in responding to policy interference
In terms of passive components, under the influence of the new round of US tariff policies, global supply chains continue to adjust.In the short term, in order to respond to uncertainty, the industry has prepared stocks in advance, and the strong shipment of AI servers has driven the output value of Taiwan's passive components in the first half of 2025 to NT$127.6 billion, an annual increase of 9.6%; the annual output value is expected to reach NT$250.7 billion, an annual increase of 4.2%.However, the Industrial Research Institute reminds that it is necessary to carefully observe whether the stocking effect causes a decline in demand in the second half of the year, and to deal with market fluctuations caused by external political and economic changes.
The PCB industry is driven by AI servers and satellite communications, and continues to play the main driving force for the growth of Taiwan's PCB industry in 2025. It is estimated that the scale of Taiwan's PCB industry will reach NT$866.1 billion in 2025, a growth of 6.0%.However, with the impact of tariff policies, operators ship in advance, which may reduce subsequent market demand and should be cautious in responding to changes in rhythm.
Overall, the electronic components industry is at a critical moment when emerging demand and global turmoil are intertwined in 2025. Applications such as AI, electric vehicles, and the Internet of Things are rapidly expanding, driving a huge demand for high-efficiency and high-reliability components.However, the uncertainty of the international political and economic environment also tests the operator's ability to adapt.
The rise of quantum technology Taiwan should seize the dominance of application and ecological
In the global wave of rapid development of quantum technology, the seminar focused on quantum computers, components and application scenarios on the second day.With the fierce global technological competition, quantum technology is reshaping the future industrial structure with unprecedented potential.Global quantum technology is expected to generate US$850 billion in economic value by 2040, and the amount of new investment in 2024 has reached US$8.5 billion, indicating its market growth potential.Among them, quantum hardware technologies such as ion traps have shown practical potential in the fields of finance, new materials, etc., and quantum control circuit technology will also become the key to industrialization.The Institute of Technology and Research calls on Taiwan to combine the advantages of semiconductors and ICT to develop niche applications such as hybrid quantum computing, low-temperature control components and encrypted communications, and actively promote international cooperation and talent cultivation, accelerate the construction of an on-site quantum technology innovation ecosystem, and seize the key position in future global scientific and technological competition.
