The Industrial Research Institute won the top 100 innovation institutions awards in the world for six consecutive times
Organize/Editor Department
In the 2022 Top 100 Innovation Institutions Awards, a total of 9 institutions in Taiwan have been listed.Among them, the Industrial Research Institute is the only institution in Taiwan that has won six awards for five consecutive years, and is also the only three award-winning research institutions in the world.
Taiwan’s innovative technology research and development energy and smart property rights have shined internationally again!In 2022, a total of 9 institutions in Taiwan won the "Global 100 Innovation Institutions Award" in Clarivate, and its outstanding performance became the best in Asian countries.With the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Industrial Research Institute won six awards with its excellent intellectual and financial layout. In addition to being the ever-winning army with the most awards in Taiwan, it is also the only three in the world that have won the top 100 innovative R&D institutions.
Since 2012, Clarivate, an internationally renowned research institution, has actively investigated, analyzed and published reports on the world's top 100 innovative institutions every year, evaluating the world's most innovative enterprises and institutions.This year's "2022 Global Top 100 Innovation Institution Awards" have 9 institutions in Taiwan, including the 6-time winners of Industrial Research Institute, Hon Hai, which has won 5 times, MediaTek and Quanta, which have won 4 times, TSMC, which has won 2 times, and AUO Optoelectronics, Delta Electronics, Wistron Zip and Realtek Semiconductor, which have been selected for the first time.The number of players listed in the list is second only to Japan and the United States, and is tied for the third place in the world with Germany.
The selection criteria have become strict, it is not easy to be on the list this year
Clarivian said that the in-depth selection of various indicators this year is stricter than in previous years, except for the "influence" indicator combined with technical fields, countries and other conditions; in previous years, the "globalization" indicator originally refers to China, the United States, Japan and Europe, and has expanded to the world this year;this year, it also added "technical uniqueness" as the selection indicator for the first time, focusing on evaluating whether each patent applied for by an institution is unique, whether the technical dimensions have the same trajectory as other institutions, etc.
Among the top 100 innovation institutions in the world in 2022, the Industrial Research Institute ranked in the top 50, and performed particularly well in five major patent indicators: technological uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity.It set a record of winning the sixth time for five consecutive years. It is the institution with the most awards in Taiwan and one of the only three research institutions in the world. It is the first of the awards won by the Asia-Pacific R&D institutions.
Clarivian pointed out that the Institute of Technology is a first-class class among the top 100 innovative institutions in the world.In recent years, it has actively strengthened its international layout and demonstrated its achievements, making patents ranked among the top 25 in the world in globalization and successful footprints, fully demonstrating the hard power of cross-technical fields that combine depth and breadth.Behind such outstanding achievements is the result of the cooperation of 15 institutes and centers of the Institute of Technology and Research Institute with the cooperation of teams in different technical fields.
Domestic patent resource library assists Taiwanese factories to seize business opportunities
"Smart property rights have always been the key to the competition of Taiwan's industry," said Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research.The Institute of Technology has assisted Taiwan's industry in achieving competitive advantages in the international market from two major aspects. First, we cooperate with industry, academia and research to form a "IP Bank" (IP Bank) to build a patent pool including 5G, electric vehicles and net zero carbon emission related to it, create a patent resource library for domestic industries, and combine domestic intelligence experts and AI analysis systems to become the best support team for domestic manufacturers to win the International Cup.
Second, link the capital market and the technology market, join hands with the "Small and Medium Enterprises Credit Guarantee Fund" and 26 banks to expand the promotion of intangible asset evaluation and financing to assist enterprises in solving technical and financial problems at the same time.In the past two years, it has assisted nearly 50 Chinese enterprises in applying for financing from financial institutions with high-quality patents and technologies, and the accumulated financing amount has reached over NT$100 million. This not only enables the company to successfully obtain funds, but also establishes a high-quality image of the manufacturer's high-tech content, which will help attract venture capital attention, and provide manufacturer's technology and funds to assist the company in growing steadily.
With the support of the Ministry of Economic Research Special Project, the Institute of Industrial Research actively draws up the "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint", deepens cross-field forward-looking technology research and development, and deepens global patent layout, assists domestic enterprises to improve their competitiveness, and also targets new trends, assists the industry in laying out important patents, improves the intellectual finance protection network and builds an industrial ecological chain.For example, we cooperate with manufacturers such as Pegatron United Technology to provide integrated solution technologies and patents such as rapid deployment of 5G private network small base stations and optimize performance; we also work with Renbao Computer, Xiuchuan Medical System and Daming Robot to jointly develop the first "5G long-distance ultrasonic technology" in the country through patented technologies such as mobile edge computing, providing long-distance ultrasonic detection consultation services to seize the long-distance medical market.
In terms of next-generation semiconductor applications, the Industrial Research Institute has cooperated with automotive and electrical industry operator Zhengmei and health sports industry operator Huanbo Technology to develop soft hybrid electronic technology, actively introduce automotive electronics and biotechnology medical fields, including solving the problem of pattern deformation of automotive center console lines, and creating the world's first soft composite smart knee pads.
In terms of innovative energy technology and systems, solar photoelectric modules must withstand the rigorous test of outdoor natural environment, and related testing becomes more important. The Industrial Research Institute and Tianxing Energy develop an action-oriented solar photoelectric test vehicle, including module defect detection, power generation power testing, module fragmentation or defects and module insulation measurement, through exclusive patented technology, improve the flexibility and mobility of test verification, reduce the time and risks of module transportation, assist enterprises in checking the return on investment and improving quality, and helping industrial value leap.
Top innovation capabilities Taiwan is a country of science and technology
Clarivate analysis has extended the list to "the world's top 2,000 innovative institutions", with nearly 100 institutions in Taiwan, and nearly 40 more than 500 institutions in the top 500; in the top 500 electronics and computer fields and the top 500 semiconductor fields, the number of Taiwanese institutions entering the list reaches one-third, which shows that Taiwan is a technology country.
Chen Yiling, Secretary-General of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, also said that Taiwan’s number of award-winning institutions hit a new high this year, leading the UK, France and other countries, ranking third in the world, demonstrating Taiwan’s competitiveness in innovative R&D, and echoing the 2021 World Competitiveness Report of the Lausanne School of Management in Switzerland. Taiwan ranks among the top three in the world in many R&D related evaluations. Its excellent creativity and new thinking are obvious to all internationally.
In the future, the Industrial Research Institute will continue to deepen its efforts in the field of smart property rights, use the patent ecosystem operation strategy, master international patent layout, and assist Taiwanese manufacturers in creating value and moving towards a new blue ocean market through flexible and diverse methods such as patent financing, patent valuation, and patent pools.
