Clarification Note
Crack down on false advertising and protect consumer rights. Three major actions of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research to defend justice and solemnly warn infringement manufacturers

Date: 2022/03/03
Don't be fooled!Recently, some people who are interested in using the celebrity effect of the Institute of Technology Research and tamper with their photos, and in the form of false advertising, they claimed to cooperate with the Institute of Technology Research and sell medical products of unknown origin such as "acupoint moxibustion eye treatment instruments" and "magnetic therapy and glucose-lowering instruments".This illegal act not only defrauds consumers, but also violates relevant provisions such as my country's Criminal Law, Fair Trading Law, and Civil Law, but also infringes on the relevant rights and interests of the Institute of Industrial Research.The Institute of Industrial Research and Research held a press conference on the 3rd to clarify false advertisements, solemnly stated that it has taken three major actions to defend justice, including notifying the source to block fraudulent advertisements, taking legal actions to report to the police, and issuing clarification announcements to avoid continued harm to consumers.The Institute of Technology will never lenient loans in the face of illegal behavior and reminds consumers not to be deceived to protect their own rights and interests.
Starting from November 2021, the Institute of Industrial Research has found that member accounts have linked "one-page advertising" on Facebook pages, falsely claiming its technical cooperation relationship with the Institute of Industrial Research, and using photos such as the Chairman, Dean, and Academicians of the Institute of Industrial Research to sell medical products from unknown sources. After investigation, it was found that the unscrupulous businessman registered and obtained website addresses from US and mainland China through "one-page advertising" method, provided consumers to link "one-page advertising" websites and ordered fraudulent products; and exposed promotion through Facebook accounts to attract more consumers, and connected to the websites of "one-page advertising" and ordered fraudulent products.
Wang Pengyu, the legal director of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, pointed out that the above-mentioned advertising system arbitrarily stolen and edited the activity photos and trademarks of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, and deceived consumers by fake and real. In addition to infringing on the copyright and trademark rights of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, it also infringes on the portrait rights of the chairman, president and academicians of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute in the photos, and constitutes fraud in consumers. His behavior has violated my country's Criminal Law, Fair Trading Law, and Civil Law and other relevant provisions.Wang Pengyu said that the Institute of Technology has taken three major actions to defend justice, solemnly warns unscrupulous manufacturers, and protects consumers' rights and interests: 1. Notify the source (domain company, Facebook) to block fraudulent advertisements, 2. Take legal action to report to the District Prosecutor's Office, the Bureau of Investigation and the Second Security Corps, and 3. Issuing clarification announcements to avoid consumers' continued harm.In addition to querying the official website of the Institute of Technology and Research, people can also contact the customer service dedicated line: 0800-45-8899 for verification, and at the same time, they can also notify the Police Department 165 National Anti-Fraud Network to avoid being deceived.
Chen Yiming (Wanguo Law Firm), a lawyer appointed by the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that in recent years, "one-page advertising" fraud has been rampant. This time, those who are interested have blatantly used the Institute of Industrial Research Institute's activities, trademarks and photos of related personnel to confuse the public's audio-visual. The method is extremely bad and has a huge impact on the reputation of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute.In order to protect the rights and interests of the Institute of Technology and the people, the Institute of Technology has commissioned professional lawyers to assist and has taken all necessary measures to investigate the above-mentioned "one-page advertising" fraud, and will never be tolerated.
The Industrial Research Institute is a national research institution. Its R&D results are internationally renowned, with increasing popularity and influence. It has not only won the recognition of the top 100 innovative institutions in the world 6 times, but also won the CES Innovation Award, the Top 100 Global Science and Technology R&D Award, the Edison Award, etc. Its innovation and R&D capabilities have a strong international influence. In the past two years, it has also protected medical staff and the public with a number of epidemic prevention technologies such as the national mask team and the positive pressure inspection booth.
Unscrupulous businessmen use the reputation of a national research institution to commit fraud through "one-page advertising" method. Their false statements that damage the reputation of the Industrial Research Institute not only damage the reputation of the Industrial Research Institute, but also harm the rights and interests of consumers.The Institute of Industrial Research and Research has reiterated that the goods closed in the case are not advertisements of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research and Products are not research and development and production of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research and Research. We hereby call on all walks of life to verify products of unknown origins with consumer welfare groups or advertising agencies to avoid being deceived, stop rumors and falsehoods, curb unhealthy trends, safeguard their own rights and interests, and jointly defend justice.
