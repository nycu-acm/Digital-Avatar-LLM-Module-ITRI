Carbon reduction benefits
Taking each hectare of thorny bamboo forest as an example, the total amount of total bamboo in triennial bamboo forest is about 50 tons. Therefore, 500 metric tons of total bamboo needs to be cut down 10 hectares of bamboo forest, which can promote the renewal of 10 hectares of bamboo forest every year.The amount of carbon that can be stored per hectare of bamboo forest is about 51.02 metric tons per hectare. Therefore, the carbon sequestration of 10 hectares of bamboo forest is 510 metric tons, which can reduce carbon by 59.4 metric tons per year.
