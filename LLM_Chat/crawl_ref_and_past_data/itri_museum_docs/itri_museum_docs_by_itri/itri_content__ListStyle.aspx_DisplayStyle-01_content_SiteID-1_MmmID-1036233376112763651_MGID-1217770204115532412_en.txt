Technology introduction
Smart navigation and cleaning robot.
In recent years, the concept of infectious disease prevention has gradually become popular.Among them, in medical hospitals, stations, airports and other public places, the virus is the most likely to remain and requires frequent large-scale environmental cleaning and disinfection. In addition to increasing the workload of workers, it may also increase the risk of infection.
Features and innovation
The "smart navigation and cleaning robot" developed by the Institute of Technology Research does not require setting up tracks or marks. The best path can be found through smart calculations. It has the function of autonomous obstacle avoidance, which can easily complete large-scale cleaning tasks, save 50% of the workload of cleaning personnel, increase efficiency by two to three times, and reduce contact between people, greatly reducing the chances of front-line personnel infected and spreading the virus.
Application and benefits
Field tests have been conducted at the Hsinchu Branch of China University of Medicine and Taoyuan International Airport.
