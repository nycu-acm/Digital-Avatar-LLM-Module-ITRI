Academician profile
Academician Lin Benjian
Deputy General Manager Lin Benjian is the deputy general manager of research and development of Taiwan Instrumental Circuit Manufacturing Co., Ltd., and has devoted his life to semiconductor process technology.His forward-looking R&D has continuously rewrites semiconductor history and assisted TSMC in establishing Taiwan's complete semiconductor industry system.Deputy General Manager Lin has worked at IBM Watson Laboratory for 22 years, and led the R&D team to create many world-first technologies, including 1 micron, 0.75 micron, and 0.5 micron lithography technology.After joining TSMC, it developed the 193-nanometer Immersion microfilm technology in 2002, allowing TSMC to jump to three technological generations in one go and become the world's leader. This technology has made great contributions to the global semiconductor industry and TSMC's advanced processes below 65 nm.
Since then, even when the world's semiconductor R&D team focused on 157 nanometer wavelength technology, Deputy General Manager Lin used 193 nanometer wavelength light to further transmit water intermediary and could shorten it to 134 nanometers.The paper was published, and it also made TSMC dominate the industry for the first time, allowing many industries including IBM that have ordered 157 dry machines to return orders and follow up with TSMC.At the same time, the world's shortest wavelength light, first developed by Deputy General Manager Lin and named Deep UV, has been in the industry for nearly 30 years and is still used by many semiconductor factories.
Deputy General Manager Lin has written many records for the semiconductor industry with his technological innovation and invention and decades of persistence. His outstanding R&D achievements have made him awarded many honors including NAE Member, IEEE Fellow, SPIE Fellow and other honors, which not only has a huge and far-reaching impact on TSMC, but also Taiwan’s semiconductor industry.
