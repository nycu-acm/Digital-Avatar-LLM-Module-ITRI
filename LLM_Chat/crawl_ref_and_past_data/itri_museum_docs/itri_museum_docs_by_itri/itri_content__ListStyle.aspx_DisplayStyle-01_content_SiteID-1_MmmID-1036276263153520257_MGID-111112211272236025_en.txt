Date: 2022/11/22
The Global Energy Award hosted by the Austrian Energy Global Foundation, the Institute of Technology won the 2022 Taiwan Championship and recently awarded the award at the Austrian Business Office.From the left picture, Wang Hanying, director of the Green Energy Institute of Industrial Research Institute, Luo Huiqi, deputy head of the Energy Bureau's energy conservation group, and Hermann Ortner, representative of the Austrian Business Office.
The Institute of Industrial Research has sent another good news and has won international recognition for its innovative green energy technology! The Energy Globe Award, the world's largest and known as the Oscars in the environmental protection industry, recently announced the list of winners of the 2022 National Energy Globe Award.With the support of the Energy Bureau of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research stood out with "Innovative Low Carbon Ceiling Fan Technology" and won the Taiwan Championship of the Global Energy National Award.This time, after the Industrial Research Institute won the Global Energy Award for three consecutive years after it won the award as the four best solutions in the "Air" category for the Global Energy Awards for the "Energy-Saving Equipment Information APP Smart Service Plan" in 2020, fully demonstrating the Industrial Research Institute's innovative technology energy in the field of net zero emissions.
Faced with the rise of the international low-carbon energy trend, if you want to reduce carbon emissions, you can start with energy conservation and energy efficiency.For example: Some companies expand energy conservation through green buildings, saving up to one-third of the electricity compared to the original one.In addition to changes in construction, improving air conditioning efficiency is also the key to energy conservation and carbon reduction. At present, with the advent of extreme climate, the demand for indoor air conditioning-related equipment has also increased sharply. The annual sales of ceiling fans worldwide exceeds 200 million sets, and the huge electricity consumption also poses a huge burden on the earth's environment.Wang Hanying, director of the Green Energy and Environment Research Institute of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the "innovative low-carbon ceiling fan" developed by the Institute of Industrial Research Institute is a permanent magnet brushless motor combined with a special airfoil fan blade design, which can maintain extremely high energy efficiency at low speeds and consume less than half of the power of a traditional ceiling fan.If 2% of low-carbon ceiling fans can be successfully promoted to enter the market every year, 37.5 billion kWh of electricity can be saved globally by 2050, which will make a significant contribution to global energy conservation and carbon reduction.
Wang Hanying further pointed out that in addition to innovative energy-saving design, the Industries Institute of Technology will also promote the world's first carbon-label ceiling fan home appliance product, which is also one of the main reasons for the review. The R&D team of the Institute of Technology calculates carbon emissions from the five life cycles of product design, including raw materials, manufacturing, transportation, use and recycling.In addition to using recyclable materials and ultra-high efficiency design, the process also reduces production and manufacturing time and electricity consumption through automated assembly and quality control technology, and thus achieves low-carbon ceiling fan products, which can be used as a classic example of future home appliance-related products.The Institute of Technology Research has conducted power saving demonstrations in Taiwan's primary and secondary schools and other fields, which can reduce carbon emissions by more than 60% each year, and is sold in combination with Taiwan's leading ceiling fans (such as Pioneer, Apollo, etc.). In the future, low-carbon ceiling fan product design technology can be applied to other fan types, such as electric fans, air conditioners, dehumidifiers and purifiers for home appliances, and other products such as cooling fans and ventilation fans.
The Global Energy Awards encourage countries to propose innovative projects to solve environmental and energy problems, and also call on more companies to invest in green commodity research and development.Last year, the winners included the Electric Thermal Energy Storage (ETES) system of renewable energy from Germany's Siemens Gamesa Renewable Energy and the hydrogen dual-fuel engine from Anglo Belgian Corporation.
【News Dictionary】
The Energy Globe Award was established in 1999 and held its first in Austria in 2000. It was then hosted by the Austrian Energy Global Foundation every year. Its partners include the United Nations Industrial Development Organization (UNIDO), Global Chamber Platform, etc. In recent years, more than 180 countries have participated in the competition every year, with a total of more than 25,000 projects. It is the largest environmental award in the world and can be called the Oscar of the environmental protection industry.
The "Innovative Low Carbon Ceiling Fan" won the Global Energy Award by the Institute of Technology has conducted power saving demonstrations in Taiwan's small and medium-sized fields, and can reduce carbon emissions by more than 60% each year.
