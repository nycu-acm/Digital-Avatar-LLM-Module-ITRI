Innovative R&D Energy. The "Innovative Double-Target Glaucoma Drug" which won the Edison Gold Medal This time, the "Innovative Double-Target Glaucoma Drug" has better efficacy than the current drug. It has the characteristics of "3S", including precise clearance (Smart), which can improve the drainage function of the trabecular network; effectively reduce intraocular pressure (Sharp), aiming at high intraocular pressure glaucoma that is not suitable for new target drugs on the market. After six to eight hours of administration, the maximum intraocular pressure reduction drug effect can still be maintained, and the effect of lowering intraocular pressure is nearly three times better than that of international manufacturers; Safety (Safe), avoiding the current situation of red eyes and drug interaction side effects caused by eye medication, and it is safer to use the drug. It has obtained technical patents in many countries such as the United States and Europe, and has converted to reliable and reliable technology, and has completed the first phase of clinical practice and prepared to enter the second phase of clinical practice. Test. The "Cell Function Key" that won the silver medal is the precise and standardized cell screening mechanism established for allogeneic cell therapy. Allogeneic cell therapy refers to the use of donated cells from others for treatment, but the challenge of finding a suitable donor must be overcome. It takes 5 to 10 years to find suitable cells. This technology aims at this pain point. It not only can filter out poor quality cells quickly, match cells with 40 indications, find out the most suitable therapeutic use of cells, shorten the screening time, and the manufacturing cost is only one-tenth of the current cell therapy. It has been used to develop treatments for myocardial infarction repair and diabetes wound repair. The technology has been transferred to Taiwan Boyi Energy Biotech, a subsidiary of Singapore RMT Holding Group. Domestic manufacturers have entered the human clinical trial stage. As for the "high-efficiency clean polyester dissolution and decolorization technology" that won the silver medal, it is mainly aimed at the textile industry in response to the global market's demand for environmentally friendly and recycled fabrics. With the environmentally friendly water-based decolorization accelerator, the fabric can be deeply decolorized within two to four hours, and high-quality colorless polyester raw materials can be recycled to solve the poor decolorization rate of domestic polyester fibers, resulting in residual dyes that make recycling fibers easy to crack and cannot become silk, and can only be recycled into dark textiles or downgraded use. It effectively improves the recyclability of textile recycling polyester and can reduce carbon emissions by 45%. It is currently being verified with domestic textile industry operators. LED full color display module "is mainly developed for Micro LED, which is regarded as the dominant power of the next generation of display technology.
