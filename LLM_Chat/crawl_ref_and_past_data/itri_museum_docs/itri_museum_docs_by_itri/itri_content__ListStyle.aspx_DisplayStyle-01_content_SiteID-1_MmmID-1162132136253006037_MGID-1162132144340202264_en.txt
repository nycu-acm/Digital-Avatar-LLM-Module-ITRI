Service Introduction
Grid talent.
In response to the talent gap in the power field, as well as challenges and opportunities such as net zero carbon emissions and energy transformation, the Institute of Technology has established a "Grid School" to nurture green talents for the industry through professional courses, and carried out the training of power grid talents in three major aspects, including social education, vocational education and school education, to assist the industry in moving towards a sustainable future.
Scope of service
Social education: Hold new trends on key topics of energy and electricity, build smart grid online learning channels, provide on-the-select reading and new learning models; to improve the energy literacy of the whole people.
Vocational Education: Launches nine series of professional engineers and manager training courses for smart grids, charging piles, data analysis, and power market business model to cultivate green-leading talents for the industry.
School education: Through industrial industry special credit classes and scholarships, young students can learn about the prospects of the industry, and then study the electrical engineering field and reserve industry talents.
Service cases
