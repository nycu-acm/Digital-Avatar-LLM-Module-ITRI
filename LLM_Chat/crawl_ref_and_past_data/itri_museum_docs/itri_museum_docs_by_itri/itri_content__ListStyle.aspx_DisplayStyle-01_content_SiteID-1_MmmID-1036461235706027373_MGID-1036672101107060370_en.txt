Introduction
Since the early stage of restoration, my country's mold industry has been gradually thriving with the pace of domestic industrial development. In the early stage, the mold industry has not yet taken shape in detail. However, with the stability of the political situation and the implementation of the "Reward Investment Regulations" issued by the government in 49 of the Republic of China, which greatly affects future industrial development, private enterprises have gradually shown vitality and driven the growth of various people's livelihood and technical industries. In order to support industries to accelerate industrial development, the government established the "Metal Industry Development Center" in the 52nd year of the Republic of China. Assisting the industry to develop technology and lay the foundation for development. At this time, professional mold factories have also begun to be established among the people, and the number of mold manufacturing and maintenance businesses has also increased year by year. The main products are concentrated in general household appliance molds, casting molds, wood molds, glass, ceramic molds and rubber molds. Until the 1971st year of the Republic of China, given the increasing importance of the mold industry to industrial development, the government has included the mold industry in the "Strategic Industrial Application Scope" and vigorously promoted the development of the mold industry to cooperate with the relevant industrial product sales strategies to develop the overall economy with all efforts.
