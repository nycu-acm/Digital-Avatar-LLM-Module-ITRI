Technology introduction
Material multi-scale simulation interactive platform.
The multi-scale simulation research office of the Institute of Technology has deeply cultivated the multi-scale simulation interactive platform technology of materials, committed to assisting the development and design of key materials in related industries in Taiwan, and seizing huge business opportunities in related manufacturing industries around the world.Among them, each simulation technology block continues to cooperate with domestic research/academic units and verify it to respond to the research and development and processing process problems of various new materials in the industry.
Features and innovation
Provides material end design and simulation, process end simulation and regulation, and functional prediction of various complex polymers/organic substances.
Customized material property and structural database was established.
Customized materials micro mesoscopic simulation platform interface establishment and technology transfer.
Application and benefits
Application results.
Fast physical properties prediction technology for materials.
Micromesometric simulation prediction technology of polymers.
Simulation prediction and regulation technology of particle dispersion.
Analysis of micro mesoscopic structure simulation of processing process.
Micro mesoscopic simulation prediction technology of inorganic composites.
