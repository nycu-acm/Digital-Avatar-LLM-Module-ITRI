Technology introduction
Production line application diagram.
The robotic arm accuracy monitoring module directly measures the deviation at the end of the robotic arm on the production line in a non-contact manner, and issues a warning message as soon as the accuracy of the robotic arm changes, so that the producer can instantly grasp the operating status of the production line.
Features and innovation
Contactless measurement: The light beam is projected on the accuracy detection device through the light projector for accuracy monitoring to avoid the offset on the hardware caused by contact measurement.
Instant monitoring of production line: The lightweight design is easy to place on the production line for monitoring tasks, and the task of accuracy monitoring is achieved without relying on expensive measuring instruments.
Application and benefits
Assist domestic manufacturers to monitor the accuracy performance of robotic arms during production line automation, thereby maintaining product yield.
China has cooperated with Hybrid Xinye (HCG) and introduced it to the polishing and grinding production line.
