Academician Yang Panchi
Academician Yang Panchi is a professor of internal medicine at the School of Medicine of National Taiwan University and an academician of the Central Academy of Sciences.He is currently the vice president of the National Biotechnology and Medical Industry Promotion Association. Academician Yang, a famous thoracic doctor and an authoritative international lung cancer research, led a cross-unit research team to crack the pathogenic mechanism of lung cancer patients in East Asians. It is caused by the exposure of specific gene mutations and carcinogens. It not only helps to early screening of potential high-risk lung adenocarcinoma patients, but also brings new opportunities to cancer drug treatment and makes great contributions to lung cancer prevention and treatment.
Academician Yang graduated from the Department of Medicine of National Taiwan University and later obtained a doctorate degree from the Institute of Clinical Medicine, School of Medicine, National Taiwan University.Academician Yang developed ultrasonic technology in his early years, and was the first in the international medical field. He successfully used ultrasonics for the diagnosis of thoracic diseases, becoming a pioneer in this field and benefited countless patients. At the age of 42, he became the youngest internal medicine director of NTU Hospital at that time, and was awarded the top ten outstanding youths and outstanding research awards of the National Science and Technology Association.
In basic research, Academician Yang continues to devote himself to the study of the pathogenic mechanism and metastasis of lung cancer in Chinese people.He deeply felt that lung cancer has long been the top of the cause of death in Chinese cancer. However, 90% of women with lung cancer in China have not smoked. So he led a team to conduct prospective research on lung cancer in Chinese women since 2000, and has been listed in internationally renowned journals many times. Later, he also found out the correlation between genetic abnormalities and lung adenocarcinoma with the research team, and the research results were remarkable.He was selected as an academician of the Central Research Institute in 2006. Because his study experience is completed in China, he is known as the "MIT (Made In Taiwan) Academician".
In 2013, Academician Yang Shenfu Zhongwang was elected as the president of Taiwan University, becoming the first president to be completely selected by the selection process since its founding.During his tenure as president of National Taiwan University, he encouraged altruism and strengthened his sense of social responsibility, and was also committed to balanced development in various fields.In the global university rankings of various disciplines announced by the UK Higher Education Institution (QS) in 2017, NTU ranked in the top 50 in five major fields, for the first time, in the arts and humanities, engineering and science, life sciences and medicine, natural sciences, social sciences and management, and its school management ability is obvious to all.
The new crown epidemic swept the world in 2020. Academician Yang and his team developed the bait antibody ACE2-Fc, which can block viral infection, with an experimental success rate of 90%. It was published in the journal EMBO Molecular Medicine.In recent years, Academician Yang has served as the vice president of the National Biotechnology Medical Industry Promotion Association, actively promoted smart medical care, established a sustainable platform for precision health big data, gathered two major strengths in Taiwan's key technologies and medical care, and created a Chinese-centered biomedical and precision health industry, and gave full play to the unique characteristics and influence of Taiwan's medical care in the international community.
