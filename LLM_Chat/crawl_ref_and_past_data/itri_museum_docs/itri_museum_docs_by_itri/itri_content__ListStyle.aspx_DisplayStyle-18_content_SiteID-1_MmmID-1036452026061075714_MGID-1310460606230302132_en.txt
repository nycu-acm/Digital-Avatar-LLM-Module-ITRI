Innovation in laser welding of H-shaped steel
Written by Lai Wanjing
Double breakthrough in efficiency carbon emissions
Using the smart weld bead tracking system, the heated steel plate is melted directly into a metal liquid with a small area and is naturally joined after cooling. It is a high-efficiency welding method with almost zero consumables and low pollution.
The traditional H-shaped steel welding method is highly energy-consuming, cumbersome, and it relies heavily on the experience of the master.It faces intelligent bottlenecks, and the production line capacity is also greatly limited. The Institute of Technology has broken through the limitations of laser welding technology and created a high-power laser welding system applied to H-shaped steel.It combines three major technologies: digital twin simulation, smart bead tracking and low-oxygen atmosphere control to achieve new methods of high efficiency and low carbon emissions, and promotes the formulation of domestic CNS standards. This innovative achievement was recognized as the Gold Medal of Outstanding Research Award.
In order to ensure public safety of buildings, the welding of H-shaped steel has long been dependent on manual operations by senior craftsmen, but due to the high heat and high risks in the working environment, the welding has a serious shortage of work, and the welding is highly dependent on experience to adjust parameters, and it also faces a technical fault crisis.On the other hand, traditional submerged arc welding often consumes 60,000 to 80,000 watts of electricity. In order to ensure the stability of the steel plates, iron sand and welded materials need to be repeatedly filled with melt stacking, and then loading and unloading and parameter adjustments are repeated according to the specifications of the steel. The overall process is time-consuming and the consumables are highly carbon-emission.
"Traditional welding of a H-shaped steel consumes 130 kWh of electricity, and subsequent processing is also very troublesome. High-temperature welding can easily deform the steel structure and needs to be adjusted with a thermal straightener. The residual iron sand welding slag on the welding bead after welding also needs to be completely removed. The welding slag is harmful to the environment and needs to be treated environmentally friendly before removal, which is labor-intensive and financially consuming. Not only Taiwan, but also the world is producing in similar ways." Wang Yongxing, manager of the South Branch of the Industrial Research Institute, said that in the face of the dual trends of low carbon and smart manufacturing, the industry urgently needs innovative solutions that can simultaneously take into account energy saving and carbon reduction, improve accuracy and production efficiency.
The Industrial Research Institute has invested in laser welding research and development for nearly 10 years. In the past, it has been mostly used in thin plate industrial metals, such as metal processing and manufacturing with moderate size and plate thickness below 5mm."We need to use laser to weld H-shaped steel a few centimeters thick? At first, I always felt a little whimsical. The welding thickness of H-shaped steel is often as high as 50 to 60mm, far exceeding the capabilities of Taiwan's laser equipment at that time." Wang Yongxing admitted that the maximum power in the hospital was only 6,000 watts, and the maximum thickness of materials could only be processed in 10mm, which was far lower than the demand.Until Taiwan's photomontress seeks an opportunity for the transformation of steel structure manufacturing, the two sides cooperated to launch this challenging journey of laser welding of large steel structures.
The challenge of high-power laser welding started from 16,000 watts and eventually gradually expanded to 30,000 watts."When laser welding was used for the first time, the light was dazzling and the laboratory was filled with smoke. The original air pumping device was completely unable to cope with, and the laser lens was even damaged on the spot." This setback made the team realize that to allow lasers to truly enter the steel structure industry, they had to create a complete set of solutions from scratch.
Three major technologies to create intelligent steel structure technology
In order to overcome the "10,000-watt-class" laser welding challenge, the team has built a complete technical system from three aspects.First of all, there is a digital twin simulation system. The team collected a large amount of relevant data to build a digital twin system. As long as the size and laser parameters of the H-shaped steel are input, the depth and width of the weld bead can be predicted, which helps R&D personnel master the welding results before implementation, reduce the number of cutting and metallographic observations during the adjustment of lightning welding parameters, and quickly converge the welding parameter settings under different steel structure sizes.
The second is the smart weld bead tracking system.Because the steel plate is thick and heavy, the error of factory size varies greatly, and the combination of 3 steel plates can easily lead to inaccurate alignment during lightning welding.To this end, the team developed an instant sensing and automatic correction system, scan the position to be welded through the sensor at the front end of the welding head, and automatically correct the welding joint trajectory. The steel plate size error can be adjusted immediately within 2 mm to ensure the accurate position of the welding bead.The high power of laser can weld the steel plates at one time. The heated steel plates are small in area and melt directly into metal liquid. After cooling, they are naturally joined, achieving a high-efficiency welding method with almost zero consumables and low pollution, and greatly simplifying the process.
The third technology is low oxygen atmosphere control and smoke removal system.Because the high energy of laser welding will instantly vaporize some steel and the substances in the surrounding air, and generate a large amount of splashing and smoke to block the laser beam, the welding area will also be used to avoid diluting and extruding the argon gas from the oxidation of the welding bead.To this end, the team simulated the air flow line and designed an exclusive protection gas path and exhaust path to allow what should be pumped and what should be protected to remain, and stabilize the welding quality.
Fast production, excellent carbon reduction and lead the new era of steel structure
The Institute of Technology and Research has also worked with Taiwan’s photomask to create an automated laser welding process, introducing three smart technologies, and pre-decided welding parameters through the digital twin simulation system, and combined with online real-time image sensing and bead tracking compensation technology, allowing lasers to complete bilateral and simultaneous welding of steel structures without the need to slowly weld from different corners in batches. The production speed will be increased by 5 times in one breath, and the energy consumption during the welding process will also be greatly reduced. The manufacturing cost is only less than two-thirds of the traditional processing method. Each metric ton of H-shaped steel produced will save 130 kWh of electricity, and the carbon reduction will be 52.1 kg (88% carbon reduction).
However, to make the industry truly accept this technology, it is necessary to break through the barriers of lack of certification standards. "In the early days, many steel structure factories were worried that laser welding did not have verification standards. How to ensure the structure is safe and convince customers to adopt it?" Due to the traditional welding certification method, the strength is judged based on the filler volume at the welding site. As long as that piece is large enough, it is considered to be welded firmly."Laser welding materials are used to save use, so there is no filler volume for judgment, so the original standards cannot be applied."
To this end, the team first let all laser welding test pieces perform breaking, stretching and extrusion tests in accordance with the original regulations, and then pass the inspection one by one and then find the third-party unit "SGS Taiwan Inspection Technology Co., Ltd." to assist in establishing a laser-exclusive certification mechanism. It also works with the Welding Association and the Standard Inspection Bureau to promote the formulation of the world's first edition of the "Laser Welding Standards and Specifications". The steel structure has passed the national earthquake center durability test. "Only with inspection standards, manufacturers dare to use it, and the technology has the opportunity to truly implement and bear fruit." This system with the three advantages of high yield, speed, low carbon emissions, and high energy conservation not only brings a smart transformation opportunity to the domestic steel structure industry, but also provides a path for low-carbon and high-efficiency steel structure manufacturing for the global market.
