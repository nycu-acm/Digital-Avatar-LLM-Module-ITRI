Technology introduction
Advanced PCB wet process particle cleanliness sensing technology.
The optical sensing technology is used to develop and use the sensing module for continuous monitoring of particle count in high-end wet process production lines, replacing the problem of non-instant detection of manpower, and discovering abnormal particles in the tank liquid in advance to avoid abnormal contamination of the substrate and producing waste products.
Application scope
Using optical sensing technology, a high sensitivity and accuracy microparticle (2.5~10µm) sensor is developed, which can be detected in stages and regulated according to different tank fluids.Through self-compensation technology, the cleanliness monitoring of different tank liquid components on the production line is applied, which improves the accuracy of particle size detection and avoids misjudgment of particle number.At the same time, it has wide networking, continuous monitoring, acid and alkali resistance, corrosion resistance, suitable for wet process production lines, instead of real-time labor detection, can detect abnormal cleanliness problems in tank liquid in advance, and dispose of process waste in advance.
Carbon reduction benefits
The high-level cleanliness sensing control system provides instant tank liquid cleanliness in IC carrier board production lines, provides warnings of abnormalities beyond the standard, and performs early disposal and greatly reduces automatic optical repairs. It effectively reduces waste at key sites and assists the industry in reducing carbon and improving yields from the production side. Each production line can reduce carbon by 450~750 tons per year (estimated based on production line cases).
