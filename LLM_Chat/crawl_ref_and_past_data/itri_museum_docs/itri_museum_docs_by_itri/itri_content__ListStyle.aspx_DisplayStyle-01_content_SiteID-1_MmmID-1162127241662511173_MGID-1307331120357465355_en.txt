Technology introduction
Distillation process technology.
Due to the lack of independent energy-saving design and control technology in the domestic distillation process, the intelligent control model is established through low-temperature steam pressurization and thermal integration design and the introduction of AI technology to effectively reduce energy consumption and energy saving efficiency is expected to reach 40%.
Application scope
• The distillation process is one of the most energy-consuming programs in chemical processes, and the construction of domestic distillation systems relies on foreign technology and lacks independent design and energy-saving control technology.
• The existing distillation and energy-saving technology mainly adopts a multi-effect distillation process. This technology further develops heat pump system design technology based on the isolate system and distillation system. Through the integration of low-temperature steam pressurization and thermal circulation, the system's thermal energy can be recycled and replaced most of the external steam. The energy-saving efficiency is expected to be improved by another 40%.
• Existing distillation control depends on experience and lacks a suitable energy-saving control model for new systems designed with complex or energy-saving integration.Introduce artificial intelligence technology to establish a distillation intelligent control model, which can accurately predict product quality and energy consumption based on different feed compositions and processing volume changes, and provide the best energy-saving operation design parameters to achieve energy saving purposes.
Carbon reduction benefits
•This development technology can be applied to distillation systems in the petrochemical, chemical and electronic industries to help reduce energy consumption and carbon emissions in the distillation process.
