Industrial Research Institute and MIH Open Electric Vehicle Alliance signed MoU Taiwan AI Evaluation Verification Splicing Rail International

Date: 2023/03/15
The Industrial Research Institute announced that it had signed a letter of intent for cooperation with the MIH Consortium, which will assist the alliance industry in formulating AI evaluation standards, such as driving behavior detection systems, automotive regulations and safety. The picture shows a conceptual schematic diagram of the Project X 3-seater model provided by MIH, not an electric vehicle that uses AI evaluation standards in the future.
Imagine that there is a virtual assistant responding when driving in the future, or discussing the decision to move toward your destination.In the future, cars will become people's second living space.The ChatGPT boom has set off unlimited imaginations for AI application services, and has also made all sectors pay attention to the formulation of AI specifications and standards.On the 15th, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research announced that it had signed a letter of intent for cooperation with the MIH Consortium. It will use the integration of AI evaluation with international standards and assist the alliance industry in formulating AI evaluation standards, such as driving behavior detection systems, automotive regulations and safety, etc., and hopes to complete domestic systems and align with international standards to seize the international market.
Ding Bang'an, director of the Information and Communications Research Institute of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that with the support of the Digital Industry Agency of the Ministry of Digital Development and the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Development, the Institute of Industrial Research has accumulated a large amount of data and models and deep technical energy in the past, combined with partners in the industry and academia.For trusted AI, the field of artificial intelligence technology in the "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" that has drawn up the "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" has also been included in the planning of explaining AI, AI model protection, taking into account privacy and data protection related technologies.Last year, the Industrial Research Institute held the "2022 Trusted AI International Seminar" to grasp international trends.This time, we have joined hands with MIH to sign MoU, hoping to enhance the international competitiveness of my country's smart products in the industry, and through global mutual recognition, the planning of the NML National Weights and Metrics Laboratory and the construction and cooperation evaluation laboratory of the US NIST, France LNE, and the UK NPL will promote the integration of Taiwan's AI technology and applications internationally.
Zheng Xiancong, CEO of MIH Open Electric Vehicle Alliance, said that AI technology has been widely used in vehicle software and chips, such as vehicle identification, pedestrian identification, traffic light identification for autonomous driving; driver behavior detection system (DMS) also uses AI technology to detect driver fatigue and the possibility of drunk driving.MIH cooperated with the Institute of Technology and Technology to invite the Institute of Technology to become the co-chair of the Test & Certification working group, specializing in jointly establishing AI evaluation standards, as an indicator of accuracy, safety and robustness applied to vehicles in the future, providing AI technology trust, to improve the reliability of vehicle software and hardware and promote vehicle safety.
Not only should the car manufacturer comply with the regulations, but the entire vehicle or system and sub-system must also be tested and verified.Therefore, the MIH Alliance is also very concerned about the development trends and applications of AI. Therefore, there are four major aspects of cooperation between the two parties.The Institute of Technology and Research continues to observe the latest international AI information and regulations and share first-hand international information; industry, officials, academic and research jointly discusses the standards and standards for promoting Taiwan's integration with the international community; at the same time, it provides relevant professional and technical strength according to the needs of the alliance industry; integrates and assists alliance members to gain resources, and provides technical information needed for the industry to set standards, specifications, and establishes laboratories, hoping to jointly strengthen the development of AI smart driving in my country.
The Institute of Technology has drawn up the "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" to plan the field of artificial intelligence technology for intelligent enabling technology, and hopes to support innovative systems and application services developed in the three major application fields of smart life, healthy and happy life, and sustainable environment.This year, we will focus on forward-looking research and development of "trusted AI" related technologies, hoping to develop trustworthy AI technologies, and even drive the industry to promote more application possibilities, and strengthen my country's industrial strength.
