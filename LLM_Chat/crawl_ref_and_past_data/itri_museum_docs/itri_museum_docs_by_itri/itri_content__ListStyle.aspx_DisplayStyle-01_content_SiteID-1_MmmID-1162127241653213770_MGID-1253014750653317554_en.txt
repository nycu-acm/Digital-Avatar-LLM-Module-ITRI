Technology introduction
Hydrogen metering and leakage detection technology.
A hydrogen leak monitoring solution based on machine vision and fast, sensitive and precise positioning.
Application scope
Hydrogen leakage usually starts at tiny leak points, and then gradually expands and causes disasters.This technology solves the problem that traditional leak detection methods are not suitable for tiny leaks and the inability to accurately locate leakage points, and improves the safety of hydrogen application areas."Hydrogen leak monitoring technology" has the following characteristics:
Based on machine vision, the hydrogen leakage location is located in the centimeter level.
Suitable for accurate alarms of leaks from small to large leaks to 1L/min.
No need to wait for the hydrogen to diffuse to the detection position, and quickly measure leakage within 1 minute of close range.
The sensing film does not require power supply, and the visual monitoring equipment can directly integrate the original photography equipment in the factory.
The sensing film is easy to install, and the hydrogen supply pipeline does not need to be shut down.
Carbon reduction benefits
"Hydrogen leakage monitoring technology" has the characteristics of quickly positioning hydrogen leakage, improving the efficiency of post-hydrogen leakage, and thus improving the safety of the hydrogen use environment, which can reduce industry and people's concerns about the safety of hydrogen-related equipment and promote the rapid development of related industries.
