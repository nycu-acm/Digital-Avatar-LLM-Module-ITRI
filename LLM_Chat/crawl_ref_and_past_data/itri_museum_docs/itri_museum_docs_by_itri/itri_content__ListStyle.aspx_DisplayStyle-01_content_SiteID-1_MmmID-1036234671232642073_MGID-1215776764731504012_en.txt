111th Annual "Academician of the Institute of Technology" Conference
Group photo of the 11th Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (111 years)
Time: November 11, 2011
Location: Conference Room 604, Hall 51, No. 195, Section 4, Zhongxing Road, Zhudong Town, Hsinchu County (Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research)
Chairman: Academician Shi Zhenrong
Topic: Establish a net zero carbon emissions perpetual fund to promote the industrialization of net zero technology
The 111th Annual Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Industrial Research was held in the Zhongxing Academy of Industrial Research and was chaired by Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of the Zhirong Foundation. Through the combination of physical and online methods, a total of 12 academicians of the Institute of Industrial Research were invited to participate to discuss the establishment of a net zero carbon emissions perpetual fund to promote the industrialization of net zero technology.Participating in the academician meeting included Zheng Chonghua, founder of Delta Electronics, Zhu Zhiyang, president of Youjia Group, Yu Huanian, honorary chairman and senior consultant of the Foresight Technical Steering Committee of the Institute of Technology Research, Chen Xingshi, founder of Ronggang Group, Lin Genghua, academician of the Central Research Institute, Wang Boyuan, honorary chairman of Zhonglei Electronics, Xiao Cifei, director and senior consultant of Changxing Materials, Hai Yingjun, chairman of Delta Electronics, and Li Mouwei, president of Li Changrong Group, etc.; online attendance will include three academicians, including Thor Professor of the Department of Electrical Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles, Yang Zhentong, president of Yali Electric, and Lin Jiannan, chairman of Taiwan Plastics.Chairman of the Institute of Technology Research Institute Li Shiguang, President Liu Wenxiong and Deputy Director of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economics Lin Desheng also attended the meeting.
Meeting conclusion focus:
The government needs to demonstrate its determination to take the lead and set the schedule for the official office to fully introduce green buildings, electrification of official vehicles and electric buses.
The government needs to think about the source of energy and think about the net zero issue for a long time for more than 30 years. Policy requires long-term investment, and the domestic demand market will move from policy to long-term internationalization in the initial stage.
Internalizing the external cost of carbon emissions requires technology to match business models. The key lies in the correction of regulations and the establishment of the environment. State-owned enterprises can take the lead in finding examples.
To understand the difficulties of small and medium-sized enterprises in net zero investment, you can refer to the past government investment or R&D offsets and banks provide financing assistance.
Innovate from the three aspects of technology/policy/market, cooperate with energy transformation, from saving energy to improving energy efficiency, investing in the research and development of zero-carbon electricity, and reducing the carbon emission coefficient of the power, so as to assist enterprises to achieve net zero.
The Institute of Technology and Research can cooperate with the Ministry of Education/National Science and Technology Association to cooperate with the school to start with education, provide incentives to cultivate ESG talents and improve social awareness.
Find the main sources of carbon emissions, Smarter looks for innovative technologies, integrated services that reduce carbon efficiency, Greener's business philosophy, and Together to solve problems; the Institute of Technology can develop specific topics, cooperate with outstanding professors in the school, and companies cooperate with resources to encourage investment.
Energy sources should be diversified, and enterprises should be competitive in how to develop new energy (such as hydrogen energy, SMR, etc.) and take into account environmental protection.
The academicians of the Institute of Technology Research and Research propose to initiate the establishment of the "Net Zero Sustainable Fund" to assist in the research and development and industrialization of net zero technology; take advantage of international venture capital experience, find global innovative technologies, and support the development of domestic R&D institutions in a certain proportion; and use the academician exchange platform to discuss and propose specific implementation plans as soon as possible.
