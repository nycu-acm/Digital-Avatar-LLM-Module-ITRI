From site selection to logistics sales AI solves key pain points in retail
Written by Lai Wanjing
New digital upgrade engine for retail
The Industrial Research Institute integrates technologies such as AI, automation, and cloud platforms to create a one-stop AI smart retail solution, making tedious processes that rely on manpower easier and more efficient.
Faced with challenges such as numerous products in the retail industry, labor shortage, transformation difficulties, and rising costs, the Institute of Technology has integrated AI, automation and cloud platform technologies to create a "one-stop AI smart retail solution (Solution Hub)", from opening a store location, picking and replenishing scheduling to promotion and logistics management, comprehensively assisting operators to improve efficiency, reduce costs, and accelerate digital transformation, and won the Gold Medal for Industrialization Contribution.
"When the retail service industry faces challenges such as a wide variety of products, rapid demand changes, limited human resources, and rising operating costs, smart transformation is imperative, but actual promotion is difficult." Hong Yunwei, head of the Service System Science and Technology Center of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, pointed out that in order to assist the operators in facing the pressure of digital transformation, the team integrates AI, automation, cloud platforms and other technologies to create a one-stop AI smart retail solution (Solution Hub), providing all-round solutions for various pain points in the retail industry."From site selection before opening a store, to replenishment and labor scheduling in operation, to product promotion and back-end inventory management, Solution Hub is like a comprehensive consultant in the retail industry, which can help operators get in place and solve various problems on the road to transformation."
AI makes retail smart, labor-saving, and making money
The team plays the role of a "central factory", and builds a series of AI smart retail solutions based on the common needs of the industry, making cumbersome processes that rely on manpower easier and more efficient.After introducing smart tools, the operators can not only save more than 50% of manual labor hours and significantly reduce out-of-stock losses by 70%, but also increase estimated revenue by 20% through smart pricing and spot-based products control and promotion, injecting new vitality into the retail industry with limited gross profit and difficult smart transformation.
Taking the location selection as an example, in the past, we had to rely on manpower to visit on-site, compare the flow of people, compete for stores and surrounding facilities, and then score based on experience. The process was time-consuming and laborious.Nowadays, just enter the address, the system can automatically collect relevant information and quickly analyze the pros and cons, so that the analysis efficiency will jump from 5 cases per week to more than 10 cases, greatly improving the efficiency and accuracy of investment decisions.After the store is confirmed, the system can also support intelligent analysis of driving line design and product display.
After official operation, on the operation management side, AI can automate the entire process from product validity identification, automatic inventory to sales forecasting and inventory adjustment, which not only saves manpower, but also makes the operation more accurate.In marketing promotion, automatic graphics and text generation and customer feature identification functions are added to assist operators in better understanding customer needs and formulating personalized marketing strategies.
In addition to store operations, the team is also committed to improving the efficiency of supply forward warehouses. In recent years, it has helped eight operators including Hsinchu Logistics, Dongsen Shopping, and Yahoo Qimo Shopping Center optimize the product processing process, and the utilization rate of warehousing space has been increased by 50%, the shipment time has been shortened by 60%, and the manpower efficiency of operation has been improved by 50%.The system also supports logistics personnel to optimize delivery schedules, and is equipped with digital driving assistants to optimize the delivery process from warehousing to stores. It can even assist the three major delivery platforms to flexibly deliver cars when delivery staff is short of, allowing consumers to get goods faster, effectively save resources and reduce carbon emissions.
In addition, for the packaging materials needed for transportation, Lin Dingwei, special project manager of the Service Science Center of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the Institute of Industrial Research Institute has developed a durable, easy to clean, and can be recycled and reused multiple times. Combined with RFID and Internet of Things technology, it can instantly track the number and status of the boxes, and build a recycling packaging material recycling mechanism between e-commerce, logistics, channels and consumers. It has now integrated the introduction of three major online shopping platforms in Taiwan, and used more than 13,000 stores of three major supermarkets as recycling points to create a new model of green logistics.
From Ganzai Store to Smart Field AI walks into every corner of the street
AI technology has also begun to enter a community path closer to life.Recently, the Institute of Technology and Research has cooperated with Meilianshe, which has 800 stores across Taiwan to create a smart retail demonstration field.Meilianshe takes the small community ganzai store as its brand positioning. In the past, it mostly relies on store experience to prepare stocks. Now it has introduced an AI demand forecast model and added variables such as weather, festivals, and promotions to estimate it. The inventory turnover rate has increased by more than 15%, and it has effectively reduced the scrap rate.
In terms of price management, the Associated Press has also taken the lead in piloting "electronic labels" and "dynamic pricing". The system can actively adjust the price in the background according to the product validity period, sales status or promotion schedule, and directly display it on the shelf label labels, eliminating manual label replacement, and accurately controlling the quantity and validity period of the product.Hong Yunwei said that after years of cooperation, Meilianshe, which originally relies more on manpower, has taken the first step in introducing AI technology, optimized the operation process, and became a reference model for the industry. For the team of the Industrial Research Institute, it is a very good stage for the implementation of AI, and can also accumulate more practical experience on-site and establish more foundations for contributing to the industry.
Tu Zhaohui, deputy manager of the Service Science Center of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, pointed out that what most retailers are most concerned about before importing the system is nothing more than "How much does it cost? How much benefits can it bring? Will the data be outflowed?" The Institute of Industrial Research Institute is a scientific research institution, not a commercial system seller. It solves problems for operators from the perspective of assisting industrial upgrading and promoting intelligence, so that retailers are more willing to express actual needs and challenges, and introduce technology to truly prescribe the right medicine.
At the same time, in the face of pain points such as rapid AI technology, high application thresholds, and shortage of IT manpower, one-stop AI smart retail solutions can provide a service architecture that is flexible to be enabled according to demand through modular design and cloud platform. Whether it is a subscription system or a rental system, it can allow retailers to obtain advanced technology with the lowest burden.In addition, remote maintenance and transportation support has also significantly lowered the labor and cost thresholds for AI introduction, truly allowing small and medium-sized retail industries to keep up with the wave of wisdom, becoming an important driving force for promoting Taiwan’s retail service industry to a new era.
