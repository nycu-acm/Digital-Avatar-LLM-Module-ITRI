Accelerate the AIization of the industry, and the AIdea edge AI problem-solving platform is launched
Share to Facebook
Share to Twitter
Share to my Line
Contact Us
Date: 2021/04/29
Since its launch in August 2018, the AIdea artificial intelligence co-creation platform has accumulated more than 50 Taiwanese industry cases. It has rich experience in AI artificial intelligence applications in manufacturing, agriculture, animal husbandry, transportation, environmental protection, epidemic prevention and other industries. In recent years, Edge AI has been widely used in popular technologies such as self-driving technology, wearable health monitoring devices, and image monitoring. In April, the Industrial Research Institute launched the world's first Edge AI online problem-solving platform to introduce fully automated embedded systems to help Taiwanese operators obtain the best AI model.
Taiwan’s industrial AI-based propulsion launches!The Ministry of Economic Affairs' Technology Department is optimistic about the high market development of Edge AI. Through scientific and technological projects, it continues to support the AIdea artificial intelligence co-creation platform of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute and innovatively provides AI model development (problem solving) services. In April, it launched the world's first Edge AI online problem-solving platform to introduce fully automated embedded systems. It has four major characteristics: rapid problem solving, precise verification, automation, and customized integration.It is expected to become a powerful tool to accelerate Taiwan's industrial AIization.
Edge AI applications have the characteristics of low latency, low cost and high accuracy. They are widely used in popular technologies such as self-driving cars, wearable health monitoring devices, and image monitoring. They have deep challenges and development potential. According to TrendForce's Tuoqi Industry Research Institute, with the support of Edge AI, the global smart manufacturing market size will approach US$370 billion in 2022, with an annual compound growth rate of 10.7%; ABI Research's report also pointed out that the Edge AI chip market size will reach US$12.2 billion in 2025, exceeding the US$11.9 billion in the cloud AI chip market size for the first time.
The Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs pointed out that Taiwan has the strong strength of Edge AI embedded system manufacturers in hardware. If it can match high-precision AI models in software, timely leverage the hard power of soft and hard integration, and launch high-quality AI artificial intelligence application products, it will open up another wave of peak for Taiwan's industry. Therefore, through a special project, the Industrial Research Institute has supported the launch of the world's first Edge AI online problem-solving platform to introduce fully automated embedded systems, and assist Taiwanese operators in obtaining the best AI model.
Feng Wensheng, CEO of Juliang Information Technology Center of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the Institute of Industrial Research Institute has planned a 2030 technical strategy and blueprint to assist the industrial AI and AI industrialization through intelligent enabling technology. The AIdea Edge AI online problem-solving platform connects external R&D forces through the innovative model of "connected development", brings together the capabilities and creativity of tens of thousands of data scientists on the AIdea platform. AIdea connects Taiwan's Edge AI embedded system manufacturers and Edge AI application software developers to achieve a win-win co-creation mechanism.
Hong Shushen, deputy head of the huge capital center of the Institute of Technology Research and Technology and host of the AIdea Artificial Intelligence Co-creation Platform, said that the difficulties in developing edge AI models include: 1. It must be coordinated with hardware restrictions, such as memory, computing resources, etc.; 2. The accuracy of judgment and inference after model compression is affected; 3. There is a gap between the training accuracy and practical effect;4. Verification is not easy. The AIdea Edge AI online problem-solving platform adopts a fully automated embedded device test mode, embeds the analysis model uploaded by the problem-solver into the edge device for actual measurement, and then scores and ranks based on the actual measurement results, confirms the effectiveness of the analysis model, and greatly enhances the feasibility of the implementation application. Its four major features include: 1. Through public problem-solving by 10,000 people, quickly obtains the best Edge AI model; 2. Directly measure the model results in the hard-core to meet actual needs; 3. The online problem-solving process is fully automated and fast and effective; 4. It is not limited to any Edge AI application, which can support various hardware and provide customized integration.
Since its launch in August 2018, the AIdea Artificial Intelligence Co-creation Platform has accumulated more than 50 Taiwanese industrial cases. It has rich experience in AI artificial intelligence applications in manufacturing, agriculture, animal husbandry, transportation, environmental protection, epidemic prevention and other industries. More than 10,000 members have participated in problem-solving competitions, providing strong problem-solving energy, and promoting co-creation cooperation in more than 18 cases. AIdea also supports the school’s AI course teaching topics, and has provided more than 100 cases to assist universities in cultivating AI talents.The Edge AI online problem-solving platform will be launched from now on, breaking through the previous model of submitting test set problem-solving results, and perform model analysis directly on the hardware device through automated processes and collecting relevant evaluation requirements, assisting operators in obtaining the best Edge AI analysis model and creating the greatest value, improving the feasibility of implementation. Relevant companies are welcome to make more use of it.
