Core Values ​​of Industrial Research Institute:
The core value of the Institute of Industrial Research is "innovation, integrity, and sharing". The Institute of Industrial Research attaches great importance to the establishment and promotion of the "integrity" culture; in order to deepen the culture of integrity of the Institute of Industrial Research, the Institute of Industrial Research formulates "Integrity Business Standards", "Measures for the Setting of Integrity Business Committees" and "Key Points of Handling Business in Honesty Business", so as to hope that all colleagues can implement honest business in their work.
The situation of honest business management in the Industrial Research Institute:
(I) Establish an honest management committee
The Institute of Technology and Research has established a "Integrity Management Committee" under the board of directors.
In order to promote honest business matters, the Institute of Technology has established a "Integrity Business Committee" under the Board of Directors, which has five to seven members, responsible for formulating honest business norms, promoting and promoting honest business policies, handling education and training, planning and establishing a reporting system, establishing a punishment and appeal system for violating honest business norms, and assisting the board of directors and management to evaluate whether the honest business norms are operating effectively; and must report to the board of directors regularly every year.
(II) Provide education and training
In order to deepen the understanding and understanding of integrity among all colleagues, the "Integrity Management Committee" has recorded an education and training video for integrity management, introducing the relevant standards for integrity, common integrity risks and reporting channels of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, and requiring colleagues to conduct online evaluation.
(III) Sign employee behavior codes
In order to strengthen the organizational culture and management of honest management, the "Integrity Management Committee" comprehensively summarizes important integrity concepts and specific precautions in "Employee Conduct Code", and asks colleagues from the Industrial Research Institute to sign to achieve a reminder role.
