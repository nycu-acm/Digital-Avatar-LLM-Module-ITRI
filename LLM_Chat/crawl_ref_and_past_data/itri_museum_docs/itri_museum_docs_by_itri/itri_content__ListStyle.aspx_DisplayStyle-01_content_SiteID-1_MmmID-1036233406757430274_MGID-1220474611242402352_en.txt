The Institute of Technology and Research and Research and Development joined hands with the Kawasaki City Industrial Revitalization Consortium in Japan to sign a memorandum of cooperation.
The Institute of Technology and the Japanese public welfare consortium, Kawasaki City Industrial Revitalization Foundation (KIIP), signed a MOU on October 23, hoping to provide international cooperation between innovative technology and talent exchanges in Taiwan and Japan, and leverage investment funds to assist in international cooperation between new and innovative enterprises.The "Comprehensive Cooperation Agreement" signed this time will establish a R&D cooperation platform to promote information and technology exchanges in areas such as semiconductors, biomedicine, green energy environment, and new innovation support, so as to expand research and cooperation between Taiwan and Japan.
The Kawasaki City Industrial Revitalization Foundation (KIIP) was established in 1988 and has cultivation facilities such as Kawasaki New Industry Creation Center (KBIC), Nano Medical Innovation Center (iCONM), and the main task is to promote regional industrial information exchange, cultivate talents, and revitalize the local economy.
