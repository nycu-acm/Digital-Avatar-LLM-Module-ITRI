Dye-sensitized batteries are used in smart home technology - 2020 Top 100 Global Science and Technology R&D Awards Winner
Date: 2022/11/07
The dye-sensitized batteries developed by the Institute of Technology have the advantages of low power generation threshold, lightweight, low cost and 100% technical mastery. Unlike traditional solar cells that require strong light power generation, they can generate electricity as long as the brightness of about 3 candles.
