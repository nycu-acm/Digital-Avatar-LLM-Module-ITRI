Technology introduction

Use a DC high-torque motor and positioning roller to reduce the jumping of bamboo strips, design tool seats, and increase the yield of bamboo strips to 80%.
Application scope
The Forestry Administration of the Ministry of Agriculture actively promotes the development of emerging bamboo industries.In addition, the World Bamboo Fair International Forum and Expo held in 2024, bamboo strips, as basic raw materials, have significantly increased their demand.However, the currently available bamboo strip processing equipment has problems of inconvenience in operation, and the adjustment is still based on experience, which increases the uncertainty of operation.In addition, during the cutting process of bamboo strips, it is easy to cause uneven size problems when encountering bamboo joints, which affects the consistency and quality of the product.
Design a tool holder with fast fine-tuning function to accurately control bamboo strip thickness.At the same time, extend the feed track and adjust the rotation distance to effectively solve the problems of joints encountered by bamboo materials during the cutting process, and improve the cutting stability and finished product quality.
Equipped with DC high torque motor, lightweight and weight reduction of 30%.Three sets of positioning rollers are designed to reduce the shaking of bamboo material.Tool adjustment seat to increase cutting accuracy.
Carbon reduction benefits
The improved automatic bamboo strip machine effectively solves the shortcomings of traditional machines through innovative design, such as inconvenient operation and unstable quality, meeting diversified market needs, and can promote bamboo processing from manual to automation and promote the upgrading of the bamboo industry.The increase in bamboo yields effectively reduces dependence on forest resources and demonstrates significant social and environmental influence.
The bamboo strips will produce scraps. These bamboo materials can be finely crushed and then made into bamboo biomass fuel pellets. In addition to providing boiler heat source to reduce carbon emissions and reduce carbon cost expenditure, they can also be carbonized to make bamboo charcoal and bamboo vinegar liquid, and compound soil improvement and control agents to achieve a net zero cycle.
