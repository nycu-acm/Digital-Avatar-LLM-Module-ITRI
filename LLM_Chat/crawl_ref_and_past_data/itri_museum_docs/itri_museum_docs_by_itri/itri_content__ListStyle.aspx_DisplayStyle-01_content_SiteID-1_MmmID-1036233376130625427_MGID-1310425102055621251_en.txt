Technology introduction
Develop novel "polyploid CD40L-tumor-specific antigen fusion mRNA" for dendritic cell cancer vaccine immunotherapy.
mRNA was translated into fusion proteins of secreted polyploid CD40 ligand and shared MHC I-neogenic antigen peptide.mRNA is coated with lymph node-targeted nanoparticles, and mRNA is sent to dendritic cells in mice for expression through intravenous transmission.Dendritic cells are not only activated by polyploid CD40L after mRNA transfection, but the neogenic antigen peptide degraded in the cell can also successfully activate the peptide-specific T cells and successfully kill tumors.
Features and innovation
Technical Features
Compared with tumor cells or vaccines where peptides are antigens, mRNA has the advantage of rapid production and direct activation of T cells through dendritic cells.
Multivalent CD40L has an adjuvant effect and can also induce immune cells to produce memory responses to antigens and prolong protection.
Combining next-generation sequencing and AI big data analysis can assist in the development of personalized cancer vaccines.
