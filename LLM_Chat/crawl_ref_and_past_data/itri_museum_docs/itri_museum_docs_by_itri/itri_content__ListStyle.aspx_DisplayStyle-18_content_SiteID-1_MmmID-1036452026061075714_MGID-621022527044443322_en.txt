Head-mounted monitor Use volley touch
Luo Hongxu
Head-mounted monitor using volleyball touch Air Touch technology
Let doctors become medical steel
Can you imagine that in the movies "Key Report" and "Iron Man", the situation where the protagonist does not need to actually touch the screen and controls three-dimensional floating images from afar will come true one day?
In the past few years, touch and display technology have changed with each passing day, and human interaction technology has undergone significant changes, which has prompted the continuous improvement of the use interface and intelligent design. Air Touch is the most advanced technology. As long as you wear a specially designed glasses display monitor, you can see the transparent picture image displayed in the volley through the device in a specific work space, and interact with the transparent picture touch with the finger. It is the latest human-computer interaction interface technology to accurately detect subtle movement changes of the fingers.
This technology can not only sense the user's fingers, but also sense the pen or chopsticks held by the user, allowing the user to browse the web, draw graphics and play games without touching the screen, which is as simple as taking objects from a bag.
For example, drawing a figure in the air with your hands will immediately appear on the screen; pressing the button in the air with your fingers will immediately indicate the system to start the action, grab and pull the object with your hands, and the object can present three-dimensional images of different orientations. This is the volleyball touch technology, which can be applied to a series of devices such as smartphones, tablets, cars, refrigerators, etc. Such technology will essentially change the way human-computer interactions and break the wall between human-computers.
This technology that transcends the times is no longer a research and development project in the laboratory, but a technology that already has exact product application and display.During the 100th anniversary of the founding of the People's Republic of China, the Technology Innovation Pavilion of the Institute of Technology has already displayed this technology in the hands of the Chinese. The "Space-Time Telephone Booth" in the exhibition hall is designed by the Institute of Technology with a "floating three-dimensional image system". It uses advanced projection technology and optical imaging design to present 3D images in the air, and combines the floating touch design to experience the magic of floating images.
This year, the Industrial Research Institute won the award again for the "Wireless Air Touch Technology for Head-mounted Monitors" in the world's top 100 Science and Technology R&D 100 Awards, known as the Os in the technology industry. The three technical strengths have been recognized again. This new technology has gathered the achievements of the related technologies of the Industrial Research Institute in the past, allowing the head-mounted Monitors to use volley air Touch, and the user experience has been further improved.
Multi-point space tracking algorithm and gesture recognition technology to identify subtle actions of fingers
The head-mounted monitor developed by the Industrial Research Institute uses the "Luikong Touch Air Touch" technology. You can interact with the virtual image in front of you just by moving your finger or waving your hand.
Unlike general gesture control, through the "Volve Touch Air Touch" technology, users can see a virtual screen about 10 inches 40 cm in front of their eyes, and touch the virtual screen in the air with their fingers. The sensor on the display can detect the space position of the fingers and interact directly with the images they see, creating a different experience than physical panel touch. It can be said to be a brand new human-computer interaction interface.
The touch technology of "Lingkong Touch Air Touch" uses the design of optical lenses to present the real image of an object and let the image float outside the glasses.For the user, it is equivalent to a Lingxu item appearing about 30 cm in front of the eyes. The "Lingkong Touch Air Touch" technology is more equipped with infrared detection technology. If you extend your hand to "touch" these image objects, you don't actually need to touch anything. The system will react relative to the user's movements and position to achieve the touch effect.
Diao Guodong, deputy director of the Institute of Electrical and Optical Research Institute of Industrial Research Institute, pointed out that the "Lingkick Touch Air Touch" technology is a cross-domain technology developed by combining floating three-dimensional system virtual images and another spatial gesture recognition and tracking system technology.It is precisely because this technology has multiple receptions of cross-domain and virtual and real information that it has been favored by R&D 100 Awards.
Originally, the Industrial Research Institute already had a space gesture recognition and tracking system. This uses the original multi-point space trace algorithm and gesture recognition technology to break through the traditional touch-type touch screen operation.Users can use the gesture recognition module on their left and right hands to transmit action signals and receive through the RF wireless transmission module, and can perform multi-point operations and diversified action input recognition in the air, and apply it to various operating interface environments. Because the recognition ability can be as thin as your fingers, it is more convenient for users to flip the picture, or enlarge and shrink a specific area on the picture.The application areas include Windows Media Center, Set Top Box, multi-person conference briefing system, home entertainment system, somatosensory games, multi-point operation interface, etc.
Compared to the Wii that requires holding a remote control, or Kinect can only recognize large movements of the body, the Gesture Recognition System of the Institute of Technology is more advanced and can already recognize the subtle movements of ten fingers.Diao Guodong smiled and said, "The technology of the Institute of Technology is progressing year by year. In order to identify fingers, it was necessary to put a positioning point on the fingers to allow the image system to identify them. This can certainly make the technology faster and more practical, but it does not conform to the use habits of consumers."
Just to omit the little effort of putting a positioning point on your finger, the R&D staff of the Institute of Technology took all their efforts, because for the optical recognition of computers, if there is no positioning point and a finger moves in front of the lens, the system will not be able to identify whether it is a foreign object that breaks into the range of the lens, or a normal background image? Adjusting the system to be extremely sensitive may even fly into the camera as fingers that must be identified.If the sensitivity value of the system is reduced, even the user's fingers will be regarded as images, and the system will skip them to avoid identification.
What is even more difficult is that for this kind of photography to identify finger movements, Diao Guodong pointed out that "the most difficult part is how to expand to the third dimension, that is, the movement of the object depth."
Head-mounted device Enjoy the quality of 3D theater audio and video
The volleyball touch technology of the Institute of Technology is composed of a depth camera module with a built-in complementary metal oxide semiconductor sensor (CMOS sensor). It is composed of a calculation software that can calculate the distance between the finger and the image. It captures the X and Y axis actions of the plane, and further captures the Z-axis action of the depth three-dimensional sense. It successfully overcomes the misjudgment caused by insufficient light, background approximation, or background interference. It can effectively separate the distance between the user and the flowing background, so it can detect the front, back, left and right distances of the user's fingers, and achieve accurate touch response by calculating the time point of the touch.
In addition to the three-dimensional Air Touch technology, another important part of "head-mounted monitors using volley touch" is the head-mounted monitor. Perhaps it is more appropriate to call them with smart glasses. At first glance, it looks like the head-mounted monitors or head-mounted theaters that are already on sale on the market, but the actual technology is far from the same.
Head-mounted monitors or head-mounted theaters focus on audio-visual enjoyment. The viewing angle of this type of head-mounted glasses exceeds the wide viewing angle of 45 degrees, which is beyond the perspective range of the human eye. With the matching 5.1-channel surround sound, the audio-visual effect is not inferior to watching 3D videos in the cinema.In addition, it is worn on the user's head in a head-mounted manner, and even if the user lies down, it will not prevent him from watching movies.A group only costs about NT$20,000, which is more approachable than a full set of 3D theaters.
Diao Guodong pointed out that because this type of product focuses on "personal entertainment", after wearing a head-mounted theater, users are basically isolated from the outside world, and the information, images, and sounds obtained are all information provided by the head-mounted theater. However, the "head-mounted display uses volley air touch technology" developed by the Institute of Technology and Research. Users can not only accept 3D images and information provided from computer systems or the Internet, but also make volley input, but it does not prevent users from observing things around them at all. In other words, it is a bit like Google Glass released by Google in 2012, which can not only see messages from the Internet, but also not prevent them from watching the surrounding world normally.
Google glasses that use Android operating system mainly include processors, memory, photography lenses and microphones, speakers, and wireless network devices. They include a touch navigation device on the right side of the frame, and also built-in gyroscopes, accelerometers and sensors.
For users, Google is trying to replace the screen of smartphones with glasses. The main input method is to identify the voice for language. The user gives instructions to speak. The system converts voice into instructions, which can connect to the Internet to obtain information, or perform functions such as taking photos, recording, sending messages, navigation, and instant translation through instructions. Simply put, users can complete all the work that computers can currently do through voice control.
"But completing all the work through voice control is also the biggest problem for Google Glasses." Diao Guodong believes that the problem with this product is that voice control is an external communication action. It is difficult to avoid being noticed by the people next to you.
The germs don’t get started!Instant processing of surgical information
However, "the head-mounted display uses volleyball touch Air Touch technology" can use both voice input and volleyball stroke input. For users, it is equivalent to combining the concept of "three fireflies and one cloud" on one device to allow a single user to use it freely, which has more advantages over Google glasses.
This system can be used for non-contact control operations, "including places with concerns about germ infection, such as elevators in hospitals, public places, and human-machine interfaces of medical equipment in the operating room, etc.." Diao Guodong pointed out: "Take the operation table as an example, the doctor is fully sterile as soon as he goes on the operating table. If he encounters an X-ray of the patient, the doctor cannot leave the sterile area to get the film." In the past, when encountering such emergencies, he relied on an assistant or nurse to get the film. However, if the doctor wears a "head-mounted monitor using volley touch Air Touch technology" on his head, he does not need to leave the operating table. Press a few information buttons in the air to retrieve all the patient's resumes, and even the patient's immediate blood pressure, body temperature, and pulse can be displayed on the head-mounted monitor.
Diao Guodong said optimistically: "The head-mounted monitor can also be combined with the imaging medical materials of the Institute of Technology and Research in the future to achieve more accurate surgery."
The imaging medical material currently cooperated with Xiuchuan Hospital is to use minimally invasive surgical image transmission system to intercept the endoscope 2D image signal, and on another 3D display, the 2D image is simultaneously converted into 3D image display, allowing surgeons to obtain 3D stereoscopic images during surgery.
However, at present, 3D stereo images can only be displayed on the 3D TV next to the operating table, so it may be awkward situation where the doctor's head looks at the screen on the right, but his hands are facing the patient on the left. However, if these 3D stereo images are directly projected on the latest head-mounted monitor, the image will appear wherever the doctor's head turns. This will be a great leap forward in the cross-border combination of electronic technology and the medical industry, and will also bring a historic jump to human medical technology.
