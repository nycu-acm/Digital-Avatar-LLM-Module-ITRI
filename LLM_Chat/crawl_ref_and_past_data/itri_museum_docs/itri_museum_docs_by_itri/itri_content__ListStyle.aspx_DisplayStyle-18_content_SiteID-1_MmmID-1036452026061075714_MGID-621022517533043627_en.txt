Low-temperature atmospheric pressure plasma coating reduces costs and takes into account environmental protection
Tao Xiaoman
In the past, the most critical "metal-oxide" coating process in the optoelectronics industry must be carried out in a vacuum, high temperature of more than 500 degrees and highly toxic environment, making it difficult to take into account both environmental protection and cost.The "low-temperature atmospheric pressure plasma coating" technology developed by the Institute of Technology now allows the coating of transparent conductive film (TCO) in a few minutes in an atmospheric environment by using air and aqueous solution containing metal ions!
From 2005 to the end of 2011, Zhang Qiang, manager of the plasma application technology department of the Advanced Manufacturing Core Technology Group of the Institute of Machinery, held a coating nozzle that combines the principles of "tornado" and "lightning" and had 100% confidence in the safety of the technology.He recalled that the biggest bottleneck of this technology was to find superalloy materials suitable for mass production of plasma. "We spent half of our time trying the most suitable superalloy, and during this period we worked with military products manufacturers."
This technology can be widely used in industries such as tablet computers, touch panels, solar energy, etc. It not only meets environmental protection requirements from design, raw materials, processes and equipment. It is the world's only green manufacturing technology (ECO Technology), but also can press the cost of panel production to between 20 and 33% of the existing process.
"What is even more exciting is that this set of equipment was chosen by German Linde at the German Dental Equipment Exhibition, and will go through the field of biomedical medicine that whitens the laser teeth and sterilize! " Zhang Qiang said that he signed a contract with Linde in March this year, hoping that this technology can not only benefit the industry, but also do its best for the health of the people.
