Chen Junsheng Academician
Academician Chen Junsheng is the chairman and CEO of Acer. He has more than 30 years of experience in technology industry management. With a positive leadership style, he focuses on corporate advantages and opportunities, leads the team to develop steadily, turn losses into profits and continues to make profits; by thinking through the frame, he opens up new business maps, masters new business opportunities, and continuously breaks through peaks in business results, and builds Acer as a resilient and sustainable enterprise, becoming a new benchmark for the innovation and development of Taiwan's industry.
Academician Chen graduated from the University of Missouri, USA with a master's degree in corporate management. After completing his studies, he returned to Taiwan to work at IBM Taiwan Corporation.In 1991, he was transferred to Intel Corporation, and later in charge of business and marketing at the US headquarters. He served as global vice president and was familiar with the international market environment.In 2005, he changed his position at Taiwan Jieyi Circuit Manufacturing Co., Ltd., and is familiar with the semiconductor and personal computer (PC) market industry chain.
In 2013, he was recruited by Acer founder Shi Zhenrong, and became the global president and CEO in 2014, and in 2017, he served as the chairman and CEO of Acer. He has implemented drastic reform plans to improve financial situation and turn Acer into profits.In addition, Acer established the advanced player e-sports brand "Predator" in 2015 to further enhance its brand value, and won the German Red Dot Brand Award in 2019, becoming the world's first award-winning e-sports brand, establishing Acer as a global first-tier e-sports brand; at the same time, it entered the "lifestyle brand", breaking away from the mature PC industry in the past, and deepening its operational tentacles into every aspect of consumers' lives.In 2016, the internal construction was launched, the "Tiger Team" strategy was launched, and the public offering (IPO) of its subsidiaries was launched to expand the group's strength and popularity.
Academician Chen led Acer Group toward the sustainable development of ESG, built multiple business engines to enhance corporate resilience, and promoted AI to fully exert its effects in the medical and health field. It brings great value to Acer Group and is one of the examples of cross-field development of Taiwan's electronics and information communications industry.
