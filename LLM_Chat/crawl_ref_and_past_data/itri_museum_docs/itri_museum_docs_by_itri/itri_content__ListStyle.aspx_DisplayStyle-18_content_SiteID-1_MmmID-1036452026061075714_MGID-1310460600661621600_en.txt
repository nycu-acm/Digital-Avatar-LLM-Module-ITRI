Industry Focus Spotlights
Build a trusted semiconductor supply chain partner
Written by Tu Xinyi
Welcome to the global AI trend
The Institute of Technology held a Global Semiconductor Supply Chain Partner Forum to implement the "Global Semiconductor Democratic Supply Chain Partner Initiative" proposed by the government, attracting representatives from many countries such as the United States, Japan, and Europe and more than 700 people from semiconductor materials, equipment, factory affairs, finance and other industries to attend the meeting.
At a time when geopolitical risks are rising and supply chain restructuring is accelerating, Taiwan is transforming from a "silicon shield" to a "silicon bridge" connecting the world.The Institute of Technology held the "Global Semiconductor Supply Chain Partner Forum", bringing together representatives from the United States, Japan, Europe, the United Kingdom, the Netherlands, France, and the Czech Republic to Taiwan and industrial consensus. With "innovation, security, resilience, and co-prosperity" as the main axis, it laid a strategic foundation for Taiwan to become the core fulcrum of the AI ​​era and demonstrated Taiwan’s key role in the global strategic landscape.
Faced with geopolitical risks and supply chain restructuring, the Institute of Industrial Research recently held a Global Semiconductor Supply Chain Partner Forum with the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the National Development and Technology Conference, and the Ministry of Foreign Affairs to specifically implement the "Global Semiconductor Democratic Supply Chain Partner Initiative" proposed by the government, attracting representatives from many countries such as the United States, Japan, and Europe and more than 700 people from semiconductor materials, equipment, factory affairs, finance and other industries to attend the meeting.
The President calls on all countries to work together to strengthen the resilience of semiconductor chains
President Lai Ching-te said that now, the international community is facing the challenge of low-price dumping of semiconductor processes, so countries must work together to actively face it and form a global semiconductor supply chain partner.Looking at the advantages of various countries, the United States has excellent semiconductor materials and technology; the Netherlands has advanced process key equipment; Japan has raw materials and equipment; and Taiwan's hardware development plays an important role in the international community.Taiwan has the responsibility and is willing to become the fulcrum of technological development in the era of AI artificial intelligence.To this end, the government cooperates with the industry to help the industry develop through national policies, finance, tax incentives and other measures, and build data centers. It also creates supercomputers in Tainan Shalun and Science Park to assist all industries in developing life application solutions.Representatives from all countries gathered at this forum, showing everyone's attention and concern for the semiconductor industry. We look forward to working with all countries to strengthen the resilience of the global supply chain and build a vision of shared prosperity.
Minister of Economic Affairs Guo Zhihui pointed out that Taiwan is the core partner of the global semiconductor supply chain. Relying on the President's "Global Semiconductor Democratic Supply Chain Partner Initiative", Taiwan has four core cores: strengthening supply chain resilience, establishing a diversified supply system, developing an AI chip industry chain alliance, and establishing a shared advanced chip democratic supply chain as an industrial strategy and a positive commitment to global supply chain reconstruction.
The Ministry of Economic Affairs invites international supply chain partners to invest in Taiwan, jointly construct value chains, explore emerging markets with rigid demand, and discover new growth momentum; it also cooperates with democratic countries similar to those of the concepts, establish a credible and self-disciplined market mechanism, and jointly protect the trusted supply chain system.Taiwan has deep semiconductor talents and is the best capital to connect with the international community. The Ministry of Economic Affairs simultaneously promotes the multinational talent support mechanism, through bilateral cooperation and extension of talent cultivation in AI, drones, big health, next-generation communication and other fields, build a diversified and resilient innovative talent network to enhance the competitiveness of democratic supply chain partners.
Industrial Research Institute leads the layout and builds an open semiconductor ecosystem
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, pointed out that semiconductors have become the core strategic asset of international economic and technological competition and require a highly mutually dependent global supply chain.Innovation is the driving force for industrial growth. The Institute of Technology will focus on potential niche markets, continue to play the role of key technology development, and assist industry upgrading; in terms of ensuring safety, we look forward to cooperation with all parties to strengthen supply chain transparency and security management, and reduce the risk of chain breakage; the Institute of Technology will continue to deepen international cooperation with the United States, Japan, Europe, the United Kingdom, etc., from materials, manufacturing to talents, improve the stability and adaptability of the overall supply chain, actively explore innovation and "blue ocean" opportunities, and jointly build an open, inclusive and shared semiconductor ecosystem, so that all participants can create value, share results, and create prosperity together.
Global consensus takes shape and build a trusted supply system
In the forum, representatives from the United States, Japan, Europe and other countries also reached a consensus with Taiwan to establish a "trusted supply chain alliance."
Kazuyuki Katayama, representative of the Japan Taiwan Exchange Association, emphasized that building a resilient semiconductor supply chain requires global cooperation. Japan and Europe have advantages in materials and equipment, the United States is good at design, and Taiwan has outstanding performance in the manufacturing field.Such a resilient supply chain is not only crucial to business development, but also indispensable in maintaining economic security.
Jeremy Cornforth, deputy director of the American Association in Taiwan, pointed out that the United States and Taiwan have a long-term and close partnership, and semiconductors are a key area of ​​cooperation between the two sides.Deputy Director Ke Jiemin emphasized that bilateral industries are actively expanding investment, strengthening supply chain resilience and technological security, and jointly creating prosperity and innovation of the global semiconductor ecosystem.
Lutz Güllner, Director of the European Economic and Trade Office, stressed that the EU is actively working closely with partners with similar ideas to strengthen the resilience and security of the semiconductor supply chain and build a solid artificial intelligence infrastructure.
Taiwan is a key and trustworthy partner of the EU in the process.With TSMC's investment in Germany and the continued deepening of cooperation between Hon Hai and European companies in the semiconductor and space industries, the EU will be committed to strengthening cooperation with Taiwan.
Ruth Bradley-Jones, the UK's representative in Taiwan, said that cooperation is the core of promoting and consolidating the development of semiconductor and artificial intelligence supply chains.The UK will continue to deepen international partnerships, promote innovative development, and build a policy and regulatory framework that contributes to the resilience and diversity of global supply chains.
Matthijs van der Hoorn, deputy representative of the Netherlands’ office in Taiwan, pointed out that semiconductors are a global industry and no country can fight alone. Only cooperation can respond to challenges.As a key role in the value chain, the Netherlands will continue to work with governments, industries and research institutions to jointly build a resilient, reliable and sustainable supply chain.
Gan Liming, honorary president of the Japan Semiconductor Strategic Promotion Association, said that semiconductors are the foundation for promoting digital social development. Starting from the alliance between Japan and Taiwan, we invite common value members to build a stable supply chain, and ensure network security and technological autonomy at the technical level. From design to manufacturing, multiple companies should participate, diversify economic security risks, so as to ensure the stable development of global industries.
