The Industrial Energy Saving Research Institute has a background in chemistry, chemical industry and mechanical technology, and is committed to the development of industrial energy-saving materials and application systems. The existing technology takes the development of high adsorption and low-energy-consuming pore materials (metal organic framework MOF) as the main axis, and develops material trial production and molding technology, so that low-energy-consuming MOF adsorbents can be widely promoted and applied in the form of powders, slurries, particles, films and wheels, and achieves the ultimate goal of reducing drying power consumption.

MOF Products
Adsorbents are industrial basic materials and are widely used in industries such as industrial drying, chemical separation, and gas purification.Among them, low-humidity drying equipment is one of the main energy-consuming equipment in the industrial sector, accounting for 12% of the industrial energy consumption (about 6.6 billion kWh).The adsorbent used in existing low-wet drying equipment consumes too much energy due to low water absorption rate (5%) and high regeneration temperature (160℃).Metal Organic Framework (MOF) has the characteristics of high specific surface area and adjustable pore size, and can be regenerated at low temperature (80℃), which is expected to solve the high energy consumption problem of traditional adsorbents.

Testing Testing Service
Particle strength test
The resistance of the particles of the product using a texture Analyzer (Texture Analyzer, model: TA.XT Plus C) is to resist external forces. The stronger the particle strength, the harder the particles, and the more they can maintain the original integrity of the particles during industrial applications. The test data provide particle size and particle strength distribution.
Particle strength test
Wear rate test
The particle wear rate test provides the ability of the product particle surface to resist friction.The lower the wear rate, the less dust generated by wear in the process of transportation or industrial applications. The test of particle wear rate is carried out according to the test standard (D4058-96).
Wear rate test
Stacking density measurement
By measuring the bulk density of particles, the maximum weight of filling under the same volume can be converted.In the application field of fixed volume, the bulk density is a key factor, such as industrial water vapor adsorption (drying). The higher the particle bulk density, the more materials can be filled under the same volume, and the amount of water vapor adsorption in a single cycle also increases.
Stacking density measurement
