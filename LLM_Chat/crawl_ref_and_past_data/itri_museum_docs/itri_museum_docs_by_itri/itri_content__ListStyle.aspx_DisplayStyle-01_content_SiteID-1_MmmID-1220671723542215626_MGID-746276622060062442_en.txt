Technology introduction
Materials digital technology service platform—Integrated application of AI artificial intelligence and materials industry.
Due to factors such as equipment aging and process modification, the production efficiency of petrochemical plants generally deviates from the best point, and traditionally, data-driven is used as the core.
The AI ​​technology of the heart can only be optimized from historical data and existing operating ranges, limiting the possibility of improving production efficiency.The Institute of Technology has invested in the development of "AI-enhanced Intelligent Production Efficiency Assistance System Technology" to maintain the best production efficiency of petrochemical process systems through smart navigation, assisting the petrochemical industry to move from automated control to intelligent operation, and creating new value for production management.This technology develops an AI-enhanced model, combined with a predictive chemical engineering theoretical model, greatly improving the industrial applicability and prediction accuracy of AI technology.The recommended process operating parameters are provided to technical cooperation manufacturers to verify. After the recommended process parameters, the product yield will be increased by more than 1-2% compared with the manufacturer's best value in previous years.
When the materials industry wants to introduce AI artificial intelligence technology, it often faces the following problems: (1) Technical threshold and cross-field talents, (2) What topics are suitable for import? (3) The accumulation of materials industry data is not easy, and the amount of data is small.In terms of cross-field technology introduction and material AI application technology, the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute can provide evaluation and technical support.
In recent years, the Institute of Technology has actively integrated digital technologies such as materials industry and data science, and combined with rich expert experience in the industry, it has introduced digital technologies such as AI artificial intelligence and the Internet of Things (IoT) into materials research and development, product design and intelligent production management, assisting the material industry in digital upgrades, and also lowering the threshold for technology introduction to the industry.
Features and innovation
The materials industry and data science are integrated across fields, and AI artificial intelligence technology is introduced into materials research and development, product design and intelligent production management to solve practical issues in the materials industry.
Application and benefits
New material design and development, optimization and improvement of material formula and process system, intelligent monitoring and control of production and manufacturing, and AI technology introduction and evaluation and digital talent training.
Automatic monitoring and intelligent control of water purification plants
Together with the Water Technology Research Group of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering, the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, we assisted in the collection and integration of IoT data of the water purification plant, carried out automated monitoring, and introduced AI artificial intelligence technology to assist in intelligent water purification process control; through IoT real-time data, predict the optimal water purification dose, reduce the turbidity of raw water to the specified value, and connect the automated dosing system for control; this case not only effectively reduces the manpower required for water purification process control, but also converts human experience judgments into digital and intelligent predictions.
5G composite material system formula optimization
Assist domestic manufacturers in introducing AI artificial intelligence technology and applying it to the optimization and design of 5G composite materials formulas; this material system covers a total of 96 material ratios, and requires 12 system specifications at the same time. It is not easy to fine-tune the specifications based on expert knowledge alone. After introducing AI artificial intelligence technology, the digital prediction model can quickly find the corresponding material ratios based on the customer's system specifications and needs, greatly improving the design and customized service capabilities.
High toughness insole composite structural design
Different arrangements of material microstructures will significantly affect the soft and hard functions of the overall material, and have different application fields; functional insoles emphasize high toughness and lightweight, so this case uses simulation technology to design the microstructure of material, and uses AI artificial intelligence technology to quickly identify the soft and hard functions of the overall material, and find a material structure with high toughness and lightweight.
