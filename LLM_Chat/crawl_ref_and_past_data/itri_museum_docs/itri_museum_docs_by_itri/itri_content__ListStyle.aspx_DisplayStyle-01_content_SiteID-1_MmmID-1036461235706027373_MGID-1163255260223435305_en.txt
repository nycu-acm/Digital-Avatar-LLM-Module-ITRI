Taiwan 3D Interactive Image Display Industry Association (3DIDA) is the best platform for international exchanges in the development of 3D technology and application. It combines relevant institutions, groups and personnel such as industry, officials, academics, and research to promote domestic 3D interactive image display technology and industrial standards, jointly build a 3D interactive image display industry chain, gather energy to propose directions and build consensus, create competitive advantages of Taiwan's industry, and make Taiwan a major center for the global 3D interactive image industry technology research and development.
