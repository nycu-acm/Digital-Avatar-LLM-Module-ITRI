Zhang Anping Academician
Academician Zhang Anping is currently the CEO of the Taiwan Cam Enterprise Group, the Chairman of the Taiwan Cam Enterprise, and the CEO of Yunlang Tourism Group. He is an entrepreneur with both humanistic background and innovative business thinking.Chairman Zhang led Taiwan's leading cement industry in Taiwan to practice communication between enterprises and society, set circular sustainability as the development goal, and strive to become a green environmental engineering company that emphasizes both industry and nature.
Chairman Zhang is an innovative helmsman and has cross-domain integration between different fields. He founded many companies such as China Jiatong, CITIC Securities, and Yunlang Tourism Group. His career map spans the cement, software, the Internet, securities, media, and tourist hotel industries. He has accumulated more than 40 years of industrial experience so far.With technology as the driving force of the industry and the management perspective of humanities and arts, we have repeatedly injected innovation into tradition.
In 2017, Chairman Zhang became the chairman of Taiwan Cement, leading the company to a green transformation path. He laid out markets such as the Middle East, Europe and Africa in two major directions: circular economy and globalization, and moved towards internationalization and diversified business groups.At the same time, we actively invest in the ecological and environmental protection industry, work with the Institute of Technology to innovate carbon capture and microalgae breeding and reuse technologies, and build a composite renewable energy combining photoelectricity and wind power in the Changbin Industrial Park. At the same time, we use cement kilns to coordinate the treatment of garbage at high temperatures to solve problems for society, create a waste-free home, and become an excellent example of the circular economy in Asia.
Chairman Zhang Anping is people-oriented, advocates social enterprise responsibilities, founded the "Taipei Cement Workshop", aiming to inject cement planting into cultural and creative courses, and leads the public to have a new understanding and impression of the cement industry through hand-made experience; he invests in building the first open ecological circulation factory in Taiwan, committed to the coexistence and prosperity of community settlements and industries.For Chairman Zhang, life is the most worthwhile thing. Combining humanities and technology, practicing the spirit of natural sustainability will push the corporate value to a higher level.
