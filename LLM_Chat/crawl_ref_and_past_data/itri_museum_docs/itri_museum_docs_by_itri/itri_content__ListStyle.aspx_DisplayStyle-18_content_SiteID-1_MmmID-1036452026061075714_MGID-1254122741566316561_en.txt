Core technologies of the international cell therapy industry
Written by Lai Wanjing
The Institute of Industrial Research and Research has designed the complex cell cultivation process into a consistent core technology of the cell therapy industry. Through platform integration, it greatly simplifies the pre-operation of cell therapy, and won the Gold Medal Award for Industrial Research and Research and Research.
In order to promote the domestic regenerative medical industry, the Institute of Technology has integrated the fields of biomedical and mechanical industries across the domains, and worked with clinical medical institutions to consistently integrate the complex cell production process.After years of hard work, it has successfully developed a mature and stable core technology platform for the cell therapy industry and won the Gold Medal of Industrial Research Institute Industrialization Contribution Award.
The advancement of precision medical technology has brought about innovation in medical trends, among which regenerative medicine has attracted much attention. The effects that traditional drugs are difficult to achieve can be used to regenerative cell therapy to bring treatment hope to patients. More and more clinical studies have also verified the excellent effects of cell therapy, and have become an emerging treatment method that is extremely concerned by the global medical community.
"Cell therapy is not an innovative technology, blood transfusion is the most well-known cell therapy." Wang Yuqi, head of the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, explained.The concept of cell therapy is very simple, which is to find the right cells to patients in need to achieve the treatment purpose.The sources of cells are divided into two categories: autologous cells and allogeneic cells. Autologous cells refer to the acquisition of cells from the patient's body, and then re-introduced into the patient's body after processing. The advantage of choosing autologous cells is that the cells originate from the patient and there is no problem of rejection, but it is often because the patient is in a disease state or the number of cell quality may be insufficient. Therefore, how to expand the quantity and quality of cells and return it to the patient's body for treatment has become a major challenge.
Take it right away and grasp the golden treatment period
At present, internationally, regenerative medicine has gradually moved from autologous cell therapy to Off-the-shelf (spot type, ready to use) allogeneic cell therapy.In other words, cell therapy is converted into mass production of similar drugs. After the allogeneic cells are producted and mass produced, they can be used immediately based on the patient's symptoms, which can greatly reduce the individual verification costs of related cells and shorten the relevant verification schedule.In order to build Taiwan's regenerative medical capacity and accelerate the industrialization of cell therapy, the one-stop supply chain above and below the middle and downstream of the Institute of Technology Research and Research has been constructed to build a "core technology of the cell therapy industry", from the source screening, amplification and culture of allogeneic stem cells to clinical application.It not only provides high-quality, high yield and high stability regenerative medical products, benefiting the health of the Chinese people, but also injecting new impetus for rapid development into the domestic cell therapy industry.
Allogeneic cells treat mesenchymal stem cells (MSCs) from healthy donors, that is, they need to find the appropriate donor first, screen out the cell characteristics through technology, and then culture, amplify the quantity and set aside.The "key for cell function" is to establish an accurate and standardized cell screening mechanism for allogeneic cell treatment, use accurate single-molecule identification technology to analyze cell molecules and their immune characteristics, filter out poor-quality cells, and more accurately select cells suitable for specific clinical applications. At present, 40 supercells with indications have been successfully established, including cells that need good vascular regeneration capabilities in the treatment of myocardial infarction or diabetes wounds.
Wang Yuqi said that to identify cell quality, donor age is not the only consideration, and each source has its own uniqueness. Therefore, finding out cell characteristics is the key, and it is used for different indications, including acute myocardial infarction, diabetic wounds, immune regulation, etc.; finding out the cell characteristics and then analyzing and culture to amplify the number to confirm the safety and effectiveness of cells in compliance with regulations.
In addition, a "allomal mesenchymal stem cell (MSCs) raw material library" was established, which can produce hundreds of thousands of doses of allogeneic MSC bone marrow stem cells. It adopts ready-to-use frozen storage technology, and has high survival and cell activity.For acute myocardial infarction and other acute diseases, use it immediately within 6 to 7 hours of golden treatment.Currently, there are cases of using this cell bank to conduct human trials for myocardial infarction cell therapy, so that patients included in the trial can use it when they are in urgent need, just like the blood removal bank is the same.
The process of mass production of cells is cumbersome. In order to optimize the culture process of cell therapy, the Institute of Technology and Research has combined its expertise in the fields of biomedical and mechanical engineering to bring the "factory concept" into regenerative medicine, and developed Taiwan's first MIT attached cell automation production system, integrating the world's most complete system module, combining robotic arms to produce "attached stem cells and somatic cells" as biological agents or biological drugs, saving 30 to 40% of manpower needs, can elastically optimize the process and improve production efficiency.
Recalling the process of establishing the cell therapy technology platform, Wang Yuqi pointed out that the Industrial Research Institute built the first GTP (Good Tissue Practice) field in Taiwan in 2005 to treat leukoplakia; and the 2015 dust explosion incident in the Eight Immortals Paradise, a large number of burns and scald patients poured in, which put the medical system's rescue capabilities to the test.Therefore, under the leadership of former deputy director Dr. Shen Xinxin, the Institute of Technology has convened cross-field groups covering medical, life sciences, biomedicine, and electricity, launched a cell therapy research plan to improve treatment efficiency. After years of hard work, the core technology platform of the cell therapy industry has become more mature.
Reduce labor costs and make cell therapy technology more accurate
"The members of the R&D team include professionals such as medical examiners, nurses, pharmacists, etc., but they are not frontline clinical medical personnel. Therefore, when choosing indications, the participation of doctors is crucial to assist the team in interpreting data and finding possible effects." Wang Yuqi is very grateful to many medical centers from south to north for their willingness to provide assistance.
Internationally, the United States and Japan are the best in cell therapy; although Taiwan is not a leader in single technology, it has the highest degree of integration.For example, the Industrial Research Institute combines expertise in the fields of biomedical and mechanical engineering, and cooperates with Taiwanese precision processing industry across the domain to target the development of automation systems and robotic arms, so that the cell culture process can be operated accurately."Cell culture is a "handi industry" that invests in many high-end talents, but no matter how cautious people are, people are still the biggest source of pollution. Through the technology platform, it can not only reduce labor costs by one-third, but also work at night. Using robotic arm operations can ensure that the process remains sterile."
Wang Yuqi said that the information collected by the team is mainly Asians, and in the future, it will integrate data from different races to build a stronger platform.Taking into account the scale of the Taiwan market, the team also retains scalability when designing the platform. Similar to Lego modules can be selected and expanded according to needs.This design not only prevents the drug and medical practitioners from going to the wrong way, but also promotes resource sharing among the operators. In-depth research on the cells they develop will help promote the vigorous development of the field of regenerative medicine.
