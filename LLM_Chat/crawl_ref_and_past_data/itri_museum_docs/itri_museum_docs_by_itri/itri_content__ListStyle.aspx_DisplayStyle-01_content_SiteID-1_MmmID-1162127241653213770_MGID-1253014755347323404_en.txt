Technology introduction
Powered fuel cell stack.
Through material design and operation logic development, the performance of the stack unit will be improved to a comparable or even surpass the international level.In the future, the hydrogen-energy mobile vehicle market with long-term endurance and long-range transportation will be introduced, including 3.5-ton hydrogen-energy electric vehicle vehicles, hydrogen-energy electric buses, etc.
Application scope
Establishing a design of low-current resistance autonomous large-area power stack unit
The single stack design power is ≧ 40kW (53hp), and the output can be up to 100kW through multi-stack integration, which is sufficient for continuous operation of logistics/bus vehicles.
Carbon reduction benefits
It is estimated that fuel cell powered vehicles will replace existing fuel buses/logistics vehicles (domestic 5% replacement rate, about 2,000 vehicles) by 2030, and the carbon reduction will reach 127,500 tons/year.
