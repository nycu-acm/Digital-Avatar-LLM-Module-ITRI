Service Description
Power module testing laboratory.
The Power Module Testing Laboratory of the Institute of Technology provides testing and verification services for power modules.All tests comply with international standards, including IEC-60747 and automotive standards AEC-Q101 (US regulations) and AQG-324 (European regulations).The test projects include dynamic and static testing and complete reliability testing, and are also the only unit in the country that provides the testing capabilities of large power modules.
