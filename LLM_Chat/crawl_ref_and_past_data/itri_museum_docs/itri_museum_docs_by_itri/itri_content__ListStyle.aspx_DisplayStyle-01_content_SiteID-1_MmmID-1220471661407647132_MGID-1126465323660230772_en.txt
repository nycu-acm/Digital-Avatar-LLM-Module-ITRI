Multi-dimensional temperature control bonding technology.
Technology introduction
The multi-dimensional temperature-controlled bonding process is completed in a vacuum environment by combining the airbag and the temperature-controlled mold.
It can correspond to the fitting requirements of the fitting curvature Rx 1,800mm/Ry 1,800mm and the fitting curvature Rx 60mm/Ry20mm. The fitting height is ≤150mm and the fitting accuracy is ±500μm.
The dual-platform design can correspond to the concave, convex and S-type fitting production processes.
Application and benefits
Build any form display and sensing manufacturing verification facilities to fill the gap in the multi-dimensional curved surface fitting process of panels and module factories.
Assist new innovation and small and medium-sized enterprises in innovative product development, drive the adjustment of production lines of panel and sensing industries and develop new customer business opportunities.
