Technology introduction
Ultrafast laser glass welding light engine module.
The laser light knife engine developed by the Institute of Technology has included a variable beam length cutting module and a continuous variable length welding module.After the laser beam is focused through the module, a linear beam with variable length is formed at the focus point and the linear beam length can be adjusted without segmentation. It is matched with domestic ultra-fast laser source and wavelength conversion, so that the laser long light knife can cover all the material layers of the object to be processed, cutting or welding multi-layer materials in one pass.
Application and benefits
This technology can be applied to fine processing of multi-layer hard and brittle materials such as vehicle panels, transparent displays, sensors and 5G heterogeneous integration.
