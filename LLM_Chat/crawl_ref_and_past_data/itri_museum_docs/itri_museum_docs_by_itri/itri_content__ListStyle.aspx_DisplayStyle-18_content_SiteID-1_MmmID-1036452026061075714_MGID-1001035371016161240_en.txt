Life-saving light in the dark and evil fire
Writing/Editorial Department
The Institute of Technology has won the Edison Award, known as the "Oscar of Innovation" for four times!The "Fluid-driven Emergency Rescue Technology" developed by the Institute of Technology combines the water columns and lighting for disaster relief, allowing firefighters to shoot out strong light to illuminate the fire while extinguishing the fire, greatly improving safety and rescue efficiency. Therefore, they won the silver award of the "Commercial Safety Category" of the Edison Award.
The Institute of Technology and Research Institute won the Edison Award for the fourth time for "Fluid-driven Emergency Rescue Technology", and was awarded by Liao Ronghuang, manager of the Institute of Green Energy and Environment.
In the fire scene in the movie, I saw orange-red flames rushing to the sky, but the fire scene in reality is not as bright as the movie. When it is not completely burned, most of them are dense smoke, and firefighters must lie on the ground to find a way.With headlights, chest lamps and portable lamps alone, the lighting effect is quite limited. In addition, the equipment on the body weighs 30 kilograms. A search and rescue operation that is always fought for every second is also a test of the life safety of firefighters.
"Since the fire environment is so harsh, what can the Institute of Technology Research and Technology do?" Liao Ronghuang, manager of the Institute of Green Energy and Environment of the Institute of Technology Research and Technology, began to think that what is needed most in a fire scene is water and lighting. If two functions can be combined, the symbiosis concept of water and light can be created to create disaster relief tools, which can not only improve disaster relief efficiency, but also ensure the safety of firefighters. Therefore, "Fluid-driven emergency rescue technology" was born.
Water and electricity symbiosis, water comes with lights
"Fluid-driven emergency rescue technology" uses the principle of hydropower symbiosis to improve the fire protection equipment "sight" to become "fire sight light".The shell of the sight lamp is heat-resistant plastic and can be assembled on the fire sight head in 30 seconds. An aluminum metal turbine is installed in the device. When the water column is emitted, the turbine will rotate to generate electricity and light it and can store excess electrical energy.At present, the sight light has been improved to the fourth generation, weighing only 875 grams, the LED light can have a illumination of up to 75,000 candlelight, and can be equipped with a thermal imager to help find fire points.
The technology has won many international awards and won patents from more than 10 countries. It has won the favor of Akron Bass, the largest fire-fighting equipment manufacturer in the United States and Amazon, the world's largest e-commerce website. It has also received inquiry orders from international fire-fighting manufacturers TFT and Lions Product.At present, this technology has been actually introduced into 10 counties and municipal governments across Taiwan and has been adopted by nearly 100 fire brigades, and has accumulated more than a thousand fire scenes to verify.
Frank, executive director of the Edison Award. Frank Bonafilia said that this technology developed by the Institute of Technology can be expanded to the world, protect the lives of firefighters and improve public safety.After winning the Edison Award, the Industrial Research Institute hopes to expand more applications in the future and save more lives in the critical fire scene of 100,000.
