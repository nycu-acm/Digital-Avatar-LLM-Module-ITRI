The "2022 Trusted AI International Seminar" of the Institute of Industrial Research is on the scene. Experts analyze AI risk management, AI joint learning, and AI ethical characteristics analysis

Date: 2022/08/02
The Institute of Industrial Research and Research held the "2022 Trusted AI International Symposium" for the first time today (2nd), gathering experts from home and abroad in the field of AI to participate in the event. In turn, it was Zhang Peiren, Vice President of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, Tang Feng, Executive Yuan Government Affairs Committee, Lu Zhenghua, Director of the Bureau of Industry, Li Yujie, Deputy Secretary of the National Science and Technology Committee, Elham Tabassi, Chief of Staff of NIST Information Technology Laboratory, Jeannette M. Wing, Vice President of Columbia University, Du Yijin, founder of Taiwan Artificial Intelligence Laboratory, and Huang Weizhong, Deputy Director of the Institute of Information and Communications of the Institute of Technology Research and Research.
From Google search engines, Facebook post promotions, Netflix video recommendations, to self-driving cars and innovative drones, AI algorithms are everywhere.In order to master AI systems such as machine learning and deep learning, and manage the risks brought by AI artificial intelligence, the world has gradually attached importance to the issue of "trusted AI", and countries have begun to incorporate AI governance into regulations.With the support of the Ministry of Economics and Industrial Bureau, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research held the "2022 International Workshop on Trustworthy AI" for the first time today (2nd), gathering experts in the field of AI at home and abroad to discuss issues such as AI risk management and trustworthy AI systems, and attracting more than 500 experts to sign up for the event.Executive Yuan Government Affairs Committee Member Tang Feng also praised that the Trusted AI seminar is a good start and will help promote the standardization of trusted AI and accelerate the development of my country's AI digital technology.
Executive Yuan Government Affairs Committee member Tang Feng said that AI is like the relationship between Doraemon and Nobita, and the two complement each other.AI can actually be understood as Assistive Intelligence, rather than completely replacing humans.Just like AI is the auxiliary position, human beings are the main body.She pointed out that in some places, technologies such as AI are in conflict with democratic systems, while Taiwan focuses on promoting diversified cooperation to allow technology and democracy to flourish together.We hope to exchange AI governance thinking in advanced democratic countries through this seminar, accelerate the development of AI and trustworthy AI digital technology, and practice 2030 to achieve the goal of innovative, inclusive and sustainable smart country.
Lu Zhenghua, director of the Industrial Bureau of the Ministry of Economics, pointed out that as AI artificial intelligence technology and applications mature, it is necessary to pay attention to how to manage or supervise the risks brought by artificial intelligence.For example, the US government launched the "American AI Initiative" in 2019 and the "Artificial Intelligence Application Regulatory Guidelines" in 2020, both of which listed the guidelines for trustworthy AI as their focus.He stressed that in the future, he will plan to cooperate with national laboratories such as NIST in the United States and LNE in France, link relevant technical specifications and standards of the United States and the European Union, and assist Taiwan's AI products to meet international common standards and requirements, and become an internationally trusted AI partner.
Zhang Peiren, deputy director of the Institute of Technology Research, further pointed out that the increasing popularity of AI applications has also triggered the society to discuss ethical issues, including data bias, privacy protection and security, system transparency, interpretability and other issues.For example, the "Eye Medical Imaging Assisted Diagnosis AI Technology" that the Institute of Technology just won the Edison Award this year is to use AI analysis to quickly identify and determine the severity of the lesion within 3 seconds, and can mark the location of different diseases, so the auxiliary doctor can quickly explain to the patients.Therefore, if these materials can be provided with information exchanges between different hospitals under the premise of privacy protection and security, joint learning will be able to push up a new level of AI medical care in Taiwan.
This seminar is even rare to invite Elham Tabassi, chief of staff of the Information Technology Laboratory of the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST). She also shared that NIST's AI risk management tools can make AI reasoning as fair, safe, and even explainable as possible under the basis of trustworthy AI models; Jeannette M. Wing, vice president of Columbia University, believes that the trustworthiness of AI systems can be increased with the expansion of trustworthy computing architecture.In addition, there are also experts such as Du Yijin, founder of Taiwan AI Laboratory, Li Yujie, deputy secretary of the National Science and Technology Commission, Xu Yongzhen, professor of the Department of Assets and Workers of National Taiwan University, Li Weibin, CEO of Hon Hai Research Institute, Zhou Shangwei, chief engineer of SHIELD, Lin Zengyao, director of the National Weights and Measurement Laboratory and CEO of the Measuring Center of the Institute of Engineering Research Institute, and Hua Kailong, deputy director of the Institute of Engineering Research Institute, to share the development trend of trustworthy AI, discuss domestic and foreign experiences, and jointly discuss the development blueprint for Taiwan's integration into the global trustworthy AI ecosystem.
The Institute of Technology has drawn up the "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" to plan the field of artificial intelligence technology for intelligent enabling technology, and hopes to support innovative systems and application services developed in the three major application fields of smart life, healthy and happy life, and sustainable environment.This year, we will focus on forward-looking research and development of "trusted AI" related technologies, hoping to develop trustworthy AI technologies, and even drive the industry to promote more application possibilities, and strengthen my country's industrial strength.
The "2022 International Workshop on Trustworthy AI" of the Institute of Technology Research and Research explores trustworthy AI issues that have attracted worldwide attention. It hopes to promote trustworthy AI standardization and accelerate the development of my country's AI digital technology. The seminar attracts more than 500 people from Europe, America, Germany, Hong Kong, Japan, Singapore, Malaysia, Saudi Arabia and other countries to register online.
