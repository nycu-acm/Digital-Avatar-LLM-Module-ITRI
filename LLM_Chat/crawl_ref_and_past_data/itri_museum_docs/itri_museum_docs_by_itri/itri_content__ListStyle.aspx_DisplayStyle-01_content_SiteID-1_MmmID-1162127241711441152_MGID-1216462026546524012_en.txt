Technology introduction
Solid amine adsorbent.
Traditional wet absorption method - amine solvent carbon capture, due to the solvent evaporation heat and sensible heat, there is a problem of excessive energy consumption.Developing solvent-free solid amine adsorbent technology can complete regeneration below 100 °C, which is conducive to reducing capture energy consumption. Using the high specific surface area and -OH group-rich of porous silicon substrates, the adsorption capacity and stability are improved using polyamine modification technology.
Application scope
It is applied to low concentration CO2 situations and still has excellent adsorption capacity (1-2 mmol CO2/g adsorbent).
It has advantages in the low-concentration CO2 exhaust carbon capture of VOC thermal decomposition furnaces, Local Scrubber and natural gas combustion in the semiconductor industry.
Carbon reduction benefits
The Institute of Technology is conducting the design of a solid adsorbent capture system, and will use a double tower fixed bed to conduct program development and design to evaluate technical and economic feasibility. The system scale is designed to capture 200 kg of CO2 per year.Taking a domestic chemical factory as an example, the annual emission of about 68,000 metric tons of CO2 is about 68,000 metric tons of CO2 if 90% CO2 is captured, 61,000 metric tons of CO2 can be captured annually.
