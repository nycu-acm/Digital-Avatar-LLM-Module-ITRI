Solar Energy + Wireless Communications Institute of Industrial Research Institute's "Green Energy Antenna Technology" won the SIA Award
Text/Ding Jialin
The Institute of Technology recently won the Outstanding Group Award from the British solar energy authoritative media "Angel Business Communication Ltd."This award is mainly awarded to solar technology companies, and the recipients of the award are solar cell-related products. But in fact, the technology of the Institute of Technology is not a pure solar product.Green energy antenna is an innovative technology with the "thin-shaped high-gain antenna" originally developed by the Institute of Technology, integrating the power storage technology of solar panels, becoming an innovative technology with environmentally friendly concepts.If this technology is used, the common mobile phone base stations can not only transform from a cold and hard iron frame to an aesthetic structure, but also use solar panels to provide part of the power required to achieve the goal of energy saving.
The 2011 Solar Industry Awards (SIA), one of the most authoritative awards in the energy field, was recently announced in Hamburg, Germany by the organizer, the authoritative British solar energy media "Angel Business Communication Ltd." With the support of the Science and Technology Project of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology won the favor of the jury for its "green energy antenna technology", stood out from the fierce competition of 2,000 cases, and won the ITRI as Excellent Organization.It is as famous as advanced solar technology companies such as 3M, Oerlikon Solar, and REC, creating excellent results in solar technology research and development. Director Wu Chengwen of the Institute of Technology received the awards at the 26th European Photovoltaic Solar Energy Conference and Exhibition (EU PVSEC).
In the fierce competition of various technologies around the world, the Industrial Research Institute defeated all the experts with the "green energy antenna technology" that promotes the integration of the two major industries of solar energy and wireless communications. David Ridsdale, editor-in-chief of SIA organizer Angel Business Communication Ltd., highly praised the technology of the Institute of Technology.He said that the Institute of Technology and Research from Taiwan has a glorious history and inter-field integrated research, so the jury believes that this technology will help promote the development of solar photoelectric technology and will have a significant impact on society and the solar photoelectric industry, thus standing out and winning the Outstanding Group Award.
Wu Chengwen, director of the Institute of Information Technology and Communications, who received the award, said that the Institute of Information Technology and Communications Technology spared no effort in the research and development of computer and communication system technology.This time, with excellent cross-field results, it integrates the technology energy of antenna and solar photovoltaic panels, creates technology that can take into account the efficiency of antenna and solar panels, and develops advanced green communication equipment energy-saving technology leading the world.In the future, we will continue to develop newer production solutions unremittingly. This technology is currently seeking technology transfer manufacturers and international cooperation partners.
Wu Chengwen pointed out that the award-winning green energy antenna technology, with the thin high-gain antenna developed by the Institute of Technology, successfully integrates antenna transmission and solar panel power storage technology, which can take into account the efficiency of solar photoelectric conversion and antenna reception and emission efficiency.It can not only improve signal intensity and reduce transmission power, but also reduce the antenna size and thickness, but also accumulate electrical energy at the same time.Small handheld mobile devices (PDA, mobile phone, personal navigation devices, etc.), medium and large base station antennas can be widely used.In the future, if this green energy antenna technology replaces the existing 3G base station (energy consumption is estimated to be about 500W), it is expected to provide 30% of the auxiliary power, effectively slowing down the load on the power network during the daytime power consumption peak.
The Solar Industry Awards, the most prestigious solar industry award, is sponsored by Angel Business Communication Ltd., a senior professional media group in the UK. It is sponsored by the "Global Solar Industry Alliance" (PV Group), composed of major global solar photovoltaic industry leaders, the "International Photovoltaic Equipment Association" (IPVEA), which represents European and American photovoltaic manufacturers and suppliers, the Dutch solar photovoltaic industry research organization SolarPlaza, and Loughborough University in the UK.It is one of the most authoritative awards in the solar energy industry. Since its establishment in 2009, a jury consists of the magazine's editor-in-chief and experts in the field of solar photoelectricity, and each year selects the most innovative and influential solar products, creativity, organizations, etc. to praise it.In 2010, he was the well-known British space technology and solar energy research and development company TS Space Systems, and in 2009, he was the leader of the solar photovoltaics industry.
