105 Annual "Academician of the Institute of Technology" Conference
Group photo of the Fifth Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (105 years)
Time: November 1, 2015
Location: Conference Room 604, Hall 51, No. 195, Section 4, Zhongxing Road, Zhudong Town, Hsinchu County (Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research)
Chairman: Academician Shi Zhenrong
The 2010 2019 Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Industrial Research was held in the ZTE Institute of Industrial Research and was chaired by Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of Zhirong Group.The theme of the conference focused on opening up opportunities and strategies for innovative services to the international market. The conference also held dialogues and discussions on topics such as how to develop the international market with Taiwanese characteristics or local advantages, how to use policy power to cultivate soft and hard integration talents, and establish an energy-based innovation system.The academicians attending the meeting included Shi Chongtang, Chairman of Asus Group, Chen Xingshi, Chairman of Ronggang, Chen Xianzhang, Vice President of Changchun Group General Management Office, Lu Zhiyuan, General Manager of Wanghong Electronics/ Founder and Chairman of Xinquan Technology, Shi Min, Academician of China Research Institute, Hong Zhenhai, Chairman of Ruhong Enterprise, Lin Genghua, Academician of China Research Institute, Song Gongyuan, Chairman of Guangbao Group, Hu Zhengming, Outstanding Professor of TSMC at the University of California, and Sun Hong, Chairman of Mengli Automation, etc., and a total of 11 academicians participated.In addition, the attendees were Fu Weixiang, Director of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics, Wu Zhengzhong, Chairman of the Institute of Technology Research and others.
