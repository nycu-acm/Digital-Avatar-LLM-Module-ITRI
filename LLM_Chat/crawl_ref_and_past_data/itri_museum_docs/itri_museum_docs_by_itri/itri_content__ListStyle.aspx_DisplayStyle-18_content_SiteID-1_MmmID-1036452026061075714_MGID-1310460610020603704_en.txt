Omen diagnostic technology achieves predictive maintenance
Written by Tu Xinyi
The key tool for smart manufacturing
"Obsolute Diagnostic Technology and Application Services" effectively avoids sudden downtime, improves equipment productivity and availability, and significantly reduces maintenance costs, and has great industrial expansion space and commercial value.
Faced with the wave of smart manufacturing and industrial needs, smart transformation of equipment maintenance has become an inevitable trend.The Industrial Research Institute leads the industry and launches "Obsolute Diagnosis Technology and Application Services".Its three core technologies - health evaluation, life expectancy, and fault diagnosis, successfully elevating traditional preventive maintenance to predictive maintenance. This technology has been successfully introduced into many categories of manufacturing operators, not only showing high industrial diffusion and huge commercial potential, but also won the Gold Medal for Industrialization Contribution Award.
Under the sweeping trend of global smart manufacturing, the Industrial Research Institute has successfully created a core technology "omen diagnosis system" that allows the equipment to "self-diagnose and predict lifespan".The system combines vibration analysis logic and AI architecture to realize automated fault judgment and can accurately identify common problems such as shaft imbalance, bearing damage, gear abnormality, etc. In this way, the equipment no longer needs to be "repaired when it is broken", but can actively warn and maintain it in advance, truly implementing smart diagnosis, and injecting key momentum into Taiwan's smart manufacturing.
25 years of hard work, forecasting and maintenance leads the new industry situation
"In the past, factories relied heavily on handheld instruments for measurement and judgment in factory prevention and maintenance. However, these valuable experiences are only in the human mind. Once the experts retire or lack of manpower, will the entire maintenance process be at risk of interruption?"Wang Junjie, head of the Institute of Mechanical and Mechanical Systems of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, talked about the original intention of "obstructive diagnostic technology and application services", and pointed out this lingering question.
With the accelerated evolution of Industry 4.0 and smart manufacturing, Wang Junjie knew that predictive maintenance operations must also be upgraded simultaneously.Therefore, he resolutely devoted himself to the "Obsolute Diagnosis" research, focusing on sensor data analysis and the development of intelligent algorithms, aiming to accurately identify the damage status of key equipment such as motors, bearings, gears, etc., and present clear and easy-to-understand diagnostic results through an intuitive software interface. Since joining the Industrial Research Institute in 2008, he has been committed to the industrialization of technology.He led the team to go deep into major industrial sites, and after years of field visits and rigorous technical verification, he finally created a set of universal prediction and maintenance tool "Obsolute Diagnosis System" suitable for various rotary machinery and equipment such as motors, gearboxes, generators, etc.
Three smart modules to create instant prediction and diagnosis
The "Observation Diagnosis System" consists of three core modules: health evaluation, life expectancy and fault diagnosis.This solution emphasizes instant grasp of the equipment status, accurately predicting the operating lifespan, and intelligently judging the causes of abnormalities, so as to change maintenance operations from passive response to active "taking action in advance".
Wang Junjie further explained that "health evaluation" is that through the vibration sensor installed on the equipment, the system can continuously collect key data such as vibration speed, acceleration, spectrum, etc., and build a "health index model" based on this. This is not just a simple data monitoring, but also like a "health window" that can understand the machine language, which can understand the machine's condition.
In terms of "life expectation", the system will predict the future health trend of the equipment based on the equipment operation data, set up a warning threshold, and notify maintenance needs in advance, so that maintenance operations no longer rely on fixed cycles or temporary disposal, but enter a new rhythm of "appointment maintenance".
Finally, when the health indicators are below the safety value, the system will immediately automatically activate the "Fault Diagnosis" function, which can provide specific abnormal interpretation results and assist on-site personnel in quickly responding and making accurate maintenance decisions.
From tools to value to become a driving force for industrial upgrading
The birth of "Obsolute Diagnostic Technology and Application Services" has enabled manufacturers of widely used rotary machinery and equipment to become the most direct beneficiaries.This technology can not only accurately predict the time of failure, effectively avoid sudden downtime, significantly improve equipment productivity and availability, but also greatly reduce maintenance costs. It no longer depends on regular maintenance frequency and avoid excessive repairs or waste of spares.On the other hand, this technology also strengthens the transparency of equipment status, allowing the health index of each machine to be visualized. Managers can instantly grasp the equipment status through the remote end, and can even further integrate it into the manufacturing execution system (MES) or monitoring control and data acquisition (SCADA) system to achieve a comprehensive digital transformation.
The omen diagnostic system has been deeply rooted in the industry for many years, and Wang Junjie firmly believes that the application potential of this technology is far more than the role of "repair tool".Due to the huge number of rotary machinery and equipment worldwide, the annual output is as high as 3 billion to 5 billion, which makes this technology have great industrial expansion space and commercial value.
"The proportion of rotary machinery and equipment in the overall equipment industry exceeds 70%, and the demand is amazing." Wang Junjie explained that because of this, many equipment manufacturers are closely cooperating with the Industrial Research Institute to actively promote the integration of software and hardware, and further create a complete value-added product solution."If equipment manufacturers only sell hardware, they are prone to falling into price competition; but if they can add intelligent functions such as omen diagnosis, the value of the product will immediately leap, and their competitiveness will naturally increase significantly." Wang Junjie affirmed that this upgrade model of soft and hard integration has become an important key technology for Taiwan's smart manufacturing transformation, and omen diagnosis system is undoubtedly an upgrade tool in the industrial value chain.
From academic research in the laboratory to close connection and implementation with the industry, the development of omen diagnostic systems is not only a major breakthrough in software technology, but also an important milestone established by Taiwan in the field of smart manufacturing.Through this smart maintenance solution with international competitiveness, Taiwan’s manufacturing industry is opening a new high-value development curve.
