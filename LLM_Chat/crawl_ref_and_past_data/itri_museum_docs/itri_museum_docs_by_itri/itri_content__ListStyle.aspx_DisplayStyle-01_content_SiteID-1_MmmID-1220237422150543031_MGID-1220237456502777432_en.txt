Technology introduction
Sustainable energy system.
The industry's first permanent cycle power solution will allow the power unit to enter the permanent cycle and use throughout the cycle, from on-board electricity to the power supplement system, and finally to the energy storage system and the power grid. Each stage can be fully utilized in combination with different batteries of new, old and different types, thereby extending the system life.
In addition, this plan regards power sources from all parties (such as solar energy, batteries, etc.) as electrical energy storage units (LVPUs) of different sizes. Using dynamic power adjustment technology, different power units can be aggregated (MSPA) more elastically and quickly to generate the required AC power supply system, reducing energy conversion losses and reducing construction costs by 30%.
In addition, the power units can be quickly separated and reconstructed according to demand, corresponding to the power consumption forms and needs of multiple parties, achieving the best economic benefits of sharing.At present, Taiwan's CNPC Smart Green Energy Demonstration Station (including electric vehicle operating environments such as charging, battery swap and energy storage) has been actually introduced to provide key technologies and services for net zero emissions.
Application and benefits
This system is a set of sustainable cycling electrical energy solutions, from mobile power consumption (such as electric vehicles), turnover power consumption (such as electric energy supplement system) to dispatched power consumption (such as energy storage systems and power grids), which can greatly extend the service life of different power units, reduce energy losses and reduce construction costs by 30%.
Through dynamic adjustment of power energy technology, different power units can be aggregated or separated more elastically and quickly to support the demand scheduling of virtual power plants.
