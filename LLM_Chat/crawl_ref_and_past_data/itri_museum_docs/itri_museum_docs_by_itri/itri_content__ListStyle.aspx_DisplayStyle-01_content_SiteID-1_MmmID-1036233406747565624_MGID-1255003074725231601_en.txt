The president of the Institute of Technology and Research signed a cooperation agreement with Tokyo University of Technology. In the future, the two parties will focus more on cooperation in semiconductors, net zero emissions, biotechnology and medical care, and new ventures.
The Institute of Technology has celebrated its 50th anniversary.In order to deepen Taiwan-Japan relations and expand cooperation, it has signed a cooperation agreement with Tokyo University of Technology to strengthen cooperation between the two parties in many fields such as semiconductors, net zero emissions, biotechnology and medical care, and new ventures.The signing of this contract was personally hosted by Liu Wenxiong, President of the Institute of Technology and Iichiya, President of the University of Technology of Tokyo, marking a new milestone in cooperation between the two sides.The Institute of Technology and Tokyo Institute of Technology has a history of cooperation for more than 20 years and has achieved fruitful results in innovative materials and semiconductor technologies in the past. The cooperation has expanded to more areas and will help solve global challenges, especially technological innovation in the fields of semiconductors and net zero emissions.
President of Tokyo University of Technology, Ichiya, expressed high expectations for this cooperation, pointing out that the cooperation not only continues the existing cooperation in the fields of semiconductors and materials, but will also expand to biotechnology and medical care and new ventures, further strengthening the influence of both parties in global scientific and technological innovation.Given that Tokyo University of Technology and Tokyo Medical Dental University are integrated into the "Tokyo University of Science", the cooperation between the two parties will be further deepened, especially in the fields of semiconductors and biomedicine.
This cooperation agreement and the first joint seminar demonstrate the firm commitment of the Institute of Technology and the Tokyo University of Technology in promoting scientific and technological innovation and strengthening cooperation between Taiwan and Japan. In the future, the two sides will lay a more solid foundation for the development of innovative technologies in Taiwan and Japan through technological exchanges and results sharing.
