Technology introduction
Electrolytic aquatic hydrogen production equipment.
This technology develops a alkaline membrane electrolytic hydrogen production technology that takes into account high efficiency and low cost.Green hydrogen can be produced by integrating green electricity and power grids, and integrated green electricity and electrolytic hydrogen production, so that green hydrogen can be produced.Technology research and development includes key low-cost nickel-based catalyst materials, high-efficiency membrane electrode group design, voltage-resistant stack assembly design and low-energy-consuming system integration technology.
Application scope
Low-cost nickel-based materials, cost savings of 90% over precious metal catalysts.
High electrolytic efficiency >80%, which is 70-80% higher than traditional electrolytic efficiency.
The carbon emissions of gray hydrogen are as high as 10-25 kg CO2/kg H2. It is estimated to produce 1 million tons of hydrogen, and the carbon reduction benefits of green hydrogen are >10 million tons of CO2. Calculated at the carbon weight price of 50 USD/ton, the market value of carbon weight exceeds NT$15 billion.
Carbon reduction benefits
The key technologies are independently developed by the Institute of Industrial Research and Research. The relevant technical indicators have reached international standards and can be applied to steel, petrochemical, power, semiconductor industries, etc. In the future, through the development of independent research and development technology, it can promote investment in domestic related materials, modules, and system manufacturers.
