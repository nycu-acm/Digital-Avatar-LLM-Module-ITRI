Technology introduction
Instant high-realistic 3D interactive display system.
The high-realistic 3D interactive system combines a multi-combination large mini-LED display.Taking the angular 3D display as an example, it is easy to place indoors and is corrected in combination with the technology developed by the Institute of Technology to highlight the nude vision 3D imaging effect.Then, based on AI algorithms as the core of development, real-time image backing and image fusion are carried out through image processing technology.Through the introduction of this technical solution, the high-realistic 3D interactive system is the world's first service solution that can live broadcast, gesture interaction, message transmission, and then transmit 3D emoji.
Features and innovation
Import generative AI technology to optimize image content, quickly convert 2D images into 3D object models, presenting more realistic visual effects.
Application and benefits
Suitable for live live broadcasts, long-distance interaction, long-distance conferences, product displays, and product details, such as: new product releases, media communications, museum cultural relics displays, and international exhibitions to negotiate in other places.
