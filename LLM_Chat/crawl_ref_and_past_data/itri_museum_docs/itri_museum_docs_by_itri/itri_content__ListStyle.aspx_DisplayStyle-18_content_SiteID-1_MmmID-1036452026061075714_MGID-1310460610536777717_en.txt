Key to Innovation
3DGS Digital Twin Innovation Engine
Written by Tu Xinyi
3D modeling popularizes diverse applications
From taking photos to modeling only takes a few minutes, 3D modeling technology is no longer out of reach!The Institute of Technology and Research has created low-threshold, fast realistic modeling tools with innovative 3DGS technology, combining the concept of digital twins, allowing everyone to become a 3D creator, reshape a new pattern of digital twins.
With the rapid evolution of AI and computing technology, 3D modeling has gradually moved from high-end technology that was exclusive to movie special effects and game scenes in the past to the mainstream stage of commercial applications, redefining the interaction between enterprises and customers, and is a key tool to improve corporate marketing efficiency and technical persuasion.
Based on the 3D Gaussian Splating (3D GS) technology that has emerged in recent years, and combined with the Digital Twin concept, the Institute of Technology further connects high-realistic 3D models with the physical and virtual, becoming the key foundation for AI training, real-time simulation and smart application. It not only breaks the obstacles of high modeling costs, high thresholds and limited mimicry, but also drives technological innovation in the fields of business exhibitions, engineering monitoring, cultural and creative entertainment, and accelerates all walks of life into a new era of digital transformation!
From "technology" to "application" to make 3D modeling popular
The Industrial Research Institute has developed 3DGS technology, making 3D modeling no longer a patent for professionals and a "civilian technology" that ordinary people can also use.
In 2023, the team of the Industrial Research Institute noticed that e-commerce and department stores at home and abroad have tried to present products in 3D to increase purchasing desire and conversion rate."But traditional 3D modeling relies on manual construction by professional art talents. It takes several days to build a product. It is not only time-consuming and costly, but also difficult to popularize quickly." Therefore, the team of the Industrial Research Institute began to invest in the field of 3D modeling, hoping to find a more efficient and simpler way for e-commerce.
"Initially we explored the potential of Neuroradiation Field Technology (NeRF) in 3D modeling, but the emergence of 3D Gaussian Splatting (3DGS) technology has made our eyes shine." Wang Enci, head of the Information and Communications Research Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that 3DGS technology can directly convert shot images into highly realistic 3D models, and the time is greatly shortened to within 30 minutes to 1 hour. This completely reverses the content production process of the 3D industry and officially starts the civilian era of 3D modeling.
Wang Enci further explained that 3DGS no longer relies on triangular grid modeling, but simulates light and shadow and object shapes in space through a large number of "Gaussian elliptical spheres". Users only need to take multi-angle photos, and the system can automatically generate realistic 3D models, with large time compressed, and the picture is lifelike and the details are exquisite.
One-stop 3D modeling application for e-commerce, disaster prevention to cultural and creative industries
After mastering the technical principles, Wang Enci worked with the team to think about how to "really popularize 3DGS"?Therefore, they have developed diversified modeling tools and auxiliary technologies for different application situations, so that 3D modeling is no longer the patent of professionals, but the "civilian technology" that ordinary people can also learn.
In the application scenarios of e-commerce and virtual exhibitions, the Industrial Research Institute launched the "low-threshold one-stop 3D modeling tool", which guides users to take photos through AR-assisted shooting interface, and then automatically modeled by AI.The "view angle coverage density calculation" function has even been further developed, which can recommend the next photo location based on the dynamics of the captured images, so that the modeling quality and efficiency can be improved simultaneously.
Not only that, in order to solve the problem of "reshooting and reconstruction" in the past, the team also developed "reshooting and fusion technology".Users only need to reshoot images for missing areas, and the system can integrate new and old models to accurately repair defects, saving time and cost significantly.
In the application scenarios of disaster prevention and civil engineering monitoring, the team further optimized the 3DGS process of air shooting. Through improved camera positioning and gradual modeling algorithms, the post-disaster site modeling time is shortened from 24 hours to 30 minutes to generate the initial model, which can be gradually completed. At the same time, the air shooting machine can be matched with other cameras, such as the camera on a mobile phone, and positioned through different camera perspectives. In architectural scenes, in addition to viewing the entire model from a long distance, the building can also be closer to view the material details.
In the field of interior applications, such as real estate and interior design, 3DGS also provides high-realistic visual effects and instant interactive guides.Consumers can view the house virtually, adjust furniture decorations from a distance, and even communicate decoration needs with designers directly through 3D models, making interior planning more intuitive, time-saving and labor-saving.
Going towards digital twins interactive simulation next mile
Although 3DGS has shown strong advantages in static modeling, several key challenges still need to be overcome to become a true "digital twin technology engine".Wang Enci admitted: "Now 3DGS models are mostly single geometric surfaces. To further interact and simulate with the model, we also need to let the model understand layering, object division, and even change the light and shadow structure according to the action reaction."
Therefore, the Industrial Research Institute has also simultaneously invested in the research and development of scenario segmentation and hierarchy generation.The team integrates 2D mask segmentation technology, scene calibration guided by visual language models, and reverse rendering methods to disassemble light sources and reflections, so that future 3D models can not only "see", but also "interactive", "calculation" and "simulation", and even assist AI robot training.
"Our ultimate goal is to build a complete process from mobile phone shooting, model construction, object repair, scene organization to simulated interaction, and truly realize the seamless integration of 3D modeling and digital twins." Wang Enci said that 3D modeling will not be just a tool for engineers or animators, but a life weapon that everyone can participate, create and apply!
