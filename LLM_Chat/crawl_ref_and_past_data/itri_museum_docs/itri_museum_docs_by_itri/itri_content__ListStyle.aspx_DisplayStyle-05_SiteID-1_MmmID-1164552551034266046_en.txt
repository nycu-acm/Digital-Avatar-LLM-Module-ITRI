Net Zero Perpetual Talent Alliance.
With the intensification of climate change, the world has successively declared that it will achieve the net zero emission target in 2050 and include it in regulations, and Taiwan's industry will face new challenges in carbon reduction; this aided this, after establishing the Net Zero Perpetual Strategy Office, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research established the "Net Zero Perpetual School" in May 2022, and joined hands with key public associations such as the All-China Federation of Industry and Commerce of the Republic of China, the Taiwan Industry and Commerce Association, the Taiwan Electrical and Electronics Industry Association, the Taiwan Machinery Industry Association, the Taiwan Chemical Industry Association, the Taiwan Circuit Board Association, the Taiwan Silk Weaving Industry Association, and the Circular Taiwan Foundation to form the "Net Zero Perpetual Talent Alliance" to play the role of initiatives, strategies and integration promotion from the needs and solutions of net zero talents, and to lead the small, help the Taiwan industry cultivate excellent green-leader talents in the net zero era, establish a net zero carbon reduction solution platform, and promote the momentum of the industry's net zero sustainable upgrade.
