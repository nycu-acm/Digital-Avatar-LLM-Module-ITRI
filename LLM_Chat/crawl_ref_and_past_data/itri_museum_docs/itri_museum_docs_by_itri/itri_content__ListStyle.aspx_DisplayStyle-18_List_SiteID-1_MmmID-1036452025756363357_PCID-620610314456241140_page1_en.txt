As the industry and machinery sectors are facing the wave of digital transformation and intelligent upgrading, motor control and transmission systems are gradually transforming from traditional driving core to a technical center for intelligent manufacturing and automation applications.From robots, self-driving vehicles, drones, electric vehicles, foot-type robots, to advanced industrial equipment, all of which are inseparable from high-efficiency, intelligent, and low-energy consumption drive components and control technologies.Whether it is the automated assembly lines of the manufacturing industry, or emerging application fields such as logistics, agriculture, and medical care, smart machinery and electric vehicles have become important means to improve efficiency and supplement labor.Motor control and transmission systems are the "muscles and nerves" of these smart machines, and they need to have high speed, high precision and intelligent response capabilities at the same time.Taiwan has a solid foundation in the manufacturing of components such as motors, controllers, sensors, etc., but it still faces challenges in system integration and autonomous technology of advanced applications (such as foot robots and unmanned vehicle systems).If artificial intelligence and software control technology can be used well and software, and the software integration design and platform development capabilities can be introduced, Taiwan will have the opportunity to play an important supply chain role in the era of smart electromechanical and electrical engineering, and even create independent brands and systems.

Under the pressure of global climate change and net zero carbon emissions, the machinery industry is transforming from traditional manufacturing to efficient, low-carbon and sustainable smart manufacturing.This transformation is not only for sustainable development, but also to enhance the competitiveness of the industry.In the past, mechanical equipment design mainly considered stability and output efficiency, and paid less attention to carbon emission management.However, in the face of increasingly strict carbon fees, carbon taxes and carbon border adjustment mechanisms, the machinery industry must shift from "passive energy conservation" to "active carbon control" and achieve high-efficiency operation of the overall system through smart technology.The machinery industry will transform from "energy consumption units" to "energy managers" and "carbon efficiency propellers", and promote the development of mechanical technology towards intelligence, electrification, low-carbonization and integration.
