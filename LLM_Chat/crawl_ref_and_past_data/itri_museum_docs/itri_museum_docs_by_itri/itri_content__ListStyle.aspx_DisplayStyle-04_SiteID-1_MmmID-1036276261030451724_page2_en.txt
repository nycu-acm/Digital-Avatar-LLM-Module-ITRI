Recycling waste liquid crystals, all gold
Date: 2020/05/21
The Institute of Technology has developed the "waste LCD panel reuse and treatment system" to roll large panels into pieces, then throw the fragments into large iron barrels, separate the liquid crystal and indium on the glass, and refine them into a transparent conductive film, which can be returned to the panel process and re-used.
