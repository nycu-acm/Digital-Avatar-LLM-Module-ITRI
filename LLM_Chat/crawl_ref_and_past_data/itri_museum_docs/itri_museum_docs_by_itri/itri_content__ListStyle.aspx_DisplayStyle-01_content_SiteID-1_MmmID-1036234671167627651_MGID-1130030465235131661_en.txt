Academician Wu Jincheng
Academician Wu Jincheng is the convener of the Forward-looking Technical Steering Committee of the Institute of Industrial Research, an advisor to the Forward-looking Technical Steering Committee of the Innovation, and a partner of the Science and Technology Fund of Taishan Investment Management Consultant Co., Ltd., specializes in systems, storage, cloud, telecommunications, etc., and has rich experience in the technology and communications industry and startup companies.He is a successful serial entrepreneur and angel investor. Academician Wu’s achievements in the Internet and communications industry have not only become a legend on Wall Street, but have also been selected as one of the “25 top behind-the-scenes heroes on the Internet” by the technology magazine “Interactive Week”.
With his sensitivity to technological trends, Academician Wu successfully started his business in the United States in 4 degrees, creating a total market value of NT$180 billion.In 1995, Arris Networks, the first company, was founded. In just 6 months, it was favored by Cascade Communication and acquired for US$150 million. In 1997, ArrowPoint Communications was founded. In 2000, Netcom was booming and successfully listed on NASDAQ in the United States. Later, it was acquired by Cisco Systems for US$5.7 billion, becoming the second highest-priced acquisition in Massachusetts' history. At the same time, Academician Wu was also invited to serve as vice president of the content exchange technology division of Cisco Systems.
In 2002, Academician Wu had a unique vision and had an insight into "the opportunity of the Internet is on the edge" and founded Acopia Networks 3 times to develop network storage technologies and products, and use information exchange technology to store virtual archives.This is the solution that large companies need most in the era of online information explosion. Therefore, in 2007, it was acquired by F5 Internet Company; in 2008, it was determined that the general trend of mobile devices was adopted. Academician Wu established Azuki Systems to develop software platform, put real-time information, data, and subtitles on TV or online video to create a new visual interaction, and was incorporated into Ericsson in 2014.
With the spirit of continuous innovation and entrepreneurship, Academician Wu was recruited by Taishan Investment from the Executive Yuan Guofa Fund, Industrial Research Institute Innovation Company, and Taiwan Bank.He served as a partner of its Taishan Technology Fund, assisting in connecting emerging key technologies and international resources, using rich connections and successful entrepreneurial experience, and adding value to the invested company. He believes that technology itself is a changing body of continuous transformation, and entrepreneurship is the driving force for economic growth.No matter how new technology is, it will be surpassed by the next wave of breakthrough changes, the focus is on mastering the next wave of key technologies.Therefore, Academician Wu is very concerned about Taiwan's technological transformation and innovation and entrepreneurship environment. He has been in the United States for a long time and has also been invited to serve as an consultant for the Institute of Industrial Research and Research Institute and the Investment Policy Association.He travels to and from Taiwan to the United States regularly, contributes his experience in the Internet era and software-oriented industries, and provides advice on the development direction of Taiwan's next generation of key technologies.
