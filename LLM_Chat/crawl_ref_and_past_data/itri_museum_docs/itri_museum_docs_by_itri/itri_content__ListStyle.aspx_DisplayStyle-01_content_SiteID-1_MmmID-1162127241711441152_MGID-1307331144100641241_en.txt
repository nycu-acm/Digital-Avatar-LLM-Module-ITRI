Technology introduction
Full cycle single polyester material low-carbon shoes.
Use recycled polyester sources to perform the depolymerization/copolymerization process, develop low-carbon rTPEE elastomer raw materials, and adjust the material performance through mixing and modification, so that it can be applied to sports shoe parts requirements.At the same time, the development of single material of all shoes is completed.
Application scope
Using PET as a low-carbon material source, chemical depolymerization is carried out and a one-pot copolymerization technology is constructed to obtain low-carbon emission r-TPEE elastomer materials.
The structure control is used to control its viscoelasticity and rheological properties, so that it has excellent properties such as high rebound, high performance and low compression deformation.
Combined with physical injection foaming, lightweight shoe midsole and insole are obtained, and the anti-slip and wear resistance of r-TPEE is improved through mixing technology and applied to shoe outsole components.
The upper is made of r-PET recycling yarn and integrated braiding process, and finally completed the development of the entire polyester shoe, and it was confirmed that it can be directly crushed and recycled and then made into different shoe parts products, truly achieving carbon reduction and recycling.
Carbon reduction benefits
This technology has completed the material development for the most important midsole and outsole components of all shoes and has entered the trial production scale.In the future, we will achieve benefits through the sale of key raw materials and processed semi-finished products. At the same time, we will also jointly develop related shoe products with domestic shoe brands or luxury brands for sale.To drive domestic footwear manufacturers to join in low-carbon transformation and lay out orders for international mainstream brands.
