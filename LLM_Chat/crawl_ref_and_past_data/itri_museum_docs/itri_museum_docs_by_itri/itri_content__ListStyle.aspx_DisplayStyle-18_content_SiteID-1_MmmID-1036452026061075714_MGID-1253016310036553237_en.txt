The Institute of Technology won the top 100 innovation institutions award in the world for eight times
Writing/Editorial Department
The Institute of Technology and Research has won the Top 100 Innovation Institution Award in the World for 800 times. Patent Energy has performed outstandingly in five major indicators, including technological uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity, and has achieved remarkable innovation results.
Clarivate released the 2024 "Top 100 Global Innovation Institutions" report. The Institute of Technology has won the 8th largest innovation institution award for the world for seven consecutive years.This year, only three research institutions around the world were selected, with one of them being one of them, setting a record for the most awards won by R&D institutions in the Asia-Pacific region.
Clarivate Security Ball’s top 100 innovative institutions report has been actively analyzing the innovation energy of global enterprises or institutions through patent data for a long time, and selecting them based on scientific patent indicators to commend top institutions’ persistence in innovation and their excellent creativity.The Institute of Technology has won the 8th award of the Top 100 Innovation Institutions in the World for 7 consecutive years. Patent Energy has performed outstandingly in five major indicators such as technological uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity. It is comparable to internationally renowned companies ASML, GE, Johnson & Johnson, Siemens, etc., with remarkable innovation results.
Gordon Samson, president of Clarivate Intelligent Property Rights Group, said that congratulations to the Industrial Research Institute for its eighth and seven consecutive years to be selected as the top 100 innovative institutions in the world. It is no easy task to be selected as the top 100 innovative institutions in the world, because maintaining advantages in the innovation ecosystem is more difficult than before.This year, only three R&D units in the world are on the list, and the top research institutes have shown great influence and achievements on the international stage.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that in the face of opportunities and challenges in the new era, the Institute of Technology Research focuses on market-oriented research and development and plans the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint", and provides innovative and market-oriented solutions to the industry with four major application areas such as smart life, healthy and happy life, sustainable environment, and resilient society, as well as intelligent enabling technologies that promote the development of the application field.In addition, through innovative application models such as innovative innovation of new ventures, patents and technical intangible asset financing, and Intellectual Property Innovation Corporation (IPIC), we can innovate value and sustainable management with the industry.
At present, 57 small and medium-sized and new start-up enterprises in China have assisted 57 domestic small and medium-sized enterprises to apply for intangible asset financing, and 28 companies have successfully obtained intangible asset financing of more than 500 million yuan through patent evaluation financing or technology added financing, which has caused countless investment benefits, and actively used smart property links to the technology market and the capital market.
