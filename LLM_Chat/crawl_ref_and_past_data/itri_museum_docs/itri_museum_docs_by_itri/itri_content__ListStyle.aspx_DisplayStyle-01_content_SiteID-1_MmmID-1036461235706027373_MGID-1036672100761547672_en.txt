Introduction
This association handles various domestic and foreign international exhibitions and foreign machinery marketing groups to expand overseas markets to strive for business opportunities, and promotes the completion of TAMINet common platform mechanism and online quotation system in the machinery industry, strengthens the community settlement effect between central factories and satellite manufacturers, and expands the general machinery industry's trading mechanism, machinery industry CE certification, various technical cooperation and exchanges, smart machinery forums and various professional seminars to enhance the competitiveness of the machinery industry.
The Association now has more than 2,700 member manufacturers, and with the most sincere attitude, we provide the most enthusiastic and thoughtful services to the machinery industry peers and domestic and foreign industrial and commercial enterprises.
