Introduction to Net Zero Carbon Inspection Service
Greenhouse gas verification.
In response to the international net zero carbon emission trend, the Greenhouse Gas Verification Agency of the Institute of Industrial Research provides verification services for the greenhouse gas inspection report of the applicant unit based on ISO 14065 and TAF "Certification Standards" of Confirmation and Verification Agency Certification Standards.
The greenhouse gases verified include carbon dioxide (CO2), nitrous oxide (N2O), methane (CH4), hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), perfluorocarbons (PFCs), sulfur hexafluoride (SF6) and nitrogen trifluoride (NF3).
Scope of service
The scope of verification services includes ISO 14064-1 (CNS 14064-1) organizational level greenhouse gas verification (voluntary plan), conduct review of the inspection report, and go to the factory to verify supporting information and use equipment.
The acceptance unit includes all domestic organizations (such as government departments, legal entities, public and private institutions and manufacturers, etc.).
