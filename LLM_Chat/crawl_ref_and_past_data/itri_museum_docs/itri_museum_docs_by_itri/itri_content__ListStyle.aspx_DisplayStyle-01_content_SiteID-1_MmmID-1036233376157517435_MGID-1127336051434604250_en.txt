Technology introduction
PoseFit senior muscle detection lens.
Research by the National Health Center pointed out that "muscle mass" is one of the important keys that affect the elderly's premature aging and death risk.Weak muscle strength has a great impact on engaging in daily activities, including weakness and slowness in human movements and steps, increasing the chance of fractures and falls.The "PoseFit Senior Skin Energy Detection Spectrum" developed by the Institute of Technology has discovered problems with the elderly as soon as possible and provided suggestions for empowerment. It uses 2D dimensional posture recognition technology to perform skeleton extraction, analyzes the various muscle groups, joint angles, and muscle extension degree (Motional tracking dynamic estimation), and completes energy consumption estimation, motion stability, softness and balance evaluation. In turn, we provide empowerment suggestions for body parts and postures that need to be paid attention to when training, improvement or exercise.
Features and innovation
This technology replaces maintenance and maintenance by preventing premature aging, improves the quality of life of older age, and extends smart long-term care services into the community as its goal.The detection movement is designed by professional sports and health care experts, and is also used to study at home and abroad and recommend muscle strength, endurance and softness training suggestions for older people. It provides intuitive and intelligent operations with high ease, so that both operators and elders can easily get started.
