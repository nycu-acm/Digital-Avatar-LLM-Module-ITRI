Smart cockpit driving ECG perception system.
Technology introduction
Develop a driving physiological sensing steering wheel for smart cockpits, integrating a soft coupled ECG sensing system and a full-area coupled sensing electrode for steering wheel.
Through the high-coupling quantity sensing system combined with noise filtering and signal compensation processing technology, driving electronic cardiac signals can be measured at 1.5mm leather, achieving a fast heart rate measurement value.
The heart rate range can be measured by 40~130 BPM and the error value is ≤±3 BPM, and the driving physiological indicators HRV calculation and pressure tension indicator are integrated and calculated.
Application and benefits
It has a non-skin contact physiological monitoring ECG steering wheel and steering wheel cover to monitor driving physiological status to improve driving safety.
In the future, it can be applied to the driving of the smart cockpit and the physiological monitoring of the passengers, as well as smart mobility and smart care.
