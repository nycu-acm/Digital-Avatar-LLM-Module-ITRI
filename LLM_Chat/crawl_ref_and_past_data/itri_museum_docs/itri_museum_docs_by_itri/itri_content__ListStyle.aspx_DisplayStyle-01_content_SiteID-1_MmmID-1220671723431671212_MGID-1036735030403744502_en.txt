Technology introduction
CFRP laser cutting platform and housing construction
The Institute of Technology has developed CFRP laser cutting technology, and can carry out diverse processing applications based on the national production of localized laser processing equipment, integrating domestic brand 6kW laser source, domestic independent laser controller and independent laser cutting heads of the Institute of Technology has developed it.
Features and innovation
Compared with traditional mechanical processing processes, non-contact laser processing has the characteristics of lower processing costs, higher processing efficiency and lower dust pollution; and in response to the harmful gas derivation conditions during laser cutting CFRP, a triple safety protection system is built to ensure the safety of the processing process.
Technical Specifications
Processing types: thermosetting composite materials, thermoplastic composite materials.
Processing materials: carbon fiber composite material, glass fiber composite material, Kevila fiber composite material.
Processing thickness: ≦ 5mm.
Application and benefits
Laser cutting CFRP achieves the characteristics of fast, clean and high degree of freedom, which can solve the problems of tool loss and working dust in traditional processing. It can perform high-speed rough processing for high-hardness CFRP, and then perform high-speed finishing through the milling process. It has high-precision, high production efficiency, low pollution, and digitalization. It is very suitable for high-value industrial applications and solves the processing bottlenecks of traditional domestic composite drilling and milling methods.
Advantages of laser processing
Contactless processing.
No tool wear (reduced processing costs).
There is little dust and contaminated particles.
Low noise.
Laser-cut carbon fiber composite samples.
Lightning milling composite high-speed drilling processing.
