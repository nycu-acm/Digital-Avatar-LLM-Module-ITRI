Technology introduction
Multi-head laser powder bed lamination manufacturing technology (3D printing).
Establish multi-laser synchronous processing technology for laminate manufacturing, break through the limitations of single-head laser aggregation layer manufacturing, can be developed in accordance with the needs of different production areas, and control synchronous processing or partitioned processing in the same area, expand the area that can be made in powder bed aggregation layer manufacturing, establish large-size pattern splicing, continuous scanning and elastic energy distribution, effectively improve the production capacity of laminate manufacturing, and expand industrial applications.
Features and innovation
Layer manufacturing platform module technology (AM100/AM250/AM500).
Layer manufacturing control technology (single laser/four laser).
Flow field design technology for laminate manufacturing.
Technical Specifications
Layer manufacturing technology with more than 2 lasers.
Technical application
Metal components are made, such as: near-shaped waterway molds, heat exchange components/car locomotive components, aerospace lightweight components.
