Technology introduction
Real scene of the new type of mushroom-danced bottle planting.
Traditional greenhouse monitoring systems require facilities such as wire layout and power supply protection, which not only occupies space and is inconvenient to erect, but also has high construction costs.The greenhouse growth monitoring and cultivation system developed by the Institute of Technology uses wireless sensing transmission, which can integrate solar panels for power supply, simplify the complexity of facilities and reduce costs. Features and innovation
High yield three-dimensional cultivation technology
This technology develops a three-dimensional strawberry cultivation system. The three-dimensional cultivation structure can increase the number of strawberry cultivation per unit area and can extend the planting of a variety of crops.The semi-open wetnet room is equipped with local cooling, shade and rain-shading environment control facilities, which can adjust the strawberry yield period and achieve the advantage of extending the strawberry yield season. This system has the ability to produce high yields, and its output is more than three times that of ground cultivation.

Bing Mushroom has the effect of anti-cancer and enhancing immunity, and requires a cultivation environment with high oxygen and high humidity.
