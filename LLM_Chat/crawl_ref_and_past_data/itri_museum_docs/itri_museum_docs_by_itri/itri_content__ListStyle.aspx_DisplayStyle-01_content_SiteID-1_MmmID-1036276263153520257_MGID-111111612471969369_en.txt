Date: 2022/11/16
ASOCIO 2022 ICT Award has 10 member states competing for awards worldwide, and Taiwan won four awards, among which the "iPetWeaR" smart pet collar developed by the Institute of Technology won the "HealthTech Award" award, winning glory for the country. From left: You Mingde, assistant director of Datong World Technology Co., Ltd., Chen Futian, director of the New Taipei City Government Information Center, Shen Baiyan, chairman of the Information Software Association of the Republic of China and the new chairman of ASOCIO, Gao Haojie, general manager of Kaiju International Technology Co., Ltd., and Lin Hongdun, head of the Technology Center of the Service System of the Institute of Technology Research.
Emerging technologies for child care have attracted global attention. The "iPetWeaR" smart pet collar developed by the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs has been recognized by the Asia-Oceanian Computing Industry Organization (ASOCIO), the oldest and most influential international ICT trade organization in the Asia-Pacific region.It was awarded the "HealthTech Award" of the "ASOCIO 2022 ICT Award". At the annual summit where industry officials and academic experts gathered, it demonstrated the innovative R&D capabilities of the Institute of Industry and Research in health care.
ASOCIO 2022 ICT Award has 10 member states competing for awards worldwide. Among the 53 proposed works, the "iPetWeaR" smart pet collar developed by the Ministry of Economic Affairs and Technology with a special science and technology project subsidy for the Industrial Research Institute. It won the "HealthTech Award" award for its technological originality, bringing glory to the country.The Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs actively promotes the development of emerging industries and industrial upgrading and transformation of my country, locks in pet care as a new business opportunity that has rapidly risen in recent years, and supports the Industrial Research Institute to develop the "iPetWeaR" smart pet collar and lay out global pet business opportunities.This technology also won the Innovation Award at the 2022 American Consumer Electronics Exhibition (CES) this year, which not only shows that my country's technological research and development energy has been recognized internationally, but also has unlimited potential in the future electronic consumer market.
Zheng Renjie, CEO of the Service System Science and Technology Center of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that according to the global market insights survey report, in the era of the epidemic, the global pet market grew by 63%, with a market size of up to US$350 billion.The "iPetWeaR" smart pet collar created by the Institute of Technology uses contactless "innovation low-power radar physiological sensing" and "motional state analysis algorithm" to provide the continuous heartbeat and breathing of various pets.The radar can read pet health data without being limited by the pet's body shape or hair length. In addition, there are also monitoring of activity volume and loss warnings, allowing owners to easily grasp the pet's health and emotional state. The information application products developed by the Institute of Industrial Research and Research combine innovative thinking and ICT technology to exert the output value and influence of the information service industry, and can better connect international market demand.
ASOCIO holds the ICT Award (Asia Pacific Information Communication Technology Application Award) every year, which is known as the "Oscar of the Information and Communications Industry", selects representative landing application cases and solutions from various countries, certifies landing application cases and solutions from various countries that meet the award categories, encourages technology to bring innovation, bridge the digital gap, and drives digital industrialization and government digital transformation.In recent years, the Institute of Technology Research has actively formulated the "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint". In the field of smart life, it has identified the diversified development of science and technology industry themes and the life trends of modern society, and has invested in digital transformation and innovative technology introduction, so that technology can be truly implemented in daily health care and actively displayed on the international stage.
【News Dictionary】Asia-Pacific Information Service Industry Organization
The Asia-Oceanian Computing Industry Organization (ASOCIO) is a group composed of the Asia-Pacific Information and Communication Services Association. It was established in 1984. It aims to promote, encourage and support relations and trade between its members, and is also committed to assisting various regions in developing the information and communication industry.ASOCIO represents the interests of 29 economies, including 24 members, from Japan, Taiwan, Bangladesh, Hong Kong, India, Cambodia, Indonesia, South Korea, Laos, Macao, Malaysia, Mongolia, Myanmar, Nepal, New Zealand, Pakistan, the Philippines, Sri Lanka, Brunei, Thailand, Vietnam, and seven special invited members, from the United States, the United Kingdom, Canada, Spain, Russia, France and Kenya, and are an important and large-scale international organization in the communications industry in the Asia-Pacific region.
