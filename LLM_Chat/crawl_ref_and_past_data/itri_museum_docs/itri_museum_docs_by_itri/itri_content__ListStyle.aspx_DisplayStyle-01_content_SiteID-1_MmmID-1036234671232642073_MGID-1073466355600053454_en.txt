Meeting conclusion focus
New services are no longer a single industry, but an ecosystem;the overall industry should be jointly developed from the application side, from ICT, software to services, products should be easy to use, and the design of the system architecture platform is very important, and from functional modules to terminal applications can develop simultaneously.We must take the world as the market and also include making good use of the mainland market.
We must develop a user-centered system architecture, shifting value from linear to network, and forming a digital economy with intelligent networking.
The country's long-term industrial development and innovation opportunities, and the industry needs talents, so long-term cultivation is very important; basic talents are trained by schools, and the Institute of Technology cultivates the talents needed for the industry for 5 to 10 years, becoming an industrial talent pool.
The establishment of a service system should re-learn cooperation methods and sharing of interests, so that the people can participate, and the government should take the lead in creating successful cases.
It is recommended to participate more in the formulation of international standards to grasp business opportunities.When formulating cross-strait standards, the government should encourage the Industrial Research Institute and the Investment Policy Association to actively participate.
Suggestions for technological development, talent cultivation and regulatory review:
There are two types of technologies: one is application-oriented technology, related to the local market, and the other is basic technology, which is available all over the world. The Institute of Technology and Research can connect these two.
Talent cultivation has two directions: one is to make good use of expertise and apply to innovation; the other is to make basic knowledge and technological breakthroughs.China Research Institute and Industrial Research Institute have made breakthroughs in basic knowledge and technology, so that enterprises and senior engineers can easily use it.
The government should fully support the Institute of Technology to invest in innovative technology, including key components and designs required by precision machinery.
We must make good use of the cross-field capabilities of the Institute of Industrial Research, including ICT, to assist medical and biotechnology manufacturing, and the Institute of Industrial Research provides test field verification and bear risks.
The subsequent application of medical-related monitoring information and the research and development of instruments must take into account the role of the doctor, and thus jointly promote the market with the doctor.
Information security is a major issue and requires cooperation between industry, officials, academics and research.The Institute of Technology has invested a lot in software and hardware in communications, and can establish organizations and invest in related work, including the establishment of laws, verification mechanisms (products meet safety requirements) and education, etc., and can also connect everyone together.
Traditional industries are equally important as high technology. Traditional industries such as cement and petrochemicals need to provide materials for electronic products, and need to develop carbon capture technology, waste treatment technology and methods, and also need to review and adjust relevant regulations.The Institute of Technology should consider assisting traditional industries in response to the trends and requirements of international zero carbon emissions, including the amendment of legal standards.
The development of technology is extremely important for the stability, safety and reliability of smart power grids and requires more investment.
The Institute of Industrial Research and Research should take the lead in taking on innovation risks. The government should allow the Institute of Industrial Research and Research and Research to try R&D from multiple parties, and support large R&D risks in terms of funds.
