Technology introduction
Smart mechanical cloud.
In order to improve the production efficiency of the machinery industry, promote digital transformation, and improve industrial competitiveness, the Industrial Research Institute launched the "Smart Machinery Cloud" to assist traditional machinery and equipment operators in transforming into professional system integrators (SIs). It has now successfully cultivated 58 home operators to transform.
Features and innovation
Through technology replication and experience sharing, operators can provide efficient system integration services through smart mechanical cloud, and at the same time, combining the platform's standard execution environment, niche applications (Apps) are put into the store, realizing the advantages of software sharing and maximizing efficiency.
Application and benefits
This platform has not only been recognized by the Asia-Pacific Economic Cooperation Conference (APEC), but also successfully entered the Southeast Asian smart manufacturing market with cloud services. It has piloted five major industries such as tool machines, electronic equipment, metal forming equipment, plastic injection machines and textile equipment, leading small and medium-sized enterprises to join the international market and enter the global supply chain.
