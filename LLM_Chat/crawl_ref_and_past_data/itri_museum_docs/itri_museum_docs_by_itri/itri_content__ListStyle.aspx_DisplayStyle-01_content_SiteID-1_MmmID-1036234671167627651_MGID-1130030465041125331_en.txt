Academician Cai Lixing
Academician Cai Lixing is the vice chairman and CEO of MediaTek Technology Co., Ltd., and has devoted himself to the semiconductor industry for more than 30 years. He has been committed to the innovation and industrial upgrading of semiconductor, IC design and electronic communication technology in Taiwan, and has made outstanding contributions.Academician Cai served as a Taiwan integrated circuit manufacturing company for 25 years, achieving the key to independent research and development of TSMC; when he was transferred to the chairman of China Telecom, he was the first to launch 4G services in Taiwan to promote the transformation and upgrading of the telecom leader; in 2017, he took over as co-executive director of MediaTek and led the company to grow all the way with Chairman Cai Mingjie.The group's revenue reached 10 billion US dollars for the first time, becoming the global leader in smartphone chips.
Academician Cai graduated from the Department of Physics of National Taiwan University and obtained a PhD in Materials Science and Engineering from Cornell University in 1981. After returning to the country in 1989, he started as an engineering manager and was responsible for building TSMC's first and second 8-inch wafer factories in Taiwan, setting a record of the shortest waiting time for completion and mass production within two years. Later, he led the team to develop the 0.13 micron copper process, which is second only to Intel and the second 0.13 micron process to successfully mass production.It has made TSMC's "from nothing to something" and enter the historical key of "independent research and development", laying a solid foundation for TSMC to lead the world in advanced processes in the future.
In 2014, Academician Cai switched to the telecommunications field and served as the chairman of China Telecom. He applied his experience in semiconductor industry management to the management of telecom leaders: he was the first to launch 4G services in Taiwan, develop 4G smart city applications in 15 counties and cities, and strengthened the construction of rural areas to reduce digital gaps; he also used his management skills to strengthen corporate strength.During his tenure, China Telecom's market value grew by more than 100 billion yuan, and also gave birth to emerging companies such as China Jingchao and Qinwei, and was selected as one of the three best CEOs in Taiwan in 2016.
With rich experience in semiconductor manufacturing and telecommunications industries, Academician Cai was appointed by Cai Mingjie, Chairman of MediaTek in 2017 and took over as co-executive director, implementing the concept of "creating value with technology".At the end of 2019, he led the industry and launched the world's first 5G chip "Dimensity 1000", pushing MediaTek to the top of its technology. In 2020, under the influence of the epidemic, the demand for long-distance work, learning and entertainment increased significantly.He and his team led the company to ride the wind with agile execution, setting a new high in the group's annual revenue, and reached the world's largest smartphone chip market in the third quarter of the same year.
Academician Cai enriches his experience in the science and technology industry and has influenced all over the country.He is also committed to promoting semiconductor industry-university cooperation, aiming at leading technology, actively discovering talents, providing Taiwan's semiconductor industry with a steady stream of growth momentum, and also providing Taiwan with a continuous display of impressive results under the fierce international competition.
