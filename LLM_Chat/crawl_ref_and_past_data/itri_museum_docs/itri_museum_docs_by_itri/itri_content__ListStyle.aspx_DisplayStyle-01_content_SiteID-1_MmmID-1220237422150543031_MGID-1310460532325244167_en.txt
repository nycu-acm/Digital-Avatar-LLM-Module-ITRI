Technology introduction
Key technologies in space.
Against the backdrop of increasing interactions between the global space industry and geopolitical, space communications have become a key strategic asset to strengthen national resilience.Countries are investing in the development of independent satellite systems, and Taiwan also urgently needs to build key technologies and improve its domestic capabilities.The Institute of Technology focuses on the research and development of core technologies such as ultra-low power reaction wheels, 3-meter-level synthetic aperture radar (SAR) satellite technology, and large-size ultra-high-precision optical lenses, integrating domestic industrial resources and promoting the construction of a key space component manufacturing and production ecosystem.At the same time, the Institute of Technology and Research actively links academic institutions and local electromechanical manufacturers, focusing on the research and development of innovative technologies such as high precision and low power consumption, so as to achieve the domestic goal of key space components such as reaction wheel modules, large optical lenses, and expandable antennas, and strengthen the independent energy and international competitiveness of my country's space industry.
Features and innovation
On the other hand, the Industrial Research Institute is also leading the layout of key technologies for non-ground networks (NTNs) and developing the "B5G/6G NTN wide coverage base station system", so that service providers do not need to update existing hardware devices, and can directly connect to satellite communication through software updates to experience the mainland.
High-speed networking without blind spots for sea and air 3D communication.
Application and benefits
This technology works with the industry to complete end-to-end verification of multi-orbit satellites, such as high-orbit satellites, low-orbit satellites and Lianfa Technology, etc., to implement the empirical results, and participate in the proposals of international standards organizations, lay out the intellectual property rights of NTN independent technology standards, and hope to build my country's NTN satellite communication supply chain, connecting the upstream, mid- and downstream industries to integrate into the international ecosystem from chips, antenna modules, base platforms, user terminals, telecom operators, etc. From space components to communication systems to dual-track progress, we will improve our self-made capacity to establish more resilient communication capabilities and create a space industry supply chain belonging to Taiwan.
