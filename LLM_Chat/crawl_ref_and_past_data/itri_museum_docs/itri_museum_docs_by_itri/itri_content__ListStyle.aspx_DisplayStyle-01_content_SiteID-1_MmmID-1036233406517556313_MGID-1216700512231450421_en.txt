About the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials
Promise a healthy and happy future
Technology has always come from human needs. The global population is gradually becoming older, and diseases threaten human life and quality of life. Therefore, the advancement of forward-looking biomedicine and innovative medical equipment technology will become a common and key development in the future international development. The output value of the biomedical and medical equipment technology industry has grown by double digits in recent years, and has now become a key development industry selected by Taiwan. The Institute of Industrial Research and Research has been listed as one of the main strategic research and development fields in cooperation with government policies.With the increase of civilized diseases and chronic diseases and the rise of preventive medicine concepts, the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials has invested huge research and development energy in all aspects from prevention, diagnosis, medicine, surgical technology to medical materials and care, and has achieved remarkable results.It is the strongest backing for Taiwan's industrial development.
Vision
Become a world-class biomedical and medical equipment research and development center
mission
Combining cross-field technology, leading the high value-added biomedical and medical equipment industry
human Resources
The Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials has a 100-person doctoral group, nearly 90% of them have master's degrees or above, and are specialized in medicine, biochemistry, materials, medical engineering, information and other fields. With this, a research team composed of highly professional talents has repeatedly achieved world-renowned research achievements and has won many national invention and creation honors.
