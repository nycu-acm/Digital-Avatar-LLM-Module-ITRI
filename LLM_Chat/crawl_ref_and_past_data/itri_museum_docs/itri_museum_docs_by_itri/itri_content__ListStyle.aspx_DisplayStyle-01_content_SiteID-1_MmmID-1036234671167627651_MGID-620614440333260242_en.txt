Lin Baili Academician
Chairman Lin Baili is the founder and chairman of Quanda Computer Company. He has a bachelor's and master's degree in the Department of Electrical Engineering of National Taiwan University.He has accumulated more than 30 years of industrial experience so far and continues to provide active strategic guidance in the long-term business development and R&D direction of Quanda Group.In 1972, he was awarded the first "Youth Medal" by then Premier Chiang Ching-kuo; in 1988, he founded Quanta Computer with Liang Cizhen and others, and in 1999 he became the world's leader in "laptop computers" OEM.In recent years, the 3C concept (CloudComputing, Connecting, Client Device) has been proposed as a new high point for career breakthroughs. Chairman Lin was selected as the "Asia Star" of Business Week in 1999 and 2001, respectively;and was selected as the 25th World's Best Manager of the Year by Business Week in 2002.
Chairman Lin Yahao Humanities and Arts is one of the most important art collectors in Asia. He once proposed "only those with taste can innovate", and established the Guangyi Foundation and the Guangda Cultural and Educational Foundation to promote Taiwan's culture, art and education, and actively practice the concept of "cultural prosperity".At the same time, he also participated in the social welfare activity "one child, one computer", allowing children in third-world countries to open a window of knowledge to the world.Chairman Lin spared no effort to promote art and culture, and led Quanda Computer to become the world's largest notebook computer manufacturer, and made great contributions to the country's growth in the economy, art and culture and the computer industry.
