Academician Wang Kanglong
Academician Wang Kanglong is currently the Thor lecturer in the Department of Electrical Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles. He is an internationally renowned semiconductor scholar. In 2017, he led a research team to discover Mayorana particles, solving the mystery that has plagued the academic community for 80 years, and is an important driving force for the development of topological quantum computers in the next generation.
After graduating from the Department of Electrical Engineering at the University of Chenggong, Academician Wang went to the United States to obtain a doctorate in electronic engineering from the MIT Institute of Technology.Since 1979, he has served as a professor of electronic engineering from the University of California, Los Angeles. Academician Wang has made many contributions to the field of spintronics, semiconductor electronics and nanotechnology, including solving many problems and challenges in semiconductor electronic components with innovative technologies;the first to bring the concept of electronic spin element into the CMOS industry, changing the stagnation of electronic research for decades and opening up a new field of electronic spin electronic components.Since 1989, he has established the Nano Electronics Core Basic Research Center at the University of Los Angeles, leading research teams from top universities in the United States to invest in research related to nanotechnology.
Academician Wang is committed to scientific research and has published more than 600 research papers and publications so far, owning more than 20 patents, and has won the annual Inventor Award of American Semiconductor Research Company for ten consecutive years since 1989. He is a member of the Guggenheim Fellow and the Society of Electrical and Electronic Engineers of Germany. He is also a member of the American Physics Society, a member of the Materials Research Society and an academician of the Central Academy of Research. In 2017, he was awarded the highest honor of components by the IEEE International Electronic Components Society (EDS), 2018 In 2019, he won the Magnetism Award and the Neel Medal, awarded by the International Society for Pure Physics and Applied Physics (IUPAP), one of the most important awards in the physics community.
Academician Wang is very concerned about Taiwan's scientific and technological development and spares no effort to promote the backward advancement.He assisted in planning the implementation and operation of the National Science and Technology Association LANGUAN Project, and also served as the supervisory professor of foreign research units such as the National Science and Technology Association Thousand-Mile Horse Project and Talent Cultivation Project.He made an indelible contribution to expanding the international horizons and international cooperation experience of domestic doctoral students and postdoctoral researchers.
