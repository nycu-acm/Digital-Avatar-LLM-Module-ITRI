About the Institute of Green Energy and Environment
Over the past half a century, the Green Energy and Environment Research Institute has accompanied Taiwan's economic growth and actively carried out research and development and innovation to assist my country in improving its technologies in resource development and utilization, renewable energy, energy conservation and carbon reduction, energy efficiency and industrial safety and environmental protection, and has made outstanding contributions to supporting industrial growth.Looking ahead, the Green Energy and Environment Research Institute will continue to invest in forward-looking green energy technology research and development, create energy conservation, carbon reduction and sustainable environmental technology, and assist the development of the green energy industry to create new green energy and environment industries to achieve the goal of sustainable development.
In the 21st century, mankind is facing difficulties such as climate change, gradual scarcity of energy and resources, and increasingly serious environmental damage and pollution. To fulfill the obligations of the world's citizens and promote sustainable development, the Green Energy and Environment Research Institute will work sincerely at all times, extend its consistent deepening and persistence to keep pace with the times, accurately display a diversified professional pattern, and explore new fields of green energy technology and low-carbon technology.
At the same time, we hope to develop the world's top technologies and applications in the three major aspects of forward-looking innovation, product development, and patent layout through outstanding talents, forward-looking planning and multinational technical cooperation.In the future, in addition to continuing to provide high-quality and high-value industrial knowledge services, we will continue to create new opportunities for green energy, resource and environmental industries in the new economic era.
position
The leader in low-carbon home technology - with green energy, environment and resource technology as the core, we will create forward-looking technology for energy conservation, carbon reduction and sustainable environment, develop green energy and environmental industries, and lead the construction of low-carbon homes.

Foresight Energy Environment Technology Benchmark, Green Energy Environment Industry Pioneer
In addition to cooperating with the existing systems and systems of the institute, the development strategy of the Green Energy and Environment Research Institute also actively introduces innovative energy, resource and environmental technology planning, and adheres to the mission of assisting in promoting industrial transformation and enhancing industrial competitiveness, and makes the development layout of various organizations.
