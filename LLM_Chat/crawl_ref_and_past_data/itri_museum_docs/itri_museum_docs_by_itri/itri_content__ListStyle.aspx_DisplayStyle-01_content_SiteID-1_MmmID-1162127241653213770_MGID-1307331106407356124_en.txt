Technology introduction
Ethylammonia to hydrogen application platform
High-efficiency ammonia cracking and hydrogen conversion technology, low-energy-consuming catalysts and high-efficiency reactor design, provides a low-carbon hydrogen source in the industry, and combines applications such as power generation, industry, and transportation to create a net zero industrial carbon reduction solution.
Application scope
Through the mature ammonia supply chain imports chlorammonia and converts hydrogen into domestic supply for low-carbon applications such as power generation, industry, and transportation, it can accelerate the industry's carbon reduction goals and enhance product export competitiveness.
Developing medium-temperature catalysts (≦600 ℃) and ammonia cracking reactors to effectively reduce the overall operating temperature, reduce the heat loss in the hydrogen production process, and reduce the use of high-temperature resistant materials and reduce the system construction cost.
Establish a hydrogen ammonia purification exhaust gas recovery mechanism, develop hydrogen ammonia combustion technology, maximize energy utilization, and improve the overall urinary ammonia to hydrogen energy efficiency.
100 Nm3/h chloramino to hydrogen system, energy efficiency ≧ 75% and 200kW hydrogen/ammonia feed SOFC system integrate the application to create a scalon chloramino to hydrogen application platform.
Carbon reduction benefits
• Layout patents for high-performance catalyst materials, develop chloramino to hydrogen, hydrogen ammonia combustion and integrated power generation application technologies, and assist enterprises in developing related products
• Complete the construction of hydrogen and hydrogen carrier production, transportation and storage areas, promote the field application of low-carbon hydrogen, and assist the industry in introducing hydrogen energy to reduce carbon emissions.
