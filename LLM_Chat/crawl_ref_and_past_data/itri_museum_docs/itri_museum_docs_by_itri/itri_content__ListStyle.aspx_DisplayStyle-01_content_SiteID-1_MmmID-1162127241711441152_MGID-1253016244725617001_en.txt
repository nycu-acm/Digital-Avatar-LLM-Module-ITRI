Technology introduction
Oil-stained wipes.
The Institute of Technology assists Taiwanese manufacturers in evaluating the introduction of low-carbon calcium from calcium chloride process.CO2, a by-product in the low-carbon calcium source process, is used as the raw material for baking soda. Taiwanese manufacturers capture CO2 purification and construct local production of green baking soda process technology.
Application scope
Common kitchen oil stains at home are troubles that you will face every day. If you use a powerful detergent, you will have any remaining doubts. If the detergent is cleaned and flows into our rivers, it may cause environmental pollution. Consumers expect a safe and non-toxic cleaning product.
Cleaning products that seek natural environmental protection can not only achieve the purpose of cleaning but also be environmentally friendly.
The Industrial Research Institute has developed and derived cleaning products using low-carbon baking soda powder, which can effectively remove acidic dirt. It can quickly dissociate oil and dirt with its own alkaline substances. When alkaline substances become free, acidic oil and dirt can be removed from the surface of the object. When alkaline substances absorb acidic oil and dirt, they can easily wipe away the dirt. Acid dirt such as: kitchen oil, dining table dirt, bathroom dirt, tea dirt, coffee dirt, unpleasant odor... etc. The exclusive formula combines plant-sourced environmentally friendly surfactants and add natural aloe to extract to achieve a golden ratio formula, creating a derivative product of green baking soda. It is an environmentally friendly and safe way to easily clean the environment, so that we can be close to life and do environmental protection!
Carbon reduction benefits
It can be used in various low-carbon and environmentally friendly cleaning products, which can reduce the carbon footprint of sea transportation of raw materials imported and reduce the carbon emissions in the air more significantly.
