About the Institute of International Strategy Development of Industrial Science and Technology
Faced with the challenge of accelerating the application trend of emerging international technologies and innovation, and the critical moment of domestic industrial innovation transformation and upgrading, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has integrated the market analysis and industrial think tank service energy of the original Industrial Economics and Trend Research Center, and established the "Industrial Technology International Strategy Development Institute of Industrial Research and Research Institute of International Center", which will focus on forward-looking technology and applications in the industrial blue ocean market strategy. At the same time, by deepening in-depth cooperation with international forward-looking technology research and development institutions, it can achieve an international development strategy that connects the global innovation ecosystem network and assists Taiwan's industry in the layout of the global forward-looking technology market, and enhances the Institute of Industrial Research and Research into a world-class think tank.
Vision and priority
We will take "promote Taiwan's industrial technological innovation and value enhancement" as our vision, and set up four priorities: scanning forward-looking technology and emerging application markets, connecting global innovation ecosystem networks, forming alliances with scientific research strategy partners to create the future, and enhancing the overall innovative technology research and development energy of industry, academia and research.We look forward to enhancing the functions of international forward-looking technology scanning, promoting industrial and technological cooperation among international leaders and institutions to enhance Taiwan's overall innovative technology research and development energy. The following are four priority tasks.
Scan the forward-looking technology and emerging application markets (Technology Scouting)
Strengthen the research on global key industries and forward-looking scientific and technological development trends: combine industrial and technical analysis energy and databases, network of international cooperative institutions, and promote overseas dispatch of industrial researchers to deeply cultivate, etc., and invest in the exploration of international key technologies development trends.It also connects and leverages the existing domestic research energy to create an open cooperation platform.
Connecting to the global innovation ecosystem network (Ecosystem Networking)
Expand participation in international network cooperation, connect the Institute of Technology and the International Innovation and Technology R&D Ecological Network: Strengthen the expansion energy of the overseas base network of the Institute of Technology and Research, play a regional business platform, assist in establishing cooperative relationships with international R&D institutions, research universities, leading manufacturers, local industrial associations and international organizations, and provide Taiwan's research teams from all walks of life, academia and research, and connect the international industrial innovation ecosystem.
Strategic Partnering
Integrate the forward-looking scientific and technological research energy of the Institute of Industrial Research, promote strategic cooperation with global leading scientific and technological research institutions, and jointly strengthen the momentum of industrialization of innovative technology: integrate the OISP R&D platform of the technical team of the Institute of Industrial Research, and leverage the R&D energy of international scientific and technological research and development benchmark scientific research institutions, and jointly invest in the blue ocean market for future global innovative technology applications through strategic cooperation and ecological alliances.
Improve the overall innovative technology research and development energy of industry, academia and research (Capability Boosting)
Integrate Taiwan's industrial technology energy, with international R&D network, strengthen the forward-looking nature of scientific and technological research and development of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research and the platform-based legal person tasks, so as to enhance the overall innovative technological research and development momentum of Taiwan's industry, academia and research: Through international network cooperation, strengthen the global forward-looking technology exploration, applied market research and international scientific research energy integration of the Institute, assist the R&D teams in the institute (each institute/center) and domestic scientific and technological research and development teams from all walks of life in the industry, academia and research (including other legal persons), etc., to drive the innovative growth of industries in Taiwan's key areas.
