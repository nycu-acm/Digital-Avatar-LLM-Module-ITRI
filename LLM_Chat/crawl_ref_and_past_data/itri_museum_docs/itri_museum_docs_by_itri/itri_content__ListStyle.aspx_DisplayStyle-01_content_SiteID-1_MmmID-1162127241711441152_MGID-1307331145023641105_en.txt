Technology introduction
Asphalt removal is converted into high-quality renewable resources.
Using biological bacterial agents and hydrothermal separation technology, the debris was reshaped into 70% recycled stone and 30% asphalt sand. Combined with ITRI asphalt regenerator, 100% returned to the pavement, and practiced the rebirth of old materials and the eternal life of cyclic materials.
Application scope
Unique bacterial agents, microbial metabolism, form surfactants with asphalt organic matter, so that asphalt emulsification on the debris achieves a viscosity-reducing effect.
The water circulation process uses water as a medium to accelerate the separation of aging asphalt and sand and gravel, and uses a water circulation system to design that water and material removal are fully recycled.
Break through the traditional dilemma of removing and reuse of materials, and solve problems such as uneven asphalt content, different aging degrees and false particle size at one time; innovative technologies separate asphalt from sand and gravel, concentrate, concentrate and homogenize the asphalt, and reduce the real particle size.
The recycled stone meets the CNS 15308 coarse pellet material standard for asphalt paving mixture, with a wear rate of 17%, and a crushed particle content of 96% and a health test of 0.4%.
Carbon reduction benefits
In 2024, we will work together with the industry to build the world's first scrap removal recycling and recycling system with an annual processing capacity of 80,000 tons.
The first innovative system can generate annual revenue of up to RMB 32 million for recycled stone (56,000 tons/year) and RMB 48 million for asphalt (24,000 tons/year of asphalt sand) for operators.
Based on the estimated output of 5 million tons of fossil removal in China every year, 3.5 million tons of recycled stone will be produced, which is equivalent to 10% of the domestic sand and gravel needs; and recycled asphalt sand will replace 150,000 tons of fossil asphalt, helping the construction industry to reduce carbon emissions by 270,000 tons per year.
