Technology introduction
The world's first AI fish tank.
In response to the future smart lifestyle and corresponding application fields, the Industrial Research Institute has developed the "Transparent Display Intelligent Interactive System Technology", combining transparent display panel modules, static information fusion, composite directive interaction and other technologies, and has built a smart transparent display interactive system combining soft and hardware to provide users' intuitive interactive experience and information services, successfully adding value to the key components of high-transparent displays.
Features and innovation
This technology can be placed in front of an object. Users can directly click on the object of interest. The monitor will immediately display information about the object. The intelligent interactive function of this technology also includes the advantages of dual-person use at the same time, high accuracy and direction, and the positioning error is less than 4mm.
Application and benefits
It can provide users with new usage experience and widely used in smart retail, smart entertainment, smart exhibition halls and other fields, carry out systematic product and service application development with high value-added creation, expand display technology and cross-field industrial cooperation, assist system planners and retail stores, catering operators, medical centers and museums and other operators, conduct innovative smart retail business operation models through field evidence, enhance the value and profit of commodity application, and create new operation models and market development opportunities.
