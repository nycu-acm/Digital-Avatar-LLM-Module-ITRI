103 Annual "Academician of the Institute of Technology" Conference
Group photo of the Third Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (103 years)
Time: December 16, 2013
Location: No. 10, Lane 6, Section 2, Chongqing South Road, Taipei City (Multi-functional Lecture Hall of Sun Yunqi Memorial Hall)
Chairman: Academician Shi Zhenrong
The meeting of academicians of the Institute of Technology Research Institute was held and chaired by Academician Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of Zhirong Group, in the multi-functional lecture hall of the Sun Yunqi Memorial Hall. The meeting discussed topics such as how to use the key core capabilities of innovation to promote the transfer of industrial models, how to establish a service-oriented industry that integrates software and hardware, and the role of the Institute of Technology Research Institute in the transformation of industrial structure. The academicians at the meeting included: Zhang Zhongmou, founder and chairman of Taiwan Instrumental Circuit Manufacturing Co., Ltd., Shi Chongtang, Chairman of Asus Computer Co., Ltd., and Delta Electronics founded. Zheng Chonghua, the Honorary Chairman of the People and Honorary Chairman, Lin Shuhong, Chairman and General Manager of Changchun Petrochemical Group, Li Bingjie, Chairman of Jingyuan Optoelectronics Technology Company, Lin Baili, Chairman of Guangda Group, Chen Xingshi, President of Ronggang Group/Chairman of Ronggang Materials Technology Co., Ltd., Lin Benjian, Deputy General Manager of Research and Development of Taiwan Integrated Circuit Manufacturing Co., Ltd., Chen Xianzhang, Vice President of Changchun Petrochemical Group, Yu Huanian, Honorary Chairman of the Foresight Technology Steering Committee of the Institute of Industrial Technology, and Lin Genghua, Academician of the Institute of Central Research.
Meeting conclusion focus
Any company must keep pace with the times, and Taiwan’s industrial transformation and upgrading should establish more new companies.A large number of talents are needed when a new company develops, and the talents of existing companies can be integrated and used.
The important task of the Institute of Technology Research is to establish a new company.
We must focus on cross-industry, cross-departmental and cross-strait integration and innovation, establish new industries with IP as Core, and develop into new Business Models and Ecosystems.
Give young people opportunities and stages, use new heads and new Business Model to establish new companies and create new industries.
We must grasp major changes and opportunities in the future, give full play to Taiwan's advantages, establish IP and system integration technology, and develop an "whole factory output" operation model, such as developing new energy conversion technologies and biomass raw materials, using foreign biomass resources to produce raw materials, so as to connect existing facilities and market channels in the petrochemical industry, and create a driving force for sustainable development of the industry.
Key core capabilities
(Key Enabling Competence, KEC)
It is a good design thinking/systemic thinking. The three elements of user value, technological breakthroughs and business model need to be promoted simultaneously across fields, and constantly interact with users and correct them to break through the industrial structure of OEM and ODM.
Human Value and beauty are the areas where Taiwan needs to strengthen breakthroughs.
The Industrial Research Institute must be able to face consumers and develop global thinking simultaneously with the industry.
We must develop ultimate customer and market verification to assist in the industrialization of innovative technologies.
