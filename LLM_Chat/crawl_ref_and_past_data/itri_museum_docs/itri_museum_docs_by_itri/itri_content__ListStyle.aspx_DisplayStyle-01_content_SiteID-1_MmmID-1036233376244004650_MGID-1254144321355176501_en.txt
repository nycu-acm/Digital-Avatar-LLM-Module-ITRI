Technology introduction
Low-cost alkaline membrane electrolysis aquatic hydrogen production technology.
More than 150 countries around the world have promised to reach net zero emissions by 2050. COP27 and COP28 resolutions must accelerate the development of low-carbon technologies, use zero-carbon and low-carbon fuels to accelerate the achievement of net zero.
Features and innovation
Combining renewable energy and electrolytic aquatic hydrogen technology to produce green hydrogen, taking into account seasonal energy storage and zero-carbon hydrogen production, it is suitable for steel, petrochemical, electricity, transportation, semiconductors, energy and other industries.
Application and benefits
The Institute of Technology has long invested in the research and development of "Anion Exchange Membrane Electrolysis; AEMEL" and has established a number of key technologies, including nickel-based catalyst development, hydrocarbon film synthesis, membrane electrode group verification, piezoelectric stack design and low-energy-consuming system integration, etc. The relevant R&D results have promoted the application and development of steel, electricity, chemical industries, catalysts and well-known Japanese chemical companies.
