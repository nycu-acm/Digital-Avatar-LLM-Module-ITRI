Technology introduction
Scanning high-energy laser welding machine.
Various high-energy laser welding technologies and equipment have been developed, which can be divided into direct writing and scanning technologies. Direct writing technology is also divided into filler and filler-free technology. Direct writing laser welding technology has been developed, combining welding road path trace and precise focus correction functions to complete large-scale special-shaped pipe fitting welding, frame welding and motor stator welding; direct writing laser welding technology combined with line feeding technology can complete defect-free laser welding of 30T thick metal plates.
Scanning laser welding technology uses scanning high-power laser welding system to perform homogeneous/heterogeneous welding of copper, aluminum, nickel, iron and other materials, which can control the welding depth and welding quality. It has been successfully introduced into the series production line of battery modules, with a production capacity of >15 modules/hour. It can quickly replace and design the production line according to different battery module designs, with a fast speed and high elasticity. It can further combine with automated production lines to achieve assembly line laser welding continuous production lines, improve production capacity and save manpower.

Application and benefits
Laser welding technology has been successfully introduced into production lines in various fields, such as special-shaped pipe fittings, heat exchangers, power lithium battery modules, turntables and other production lines. The advanced laser welding technology used can increase by 2 to 10 times compared with traditional welding production capacity. It has been successfully applied in the fields of factory systems, automobiles, electric buses, drones and energy storage cabinets.
