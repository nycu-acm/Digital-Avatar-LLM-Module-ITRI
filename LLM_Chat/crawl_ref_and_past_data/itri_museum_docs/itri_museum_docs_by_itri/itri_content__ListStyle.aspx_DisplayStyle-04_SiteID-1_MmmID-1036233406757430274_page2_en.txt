The Ministry of Economics Promotion Research Institute and British Business Oxford Instruments signed a research plan to jointly deploy Taiwan's compound semiconductor technology ahead of schedule and enter the global high-tech market

The Industrial Research Institute and British Business Oxford Instruments signed a research plan to jointly cooperate, hoping to connect the research and development energy of both parties, successfully build the development of Taiwan's compound semiconductor industry chain, and seize the global market.
Foreign companies assist in the upgrading of Taiwan's semiconductor equipment, and Taiwan works together to build an advanced laser application service center
Date: 2021/09/02
The Institute of Technology and Research, Taiwan Mechanical Industry Association and the world's leading laser application brand, Germany Chuangpu Group (TRUMPF), will work together to establish the "Taiwan Semiconductor and Electronics Industry Advanced Laser Application Service Center".

At the unveiling ceremony of DuPont Semiconductor Materials Laboratory, the Institute of Technology and DuPont jointly announced a joint cooperation to carry out next-generation semiconductor materials research and development and verification, and provide advanced semiconductor materials needs.
