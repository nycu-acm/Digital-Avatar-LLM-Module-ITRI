112 Annual "Academician of the Institute of Technology" Conference
Group photo of the 12th Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (112 years).
Time: October 19, 2012
Location: Conference Room 604, Hall 51, No. 195, Section 4, Zhongxing Road, Zhudong Town, Hsinchu County (Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research)
Chairman: Academician Shi Zhenrong
Topic: Generative AI leads industrial transformation: Opportunities and challenges
The 112 Annual Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research was held in the Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research, hosted by Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of the Zhirong Cultural and Education Foundation, and discussed the impact of "general artificial intelligence (GAI)" on Taiwan, business opportunities and talent cultivation.Participating in the Academicians of the Institute of Technology include: Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of the Zhirong Cultural and Educational Foundation, Yu Huanian, Honorary Chairman and Senior Advisor of the Foresight Technical Steering Committee of the Institute of Technology, Lin Benjian, Distinguished Research Professor of Tsinghua University, Shi Qintai, Chairman of Pan Wenyuan Cultural and Educational Foundation, Chen Xingshi, Founder of Ronggang Group, Wang Kanglong, Lethod's Professor of Electrical Engineering Department of the University of California, Los Angeles, Lin Genghua, Academician of the Central Research Institute, Du Shuwu, President and CEO of Lianqiang International Group, Yang Zhentong, Founder of Yali Electric Group, Li Changrong Group President Li Mouwei, Changxing Materials Shenzhen consultant Xiao Cifei, General Manager of Wanghong Electronics Lu Zhiyuan, Chairman and General Manager of Yi'an Health Medical Section Zhang Youde, Chairman and General Manager of Yi'an Biotechnology Yang Yumin, Honorary Professor of National Taiwan University School of Medicine Li Yuande, Professor of Internal Medicine Yang Panchi of National Taiwan University School of Medicine Yang Yingjun, Chairman of Delta Electronics Hai Yingjun, Professor of Bill Gates from Harvard University in the United States, Chairman of Advantech Co., Ltd. Meeting conclusion focus:
1. The potential impact of generative AI on industry and society
Establishing a high-quality, effective and large database is the key to whether generative AI can succeed.It is recommended that the government establish a safe, fair and competitive application policy for the data it has, and establish a responsible unit, committed to converting Taiwan's unique data into AI models. Each department should establish a data preparation and use mechanism to support the widespread application of AI models.
With the development of AI algorithms, the power consumption and water resource utilization of data centers have gradually increased.It is recommended that the government should establish standards or set goals to promote the energy efficiency of equipment and the recycling and reuse of water resources.
AI will bring profound changes to the future work style, and may also expand criminal activities such as fake information and fraud.It is recommended that the government promote the correct way of using AI to the whole people and establish complete norms based on the morality and ethics of AI.
2. How to master the business opportunities of generative AI in Taiwan
It is recommended that the Industrial Research Institute establish a basic model suitable for industrial applications, including synthetic imaging technology (text to image) applied to the manufacturing and health care industry, research and development of high-efficiency sparse quantization Transformer models applied to edge devices, research and development of AI security technology, Self-training all-purpose zero-shot transformers, and ground-end model miniaturization technology to prevent enterprise confidential leakage, heat dissipation and material technology, and improve energy use efficiency.
Technology leadership does not mean market leadership.It is difficult for Taiwan to achieve international B2C, but it can continue to master the B2B market.It is recommended to develop an AI application business model suitable for Taiwan and move towards internationalization.
Generative AI is expected to have breakthrough development in the medical field. It is recommended that the Industrial Research Institute cooperate with external research institutions (such as the National Health Center), and first develop more uncontroversial registration, patient demand response and product explanation, warm voice application services, blood or image extreme outliers, or combine it with the Internet of Things to improve the elderly medical and quality of life.
3. The impact of generative AI on education and talent cultivation
It is recommended that the government encourage campus teachers and students to invest in AI model research, use corporate data, and develop real-world applications.
There is an urgent need for AI talent training. It is recommended that the Industrial Research Institute establish an AI research institute or Task force to coordinate R&D resources, recruit experts and cultivate talents.
The Institute of Technology should strengthen exchanges with the academic community and enterprises, introduce new technologies and diversity in advance, and use the enterprise's domain knowledge and specific information to jointly develop the application technology of generative AI.
