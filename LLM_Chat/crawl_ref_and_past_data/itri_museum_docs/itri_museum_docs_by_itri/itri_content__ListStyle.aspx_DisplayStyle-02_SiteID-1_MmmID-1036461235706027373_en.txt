Taiwan Semiconductor Industry-Academic R&D Alliance (TIARA)
It is the first and most indicative industry-university super alliance in the field of semiconductors in my country. It has jointly formed an innovative industry-university cooperation mechanism with the industry, academia and the government - the industry-university crown project, which has promoted: to assist students with education, to promote production through academics, and to cultivate my country's leading semiconductor technology research and development personnel
