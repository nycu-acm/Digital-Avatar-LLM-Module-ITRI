Reduce processes, reduce costs, add-on green process creates a path to sustainable
Additive method micro electronic circuit green manufacturing technology.
Isn’t it more efficient to get things right at once than to make repeated changes?There is a difference between subtraction and addition in manufacturing.In the past, due to technology, subtraction manufacturing was almost the only option, but now there are better options.The Institute of Technology Research Institute's "Additional Micro Electronic Line Green Manufacturing Technology" uses energy-saving processes to innovate energy-saving processes, reduce manufacturing processes and reduce production costs, and has also won the "Team Innovation Leadership" award of the Ministry of Economics' 108 Industrial Innovation Award.
Traditional electronic and photoelectric processes use yellow light microfilm processes to define the circuit pattern on the substrate, and then use etching to remove unnecessary parts, which belongs to the subtraction process.This manufacturing method not only wastes materials and energy, but also requires the treatment of waste chemical liquids. Once improperly treated, it may also cause environmental pollution. In order to solve the industrial pain points, the Institute of Technology began to think about how to reduce waste and save energy through new processes.
Compared with the existing circuit board process technology, the Industrial Research Institute integrates "gravure transfer" and "metalization" technology to reduce the circuit line width to 3µm, so that more lines can be placed on the circuit board.For soft boards, 82.6% energy-saving benefits, 88.7% water-saving benefits, and 72.6% waste reduction benefits will reduce nearly 60% of production costs and are the biggest incentive to attract manufacturers' investment.
Additive method micro electronic circuit green manufacturing technology has been transformed into the domestic soft board manufacturer Jialianyi, and new soft board products are undergoing proofing and testing; at the same time, Jialianyi has also formed a R&D alliance with upstream materials and equipment manufacturers such as Miaoyin Precision Machinery, Damai and Innovation Respiratory Materials to implement the R&D results of this green production of soft boards from top to bottom to the industry, and establish a complete ecosystem.
