Weng Qihui Academician
Academician Weng Qihui is a professor of chemistry at the Scripps Research Institute in California and a special researcher at the Gene Societies Research Center of the China Academy of Research Institute. He is also the president of the National Biotechnology Medical Industry Policy Association and a member of the Biotechnology Industry Strategy Advisory Committee of the Executive Yuan.Academician Weng's research work spans chemistry and biological sciences. With his outstanding innovative R&D capabilities, his academic achievements in the international community are obvious to all. He is the first scientist in the world to successfully synthesize complex polysaccharide molecules and homogeneous glycoproteins with enzyme technology, and is also the first chemist to invent the automatic synthesis of polysaccharides by programmed automation. He has a significant impact on the development of chemical biology, sugar molecule science, gene body science and new drugs.
Academician Weng obtained a Ph.D. in chemistry at MIT in the United States and worked as a postdoctoral fellow at Harvard University for one year. Since then, he has been engaged in teaching and research.Academician Weng upholds the spirit of curiosity, challenge the unknown and constantly innovating research. His invented "enzyme synthesis polysaccharide molecule technology" and "programmed polysaccharide body synthesis technology" have turned the synthesis of complex sugar molecules and glycoproteins that were considered very difficult or impossible in the past into possible.This important breakthrough allows scientists to use this as a basis to find out the relationship between various sugars and diseases, and then develop drugs for the treatment of cancer, infectious diseases and various inflammations, and promote major progress in the biotechnology pharmaceutical industry.Academician Weng combined chemical and enzyme methods, genetic engineering and AI technology to solve the problem of the inability to synthesize complex sugar molecules for a long time, and also created a new analytical tool for sugar wafers and sugar probes; his research not only revealed the role of sugar molecules in the biological world, but also applied to disease prevention and treatment.His academic contributions have been recognized by many international awards, including the Wolf Chemistry Award, one of the world's highest achievement awards and the number one person in Taiwan.So far, Academician Weng has trained more than 500 scientists, spread all over the world, published more than 750 academic papers, more than 120 patents, and five special books. The scientific citation impact index (H-index) is as high as 144.
Academician Weng is concerned about the future development of Taiwan's biotechnology industry.In 2003, he returned to China to serve as the director of the Gene Societies Research Center of the China Academy of Research Institute, laying a solid foundation for gene Societies research, and promoting important research results through technology transformation and cultivation to support the development of Taiwan's biotechnology industry. In 2006, he was elected as the president of the China Research Institute.He devoted all his efforts to lead the China Research Institute to pursue excellence and innovation in scientific and humanities research, and assisted the government in formulating the "Regulations on the Development of Biotechnology New Drug Industry", revising the "Basic Law of Science and Technology", and establishing the "National Biotechnology Research Park". He spared no effort in my country's scientific and technological research and development and talent cultivation, and had far-reaching influence on biotechnology policies and industrial development.
