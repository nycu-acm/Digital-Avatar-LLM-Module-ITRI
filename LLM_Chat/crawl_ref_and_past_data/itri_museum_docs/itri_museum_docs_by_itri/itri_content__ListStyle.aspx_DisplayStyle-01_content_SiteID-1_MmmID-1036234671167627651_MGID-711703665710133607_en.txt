Academician Hu Zhengming
Professor Hu Zhengming is internationally renowned in the field of semiconductors. In 1999, the 3D "fin transistor" (FinFET) developed by him broke through the physical limits, smaller components and higher energy use efficiency, which is the biggest change in the semiconductor industry in more than 40 years.Professor Hu has been a member of the National Academy of Engineering and a member of the American Society of Electrical and Electronic Engineering, and is known as the "microelectronics prospector".Later, at the invitation of TSMC Chairman Zhang Zhongmou, he served as TSMC's first technical director.In order to commend his outstanding contribution to innovation and research and development, US President Obama personally favors the National Technology and Innovation Medal.
