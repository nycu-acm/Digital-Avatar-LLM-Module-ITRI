Du Shuwu Academician
Academician Du Shuwu is currently the president and CEO of Lianqiang International Group, and is an entrepreneur who is good at innovative management and precise management.President Du pioneered the concept of "professional channel business" in 1988, providing integrated services for the high-tech product supply chain, greatly improving the professionalism, service connotation and value of traditional agents, and comprehensively reversing the channel industry ecology; in 1997, it began to expand its operating model overseas, and in 2019, the global channel revenue exceeded NT$1.350 billion, becoming the largest channel group in the Asia-Pacific region and the second largest channel group in the world. It has been selected as the "Taiwan Top Ten International Brands" and "Taiwan's Best Benchmark Enterprise" for many years, and is an internationally renowned leading enterprise.
With his persistence and enthusiasm for the information and communications industry, President Du joined Shentong Computer in 1976 after graduating from the Department of Computing and Control Engineering at National Jiaotong University, introducing and committed to promoting Taiwan's first Intel microprocessor and applications. In 1979, he founded Taiwan's first magazine to promote microprocessors, "The Age of Microcomputers" and won the Golden Tripod Award, laying an important foundation for the domestic information technology industry and is known as one of the "Top Ten Key Figures in the History of Taiwan's Information Industry Development".
After 40 years of deep cultivation in the channel field, President Du has used his forward-looking insights into the development of the information and communication channel industry. As early as 30 years ago, Lianqiang vigorously invested in self-built computer systems and digitalization. 10% of the profits made in each year are used in the R&D channel operation mechanism and intelligent computer systems. He successfully broke through regional restrictions and led Lianqiang to expand the overseas channel market.His operating territory has so far spread to 38 countries and regions around the world. In 2016, he further positioned Lianqiang International as an "industrial service provider", and expanded its service scope from "channel services" to "technical services" and "operation services", etc., and fully created the group's business scope.Due to his outstanding business management capabilities, President Du was selected as one of the top ten leaders in the "Top 50 Executive Directors of Taiwan" by Harvard Business Review for two consecutive sessions in 2016 and 2018.
President Du even gave back to the industry and society with practical actions.In recent years, Lianqiang Foundation sponsored Jiaotong University to establish the "Foresight Cross-Domain Biomedical Engineering Building", which promotes Taiwan's advantages in developing the electronic biomedical industry by combining "information and communication technology" and "biological medicine".President Du firmly believes that "the sharing of knowledge and experience is the best way to give back to society." He shares the business philosophy and business management experience with the public in a long time in a column style, and has been compiled into a variety of books, including "Creating the Gene of Generals", "Generals", "Concept Talk", "The Non-Stop Yima (Simplified Chinese Version is "The Hegemony of the Road")", "3% Beyond", etc., to exert more influence.
