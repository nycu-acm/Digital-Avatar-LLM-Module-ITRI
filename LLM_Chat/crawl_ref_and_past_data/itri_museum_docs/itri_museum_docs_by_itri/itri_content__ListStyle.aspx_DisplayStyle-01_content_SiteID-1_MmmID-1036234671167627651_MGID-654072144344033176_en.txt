Zhao Yunjia Academician
In 1998, Dr. Zhao Yunjia co-founded Hermes Microvision, Inc. (hereinafter referred to as Hanweike) with his friends. It is the world's first semiconductor equipment company to research and develop technologies and products by Chinese people.The executive leader led the team to challenge traditional detection methods, promote various cutting-edge technologies of electron beam detection equipment, and greatly improve the efficiency of electron beam wafer detection, making electron beam detection equipment a powerful tool to improve the yield of wafer factories.Under the leadership of the CEO, Hanweike broke the situation that was almost exclusive to the US and Japanese semiconductor equipment manufacturers in the past, and became the leader in the electron beam semiconductor detection market, with a global market share of more than 80%. It can be said to have set the glory of Chinese people in the high-tech industry and is also the best example for Chinese people to stand on the world semiconductor equipment stage.
