Technology introduction
Long-lasting COD/SS water quality sensing module technology.
Taiwan’s river pollution mainly comes from domestic sewage, animal husbandry wastewater and business wastewater.If these wastewater is discharged into rivers without proper treatment, it will seriously affect rivers and soil. Therefore, the government has begun to build automatic monitoring systems in the past few years to maintain river water quality. However, the current commercial water quality sensors are easily placed in water for a long time, which can easily breed biofilms and affect measurement data, and require frequent maintenance and cleaning, resulting in excessive maintenance costs.
Features and innovation
The Institute of Technology has developed "Long-acting COD/SS water quality sensing module technology", which provides composite detection functions with multi-channel spectroscopy technology, and has the characteristics of optical anti-biofilm, which can delay the breeding of biofilms, extend the artificial cleaning cycle from once a week to once every four weeks, reducing labor costs by 75%.Compared with traditional solutions that use potion cleaning or mechanical brushing, this anti-biofilm technology does not require external devices, not only low power consumption, but also reduces construction and maintenance costs, and has low cost advantages. It combines the intelligent diagnosis function of the water quality system to automatically interpret water quality and system health information, and provides affordable, long-term and easy-to-maintain water quality Internet of Things solutions.
Application and benefits
In addition to being used for water monitoring of industrial and people's livelihood, this technology can also be built in water source areas, rivers, ditches, etc. that cannot be supplied with power. Expanding the monitoring coverage will help the government to build a large number of water quality Internet of Things in the future to protect the safety of rivers and people's water use.
