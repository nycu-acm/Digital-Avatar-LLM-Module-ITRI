The three 2022 CES Innovation Awards are introduced as follows:
1. Smart thermal imaging protection system Personal health monitoring secretary
In response to the trend of aging and low birth rate, the demand for "respite services" that allows long-term caregivers at home to temporarily rest has increased significantly.The innovative development of the "Smart Thermal Imaging Security System" which won the Innovation Award at the Consumer Electronics Exhibition is an innovative development of the "Smart Thermal Imaging Security System", which uses AI artificial intelligence and thermal imaging detection technology to create a personal health monitoring secretary. It can not only continuously monitor physiological signals with zero contact, but also promptly report abnormal warnings such as falling and leaving the bed. It also combines portable devices and the Internet of Things to help the remote medical needs of rural clinics and families without any distance, and reduce the burden on caregivers.
2. iPetWeaR smart pet collar, care for pets 24 hours a day
Pet care is also a new business opportunity that has risen rapidly in recent years. The Institute of Technology has locked in pet care needs. "iPetWeaR Smart Pet Collar" is a brand new contactless micro pet physiological sensing product. Through low-power physiological detection radar technology that is completely harmless to pets, as long as this collar is placed on the pet's chest, it can detect and record the pet's heartbeat, breathing and movement at any time. Then, it will receive an abnormal warning notice immediately through the App to make appropriate treatment for pet health. It is also a product with unlimited potential for global pet economy business opportunities.
3. RGB-D AI robot The world's first assisting robot with standard configuration of 3D visual sensing and smart pickup
"RGB-D AI Robot" is the world's first MIT collaborative robot that uses 3D visual sensing and intelligent object collection as standard configuration. Its visual sensor size is 168 times smaller than other brands, the sensing speed is 38.6 times higher, and the reconstruction rate of material reflection is also increased by 2.1 times. Combined with AI artificial intelligence, it automatically marks tens of thousands of training materials to quickly learn the best object collection strategy within 12 hours. Engineers do not need to spend one to two weeks writing and correcting control programs. It is an extremely competitive innovative technology under the global manufacturing automation trend. This technology has also been transferred to Daming Robot, a major domestic collaborative robot manufacturer.
