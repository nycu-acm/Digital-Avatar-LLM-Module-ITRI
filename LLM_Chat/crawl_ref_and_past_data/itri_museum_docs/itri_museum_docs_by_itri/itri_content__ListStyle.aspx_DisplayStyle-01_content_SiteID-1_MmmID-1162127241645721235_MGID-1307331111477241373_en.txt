Technology introduction
AI smart low-carbon inorganic polymer concrete.
This technology combines AI computing and advanced material theory to quickly design high-precision formulas, use 100% recycled resources to generate low-carbon concrete at room temperature, and achieve zero waste and environmental protection goals.
Application scope
Using AI platform and material theory to accelerate material characteristics prediction and formula optimization, accurate formulas can be designed quickly (>80% accuracy) and regulate raw material instability.
Innovatively use 100% recycled materials to react at room temperature to form low-carbon inorganic polymer concrete materials.
Achieve zero waste and low carbon emissions, meet the needs of green building materials and high-tech industries to expand their plants, and also have the advantages of flexible operation and low cost.
Carbon reduction benefits
This technology realizes resource recycling and regeneration. When the product output exceeds 100,000 tons, it can not only continuously reuse resource materials, but also reduce carbon emissions of tens of thousands of tons, providing low-cost, low-carbon and high-quality alternative building materials for concrete and construction industries, effectively meeting the growing demand for safety and low-carbon footprint of slope protection products, airport runways and land vehicle products.
