Reduce the high demand for transportation labor and high costs. Taiwan's first mobile solar photoelectric test vehicle is on the road
Action solar photoelectric test vehicle.
Under the trend of sustainability, renewable energy has become a global trend, among which solar equipment investment has grown the fastest, and the demand for quality inspection of solar photoelectric modules is also increasing.In response to industrial needs, the Industrial Research Institute has built the first solar photoelectric testing laboratory in the country that meets international standards. Now it has "mobile" the laboratory and launched the "mobile solar photoelectric testing vehicle". It is expected that Taiwan will run through the entire country in the second half of the year to provide testing services.
The solar panel was removed and tested, but it was destroyed after being sent back. Who is responsible?It is the pain of many manufacturers.This time, the Industrial Research Institute has built the first mobile solar photoelectric test vehicle in Taiwan. In combination with Tianxing Energy's long-term experience in the verification of solar modules and fields, it works together to develop a mobile solar photoelectric test vehicle. It can go to the solar photoelectric power plant to perform reliability detection and verification of module components, including module defect detection, power generation power testing, module fragmentation or defects and module insulation measurement, improve the flexibility and mobility of test verification, reduce the time and risk of module transportation, reduce the construction cost, and help the industry improve its value.
International verification laboratory that can walk
Considering that some solar electric fields are located in remote areas and the road is narrow, the Industrial Research Institute has lengthened the body of the small truck, and the rear platform can extend up to 7 meters, allowing the tunnel-type solar simulator to be loaded and operated. The vehicle is sealed and extincted, and there will be no stray light when measuring power, and there will be no interference from ambient light during electroluminescence testing. During the module test, the environmental control of temperature and light sources will be carried out. The reliability of the test results is completely comparable to that of conventional laboratories and will obtain the international certification of ISO 17025 testing laboratory.
