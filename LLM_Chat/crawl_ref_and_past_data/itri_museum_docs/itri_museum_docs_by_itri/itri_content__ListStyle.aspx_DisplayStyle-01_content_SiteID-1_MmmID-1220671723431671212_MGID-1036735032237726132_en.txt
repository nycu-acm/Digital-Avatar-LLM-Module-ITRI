Technology introduction
Handheld laser selective cleaning technology.
The fiber laser cleaning machine is equipped with a digital control interface and a single-axis high-speed scanning galvanometer.The handheld design can adjust the clear line width, clear energy and clear speed according to the requirements of the clear area.100 sets of parameter settings can be entered, and six built-in process situations are convenient for users to operate.
Application and benefits
It is equipped with a lightweight handheld laser single-axis scanning head and simple operating panel, which can be 10m away from the main machine, with high maneuverability, and can be easily cleaned for metal oxides (rust) or films, anode films, paint grease contaminants, etc., and has assisted manufacturers in introducing mold cleaning applications.
