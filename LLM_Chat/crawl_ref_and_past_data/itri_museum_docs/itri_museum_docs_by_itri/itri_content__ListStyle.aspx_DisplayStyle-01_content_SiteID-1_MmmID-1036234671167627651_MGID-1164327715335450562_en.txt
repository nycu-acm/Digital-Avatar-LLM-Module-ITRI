Wang Boyuan Academician
Academician Wang Boyuan is the honorary chairman and co-founder of Zhonglei Electronics Co., Ltd., chairman of Jardine Venture Capital Group, and chairman of Li Guoding Technology Development Foundation. He has been deeply engaged in the science and technology industry and venture capital industry for more than 50 years. He has deeply integrated the macro and perseverance of integrating industries and bravely innovating. He has long been actively promoting exchanges and cooperation between industry, officials, academia and research circles, providing advice for Taiwan's scientific and technological development, and promoting Taiwan's local venture capital to go international, becoming an important driving force for the birth of emerging technology industries.
Academician Wang graduated from the Department of Physics at National Taiwan University and obtained a master's degree and doctoral degree in physics from Carnegie Melon University in the United States.Later, he joined IBM in the United States as a senior manager of various computer-related departments, and then went to IBM Corporation as a senior consultant, providing strategic planning and consultation to the company's highest decision-making units.In the 1980s, he was invited by Mr. Pan Wenyuan to serve as an advisor to the Technical Steering Group (TAC) of the Institute of Industrial Technology Research. Later, he integrated foreign experts with Li Guoding to establish the "Executive Yuan Information and Electronic Industry Review Committee (TRB)" to serve as the Executive Secretary and Member of the Committee, providing industrial strategies and technical consultations for Taiwan's information, electronics and semiconductor industries, and actively participating in the planning and suggestions of Taiwan's scientific and technological industry development blueprint; in 1983, he promoted the establishment of the VLSI-TSA International Conference and served as the first chairman. It is still one of the most important semiconductor academic exchange platforms in my country.
He is confident in the development potential of Taiwan and Asia. Academician Wang returned to Taiwan in 1989 to invest in venture capital. In 1990, he established Jardine Venture Capital, invested in more than 260 companies and assisted in listing.From 1997 to 2005, he served as the chairman of the Venture Capital Association of the Republic of China (TVCA), and jointly founded the "Asian Venture Capital Alliance" with venture capital organizations from Asian countries to promote cooperation and exchanges in the venture capital industry in the Asia-Pacific region and lead the internationalization of Taiwan's venture capital industry.Zhonglei Electronics Company was established in 1992. In the 30 years since then, it has become an internationally renowned broadband, small base station, optical fiber, Cable and Netcom equipment company.From information, electronics, semiconductors to venture capital industries, Academician Wang has devoted himself and actively participated in the implementation of the planning, making outstanding contributions to my country's science and technology industry.
