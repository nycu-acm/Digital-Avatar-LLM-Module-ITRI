Welcome to the soft electronics era
Zhu Yueying
Ignite new highlight industries
Optimistic about the potential of the soft electronics industry, the Institute of Technology has increased its R&D funds for soft electronic components and soft displays in recent years, and has taken the R&D of soft electronic components and soft display technologies as the starting point. In 2007, it opened the first soft electronic mass production and development laboratory in the country, and also has the energy of mass production and technology development, from production, manufacturing to use, to assist the industry in mastering all key links at once.
Industrial research of the Institute of Technology estimates that by 2018, the overall market value of electronic paper panels will reach US$12 billion, with an annual compound growth rate of 57%, and soft displays and soft green energy applications will become increasingly widespread; in the future, printed electronics based on continuous reel-to-Roll technology will be widely used in RFID, sensors, batteries, solar cells, lamps, fuel cells, etc. It is expected that the global output value of soft electronic products will reach US$65 billion after 2022, and unlimited business opportunities are expected.
Soft pressure sensing emerges, demonstrating the benefits of R&D industrialization
The soft pressure sensing technology, which won the second place in the semiconductor category of the Wall Street Journal Science and Technology Innovation Award in 2010, has become one of the best evidences of soft electronic innovation technology, reflecting that the market development potential of soft electronics cannot be underestimated!
Seeing that this technology will have the possibility of "omnipresent" product, Hou Zhisheng, who is determined to bring "soft pressure sensors" to the international stage, is currently the general manager of Global Cement's subsidiary "Liyong Global" (formerly the Huanni Electronics Division). He laughed and said that this technology is the report card he handed over for two years in the Institute of Technology Research and Research, and he is also using this new technology to create a new niche for the time-honored company "Global Cement".
"In 2007, a young man went to the Institute of Technology Research and Development. He was attracted by the future business opportunities of soft pressure sensors under development, and then brought different changes." Hu Jiping, the group leader of the Institute of Electronics and Optoelectronics of the Institute of Technology Research and Development, said that the young man was Hou Zhisheng.
At the beginning, the soft pressure sensing application was locked in the boxing gloves, mainly measuring the impact force when swaying the fist, and then in interactive games, such as electronic pianos and electronic drums that sound as thin as paper, using soft pressure sensors to sense the playing force and make sounds. The pressure sensors were also used to make the rider's seat and massage chair.Hou Zhisheng is very grateful to the Institute of Technology for giving him the research space he freely to perform. He started alone and expanded to a research department with ten colleagues when he left in 2010.
When leaving the Industrial Research Institute, Huanmu Technology changed its soft pressure sensing technology and R&D team.Hou Zhisheng took away the original team of the Industrial Research Institute to continue sprinting. In the past two years, it has applied soft pressure sensing technology to 3C products, built a "soft pressure sensor" production line, and launched a small-scale mass production, which is currently the majority of foreign customers. In addition, it has also developed an "ultra-thin luggage weight measurement accessory". As long as you hold the monitor and lift the suitcase, the monitor will display the weight of the suitcase.
Hu Jiping said that the Institute of Technology continues to develop other applications of soft stress sensing technology, including for assisting in the diagnosis of patients with sleep disorders.He pointed out that Taiwanese people spend 1.1 billion yuan a year to purchase sleeping pills. For insomnia patients, they can use mattresses equipped with "soft pressure sensors" to detect. This mattress can record the ratio and time of patients sleeping and light sleeping, and provide doctors with diagnosis; for patients who are bedridden for a long time, they can also actively warn by detecting lying pressure, time, position, force, etc., and assist the caregiver in turning over.
Give birth to rewriteable electronic paper
Cheng Zhanglin, director of the ICITIC Technology Center, emphasized that the ICITIC aims to break through the industry's technologies of "not daring to do it", "want to do it, but haven't done it yet", and "want to do it, but can't do it."Along the way, he has experienced all the ups and downs, but he firmly believes that the "point of difference" will soon become the "highlight", and once there is a bright spot, it can become the "selling point".
Cheng Zhanglin remembers that the most important decision was the introduction of the "continuous scroll process electronic paper" technology that "Kodak" has not yet been successfully developed.Cheng Zhanglin, who once worked for Kodak, thought: "The electronic paper technology under development by the old boss may be suitable for use." With his assistance, the Institute of Technology and Research sent 20 colleagues to the United States for training and successfully undertake Kodak's technology, which indirectly gave birth to the research and development of "repeatable electronic paper" (i2R e-Paper).
Cheng Zhanglin said that this can be regarded as "the second RCA seed project in the history of the Institute of Technology Research and Research", and this time what the Institute of Technology Research and Research went to learn was the immature continuous scroll-based process electronic paper technology, which is more difficult than before.
The introduction of Kodak's continuous scroll-based electronic paper technology is a good start, but it is still very far from developing mass-produced repeatable writing electronic paper. Researchers have worked hard to explore the technology from strangeness, familiarity to application. After a year and a half, they still cannot successfully present a high-resolution pattern on the electronic paper. Until the unexpected stroke of the hair dryer was the result of the research and development.
I remember that at the end of 2008, researchers were still working hard to make high-precision electrode patterns on electronic paper. When using a hair dryer, the paper would immediately turn black by accident. This made them think of directly using the hot writing head of the fax machine to print patterns and text on the electronic paper, so they would no longer have to worry about studying how to pattern the electrodes.At the same time, I also thought that if electronic paper can be printed, it will solve the problem and can be produced in large quantities.Therefore, the team made various applications, making electronic paper into tickets, brand names, visitor certificates, etc., and can be reused. It is not a problem to print 300 times, which is completely in line with the trend of green and environmental protection.Because of this creativity, the "repeatable electronic paper" technology can be applied to various products, such as situational wallpaper, creating advertising and architecture creativity and extending new business opportunities. In 2011, it also won the top 100 science and technology research and development awards in the world, as well as the first prize in the science and technology innovation award materials and basic technology categories of the Wall Street Journal.
