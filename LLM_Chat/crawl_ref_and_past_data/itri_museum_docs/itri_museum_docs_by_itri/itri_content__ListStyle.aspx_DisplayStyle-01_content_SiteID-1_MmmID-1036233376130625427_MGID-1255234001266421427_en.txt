Technology introduction
The tumor exosome enrichment technology of the Institute of Technology combines machine learning and magnetic bead separation to increase the sensitivity of biomarker detection by more than two times, which helps accurately monitor cancer, accelerate the commercialization of diagnostic technologies, and expand the scope of application of precision medicine.
• Exosomes can convey molecular messages and are considered potential biomarkers for liquid slices.
• The Institute of Technology uses machine learning technology to detect exosome surface proteins with low blood interference and high tumor specificity, thereby improving the enrichment efficiency of tumor exosomes.
• By analyzing nucleic acids and proteins in exosomes, a combination of biomarkers for cancer diagnosis and monitoring is established.
Features and innovation
Technical Features
Machine learning technology is used to screen exosome surface proteins with low blood interference and high tumor specificity to improve the enrichment ability of tumor exosomes.
Establish specific exosome enrichment techniques to enhance disease signals and reduce background interference.
The enriched exosome magnetic beads can be directly applied to protein immunoassays or nucleic acid extraction and can be easily integrated into subsequent analysis procedures.
Supports diversified sample volumes and is compatible with size exclusion chromatography technology.
Technical Advantages
Through antibody-modifying magnetic bead enrichment technology, the proportion of tumor exosomes can be increased by more than 50%.
