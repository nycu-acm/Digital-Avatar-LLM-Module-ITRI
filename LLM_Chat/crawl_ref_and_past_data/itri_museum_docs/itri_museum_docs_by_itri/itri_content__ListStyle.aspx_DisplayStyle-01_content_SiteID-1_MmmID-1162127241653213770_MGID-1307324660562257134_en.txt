Technology introduction
Ton-grade ammonia decomposition demonstration equipment.
Imported low-carbon ammonia is used as the hydrogen support to catalyze ammonia at low temperature cracking through high-efficiency catalyst, which has low carbon emission, low energy consumption and high conversion.
Application scope
Ruthenium (Ru) catalyst is used to attach an oxide carrier in a specific magnesium/aluminum ratio to significantly increase the ammonia decomposition rate through acid and alkaline site regulation.Under the conditions of reaction temperature <500°C and high ammonia spatial flow rate, the ammonia conversion rate can reach ≥99%, showing high catalytic performance.
The catalyst is optimized through the surface alkaline strength to achieve long-term stability. In 3,000 hours of continuous operation, the ammonia conversion deactivation rate is less than 2%, effectively reducing maintenance costs and improving system reliability.
Based on laboratory results, the process simulation and amplification are carried out, and the molded catalyst is optimized to ensure that it has high mechanical strength and uniform dispersion in ton-grade equipment, and an ammonia cracking hydrogen production equipment with ton-grade production capacity is built.
Carbon reduction benefits
The integration of hydrogen production through ammonia cracking and CCU carbon capture technology can achieve low-carbon and high-efficiency hydrogen energy production, and can also use the existing domestic ammonia supply chain to seamlessly connect without additional high infrastructure investment, and quickly lower the threshold for hydrogen energy application.Further combine CO₂ for chemical synthesis to create a low-carbon, circular and globally competitive hydrogen-chemical industry ecosystem for Taiwan, and fully support the national net zero emissions and sustainable development goals.
