With three cultural DNAs: "innovative thinking", "open communication" and "agile management", the Institute of Technology has created an environment to attract young scientific and technological talents, and has been recognized by LinkedIn "Taiwan's Best Interactive Employer Brand".
Enterprise scrambles in the next 10 years will become increasingly fierce!The Institute of Technology has attracted the attention of young generations, won the "Top 1 of the Professional Service Industry" survey in 2019 and the Best Interactive Employer Brand in Taiwan in 2019, and won the "Most Attractive Employers Ranking" in Sweden's 2020 Employer Brand Power Survey on Taiwan "Most Attractive Employers" and won the sixth place in the Most Attractive Employers. According to statistics from the Ministry of Education, the number of students in Taiwan’s colleges and universities is decreasing year by year.It is estimated that only 883,000 will bottom out in the 118 academic year. Under the trend of less birth, recruiting high-quality and high-level talents has become an important key to the competitiveness of enterprises!The 2020 WMAE research report pointed out that young people in Taiwan most yearn for working in companies with two qualities: "a dynamic culture" and "a focus on human training development".How to attract millennial talents who value the value and significance of work to devote themselves to technology research and development has become the cornerstone of Taiwan's science and technology industry.The Institute of Technology has created an environment to attract young scientific and technological talents through three cultural DNA: "innovative thinking", "open communication" and "agile management".
LinkedIn, the world's largest professional social network website, recently announced 6 "Taiwan's best interactive employer brands", with Industrial Research Institute being one of the winners, and domestic leading technology companies such as TSMC and MediaTek are also listed on the list.The ranking of the world's largest employer brand power survey "Universum Most Attractive Employers". The selection results show that the Institute of Technology is favored by engineering students and ranks sixth in the top 50 engineering and information technology. Also on the list are well-known companies such as Google, TSMC, Microsoft, Apple, MediaTek, and IBM.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that the Institute of Technology has long attached great importance to talent cultivation, refining the best corporate culture, attracting like-minded talents to invest.In the 47 years since the establishment of the Institute of Technology Research, "innovation" has been an unchanging core value. In recent years, it has promoted the transformation of three cultural DNAs: 1. "Innovation thinking": promote the thinking of blue ocean strategy, encourage colleagues to break away from the framework, not be afraid of failure, try new practices and breakthroughs, and move towards value innovation with market demand as the orientation; 2. "Open communication": Create an environment of open dialogue, establish a close common emotion, and create experience in communication and communication work; 3. "Agile management": encourage teams to adopt an agile and adaptable attitude, promote colleagues to use agile work thinking and customer-oriented actions, keep pace with the market, and improve efficiency.
He Daan, director of the Human Resources Department of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, pointed out that the cultural reshaping of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute is like a journey, designing an employee-centered learning experience, and using subtle techniques to deepen the cultural DNA of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute in the hearts of every colleague.This includes promoting the international talent cultivation bridge project, connecting overseas scientific and technological consultants and foreign bases, establishing a sharing mechanism for Taiwan's independent research and development of smart property rights, and providing more flexible supporting solutions for elite talents to study abroad.And implement the "A+ Team Training Project" to launch customer-centric agile and discourse-oriented training.At the same time, senior leaders are invited to provide e-Coach with management experience inheritance, and work together internally and externally to shape a market-oriented R&D physique, and create complementary and mutually beneficial production and research results with the industry.In response to the impact of the epidemic, the implementation of "new work measures for remote work and video conferences" allows colleagues to flexibly adjust their work style, aim-oriented, improve agile management and create new team results.
With the three major corporate culture DNA of "innovative thinking", "open communication" and "agile management", the Institute of Technology encourages colleagues to pursue innovation in work, open communication within the team, and agile in the organization. Since its implementation, it has received enthusiastic reactions from colleagues and supervisors, and the results have also been shown in work. For example, in response to this epidemic, Jiang Peixin, who successfully developed the "epidemic can open the can" in response to the leadership team, thought outside the framework, and finally created international-level epidemic prevention and detection technology, and obtained all the results in the international QCMD blind sample testing.Team leader Li Guozheng, an expert in finance, led the team to focus on user needs, discover pain points through expert interviews, continuously rolling adjustments, find innovative applications of finance, and develop innovative technologies such as "smart home management system" and "PECOLA Age Companion Robot".Feng Wensheng, CEO of Juliang Information Technology Center, loves Tri-iron Sports, repeatedly challenges the limits of physical fitness and will, and also motivates team partners to challenge themselves, giving birth to Juliang Information Technology Center to become a national AI artificial intelligence think tank.With agile management and innovative thinking, he developed the "machine fault prediagnosis" overall solution and won the world's top 100 science and technology research and development award.Chen Youqi, who loves tourism, deepens the three major cultural DNA under the complete newcomer training system of the Institute of Technology Research and Development, and finds a stage for rapid growth and opportunities for self-realization.

News Dictionary
1. Universum
"Universum Most Attractive Employers" is the world's largest employer brand power survey.The survey subjects cover more than 60 countries and more than one million college students and young professionals participate in global, regional and national market surveys.The organizer Universum is headquartered in Sweden and has a history of more than 30 years. It is a world-renowned employer brand consultant and research company. The 2020 Taiwan’s Most Attractive Employers rankings released in July this year, based on four major aspects, including corporate image and reputation, work and social environment, treatment and career development, and job content, conducted a survey of job seeking intentions for nearly 3,500 students from 37 universities through questionnaires.The statistical range is from November last year to March this year, and it surveyed the most attractive companies in the minds of college students in Taiwan’s three major categories: business, engineering and information technology and humanities.
2. LinkedIn
"LinkedIn 2020 Most Engaging Employer Brand" is a statistical selection based on the number of followers and participation rate performance of the company's LinkedIn page (feelings, comments, sharing, etc. of published articles). In 2019, six companies in Taiwan won awards, ITRI was one of the winners, and domestic leading technology companies such as TSMC and MediaTek were also included in the list.
