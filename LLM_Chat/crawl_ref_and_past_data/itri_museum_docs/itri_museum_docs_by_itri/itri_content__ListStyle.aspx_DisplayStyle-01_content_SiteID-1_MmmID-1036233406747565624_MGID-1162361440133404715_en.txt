The Institute of Technology and the University of Southern California announced its official cooperation online today (26th). We look forward to promoting the next generation of semiconductor trial production and chip design through the advantages of the Institute of Technology's cross-field R&D platform and the ability to connect the R&D and production energy of the University of Southern California forward-looking chip semiconductors.The VIPs in the picture are Dr. Yannis C. Yorksos, Dean of the School of Engineering, University of Southern California (top right), Liu Wenxiong, Dean of the Institute of Technology Research, (top left), Dr. Anthony Bailey, Vice President of the University of Southern California (bottom right), and Wu Zhiyi, Director of the Institute of Electric and Optical Systems, (bottom left).
Taiwan's semiconductor industry is booming, with a complete supply chain and abundant outstanding talents.In order to continue to maintain process advantages and innovation energy, the Ministry of Economic Affairs has long supported domestic semiconductor industry and the Institute of Industrial Research and Research foresight technology to assist in accelerating the R&D of SMEs and startups; with the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research for the Development of Foresight Technology today (26th), with the University of Southern California; USC, a top international academic institution, today (26th). In the future, it will combine the advantages of cross-field integration of R&D technology and forward-looking chip semiconductor production capacity between the two parties, and further conduct forward-looking research on advanced designs and processes such as "heterogeneous integration", "next-generation computing technology" and "next-generation memory technology", so that Taiwan can accelerate its industry to become the leading group of "next-generation computing technology" in the global semiconductor industry chain.
The Ministry of Economic Affairs pointed out that due to the professional division of labor and completeness of Taiwan's semiconductor industry chain, it plays a key role in the global supply chain. The Ministry of Economic Affairs has long supported domestic semiconductor industry players to continue to develop forward-looking technologies, continuously inject resources into small and medium-sized enterprises and startups, and accelerate the gradual mass production and industrialization of small and medium-sized enterprises and startups in large-scale production and industrialization of small and medium-sized enterprises and startups.The University of Southern California has excellent scientific research results and industry-university resources. Its Viterbi Institute of Technology is ranked among the top ten research institutes in the United States. Its chip trial production service center (MOSIS) is equivalent to Taiwan's national chip center. It assists the US Department of Defense, top motor and chip laboratory circuits for long-term trial production, and provides innovative forward-looking chip design and production services. It has currently cooperated with Taiwan's TSMC and internationally renowned semiconductor wafer foundry for a long time, serving nearly a thousand US government laboratory institutions, universities and private enterprises, and also supports the optimization and upgrading of more than 200 small and medium-sized enterprises and new products.Today we witness the research and development and production energy of one plus one greater than two, and we look forward to linking important North American chip partners and laying out the new generation of AI artificial intelligence computing power.
