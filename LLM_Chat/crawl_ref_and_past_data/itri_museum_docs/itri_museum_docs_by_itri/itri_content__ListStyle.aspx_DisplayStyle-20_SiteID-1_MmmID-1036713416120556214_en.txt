ITRI ​​Europe Office
European office.
In order to implement international business and ensure the sustained and vigorous development of our country's economy in the current global industrial environment, the Institute of Technology and Research established a European office in Berlin, Germany in September 1996, in order to strengthen industrial and technological cooperation with Europe.In the early days of its inception, the European Office upheld the spirit of joint ventures of the institute, and used streamlining manpower to learn from Genghis Khan's spirit of opening up the territory, and gave full play to the effect of making a big move to catalyze the application of technology and technology.Recently, in Europe, we have selected excellent scientific and technological research and development and innovation institutions from various countries to establish cooperative relationships and information and networking channels with them. Under the premise of mutual benefit, we will bridge the industries or R&D communities of both sides, assist in promoting bilateral (multi-)lateral innovative cooperation or participating in the EU's forward-looking R&D projects.
Service content
The European Office is the pioneer troops and local service platform for the promotion of internationalization by the Institute of Technology Research and Development. The work first focuses on combining the existing resources of the Institute of Technology Research and Development and Development and Development and creating a mutually beneficial situation between the two sides.The main work items are:
Representative to the Institute of Technology and Research to participate in international organizations to improve international visibility
Assistant as the EU Program Taiwan Small and Medium Enterprises Participation Window (SME-NCP)
Promote industries and legal persons to participate in innovative R&D in the EU and European regions, and integrate into the international R&D system
Introducing innovation and commodityization opportunities
Assist in cooperation with the Institute of Technology and Research
Consultation on European cooperation strategy of the Ministry of Economic Affairs
Assist the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs to implement the cooperation plan with the European region
