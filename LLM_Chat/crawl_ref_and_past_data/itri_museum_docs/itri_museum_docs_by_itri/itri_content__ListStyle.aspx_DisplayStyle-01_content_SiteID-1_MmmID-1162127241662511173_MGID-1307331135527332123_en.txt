Technology introduction
Carbon fiber composite structure lightweight composite water jacket.
The thermal resistance effect analysis of the composite spindle is completed by numerical simulation, and the optimal thickness design of the composite material is carried out to achieve the purpose of lightening the structure.
Application scope
The hetero-binding equivalent strength analysis technology of composite metals was completed, and the analysis technology accuracy was 98%.
Establish a thermal resistance effect analysis model and parameter database of composite spindle water sleeves, and complete the thickness optimization design of water sleeves and mandrel bushings (2.2mm~3mm).
To complete the composite spindle verification vehicle, the warm-up time can be shortened from 30 minutes to within 10 minutes, the axial thermal deformation can be reduced from 42 μm to 3 μm, and the cooling energy consumption can be reduced from 2.25 kWh to 2kWh.
