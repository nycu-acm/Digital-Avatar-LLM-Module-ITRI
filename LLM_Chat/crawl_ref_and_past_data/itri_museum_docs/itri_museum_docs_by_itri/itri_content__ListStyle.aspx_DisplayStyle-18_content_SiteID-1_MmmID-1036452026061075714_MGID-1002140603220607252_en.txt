Fubao Technology Helps spinal injury friends stand up and go out
Written by Tang Zuxiang
Wu Zhenhua, founder of Fubao Technology, has created the FREE Walk exoskeleton robot to help more spinal injury friends get out of the bond of wheelchairs.
As one of the "5+2 Industrial Innovation Plan", smart machinery shoulders the important task of leading the upgrading of Taiwan's precision machinery industry.However, while giving machinery wisdom, some startups have also discovered the warm side of innovative technology.Fubao Technology, newly established by the Industrial Research Institute, developed the "FREE Walk" movement assisted robot from the perspective of disabled people, allowing disabled people to ignite hope to walk again.
"I really stood up!" A wounded man with spinal cord injury and paralyzed lower body, with the help of an exoskeleton robot developed by the Institute of Technology Research and Research team, broke away from the shackles of a wheelchair. Looking at himself, who had been sentenced to never stand up in his life, under the guidance of the auxiliary robot, his feet could stand up. He couldn't help crying with joy. His relatives and friends who witnessed the miracle on the side also had their eyes red with excitement.
"When wearing it, the injured friend showed an expectant and nervous expression on his face. The moment he stood up, the R&D team couldn't help but cheer him!" Wu Zhenhua, founder of Fubao Technology, recalled the mood when he saw his research results for the first time helping his spinal injury friend stand up.But there was only a moment of joy, and he flashed through his mind, "What should we do next to help them get more smooth?"
People-oriented design is close to the disabled.
Wu Zhen, the founder of Fubao Technology, is a doctoral candidate for the Electric Control Engineering Research Institute of Huawei Jiaotong University. In 2001, he entered the Institute of Technology Research and Research for national defense service. Before the expiration of the service period, the government promoted the smart robot industry plan. He was sent to Yingqing University in Japan to learn from the teachings; in 2006, the Institute of Technology Research and Research established a smart robot technology team, and Wu Zhenhua officially entered the field of robot technology research and development.
In the first few years, the team developed service robots, such as maintenance or home robots.In 2011, a group of disabled people asked the government for their request after a bicycle roundabout, hoping to help them stand up again through innovative technology.The Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs handed over this challenge to the Institute of Technology and Research, and Wu Zhenhua took over the task to start Taiwan's first "exoskeleton robot" project to foster birth.
"I have never tried using a robot to assist people with impaired walking in the past. When I received this task, I thought it would be meaningful to help people with paralyzed lower limbs stand up again," said Wu Zhenhua.However, the robots developed in the past were independent systems, but now they have to be loaded on people. What are the precautions during designing? Will the machinery cause the user to be injured?A series of questions made him feel very nervous.
Because the technology of wearable exoskeleton robots is very complex, the R&D team has encountered various difficulties.First of all, it is necessary to design with a "user-oriented" to clearly understand the physiological conditions and life patterns of the disabled in order to create a device that meets their needs; secondly, the structural design should be suitable for the natural use of the human body, so how to integrate the body with the machine without safety concerns and making it easy for the disabled to manipulate is a major challenge.
After many researches, Wu Zhenhua's team developed the first generation of FREE Walk in 2012. The Institute of Technology and NTU Medical College cooperated with the National Taiwan University Medical College to invite disabled people to try it out and improve it based on their feedback. The second generation of robots was launched the next year, with significantly improved thickness, weight and stability.People with disabled people can even wear it on their own, making it easier to move.
My beloved grandmother passed away and made up her mind to start a business
In Wu Zhenhua's career plan, there was no word "starting a business".After the emergence of two generations of robots, the situation changed. When the news report of the robot assisting the injured friend to stand up again was exposed, he received inquiries from all over the world: "When will it be made? Where can I buy it?" But the ICRC only had one year to implement the project. "Actually, I don't know where the next step is?" Wu Zhenhua said.
However, he made a secret wish in his heart, and this cannot be just a successful case in the laboratory."Since the current R&D results have ignited hope for those who are disabled, they must not be disappointed." So he sought resources through various channels, hoping to continue the plan. The Institute of Technology and Research also actively contacted enterprises and inquired about their willingness to undertake technology.
In November 2014, Wu Zhenhua went on a business trip to Europe to inspect local hospital systems and insurance measures, and evaluate the possibility of robots entering the overseas market, and suddenly received bad news that his dearest grandmother had passed away since childhood.Because my grandmother was bedridden due to injury in her later years and was unable to wait to use the robot he and his team developed, she left the world.Wu Zhenhua's inner pain was indescribable, and he felt more deeply about the feelings of those who were disabled and their families. So he decided to start a business and let the R&D results be industrialized.
First launch to Japan and look at the international market
2016 was a turning point. First, FREE Walk won the top 100 Science and Technology R&D 100 Awards, known as the "Oscars in the Technology World". Not long after, he received good news from Wistron to express his investment intention: Fubao Technology was officially established in October of the same year. Wu Zhenhua specially recruited the first generation of experiencers. His spinal injury friend Shu Tianzong worked in the company and participated in product development.He has now developed to the third generation. Each revision makes the FREE Walk lighter, thinner and more convenient to wear, and the adjustment time is also shortened from the first 20 minutes to 5 minutes.
Speaking of his entrepreneurial experience, Wu Zhenhua said that the company needs to be considered more, especially the market facing Fubao Technology is very new, and the relevant ecosystem still needs to be established.He used the comic "Nether of Navigation" to describe the situation of a new startup: "We are moving towards a great channel, with a group of partners working together, some pursue treasures, some enjoy the process, and there will be challenges of all sizes on the way, and there are also many unpredictable situations to overcome."
In terms of market layout, Fubao Technology chose to enter Japan, which has relatively flexible regulations. It recently announced that it will sign a contract with Japanese medical agent USCI Japan to promote it to 15 key hospitals in Japan. It is expected to win the leading brand of global lower body paralysis-specific action assisted robots by 2019; European certification is also being applied for, and after it is approved, both Hong Kong and Southeast Asian countries can be listed; as for domestic, in accordance with regulations, it is still in the clinical testing stage target, and hopes that it can be sold in the medical channel by the end of 2018.
"The current primary goal is to make sales, and then try to develop more product lines with existing technologies, such as mobile aids for silver hair and rehabilitation workers, to meet the needs of more different ethnic groups."Wu Zhenhua said that once the exoskeleton robot operation model is successful, in addition to representing Taiwan's R&D technology, it will also have special significance for industrial innovation. "It proves that Taiwan's high-tech is not only the semiconductor industry, but people-oriented Compassionate Technology can also be promoted to the global market!"
