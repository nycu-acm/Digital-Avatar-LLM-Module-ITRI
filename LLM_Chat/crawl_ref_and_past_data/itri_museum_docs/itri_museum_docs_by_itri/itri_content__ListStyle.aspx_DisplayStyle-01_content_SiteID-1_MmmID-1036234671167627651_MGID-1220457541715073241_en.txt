Tong Zixian Academician
Academician Tong Zixian is the chairman and strategy director of Heshuo United Technology Co., Ltd. and co-founder of Asus Group. He has been deeply engaged in the technology industry for more than 30 years. With outstanding leadership and market insight, he has set three milestones for Taiwan's technology industry: co-founded Asus Computers, and in three years it has become the world's largest motherboard manufacturer; he has become one of the top ten notebook computers in the world with his own brand; he has led Heshuo United Technology to become the world's top design integrated service manufacturing (DMS) enterprise.
Academician Tong is a master's degree in the Institute of Computer Communications and Control at the National Taipei University of Science and Technology, and has won honorary doctoral degree in engineering from Beijing University of Science and Technology and honorary doctoral degree in engineering from National Donghua University.We co-founded ASUS Computer with our colleagues and engaged in motherboard design business. With precise eyesight and solid technology, we became the first Taiwanese manufacturer to simultaneously launch 486 motherboards with IBM, laying the foundation for ASUS's global motherboard leader.
Academician Tong gave birth to the Asus industrial design team, injecting a sense of fashion into laptops, transforming from productivity tools into consumer electronics, winning the first German iF design gold medal for Chinese, and winning more than 100 international awards such as the Japanese Red Dot Design Award and Hood Design. Asus Computer ranked first in Taiwan for many years and became the top ten laptop computer brands in the world. In 2008, the Asus brand and OEM were separated and Heshuo United Technology was established.Academician Tong led Heshuo, combining the electronic professional manufacturing service (EMS) and commissioned design and manufacturing (ODM) industries to create an emerging DMS model, providing customers with consistent processes from creative design to systematic production and manufacturing services. It is necessary to do "Information 3M" and cover a variety of fields, including IC carrier boards, contact lenses, metal processing, wireless communication modules, beauty and so on. The group's market value is 340 billion yuan.
Academician Tong has long been committed to innovation, promoting national economic development, assisting public welfare and charity, and fulfilling corporate social responsibilities.Since 2013, he has served as a science and technology consultant of the Executive Yuan.He has served as the chairman of the Taipei Computer Association, the director of the National Zhongshan Academy of Sciences, the chairman of the Taiwan Yushan Science and Technology Association, and the member of the National Science and Technology Committee. Academician Tong is also good at literature and art, devotes himself to art and culture, sponsors art and cultural groups, promotes literary and audio-visual recording industry, and serves as the fifth director of the Taiwan Public Radio and Television Group and a director of the National Performing Arts Center, and spares no effort to promote the inheritance of culture and art.
