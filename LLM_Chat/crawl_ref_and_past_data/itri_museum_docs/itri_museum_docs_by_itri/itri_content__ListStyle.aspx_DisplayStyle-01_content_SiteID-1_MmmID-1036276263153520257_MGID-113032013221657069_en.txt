Industrial Research Institute promotes the international upgrade of AIoT industry chain and works with Arm to create a world-class system verification center

Date: 2024/03/20
The Institute of Technology and Research and Development, together with Arm, a major semiconductor chip core silicon intelligence company, established the fourth verification center after the United States, Europe and India. From left: Ding Bang'an, director of the Institute of Information and Communications of the Institute of Technology, Wu Zhiyi, senior technical expert of the Institute of Technology, Lu Zhengqin, deputy secretary-general of the Taiwan Electrical and Electronics Industry Association, Zhang Peiren, deputy director of the Institute of Technology, Zeng Zhiguang, president of Arm, Deng Yuanhan, representative of the British Office in Taiwan, and John Thompson, senior director of the Department of Software and Ecology of Arm.
The global industry has brought transformation and innovation to new generation AI technologies such as 5G, Generative AI (GAI) and Large Language Model (LLM). Today (20th), the Industrial Research Institute and Arm, the core silicon intelligence and finance manufacturer of semiconductor chips, jointly established the "ITRI･Arm SystemReady Verification Center", becoming the fourth verification center after the United States, Europe and India. With the deep R&D energy of the Industrial Research Institute and the Arm global ecosystem certification platform, it provides AIoT world-class system standard verification services, pushing domestic software to lead the global level in the diversified hardware ecosystem, and promotes the use of AI artificial intelligence and the Internet of Things in Taiwan to align with international trends.
According to the International Data Information Corporation (IDC), the global IoT output value will reach US$1.145 trillion in 2026, with huge business opportunities. AIoT is widely used in automotive, medical, industrial, transportation and other fields. It is also actively developing emerging applications of automotive and satellites, especially in the global and government accelerating the wave of electric vehicles and new energy vehicles. The technological development of 5G AIoT is crucial.The Industrial Development Agency of the Ministry of Economics has long supported the development of the verification design mechanism of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research. With the rise of GAI trend, it is necessary to integrate advanced technology development with international integration.
Zhang Peiren, executive vice president and vice president of the Institute of Industrial Research, said that under the subsidy of the Industrial Development Agency of the Ministry of Economics, the Institute of Industrial Research and Arm jointly built a new IC design platform in 2021, which not only helps new companies combine key IPs to accelerate the launch of internationally competitive products, but also enhance the global market share of Taiwan's IC design industry.This time, we have joined hands with Arm to establish the first "ITRI･Arm SystemReady Verification Center" in East Asia. Through the operation of the Industrial Research Institute, we will closely connect Taiwan's semiconductor supply chain and Arm's global ecosystem. Taiwan's equipment manufacturers can ensure smooth operation of their hardware and software and meet standards, thereby enhancing trust and visibility in the global market.
Arm Taiwan President Zeng Zhiguang said that Arm has more than 60% of the global Internet of Things industry, representing that most IoT applications in the world operate on the Arm architecture.Arm has built Arm SystemReady verification centers worldwide, including the United States, Europe and India, and has obtained more than 100 certifications from the industry around the world.Focusing on the fact that the Institute of Technology Research and Research is an international R&D institution and has a deep link with the industry, this time, we have joined hands with the Institute of Technology to establish the fourth verification center for Arm's global layout in Taiwan.It will assist Taiwan's equipment manufacturing industry in upgrading the AIoT environment, accelerate and expand software developers to focus on product innovation and added value through the standardization of devices and systems, continuously improve the security protection of devices and networking, and effectively reduce total cost of ownership. More importantly, it can connect with the vast global ecosystem partners to grasp the business opportunities of everything.
