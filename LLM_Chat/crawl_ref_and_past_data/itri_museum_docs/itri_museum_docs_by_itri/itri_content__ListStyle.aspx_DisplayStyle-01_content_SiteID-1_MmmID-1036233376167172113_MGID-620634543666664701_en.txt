Technology introduction
LCD panel reuse processing technology.
Taiwan is one of the main producers of LCD panels.According to statistics, the domestic waste LCD panels are as high as 8,000 tons per year. However, due to the lack of appropriate treatment methods, it can only be entrusted to bury and dispose of waste treatment plants.Among them, the most critical issue is liquid crystal.As for the environmental protection level, liquid crystals are mainly composed of benzene rings, cyclohexane, oxygen, nitrogen and halogen. Burying them in the soil will cause harm to the environment. Sealing and adhering to two glass substrates will also restrict the reuse of glass substrates. However, from the economic evaluation, liquid crystals are a high unit price chemical, and can only rely on imports due to patents and other issues.Based on environmental and economic considerations, the best way to treat liquid crystals in liquid crystal panels is to take them out and reuse them, while the best way to reuse liquid crystals is to make liquid crystal panels.
Features and innovation
The Institute of Technology has established a set of reuse treatment technology for liquid crystals in waste liquid crystal panels. The "waste liquid crystal panel reuse treatment system" can remove, purify and reuse liquid crystals, indium and glass from waste liquid crystal panels.Among them, the new product specifications with liquid crystal purity can be increased to 9N are returned to the original process; indium is initially concentrated to 30%, and can be refined as the raw material for indium target material in the future; glass is modified into nano-porous glass adsorption material that can be used to adsorb heavy metals, and the adsorption amount of various metal ions reaches more than 50 mg/g.
Among them, it includes a continuous liquid crystal extraction program, a liquid crystal preparation program, a liquid crystal purification program, etc. The liquid crystal processed by this technology can be made into a liquid crystal panel through actual introduction process by domestic panel manufacturers.This product can not only be displayed normally on screen, but also passes a series of product trust tests.
Unlike other related technologies that are only in the experimental stage or are only suitable for processing part of waste liquid crystal panels, this technology can be used to process various types of waste liquid crystal panels, and has reached the trial production scale.It can process 3 tons of waste liquid crystal panels per day, and the treated liquid crystal has been confirmed to be used to make liquid crystal panels to achieve the purpose of high-value applications.
Application and benefits
This technology can be applied to the processing of increasingly large number of waste liquid crystal panels in China. In addition to taking out the liquid crystals for high-value applications, the remaining glass substrates also have high-value reuse methods;in addition, the technology of mixing liquid crystals for mixing liquid crystals in the panel factory is also applied to the processing of mixed liquid crystals, which are re-mixed and purified and reused.
It also integrates soft and hard technology to assist manufacturers in establishing automated processing lines and saving processing and purchase costs.It has both environmental protection and economic benefits, and is more in line with the international trend of circular economy. It is the best treatment technology for waste liquid crystal panels. It has won the 2017 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Award and Green Technology Special Contribution Award.
