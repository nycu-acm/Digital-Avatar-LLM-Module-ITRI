Technical name:
1.5kW Maglev Pump Technology
Technology introduction
Bearingless magnetolev pumps, which abandon traditional contact bearings that are prone to wear and contaminate fluid in the pump, and the rotor is covered by a solid sealing device that is not easy to break.Through the application of the principle of magnetic levitation, the rotor is completely suspended in the sealing device covering itself without contacting it, and drives the rotor by a magnetic field formed by the coil winding on the side. The sealed box is made of Teflon, and together with the rotor magnet, a pump head is formed, and the fluid flow rate and pressure are controlled by controlling the rotor rotation speed.
Abstract
The bearingless magnetic levitation pump eliminates traditional contact bearings, which are prone to wear and contamination of the fluid inside the pump. The rotor is enclosed within a robust, shatter-resistant sealing device. Utilizing the principle of magnetic levitation, the rotor is completely suspended within the sealed enclosure without making contact. It is driven by a magnetic field generated by side-mounted coil windings. The sealed casing is made of Teflon and, together with the rotor magnet, forms the pump head. Fluid flow rate and pressure are controlled by adjusting the rotor’s rotational speed.
Technical Specifications
The specifications of the magnetolev pump are: power is 1.5kW; maximum flow rate: 140 liters/min; maximum pressure difference: 60 psi; material: PFA, PTFE (high purity fluorine-containing polymer);driver: mechanical contactless active magnetic levitation.
Technical Specification
The specifications of the magnetic levitation pump are as follows:
Power: 1.5 kW; Maximum Flow Rate: 140 liters per minute; Maximum Differential Pressure: 60 psi; Materials: PFA, PTFE (high-purity fluoropolymers); Drive Controller: Active magnetic levitation with no mechanical contact.
Technical Features
The bearing-free magnetic levitation pump has no particle pollution, which effectively solves the problem that traditional liquid pumps have mechanical friction and produces particles and contaminated liquids. It can use the chemical mechanical planarization (CMP) drug liquid transport, high purity, blood and other special liquid transport.
