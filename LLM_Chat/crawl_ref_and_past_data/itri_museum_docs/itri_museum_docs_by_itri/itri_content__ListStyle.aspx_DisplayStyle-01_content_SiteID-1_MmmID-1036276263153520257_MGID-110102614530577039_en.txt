Innovation convergence, leading the world, and the Industrial Research Institute won three 2021 top 100 global science and technology research and development awards

Date: 2021/10/26
The Ministry of Economic Affairs held the "Innovation Convergence and Leading the World 2021 R&D 100 Awards Awards Awards" today (26th), sharing six scientific and technical achievements, including seven top 100 scientific and technological research and development awards in the world, the second largest number of awards in the world and the first in Asia, surpassing China and Japan.The award-winning technical achievements were shared by Lin Quanneng, Deputy Director of the Ministry of Economics, Hu Qiuhui, Director of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics, Liu Wenxiong, Director of the Institute of Technology Research Institute (Fifth on the left), Yang Renda, Deputy Executive Director of the Institute of Green Energy Institute of the Institute of Technology Research Institute Wang Renqian, Director of the Institute of Machinery of the Institute of Technology Research Institute Hu Zhusheng, and Lin Qiwan, Director of the Institute of Biomedical Research Institute, to jointly share the award-winning technical achievements.
The R&D 100 Awards, known as the Oscars in the R&D industry, was awarded a few days ago. With the support of the Science and Technology Project of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology won the Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards in the World with three technologies, including "Promoting the Organization's Integration of Bionic 3D Printing Technology", "Isothermal High-Efficiency Water and Gas Separation Technology", and "Software Definition Enhanced Robot Joint Module", and is ranked alongside the world-renowned MIT, Oak Ridge National Laboratory, University of Tokyo, Corning, etc. This year, the Industrial Research Institute won the top 100 global science and technology research and development awards for innovative medical materials, clean water manufacturing and smart manufacturing, allowing Taiwan and the scientific and technological research and development strength created by industry and research to shine internationally!
Lin Quanneng, Deputy Minister of the Ministry of Economics, said that innovation is an important rock for building industrial development. The Ministry of Economics has long supported the research and development of legal persons with scientific and technological projects, and has won many international awards.It has won 66 top 100 global science and technology research and development awards for 14 consecutive years, showing that my country's innovation energy has been internationally certified. More importantly, most of the award-winning technologies in previous years have actually been transformed into industrial value, and more than 90% of the technology has been transferred to international manufacturers or domestic manufacturers, including Taiwan clay, Formosa Plastics, Fanxuan, Qunchuang, Ruhong and other major manufacturers; not only that, the scientific and technological projects invested by the Ministry of Economics continue to bring considerable industrial benefits. In 2020, the number of technology transfer and commissioned services for the legal person and academic science and technology projects was 4,992, which has led to 59.2 billion yuan in investment by manufacturers. The Ministry of Economics can drive five times the investment of each yuan per yuan of government investment, which shows that it is effective to exert the effect of government resource leverage, guide my country's industries to continue to show investment momentum, and bring innovative water to the industry.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that this year there are more competitors than in previous years.A total of 130 technologies were shortlisted for the top 100 science and technology research and development awards in the world, and only 20 award-winning technologies are from countries outside the United States. Taiwan’s R&D team won eight awards in such a fierce challenge, and performed very well, ranking first in Asia. The Institute of Technology Research and Development further handled three items, one of the best among Asian institutions. Over the years, it has won 47 top 100 global science and technology research and development awards.Not only does it continue to highly recognize the scientific and technological strength of the Institute of Industrial Research, but at the same time, 90% of these innovative technologies have been transformed into industrial applications, creating new value for the industry internationally.
Liu Wenxiong pointed out that before the market demand emerged, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research was the first to develop the technology needed by the industry. The calcium circuit developed ten years ago captured carbon dioxide. Today, ten years later, it has attracted wide attention from all countries for its net zero carbon emission and carbon capture technology. When the industry needs it, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has prepared the technology to help the industry connect to the market. Not only did it win the world's top 100 science and technology research and development awards in 2014, it has also cooperated with Taiwan Sil to build a test plant, becoming one of the helpers for Taiwan Sil to achieve zero carbon emissions.The semiconductor production line machine operates continuously 24 hours a day to ensure that the machine does not fail and before unexpected failures occur, it will grasp the health information of the machine earlier, which is the key to affecting the performance of major semiconductor manufacturers. The Industrial Research Institute has developed "Semiconductor Machine Fault Prediagnosis Software" and transformed into companies such as Sailing Promotion. This technology won the world's top 100 Science and Technology R&D Award in 2017, providing the industry's best equipment maintenance method, accelerating the industry's hard and soft industrial upgrading.The award-winning "Waste LCD Panel Reuse and Treatment System" in 2017 takes out the liquid crystal in the waste panel and purifies and reuses it. The panel manufacturer has already converted to build the world's first factory for extracting liquid crystals. Through innovative technology, it will become a major country in liquid crystal recycling technology. This is a very good case. Taiwan's resources are highly dependent on imports and can drive a business model of circular economy in technological innovation.Benjamin Graham, the father of Wall Street, said, "The short-term market is beauty pageant, and the long-term market is like weightlifting." This can also be used in scientific and technological research and development. Today, I saw many excellent technologies win international awards. After the beauty pageant was successful, we will rely on the marketization and industrialization of manufacturers, so that Taiwan's excellent technologies can continue to shine in the industry.
Paul Heney, deputy chief executive and editor-in-chief of R&D World, said that the number of contestants this year means it is not easy to win awards than before.In addition, we have also seen the efforts and achievements of government-supported R&D institutions in innovative technology, which has had a great impact on the industry and made the world a better place. This is also the unchanging core value of the R&D 100 Awards for many years.The Industrial Research Institute has performed amazingly over the years and won three more awards this year, which is enough to prove that it is the leader in Taiwan's scientific and technological research and development, injecting abundant innovation momentum into the industry.
Award-winning technology introduction
Promote organizational integration of bionic 3D printing technology, both porous and strong
Taiwan's elderly population will exceed 20% in 2025, and chronic diseases and degeneration problems have increased dramatically, driving the demand for bone materials. The "Promote the organization's integration of bionic 3D printing technology" developed by the Institute of Technology and Research will integrate personalized 3D model reconstruction, customized medical material design imaging engineering and 3D printing technology to create a diversified application bone material with high strength, high pores, and easy access to bone cells. It has now transformed into biotechnology, and will establish an innovative implantable medical material system that meets international certification in the future, injecting a new force into the global elderly orthopedic medical material.
Water withdrawal from the air, isothermal and high-efficiency water-gas separation, making water shortage no longer a problem
According to the United Nations statistics, more than 1 billion people around the world have no access to clean water. In addition, extreme climates have raging in recent years, water shortage has become the new normal, and a water resource crisis is imminent.Unlike the general principle of filtration of contaminated water, the "isothermal and high-efficiency water-gas separation technology" developed by the Institute of Technology is the world's first unique technology that can use air to create clean water sources. It uses an innovative film material - graphene oxide film to directly extract water free of nitrogen and oxygen from the air. It has now transformed into Qiding Technology, hoping to use the industry's strength to solve the urgent need for the semiconductor industry's water shortage.
Lighter and better use software definition enhanced robot joint module assists the maternity line toward smart manufacturing
The Institute of Industrial Research and Research not only solves problems for people's livelihood, but also continues to lead Taiwan's industry forward with innovative technologies.Today, smart manufacturing has become a new trend in the global industrial supply chain, and robots with artificial intelligence are an indispensable key technology.The "Software-defined Enhanced Robot Joint Module" developed by the Institute of Technology has the industry's highest torsional volume ratio compared to the robots of the same level, and its own weight is 40% lower than that of similar robots in the industry. Through the building block stacking design, it can quickly construct customized multi-joint module robots that meet the needs of the production line, allowing the joint modules of the collaborative robots to not only be lighter, but also lift heavier items, greatly improving work efficiency.
