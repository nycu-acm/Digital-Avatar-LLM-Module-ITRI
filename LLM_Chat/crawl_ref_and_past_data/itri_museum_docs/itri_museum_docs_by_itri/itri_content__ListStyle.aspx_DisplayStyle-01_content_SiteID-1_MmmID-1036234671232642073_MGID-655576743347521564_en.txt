104th Annual "Academician of the Institute of Technology" Conference
Group photo of the 4th Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (104 years)
Time: December 2, 2014
Location: Conference Room 604, Hall 51, No. 195, Section 4, Zhongxing Road, Zhudong Town, Hsinchu County (Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research)
Chairman: Academician Shi Zhenrong
The meeting of academicians of the Institute of Technology Research was held and chaired by Academician Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of Zhirong Group, in the Zhongxing Academy of Technology Research. The meeting also discussed topics such as how to help Taiwan increase the leading enterprises that gather strength and develop the international market, how to help Taiwan cultivate a new generation of cross-industry integrated leadership talents, build a system of innovation with Taiwan's advantage through innovation systems, and how to deepen business cooperation with international leadership innovation partners and accelerate the development of industrial innovation.The academicians also included: Zheng Chonghua, founder and honorary chairman of Delta Electronics, Shi Chongtang, Chairman of Asus, and Chang Lin Shuhong, Chairman and General Manager of Chun Petrochemical, Li Bingjie, Chairman of Jingdian, Lin Baili, Chairman of Guangda, Chen Xingshi, Chairman of Ronggang, Chen Xianzhang, Vice President of Changchun Group General Management Office, Lin Benjian, Deputy General Manager of TSMC R&D, Cai Mingjie, Chairman of MediaTek, Zhao Yunjia, Executive Director of Hanwei Technology, Hu Dinghua, Chairman of Jianbang Venture Capital, Lu Zhiyuan, General Manager and Founder and Chairman of Wanghong Electronics, Shi Min, Academician of China Research Institute, Hong Zhenhai, Chairman of Ruhong Enterprise, Yu Huanian, Academician of China Research Institute, and Lin Genghua, Academician of China Research Institute, etc., a total of 17 academicians attended the meeting.
Meeting conclusion focus
Help Taiwan increase its leading companies gathering strength and develop international markets
Taiwan must use design thinking and new business models to continuously make innovative breakthroughs from 0 to 1, and then exert Taiwan's horizontal expansion capabilities from 1 to N.
The formation of leading enterprises requires cross-border cooperation.
Only by building a platform and assisting in shaping an organizational culture of innovative breakthroughs can we achieve our achievements and amplify market value.
The policy focus directions such as the medical equipment industry, agriculture combined with ICT, intelligent energy systems, and energy conservation and carbon reduction.
Help Taiwan cultivate a new generation of cross-industry integrated leaders and create an innovation system with Taiwan's advantages through innovation systems
Cultivate a new generation of cross-industry integrated leaders, system architecture and talents to create new needs.
Senior leaders take risks and allow failure to pass on to young people to quickly improve their abilities, so that young people can invest.
Deepen business cooperation with international leadership innovation partners and accelerate the development of industrial innovation
We must think from the pattern of international competition and grasp the new direction of Taiwan's future industrial development.
Taiwan must cooperate with environmental details such as tax laws and international talent attraction.
