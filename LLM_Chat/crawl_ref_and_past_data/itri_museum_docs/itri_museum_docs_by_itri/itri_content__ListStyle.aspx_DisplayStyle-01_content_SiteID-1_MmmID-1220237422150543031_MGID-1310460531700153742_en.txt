Technology introduction
Domestic rail maintenance and testing equipment and components
Taiwan track operators have long relied on original factory supply equipment and components, resulting in high procurement costs.In order to improve independence and reduce costs, the Institute of Technology has integrated Taiwan's smart machinery manufacturing energy and is committed to developing "domestic rail maintenance and testing equipment and components" to assist in establishing a complete domestic supply chain and increasing the resilience of my country's infrastructure.
Application and benefits
At present, a number of domestic maintenance and testing equipment, electric vehicle door controllers and other components have been completed. They have been fully launched to create high-efficiency and meet domestic needs, realize industrial integration and technological innovation, and successfully get rid of the dependence on the original factory. This not only reduces the cost of equipment procurement, but also promotes the development of the domestic smart machinery industry, strengthens Taiwan's track technology independent resilience, and enhances international competitiveness.
