Technology introduction
Plasma solid gas source module technology
Fluorine-containing gas is a high-heating potential greenhouse gas, whose heating potential is more than 20,000 times that of carbon dioxide, and is mainly used in photoelectric and semiconductor processes.In response to the 2050 net zero emission target and reducing the global warming potential (GWP), it is imperative to adopt low-carbon fluorine-containing bodies such as COF2 and F2.The Institute of Technology has developed the "plasty solid gas source module technology", using solid polymer materials to replace the traditional high-GWP gas (such as CF4) process, and has successfully achieved carbon reduction equivalent of 33% in the field, significantly reducing the impact on the environment.
Application and benefits
This technology combines innovations in plasma equipment and gas source modules, and cooperates with domestic PCB manufacturers to complete the introduction and carbon reduction verification. It also further connects electronic equipment manufacturers in series to promote the integration of module technology and accelerate the replacement and application of new low-carbon fluorine-containing gas sources.It not only effectively reduces carbon emissions from semiconductor processes, but also helps the industry improve its competitiveness, create long-term value for the semiconductor and electronics industry chain, and move towards sustainable development.
