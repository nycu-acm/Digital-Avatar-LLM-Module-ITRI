Taiwan's Advanced Laser Application Service Center for Semiconductor and Electronics Industry
Taiwan’s semiconductor and electronics industry is the world leader. In order to strengthen Taiwan’s semiconductor industry ecosystem and deepen the development of advanced industrial equipment and complete industrial settlements, it has established the first Taiwan and the fourth laser application service center in Asia in the southern campus of Tainan Institute of Technology and Research.
The laser process and optical path technology of the Institute of Technology, combined with the advanced laser source of Deshang Chuangpu (Trumpf), guides relevant operators to bring terminal application needs. By improving Taiwan's equipment supply chain, conducting proofing tests and system integration, it provides local rapid proofing services for advanced laser processes in the semiconductor industry.
feature
Taiwan's first advanced laser process rapid proofing service center in the semiconductor industry, with international customized ultra-fast laser source.
It has four high-order infrared, green and ultraviolet light laser sources/femtosecond ultra-fast pulse laser sources, providing customized optical path design, rapid proofing and instant sample detection services.
Assist domestic manufacturers to accelerate their entry into the 5G/electric vehicle/AI terminal application market, reduce the early R&D costs of operators, meet the local service needs of semiconductor manufacturers, and strengthen the domestic semiconductor industry supply chain.
