False advertising instructions for eye control devices on the Internet

Date: 2021/12/17
Recently, unscrupulous elements have stolen and tampered with photos of the Institute of Engineering and Research to sell the so-called "eye control instrument" of unknown origin. This court has taken legal action and the following explanation is given:
1. This product is not developed and produced by our institute, nor is it transferred from our institute. This fraudulent advertisement has nothing to do with the Institute of Industrial Research and the staff of our institute.
2. The Institute of Technology strongly condemns consumers' audio-visual behavior in the name of the Institute of Technology and the use of synthetic photos to confuse consumers' audio-visual activities, and has been held accountable in accordance with the law.We hereby clarify to face the audio and feel free to be wary of false advertising to protect our own rights and interests.
