Technology introduction
Advantageous strain embedding technology of biochar carrier
Biotreatment technology can be used for people's livelihood purposes, pollution treatment, aquaculture water quality, pest control, soil optimization and other purposes. Microorganisms can be cultivated in an appropriate environment to reach 108CFU/mL, or even 109CFU/mL, but the number of bacteria will also decrease as the process is used. Developing a suitable support body for microorganisms to grow can not only increase the reproduction of microorganisms, but also improve the treatment efficiency and extend the life of the treatment system.
Features and innovation
Biochar stents that can be embedded in microorganisms are developed with biomass as the target.Carbonization technology can solve the problem of disposal of agricultural residues. Biochar can be made into a biological stent through modification and activation technology, replacing chemical synthesis and natural stents, solving problems such as difficult back-end processing of synthetic materials, secondary pollution, and excessive exploitation of natural stents.The microbial embedded biochar stage is also developing patented products of one-pot fermentation equipment, which not only reduces cumbersome operating procedures and reduces contamination of miscellaneous bacteria, but also brings production capacity close to mass production scale.
Application and benefits
Biochar stents are used in soil optimization to reduce carbon emissions, without secondary pollution problems, and reduce the benefits of over-development of natural stents.
One-pot fermentation equipment has multiple characteristics and the goal of expanding and engaging in bacteria to achieve production quantification, reducing the original cumbersome procedures and steps, and the product production capacity is close to the mass production scale.
The tandem biochar carbonization technology, activation and modification carrier development technology, and microbial embedding technology are suitable for processing units in different situations, extending the system life and increasing processing efficiency.
