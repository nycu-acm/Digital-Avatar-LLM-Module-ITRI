Extend the life of lithium batteries and allow electric vehicles to run 300 kilometers more
Written by/Liu Lihui Photography/Huang Dingxiang
It’s so troublesome to charge and replace electric cars frequently?The Institute of Technology and Research has innovatively developed the "Chemical SEI modified long-endurance electric vehicle lithium battery" to apply anti-aging mask on lithium batteries, which can double the endurance and extend the life span to ten years, creating unlimited possibilities for the popularization of electric vehicles and energy storage equipment.
The Institute of Technology has created the "nanometer-level SEI protective film" technology to solve the problems of short life and insufficient battery life at one time.
Electric vehicles have been developing for more than ten years and are still unable to be fully popularized. The key factor lies in the "battery" of the key components of electric vehicles.Pan Jinping, head of the Energy Storage Materials and Technology Research Group of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering, pointed out that at this stage, electric vehicles mainly use lithium batteries, which have problems such as safety, insufficient endurance, and insufficient life span, so it is still difficult to replace fuel vehicles.
Faced with the bottleneck of the development of electric vehicles, the Institute of Technology and Research has solved the safety concerns of electric vehicle lithium batteries with innovative battery materials from "Self Terminated Olygomers with hyper-branched Architecture; STOBA" many years ago. It has developed the "Chemical SEI modified long-endurance electric vehicle lithium battery" with its original "nano-level SEI protective film" technology, which not only increases the life of lithium batteries for ten years, but also doubles the endurance of ordinary lithium batteries.It solves the problems of short life and insufficient endurance of lithium batteries at one time. It is expected to accelerate the penetration rate of global electric vehicles and contribute to the sustainable development of the earth's environmental protection.

The "Chemical SEI modified long-endurance electric vehicle lithium battery" of the Institute of Technology adds the battery positive electrode material during the mixing process, just like applying skin care products. Even when facing unfavorable environments such as wind, sun and high temperature, it can still form a protective film for the lithium battery, slowing down the "aging" of the battery, increasing the number of charge and discharge times and longer life.The lithium battery modified by this technology can extend the battery life of about 70% compared to the unmodified materials.
Lithium batteries can also apply "face mask" to retain youth
"After using lithium batteries used in mobile phones, the battery life will gradually shorten after a period of use, and the battery life will be only two to three years at most," Pan Jinping explained. This is because during the charging and discharging process of lithium batteries, organic solvents will attack the electrode materials, produce electrochemical reactions to form a solid electrolyte interface mask (SEI). The SEI continues to accumulate, resulting in the lithium battery's power storage repeatedly and it will eventually be unavailable.
To solve this problem, the Industrial Research Institute uses chemical method to directly cover the surface of the electrode material with excellent stability, covering the "nanometer-level SEI film" with excellent stability to protect the electrode material, which can effectively reduce the degree of damage caused by the lithium battery to organic solvent during charging and discharging."This is like applying a layer of maintenance solution and sunscreen on the face, providing a layer of protection to the battery to extend the cycle life of the battery and enhance its endurance," said Wu Hongjun, research director of the Lithium Battery Reliability Design Research Office of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering. Just like applying a facial mask to reduce keratin hyperplasia and slow down skin aging, a "nano-level SEI film" can also reduce SEI hyperplasia and slow down battery damage.
Battery life reaches ten years, battery life doubles
In fact, the Industrial Research Institute has accumulated more than ten years of R&D energy in the field of lithium batteries.Pan Jinping recalled that the initial research team focused on safety issues and developed STOBA technology with polymer compounds with nano-level dendritic structures, which can automatically form a thin film to block the flow of lithium ions and electrons when the battery temperature rises abnormally, stabilize the battery and avoid explosions. Then, the team strives for excellence and creates the "nano-level SEI protective film" technology, increasing the number of cycle charges of lithium batteries by up to 4,000 times. Calculated by charging and discharging once a day, the battery life can exceed ten years, and the service life is great.
Afterwards, the research team further used the combination of electrode living materials with higher energy density to push the battery's volume energy density to above 1,000Wh/l, greatly improving the endurance of the lithium battery of electric vehicles.Pan Jinping pointed out that the research team applied the "nanometer-level SEI protective film" to lithium batteries equipped by a well-known electric vehicle brand. It was found that the battery coated with this film could have a range of 500 to 600 kilometers fully charged, nearly twice the original 300 to 400 kilometers.
Pan Jinping emphasized that batteries are the key component with the highest cost of electric vehicles, accounting for almost half of the cost of the entire vehicle. Therefore, once the battery life of the electric vehicle is not long enough, people's willingness to purchase electric vehicles will be reduced.Nowadays, the "Chemical SEI modified long-endurance electric vehicle lithium battery" coated with a "nanometer-level SEI protective film" can greatly improve battery life and solve the problem of frequent battery replacement of electric vehicles increasing costs due to the short battery life of electric vehicles; in addition, the increased battery life can also greatly improve the availability of electric vehicles, which will help increase consumers' willingness to switch from fuel vehicles to electric vehicles.
Used for household energy storage and accelerate the promotion of clean energy
Since the "nanometer-level SEI protective film" has greatly overcome the endurance and life of electric vehicle lithium batteries, it has quickly gained favor from manufacturers. It has now transferred technology to many domestic lithium battery factories; it is currently working with electric motorcycle manufacturers to introduce demonstration verification and testing, and it is expected to successfully promote the "nanometer-level SEI protective film" to commercialization.
In addition to electric vehicle applications, in response to the government's development of non-nuclear homes and energy-saving and carbon-reducing energy storage industry, the "Chemical SEI modified long-endurance electric vehicle lithium battery" can also be used in small household energy storage equipment to accelerate the popularization and application of clean energy.Pan Jinping explained that one of the bottlenecks in the development of clean energy is that the development of energy storage technology is not mature enough and the cost is too high.We look forward to the future "Chemical SEI modified long-endurance electric vehicle lithium battery" that can also play its advantages in home energy storage systems and contribute to the promotion and popularization of solar energy and wind power.
The "Chemical SEI modified long-endurance electric vehicle lithium battery" of the Institute of Technology has opened up a more promising future for lithium batteries, the most common energy storage technology in existence. Therefore, it has been recognized internationally and shortlisted for the 2017 Global Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Awards, which not only brings glory to Taiwan's scientific and technological innovation, but also greatly enhances the competitiveness of my country's green energy industry.
