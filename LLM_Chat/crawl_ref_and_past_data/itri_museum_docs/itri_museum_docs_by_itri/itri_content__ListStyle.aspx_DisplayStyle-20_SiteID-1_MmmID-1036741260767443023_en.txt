Introduction to the Open Laboratory
Open lab
In order to promote joint research and development of industries and cross-field technology exchanges and cooperation, and to assist manufacturers to gain a greater niche in a highly competitive environment around the world, in 1996, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research established the first open laboratory in Taiwan in the ZTE campus, providing physical office space for enterprises to apply for entry, which is of research, development, entrepreneurship cultivation, technology promotion and related professional services.Combined with the R&D team, equipment, market and technical information resources of our institute, we will build a complete and sound innovative R&D and entrepreneurship cultivation environment to enhance my country's industrial R&D innovation capabilities.
The second phase of the Restoration Innovation Park will be completed by the end of 2021. It will be completed with the "co-creation and cooperation" model to more effectively drive the connection between industrial research institutes, industries, schools and internationally, form a unique innovation settlement and ecosystem, and enhance industrial competitiveness.
Conditions for entry application
A legally registered company in China.Or an organization that has entered into an industrial strategy alliance agreement with the government or the court, jointly participates with the domestic manufacturers in this court's plans, or a foreign company that has significant benefits for the domestic industrial development.
Cooperate with our institute/center and sign a contract.
