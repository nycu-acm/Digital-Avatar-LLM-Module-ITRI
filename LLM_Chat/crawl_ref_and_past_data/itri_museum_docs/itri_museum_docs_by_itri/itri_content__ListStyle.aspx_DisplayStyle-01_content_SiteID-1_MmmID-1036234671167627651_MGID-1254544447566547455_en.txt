Academician profile
Chen Shi'an Academician
Academician Chen Shi'an is the director of Taichung Veterans General Hospital. He has been deeply engaged in the treatment and research of cardiac arrhythmia for more than 30 years. He has taken professional medical care to patients, participated in clinical and basic medical research, and actively cultivated young doctors, inherited through master-apprenticeship, cultivated people for a century, and improved the regional medical level.As a cardiologist, Academician Chen is committed to exploring the pathophysiological mechanism of atrial fibrillation, and has a leading position in the development of atrial fibrillation electroinflammatory surgery all over the world.
Academician Chen graduated from Kaohsiung Medical College (now Kaohsiung Medical University) and devoted himself to cardiology for nearly 40 years in 1986. In 1999, Professor Haissaguerre of the University of Bordeaux in France successively proposed that atrial fibrillation is caused by ectopic pulmonary vein and can be treated through electroburn surgery, which is a breakthrough new global discovery and opens a milestone in the treatment of atrial fibrillation.In 2000, it also proposed the entire theory of "non-pulmonary venous atrial fibrillation" worldwide, and in clinical practice, it pioneered the use of spectrum analysis electrophysiological signal methods to assist atrial fibrillation electroburning surgery, successfully increasing the success rate of surgery.In 2006, the American Society of Cardiac Rhythm invited the author to write the differential diagnosis and treatment of non-pulmonary venous atrial fibrillation, which was known as the "Taipei Method" by the global medical community.In addition, Academician Chen was also invited to assist countries in establishing an atrial fibrillation electrofibrillation operating room, and actively cultivate specialized doctors with arrhythmia at home and abroad.According to statistics, there are 19 medical centers in China engaged in atrial fibrillation, 17 of which are trained by Academician Chen; 19 countries and 72 medical centers around the world are also trained by Academician Chen, and more than 500 foreign electrophysiological medical staff are trained to benefit more patients with atrial fibrillation in other countries.
After serving as the president of Taichung Veterans General Hospital in 2021, Academician Chen actively introduced smart medical care to improve overall medical efficiency, improve the quality and competitiveness of medical services, and improved Taichung Rongsheng from the most emergency hospital in Taiwan to a 24-hour retention rate of less than 1%. In 2023 and 2024, he led Taichung Rongsheng for two consecutive years and was selected into the top 300 hospitals in the "World's Best Smart Hospital" of Newsweek magazine. It is the only hospital selected in Taiwan and set a major milestone for the digital transformation of Taiwan hospitals.
