Technology introduction
Indoor/Outdoor AMR, an autonomous mobile vehicle.
The independent mobile vehicle solution of the Institute of Technology combines self-driving perception technology and is equipped with 5G networked communication and open vehicle dispatch management platform, which can drive on sidewalks and rugged roads.It also breaks through the existing autonomous mobile robots that can only operate indoors.This system can operate indoors and outdoor environments at the same time, and even across floors. Through the car dispatch management platform, it can operate in multiple machines.
Features and innovation
Can be integrated through Zebra Crossing/V2X No.
Indoor and outdoor mobile/3D positioning and navigation technology
Cross-floor distribution/5G network integration technology
Application and benefits
Provide indoor + outdoor shared AMR services, build "zero contact" epidemic prevention and disinfection, logistics, preservation inspection, and food delivery applications, and drive the industrial chain of 5G, smart transportation vehicles and platform service providers.
