Technology introduction
High-efficiency dual-mode atomic layer coating system.
The semiconductor production process is complicated, and special equipment is required for each process.During the process conversion, it often faces the risks of transmission time, pollution and quality reduction. In addition, advanced wafer processing, the requirements for coating accuracy are becoming increasingly strict.
The "High-efficiency Dual-mode Atomic Layer Coating System" developed by the Institute of Technology combines multiple steps of the semiconductor front-section coating process into an All-in-one coating system to reduce transmission and pollution, improve coating quality and production capacity, and meets the requirements of semiconductor 3D high-deep and aspect ratio pore inner coating, multi-component control, conformal, and nano-film atomic layer process.
Features and innovation
Cleverly integrated into the machine to improve the coating quality and production rate, it is expected to increase the domestic self-made rate in Taiwan from 30% to more than 60%, and can also shorten the maintenance time from 7 days to 1 day.
Application and benefits
This technology has been recognized by the 2023 Global Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Award (R&D 100 wards).
