Technical name:
【Advanced Technology】AI language and image-assisted nostalgia therapy promotes emotional and cognitive activation in older people
Technology introduction
Through multi-sensory stimulation and AI analysis, this project evokes memories of older people and constructs life stories to improve cognitive function and care quality.The system integrates elements such as words, sounds, images, and even smells in the past life experience of the cared person, dynamically adapts to case needs, promotes deep memory extraction and emotional resonance, and structures archives to form a personalized digital memory bank.
Abstract
The service uses multisensory stimulation and AI analysis to evoke memories and construct life stories, enhancing cognitive function and care quality. The system integrates sound, visuals, and scents, dynamically adapting to individual needs, facilitating deep memory retrieval and emotional resonance, while structuring data into a personalized digital memory archive.
