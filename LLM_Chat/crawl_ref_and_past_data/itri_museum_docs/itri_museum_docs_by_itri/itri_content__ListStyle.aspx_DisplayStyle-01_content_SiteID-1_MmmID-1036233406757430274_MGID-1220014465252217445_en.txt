The "Portable UVC LED Flowing Water Module" developed by the Institute of Technology is the world's first smart portable Flowing Water Purification System.
Türkiye suffered a strong 7.8 earthquake in February 2023, causing huge casualties.The Industrial Research Institute announced that it would work with Türkiye's leading home appliance manufacturer Arcelik to set up a special water purification technology disaster relief project.With the R&D results supported by the Ministry of Economic Technology Department, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, "Portable UVC LED Flowing Water Module", jointly cooperated to integrate the UVC-LED sterilization module and ultrafiltration membrane filtration module, and sent the disaster relief operation water purification system to the disaster area in the shortest time. Arcelik specifically thanked the Institute of Industrial Research and Research for scientific and technological rescue, in order to provide high-efficiency sterilization and water treatment tools with low energy consumption, mercury-free, portable and intelligent design, to jointly protect the drinking water safety in the disaster area and help the victims overcome this difficulty.
