Technology introduction
High-efficiency cell preparation modulator can fully and evenly mix biomedical materials with cells, solving the problem that traditional manual mixes cannot be evenly distributed.
Regenerative medicine is a highly anticipated emerging treatment method.It has limitations in using cells or materials as a treatment method. For example, it is difficult to ensure that cells can stay in the affected area by injection, and the application of materials alone also has the problem of limited therapeutic effects.The Institute of Technology has developed an efficient cell preparation modulator, which can fully and evenly mix biomedical materials with cells, solving the problem that traditional manual mixes cannot be evenly distributed.With the combination of cells and materials, the injection of minimally invasive injection gun into an animal model of pig myocardial infarction can improve the repair effect.
Features and innovation
Technical Features
Accurate modulator of cell preparations takes constant temperature quantitative mixture, combines polymer carrier and mixes it evenly with cells
Cell preparations can be used with minimally invasive injection guns for future use in clinical cell therapy
Technical Advantages
Replace artificial mixture to ensure cell concentration, quality and activity
Currently, the test for myocardial infarction in animals can improve the ejaculation rate of left ventricular and improve myocardial function
Benefits and Applications
In order to implement the formulation of regenerative medicine, the high-efficiency cell preparation modulator integrates cells and materials into the three aspects of the system, designs and develops regenerative medicine equipment according to clinical needs, creates new product applications for cell preparations, and provides technical energy for domestic biomedical industry to make up for the gap with foreign manufacturers and helps strengthen industrial competitiveness.
