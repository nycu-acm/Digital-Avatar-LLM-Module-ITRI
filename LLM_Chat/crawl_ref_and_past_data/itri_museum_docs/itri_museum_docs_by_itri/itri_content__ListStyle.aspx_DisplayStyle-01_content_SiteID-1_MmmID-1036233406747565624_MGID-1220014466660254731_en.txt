The Institute of Technology and Research visited the UK in May 2023 to visit the Advanced Manufacturing Research Center.
As a pioneer in Taiwan's industry, the British Office Taipei invited the Industrial Research Institute to visit the UK to explore possible areas of substantive cooperation between the two sides.Faced with the global net zero emission wave, my country's industries and enterprises are moving towards net zero transformation to respond to the international carbon neutrality trend.In recent years, both countries have actively promoted carbon reduction, with clean energy as the core of development, and invested a lot of resources to develop hydrogen energy and low-carbon energy technologies.
Our hospital visited the UK for a visit and exchange from May 20 to May 27, 2023. This itinerary is based on Net Zero, and the topics cover the development and policy promotion of technology such as net zero carbon emissions, silicon photons, semiconductors, etc.; in addition to understanding the energy of the UK's technological and industrial innovation promotion, it also explores and builds future cooperation areas and models between us and the UK.This time, we visited 16 industry, academic and research institutions including the British government agencies, Cambridge University, the UK ARM, the Compound Semiconductor Application Innovation Center and the Offshore Renewable Energy Integration Development Innovation Center, and the exchange results were fruitful.
