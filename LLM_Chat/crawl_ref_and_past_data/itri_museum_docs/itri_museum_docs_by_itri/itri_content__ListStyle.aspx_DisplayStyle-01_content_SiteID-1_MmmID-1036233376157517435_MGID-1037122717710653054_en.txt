Technology introduction
Thermal imaging care technology.
IR thermal image sensing technology was developed by the Industrial Research Institute to complete a home security application system that takes into account privacy, AI recognition and affordable prices.Realize wide-area thermal image scanning and thermal image AI action behavior identification technology, change the existing single-line home care services provided by limited care workers, add safety pre-analysis tools for caregivers, and provide timely and precise safety services with more professional and diverse support systems.
Features and innovation
The dual-axis scanning mechanism is equipped with a affordable thermal image sensing module to realize thermal image AI action behavior identification technology and meet the application of home space care.
Technical Features
Affordable and private thermal imaging wide area scanning module.
Thermal image AI action behavior recognition technology.
Embedded computing module.
Mobile device APP dual notification.
Technical Specifications
It can identify care behaviors such as sitting on the bed, leaving the bed, falling down, standing for a long time, lying for a long time, and sitting for a long time, with a non-response rate of 2%.
Provides 24 daily routine statistics.
Application and benefits
It can be used in nursing homes, long-term care centers, elderly apartments and homes to shorten the time for reporting dangerous care events and reduce the risk of falling.It can be extended to industrial security, machine monitoring and security monitoring and other fields.
Fall detection.
Off-bed detection.
