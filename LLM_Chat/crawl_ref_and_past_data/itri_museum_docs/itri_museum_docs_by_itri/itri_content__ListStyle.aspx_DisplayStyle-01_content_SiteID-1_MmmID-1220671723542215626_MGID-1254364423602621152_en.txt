Technology introduction
Technology module flow chart.
This system is a digital twin system based on the first principles of chemical engineering, and is suitable for production efficiency optimization modules and equipment failure prediction modules.Through a large range of simulated data and on-site data, the best operating parameters and real-time product quality prediction can effectively improve production capacity and production efficiency.This system also provides equipment health (%), calculates health through theoretical formulas and predicts future equipment failure time points, so as to make equipment maintenance decisions in advance.
Features and innovation
1. Instant monitoring and operating parameter suggestions, taking into account industrial safety and capacity requirements to achieve optimal production efficiency.
2. Be predictable and preventive, and conduct monitoring, warning and provide judgment and decision-making before problems occur.
Application and benefits
Application Performance 1: Factory A reaction system improves product yield by 3%.
Application Performance 2: Factory B distillation system saves steam consumption by 7%.
