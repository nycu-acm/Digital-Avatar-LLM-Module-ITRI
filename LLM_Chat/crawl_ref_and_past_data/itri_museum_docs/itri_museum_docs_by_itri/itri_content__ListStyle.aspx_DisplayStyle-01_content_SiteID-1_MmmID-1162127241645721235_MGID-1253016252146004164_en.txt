Technology introduction
Kilowatt-grade HPC cooling solution.
Taking VC Lid (Vapor Chamber Lid) temperature uniformity components as the main body, it can introduce water-cooled plate heat dissipation and immersion cooling, providing a heat dissipation solution for HPC (High Performance Computing) high-performance computing system.
Application scope
VC Lid component development must focus on high heat dissipation capabilities and high strength design. Therefore, we develop optimized design analysis, introduced innovative processes, and kilowatt-level heat dissipation energy measurement technology.The new VC Lid developed currently has reached 1000W of performance, and the thermal dissipation performance is continuously advancing, and the component strength and reliability performance are continuously strengthened.We hope that relevant technology development can help the industry accelerate its entry into the HPC cooling market, establish technical thresholds, and create a new wave of huge business opportunities.
Carbon reduction benefits
In response to the kilowatt-level heat dissipation bottleneck at the HPC chip end, we cooperate with manufacturers to develop a wide variety of optimal heat dissipation solutions.Through the connection between development and the industry, we will accelerate the implementation of the HPC industry market and establish domestic technical thresholds as soon as possible.
