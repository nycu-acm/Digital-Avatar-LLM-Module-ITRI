Six major career developments in the research and development of the industry and research personnel.
The unique working environment and organizational positioning of the Institute of Technology Research has outlined a diverse and rich career blueprint for the Institute of Technology.From exploring the infinite possibilities of innovative technology to practicing cross-domain collaboration, writing a wonderful chapter of new ventures; meeting industrial challenges with a diverse perspective, combining R&D results with social welfare, actively giving back to the society, and creating unique work value for industry researchers.This is the starting point for the dream to set sail and the stage for technological innovation, allowing every step of the research team to move towards a broader future.
Six major development opportunities for industrial research
Explore innovative technology
Taking innovation as the core value, the Institute of Technology continues to use the solid technical capabilities accumulated over the years to promote research and development in application fields such as medium- and long-term technical strategies and blueprints. By continuously innovating technology, it strengthens industrial and social resilience and enhances the competitive advantages of industries and countries.
Practice cross-domain development
In response to the trend of the industrial environment, the Institute of Technology is committed to practicing cross-domain development, integrating science and technology, industry and humanities, and promoting innovation and breakthroughs.By integrating professionalism in multiple fields, we will become a bridge between science and technology and industry, promote industrial upgrading, and create a sustainable future in Taiwan.
Participate in new ventures
The Institute of Technology actively promotes and cultivates new startups, accelerates industrial technology development and nurtures emerging high-tech industries, and provides the support and resources needed by entrepreneurs through open laboratories and entrepreneurial cultivation centers to help realize their entrepreneurial dreams.
Expand multi-industry vision
The Institute of Technology encourages employees to explore and grow in diverse industries. Through cross-field cooperation and project participation, employees can broaden their horizons, deeply understand the development trends of cross-industry, cultivate professional talents with extensive knowledge and innovative thinking, and empower employees to continue to grow in their careers.
Contribute to social groups
The Institute of Technology actively uses its scientific research advantages and uses technology to do charity.Integrate the R&D technology energy of various units in the hospital, and use the assistance of vulnerable and urgent assistance and medical care as the main axis, and work with enterprises to invest together to extend the application of the research results of the Industrial Research Institute into social welfare and enhance social welfare.
Improve self-worth
The Institute of Technology provides professional functional training courses and enriches learning resources, cultivates talents needed by the industry, and enhances self-worth and organizational competitiveness.Through continuous exploration and innovation in the workplace, cross-domain practice, participation in innovation, expansion of careers, and contribution to public welfare, we will enhance the sustainable development and self-worth of talents.
