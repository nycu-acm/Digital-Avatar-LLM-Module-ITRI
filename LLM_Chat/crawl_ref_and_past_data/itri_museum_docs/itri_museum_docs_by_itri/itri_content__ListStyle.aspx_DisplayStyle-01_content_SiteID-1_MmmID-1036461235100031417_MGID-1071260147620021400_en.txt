Service Description
The Industrial Research Institute's power module trial production factory focuses on module design and development, and manufactures power modules with reliable quality to meet the needs of diverse energy-saving and safety concerns such as industrial motors, electric two-wheeled and four-wheeled vehicles, renewable energy, drones or rail vehicles.
The factory is located in the ZTE campus of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute.It meets the qualifications of ISO9001 and ISO14001. It provides customized design, providing complete power module solutions from module performance simulation and analysis, life prediction, module design, component material optimization assembly engineering, rapid trial production, small-scale production and reliability testing of power module requirements. It provides complete power module solutions to assist the industry in targeting application targets and developing high-quality products.
