Welcome to the quantum age
Oral / Zhang Qingrui, Director of Quantum Information Center of Zhongyuan University and Distinguished Professor of National Taiwan University Physics Department, Compiled by Chen Yiru
Industrial Revolution has caused another storm
Zhang Qingrui, Director of Quantum Information Center, Zhongyuan University and Distinguished Professor of Physics Department, NTU
With powerful computing speed and application potential, quantum computers are gradually entering the public's vision, and a new wave of quantum technology revolution is expected to launch in the future. What is the connotation of quantum technology?Where is the opportunity in Taiwan?Zhang Qingrui, director of the Quantum Information Center of Zhongyuan University and a Distinguished Professor of the Department of Physics at National Taiwan University and also a Director of Hon Hai, brought first-hand observation.
I think the important changes in technology in the next 30 years are probably quantum, and there will be no other topics.The United Nations officially announced that 2025 is the "International Year of Quantum Science and Technology". Why is quantum so important?The Department of Physics has four major mechanics, namely Newtonian mechanics, thermodynamics, electromagnetics and quantum mechanics.
100 years after the emergence of Newtonian mechanics, an industrial revolution occurred, and mechanical force replaced human power; then 100 years after the emergence of electromagnetics, an electromagnetic wave revolution occurred, and today we can only use mobile phones.From the previous two revolutions, we know that knowledge is not enough to become an industry, and we need to accumulate about 100 years of energy before it can be formed slowly.Now that quantum mechanics has just appeared for 100 years, it is normal to become an industry from now on.
From a historical perspective, every time scientific and technological knowledge becomes an industrial revolution, it will inevitably change the world, and at the same time, a great power will appear.The Industrial Revolution is the rise of Britain, the Radio Revolution is Germany, and the Atomic Bomb and Optoelectronic Revolution are both the United States. Now that the quantum revolution has emerged, the competition between the United States and China is fierce.Which country wins? Some people guess that it may be a battle between the snipe and the clam, and finally it is India.
The three major fields of quantum technology realize the future meta universe
Quantum technology is not only a quantum computer, but also includes three major fields: quantum computer, quantum communication, and quantum sensors. I think quantum computing is like a spear and can attack, so everyone is more concerned about it;quantum communication is like a shield, protecting you, like a golden bell cover and an iron cloth shirt; quantum sensors are like super six senses, which increase the sensitivity of eyes, ears, nose, tongue, body, mind and mind at least tens of thousands of times, and will have a higher grasp of the world in the future.
These three are combined into the quantum Internet of Things, and the algorithm is the standard quantum AI Internet of Things.Only under this condition can the metaverse be realized because of stronger connectivity, faster edge computing, and safer transmission. Many meta-universes cannot be realized now because the current classical computers and classical communications are not enough to support them.
From November last year to now, the stocks that have risen the most in the United States are quantum stocks, with an increase of more than 20 times. They are known as "quantum monster stocks", but the one that is most shocking is also quantum stocks. Huang Renxun said in January this year that useful quantum computers would have to wait for 25 years. Quantum stocks fell 45% that day.Later, Mark Zuckerberg also supported Huang Renxun's statement and continued to fall 35%.Then Bill Gates jumped out to refute them that there were three years left.
Someone asked me, these are all masters, who is right? I said 3 are correct. Because the quantum revolution is not a point, but a process. Within 5 years, quantum computers surpass classical computers in some places; 10 to 15 years are quantum advantages, and quantum computers surpass classical computers in many places;Huang Renxun’s 25 years are quantum ecology, just like mobile phones today, you can no longer live without the quantum environment. But if you come back to play in 25 years, this world is not yours.You are just working for others. If you want to enter, you have to go in now.
The microscopic world subverts known experiences, measurement is the key
The problem with quantum is that it is really not easy to understand.Quantum deals with the microscopic world, which is completely different from the macroscopic world we are familiar with. Danish physicist Niels Bohr said that there is no thing in the world unless you measure it, but this is not the case in the macro world. The moon does not look at it every day.Is the moon no longer there when I don’t look at it? This is a sentence Einstein asked, but the microscopic world tells you that if you don’t look at it, it doesn’t exist.It’s not that Einstein didn’t understand, he couldn’t accept it because he was a believer in macro experience.
But I tell you, if you don’t look at it, it’s really not there.This is actually logically inevitable, because the ratio of electrons to atoms is like the ratio of an ant to the earth. If you follow the Newtonian particle theory idea and want to find an ant on the earth, you will not have to do anything in your life, just look for an ant there.
Why can we use electrons at any time? Because we are looking for a measurement method to measure, we are not looking for it. It is a probability wave, ubiquitous.The biggest difference between the microscopic world and the macroscopic world is here. This philosophical idea is very complicated, so Boer said that if you have not shocked you, you cannot say that you understand it.In Confucius' words, it is "knowing it when you are confused". It is true in quantum mechanics because it is not your life experience.
What is the biggest difference between quantum and classical worlds?In fact, quantum has no "sub". Turning it into quantum makes many people misunderstand that quantum is a thing. It is actually a phenomenon. In the microscopic world, no matter what is calculated, there is a minimum unit of measurement, which is quantum.
In the classical world, everything is continuously changing, and we created the industrial revolution with the continuous changing physical quantities.The concept of quantum is that everything changes discontinuously. The energy steps and lasers of semiconductors are changed discontinuously. It is like you suddenly discover stairs in public places. In some places, stairs are more convenient than using barrier-free ramps.
Quantum entanglement characteristics Exponentially improve computing power
Quantum mechanics has a very important feature called "entanglement". In fact, the translation of entanglement in Chinese can easily lead people to misunderstand entanglement as foreign because you will not entangle yourself.In fact, the entanglement in French and German is inherent. When two things meet together, they become one thing. If you still want to become two things, you will always make mistakes in philosophical thoughts.
Simply put, once everything is entangled, it is a thing, which is called "holism" in quantum computing, that is, it is operated with its wholeness.If two qubits are entangled together, there will be a 2-power operation space; if three qubits are entangled together, there will be a 2-power operation space.
Quantum entanglement is like the "holy grail" in the computer world.Because of entanglement, exponential computing power, classical computers are always linear and can only obtain local optimal solutions, and quantum computers can obtain the best solutions in the entire domain.
Quantum computing has advantages in computing power, storage advantages, and bandwidth advantages. Most importantly, it is a reversible computer.Nowadays, classical computers are irreversible. They always apply the front things to follow the results, and the process will consume energy; and when quantum computers are operating, they do not consume energy without measuring them, so this is also a green product.
Taiwan can take advantage of this transformation and enter from software and platform
In the era of quantum revolution, many situations will occur. The first is to sell dog meat with a sheep's head. Many nouns will be covered with the word "quantum". In fact, it has nothing to do with quantum, just like what does the atom pen have to do with atoms?If any noun becomes popular, many adjectives will appear at the beginning.
The second is called new wine in old bottles. In the early years, operators did not like to use quantum because consumers would not understand quantum and would not buy products.Now quantum is suddenly becoming popular, and everyone is trying hard to get involved, so there are quantum mobile phones and quantum TVs. In fact, they use quantum dots, but these quantum dots are not entangled, so they are not products of the secondary quantum technology revolution. This kind of thing will happen a lot in the next five years.
What role can Taiwan play in the quantum revolution?The Taiwan National Quantum Team was established three years ago to lock in three major directions: general quantum computer hardware technology, optical quantum technology, quantum software technology and application development. There is progress at present, but international progress is faster because Taiwan has not invested enough money and people compared to international.
But no matter what, under the quantum boom, I think I have to go in even if I buy insurance.Taiwan can take this opportunity to transform from a hardware supplier to a software manufacturer, and may learn Bill Gates' strategy in personal computers and become a software introduction to work. For example, Qiskit, an open source framework for quantum computers, is currently open to resources.
Taiwan’s opportunity is semiconductor advantages.Some peripherals of quantum computers still rely on semiconductors, but don’t think that others cannot surpass them. Some emerging countries in Asia have actively entered the market, and the technology war between the United States and China has been upgraded, and the supply chain of quantum equipment and key materials is being formed. Now quantum is a world trend.Should Taiwan start from scratch or purchase directly?
Many people say that Taiwan has a semiconductor industry, so don’t worry, is this right or wrong?In fact, myth 1 is to believe that nanotechnology is equal to quantum technology.There is a saying that goes, "cultivate oneself, manage the family, govern the country, and pacify the world."Cultivating oneself is like nanotechnology, but the most important thing about quantum technology is entanglement. This is "management of the family, govern the country, and pacify the world". A family does not need 4 saints, but 4 people must divide the labor well. Each person does different things to play the role of the family.
Myth 2 is that Taiwan will definitely enter the quantum technology supply chain in the future.Many people think that there are a large number of quantum supply chains.Taiwan’s semiconductor industry is so complete, there will definitely be opportunities. But in fact, Taiwan “does not necessarily” enter the quantum technology supply chain in the future, because Taiwan is accustomed to making large-volume markets, and the market abroad is not big enough in the early stage, so Taiwan may not be able to enter.In the future, large companies will definitely monopolize the quantum general platform, small companies will definitely follow special needs, and quantum service (QCaas) operators will begin to appear.
Taiwan may not have to make quantum computers on its own from the beginning, but it can become the application experimental field of Asia-Pacific QCaas, focusing on application development in the fields of banking, financial services and insurance (BFSI) and AI, investing in platform interface tools such as API and SDK, connecting the existing cloud and hardware advantages, and forming a country for quantum computer applications and support platforms.
Truly commercial quantum products will begin to be launched from 2026 to 2029, and 2029 is not far away.There are currently nearly 100 quantum-related unicorns and quasi-unicorns in the world.In the past, everyone talked about Silicon Valley, but now everyone is scrambling to be the "Quantum Valley".
It used to be the digital generation, but now it is going to enter the Quantum Generation. People who can understand think about it, and people who cannot understand believe it, learn it, and use it. "The Quantumverse will inevitably come!".
