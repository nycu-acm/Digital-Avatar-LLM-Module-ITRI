Prenatal examination for design and give birth to high-quality chips
Written by/Lai Wanjing Photography/Huang Dingxiang
With the rise of Internet of Things technology, smart terminal devices are emerging in large numbers. To make these devices with powerful computing power "move", chip design is the key.However, how can we predict early in the design stage whether the designed chip is truly low power consumption and high efficiency in addition to meeting the functions and requirements?
The "power consumption and thermal sensing electronic system hierarchical platform technology" of the Institute of Technology has added a magic weapon to Taiwan's chip design industry and further improved competitiveness.
In response to the technological evolution, the importance of embedded system software during chip development has been improved in comparison. The early chip design process was that software could only be developed after the hardware design was completed. From the chip function and specification design and planning to design and verification of prototype chips, and after passing the test, it will be sent to the wafer factory for production. The entire development process takes about one and a half years to two years, and high-complex chips even take longer.If the chip developed only discovered that there were defects such as overheating and power consumption after one and a half years, the chip could no longer be changed and could only be redesigned, which means that the investment efforts of this year and a half are all gone.
Through the "power consumption and thermal sensing electronic system hierarchical platform technology", the development time of the chip model has been greatly shortened, and its accuracy has also been recognized internationally.
Discover problems early to avoid losses
"In early chip design processes, verification can usually be done at lower levels, but when the chip functions become more and more powerful and complex, the verification process must be pulled up to the system level," said Wu Wenqing, head of the embedded system and chip technology group of the Information and Communications Institute of Industrial Research Institute. If there is an environment for simulation in the early stage of design, allowing chip designers to explore the architecture and find out design bottlenecks and flaws, it will not only make the chip performance better, but also increase the success rate of chip design.
How to avoid losses caused by unverified defects during the long development process?This is exactly the focus of the research and development of the "Power Consumption and Thermal Sensing Electronic Systems Level Platform Technology" software team.Wu Wenqing said, "I hope to help chip designers know what problems will happen when applying chips to the system through platform verification and simulation." Through this system-level simulation platform, the development time of chip models has been shortened from more than three to six months to one month, and the accuracy and speed of simulation have also been recognized internationally and was shortlisted for the 2017 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards.
System-level chip verification is not only crucial to designers, but also its impact on end products cannot be ignored.Zheng Liangjia, manager of the design process development department of the Design Automation Technology Group of the Institute of Technology Research Institute, gave an example. Apple iPhone had a "chip door incident". Apple also used chips from Samsung and TSMC, but because one of them had better heat dissipation and battery life, it attracted consumers' attention to the quality of mobile phone chips.The power consumption and thermal sensing electronic system hierarchical platform technology of the Institute of Technology extends chip verification to the function of "exploration process", pre-analyzing which process is better for the designed chips to adopt, and providing correct guidance for designers as soon as possible.
System-level platform can be verified before output
Lu Junming, deputy head of the embedded system and chip technology group of the Zitong Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that if the future use situation of a mobile phone is to listen to music, play games, play videos, surf the Internet or take photos, the mobile phone chip should be tested and checked during the development process to ensure that the chip can meet the usage needs in terms of functions, performance, energy consumption, etc. To do this, the level of chip design verification must be improved in order to complete chip verification from the perspective of software applications within a reasonable time.The challenge is simulation speed and accuracy.
Lu Junming pointed out, "Even if you simulate a simple application situation, such as playing a video for one second, it is a great challenge for the designer, because the time unit of chip simulation is nanosecond, which is 10 to the power of 9 (1 second = 109 nanosecond). This used to take several months of simulation time, but now it only takes half a day to use this platform, which is 200 times faster. At the same time, the patented technology independently developed by the Industrial Research Institute team can also improve the overall accuracy to more than 90%.
The power consumption and heat of the chip are visible through the platform
Lu Junming said that "power consumption and overheating" are the focus of chip design in recent years, so the research team puts the simulation of power and heat into the system. Through this platform, we can understand the power consumption and heat dissipation of different blocks in the chip in various application situations. For example, if designers want to know whether the chip will overheat when playing 3D drawing games on their phones in the future, they can use this platform to simulate to understand the surface temperature of the chip when it is running in a set usage situation, and even know where the chip hotspot is located and when it occurs, greatly improving the efficiency of chip design and verification.
"This technology has been introduced into the industry and applied to actual design cases," said Lu Junming. "To leverage the benefits of this technology, the industry needs to work together to establish an ecosystem to enable the same standards for chips from design to manufacturing," which is the team's goal for the future.
According to statistics from research and research agency IC Insights, Taiwan's chip design industry accounted for 18% in 2016, ranking second in the world, second only to the United States.Entering the era of Internet of Things and artificial intelligence, both chip demand and chip complexity will make a significant leap forward. Faced with the beginning of a new opportunity, the speed and product quality of the chip design industry are the key to the future.The power consumption and thermal sensing electronic system hierarchical platform technology of the Institute of Technology has added a magic weapon to Taiwan's chip design industry, and its market potential is promising.
