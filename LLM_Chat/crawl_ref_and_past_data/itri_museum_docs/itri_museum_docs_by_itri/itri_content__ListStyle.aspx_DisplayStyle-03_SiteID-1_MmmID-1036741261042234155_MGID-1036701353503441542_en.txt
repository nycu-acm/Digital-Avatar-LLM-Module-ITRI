South Taiwan Innovation Park
South Taiwan Innovation Park.
In order to promote balanced development of the north and south regions, catalyze the industrial upgrading and transformation of the southern region, and activate the resources for scientific and technological innovation in the southern region, the Ministry of Economics established the "South Taiwan Innovation Park", introduced legal person R&D units to settle in, and construct a high-quality environment for industrial scientific and technological research and development and innovation.We hope to inject innovative R&D energy, integrate R&D resources in the south, create emerging enterprises and industries, and accelerate the transformation and upgrading of southern Taiwan's industrial transformation and upgrading.
Services
The Joint Service Center of the South Taiwan Innovation Park has set up a single service window for the South Taiwan industry. Its function is to integrate and invoke information related to government-assisted enterprises' research and development plans, and assist enterprises in making full use of government-assisted R&D auxiliary resources.Specific practices include: combining the R&D energy of 12 southern universities such as Chengda, Zhongshan, and South Taiwan University of Science and Technology to establish a tutoring mechanism for assisting enterprises in researching and developing R&D plans.Through the joint service center of the Innovation Park, the government’s relevant innovation and R&D guidance plans and technical resources in the academic and research sectors will be effectively applied to the industry, driving industrial upgrading and enterprise transformation.
Resources available to you:
Innovation and open laboratories:
The park has rich R&D resources, and has introduced legal person research units such as the Industrial Research Institute and Food Institute to settle in, assisting new and traditional enterprises with rich technical energy and upgrading and transformation.
Save multiple costs required for the R&D and entrepreneurship period of new startups and reduce entrepreneurship risks.
It provides more than 90 novel offices, physics and chemistry laboratories, providing a complete R&D and office environment to enhance corporate image and value.

STIR Chuangxinyuan:
In order to support friends with entrepreneurial motivation in the southern region and realize their entrepreneurship, we will promote the "STIR Chuangxinyuan", and use the Nanchuangyuan as an accelerated entrepreneurship platform, and combine various industries and units to provide "free" courses and resource links for entrepreneurs' new creation, effectively shaping the entrepreneurial cluster effect and driving the overall entrepreneurial atmosphere in the southern region.
Talent training, conference room:
Integrate the rich training resources of the Southern region to create exclusive courses for enterprises: Nanchuang Academy, advanced technology, accelerated entrepreneurship development, innovative R&D, enterprise operation management, etc., with an average of more than 100 sessions and 5,000 visitors per year.
High-quality conference and training space, with multi-functional conference rooms and training classrooms, providing venue rental for corporate personnel training, seminars, briefings, and business meetings.
Local SBIR R&D Resource Subsidy:
The Planning Office assists manufacturers in how to use and apply for R&D subsidy resources, and provides consulting services such as project content, qualification application, and project book writing.In addition, the park also held a number of free government R&D innovation resource briefings, government R&D subsidy project writing training camps, government R&D subsidy project implementation seminars, and a number of government project application instructions and written materials for brief introductions.
