2024/7/18 Japan's Mitsui Real Estate came to Taiwan to sign a cooperation agreement with the Institute of Technology Research.The contracted representatives of the two parties were Lin Zhaoxian, deputy general manager of the Institute of Obstetrics and International, and Yu Fuji, executive executive officer of the Mitsui Real Estate, and Su Mengzong, senior vice president and assistant director of the Institute of Technology Research Research, served as witnesses. This contract is based on the principle of friendship, equality and mutual benefit between Taiwan and Japan. In the future, the two parties will jointly promote the industrial ecosystem cooperation between Taiwan and Japan's semiconductors.In addition to signing a cooperation agreement, the Institute will learn about ITRI's technical energy and R&D achievements and visit the exhibition hall simultaneously.
