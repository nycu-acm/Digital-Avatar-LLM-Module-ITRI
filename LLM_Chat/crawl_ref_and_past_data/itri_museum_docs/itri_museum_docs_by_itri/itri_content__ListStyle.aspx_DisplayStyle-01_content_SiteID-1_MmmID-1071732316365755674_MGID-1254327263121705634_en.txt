Technology introduction
Panel-level fan-out packaging technology.
Technologies such as low warping multi-layer RDL, large-size and high-even copper plating were introduced into the panel factory, and the 3.5th generation old production line was transformed into a semiconductor fan-out packaging production line, and IC packaging was carried out using the panel production line, which benefited its square area and had a higher utilization rate than the circular shape of the wafer, reaching 95%.
Features and innovation
Because of the characteristics of the panel production line, the chip and loss of the packaging process are lower, and the circuit line width of the packaged circuit can reach 2mm-10mm level through this technology.
Application and benefits
This technology can use 70% of the facilities and equipment of the panel factory. A 3.5-generation panel production line with nearly 1,000 people can be transferred to a panel-level fan-out packaging production line after simple training. It can effectively activate the panel production line that is no longer productive, and can also drive the transformation of the supply chain of related panel equipment and materials, increase the industrial value by nearly 10 times, and have advantages in production capacity and cost.
