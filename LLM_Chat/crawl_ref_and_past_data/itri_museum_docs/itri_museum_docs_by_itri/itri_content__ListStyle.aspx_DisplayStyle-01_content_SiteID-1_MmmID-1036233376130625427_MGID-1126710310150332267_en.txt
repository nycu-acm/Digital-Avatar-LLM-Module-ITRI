Technology introduction
Automatic liquid change procedures are set throughout the closed cabin, which reduces the risk of artificial pollution in a sterile environment and saves 50% of human labor costs. Through stable cell culture, cells can grow and amplify and cultivate more than 1,000 times from generation to generation.
Regenerative medicine and cell therapy have become the future medical trend.Chronic diseases such as cardiovascular diseases, neuropathy, and diabetes can all be regenerated and repaired through the regeneration and repair of autologous cells to achieve the best medical results.However, the current technology used in cell culture is high, and cell production is mainly based on human operation, so it is impossible to obtain a sufficient number of high-quality cells in a short period of time. At this stage, there is no cell production GMP factory in China, and the GTP laboratory is full of loads, which can no longer meet the huge demands of the industry. Cell products urgently need large-scale, standardized and industrialized production technologies.
The Institute of Technology has developed a "cell production automation system" in response to the application needs of cell therapy. It can instantly monitor the cell growth environment, cell number, and cell type, detect whether the environment is contaminated, and set an automatic liquid change program in the closed cabin to reduce the risk of artificial pollution in a sterile environment, and save 50% of human labor costs. Through stable cell culture, cells can grow and amplify and cultivate more than 1,000 times from generation to generation.This system is the first system with complete functions such as cell culture, quality detection and harvesting and succession, and can be customized and matched with various module applications according to the cell production process.
