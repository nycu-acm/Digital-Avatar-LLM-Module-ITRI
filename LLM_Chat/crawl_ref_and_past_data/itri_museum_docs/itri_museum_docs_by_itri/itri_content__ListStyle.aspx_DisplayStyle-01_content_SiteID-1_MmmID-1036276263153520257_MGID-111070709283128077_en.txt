Ministry of Economic Affairs assisted to spread the news, 5G industry shines international, Industrial Research Institute, Pegatron 5G O-RAN energy-saving dedicated network solution, won the 2022 Small Base Station Forum Award

Date: 2022/07/07
The Institute of Technology and Research and Development worked with Pegatron to develop 5G O-RAN energy-saving private network solutions (5G Energy-Saving O-RAN System), and won the "2022 Small Cell Awards for commercial small base stations" in London, UK, the annual award of the global mobile communications industry.
The global base platform open architecture (Open RAN) continues to develop, driving new business opportunities in the 5G industry. The Ministry of Economic Affairs actively promotes the development of Taiwan's 5G industry and uses forward-looking technology for legal person research and development to drive industrial upgrading and transformation.Among them, the Institute of Technology and Research and Development worked with Pegatron to deliver good news with 5G O-RAN energy-saving private network solution (5G Energy-Saving O-RAN System), and won the "2022 Small Cell Awards 2022" commercial small base station outstanding software and service technology in London, UK.
Global telecom operators look forward to reducing construction costs and accelerating the construction of 5G networks through Open RAN. The Ministry of Economic Affairs has also invested in the development of Open RAN, including the development of 5G small base stations and 5G private networks with Open RAN architecture, and established a 5G open network test platform, which has been recognized by the international open architecture organization TIP (Telecom Infra Project).Under the global net zero carbon emission trend, energy conservation in the telecommunications industry has become a must-have.The Ministry of Economic Affairs has also promoted the Industrial Research Institute and Pegatron to work together to develop energy-saving dedicated networks, hoping to continue to help Taiwanese factories enter the international 5G open architecture network supply chain.
This time, the 5G O-RAN energy-saving private network solution of Industrial Research Institute and Pegatron is a 5G system solution that integrates soft and hard.It won the 2022 Small Base Station Forum Award together with internationally renowned manufacturers such as Qualcomm, Rakuten, Ericsson, American telecommunications company Airspan, semiconductor company Picocom, and American network software company Mavenir, etc., showing that Taiwan's technology and industrial commodity development are on par with the international level.According to the SCF market forecast report, enterprise private network services will gradually become the largest proportion, with an estimated 62% by 2026, indicating that global 5G private network business opportunities will continue to flourish.
Ding Bang'an, director of the Information and Communications Research Institute of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the energy-saving dedicated network developed by the Institute of Industrial Research Institute and Pegatron has three characteristics, including "high construction efficiency", which can simulate the number of base stations with one click and assist in the construction and planning of the dedicated network;"high energy-saving and efficiency", through artificial intelligence and machine learning algorithms combined with traffic monitoring and traffic diversion technology, it can be intelligently allocated to allow idle base stations to rest. Even base stations can be plug-in and play, automatically optimized, monitored, managed and repaired, and reduced maintenance costs;"high elastic application" is to intelligently modularize this system, just like applying the app to different private network applications, such as smart factories, smart hospitals, smart entertainment, smart warehousing and logistics, drones and other 5G vertical application fields.
Feng Zhenyu, general manager of the Sixth Business Department of Heshuo United Technology, said that since 2020, Heshuo has been committed to the product research and development of 5G O-RAN open base stations, providing end-to-end 5G enterprise private network solutions that meet O-RAN standards, and mastering key 5G software and hardware technologies, and has developed the lightest and most mobile 5G Private Network Portable solution, plus it has network function virtualization, network slicing, AI edge computing and smart base station network management systems, and can provide more diverse 5G network architectures according to the field customization needs.The 5G energy-saving dedicated network solution that Pegatron and Industrial Research Institute have cooperated again this time can be widely used in different vertical application fields such as smart factories, smart transportation, emergency rescue, etc. This energy-saving private network solution can provide the most effective performance management for the base station, achieving a private network solution that effectively reduces the operation and management costs of enterprises deploying 5G private networks.
Observe the top three key factors for enterprises to develop 5G private networks, namely, simplicity of layout, cost-effectiveness, and integration with existing equipment.The 5G O-RAN energy-saving private network solution jointly with Pegatron hopes to quickly replicate and strengthen my country's private network applications, and drive the digital transformation and vigorous development of Taiwan's industry.The Institute of Technology has drawn up the "2030 Technology Development Strategy and Blueprint" and will continue to invest in 5G technology research and development in the future, use intelligent energy to develop in the field of sustainable environmental applications, focus on improving renewable energy efficiency, invest in the development of new energy technology, and use green energy to drive scientific and technological innovation research and development, and create momentum for economic growth.
The 5G O-RAN energy-saving dedicated network solution uses smart energy-saving algorithms combined with network traffic monitoring and traffic redirection technology. Without reducing service quality, more base stations can enter dormant and reduce power consumption to save costs.
