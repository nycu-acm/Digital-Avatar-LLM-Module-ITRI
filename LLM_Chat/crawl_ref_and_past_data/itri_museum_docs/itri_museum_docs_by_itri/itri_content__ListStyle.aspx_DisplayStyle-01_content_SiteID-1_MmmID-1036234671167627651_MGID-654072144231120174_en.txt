Academician Hong Zhenhai
Chairman Hong Zhenhai is currently the chairman of Ruhong Enterprise Co., Ltd., leading Ruhong Enterprise to become the largest elastic and functional cloth manufacturer in Asia.Chairman Hong upholds the belief of "remaining the roots of Taiwan", and is committed to introducing high-tech into the textile industry, seeking innovation and research and development of textile technology, promoting the domestic textile industry to be refined and technological, and creating a domestic and foreign sales boom in the upper, middle and lower reaches of the domestic textile industry, and earning foreign exchange for the country with quality and goodwill, making outstanding contributions to domestic economic development and industrial development.Chairman Hong has been involved in the textile industry for nearly 40 years, actively improving the competitiveness and added value of the industry, and has been widely recognized for its focus on operations. In 2010, he was awarded the 31st Textile Engineering Medal by the Textile Engineering Association of the Republic of China. This is the highest honor gold medal in the domestic textile engineering industry, commending his achievements to the textile industry, the country and society.
