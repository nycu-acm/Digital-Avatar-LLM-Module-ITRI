Technology introduction
A new breakthrough in green hydrogen production.
The energy efficiency of the hydrogen production in the fifth grade exceeds 65% (LHV).In the future, it can cooperate with the needs of large-scale renewable energy regulation and power grid scheduling, and quickly adjust the hydrogen production power in response to the changing characteristics of renewable energy, conduct low-carbon hydrogen energy storage or power generation, and improve the utilization rate of renewable energy.
Application scope
It can be applied to electrolytic hydrogen production systems to meet the future large-scale renewable energy regulation and power grid scheduling needs.
Hydrogen energy storage can be transferred to various application departments, helping net zero targets and enterprises to reduce carbon.
Users can introduce the on-site hydrogen production mode, which can be used as it is produced and has the flexibility to move.
The solution adopts AEM architecture design to improve the shortcomings of traditional PEM relying on precious metals and AE, and only requires low-concentration alkaline electrolyte to produce hydrogen.
It can be output by pressure storage, reducing the power consumption of mechanical compression during hydrogen application.
The low-impedance electrolytic cell has zero gap design to reach ampere hydrogen evolution current density and higher hydrogen production power bulk density.
Master the non-precious metal process and doped alloy catalyst technology to overcome the scarcity of precious metals such as rubidium and iridium during large-scale mass production.
