AI listens to water pipes and grabs leaks with great accuracy
Compiled/Lai Wanjing
The master's ears turn into AI
The "Smart Pipe Network Leakage Auscultation System" has evolved to a large number of AI digital detection technologies that can quickly lock the leak point and accelerate maintenance and strain.
Faced with the dual challenges of climate change and increasing water resources, how to effectively reduce underground pipeline water leakage has become a major issue in infrastructure management in various countries.The Institute of Technology uses AI to combine sound recognition technology to create a "smart pipeline leakage auscultation system", successfully transforming the experience of senior audio players into a diffusible smart system, greatly improving leakage detection efficiency and accuracy, providing a new solution for water resource management, and winning the Gold Medal Award for Outstanding Research Award.
With the change of climate, the environment is facing unprecedented pressure, and "water" is no longer a natural and endless resource.However, the average water leakage rate of Taiwan's underground pipeline remains high. According to statistics, 13% to 16% of tap water is lost during the transportation process, with an annual loss of up to 440 million tons, which is equivalent to the water storage capacity of the two Shimen Reservoirs, which is a considerable economic loss.
In order to solve the problem, the Industrial Research Institute has developed a "smart pipeline leakage auscultation system" combining AI and audio analysis technology, allowing previous operations that rely on experience and manual leakage recognition to evolve into a large number of AI digital detection technology. Combined with AI analysis and 5G low-latency transmission, it greatly improves detection accuracy and efficiency, quickly locks leakage points, accelerates maintenance and strain, and brings a new solution to water resource management.
The wisdom assets of traditional listening teachers
Wang Lihua, head of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering, said that in the past, most of the leak detection of tap water pipelines relies on listeners carrying listening equipment and headphones, and step by step along the path of the underground pipeline on the road surface to find abnormal sounds. Based on experience, the leakage areas were excavated and repaired. However, with the decline of manpower, there were less than 80 experienced senior listeners in Taiwan, which seriously affected the leakage check efficiency."These masters with valuable experience are like submarine sonar soldiers who are trapping in the leak world. They can judge whether there is any leakage from similar "boom boom boom" sounds. The team uses AI to analyze the master's identification experience."
From artificial auscultation to AI voiceprint analysis
The starting point of the "Smart Pipe Network Leakage Auscultation System" is to collect a large amount of sound data recorded by the masters during on-site inspections. At present, the database has accumulated 210,000 audio samples, and is accompanied by nearly 834 actual excavation verification records, with an accuracy rate of more than 98%. Through big data modeling and AI training, the system can distinguish between "leakage", "no leaks" and "unrecognizable" sound types.
After having the database, the ultra-low frequency and high sensitivity sound transmitter and reception probe module is used to deploy it on the meter head, fire hydrant or any surface facility of the water pipe pipeline. It is equipped with a sound-vibration coupled filter leakage diagnosis module that can wirelessly transmit data to the cloud platform, and a multi-point active sound field degradation diagnosis module. The collected information is assisted by AI to analyze and judge, effectively improving the sensitivity and accuracy of pipeline detection. In addition, in addition to passive sound reception, the team also developed the "active listening" technology, installing a sound wave transmitting device at the opening of the pipeline, giving the pipeline audio stimulation like a "flute blowing", and using the sound wave feedback mode to determine whether the pipeline is abnormal. The combination of active sonar and passive sound recognition can greatly improve the detection sensitivity.
Wang Lihua shared that the first generation of AI listening system focuses on "point" detection, that is, installing a sensing device at one location, but the team further thought that if the sensor can be dispatched like a delivery platform and quickly deploy points in the entire community, can a sound map be built?To this end, the "Smart Pipe Network Leakage Auscultation System" designed a fleet to carry sensors to patrol the community, compare it with the area by triangular positioning, quickly locking the area where abnormal sound occurs, and even further use "line" detection method for large pipelines such as main pipes that are difficult to recharge on the ground, insert sensors at both ends of the pipeline, and determine the location of the leakage point based on the sound transmission time difference.
Digital optimization of efficiency, accuracy and manpower
Traditional missed-checking operations are often time-consuming and labor-intensive. Now, through AI and GPS positioning, the single-point recognition time is shortened to 3 seconds, and each sound data can be marked to repeat the comparison. The actual data shows that the AI ​​recognition accuracy rate is as high as more than 98%, which is 75% compared to traditional manual inspection accuracy, effectively reducing the risk of misjudgment and misjudgment.The underground detection depth is 1.5 meters. On-site tests indicate that the excavation area of ​​the leak detection point can be compressed to within 3 square meters. The pipe repair can be accurately excavated and repaired. The entire process can be completed within half a day, reducing the interference of construction to water use and traffic.
The "Smart Pipe Network Leakage Auscultation System" is not only suitable for tap water pipes, but can also be expanded to various fields such as fire protection pipelines, wastewater discharge pipelines, industrial oil and gas and natural gas pipelines. It has attracted many domestic and foreign businesses to actively negotiate and successfully sign contracts with international audio speaker agents to export technology to emerging markets with serious water leakage problems such as Southeast Asia.
Faced with the increasing improvement of AI technology, Wang Lihua emphasized that the smart pipeline leakage auscultation system does not want to replace the master, but promotes their wisdom and experience. Since the terrain, pavement and water use habits will affect the sound performance, AI still needs to continue to learn the differences in voiceprints in different fields. The master's experience and judgment are still an indispensable part of adjusting the model. The staff and technology work together to establish a defense line for protecting water resources so that every difficult-to-earth precious water resource is not wasted.
