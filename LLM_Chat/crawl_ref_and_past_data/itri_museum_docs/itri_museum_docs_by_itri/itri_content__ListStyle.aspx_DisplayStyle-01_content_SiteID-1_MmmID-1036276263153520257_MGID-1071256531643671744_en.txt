The Institute of Technology has developed the "bionic polyprotic magnetic bead preparation technology (iKNOBEADS)" to treat cancer. It uses the globally original tentacle design to improve the activation efficiency of T cells and allows dormant T cells to jump again, which is expected to add a new therapeutic tool for Taiwan's next-generation immune cell therapy and expand new business opportunities for the precision medical market.
The precision medical technology of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute is now recognized internationally!With the support of the Science and Technology Project of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology has developed the "Bionic Multi-Channel Magnetic Bead Preparation Technology (iKNOBEADS)" for the treatment of cancer. After winning the top 100 global science and technology innovation awards in 2019, the Edison Awards, which won the "Oscar of the Innovative World" today (19th). It won the award with internationally renowned institutions such as 3M, Dow Chemical, and IBM. The judges unanimously agreed that "the iKNOBEADS of the Institute of Technology is an innovative technology that flips the rules (game-changing)." It has cooperated with relevant medical centers and is expected to launch clinical GMP (Good Manufacturing Practice) level products in the second half of this year, which is expected to add a new therapeutic tool to Taiwan's next generation of immune cell therapies and expand new business opportunities for the precision medical market.
The Institute of Technology won the Edison Awards this year for its "bionic polypropilation magnetic bead preparation technology", and Frank, executive director of the Edison Award.Frank Bonafilia said, "After careful review, the reviewers unanimously agreed that the iKNOBEADS of the IKNOBEADS is a game-changing innovative technology, thus standing out among the best innovative products and services in this category."Liu Wenxiong, director of the IKNOBEADS, also believes that this technology not only demonstrates Taiwan's innovation energy and scientific and technological R&D strength alongside the international level, but also plays a great significance to human health. "Bionic multi-provoked magnetic bead preparation technology" has breakthrough global unique synagogue antennas, which can strengthen and activate immune cells and allow inactivated T cells to "jump" again, making it more powerful to fight cancer cells.Liu Wenxiong also pointed out that innovation is an important driving force for promoting industrial development. The Institute of Industrial Research is not only brave enough to innovate, challenge the international market and stage, but also committed to industrialization.In the field of "healthy and happy" application in the 2030 technology development blueprint, it integrates the research and development technology of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research and the energy of Taiwan's biomedical industry to promote new opportunities for cell therapy research and development.
The award-winning "Bionic Multi-Chapter Magnetic Bead Preparation Technology" this year uses magnetic beads to activate T cells to fight cancer!The use of magnetic beads as auxiliary materials for in vitro activation of T cells is currently the mainstream method for immune cell therapy, and the current commercially available magnetic beads are spherical."Bionic polyprotic magnetic bead preparation technology" effectively improves the activation efficiency of T cells with its unique synaptic structure, and can amplify the number of T cells to a hundred times. The amount of magnetic beads used only needs one-third of the commercially available magnetic beads, so that dormant T cells can become combat troops and have memory.By changing the surface modification substance of the magnetic beads, the "bionic polyprotrusion magnetic bead preparation technology" can also be used for the activation and amplification of immune γδ T cells (note), becoming the world's first and only magnetic bead product used for the activation and expansion of γδ T cells.
The team is currently conducting research and development for blood cancers such as leukemia, lymphoma, and bone cancer. It has also negotiated and cooperated with many medical centers at home and abroad with biotechnical workers. Using "bionic multi-splitting magnetic bead preparation technology" as a platform, it will launch clinical GMP-level products in the second half of 2020. In the future, it is expected to be used in various immune cell treatments, especially solid cancers, so that more cancer friends can benefit, successfully connect with the industrial chain, and become a new weapon for the next generation of immune cell therapy.
Since 2017, the Institute of Technology and Research has won the Edison Awards for four consecutive times with high-speed rechargeable aluminum batteries, fluid-driven emergency lighting, and iRoadSafe smart road safety warning system. This year, it has stood out from nearly 400 products/services/solutions worldwide with bionic multi-protrusion magnetic bead preparation technology. It has won the 2020 Edison Awards with international manufacturers such as Dow Chemical, Adobe, P&G, NVIDIA, Bose, GE Healthcare, Thermo Fisher Scientific, Lenovo, and Osram, allowing Taiwan's innovative biomedical technology to exert its influence internationally.
【R&D Story】
NG magnetic beads become a breakthrough in research
Walking into the conference room of the Institute of Technology Research, a group of R&D teams are enthusiastically discussing the next plan to cooperate with large medical centers.In this team, Chen Tingshuo, the head of the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials Research Institute of Industrial Research Institute, is the helmsman responsible for finding resources and setting project goals.Jiang Peixin, deputy team leader, keeps a close eye on the process and modulation formula, and has a complete grasp of the details. Chen Zhentai, the host of the project, led the team to practice operations on the spot, strive to achieve perfection in the technology, and each other's brain power is stimulated, and everyone is willing to throw out ideas.It is this "iron triangle" with no communication that leads the team to develop the world's unique "bionic polypropilation magnetic bead preparation technology" (iKNOBEADS).
Recalling the beginning of R&D, Jiang Peixin said, "Although magnetic beads are currently the mainstream method of immune cell therapy, all commercially available magnetic beads are spherical and only provided by two international manufacturers." Therefore, in the first year of R&D, the team aimed to present a "uniform circle" appearance of the magnetic beads, and even studied the needs of semiconductor manufacturing processes, and was committed to creating a perfect "uniform circle".The research and development results were quite good at first. After one year of research, the amplification efficiency of spherical magnetic beads alone was much better than that of large commercially available manufacturers, which gave the team deep confidence in their own magnetic bead preparation technology.
How did it turn into a protruding magnetic beads later?Jiang Peixin revealed, "This comes from a sudden idea." When the team used chemical proportional formula to adjust the production of round magnetic beads, the yield was about 99%, and 1% of NG magnetic beads would have irregular shapes.From the microscope, these NG magnetic beads with antennae look very similar to "dendritic cells" with synapses. So the team thought: Will these multi-pronounced magnetic beads increase more surface area in contact with T cells, stimulate T cell activation, and achieve better quality and quantity amplification effect?
Because it is different from the mainstream method, the team has also struggled. After two months of dialectics and discussion, it decided to go in a more provocative direction."The application of magnetic bead technology in cell immunotherapy is one of the forward-looking plans of the Institute of Technology Research. The so-called forward-looking is to pursue "innovation, breakthrough and difference". Only with uniqueness can we achieve more." So the team regards such ideas as an opportunity for breakthroughs and resolutely embarks on the path of innovation that is different from others.
Preparing multi-split properties is difficult and high
However, the challenge is about to begin: Which polysomalous can best match T cells?How to set the tone for preparing multi-split formulas?How to regulate the qualities of synapses?How to control the relevant product control process?Chen Zhentai, who has a rigorous personality, can only tell himself that there is no shortcut to success, he can only try continuously until he finds the direction and method.
The team also understands that the use of magnetic beads is closely related to human health, and it can be said that it is a slight difference and a huge loss. Therefore, this technology cannot be just "workable", it must be "perfect".Moreover, the treatment process of cancer cells is very complicated. Only by optimizing multi-split magnetic beads and enhancing the stability of the preparation of magnetic beads can the beads be truly introduced into treatment and enter the stage of actually helping patients.
Fortunately, many noble people have helped each other in the process, such as the selfless sharing between the clinical team of NTU Hospital and the doctors, so that the immune cell culture process suitable for clinical use can be completely copied and built in the Industrial Research Institute. With the mutual assistance and cooperation between the two parties, the two places can be synchronized cross-comparison and research.Therefore, iKNOBEADS can be successfully developed in the second year of cooperation and provided for back-end immune cell therapy applications.

Edison Awards
Established in 1987, it enjoys the reputation of the "Oscars in Innovation" in the United States. It commends global innovative products and corporate leaders every year, commemorates the spirit of inventor Edison's pursuit of creativity and excellence. The winners' representatives provide products and services, innovative excellence and leadership to create new projects. The selection criteria are concepts, value, communication and influence. The awards are divided into 15 categories. The winners of the past include deceased Apple founder Jabers, Tesla founder Elon Musk, and Levono President Yang Yuanqing.
