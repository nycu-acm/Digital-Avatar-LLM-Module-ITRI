Date: 2019/11/11
The "PECOLA Age Companion Robot" of the Institute of Technology Research has obtained the 2020 Innovation Awards of the US CES. It can not only identify and analyze the living conditions, diet and daily life of the elderly, but also detect the fall situation of the elderly through image fall detection technology and issue a notice.
Imagine having AI smart robots at home to take care of your daily life like a family?Or if you don’t ask for a comfortable massage, you can go wherever you put on a piece of clothing?These innovative and exciting next-generation technologies are released!With the support of the Ministry of Economics Technology Department, the Institute of Technology stood out among many exhibitors at the 2020 Innovation Awards of the 2020 Consumer Electronics Exhibition, allowing Taiwan's technology to shine around the world. "PECOLA Age Companion Robot" won the Innovation Award in the Smart Home category, and "iStimUweaR Composite Smart Wearing System" won the Wearable Technologies Innovation Award. These two technologies use AI and intelligent systems to introduce wisdom to technology and add convenience to life. At the same time, the awards include international manufacturers such as HP, Garmin, Nikon, and Bosch. The Institute of Technology is the only research institution that won the award in Taiwan.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that the Institute of Technology has won the Innovation Award of the Consumer Electronics Exhibition for three consecutive years;this consumer product-oriented award proves that the Institute of Technology Research has been recognized for its market-oriented R&D results, and can assist manufacturers in developing innovative products quickly.The award-winning "PECOLA Age Companion Robot" can not only identify and analyze the elderly's living conditions, diet and daily life, but also detect the elderly's fall and issue a notice through image fall detection technology.Another "iStimUweaR composite intelligent wearable system" adds value for low-circular conduction massage on clothes to promote blood circulation, relieve or relieve pain, and has the characteristics of comfort and washing resistance.Both technologies are developed system services that are close to needs based on human needs, and are also the best case for the Industrial Research Institute to extend the advantages of innovative ICT to service value and develop cross-field integrated services.
"PECOLA Senior Companion Robot" provides warm companionship and service
According to statistics from the United Nations, the world population is estimated to be 9.8 billion by 2050, of which the elderly population over 65 years old will exceed 1.5 billion, accounting for about 16% of the total population. Compared with the elderly population in 2017, the population structure is rapidly aging, and the demand for long-term care and services of the elderly population is expected to increase accordingly.In addition, according to the International Institute of Obstetrics and Research Institute of Technology, the global output value of care robots will exceed US$17 billion in 2020, and the demand will surpass industrial robots.
The Institute of Technology has created the "PECOLA Age Companion Robot" and uses Ambient Intelligence technology to care for elderly people living alone to eat well, sleep well, be in good health, and to collect information for their children to achieve the purpose of finding topics between elders and children and establishing communication.PECOLA can set the object to care for through facial recognition, and can follow and accompany and assist. Image recognition technology can analyze the diet and dishes of elderly people living alone, and compare the differences in meals before and after eating to summarize the diet profile.At the same time, in order to care about the elderly’s sleep and physiological conditions at night, PECOLA can also detect the breathing rate during sleep through WiFi signals.In addition, elders are most worried about falling and falling at home. PECOLA uses "deep learning technology" to detect fall incidents and actively dial up to establish two-way video for their children to improve home safety.PECOLA also continues to integrate third-party services to provide all-round thoughtful services such as social entertainment for elders, convenient life, health care, smart family and caring and companionship.
"iStimUweaR composite smart wearable system" The pain relief helps you relieve your clothes while walking
Have you felt sore neck, shoulders and waist when working in the office for a long time?Does muscle soreness often occur after intense exercise? When many people and physical therapists treat patients, they will use "low cycle treatment" to relax the muscles and temporarily relieve the pain. The principle is to input the appropriate current into the body to stimulate the nervous system, so that the patient can produce the effect of muscle relaxation, soothing and relieve discomfort symptoms.The "iStimUweaR Composite Intelligent Wearing System" of the Institute of Technology Research is a health assistance system combining low-circular electrotherapy and clothing. After analyzing the human comfort state through the mobile APP, the system can directly provide low-circular microelectric stimulation through clothes based on the physical condition, providing the required personalized muscle strength promotion and pain relief, eliminating the need for wiring of traditional electric films. It is a pain relief helper that can be worn.
