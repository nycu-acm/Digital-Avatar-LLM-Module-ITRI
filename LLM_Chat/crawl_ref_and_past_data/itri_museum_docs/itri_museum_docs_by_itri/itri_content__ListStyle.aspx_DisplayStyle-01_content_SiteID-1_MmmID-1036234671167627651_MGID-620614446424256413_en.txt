Academician profile
Academician Yu Huanian
Chairman Yu Huanian is the honorary chairman and senior consultant of the Forward-looking Technology Steering Committee of the Institute of Industrial Technology. He also serves as the American Technical Advisory League Committee and Chairman for many years. He first entered IBM Corporation in the United States to serve and invest in the research and development of various solid-state component technologies. He has made great contributions in the field of semiconductor component research and development.He is one of the global experts in semiconductor component technology and super-large integrated circuit design. Chairman Yu's outstanding R&D achievements and contributions to technological innovation have made him awarded many honors including IEEE Fellow, academician of the Eurasian Academy of Sciences, academician of the Central Academy of Sciences, and academician of the Central Academy of Sciences.Chairman Yu participated in the research and development plan of my country's electronic industry technology, cultivated semiconductor and high-tech industries, and promoted the integrated circuit project to have a significant impact on the national scientific and technological industry and economic progress.
