107 Annual "Academician of the Institute of Technology" Conference
Group photo of the 7th Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (107 years)
Time: December 18, 2017
Location: Conference Room 604, Hall 51, No. 195, Section 4, Zhongxing Road, Zhudong Town, Hsinchu County (Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research)
Chairman: Academician Shi Zhenrong
The 107th Annual Academician Conference of the Institute of Technology Research was held in the Zhongxing Academy of Technology Research, and was chaired by Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of the Zhirong Cultural and Education Foundation of the Consortium. The academicians attended include Zheng Chonghua, founder and honorary chairman of Delta Electronics, Lin Baili, President of Guangda Group, Chen Xingshi, founder of Ronggang Materials Technology Company, Lin Benjian, Distinguished Professor of Tsinghua University, Yu Huanian, Honorary Chairman of the Foresight Technical Steering Committee of the Institute of Technology Research, Lin Genghua, Academician of the China Research Institute, Hu Zhengming, Distinguished Professor of TSMC at the University of California, Sun Hong, Chairman of Mengli Automation, Shi Qintai, Chairman of Tsinghua University, Lu Mingguang, Chairman of Sino-US Silicon Crystal, Wang Kanglong, Professor of Thunder of the Department of Electrical Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles, Zhu Zhiyang, President of Youjia Group, Miao Fengqiang, Chairman of Lianhua Shentong Group, and Zhang Youde, Chairman of Yi'an Shengyi Medical.In addition, Luo Dasheng, Director of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, Ye Zheliang, Deputy Executive Secretary of the Science and Technology Association Office, and Li Shiguang, Chairman of the Institute of Technology Research and Research, and Liu Wenxiong, President of the Conference also attended the meeting.
Meeting conclusion focus
In order to enhance competitiveness, Industry 4.0 is necessary for activities and investments, it is recommended that the government have tax deduction discounts for enterprises' transformation investment in Industry 4.0, including financing assistance provided by banks, to drive exports with domestic demand and promote economic development.(For example: Currently, the investment in smart equipment purchases is limited to 3-5%, and it is recommended to increase it to 15-20%)
International trade and products are becoming more and more complex, and the development of Taiwan's service industry accounts for a large amount of GDP. The smile curve should be extended from R&D, design, manufacturing, marketing to services, and the establishment of the overall industry's eco-system. The government should actively promote development. For example, BOT should be more open, expand from hardware, including software and systems, and make good use of the new business model outsourcing to promote the development of the industrial economy.
It is recommended that the government's southbound policy should consider long-term energy security and food supply security, and guarantee of innovative talent sources.We can combine technology with industrial development from strategies for energy acquisition and food supply safety to establish a safe and stable supply chain in Southeast Asia.
Taiwan should retain outstanding foreign students to work in Taiwan and continue to send talents to Europe and the United States to study.In the name of the academician meeting, the Institute of Technology asked the government to initiate inter-departmental meetings to discuss policies and regulations for retaining outstanding foreign students (such as Southeast Asia, India) to help the needs of the society with a small birth, the southward direction, and the promotion of internationalization.
Smart urban and rural areas are based on service thinking. Smart urban and rural promotion and Industry 4.0 should be used to drive the development of Taiwan's economy. The government should integrate investment across ministries, and private enterprises can also invest in development.For example: The self-driving electric bus system industry is beneficial to smart urban and rural construction and can drive the international export development of Taiwan's smart industry. It is recommended that the government should include it as one of the forward-looking infrastructure and give priority to investment.
Taiwan's biotechnology development can combine the electronics industry with biomedical medicine, for example, choose applications that FDA can quickly authenticate, or refer to the practice of Swiss companies and set up branches near the certification unit.
The academician expressed concern about the allocation of scientific and technological research and development resources of the Executive Yuan and the reduction of forward-looking funds for the Institute of Industrial Research and Research. It will be detrimental to the economic development of science and technology. It is recommended that the government increase the R&D resources of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and enhance the forward-looking R&D plans of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research.
