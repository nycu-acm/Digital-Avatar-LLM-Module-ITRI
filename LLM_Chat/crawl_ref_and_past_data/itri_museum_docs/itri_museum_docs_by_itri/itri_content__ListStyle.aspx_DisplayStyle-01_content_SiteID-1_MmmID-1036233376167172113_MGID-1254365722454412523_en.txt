Technology introduction
Ethylene oligomerization reaction system.
α-olefins such as 1-hexene and 1-octene are important key monomers of higher-order polyolefin materials (such as C6/C8-LLDPE, POE, etc.), and can be used in high-spec demand areas such as shoe materials, packaging materials, automotive components, solar panel packaging materials, etc. The main production method of α-olefins is to use ethylene for oligomerization, and the reaction is accurately controlled using the catalyst system.Each tends to be trimerized (produced 1-hexene) or tetramerized (produced 1-octene). There is currently no relevant production technology in China.
Features and innovation
This team has established: (1) Low water oxygen environment operation technology, which can make the ambient water oxygen value of ethylene oligomerization reaction <10PPM, fully protect the catalyst system from deterioration by water oxygen corrosion; (2) Ethylene oligomerization catalyst synthesis technology, adjusts the reaction trend to achieve high selectivity of the target product by synthesizing ethylene oligomerization catalyst and catalyst structures of different structures; (3) Ethylene oligomerization technology, including applicable high-pressure reaction systems, feed/discharge control, product analysis/separation and other related technologies.
Currently, through catalyst synthesis and oligomerization reaction tests, a new high-reactive catalyst system has been developed. The 1-hexene selection rate of ethylene trimerization reaction is >90% and the 1-octene selection rate of ethylene tetramerization reaction can be >70%. Application and benefits
Domestic polymer application industries such as shoe materials and automotive components are developing vigorously, but due to the failure of α-olefins and derived high-order polyolefin materials in China, the production capacity and quality are constrained by foreign suppliers.The goal of the development of ethylene precision oligomerization technology is to start with α-olefins, gradually build a domestic supply chain of independent polyolefin materials, and reduce the demand for imported raw materials.At present, domestic manufacturers have invested funds to participate in technical cooperation.
