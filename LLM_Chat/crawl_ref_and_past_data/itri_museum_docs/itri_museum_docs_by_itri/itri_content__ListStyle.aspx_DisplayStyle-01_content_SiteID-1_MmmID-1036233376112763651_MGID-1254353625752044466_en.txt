Technology introduction
V2X See Through Smart Cockpit Technology.
According to statistics on traffic accidents, Taiwan's annual economic losses are as high as NT$500 billion, of which as much as 55.8% of the accidents are due to visual obstruction, and vehicle visual obstruction accounts for the largest number (such as a collision accident).
Features and innovation
Innovative patented technology:
Combined with V2X in-vehicle communication, image recognition, and image stitching technology, it provides the front vehicle penetration and fusion image, providing instant and high-quality front vehicle driving images from the perspective of the front vehicle.
Workshop communication high quality:
The V2X vehicle communication standard and the 5.9GHz ITS special frequency band are used to transmit each other in the workshop, with an update frequency of 10 FPS, so that the point-to-point transmission and operation delay is less than 100ms (the average human reaction time is about 300ms).
Application and benefits
Solve the problem of sight separation in traffic accidents, improve road safety, and focus on assisting my country's automotive and electrical industry operators to enter the global automotive safety market with a total of more than 100 billion US dollars.
