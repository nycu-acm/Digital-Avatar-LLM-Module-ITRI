Academician profile
Academician Yang Zhentong
Academician Yang Zhentong is the founder of Yali Electric Group. He has been deeply engaged in the industry for 50 years and has led the group to become the most important distribution board manufacturer in China. He is also committed to the major construction of Taiwan's power and has made outstanding contributions to the development of the heavy power power energy industry.He has served as deputy chairman of the Heavy Power Committee of the Taiwan Electrical and Electronics Industry Association for more than 30 years, executive supervisor of the Taiwan Electric Power and Energy Engineering Association, and executive director of the Taiwan Vehicle and Electrical Symbiosis Association.He has participated in the preparation of the Taiwan Power Research and Test Center, which has a far-reaching impact on the development of my country's heavy power industry.
President Yang worked in Zhonghua Wire and Cable Company in his early years, and his work performance was highly recognized.In 1968, he founded Aeli Motor, whose main products are industrial and communication electronics, distribution boards, transformers, etc. In the 1970s, the government developed basic industries and heavy industries. During the period of rapid growth of electricity, Ali Motor provided heavy motor products needed by Taiwan Electric Power, CNPC, Telecommunications Bureau, and other well-known private institutions. It played an important role in power supply in the era when Taiwan's economy took off and major construction was gradually developing.
Under the leadership of President Yang, Ali Motor strives to make breakthroughs, with new product sales accounting for 20% of its turnover each year, and has set up a research and development office and a power electronics R&D center to actively establish independent development capabilities to achieve sustainable operation of the enterprise.At the same time, we are optimistic that the motor industry will develop in the future towards intelligence and green energy. Ali Motor will gradually expand its international market, establish a global marketing network in Europe, North America, Southeast Asia and South Africa, and successfully enter overseas power markets such as Indonesia through diversified operations and strategic alliances.
Not only is he actively developing in his career, President Yang is impressed by the critical influence of vocational education on the country's industrial development, he has participated in vocational training for a long time, paid attention to the system of technical and vocational certificate examinations, the development of vocational training system, technical and vocational education system, and the international competitiveness of students in the technical and vocational system, and participated in the formulation of Class A, Class B and Class C technical and technical and technical school standards.President Yang taught at Longhua University of Science and Technology in addition to work, inheriting his experience to more young students and contributing to technical and vocational education.
