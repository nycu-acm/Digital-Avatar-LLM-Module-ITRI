Academician Shi Min
Professor Shi Min is the inventor of non-volatile semiconductor memory (NVSM). He is currently a national lecture of the Ministry of Education and honorary lecturer at National Jiaotong University.His book "Physics of Semiconductor Devices" is a classic in the fields of modern engineering and applied science.It has been translated into six languages, sold more than 1.5 million copies, and has been cited more than 25,000 times. It is known as the "Bible of the semiconductor world." Professor Shi jointly invented non-volatile memory with Korean Dr. Jiang Dayuan in 1967, which derived EPROM, EEPROM and flash memory.This epoch-making invention led to the revolutionary development of information storage and opened up the digital age.This memory has been widely used in electronic products such as mobile phones, tablets, portable disks, cloud computing, digital cameras, digital TVs, global positioning systems, etc.
Shi Min, an academician of the Institute of Technology Research.
