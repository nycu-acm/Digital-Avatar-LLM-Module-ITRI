Academician profile
Academician Sun Hong
Chairman Sun Hong is the first chairman of Mengli Automation Company in Taiwan to provide overall solutions for automation equipment.In 1989, he led a hundred colleagues to establish Mengli Automation as the deputy director of the Institute of Mechanical Research Institute.Chairman Sun has insight into industry trends, and has made the TFT-LCD process automation, and has developed a "clean room automatic warehousing solution", which is the first in Taiwan's market share; his "all-electric plastic injection machine controller" uses a homemade servo motor, which is precise and energy-saving.He has served as the director and supervisor of the Science Industrial Park Association for more than 20 years, and has devoted himself to the development of the science and technology industry.
