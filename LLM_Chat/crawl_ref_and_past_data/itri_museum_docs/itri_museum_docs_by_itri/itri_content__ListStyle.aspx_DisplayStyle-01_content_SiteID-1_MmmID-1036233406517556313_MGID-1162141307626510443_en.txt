About the Institute of Electronics and Optoelectronic Systems
The Institute of Electronics and Optoelectronics Systems has promoted the rise and prosperity of Taiwan's overall semiconductor, display and optoelectronics industry through technology research and development, services, transfer and establishment of derivative companies for half a century. In response to the rapid market changes, we continue to develop technologies to strengthen the country's development competitiveness: AI chips, compound semiconductors, digital/analogy and hybrid signal chip design, heterogeneous integration, quantum computers and smart display interactive systems and other forward-looking technologies, becoming a global provider of intelligent integration solutions, leading Taiwan's electronics and optoelectronics industry to leap again!
We actively build a high-quality environment to allow colleagues to focus on research and development of forward-looking technologies that are integrated and application-oriented, deepen industrial independence capabilities, drive industrial innovation, promote new and innovative development, strengthen international and academic cooperation, promote overall industrial value, and create my country's industry's core global competitive advantages!
