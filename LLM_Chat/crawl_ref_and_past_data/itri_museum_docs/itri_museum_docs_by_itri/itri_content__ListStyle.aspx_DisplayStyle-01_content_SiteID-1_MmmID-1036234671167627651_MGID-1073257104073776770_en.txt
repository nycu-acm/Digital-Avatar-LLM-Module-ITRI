Wei Zhejia Academician
Academician Wei Zhejia is the president of Taiwan Jieti Circuit Manufacturing Co., Ltd. (TSMC). He has the professional technology and flexible business capabilities of semiconductors, and is responsible for TSMC's overall business strategy and operational policies.Previously, President Wei served as the senior deputy general manager of TSMC's mainstream technology career, senior deputy general manager of business development, executive deputy general manager and co-operation director, general manager and co-executive director, and has rich practical experience.In the 12% recession of the global semiconductor industry in 2019, TSMC's performance led by President Wei continued to grow against the trend, with revenue reaching 169.99 billion yuan, a record high, and continued to maintain a leading position in the global logic integrated circuit industry.
President Wei obtained a bachelor's and master's degree in the Department of Electrical Engineering at National Jiaotong University, and later obtained a doctorate in electrical engineering at Yale University in the United States. He has long been investing in the field of semiconductors. He has served as senior technical deputy general manager of Singapore Chartered Semiconductor Company and senior manager of logic and SRAM technology development at Texas STMicroelectronics.In 1998, President Wei joined TSMC and later took charge of the mainstream technology business group. He was good at discovering new blue oceans of mature technologies. He successfully upgraded the eight-inch factory and seized the business opportunities of fingerprint recognition, micro-mechanical, wearable and photosensitive components and automotive electronic chips, bringing new revenue momentum to TSMC.In 2003, he led the team to improve the yield of 0.13 micron copper process, successfully opening up the gap between TSMC and its competitors.
President Wei has rich practical experience in the fields of technology and operation management, and has solid business capabilities. With decisive decision-making and courage to challenge, he has created many highlights for TSMC: In the early stages of the infancy of smart phones, President Wei has led the team to take the lead in the development of forward-looking technologies such as audio and video, imaging, and display, and successfully completed a complete 3D sensing solution, so that the more advanced 3D sensing technology of smart phones in the world are closely related to TSMC.In addition, extreme ultraviolet light (EUV) microfilm technology is the most important technological change in the semiconductor industry in recent years. Under the leadership of President Wei, TSMC has become the first semiconductor company in the world to introduce mass production of extreme ultraviolet light.
Entering a new era of intelligence, President Wei led TSMC to work closely with customers, committed to accelerating the development of technology and improving the ability of excellent manufacturing, thereby consolidating the company's long-term competitive advantage, not only gaining global customers' trust, but also laying a solid foundation for the sustainable development of the semiconductor industry.
