Taiwan’s strong manufacturing ecosystem has won international recognition, and the Industrial Research Institute has won the award of “Social and Economic Development and Resilience Award” by High Level Forum, France

Date: 2021/12/09
The Institute of Technology was awarded the "Social and Economic Development and Resilience Award" by the High Level Forum in France, commending the contributions made by the "Hsinchu Ecosystem" led by the Institute of Technology to Taiwan's economic, social welfare and industrial resilience during the COVID-19 epidemic. The award was received by Liu Bangzhi (left), deputy representative of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in France.
The COVID-19 epidemic has impacted the world, and the short-chain revolution has driven the restructuring and reshuffle of the supply chain.Taiwan’s strong explosive power to break through under adversity has won international recognition!The internationally renowned innovation forum High Level Forum (HLF) held a dinner and award ceremony on November 8, French time.The Institute of Technology won the first "Social and Economic Development and Resilience Award" with its outstanding scientific and technological epidemic prevention energy. Due to the epidemic, the deputy representative of the Ministry of Foreign Affairs in France, Liu Bangzhi, received the award.
The 2021 HLF held a three-day summit forum in Grenoble, France from November 7 to 9, exploring how to strengthen global epidemic prevention resilience with the "Innovation Ecosystems, key players in reinventing industry to support a resilient society" as the main axis.Su Mengzong, director of the Institute of Industrial Technology International Strategy Development of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, attended the forum as a representative of Taiwan and delivered a special speech online, sharing that the Institute of Industrial Research Institute has successively adopted three major epidemic prevention technologies: iPMx molecular rapid inspection system set (referred to as the epidemic can opening set), smart care home management system, and AI thermal imaging body temperature abnormality detection technology to enter the frontline of epidemic prevention and control.Su Mengzong also proposed that Taiwan should strengthen international links, provide a strong industrial ecological chain, assist in innovative applications through scientific and technological research and development, and strive to maintain sustainable environment and share prosperity with global partners to strengthen Taiwan’s key position in the global value chain.
The organizer emphasized that under the impact of the new crown pneumonia epidemic, the "Hsinchu Ecosystem" led by the Institute of Technology Research continues to apply forward-looking technology to strengthen safety work, ensure uninterrupted operation of the industry, to consolidate Taiwan's key position in the global value chain and demonstrate the abundant energy of scientific and technological epidemic prevention.Therefore, the Institute of Technology and Research won the "Social and Economic Development and Resilience Award" for its "Resilient Manufacturing Ecochain" to commend its contribution to Taiwan's economy, social welfare and industrial resilience.
The internationally renowned innovation forum HLF was founded by the French research institution Atomic Energy and Alternative Energy Commission (CEA) in 2012 in the French city of Grenoble, as an interactive platform for maintaining and creating global regional ecosystems. Members are from leaders in the field of innovation in the international community, including: CEO of the Industrial Technology Research Department of the Atomic Energy and Alternative Energy Agency and Chairman of the Grenoble Innovation for Advanced New Technologies in France Stephane Siebert, Mayor of Grenoble, France Eric Piolle, Director General of the European Synchronous Radiation Light Source (ESRF), Richard, Director of the US-Asia Technology Management Center at Stanford University Dasher and other research and industry, higher education, enterprises and public welfare organs, policy makers and politicians are committed to managing or promoting innovation in their regional ecosystems. The annual summit forum invites opinion leaders from all countries to exchange and discuss current global important issues.
HLF continues to invite cities and experts from all over the world to join. Currently, 45 cities from 32 countries, including the United States, Japan, Britain, France, Germany, South Korea, and Singapore have joined.Since 2012, Taiwan has been the first to join HLF by Ji Guozhong, a professor at the Department of Optoelectronic Engineering of National Jiaotong University (now Yangming Jiaotong University), to promote the "Hsinchu Science City Ecology Department" to participate in the first HLF International Conference in conjunction with the municipal government, the Science Park Administration, the Institute of Technology Research, the Institute of Technology, the National Institute of Experimental Research, and other industry-official and academic research institutions. In 2016, the Institute of Technology Research, the Vice President Peng Yumin continued to lead a number of technical cooperation with the French CEA, and joined the Steering Committee in 2017 to introduce Taiwan's scientific and technological industry development and governance experience to the world.In 2018, Su Mengzong, director of the Institute of Obstetrics and International Obstetrics, led the new members such as Tsinghua University and the Electric Power Association to actively participate. This year, he won the HLF awards for the first time.
