Technology introduction
3D printing material crystal light control engine.
The 3D printing material crystal light control engine developed by the Industrial Research Institute can print metal workpieces with different internal and external toughness and hardness!Unlike metal 3D printing equipment that can only print one material strength on the market, the metal 3D printing equipment developed by the Institute of Technology is equipped with this laser optical engine, which can instantly and dynamically control the temperature changes and crystallization characteristics of the material when solidification, and arbitrarily control the structure required inside and outside the workpiece and the required mechanical properties such as strength, hardness, toughness, and toughness according to product requirements.If the turbine blades need to be wear-resistant on the surface of the shaft center and the blades need high temperature toughness, the 3D printing material crystal light control engine of the Institute of Technology can print out the turbine blades with hard inside and tough outside at one time. This patented special optical path technology can greatly improve the product's efficiency and service life, and is used in automotive, biomedical orthopedic materials and aerospace components.
Features and innovation
It can meet the needs of local strengthening or improving toughness according to the needs of parts.
The material micrometallographic structure can be designed according to the use situation, such as isotropic crystallization and isotropic fine crystallization.
Application and benefits
Single crystal/equal directional crystallization turbine blades.
Artificial hip joint with local high hardness and high toughness design.
Improve the efficiency and service life of key mechanical components.
