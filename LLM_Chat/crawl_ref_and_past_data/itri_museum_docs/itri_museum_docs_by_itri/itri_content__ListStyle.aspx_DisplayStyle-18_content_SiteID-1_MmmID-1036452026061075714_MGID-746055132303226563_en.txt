Accurate and fast energy consumption diagnosis and high cloud management efficiency
Written by/Lai Wanjing Photography/Huang Dingxiang
One-third of the world's energy is consumed by buildings. Even though the concept of green building has been established, the focus is on a large number of existing buildings' energy conservation and improvement, but the energy conservation diagnosis of existing buildings requires a lot of time and cost.The "Artificial Intelligent Building Energy-Saving System Platform (BESTAI)" developed by the Institute of Technology can overcome current technical difficulties and significantly lower the threshold for building energy-saving diagnosis.
Last year (2016), Taiwan's commercial construction electricity use accounted for 19.34% of the total electricity consumption, about 48.3 billion kWh, of which 11,199 chain supermarkets and 6,232 banks were the highest density in the world.These two industries consume about 4.8 billion kWh of electricity per year, accounting for 10% of the electricity consumed by commercial buildings. This does not yet include the energy consumption of mass stores, chain restaurants, pharmaceutical stores, etc. The energy consumption of chain stores cannot be ignored.
The "Artificial Intelligent Building Energy-Saving System Platform" developed by the research team of the Institute of Technology has the advantages of ease of use, fast and low cost. It was shortlisted for the 2017 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards.
Energy-saving diagnosis is expensive and time-consuming, and the owner is relatively hesitant
While countries around the world advocate energy conservation, energy conservation in buildings is also an important issue.However, how to understand the energy consumption status of buildings and how to plan energy-saving strategies? Although there are professional energy-saving companies providing services, the energy-saving diagnosis process is time-consuming and expensive.In addition, the software commonly used internationally is complex and difficult to operate, and cannot be promoted to ordinary users. Various problems will discourage stores who are interested in improving the energy consumption of buildings, resulting in poor promotion of building energy conservation.
To this end, the Institute of Technology actively develops an energy-saving diagnosis platform, widely summarizes the opinions of architects, air conditioning technicians, electromechanical technicians, and equipment manufacturers, and discusses and exchanges with academic professors, launches the "Artificial Intelligent Building Energy-saving System Platform", and is shortlisted for the 2017 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards.
Lin Hongwen, a special project manager of the Smart Energy Saving System Technology Group of the Institute of Green Energy and Environment of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the "Artificial Intelligent Building Energy Saving System Platform" developed by the Institute of Industrial Research integrates common building materials and equipment in Taiwan, collects nearly 400 types of building materials, and connects nearly 7,000 types of energy-saving marking equipment from the database of the Energy Bureau of the Ministry of Economic Affairs. The platform interface is simplified into five steps. Users can easily apply standard building models and preset parameter values ​​to quickly complete the setting of building energy models.At the same time, searching for similar devices, the platform will sort users based on the filtering conditions of the highest energy saving or the shortest recovery period, and provide an optimized equipment list.
Spend only one tenth of energy saving efficiency
"Because there is no need to collect annual building energy consumption data during diagnosis, the diagnosis time and cost can be greatly reduced, and the system interface is friendly, the database is updated quickly, and non-experts can operate, and the accuracy of energy-saving assessment is improved, which is better than the international standards of the American Institute of Air Conditioning and Refrigeration Engineers (ASHRAE)," said Lin Hongwen. This has greatly increased the willingness of chain enterprises such as mass sales stores, convenience stores, and financial banking industries to invest in store energy-saving physical examinations.
The platform successfully assisted South China Bank to introduce rapid building diagnosis functions, promote the average power saving of 5-15% for each bank branch, and was awarded the Ministry of Economic Affairs' Excellent Energy Saving Performance Manufacturer Award; it also assisted chain convenience store shells to improve energy saving and improvement assessment, and suggested that the supermarket company avoid energy-consuming building shells, saving an average of 350,000 to 500,000 yuan for each branch of the company.
A set of fully functional artificial intelligence building energy-saving system platforms can only be completed by brainstorming from many experts and scholars.Zhang Wenkui, a senior researcher at the Smart Energy Saving System Technology Group of the Institute of Green Energy and is also responsible for platform construction, said that the initial plan hopes to achieve "humanization" and "intuitive operation". Using simple and clear interfaces, pull-down menus and search methods, a standard building energy model and localized database will be built, including common building types and commonly used building materials and equipment in Taiwan, greatly improving the authenticity of building energy models and the accuracy of analysis results.
Humanized platform and complete database Accurate simulation
Lin Hongwen said that the research team has applied the platform to the Taiwan market and successfully completed the evaluation and analysis of energy-saving buildings in 189 chain banks and 23 supermarkets and other buildings in South China Bank in a very short time. With such efficiency and accuracy, no other building energy simulation analysis software has compared with the market.The artificial intelligence building energy-saving system platform can also integrate energy management systems, gradually update building energy models through instant energy usage data to improve simulation accuracy, and provide additional value-added service functions, such as energy maps and enterprise energy-saving competition tools, etc.
The Institute of Technology hopes that this platform can not only do building energy-saving diagnosis, but also expand its service scope, such as integrating building energy-saving analysis with energy management systems and artificial intelligence technology, reading the real data measured by the meter and sensor and the start and stop state of the equipment operation."Simply put, the chain enterprise headquarter can know the energy usage status of each branch through the platform and summarize the status of customers coming to the store. Then, you can know how to configure energy, and even predict equipment abnormalities and perform maintenance and management instantly."
The functions of this "Artificial Intelligent Building Energy Saving System Platform" have been modularized and can be customized according to different regional conditions and usage needs. The platform can use artificial intelligence to search for local building materials, lighting, socket equipment, air conditioning equipment and other product information in various regions through artificial intelligence, and then build an energy-saving equipment database and standard building energy model in the region. Especially Southeast Asia and South China regions with similar climatic conditions to Taiwan. The existing database of the platform can continue to accumulate and expand data, and quickly promote and apply the platform, allowing Taiwan to do its part to global energy conservation and carbon reduction and successfully expand business opportunities.
