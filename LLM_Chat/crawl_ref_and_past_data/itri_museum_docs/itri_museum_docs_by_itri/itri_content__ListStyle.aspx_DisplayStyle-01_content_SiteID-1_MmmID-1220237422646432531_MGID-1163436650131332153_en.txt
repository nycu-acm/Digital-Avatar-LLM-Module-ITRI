Technology introduction
RGB-AI robot.
Faced with the transformation of industrial smart manufacturing, the Industrial Research Institute has developed the "RGB-D AI Robot".Combined with AI independent learning technology, it is like installing a robotic arm on the brain. It can automatically mark tens of thousands of training materials and quickly learn the best picking strategy within 12 hours. As the AI ​​robotic arm becomes smarter and can identify the grasping or sucking points of any new item.
Features and innovation
This technology is the world's first MIT collaborative robot that uses 3D visual sensing and smart take as standard configuration. Its visual sensor size is 168 times smaller than other brands, the sensing speed is 38.6 times higher, and the reconstruction rate of material reflection is also increased by 2.1 times. It can meet the automation needs of manufacturing, logistics, retail, etc., and create a new opportunity for industrial transformation.
Application and benefits
This technology won the 2022 Innovation Awards for Consumer Electronics Exhibition 2022.
