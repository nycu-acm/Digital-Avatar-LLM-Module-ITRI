Li Mouwei Academician
Academician Li Mouwei is the president of Li Changrong Group. He specializes in chemical engineering and enterprise management. He has a deep sense of mission for the sustainable development of the materials industry and the cultivation of talents. He actively participates in industrial promotion and education organization services. He provides scholarships from many schools for a long time to help outstanding young people in Taiwan enter international first-class universities for further studies.During his tenure as chairman of Li Changrong Group, Academician Li adhered to the vision of building Li Changrong Group into a world-class enterprise. Through policies such as external investment, mergers and acquisitions, and effective management of internal systems, Li Changrong Group led Li Changrong Group to set a record of 20 times of revenue and profit growth in 23 years, becoming an important member of the global materials industry.
Academician Li returned to Taiwan in 1982 and joined the Li Changrong Group.He took over as General Manager of Li Changrong Chemical in 1990, served as chairman since 2004, and was transferred to the group president since 2017. Under its leadership, Li Changrong Group has laid the foundation for Taiwan, laid the foundation for the world, coordinated with the industrial pulse, and actively developed high-value-added products, playing a key role in Taiwan's economic development.Academician Li served as the chairman of the Taiwan Chemical Industry Association (TCIA) from 2015 to 2021. After years of hard work, TCIA officially became a permanent observer of the International Chemical Industry Alliance (ICCA) in 2019, paving the way for Taiwan's chemical industry to connect with international organizations and greatly enhancing the competitiveness of my country's chemical industry.
Academician Li planned a net zero emission target, invested in solar energy and geothermal power generation, and led the group to re-examine and set the use path of petrochemical raw materials ten years ago. From the four major strategies of finding petrochemical alternative materials (green products from non-petrochemical sources, such as biosuccinic acid/carotenoids), new material research and development (green products that are more conducive to recycling), recycling technology improvement, and more sustainable processes, it began to build a "high-value n-cycle cycle".The group led the group to launch the global original "EIPA dual cycle recycling" technology to assist the semiconductor industry in recycling waste generated in the process and reduce carbon footprint.By extending the service life of carbon products, reducing the dependence of petrochemical raw materials, we will move towards a zero-carbon future.
