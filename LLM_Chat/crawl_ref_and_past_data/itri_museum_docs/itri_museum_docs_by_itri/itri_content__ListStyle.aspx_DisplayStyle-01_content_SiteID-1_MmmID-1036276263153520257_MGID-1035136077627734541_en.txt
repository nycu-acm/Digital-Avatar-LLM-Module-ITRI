Dominate Asia!The Institute of Technology won the award of the top 100 innovative institutions in the world for 3 years, and played alongside the world's top companies to showcase Taiwan's innovation capabilities

Date: 2019/04/12
Fan Yongyin (right), general manager of the Derwinter and Standard Business Group of Clarivate Greater China, and general manager of Taiwan, was awarded the "Derwinter Global 100 Innovation Institutions Award" by the Institute of Technology Research, and was awarded by Dean Liu Wenxiong (left) on behalf of Dean!
Clarivate Analytics in the United States praised the outstanding scientific and technological innovation and patent performance of the Derwinter Global 100 Innovative Institutions Award Ceremony today (12th), and praised the Institute for its outstanding performance in the four major selection criteria: number of patents, patent approval rate, degree of globalization of patent portfolios, and patent influence. Only three research institutions in the world were on the list, as famous as the Fraunhofer of Germany and the Commissariat à l'Energie Atomique; and won three awards, winning the most awarded research institution in Asia! The Industrial Research Institute has obtained invention patent certification rate of up to 81.53% in the past five years, which is awesome international.The Industrial Research Institute and international innovative companies with market value value include Google, Amazon and Microsoft, etc., to win glory for Taiwan.
The report of Clarivate Anderwinter's Top 100 Innovation Institutions has entered its eighth year since its first release in 2011. The report details and praises the most innovative institutions around the world. These institutions not only successfully develop valuable patent inventions, but also show strong commodity potential in originality, market research and whether there is an opportunity to derive other inventions.Fan Yongyin, general manager of the Derwinter and Standard Business Group of Clarivate Greater China and general manager of Taiwan, said: "In the four evaluations, the achievements of the INDUA are obvious to all, especially in the patent influence indicators, which are the degree of citation by third parties. They are more representative. They are the first among the top 100 institutions and are the TOPs. They represent that the INDUA has a very far-reaching influence on global industrial innovation. This year, I am very happy that the INDUA was selected again. In addition to showing the INDUA's technical visibility and influence on industrial innovation, it also shows excellent innovation quality and a high global layout of technology. We look forward to more Taiwanese institutions in the future to continue to show the energy and influence of innovation on the global stage."
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that winning the Top 100 Innovation Institution Award this time is an affirmation of the Institute of Technology Research, pursuing excellent innovation research and development and outputting high-quality patents and applications.In the process of pursuing innovation, the Institute of Technology attaches great importance to whether it conforms to the elements of the "innovation life cycle", that is, whether it covers the complete process from R&D, protection, and commercialization at the same time, because an interesting innovative concept, if there is a lack of patent protection or commercialization behind it, it is at best just a short-lived concept.The strengths of the Institute of Technology Research and Research are not only R&D, but also pay more attention to R&D quality and patent influence. For a long time, the Institute of Technology Research and Research has continued to use high-quality patents to help the industry consolidate the competitive weapon of the international market. Through revitalization, it can not only be combined with commercial strategies, but also enhance product competitiveness, counter international litigation interference, and enter the global market, thereby assisting the Taiwan industry in breaking through the patent technical barriers of international major manufacturers and continuing the brand value of Taiwan.In the future, the Institute of Technology will continue to maintain its R&D and innovation vitality, connect the growth of the technology industry with more sophisticated smart property creation, and assist more manufacturers to thrive steadily and create a new blue ocean in Taiwan’s niche in the global technology competition!
Speaking of the importance of patents to enterprises, Liu Wenxiong pointed out that Taiwan's industry must transform, and the most important key is innovation. Innovation can transform the structure rather than a flash in the pan; we look at the short-term market as beauty pageant, and the long-term market as weight lifting. The process from beauty pageant to weight lifting depends on the diversified application of patents and smart property rights, because patents and smart property are not only tools to protect innovation, but also weapons that can assist the industry in attack.
Wang Pengyu, director of the Technology Transfer and Law Center of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the Institute of Industrial Research Institute adheres to the strategy of "exploring creativity and accurately laying out high-value patents", focuses on future market demand, and actively lays out patents in important countries around the world; it has been awarded the Asia IP Elite for six consecutive years, and through the large number of high-quality patents produced, it helps domestic companies improve their competitiveness and create value.By the end of 2018, the Institute of Technology and Research has accumulated 17,303 validly certified patents, of which the proportion of invention patents reached 98%.Patented technology covers a wide range of research and development fields, including optoelectronics, communications, machinery, materials and chemicals, biomedicine, and green energy.It has deepened its layout in response to cutting-edge technological trends such as AI technology and drones, which will help manufacturers enter the blue ocean market and seize the initiative!
In recent years, the achievements of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research in smart property rights and innovative patent development have attracted much attention from the outside world, assisting enterprises in breaking through and upgrading and opening up new blue oceans.In 2017, it has assisted Songteng, a major domestic sweeping robot manufacturer, to reach a settlement with iRobot, an international sweeping robot manufacturer, in the posture of a small shrimp against a big whale. In April 2017, iRobot sued 11 manufacturers around the world, including the United States, Canada, China and Taiwan, infringing on its patent rights in the field of smart robot technology, and has been deployed in more than 100 high-quality patents with more than 100 more than 100 pieces as a counterclaim weapon. Therefore, it is not afraid to face an opponent with a market value of 25 times greater than him, and resolutely respond to the challenge in an all-round way.The Institute of Technology provides key patent combinations such as core robot technologies including estimating carriers and map construction systems and methods, positioning system modules, mobile platforms, and clean units, which successfully prompted iRobot to agree to a settlement and withdraw the lawsuit.Not only did Songteng Company not be forced to withdraw from the market like other manufacturers, it also became the main supplier of sweeping robots in the US market with iRobot. It further cooperated with further cooperation, and its performance in order acceptance and shipment increased significantly, and it also invested in factory building and expansion, driving the development of the domestic component industry supply chain.
The Institute of Technology not only has high competitiveness in patents related to sweeping robots, but also has made great achievements in the field of popular electric locomotives today. For example, this year, the Institute of Technology signed a technical contract for electric locomotive energy management system with KYMCO, the Guangyang Group, which has the largest market share in fuel locomotives. The two parties cooperated on 36 patented technologies such as "self-learning dynamic power estimate" technology to develop a new generation of AI concept electric locomotives. According to the different riding modes of the rider, the AI ​​of the power management system can be automatically memorized and learned, providing accurate riding mileage of the electric locomotives, and displaying the motor output status; breaking through the rough situation that electric locomotives are displayed in the current stage by percentage of electricity (%), and warning the rider in advance before power outage, not only provides safer riding experience for the rider, but also leads domestic electric locomotives from the smart generation to the new generation of AI, opening a new era of electric locomotives.
Since the patents of the Institute of Technology are the top inventions in scientific and technological research, and are not usually applied for patents for specific products in the general industry, the patents of the Institute of Technology have a stable characteristic of a wide range of applications and do not lag behind with product cycle changes;they are worthy of the Taiwan industry to be good at using them and become a helper for entering the international market.
