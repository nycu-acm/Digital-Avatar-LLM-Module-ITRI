Integrates 400kW DC transformer with 3300V/400A silicon carbide power module.
Technology introduction
This 400kW DC transformer adopts a standard full-bridge topology and solid-state transformer design, and can be directly converted to 11kV, eliminating the need for traditional boost transformers.
The main side of the system is equipped with a 3.3kV/400A silicon carbide power module, which reduces the overall switching loss by about 30%, and ensures stable operation during the process without obvious overvoltage or ringing.The system achieves conversion efficiency up to 98.3%.
The 3.3V/400A SiC power module of the Institute of Technology uses stacked substrates, symmetrical circuit design and patented housing to achieve low parasitic inductance, low thermal resistance and reduce switching losses.
This module has been optimized for applications such as renewable energy, rail transit and power systems, and is suitable for various high-voltage or high-power power conversion systems.
Application and benefits
1. Integrate 3.3kV/400A silicon carbide power module
- Design of substrate stacking, patented housing and line symmetry
- Thermal resistance ≤ 0.06 K/W
- Parasitic inductance ≤ 10 nH
2. DC conversion voltage: 1800V~1500V
3. Rated current: 400A
4. Rated power: 400kW
5. System efficiency > 98%
