Technology introduction
Dynamic force sensing module.
The piezoelectric dynamic force sensing module has the characteristics of high dynamic response and high reliability.It instantly monitors the stamping process of the equipment through sensor signals and instantly diagnoses the health of the machine.
Features and innovation
With a patented innovative double-end suspension structure design, it improves the reliability of the sensor and has a patented reading circuit to extend the charge release time, ensure that the sensed output signal is not distorted, and improves the accuracy of the sensing signal.
Technical Features
High reliability structural design (Overload: 150%).
Compatible with industry standard size instant machine health diagnosis.
Instant process abnormal monitoring.
Technical Specifications
Force Range: Max.: 2000N.
Overload Capability > 150%.
Discharge Time Constant: >10Sec.
Bandwidth: >20KHz.
Application and benefits
This technology has been actually applied to the production lines of large domestic fasteners for stamping process monitoring.In the future, it can be applied to the production lines such as metal stamping and forging to improve product quality.
