Technology support is not absent!Industrial Research Institute innovates energy storage equipment to support disaster areas and support the heroes on the front line

Date: 2025/07/13
The mobile optical storage system of the Institute of Technology Research Institute "E-CUBE" immediately assists people in Tainan's wind-hit power outage areas to urgently use electricity. The "E-CUBE" in the picture is located in Yonglong Palace, Beimen District, Tainan City.
The middle-Taiwan Danas hit nearly 2,500 telephone poles in Taiwan, far exceeding the record of nearly 1,000 broken poles set by Sudil, a strong Taiwan in 2015. The Taiwanese Electrician colleagues have repaired the repairs day and night for days and have almost completely resumed power.The Institute of Industrial Research and Research has supported Taiwan with scientific and technological support, and urgently dispatched the "E-CUBE" mobile optical storage system during the disaster, and worked with the startup company Hydrogen Fuel Cell to provide emergency power for people's livelihood, medical care, base stations and other emergency power supplies in the disaster area.In order to support the emergency repair hero who runs day and night, the Industrial Research Institute also took the initiative to open the staff dormitory of the Lokka campus to serve as a safe haven for Taiwan Television personnel to rest and set off after preparation.
Faced with the collapse of a large number of telephone poles, re-energizing in a short period of time is an unprecedented test for manpower and material resources. Some colleagues from Taiwan Electric worked in water, and many employees' families said online that "the family has not been home for many days." Netizens praised "Thank you, it's great to have you."In order to achieve the mission, Taiwan Electric Power personnel went out early and returned late every day for more than 12 hours, went out early in the morning and stopped working with their tired bodies in the early morning. The Institute of Technology and Research unconditionally provided a warm place in the Lokka campus, providing resources such as drinking water, cooked food and washing facilities, and making every effort to serve as the backing of Taiwan Electric Power emergency repair personnel.More than 300 maintenance heroes have arrived within four days, allowing this group of frontline personnel who go out early and come back late, fearlessly, to rest briefly, replenish their energy, complete key tasks as soon as possible, light up every light, and warm every home!
Since most of the disaster areas live alone in elderly people, it is urgently necessary to invest manpower to assist in cleaning or rebuilding the home, the Institute of Technology and Research has launched care measures in the hospital and announced that colleagues who have the need for disaster reconstruction can flexibly apply for suspension of shifts or work remotely, assist colleagues in taking into account their families and work, and spending special periods with peace of mind.At the same time, the Institute of Technology and Research responded to the call and encouraged colleagues to actively apply for volunteer leave, assist in disaster relief nearby, give full play to social responsibilities, act together to gather strength, and work together to overcome difficulties.
In order to urgently assist in power supply and strengthen the southern power supply system, the Industrial Research Institute and the disaster area command center urgently coordinated with solar photoelectric operators to dispatch relevant energy storage equipment and backup generators, and give priority to supporting temporary bases and offices in the disaster area to prepare power, so as to prevent disaster relief and reconstruction progress.The Institute of Technology has also dispatched its own developed energy storage equipment "E-CUBE" to enter the disaster area and multiple community bases including Yong'an Palace to assist in providing basic power and charging services and support the front-line demand after the disaster."E-CUBE" is a mobile optical storage system that integrates photoelectric power generation, energy storage and low-carbon backup power. It has solar panels and energy storage equipment at the top, and has modular and expandable characteristics. It can be deployed quickly. It provides key electricity for mobile phones, lighting, medical equipment and other key electricity during power shortage, and is ready for people to carry charging cables and plugs.
Due to the power outage, the two disaster areas informed E-CUBE of the power supply messages on their own, and many people lined up to charge the power supply on the spot. Some people who had long-term care were also pushed to "E-CUBE" by their families to charge medical equipment. The families anxiously said that the children who originally relied on phlegm pumping devices to make a living at home were fortunately "E-CUBE" arrived in time, stabilizing power supply to restore medical equipment, and successfully resolved the urgent need.
In addition to daily electricity, a communication system that contacts relatives and friends to report safety after the disaster is also an indispensable equipment.The new startup company of the Industrial Research Institute, "Hydrogen Green Energy", has adopted the hydrogen fuel cell system that is usually used for technology factories to prepare for the first time into Xiliao Village, Qigu, to support the power supply of the China Telecom Base Station, assist in the restoration of on-site communications, and give full play to stable and low-carbon power support, so that care is free of blind spots.
The "E-CUBE" mobile optical storage system of the Institute of Technology and Research has entered the wind disaster power outage area to assist people in need of long-term care to charge medical equipment.
After a whole day of emergency repairs and rushing, the Institute of Technology lit a lamp to welcome the return of these silent heroes.At night, they arrived at the Lokka campus and finally had a rest.
The Liujia Campus of the Institute of Technology Research Institute provides warm rest space and sufficient supplies. After a brief rest, the Taiwanese electrician emergency repair staff were fully equipped with energy and set off.
