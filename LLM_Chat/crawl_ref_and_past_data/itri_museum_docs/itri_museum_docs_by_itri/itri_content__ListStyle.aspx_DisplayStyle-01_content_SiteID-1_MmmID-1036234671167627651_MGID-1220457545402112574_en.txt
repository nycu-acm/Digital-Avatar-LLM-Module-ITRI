Academician Chan Qihong
Academician Chan was chairman of the Ming Kiji Group, joined Ming Kee (the predecessor of Jiada) as the director of R&D in 1991 for more than 32 years.Responsible for CRT/LCD monitors, projectors, LCD TVs, digital cameras and other products, and has served as general manager of digital media business groups and general managers of product technology centers.In 2014, he took over as General Manager of Jiashi, leading Jiada to obtain the world's second largest LCD design and manufacturing, and the world's first DLP projector manufacturing and brand's top status.
In response to information products, grosser and low-weiance, the Group's Group's value transformation, affiliate investment growth strategy, to organize the "United Fleet" to enter the new career of high-additional value.With Jiada as the resource integration platform, the group's power, nicknamed the Taiwanese invisible champion to join, gradually establish a new career such as medical business, networking career and intelligent solution AIoT, in 2022 reached "high-additional valueThe vision of the industry's revenues" has successfully transformed into a cross-sphere technology service and medical group, which merges the historic newly surpassed up to up to 23.98 billion yuan, a ten-fold profit increase from 2013, highlighting the effectiveness of value transformation strategy.The Chan General's General Assembly took advantage of the pursuit and launched the second phase of value transformation, with the goal of reaching the vision of the "Half-Additional Value Profit" in 2027.
In the implementation of the results of sustainability, Academician Chan is committed to promoting green product design, leading Ka Shida to obtain the world's first 20-in-law LCD and projector carbon footprint certificate, leading the industry to establish a product carbon footprint management platform, and has won more than 50 awards such as international carbon footprint certification, green packaging design, AEAG Green Leadership Award.In 2022, Jiada joined RE100, pledged to use renewable energy in 2040, becoming the first of Taiwan's first joining RE100 computer peripheral technology group, more with the big fleet and suppliers together to reduce carbon carbon, moving towards 2030 carbon 20% target, demonstrating the sustainability and international connection.
