Nangang IC Design and Breeding Center
Nangang IC Design and Breeding Center
The center takes knowledge-based value-added services as the main axis and provides an excellent and complete design environment, including professional consultations such as finance, law, accounting, technology and management.
Provide members with a comprehensive company operating environment, so that operators can focus more on design and research and development, promote stable growth of the company and create new prosperity for Taiwan's electronics industry.
Vision
Based on Taiwan's semiconductor manufacturing, combined with IC and system design, we will promote the integration of software, hardware and systems to optimize the existing industrial architecture and promote emerging application fields, cultivate Taiwan's smart electronics industry, enhance the industrial competitiveness of this field, and develop an innovation-oriented knowledge economy to lay the international status of Taiwan's science and technology industry.
We will fully support my country's newly established small and medium-sized enterprises, provide different support at all stages of entrepreneurship, and create a good environment for the development of small and medium-sized enterprises.
Resources provided
Office space: three flexible office spaces, including independent offices, personal business seats and co-creation spaces.
EDA tool: Provides flexible and affordable rental solutions for startups.
Information room: It is equipped with UPS continuous power system, server fault-tolerant transfer, and high-security network connection.
Measuring instruments: general/high-order digital measurement equipment (such as: oscilloscopes, logic analyzers, power supply devices, etc.).
Eligibility for entry in the Cultivation Center
Company qualification: Small and medium-sized enterprises registered and established in Taiwan that have not been listed/countered.
Year of entry: four years (you can apply for an extension if necessary, each extension is limited to one year, and you can apply for up to three times).
You must pass the review of the evaluation committee before you can become a member of the Cultivation Center.
