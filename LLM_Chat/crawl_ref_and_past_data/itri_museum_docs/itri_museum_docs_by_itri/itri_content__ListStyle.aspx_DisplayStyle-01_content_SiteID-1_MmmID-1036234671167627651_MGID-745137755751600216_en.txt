Academician Gao Yingshi
Academician Gao Yingshi is the founder of Changxing Materials Industry Co., Ltd., feeling the importance of independence of raw materials and technology. He founded Changxing Chemical Company in 1964 to supply resins for coatings. In 1985, he entered the field of electronic chemical materials and produced printed circuit boards. Later, he cooperated with the Industrial Research Institute to develop dry film photoresist, breaking the situation where photoresist is monopolized by foreign countries, and the current production capacity and output value are the world's first.
Academician Gao Yingshi was engaged in the paint industry in his early years.Given that resin is the main raw material for paint, most of the resins are imported from Japan, and the extrusion profit is very high.Optimistic about the takeoff of domestic demand, he resolutely entered the resin industry and founded Changxing Chemical, and transferred Japanese technology in the early stage to drive more than 20% of Changxing's annual growth.Based on its persistence in technological independence and future competitiveness, Gao founder attached great importance to R&D. Later, he will be independent of the R&D department and set up Changxing Research Institute to master the core materials technology.
Through deepening research and development and self-made materials, Changxing has reduced the cost of electronic chemical materials and improved the competitiveness of Taiwan's semiconductor and optoelectronics industries. Changxing's diversified product strategy has also played a complementary and comprehensive effect in a changing environment to ensure that it can still maintain stable growth and profit in the face of the change cycle of different industries.In addition to dry film photoresist, Changxing is also the world's second largest ultraviolet photocuring material and the largest synthetic resin manufacturer in Asia.
In order to expand the business landscape, Changxing was renamed "Changxing Materials Industry" in 2013. It has transformed from a chemical raw material manufacturer to a material supplier, focusing on the innovative research and development of key materials in various industries, covering green energy/energy storage, biomedical materials, soft electronic materials and advanced component materials, and is moving forward with emerging industries.The founder of Gao Yingshi is enthusiastic about talent cultivation, donating scholarships from many universities, and donating to build libraries and chemical buildings to inspire students to learn and contribute to society.
