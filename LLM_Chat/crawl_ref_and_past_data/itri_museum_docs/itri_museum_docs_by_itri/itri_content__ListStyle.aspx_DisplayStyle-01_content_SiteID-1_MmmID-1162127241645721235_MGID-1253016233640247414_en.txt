Technology introduction
Energy-saving house.
The Energy-saving Display House of the Institute of Industrial Research has achieved the development of technologies such as different energy-saving situations, optimization of energy management, intelligent application, maximization of household renewable energy, and comfortable environment, and carried out system integration, product empirical and cooperative media functions.
Application scope
The Institute of Technology and Research will integrate energy-saving technology with construction technology, simulate the common single-family, double-unit, and townhouse forms in South Taiwan, and build an "energy-saving display house". This display house combines the participation of 12 companies including Delta Electric and Formosa Plastics to develop the integrated application of high-efficiency equipment, sensors, lighting, smart control and other technologies. Unlike traditional green electric buildings, it reduces energy consumption by half compared to general buildings, which can be regarded as the best display of Taiwan's advanced green building technology.
Carbon reduction benefits
From energy creation, energy storage, energy conservation to energy management, energy-saving display houses use smart green energy technology, which saves energy by more than 50% compared to ordinary residential buildings. Currently, single-family energy-saving houses have reached the goal of self-sufficiency in electricity and practiced the goal of zero-energy-consuming residential buildings, and the dual-unit form is also close to zero-energy-consuming buildings.In addition, the energy-saving house is also the first smart dispatch testing base for community-type decentralized energy systems in Taiwan, assisting the power industry in conducting power carrying tests.
