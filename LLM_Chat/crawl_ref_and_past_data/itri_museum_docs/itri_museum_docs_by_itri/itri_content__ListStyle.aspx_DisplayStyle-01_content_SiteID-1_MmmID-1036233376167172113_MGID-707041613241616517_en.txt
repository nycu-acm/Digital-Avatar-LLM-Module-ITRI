Technology introduction
Thermal and electrical materials and module technology of the Institute of Technology Research and Research can directly convert electrical energy through temperature difference, and have low temperature, high stability, and small module size. It can solve the problem of difficult waste heat to transport. It can perform thermoelectric conversion operations on the spot, so that small waste heat has economic value for recycling, high efficiency and is not restricted by external environmental factors, and can operate continuously throughout the day.This achievement won the 2012 Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Awards (R&D 100 Awards).
A thermoelectric material is a semiconductor material that can convert heat and electrical energy to each other. It connects two metal conductors or semiconductors of different components into a closed loop. When the two joint ends are placed at different temperatures, this loop will form a thermoelectric electromotive force, thereby generating a current.1.1. It is suitable for small active cooling systems, and related achievements have won the 2012 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D 100 Awards.
Features and innovation
By directly converting electrical energy through temperature difference, the maintenance cost is low, and the micro-nano composite grain structure bismuth antimonide material is developed with rapid solidification technology. The ZT value is 1.57. It has reached the top international standard and has low temperature characteristics. It is suitable for thermal waste between 100-300°C. The heat conversion rate at 200°C is 6%, which is 30% better than traditional power generation.The special structure in the module (composite electrode and dimetal joint layer) makes the module still have high stability at >450°C. The module is small in size, which can solve the problem of difficult waste heat transfer. The thermoelectric conversion operation is carried out on-site, so that the small waste heat has economic value for recycling, high productivity, and is not restricted by external environmental factors, and can operate continuously throughout the day.
Application and benefits
In addition to temperature difference power generation, thermoelectric materials also have the function of energizing heat pumps, so you can achieve lower than the ambient temperature and precise temperature control effects without using a compressor.The maximum temperature difference between the hot and cold ends of the thermoelectric cooling module can reach 70°C, and the energy efficiency ratio reaches 1.1.
