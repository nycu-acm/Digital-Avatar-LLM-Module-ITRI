Net Zero Service Scope
Net zero carbon reduction commercial model construction:
When net zero carbon emissions become a specific operating cost, carbon reduction technologies and applications will bring great changes to the business model of enterprises. For example, machines with smart scheduling and dormant systems have become popular models in the past due to cost issues and were included in net zero carbon emission costs. This shows that the change in the carbon emission cost structure will bring more business models to the industry.Net Zero Earth Network will bring together carbon reduction experts, benchmark cases, and assist enterprises in integrating net zero carbon reduction plans for technical, business and strategic aspects to shape new business models and prepare for the future.
Carbon rights certification trading services:
A free market mechanism is always the best solution to improve the system. In various countries, the development of towards zero-carbon generation will be standardized. Through policy tools such as carbon tax, carbon fees, and carbon trading mechanisms will help improve the effectiveness of net zero carbon reduction.According to statistics from the World Bank in 2021, more than 30 countries and regions have established carbon exchanges in the world, and the scale of carbon trading has exceeded 35% of global carbon emissions; carbon rights certification, carbon offset mechanism and trading strategies will become an important part of the company's operational layout; Net Zero Earth Network will assist enterprises in grasping the trading information and trends of carbon rights, and optimize the grasp and layout planning of carbon rights based on the company's operations.
