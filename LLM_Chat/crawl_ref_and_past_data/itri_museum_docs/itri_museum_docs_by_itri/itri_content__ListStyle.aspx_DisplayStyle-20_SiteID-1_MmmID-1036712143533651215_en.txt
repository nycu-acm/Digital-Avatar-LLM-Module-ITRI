How to get to the Institute of Technology
How to get to the Institute of Technology and Research: Instructions for driving by yourself or taking the high-speed rail and Taiwan Railway
Drive by yourself
Going south to Hsinchu
National Highway 1: Hsinchu Interchange (95A) to Hsinchu Guangfu Road Exit -> Turn left Guangfu Road and go straight to Zhudong Zhongxing Road -> Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research Institute
National Highway No. 3: Bamboo Interchange (90) to Zhudong Zhulin Exit-> County Road 120->Zhongxing Road->Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research Institute
Go north to Hsinchu
National Highway 1: Hsinchu Interchange (95A) to Hsinchu Guangfu Road Exit -> Turn left Guangfu Road and go straight to Zhudong Zhongxing Road -> Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research Institute
National Highway 3: Hsinchu system exit interchange (100) National Highway 1 to Hsinchu->Hsinchu Interchange (95A) to Hsinchu->Guangfu Road Exit->Road Right->Guangfu Road Direction->Zhudong Zhongxing Road->Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research Institute
Take the high-speed rail
High-speed rail Hsinchu Station -> Taxi (about 20 minutes) -> Industrial Research Institute
High-speed railway Hsinchu Station->Taiwan Railway Zhuzhong Station->Taxi (about 5 minutes)->Industrial Research Institute
Take the Taiwan Railway
Taiwan Railway Hsinchu Station->Taiwan Railway Zhuzhong Station->Taxi (about 5 minutes)->Industrial Research Institute
