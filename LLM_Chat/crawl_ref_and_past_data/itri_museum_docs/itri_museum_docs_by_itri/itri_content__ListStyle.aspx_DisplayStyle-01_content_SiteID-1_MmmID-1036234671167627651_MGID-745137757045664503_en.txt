Academician profile
Schentai Academician
Academician Shi Qintai, a pioneer in promoting Taiwan's semiconductor industry, joined the Institute of Technology and Research after returning to China at the age of 30, participated in the first wave of semiconductor technology introduction, assisted the establishment of derivative companies such as UMC, TSMC, and the world's advanced, and opened a new page in Taiwan's semiconductor industry.
1994 When he served as the director of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research from 2003, he adhered to the business philosophy of "specialization and pragmatism", established an open laboratory to promote the joint research and development of technology development and innovation cultivation centers and emerging fields; and actively promoted "organizational activation" and "full resource management", which made the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, which had been established for 30 years, become an internationally renowned research institution.When he served as the chairman of the Institute of Industrial Research in 2008, he continued to assist Taiwan's industry in moving internationally, enhance international horizons, and contributed outstandingly to Taiwan's industry.
Academician Shi made an indelible contribution to cultivating domestic scientific and technological management talents. In 2004, he was recruited by Tsinghua University and served as the dean of the School of Science and Technology Management.With his many years of scientific and technological management experience, he injected abundant energy into scientific management education, and encouraged cooperation between industry and academia.He cooperated with Harvard University to write cases of outstanding local enterprises in Taiwan and set up the first national service science institute to cultivate outstanding management talents for Taiwan.
In addition, Academician Shi also actively assisted Taiwan’s society in progress. When he served as the chairman of the China Securities Association since 2009, he built a "people-oriented" smart life with the vision of "combining science and culture, innovation and openness" through the combination of technology and culture.In 2010, the association won the "First Award for Outstanding Contributions to Global Information Application" awarded by the World Information Technology Service Alliance (WITSA) and played an important role in Taiwan's transformation from a non-information society to an information society.
