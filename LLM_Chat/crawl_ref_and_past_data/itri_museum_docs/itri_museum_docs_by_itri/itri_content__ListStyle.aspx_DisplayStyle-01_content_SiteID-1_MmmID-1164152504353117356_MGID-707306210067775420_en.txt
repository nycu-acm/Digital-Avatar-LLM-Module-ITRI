Technology introduction
Capacitive micromanometer.
This capacitive pressure gauge technology has the characteristics of low energy consumption, miniaturization and high output resolution.It uses the innovative absolute pressure gauge structure and patented design of MEMS processing method (certified), and is combined with low noise capacitor signal processing and adaptive temperature compensation circuit, so that this product technology has the additional functions of intelligent correction and temperature compensation.
Features and innovation
This technology has a patented structural design and can customize the pressure sensing range according to requirements.
Technical Features
Overload protection.
It can be made into absolute pressure/relative pressure type according to requirements.
Built-in reference capacitor.
Technical Specifications
Range: 30~110 kPa.
Sensitivity: 1.5 mV/10 Pa.
RMS noise: 1 Pa.
Application and benefits
This technology can be applied to mobile phones/tablets, wearable devices, car navigation, tire pressure monitoring, safety monitoring, personnel tracking, etc.
