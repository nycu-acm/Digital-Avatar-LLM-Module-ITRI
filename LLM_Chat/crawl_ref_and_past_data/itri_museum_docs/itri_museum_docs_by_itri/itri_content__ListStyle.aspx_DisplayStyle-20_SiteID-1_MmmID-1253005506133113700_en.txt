Southeast Asia Office
On February 28, 2024, the Southeast Asia Office of the Institute of Technology Research and Research was officially established in Thailand.
Faced with global supply chain restructuring, regional economic cooperation has become a key strategy for Taiwan to maintain competitiveness in the global market.With the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology has long played the role of engine of innovation and transformation of Taiwanese businessmen. In order to help Taiwanese businessmen deepen their Southeast Asian market layout and expand industrial cooperation between Taiwan and Southeast Asian countries, the Institute of Technology of Technology officially established the Southeast Asia Office in Thailand on February 28, 2024, symbolizing that Taiwan and Southeast Asian cooperation have entered a key milestone.The Institute of Technology and Research has promoted the Southeast Asian market with three major strategies, including: improving low-carbon and smart manufacturing capabilities through technology and developing new markets; at the same time, introducing biomaterial sources combined with the Institute of Technology and Research to promote sustainable development; through digital training, it assists local enterprises in making full use of talents, and will focus on key areas such as net zero emissions, smart manufacturing, smart medical care, and smart agriculture.The Southeast Asia Office of the Institute of Technology Research will play an important role as a bridge for the upgrading and transformation of Taiwanese businessmen, and will also be a strategic partner for Taiwan's industries to enter the Southeast Asian market.
Service content
Promote exchanges with local academic and research institutions, and promote knowledge sharing and cooperation between the two parties in professional fields
Provide industrial training to assist Taiwanese businessmen to acquire new industrial trends and cultivate talents
Link to the cross-field R&D energy of the Headquarters of the Institute of Technology and Research, and provide technical guidance on transformation and upgrading of Taiwanese businessmen
Provide corporate investment consulting and market intelligence services
The Institute of Technology and Research and Research signed a partner agreement with the Taiwan Business Association of Thailand.
On February 27, 2024, the Institute of Technology Research and the National Academy of Sciences of Thailand (NSTDA) held the first joint seminar.
On February 29, 2024, the team of the Institute of Technology visited the Thai Taiwanese Business Jingbao Precision.
