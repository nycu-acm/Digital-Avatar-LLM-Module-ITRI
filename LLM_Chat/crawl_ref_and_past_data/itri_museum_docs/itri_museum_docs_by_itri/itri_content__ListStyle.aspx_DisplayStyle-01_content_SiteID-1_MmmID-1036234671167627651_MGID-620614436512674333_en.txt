Academician profile
Academician Hu Dinghua
Chairman Hu led the team to introduce semiconductor technology of the US RCA company, and promoted the development of Taiwan's electronics, information, and semiconductor industry technology research and development, technological innovation, business management and risk venture capital, successfully assisted in the establishment of Taiwan's IC industry cluster, laying the foundation for Taiwan's rapid development of the semiconductor industry, and making significant contributions.He has won many honors including the Engineering Medal of the Chinese Society of Engineers, the Excellent Businessman Golden Merchants Award of the Federation of Commerce, the Science and Technology Management Medal of the Republic of China Society of Science and Technology Management and the First Class Economic Medal of the Ministry of Economics.
