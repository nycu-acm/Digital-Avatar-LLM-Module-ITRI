Technology introduction
Ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene products.
The global ethylene and polyethylene production capacity continues to expand, and the red ocean market situation with oversupply and price cuts competition will inevitably impact the profits and capacity utilization of domestic manufacturers.China must establish high-value ethylene product technology, including homemade catalysts and polymerization technology development, to enhance industrial competitiveness.Ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene has excellent wear resistance and impact resistance than general polyethylene, and can be used in special high-value products.
Features and innovation
Novel catalyst design, synthesis, catalyst bearing and slurry polymerization system technology can control molecular weight by 1 million to 5 million.
Application and benefits
Depending on different molecular weight and physical needs, ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene can be used in green energy industry (battery isolation membrane), biomedical industry (artificial joints/filter materials), aerospace industry (cables/composite materials), national defense and people's livelihood (bodyproof vests, cut-proof vests), etc., to increase the added value of products.
