Academician Lin Xianming
Academician Lin Xianming is the chairman of Wistron Zitong Co., Ltd., leading Wistron Zitong innovative research and development, becoming a leading manufacturer in the global information and communications industry.Chairman Lin has made outstanding contributions to the telecommunications industry. Wistron has won the 2,000 global corporate honors for 12 consecutive years. In 2009, it was ranked among the top 100 global science and technology factories of Thomson Reuters in 2018, and won the 500 global corporate honors of Fortune in five years from 2011 to 2012 and 2018 to 2020.
Chairman Lin graduated from the Department of Computing and Control Engineering of National Jiaotong University. He joined Acer Computer Technology Company in 1979. He has served in many important positions in Pan Acer Group and has been promoted to General Manager of Acer Computer Group.In 2001, it led the R&D and manufacturing service business (DMS) from Acer Computer and established Wistron, which focuses on manufacturing, R&D and services.With its more than 20 years of product development experience, we have led the group to the international level, providing customers with a comprehensive design, production and after-sales service system, and winning market trust with excellent integration capabilities.Our customers include major global information leading brands, making Wistron Zitong one of the world's largest professional design and OEM factories for information and communication products.
Chairman Lin is known as an outstanding entrepreneur who leads the company to continue to grow and manage successfully with wisdom and perseverance.It uses the value of "circular outside and square inside" and uses conscience as the foundation to run a business in a harmonious way.Chairman Lin is good at understanding the needs of the team and founded the "Simon's Talk" communication mechanism to gather the centripetal force within the company; attaches importance to talent cultivation and cultivates colleagues to develop leadership skills; encourages the team to spend at least 20% of the time every day to think deeply to promote innovation. His business philosophy of "highly authorized and encourages innovation" has been widely recognized by the industry. He was awarded the 2008 Asian Best Enterprise Leader Award by CNBC Asia, and was awarded the National Excellence Award by the Association of Enterprise Managers of the Republic of China in 2013.
In recent years, Chairman Lin has diversified his forward-looking business.The group's Weiying Technology and Industrial Research Institute have cooperated with the development of the domestic cloud integration solution Cloud OS, breaking through the situation of foreign-invested cloud system software market and accelerating the upgrading and transformation of Taiwan's information and communication industry.At the same time, it actively leads the group to move towards emerging businesses such as cloud computing, software services, smart medical care, and AI, and is committed to practicing the group's "DNA transfer", and expanding new cross-field opportunities for the information and communications industry to integrate software, hardware and cloud.
