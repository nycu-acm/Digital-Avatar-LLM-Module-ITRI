Fast energy-saving and customized biochar expert system to effectively reuse surplus agricultural resources
Energy-saving and fast biochar expert system module.
Taiwan has tens of millions of metric tons of agricultural and forestry residue every year.For example, Changhua County, which mainly produces grapes, has to deal with a large number of abandoned vines every year. In the past, the most common way of treatment was concentrated combustion, but it is also accompanied by air pollution and carbon emissions.
If the zero-waste recycling method can be used to generate high value-added biomass and energy recycling, it will increase the output value of the reuse of the agricultural circular economy and reduce the cost of agricultural waste treatment. In order to solve the problem of large amounts of carbon emissions caused by open-pit combustion, the Industrial and Research Institute has developed a carbon reduction conversion technology for agricultural residues, and uses a digital energy-saving and rapid carbonization system to convert agricultural residues on-site into functional ecological materials (can reduce chemical fertilizers and reduce greenhouse gases generated by later-transferred compost).
In addition to reducing CO2 emissions by 9.36 metric tons per metric ton of agricultural residues treated, biochar and natural vinegar liquid can be produced, and the process can also be synchronized for thermal energy utilization.The feed sources include biomass materials such as grape vines, bamboo, pruning, drifting wood or rice husks.The system is also combined with composite modification processes such as bacterial strain embedding technology, and widely used materials in long-term soil optimization, plant growth promotion, organic pest control agents, as well as diversified products such as air purification substrates, fruit and vegetable preservation materials, green building materials, electromagnetic wave resistance materials, etc.
At present, regional demonstration systems have been established in Erlin Town Office, Hualien Fuli Township Farmers Association and Luoshan Community, and work with local governments to move towards the net zero emission target with ecological technology.
