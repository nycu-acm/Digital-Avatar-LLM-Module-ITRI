Technology introduction
Instant online monitoring of fastener process.
There are more than 2,000 fasteners-related operators in Taiwan, with an annual output of 160 tons in the fastener industry, making it the world's third largest exporter and a very important industry in Taiwan.The molding process is the most critical process to determine the quality of fasteners. Therefore, introducing real-time process inspection and failure cause analysis in the process has become the current trend of industrial development.The Institute of Technology has developed the "online instant monitoring of fastener process" intelligent sensing module, which uses an innovative double-end expansion buckle design to provide a high-stability and convenient disassembly and assembly force sensor, plus a patented simulan static reading circuit to reduce signal distortion.
