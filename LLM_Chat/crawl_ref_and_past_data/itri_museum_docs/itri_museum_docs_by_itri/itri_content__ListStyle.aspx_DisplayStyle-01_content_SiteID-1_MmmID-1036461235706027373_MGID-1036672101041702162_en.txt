Introduction
Since its establishment in June 1979, our association has always adhered to the position of non-profit, professional and public welfare association legal persons, actively served as a communication bridge between the industry and the government, coordinated and adjusted the opinions and consensus of the industry, and strived for the government's attention and guidance to the industry, so as to accelerate the upgrading and development of Taiwan's fluid transmission industry.The future development of our association's conference affairs still depends on the continued support and encouragement of all our industry leaders to implement the purpose of improving the upgrading and development of Taiwan's fluid transmission industry.
