Ministry of Economic Affairs promotes Taiwan's first domestic self-driving logistics trial launch, Industrial Research Institute joins hands with Hsinchu Logistics and Hsinchu Municipal Government to expand a new smart future

Date: 2021/11/15
On the 15th, the Ministry of Economic Affairs jointly held the "Self-Driving Logistics•Smart Future, Taiwan's First Self-Driving Logistics Unveiling Activity" with the Institute of Industrial Research, Hsinchu Logistics and the Hsinchu Municipal Government, announcing the launch of self-driving logistics services in Hsinchu city.The VIPs in the picture are from right to left, Liu Wenxiong, Director of the Institute of Technology, Qiu Qiuhui, Director of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics, Lin Quanneng, Deputy Director of the Ministry of Economics, Lin Zhijian, Mayor of Hsinchu, Li Zhengyi, General Manager of Hsinchu Logistics, and He Nuanxuan, Chief General Consultant of Yangde Group.
The Ministry of Economic Affairs (15) jointly held the "Self-Driving Logistics • Smart Future, Taiwan's First Self-Driving Logistics Unveiling Activity" with the Institute of Technology Research, Hsinchu Logistics, and the Hsinchu Municipal Government, and announced the launch of self-driving logistics services in Hsinchu City. This is the first self-driving logistics experimental test case in Taiwan directly on an open real human-vehicle hybrid road. The total length is about 1.9 kilometers. Through the feasibility verification of the service, it will introduce innovative operation models to the domestic logistics operation industry, and it is an important milestone in the commercialization of domestic smart logistics.
Deputy Minister Lin Quanneng of the Ministry of Economic Affairs said that according to the World Economic Forum, the demand for e-commerce delivery in 2030 will drive a 36% increase in logistics vehicles. Coupled with the international trend of unmanned self-driving logistics under zero contact, it is predicted that the global output value of fully autonomous driving will reach as high as US$7 trillion in 2050. The Ministry of Economic Affairs has two key tasks in promoting self-driving technology.First: formulate the Regulations on the Innovation Experiment of Unmanned Vehicles to provide an experimental environment for operators to develop unmanned Vehicles’ innovative technology. At present, the plan has approved 11 cases and led 35 operators to experiment on the road, which is estimated to drive 400 million yuan in investment; Second: invest in the establishment of key technologies for self-driving vehicles, including perception, decision-making and control key subsystems, with the goal of nurturing large system integration factories and promoting Taiwan to establish an autonomous driving industry ecosystem chain to enter the international market.This transformation meets the practical application of domestic five-ton self-driving trucks required by logistics operators. We hope to gradually solve the operational pain points of the industry, and also develop innovative operation models in this process to let the people experience better services.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that the self-driving car system developed by the Institute of Technology is designed for the special road environments of Taiwan and Asia, and has overcome domestic environments such as mixed transportation of automobile and locomotives and long rainy seasons.This is the first case in Taiwan, and it cooperates with Hsinchu Logistics, a leading brand in the domestic logistics service industry. This special application situation is from the self-driving delivery from warehouse to warehouse, and is driving in the city of Hsinchu's busiest mixed traffic of automobiles and locomotives. The efficient calculation system handles the complexity of information such as people, cars, roads, etc., and is completely operated in Taiwan's unique automobiles and locomotives complex traffic environment. This self-driving logistics test will be a major breakthrough.The Industrial Research Institute will continue to work with manufacturers to build a self-driving industry chain, use key technologies to assist more vehicle manufacturers, operation service providers, vehicle networking, automotive sensors, automotive electronics and other industries to enter the new field of self-driving, drive Taiwan's technological strength, create smart life, and create new opportunities for commercial development.
Hsinchu Mayor Lin Zhijian said that Hsinchu is the youngest city in Taiwan’s counties and cities with the top competitiveness and happiness index. The Hsinchu Municipal Government regards itself as a platform and upholds the spirit of "friendly, innovative and open field". Two years ago, Hsinchu City and the Institute of Technology and Research won the first gold medal in the country's "Taiwan No. 0001" self-driving special test license plate. Currently, two years of verification tests have been completed at Nanliao Tourism Fishing Port.This time, we have cooperated again with Hsinchu Logistics, the largest and best-quality logistics system in Taiwan and the strongest self-driving team of Taiwan's Institute of Technology to fully demonstrate the innovative vision of a science and technology city. We hope to drive Taiwanese logistics operators to undergo digital transformation of smart logistics and create a new logistics operation service model through the development of self-driving logistics vehicle technology.
Li Zhengyi, general manager of Hsinchu Logistics under Yangde Group, said that with this cooperation case, the self-driving logistics system can be copied to many business offices and transfer centers across Taiwan after the success of the self-driving logistics system in the future. Hsinchu Logistics has 450,000 pieces of cargo per month in Da Hsinchu District. After the self-driving technology is introduced into the Hsinchu logistics system, the first stage will not enter the transfer center by 20% of the cargo volume in the region, which will initially reduce 10,000 (pieces/month) into the transfer center, and will be directly delivered between the business offices to solve the problem of insufficient drivers in the logistics industry, and at the same time reduce the phenomenon of drivers working long hours late at night.In addition to the introduction of Hsinchu logistics self-driving technology in this case, the group's Shilin Motor also has a series of commercial electric vehicles technical cooperation with the Industrial Research Institute and the leading domestic commercial vehicle manufacturer Zhonghua Automobile, hoping to play a leading role in the field of logistics electric vehicles in the future.
Hu Zhusheng, director of the Institute of Mechanical and Mechanical Systems of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the Gold Medal Self-Driving Team of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute adopts a modular software design, and has the only advantage in Taiwan that can be quickly installed into various models. It can quickly transplant a variety of models according to the field and functional needs. It has successfully conducted self-driving technology verification in small trucks, cars, RVs, large trucks, buses and other vehicles. It has completed international self-driving cooperation projects and testing and verification in open fields such as Nanliao, Hsinchu and Taichung Shuishang in China (including various weather and time periods), and has accumulated a self-driving mileage of more than 6,000 kilometers. It is the self-driving team with the most models and the most practical experience in Taiwan.The Institute of Technology will also draw up the 2030 independent mobile technology strategy and blueprint, and work with manufacturers to build a self-driving industry chain.

It announced that the Institute of Industrial Research and Technology and Hsinchu Logistics will cooperate with the Institute of Industrial Research and Technology and the first in Taiwan to deliver goods from warehouses to warehouses through self-driving and driving in the busiest urban area of ​​Hsinchu City with the busiest mixed traffic of automobiles and locomotives in Hsinchu.
