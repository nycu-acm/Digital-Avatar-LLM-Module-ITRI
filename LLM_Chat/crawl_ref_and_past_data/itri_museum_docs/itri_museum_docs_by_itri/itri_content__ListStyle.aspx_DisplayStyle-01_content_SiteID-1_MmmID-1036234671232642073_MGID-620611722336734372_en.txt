Group photo of the First Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (101 Year)
Time: July 4, 2011
Location: Conference 604, Hall 51, No. 195, Section 4, Zhongxing Road, Zhudong Town, Hsinchu County (Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research)
Chairman: Chairman Cai Qingyan
Meeting conclusion focus
Strengthen investment to connect basic research to industrialized research and development to implement industrial benefits:
It is recommended that the government adjust the allocation of scientific research funds, combine it with the industry, and strengthen investment from the perspective of market pulling force to connect basic research and development between industrialization to achieve industrial application.It is recommended that the Industrial Research Institute should establish the R&D management mechanism and culture required for connection from the industry/market pull, so that people-oriented design thinking can improve cross-field cooperation and insight capabilities through internalization.The connection between upstream operators and clients can play the role of catalysts, allowing innovative technologies to be implemented in industrial applications.For example, innovative achievements in the petrochemical or materials industry can be introduced into the domestic electronics industry.
Reshape the entrepreneurial culture and innovation atmosphere and liven up early stage venture capital:
Many countries have encouraged the development of startups, believing that it can not only provide a higher employment rate, but also promote economic development.However, Taiwan’s current venture capital environment is not favorable. Under the gradual hidden worries, the government should review the venture capital environment more carefully and vigorously improve it to facilitate the development of early venture capital, enhance Taiwan’s entrepreneurial environment, and shape an attractive innovation island.With an effective commercialization mechanism, more startups will be created. In the future, as long as you are in Taiwan, you can grasp the global venture capital trends and pulses.
Master industrial division and combination, breakthrough thinking, and create industrial value:
When the industry is re-split and combined, there are many opportunities. South Korea and mainland China adopt the state capitalist model. Taiwan’s approach is different from them. Therefore, it is necessary to use a role like the Industrial Research Institute to assist the industry in crossing the valley of death from a long-term development perspective and find the most suitable layout strategy from investment benefits and risks.For example, the development of the technology industry in the next decade has gradually strolled from personal computing to the cloud; while the power of Wintel (Microsoft's Windows software platform + Intel's hardware architecture) that was unattainable in the past, has recently created many opportunities for Taiwan's technology industry due to the rise of ARM based architecture and open operating systems. In the long run, Taiwan does not have independent CPUs and operating systems, and it is difficult for a single enterprise to develop related technologies. The government and the Institute of Technology should think about how to break through difficulties and create more industrial value and opportunities for Taiwan.
Patent layout to strengthen the overall thinking of the industrial chain:
In addition to deepening the research and development of key technologies, the Industrial Research Institute should also actively invest resources to carry out the overall patent integration layout of the relevant upstream and downstream industrial chains, and at the same time understand the patent strategies of competitors, seize the initiative, and enhance the competitiveness of industrial development.
