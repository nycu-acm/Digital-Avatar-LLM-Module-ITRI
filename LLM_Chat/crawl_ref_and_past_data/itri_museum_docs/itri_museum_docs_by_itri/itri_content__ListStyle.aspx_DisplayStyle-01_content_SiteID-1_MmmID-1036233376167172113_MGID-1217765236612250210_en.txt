Technology introduction
Regenerate PET esters and fibers.
The global regeneration PET target demand in 2030 is expected to rise to 30 million tons.With the increasing demand for the recycling of polyester packaging materials and textiles by international regulations and brands, the closed cycle of "bottle to bottle" and "fiber to fiber" will become the direction of future cycle development.Due to the high content of impurities such as dyes and polyester fabrics in waste variegated bottles and polyester fabrics, the quality and reuse of chemical recycling products are seriously affected. The Industrial Research Institute can effectively improve the removal and purification effects of dyes and heterogeneous monomers through integrated process technology development such as catalysts, depolymerization and purification, and can obtain high-purity and low-color BHET recycled raw materials.
Features and innovation
This team developed heterophase catalyst technology with low metal dissolution and high-efficiency color removal integrated process technology. Through the process end, selective processing procedures are introduced to improve dye removal efficiency. The low-cost recrystallization process is used to replace the high-energy and high-cost molecular distillation program. High-purity and low-color BHET can be stably prepared, and the reaggregation PET characteristics are similar to those of new petrochemical materials.Compared with the carbon footprint of the final incinerated PET using this technology, the carbon emissions can be reduced by ~60%.
Application and benefits
Recycled and remade low-carbon BHET as PET monomer raw material can be widely used in the development of low-carbon products such as polyester packaging materials, fabrics/garments, functional polyester/resin. If the research and development and production energy of the domestic polyester industry can be integrated, it will be able to establish domestic independent key process technology and stabilize Taiwan's important supply chain position in the global brand clothing/packing material regeneration market.
