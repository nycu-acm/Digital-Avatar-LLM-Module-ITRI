A sneak peek!The Institute of Industrial Research won two 2018 CES "Innovation Awards" to protect smart life with innovative intelligent technology

Date: 2017/11/10
The Institute of Technology won the innovation awards in 2018 CES Robotics and Drones and Home Appliances for its intelligent vision system robot and random smart fruit and vegetable pesticide detector. The intelligent vision system robot uses the intelligent vision technology and robotic arms developed by the Institute of Technology, and has deep visual sensing, self-learning, thinking and judgment capabilities. This year, it will play Scrabble with visitors. It is a robot that combines wisdom and companionship.
The Light of Taiwan, the Institute of Technology and Research won the 2018 CES Innovation Award!The 2018 CES Innovation Awards announced the list of winners on the 9th Eastern Time.With the support of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology stood out among the technical products of many exhibitors around the world with its smart vision system robots and casual smart fruit and vegetable pesticide detectors. It won the innovation awards in robots and drones and home appliances respectively. It won the awards together with Intel, Dell, Nissan, AMD, Kensington and other companies, allowing Taiwan's technology to shine again in the world.
The CES Innovation Awards is an award established by the American Consumer Technology Association (CTA), which hosts the CES exhibition, for the CES exhibition, commending outstanding product design and engineering technology in 28 major categories of products.Each category consists of a jury of consumer technology designers, engineers and media members. They are selected based on the standards of product engineering quality, art design, consumer demands, social/industry influence, originality, product market competitiveness, and awards innovation awards and best innovation awards respectively.Google, Bosch, Qualcomm, HP, and Intel have all won the CES Best Innovation Award and Innovation Award. This is an excellent honor for the exposure of new products from major manufacturers in global brands, and it is also a must-fight for major manufacturers over the years.
Liu Zhongming, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that the CES Innovation Award is a world-renowned consumer electronic technology award.Many global brand manufacturers actively compete for awards every year.As long as the products participating in the competition are the best results of each unit, winning the award is the greatest recognition of technological research, development, innovation and design. In recent years, the Institute of Technology has entered technology development from user needs, connected to open innovation platforms, established cross-domain system technologies and products, and created differentiated value.The two technologies that won this award are based on the integrated results of the research and development of open platforms in response to the needs of smart life.The smart vision robot system is developed integrated with the application of AI, machinery and system technologies. It has self-learning and sensing functions and can be used for home companionship, care and industrial use. The Institute of Technology also uses technology to check food safety.The casual smart fruit and vegetable pesticide detector is a detection device that allows people to "safe and safely see", which provides an additional layer of control over the health of the people, so that everyone can eat healthily and safely.
"Smart Vision System Robot" uses smart vision technology and robotic arms developed by the Institute of Technology and Research, and has deep visual sensing, self-learning, thinking and judgment capabilities.When playing scrabble games with your opponent, you do not need to use special devices. The intelligent vision system robot can use a depth vision sensor to detect the position of the square first, and will also identify the position of the building blocks, letters, and spelling completed by the opponent. It can also determine the highest score to judge the possible combination of letters for spelling.The intelligent vision robot system can first accurately correct the 3D spatial position of the robot arm, detect the position of the building block, convert the 3D image into the 3D spatial position, calculate the spatial position and relative relationship of the robot glasses, the robot arm and the building block, and then recognize the letters on the 2D image on the surface of the building block and drive the arm to clip. It is a robot that combines both wisdom and companionship.
The "Suspicious Smart Fruit and Vegetable Pesticide Detector" uses the micro-chip technology patented by the Institute of Technology and Research, integrating multi-wavelength miniaturization detection technology, and designs the large optical detector in the laboratory into a lightweight appearance that is mastered by one hand. By detecting the specific spectrum of pesticide residues, we can understand whether the fruit and vegetables are washed clean, and the light signals can be clearly displayed through the red, yellow and green lights.In addition, in response to different usage habits around the world, detectors can customize pesticide databases. For example, many common pesticides in the United States, such as Yidamin, Atomin, Berkel and Huttaning, can all have a detection limit of 0.5ppm. Because this technology has great potential in the international market, it has won the Innovation Award at the Consumer Electronics Exhibition this year after being shortlisted for this year's Top 100 Science and Technology Awards, which can be called the glory of Taiwan.
The Institute of Technology won the innovation awards in 2018 for its intelligent vision system robot and random-type intelligent fruit and vegetable pesticide detector. Among them, the random-type intelligent fruit and vegetable pesticide detector uses micro-chip technology to measure the specific spectra of pesticide residues, integrates multi-wavelength miniaturization detection technology to understand whether fruit and vegetable are cleaned, and makes the large optical detector in the laboratory become a lightweight appearance that is mastered by one hand.
