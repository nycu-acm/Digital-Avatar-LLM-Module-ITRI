Taiwan's 5G small base station intelligent management technology won international awards!The intelligent planning and management technology of small base stations in the Industrial Research Institute has been recognized internationally and has once again won the annual award of the global action communications industry - the Special Jury Award of the Small Cell Forum (SCF).
Taiwan's 5G small base station intelligent management technology won international awards!The "Athena SON - RAN Planning & Optimization Service" developed by the Institute of Technology won the Judges’ Choice of the SCF Awards 2020 for the Global Action Communications Industry.This is the second time that the Intelligent Management Technology of the Institute of Technology has won the jury special award. The key to this award is to provide the best configuration solution in the network layout and optimization of small base stations through AI artificial intelligence, assisting Netcom equipment providers and system integrators to have optimal configuration suggestions when building a dedicated network field, such as the number, location and radio frequency of small base stations, and can automatically optimize signal coverage of the private network field.At present, this technology has been cooperated with Mingtai Technology.
Small Cell Forum (SCF) is an international organization that promotes the application of small base stations. It selects contributors in various related fields such as technological innovation breakthroughs and industrial promotion in the global small base station industry every year, and awards the Small Cell Forum Awards, including commercial layout awards, small base station innovation awards, and special jury awards.Most of the winners in previous years have been internationally renowned industry industry, such as AT&T, Verizon, SoftBank, Nokia, Cisco, Intel, Qualcomm, T-Mobile, Vodafone, Commscope, etc.
Que Zhike, director of the Information and Communications Research Institute of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that 5G technology has begun to enter commercial applications this year.This year, the number of participants in SCF Awards 2020 hits a new high, and the competition is quite fierce. It is not easy for the "small base station network intelligent planning and management technology products" of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute to stand out.It is obvious that Taiwan's small base station intelligent technology has been on par with the international side. He emphasized that with the support of the Science and Technology Project of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology has continued to deepen the intelligent management technology of small base stations, from the most basic base station management and network optimization to the ability to actively track user locations to provide dynamic network optimization.It has now expanded to assisting network manufacturers and private network system integration manufacturers to carry out precise network deployment, so that domestic 5G private network applications such as smart medical care, smart manufacturing, smart transportation, and smart commerce can be implemented smoothly, and the application situation of accurate planning and smart optimization of 5G private networks can be achieved, and the labor and time costs of the private network during the deployment and maintenance period are reduced.
Lin Yuqin, general manager of Mingtai Technology, said that after he won the first prize at the SCF Awards in 2018, he felt honored to win the award with the SCF Awards again this year.Mingtai Technology has accumulated a lot of experience in the research and development of small base stations since the 3G era, especially in the development of key technologies for small base stations L1 PHY (Port Physical Layer), antenna harness waveforming (Beamforming) and processing capabilities, it has shown core competitiveness, and meets the goal of 5G's high transmission speed and high capacity in terms of transmission capacity, coverage and efficiency.Mingtai actively responds to the government's 5G policy and has worked closely with the Institute of Industrial Research for a long time in many special project fields, including public utility power Internet of Things, smart medical care for the General Hospital of the Three Military Academy, and smart exhibitions.
The layout and maintenance of traditional small base station networks require experienced engineers to plan, optimize, monitor and maintain, which consumes a lot of time and operating costs.For example, in order to meet the signal coverage and performance goals that meet user needs, in the early stage of the construction of a dedicated network, a number of field surveys, sand table deductions, and simulations are required to have preliminary planning; after setting up the base station, it is necessary to optimize and adjust the environmental signal measurement and base station parameter configuration to obtain the best configuration combination.If the user needs change, you have to start over. The "Small Base Station Network Intelligent Planning and Management Technology" developed by the Institute of Technology can shorten the layout and planning time of the dedicated network field of general enterprises from days or weeks to hours to find the best network planning suggestions. It can quickly respond and flexibly adjust the network configuration of small base stations, which is closer to user usage needs.
About Small Cell Forum (SCF)
This organization is the world's largest international organization dedicated to promoting the technological development, standard formulation, interoperability verification, field application, and enterprise vertical dedicated network application of small base stations around the world.Participants include front-line international telecom operators, equipment vendors, test equipment vendors, system integrators, and enterprise vertical dedicated network operators.
SCF selects contributors to the global small base station industry in the UK every year and awards the Small Base Station Forum Awards (SCF Awards), which is the annual award of the global mobile communications industry. The winners of the past years include AT&T, Verizon, SoftBank, Nokia, Cisco, Intel, Qualcomm, T-Mobile, Vodafone, Commscope, etc. The SCF award is independently evaluated by 10 international analysts, professional journalists and Netcom experts.
