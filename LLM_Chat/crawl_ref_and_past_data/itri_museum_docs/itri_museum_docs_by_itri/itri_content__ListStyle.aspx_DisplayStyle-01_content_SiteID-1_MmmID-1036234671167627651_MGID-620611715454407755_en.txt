Academician Zhang Zhongmou
The Taiwan Integrated Circuit Manufacturing Company founded by Chairman Zhang is a pioneer in the global "wafer foundry" industry. It has driven the development of packaging, design industries, etc., established Taiwan's complete semiconductor industry system, contributed a lot to Taiwan's economic development, and won many honors such as the IEEE Honor Medal, Regional Growth Contribution Award, and Model Leadership Award.The TSMC Cultural and Education Foundation, founded by Chairman Zhang in 1998, aims to promote various public welfare activities, and is committed to giving back to the society and making great contributions to the national and social development.
