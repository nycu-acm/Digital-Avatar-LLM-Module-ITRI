Technology introduction
Multi-diversity navigation service robot (iAGV).
The diversified navigation service robot of the Institute of Technology has a diversified positioning navigation iAGV (Intelligent Automated Guided Vehicle) environment with frequent temporary obstacles on the ground, and the ceiling positioning navigation has a dynamic line computing engine for "optimization of dynamic lines" and "avoiding congestion", which greatly improves operating efficiency.
Solve industry problems
The goods in the logistics center are often piled up in a mess, and it takes time to find goods.
The cart trolley personnel need to confirm the distribution area of ​​each item by themselves, and then sort the shipments from each area one by one.
Pure ground type AGVs (self-guided vehicles) are difficult to locate and carry.
Features and innovation
Diversified Positioning Navigation iAGV (Intelligent Automated Guided Vehicle)
Environments with frequent obstacles on the ground → Ceiling positioning navigation.
It has a dynamic line computing engine with "optimization of dynamic line" and "obstruction avoidance", which greatly improves operating efficiency.
Ceiling feature depth identification positioning navigation technology (the world's first)
