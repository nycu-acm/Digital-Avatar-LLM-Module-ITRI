Vice President Peng Yumin of the Institute of Technology Research, President Naoki Shinohara (online), Director Qiu Qiu Qiuhui of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics, and Chief Deputy Representative of Hoshino Hikari, Taipei Office of the Taiwan Exchange Association of Japan, etc.
The Institute of Technology and Research has developed the "Epidemic Can Opening Kit" with excellent testing characteristics and has obtained the first overseas order.With the cooperation between Japan Biotechno Pharma (JBP) and the Trade Union Group, it has successfully entered the Japanese market and obtained a sales license from the Ministry of Health, Labor and Welfare of Japan. It will be sold nationwide from now on. In the future, it will expand its application in overseas markets and provide use in Japanese hospitals in emerging countries, showing Taiwan's outstanding epidemic prevention technology to assist the international community, and contribute to global epidemic prevention.
