Welcoming the first year of energy storage after the table, the Industrial Research Institute joins hands with the official research industry, and helps operators seize the opportunity of giant yuan

Date: 2025/06/11
The Institute of Technology and Research held the "Symposium on the Application of Technology Energy Storage and System Resilience", calling on industry officials and research experts to discuss the prospects of the energy storage industry and share practical success stories. From the left picture, Chen Yiping, general manager of Yongyu Intelligent, Liu Zhiwen, director of the Green Energy Institute of Industrial Research Institute, Wang Yaoting, general manager of Taiwan Electric Power Research Institute, Liu Wenxiong, president of Taiwan Electric Power and Energy Engineering Association, Wu Zhiwei, deputy director of the Energy Administration of the Ministry of Economics, Wu Jinzhong, deputy general manager of Taiwan Electric Power Company, and Zheng Zhiwen, CEO of Anserwei.
The global AI wave drives the growth of power demand, and the combined power storage system helps stabilize the resilience and efficiency of the power grid. Therefore, all countries are actively deploying it. According to statistics from the International Energy Agency (IEA), by 2030, the global overall energy storage capacity will increase by as much as 6 times the current level. US research agency Research Nester also estimates that the market size will exceed US$661.27 billion in 2037, with unlimited business opportunities.The Institute of Technology and Research and Development worked with the Taiwan Electric Power and Energy Engineering Association to hold the "Symposium on the Application of Technology Energy Storage and System Resilience" on the 11th, inviting experts from the Ministry of Economics Energy Administration, Taiwan Electric Power, Yongyu Intelligence, Anselwei, etc. to share the prospects of the energy storage industry and actual successful cases. We hope to encourage more cross-domain industries to invest together, build and improve the energy storage industry chain, strengthen the resilience of the power grid and seize the business opportunities of the international energy storage market.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research and Technology and chairman of the Taiwan Electric Power and Energy Engineering Association, said that how to take into account the trend of stable power supply and carbon reduction has become the key to Taiwan's energy transformation. Only by working together with supply and demand to establish a resilient energy strategy can we respond to future challenges.We can use Mirror International to introduce integrated resource planning analysis, and after comprehensively considering the resources of both power supply and demand, we can find the power usage combination with the lowest cost and best efficiency in a rational way to avoid resource waste and excessive investment.From the power demand side, starting from improving energy efficiency and smart electricity consumption can also contribute to the stability of power supply; and on the supply side, in addition to building renewable energy facilities, energy storage systems are also indispensable, because renewable energy and energy storage complement each other like dance partners, which can effectively solve the intermittent characteristics of renewable energy, balance the peaks of electricity consumption, and help the grid stability and energy sustainability.This seminar gathers Taiwan Power and the industry to share energy storage cases, hoping to encourage more operators to invest in the energy storage field, helping power to be stable and resilient.
Wang Yaoting, general manager of Taiwan Electric Power Grid, said that the power grid is Taiwan’s most important common asset and will continue to invest and strengthen its resilience. Faced with the expectation of power stability in industries and people’s livelihood, Taiwan Electric Power Company actively implements the secondary energy transformation policy and promotes strategies such as supply based on demand, diversified green energy, deep energy conservation, and advanced energy storage systems.At present, TaiPhone has built large grid-level energy storage systems in Taoyuan, Tainan, Kaohsiung and other places. At the same time, through the power trading platform that will be launched in 2021, private energy storage systems can participate in diversified power commodities, improve grid resilience and create new business opportunities; in addition, the currently being promoted technology energy storage policies encourage users to set up energy storage, TaiPhone redesigns time electricity price rates, raises the sharp-to-peak price difference, and provides operators with enough incentives to set up.This seminar is a good opportunity to share and communicate with the industry. Taiwan Power hopes to work with the industry to help Taiwan's energy transformation and upgrading.
The seminar specially invited Wu Zhiwei, Deputy Director of the Energy Administration of the Ministry of Economics, Wu Jinzhong, Deputy General Manager of Taiwan Electric, Zheng Zhiwen, CEO of Anselway, and Chen Yiping, General Manager of Yongyu Intelligence, to give special speeches on the topics of "Energy Storage Promotion Policy", "Evaluation and Strengthening of System Resilience", "Discussion on Multi-Application Mechanisms of Energy Storage", and "Achievement of Energy Storage Systems in Industrial Applications".
Wu Zhiwei, deputy director of the Energy Administration of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, pointed out that at this stage, my country's power generation and grid energy storage have developed steadily, and the grid energy storage has also reached the 2025 1,000MW target; the Ministry of Economic Affairs will use "technical energy storage" as the focus of user-side energy storage in the future. Science and technology energy storage is to encourage industrial users to import post-meter energy storage systems to transfer power during peak peaks of the power system. At the same time, operators can also manage power consumption through post-meter energy storage to improve power quality.
Wu Jinzhong, deputy general manager of Taiwan Electric Power, said in his speech that Taiwan Electric Power has a complete plan to strengthen the resilience and stability of the power grid. In terms of capacity reserve, Taiwan Electric Power cooperated with the Ministry of Economic Affairs to adjust the power supply light rating system to improve information transparency and provide specific power saving suggestions; in response to the increase in renewable energy, Taiwan Electric Power and the Institute of Technology jointly studied the technical conditions for energy storage to provide virtual inertia, verify that energy storage can act according to the design specifications, so that energy storage can perform ROCOF measurements, and conduct actual reactions to output power, providing system virtual inertia and frequency modulation.
Zheng Zhiwen, CEO of Anselway, said that Taiwan's energy storage applications are divided into two types: "before the table" and "after the table". The front of the table refers to installation at the power plant or grid end. Because the market design is currently mostly built or utilized by Taiwan Power itself;the energy storage after the table is installed at the user end, such as the company factory office, etc. Among them, in addition to being able to sell through Taiwan's power trading platform, "post-meter energy storage" in Taiwan can also enter factories with the extra energy, and the future is promising.For enterprises, investing in post-meter energy storage to establish an energy moat to ensure stability and safety of electricity use.
Chen Yiping, general manager of Yongyu Intelligent, said that the domestic energy storage system has achieved many application results in the industrial field. Taking Yongfengyu as an example, the first gas-electric symbiosis combined with meter-meter energy storage system in Taiwan has been built to integrate energy creation, energy storage, energy consumption and energy regulation to optimize the energy use efficiency of the plant;and an industrial microgrid energy storage system is built in Yongfengyu's own papermaking factory, combining renewable energy and diversified generator sets in the factory to optimize energy dispatch and application, effectively reducing operating costs.
In response to the global net zero emission trend demand, the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" has been drawn up by the Institute of Technology to include the technology of smart grids and energy storage and related talent cultivation in the field of "sustainable environment" to take the lead in future competitiveness.
