Features and innovation
HYA-HEAL
+
It is an exclusive material developed by the Institute of Materials Chemicals of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute. It is a small molecule hyaluronic acid modified by amino acids.With the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs' science and technology project, it was originally developed as a coating material for controlled release of cancer drugs, so that the drugs will not cause harm to the tissues around the body while killing cancer cells.When designing materials, a high degree of biosafety and compatibility are particularly required to cover hydrophobic colorectal cancer drugs.
Drug development must conduct a series of tests. During the testing process, the R&D team unexpectedly discovered that this material is very effective in inhibiting MMPs (matrix metalloprotein enzyme) and protecting collagen from degradation by MMPs.Therefore, the drugs originally used for cancer treatment have transformed into goalkeepers with beautiful women, helping the vast number of female friends who want to fight skin aging to protect their youth.
