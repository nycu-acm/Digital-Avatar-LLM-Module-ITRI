False advertising instructions for online glucose-lowering meter

Date: 2022/01/03
Recently, unscrupulous individuals stole and tampered with photos on the official website of the Institute of Engineering Research and Development to sell "Glycemic Reduction Meters" of unknown origin. This court has taken legal action and the following explanation is given:
1. This product is not developed and produced by our institute, nor is it transferred from our institute. This fraudulent advertisement has nothing to do with the Institute of Industrial Research and the staff of our institute.
2. The Institute of Technology strongly condemns consumers' audio-visual behavior in the name of the Institute of Technology and the use of synthetic photos to confuse consumers' audio-visual activities, and has been held accountable in accordance with the law.
3. We hereby clarify to face the audio and feel free to be wary of false advertising to protect our own rights and interests.
