The bamboo cutting machinery designed by this technology is installed at the end of the forearm of the excavator and uses the excavator oil pressure system as the main power source.The operator can perform five functional operations: (1) The machine rotates.(2) Open and close the jaws. (3) The chain saw rotates at high speed.(4) The direction of rotation of the chain saw.(5) Sticking crossbar.These functions make the machine have efficient and flexible working capabilities to adapt to bamboo logging needs under different conditions.
Abstract
The bamboo falling machine designed by this technology is installed at the end of the excavator’s forearm and uses the excavator’s hydroulic system as the main power source. The operator can perform five functions: (1) Machine rotation. (2) Opening and closing of the jaws. (3) The chain saw rotates at high speed. (4) The direction of rotation of the chain saw blade. (5) Material blocking cross bar. These functions enable the machine to operate efficiently and flexible, adapting to the needs of bamboo harvesting under different conditions.
