Song Gongyuan Academician
Chairman Song Gongyuan is a pioneer in Taiwan's optoelectronics industry. In 1975, he founded Optoelectronics and was the first manufacturer in Taiwan to mass-produce optical disc machines, printers, computers, mobile phone keyboards and image sensing machines.In order to focus on integrating the group's resources, Chairman Song proposed the strategy of "pursuing the world's absolute first" and moved towards a century-old enterprise with a new trend. The group's top three products in the world account for 80% of the company's revenue, while laptop computer power supply, color contact image sensor, and mobile phone buttons are the world's number one.
