Hsinchu Entrepreneurship Cultivation Center
It is located in the Hsinchu Zhongxing Campus and the Guangfu Campus. It has a total of nearly 6,000 square meters of space for enterprises to apply for entry. It is close to Hsinchu Science Park and two well-known universities, Tsinghua and Yangming Jiaotong University.With thousands of research and professional talents from the Institute of Technology Research, it is an ideal entrepreneurial location for the high-tech industry.
This college provides cultivation space and software and hardware facilities, and combines industrial networks, venture capital funds, universities and government resources to build an innovation and entrepreneurship ecosystem, and becomes the best entrepreneurial partner for new science and technology ventures.With the core energy accumulated over the years, it won the 17th National New Creation Award-1st New Creation Award in 2020 and has won many domestic/international entrepreneurship institutions certifications.The new education and tutoring experience will also be reproduced and spread, and the entrusted operation of t.Hub Internal Medicine Innovation and Cultivation Base (Taipei Municipal Government BOT Case) and other fields.
Service Description
If a startup company or a company's new business department has been established within 5 years, the paid-in capital amount is less than NT$80 million or the regular employees are less than 200 employees, you can apply for entry and receive relevant entrepreneurial education tutoring and assistance.
Basic services: course lectures and training, industrial intelligence provision, government project information, technical cooperation recommendation, application and guidance for the creation of cabinets, marketing and media support, overseas chamber of commerce docking, accounting consulting services
Value-added services: international exhibitions, business model tutoring, financial planning, fundraising support
