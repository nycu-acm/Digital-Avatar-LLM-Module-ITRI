Technology introduction
PRS dust filter bag regeneration (cleaning) system.
The non-destructive waste filter bag regeneration technology is mainly through a waste dust collection filter bag regeneration system.With dry pressure-transforming cleaning technology, it can effectively remove >95% fly ash (dust), and can be directly returned to the needs of coal-fired power plants, cement and other gas-solid separation industries. It not only reduces the cost of purchasing new filter bags, but also avoids the use of traditional burial or incineration to treat waste filter bags. It is an environmentally friendly and reusable recycling technology.
The Industrial Research Institute has developed a cleaning and regeneration system for waste dust collection filter bags.Through the reciprocating operation of variable-pressure drying gas and power traction module, the waste dust collection filter bags are cleaned and recycled to reduce the large amount of carbon emissions or burial caused by incineration treatment.Taking the use of 10-meter-long dust collection filter bags in the dust collection system of coal-fired power plants as an example, a polymer with phenylene sulfide repeating structural units is a new functional engineering plastic with high thermal stability and can be used continuously in an environment exceeding 200℃, and has excellent chemical corrosion resistance.Therefore, through the waste dust collection filter bag cleaning regeneration system, the >95% fly ash (dust) contained in the waste dust collection filter bag can be removed and recycled filter bags that can be recycled back to the fueling power plant for use.
Features and innovation
The non-destructive waste dust collection filter bag regeneration technology is used to introduce variable pressure drying gas and power traction modules to complete the cleaning of the filter bag, and no waste will be generated during the cleaning process. In addition, the innovative design of power traction can remove fly ash (dust) contained in the filter bag and collect it to complete the regeneration filter bag.The basic characteristics of recycled filter bags are analyzed, including high breathability and their strength is comparable to that of new products.
Application and benefits
1. Reduce and add value of business (engineering plastic) waste.
2. Reduce carbon emissions at the process end.
