The Institute of Technology won the 2023 Edison Awards in the United States on the 21st.From the left, Wang Mingzhe, director of the North American Office of the Institute of Technology Research, Lu Ruimei, deputy director of the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials, Chen Baiwei, engineer of the Institute of Information and Communications of the Institute of Industrial Research, Liao Yanzhang, group leader of the Institute of Information and Communications of the Institute of Industrial Research, Zheng Shuzhen, deputy head of the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials of the Institute of Industrial Research, and Li Ruoping, special manager of the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials of the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials of the Institute of Engineering Research.
The Edison Awards, known as the "Oscar Award in Innovation", announced this year's winners. With the subsidy of the Ministry of Economic Affairs' Technical Department, the Institute of Technology has won awards for seven consecutive years. It has shined internationally with Dow, DuPont, 3M, LG and other major manufacturers. In the fierce competition of nearly 400 technologies/products around the world, it has won 1 gold, 1 silver and 1 copper; among them, the "O-RAN Energy-Saving Private Network Management Technology" of AI & 5G energy-saving is a software platform for intelligently managing energy-saving 5G base station system, and won the gold medal."Supramolecular Compound Technology Eye Drops" closely followed the gold medal Moderna Technology and won the silver medal, while the "Smart Radiofrequency Thermal Ablation System" won the bronze medal, once again allowing Taiwan's health care and smart technology to leap onto the international stage.
Frank, executive director of the Edison Award.Frank Bonafilia said: "Taiwan won eight Edison Awards, of which the Institute of Technology won three, which was very outstanding and amazing." He also pointed out: "The Institute of Technology is not afraid of failure and has bravely innovated, becoming the leader of various trends, fully demonstrating its essence and responsibilities. The Institute of Technology can always develop innovative technologies that can solve major world problems, support it at a national scale, and continuously innovate and develop to solve severe global challenges. Taking this award-winning technology as an example, the Institute of Technology uses non-invasive eye drops to replace eyeball acupuncture to treat macular lesions, opening up a new research and development direction in the medical ecosystem, leading innovation like never before."
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, said that in recent years, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has continued to focus on market-oriented research and development and create innovative technologies with market competitiveness through cross-field cooperation.This time, we are honored to have won three technical awards, confirming that our market-oriented R&D has been recognized.The three award-winning technologies include "O-RAN Energy-saving Private Network Management Technology", "Supramolecular Compound Technology Eye Drops", and "Smart Radio Frequency Thermal Ablation System" that have all been transferred and commercialized through different forms or applications. The manufacturers include Pegatron United Technology, Xinli Biotechnology, and Renbao Computer, with excellent industrialization benefits.With the development of science and technology, the Institute of Technology will continue to maintain innovation momentum and take into account the link between R&D and market, assist Taiwan's industry in building R&D strength, and strive for international business opportunities in transformation and upgrading.
Qiu Qiuhui, Director of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, said that Taiwan’s scientific and technological R&D strength has long been deeply recognized by internationally, and technology R&D is closely closely related to global industrial trends. The Ministry of Economic Affairs has long supported legal persons in research and development forward-looking innovative technologies with scientific and technological projects, and has won many international awards, indicating that my country’s R&D technology energy continues to be recognized by internationally.We appreciate that the Industrial Research Institute won three technologies this year, once again demonstrating Taiwan’s forward-looking innovation and research and development energy in the fields of AI artificial intelligence, 5G information communication technology, and biomedical medicine on the international stage.
The "O-RAN Energy-Saving Private Network Network Management Technology" that won the Edison Gold Medal this time is to create a software platform for intelligently managing energy-saving 5G base station system. It uses smart algorithms to redirect the terminal device to a specific base station, allowing idle base stations to enter a dormant state, reducing power consumption and saving costs.Through smart module technology, this system can be quickly optimized according to different private network needs, making the deployment of private networks as fast and simple as installing an APP. It can be quickly applied to 5G private networks such as smart factories, smart hospitals, smart entertainment, smart warehousing and logistics, and drones.This technology has not only assisted many Netcom equipment manufacturers in value-added and product upgrades, but also hopes to continue to use intelligent power-enabled technology to improve the energy efficiency of communication products in the future, and can quickly replicate and strengthen the prosperity of my country's private network applications, driving the digital transformation and vigorous development of Taiwan's industry.
The "Supramolecular Complex Technology Eye Drops" that won the silver medal this time is made of a special fundus transmission technology, using bisexual molecular carriers, with extremely hydrophilic surfaces and extremely small sizes. It can quickly pass through layers of eye tissue disorders such as the eye surface and corneal layer to directly reach the fundus lesions for treatment, effectively reducing the area of ​​vascular leakage. For ophthalmic diseases such as wet macular lesions, the patients will not need to go to the hospital for injections in the future, and can be treated at home. This technology has completed the technology transformation and entered the second phase of clinical acceptance.As for the "Smart Radiofrequency Thermal Ablation System" that won the bronze medal, it combines ultrasonic imaging guidance and guides a single adjustable ablation electrode needle to the lesion for local treatment through minimally invasive surgery. Not only does the wound be small and the recovery period is short, it is also the first advanced medical system in the world to integrate minimally invasive surgery, ultrasonic imaging and algorithms. It has completed its technical transformation and has obtained a Taiwan marketing license.
With the "O-RAN Energy-Saving Private Network Management Technology", the Institute of Industrial Research and Technology is a software platform for intelligently managing energy-saving 5G base station system. It won the Edison Gold Medal and used smart algorithms to redirect the terminal device to a specific base station, allowing idle base stations to enter a dormant state, reducing power consumption and cost savings, and can also optimize and deploy according to different private network needs through smart module technology.
The Industrial Research Institute has been following Moderna, which won the gold medal in Edison Awards with "supramolecular composite technology eye drops", and won the silver medal. This technology is an accurate eye-dose transmission technology that allows eye drops to directly reach the lesions in the fundus for treatment. It has been used to treat wet macular lesions and convert technology into reliable biotechnology.
The "Smart Radiofrequency Thermal Ablation System" of the Institute of Technology Research, which won the bronze medal in the Edison Award, combines ultrasonic imaging guidance and guides a single adjustable ablation electrode needle to the lesion for local treatment through minimally invasive surgery. It is the world's first advanced medical system that integrates minimally invasive surgery, ultrasonic imaging and algorithms. It has been converted to Renbao computer and has obtained a marketing license in Taiwan.
