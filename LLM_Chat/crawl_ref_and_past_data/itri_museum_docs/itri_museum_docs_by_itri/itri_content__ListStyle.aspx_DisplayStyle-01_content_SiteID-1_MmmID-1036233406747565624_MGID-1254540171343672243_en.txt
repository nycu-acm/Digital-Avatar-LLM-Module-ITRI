Wu Zhengzhong, Chairman of the Institute of Technology Research (third from the left in the front row), led a delegation to visit Fraunhofer IZM and had in-depth exchanges with representatives of the German Microelectronics Research and Manufacturing Platform (Research Fab Microelectronics Germany; FMD).
Wu Zhengzhong, Chairman of the Institute of Technology Research, led a delegation to visit the Fraunhofer Institute for Reliability and Microintegration IZM, the largest applied scientific research institution in Europe, and the German Microelectronics Research and Manufacturing Platform (Research Fab Microelectronics Germany; FMD) representatives for in-depth exchanges at IZM. The meeting focused on the R&D strategies and future cooperation directions of both parties, and hoped to continue to promote innovation in the global semiconductor and microelectronics technology field and achieve breakthrough progress.
The Institute of Technology and Fraunhofer IZM play an important role in promoting the development of semiconductor technology in Germany and Taiwan, and contributed a lot to the progress of global semiconductor technology.In the context of fierce global technological competition, it is crucial to cooperate with like-minded countries to enhance their technological strength.The partnership between ICITIC and Fraunhofer IZM is a successful example of international cooperation.It not only focuses on promoting innovation and improving social benefits, but also emphasizes the importance of sustainability and actively promotes the integration of science and technology and art.In the future, the two sides will jointly explore emerging fields such as AI artificial intelligence, advanced processes, silicon photons, etc., and combine my country's global leading advantages in semiconductor chip manufacturing and packaging and testing, as well as key technologies such as generative AI and green communication technology, and are committed to developing innovative applications, and to advance the layout of Taiwan's future industries and accelerate innovation breakthroughs.
