Technology introduction
Environmental control production system technology development.
The Institute of Technology has integrated core technologies such as optoelectronics, precision environmental control, materials, biotechnology and automation, and cooperated with agricultural industry, academia and research institutions to develop the optimal light source system, advanced three-dimensional cultivation facilities, pathogen detection systems, energy-saving control and automatic monitoring systems to create a smart greenhouse system suitable for subtropical climates.
Features and innovation
LED light source and environmental control system are applied to hydroponic cultivation technology
This technology provides the best customized demand design for the on-site environment, cultivation systems, environmental control facilities and plant light source characteristics of greenhouses or closed buildings. It can reduce the impact of plant growth and harvest in external climate. It does not have any differences in cultivation areas or seasons, but can produce various fruits and vegetables every year, and save more than 90% of water compared with traditional soil farming methods.In addition, LED artificial light sources provide plant light, regulate the optimal spectrum and light intensity, and have the benefits of power saving and high productivity.
Coral artificial environmental control cultivation technology
This technology introduces the environmental control cultivation technology and system construction of artificial coral breeding to regulate the optimal environmental control breeding parameters of corals: light source (spectrum, light intensity), water quality (water flow, water temperature, salinity) and nutrients.Through bioextraction technology, coral raw materials can be applied to high-value-added industries such as biotechnology, beauty, and medical materials, thereby enhancing the economic output value of related industries.
