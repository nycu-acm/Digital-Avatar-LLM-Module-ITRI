The industrial process leap forward from daily life
Written by/Li Xingyi Photography by/Huang Dingxiang
Can you imagine the low-frequency microwaves of home microwave ovens can also be applied to semiconductor processes?The microwave annealing technology, which has gathered the forces of Jiaotong University, the Institute of Technology and the National Nano Components Laboratory, has successfully overcome many technical problems, implemented the theory in component process equipment, and further demonstrated its energy-saving and cost advantages. It was shortlisted for the 2017 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards.
The material is produced and manufactured in the process of annealing, the material is heated to a specific temperature, maintained for a period of time, and then cooled at an appropriate speed, with the purpose of improving the microstructure of the material and regulating the chemical composition.Taking the semiconductor front-stage process as an example, the function of annealing is to restore the single crystal structure of the crystal through heating and activate the dopants in it. However, the temperature and speed of annealing are a big deal.With the evolution of semiconductor technology, annealing technology has also gone from traditional furnace tubes to current rapid thermal annealing (Rapid Thermal Annealing (RTA). Microwave annealing technology, which has been used in the carbon fiber field for many years, is becoming more and more attention, and it is even expected to expand its application scope to the optoelectronics industry.
The microwave annealing technology jointly developed by Jiaotong University, Institute of Technology and the National Nano Component Laboratory has overcome difficulties and implemented the theory in industrial applications, which is a successful example of cross-field cooperation.
Annealing technology that is more than temperature and speed
The semiconductor front-stage process aims at 100% yield. If the wafer yields in the first step, there will be problems with the stacking afterwards. However, with the development of the process, semiconductors have increasingly strict requirements on annealing temperature.Especially after the second five-nanometer generation, traditional silicon channels will have the opportunity to be replaced by high-mobility materials such as silicon germanium, pure germanium, and three-five clans.Moreover, negative capacitance modules are also the direction in which Moore's Law can be continued in the future.These advanced modules require low process temperatures.
Traditional furnace tube annealing can process multiple wafers at a time, but the temperature is high and the working time is long, so it is not suitable for increasingly precise and small components, which may cause circuit failure or leakage; the RTA has a low temperature and short working time, but it can only process one wafer at a time.In comparison, the microwave annealing temperature is lower than RTA, and multiple wafers can be processed in batches at the same time, making it a more ideal solution. As long as the existing annealing equipment is replaced, the original front, back, upstream and downstream processes do not need to be changed.
Huang Kunping, a senior researcher at the Institute of Mechanical and Electromechanical Systems of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the method of RTA is a traditional thermal method, which instantly heats to the wafer surface at high temperatures, and then heats the dopant substances that diffuse inside. The dopant substances can only be activated when they receive heat, and then repair the silicon lattice.Microwave annealing uses microwaves to directly penetrate the silicon lattice, directly vibrating and heating the silicon atom lattice to facilitate its energy absorption, thus eliminating the effect of high-temperature heat conduction to diffuse dopant substances and achieving the goal of low-temperature annealing.
The gorgeous transformation of low-frequency microwaves for household use
The cooperative research of Jiaotong University and the Institute of Technology has been underway in microwave annealing technology for more than three years.At the beginning, Jiaotong University needed 5.8GHz industrial-specification microwave annealing equipment due to research, but the high-frequency microwave 5.8GHz magnetron technology has not yet entered the mature stage, and the component life is not long. It takes about 500 hours to replace a microwave tube, which is about NT$200,000, and the funding burden is extremely heavy.
Zhao Tiansheng, a professor in the Department of Electronic Physics at National Jiaotong University, said that the Jiaotong University makes components and the Institute of Engineering and Research makes equipment. The two sides hit it off, and the National Nano Components Laboratory provides a clean room for semiconductor process implementation.Long before the cooperation, Jiaotong University had published several papers on microwave annealing technology. If it can be successfully promoted to commercialization, whether it is the current 12-inch or the future 18-inch wafer, it can save a lot of component process time.
After cooperating with the Industrial Research Institute, the research direction was changed to 2.45GHz, which is the frequency of ordinary household microwave ovens. Because the industrial-grade 2.45GHz magnetron cost only NT$10,000, the average life of the component is 3,000 hours, which has an absolute cost advantage over 5.8GHz.In addition, measurements found that the vibration absorption frequency of silicon crystal atoms is 2.7GHz. If doping considerations of other materials are included, the absorption frequency is about 3.5GHz to 4.0GHz. Both data are closest to the microwave frequency of 2.45GHz.
Since the microwave mode is proportional to the frequency square, 2.45GHz is obviously at a disadvantage compared to 5.8GHz.In this regard, the Industrial Research Institute has achieved two major technological breakthroughs. First, it is to increase the capacitance of the capacitor in the half-wave rectifier of the microwave front end, and to change the geometric direction of the waveguide tube to double the modal number, so the uniformity performance is even better than 5.8GHz. If the annealing time of 5.8GHz takes 300 seconds, 2.45GHz can achieve the same effect in just 100 seconds; another breakthrough is the improvement of the lining material, which improves the annealing uniformity.Overall, the uniformity of a single wafer has increased from 95% to 99.5%, which is better than the 99% required by domestic semiconductor manufacturers.
Expand the scope of application to the fields of carbon fiber and optoelectronics
In addition to semiconductors, the Institute of Technology and Jiaotong University has also conducted many research on the application of microwave annealing in other materials.At present, the most mature field of industrial application of microwave annealing is carbon fiber. Because the carbon absorption coefficient of microwaves is very high, it was first cited by a major Japanese carbon fiber manufacturer about six or seven years ago to replace the original RTA, greatly reducing energy consumption and improving production efficiency. Another advantage is to improve the raw material grade through the microwave annealing procedure and double the added value of the product.
Next, the main application directions of the Institute of Technology and Jiaotong University are optoelectronic industries such as silicon films and LEDs. Materials that are also covalent bonds are very suitable for the development of microwave annealing-related applications. For the optoelectronic industry with higher energy consumption demand, it not only reduces costs, but also greatly shortens the energy-saving effect of carbon footprint, which is in line with the trend of green and environmental protection.
Huang Kunping said that the microwave annealing technology, currently dominated by American manufacturers in the market, mainly uses variable frequency microwave or 5.8GHz. Both use high-frequency microwaves to solve the problem of uniformity. However, its control export technology and cost are too high, and domestic manufacturers find it difficult to obtain relevant technologies. Jiaotong University, Institute of Technology and the National Nano Component Laboratory work together to make up for the shortcomings of low frequency with innovative ideas to achieve an effect comparable to 5.8GHz.The technology has been transferred to domestic manufacturers, and more industrial application results can be seen in the near future.
