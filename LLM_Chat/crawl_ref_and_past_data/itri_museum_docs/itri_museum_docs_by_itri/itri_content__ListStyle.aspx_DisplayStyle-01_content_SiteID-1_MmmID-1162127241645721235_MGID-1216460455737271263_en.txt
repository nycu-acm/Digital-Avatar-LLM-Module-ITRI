Technology introduction
5G energy-saving O-RAN.
5G O-RAN private network energy-saving management technology This system provides a comprehensive energy-saving solution for 5G O-RAN private network in network planning and operation stages.
Application scope
This system achieves energy saving and efficiency through network planning and intelligent control.
Provide intelligent deployment suggestions for field base platforms to reduce manual deployment and improve network efficiency, and can reduce the number of base platforms to save network energy consumption while meeting application needs.
Using network tidal energy-saving control, smart energy-saving algorithms are used to cooperate with network traffic prediction and redirection, without reducing service quality, maximize the dormant base station entry and reduce power consumption, and combine the regulation of green energy power generation to save the operating costs of 5G private network.
Carbon reduction benefits
The verification results of the dedicated network planning in the fields of Pegatron Factory, Xiuchuan Hospital, Nanchuang, and Shalun Application Private Network can save more than 30%.
During the operation stage, dynamic tidal energy saving control and green energy power generation regulation are used to verify the Nanchuang field of Far Transmission cooperation and the Shalun field of China Telecom cooperation, which can save more than 30% of energy.
