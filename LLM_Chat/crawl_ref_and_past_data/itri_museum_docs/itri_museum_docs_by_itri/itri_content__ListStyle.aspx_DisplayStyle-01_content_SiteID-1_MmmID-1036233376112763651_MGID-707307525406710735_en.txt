Technology introduction
Long-distance floating multi-screen head-up display
With the advent of the Internet of Vehicles, driving will receive more personalized information promoted in the cloud. ITRI uses two core technologies: laser micro-projection and long-distance floating and floating modules to develop laser HUD modules, which can project a 26-inch full-color long-distance floating image 2m ahead of the driving line of sight, allowing driving to see three independent HUD images without moving its line of sight. It can also reduce the risk of fatigue when driving when looking far (car condition) and close (daster board information), and the risk of distraction from looking down at driving information, making driving safer.
Features and innovation
Innovative three-picture spectroscopy technology.
Far-range floating imaging optical module.
Ultra-micro laser projector.
High brightness optical diffusion sheet.
Intelligent image multiple-work compensation technology.
Technical Specifications
Virtual image size: 26 inches @ 2m.
HUD module volume: 300cm³.
Brightness: 13000 nits.
Maximum image resolution: 720P.
24 bits Full color.
Application and benefits
Car head-up display, or mobile devices such as smart phones and tablets.
