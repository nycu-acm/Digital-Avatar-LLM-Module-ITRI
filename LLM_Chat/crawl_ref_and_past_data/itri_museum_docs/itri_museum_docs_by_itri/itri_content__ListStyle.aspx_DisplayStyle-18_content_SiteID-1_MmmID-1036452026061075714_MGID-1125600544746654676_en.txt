The Institute of Technology won the top 100 innovation institutions award in the world for five times
Organize/Editor Department
The Institute of Technology won the "2021 Global Top 100 Innovation Institutions Award" for the fifth time. In terms of the number of awards, it is the first among Asian research institutions and the largest number of institutions in Taiwan.
The Industrial Research Institute has once again won the "Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award"!Since 2017, the Industrial Research Institute has been on the "Global Top 100 Innovation Institution Awards".It is not only as famous as international manufacturers such as Apple and Microsoft, but also shows its strength in core technology research and development and patent layout strategies.
In order to accelerate Taiwan's industrial innovation and upgrading, the Ministry of Economic Affairs continues to use science and technology project plans to encourage legal person research institutions to develop key technologies that meet market needs, and actively promote and implement global patent layout and intelligent finance combination business strategies, with dual track parallelism and excellent results.
Recently, the international research institution Clarivate released the "2021 Global Top 100 Innovation Institutions Report". With the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology won the "Global Top 100 Innovation Institutions Award" for the fifth time in a row. It is as famous as internationally renowned companies such as Apple, Microsoft, Intel, Qualcomm, Samsung, as well as R&D institutions Fraunhofer, University of California, etc., becoming the most award-winning research institution in Asia, and the number of awards ranked first among Taiwan institutions.
Clarivate has long used patent data analysis to evaluate institutions' innovation energy, market layout strategies, and the influence of R&D technology. This year it has entered its tenth year and is of unique significance in the field of global R&D innovation.Fan Yongyin, general manager of Clarivate Taiwan, said: "The research and development quality of the Institute of Technology and its influence on industrial innovation has always been one of the top 100 first-class teams in the world. In recent years, it has begun to show results in actively strengthening international layout. In today's rapidly changing world, top innovation institutions are more important social resources."
Focus on quality but not quantity, patent layout is effective
Patents are also an important weapon for new startups to base themselves on the market. They are well aware of the strategic significance of smart property to national development. The Ministry of Economic Affairs spares no effort to assist legal entities and industries, formulate internationally competitive R&D directions, and actively invest resources to promote patent layout, and build a patent shield for the industry to enter the international market. Taking the Institute of Industrial Research as an example, about 80% of patents are R&D results supported by the Ministry of Economic Affairs' science and technology projects.
Between 2017 and 2019, a total of 4,638 patents were accumulated in the legal person technology project and up to 3,552 authorized enterprise patent applications, showing the outstanding achievements of the Ministry of Economic Affairs in promoting legal persons to create the value of intelligent property rights.In the future, the Ministry of Economic Affairs will continue to use the R&D ecosystem thinking model to formulate smart property management strategies that are in line with the international level, and use the strategic thinking of "focusing on quality but not chasing quantity", promote the patent layout mechanism before R&D, and build patents that are close to market demand. It will also continue to assist the industry in strengthening patent quality, creating key core patent portfolios and optimizing the integrated application of R&D technologies. At the same time, it encourages systematic and diversified innovative practices, and uses intelligent finance to help new startups improve their competitiveness and seize global business opportunities.
Scientific research patents are promoted and the best pillars of Taiwan factories
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research, pointed out that this year, the Institute of Industrial Research has once again won the recognition of the Top 100 Innovation Institutions Award in the World, which not only shows that the Institute of Industrial Research has successfully focused on cross-field forward-looking research and development, output and applied diversified high-value patents, but also shows that the Institute of Industrial Research has reached the world's first-class level of intelligence and finance, and has global influence in the breadth and depth of cross-domain research and release.
As of the end of 2020, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has accumulated 17,022 validly certified patents, of which the proportion of invention patents reached 98%, and the number of foreign patent applications is nearly twice that of domestic applications. In the future, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research will continue to take cross-field integration and meeting industrial needs as its R&D goal, develop and implement the "explore creativity and accurately layout high-value patents", focus on core technologies with international competitiveness and future market demand, and actively deploy patents in important countries around the world; and through the research and development of a large number of high-quality patents with rich R&D output, and an excellent talent pool with rich practical experience in supporting manufacturers, it has become the best backing for Taiwan's industry to enter the international market and transform and upgrade.
In addition to continuously promoting and implementing the patent analysis layout mechanism, the Institute of Technology Research has also taken assisting industrial innovation and transformation and upgrading as the main axis. Under the "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" of the Institute of Technology Research, it will develop three application areas: "smart life", "healthy and happy life" and "sustainable environment", as well as "smart enabling technology" that supports the development of the three application areas, further assisting domestic enterprises in improving competitiveness and creating value.
Deeply aware of the importance of the link between the capital market and the technology market, the Institute of Technology has joined hands with the Small and Medium Enterprises Credit Guarantee Fund and 26 key financial banks in Taiwan to launch the "Technology Added Value Financing Guarantee Project", hoping to connect the strong funds of the Taiwan capital market with potential scientific research results, transforming from manufacturing priority to wisdom priority, creating smart value will bring the greatest benefits to Taiwan's economy and industry.
The award shows that the Intellectual Finance Management of the Institute of Industrial Research has reached the world's first-class level, and has global influence in the breadth and depth of the cross-domain research and release.
