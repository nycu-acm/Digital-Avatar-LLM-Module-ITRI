Taiwan's communications research and development energy has attracted much attention. AI artificial intelligence technology of the Institute of Technology won two WCIT global communications technology application outstanding contribution awards

Date: 2020/11/20
The Advanced AI Light Influenza System of the Institute of Technology has won the Global Outstanding Contribution Award for Information and Communication Technology Application. This system is used in urban epidemic prevention and can provide regional flow monitoring services, and provides more efficient services through technological applications. In the future, it can promote the application of activity data statistics, interactive services and augmented reality.
The Institute of Technology has won international awards again!The 24th World Congress on Information Technology (WCIT) awarded the Global ICT Excellence Award, known as the "Oscar in the Information Communications Industry". With the support of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics and the recommendation of the China Soft Association, the Institute of Technology developed the "Advanced Artificial Intelligent Light Detection and Ranging" and the "Next-Generation AI-Powered Robo-Advisor" technology; NeGAP) technology won the "Outstanding Contribution Award" and the "Outstanding Digital Innovation Award" respectively, which has once again attracted international attention from Taiwan's communications research and development energy.
Lin Zengyao, CEO of the Measuring Technology Development Center of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the Institute of Industrial Research Institute is an international R&D institution that provides solutions to industries and society through innovative R&D. As the epidemic spreads, the Institute of Industrial Research Institute uses AI artificial intelligence technology to advance deployment and assist in epidemic prevention.It has formed a scientific and technological epidemic prevention team with the Keelung Municipal Government and the US XR/AI startup AEMASS to build Taiwan’s first advanced AI light-taught influencing system at the Keelung Miaokou Night Market. As a smart city and epidemic prevention city, the Keelung Municipal Government can effectively control the crowds entering and leaving the night market, analyze the crowds and effectively grasp the crowds stagnant in the night market area, and even analyze the flow of people to maintain a safe social distance.
Feng Wensheng, CEO of Juliang Information Technology Center of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the award-winning "Next-generation AI-driven smart financial management technology" is not only the result of the implementation of AI artificial intelligence, but will also bring innovative thinking to investment.The Institute of Technology uses AI artificial intelligence deep learning technology to analyze huge amounts of heterogeneous financial information such as transaction data, economic indicators, policy reports, etc., to predict market directions with multiple models, and generate a unique "robot market view", helping investors find future investment markets and products in the face of the changing capital market, and combine investment theory to allocate assets and dynamically adjust investment portfolios to reduce investment risks.Not only petty bourgeoisie can manage finances, but also through transparent information of financial management robots, the drawbacks of human operations can also be avoided.
In addition to the application of advanced AI light-driven influenza detection system in urban epidemic prevention, it can provide regional flow monitoring services, but also promote new policies to provide more efficient services through technological applications. In the future, it can promote the application of activity data statistics, interactive services and augmented reality.This technology also allowed the Keelung Municipal Government to win the first prize of the ICT Excellence Award (Global Information Communication Technology Application Outstanding Contribution Award), which is the city government's international recognition after the "2019 ASOCIO Smart City Award".Through innovative applications of technology, we provide more convenient services for Keelung citizens and assist Keelung in building a smart new port capital.
Compared with the existing financial robots on the market, the "Next Generation AI-driven Smart Financial Management Technology" features an AI-deep enhanced learning algorithm that integrates the robot market viewpoints, which can automatically recommend increasing, deducting, and redeeming units, and can support the regular irregular operation applications of a single fund/fund investment group.Investors can provide the lowest risk portfolio or the best portfolio as long as they set the "expected rate of return" or "expected rate of interest rate"; when the robot expects market trends or fund performance changes, they can dynamically adjust their asset allocation.Among the 33 stock and bond markets around the world, using this technology to screen the investment market and then allocate assets, the return rate will have more than 80% chance of winning direct allocation, and the annualized remuneration will also improve by about 25%.
The Institute of Technology has abundant energy for communication technology, among which AI artificial intelligence will be the key to the future development of "smart cities" and "smart commerce". In this regard, the Institute of Technology has drawn up a 2030 technical strategy and blueprint, invested in the innovative research and development of AI artificial intelligence technology "common technology of intelligence", promote more application possibilities, realize AI artificial intelligence to be implemented in personal life, and make life smarter through the assistance of technology.
