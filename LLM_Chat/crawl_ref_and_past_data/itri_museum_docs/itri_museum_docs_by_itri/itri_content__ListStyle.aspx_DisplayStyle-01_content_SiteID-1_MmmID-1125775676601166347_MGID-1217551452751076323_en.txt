Clarification Note
Remind the public to verify investment information carefully and avoid misleading false advertising

Date: 2023/07/06
Recently, there are investment information books and magazines, which falsely record that "The Institute of Technology is the legal person shareholder of a certain Silicon Carbide Company". This is false information. This court hereby clarifies that it has not invested in Silicon Carbide Company and reminds: For any investment information, it is recommended that the public be cautious in verifying it to avoid being deceived due to false advertisements or false information.
