Technology introduction
5G dedicated network intelligent layout platform.
Global 5G vertical applications are booming, and the demand for 5G private networks has increased sharply.The process of dedicated network layout is cumbersome.If there is no complete plan during the layout stage, it will greatly affect the network optimization effect during subsequent operation maintenance. Therefore, a complete set of planning technologies is urgently needed in the network layout stage to ensure its network elasticity and easy maintenance operation. Especially in the high-frequency band of 5G, the attenuation characteristics are obvious. In an application field, multiple signal transmission sources are often required to serve at the same time. Therefore, the quality of the network layout planning will greatly affect the quality of the network, and the lack of planning will cause greater challenges in operation maintenance.The Institute of Technology Research and Research independently developed a 5G private network intelligent layout platform (Athena Planner), which is a technical platform designed for 5G private networks that supports 5G signal distribution computing and layout planning recommendation functions. It can customize network quality target needs according to specific application scenarios, intuitive and fast response to the signal distribution status of the field. The most significant benefit is that it can greatly reduce the layout cost under the goal of maintaining the field signal distribution Service-Level Agreement (SLA).This platform provides developers with the lowest layout cost and can meet application service goals and reliable and flexible planning suggestions, effectively saving the cost of dedicated network deployment.
Athena Planner replaces traditional unilateral deployment decisions that rely on network planning engineer experience and provides visual signal analysis. It can plan the location, quantity and parameters of the field base station according to needs. Users only need to provide the deployable location and planning targets of the signal transmission source. Athena Planner can provide layout planning suggestions (site suggestions) that plan at the lowest cost and meet the planning targets.
Features and innovation
The 5G private network intelligent layout platform is designed to achieve a high degree of simulation and intuitive visibility to cope with various application fields.In the application field of 5G private network, parameters such as the number, location, and transmission power of the transmission source are very important. The impact of line of sight (LOS) and non-line-of-sight (NLOS) will lead to the generation of signal blind spots. How to effectively and accurately determine the propagation attenuation model and create planning results that meet the field goals as much as possible according to the base station (CU/DU/RU) specifications is the key technology of this 5G private network planning software platform.
Application and benefits
In the future, with the spread of 5G applications, it is expected that this platform will be widely used in various 5G dedicated network field layout planning, reduce the cost of layout and improve maintenance operation, making 5G applications easier to spread, and form a positive cycle to create more 5G application possibilities, such as smart factories, smart hospitals, smart entertainment, smart warehousing and logistics, smart agriculture and smart cities.
