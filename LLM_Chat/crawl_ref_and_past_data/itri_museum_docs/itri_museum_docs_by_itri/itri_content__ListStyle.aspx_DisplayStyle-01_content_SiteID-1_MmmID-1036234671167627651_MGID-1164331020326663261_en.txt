Yin Yanliang Academician
Academician Yin Yanliang is the president of Runtai Group and chief engineer and research and development leader of the construction business group. Upholding the spirit of Runtai Group's support for the people's livelihood of Runtai Group, he leads the diversified development of Runtai Group. It includes textiles, construction, circulation sales, financial insurance, biotechnology and medical care, environmental protection and international trade, and is a model for the transformation of traditional industries into diversified groups.Academician Yin is not only an outstanding entrepreneur, but also a concurrent professor in the Department of Civil Engineering of Taiwan University and a lecture on outstanding entrepreneurs in the School of Engineering, engineering construction expert, philanthropist, etc. He has made outstanding contributions to academic, enterprise and society and has a profound impact.
Academician Yin graduated from the History Department of China University of Culture and later obtained a master's degree in the Institute of Business Studies at National Taiwan University and a doctorate in corporate management at National University of Political Science.We have long studied practical application of construction projects, developed efficient construction integration technology, and are committed to the research and development and application of pre-filling methods and earthquake-resistant engineering technology. We have obtained more than 650 related patents in 19 countries and led the R&D team, combining construction technology, information and engineering management, and innovatively applied to the construction of Taiwan's buildings and high-tech factories, and are a pioneer in driving the precision and construction automation of Taiwan's construction projects.His rich achievements in scientific and technological research and development have been widely recognized internationally, and Academician Yin was awarded the Industrial Progress Research Award of Academician of the National Academy of Inventors and the American Institute of Civil Engineers.
In addition, Academician Yin has long invested in causes that are beneficial to the earth and human life, such as investing in the biotechnology pharmaceutical industry and setting up hospitals; investing in medical waste treatment and industrial waste to accelerate the promotion of the circular economy; investing in electric vehicles and developing green building materials and disaster prevention smart green buildings to reduce carbon emissions.Through forward-looking layout, more talents will be allowed to invest together and improve human life and the earth's environment with practical actions.Academician Yin also promised to donate 95% of the property in 2011, of which 3 billion yuan was used to establish the "Tang Prize" known as the Oriental Nobel Prize, commending the sustainable development, biotechnology, medicine, sinology, and rule of law in the face of challenges in the 21st century, to encourage the development of more innovative development and contributions that are beneficial to mankind and the earth, and to expand public welfare actions to the world and benefit society and people.
