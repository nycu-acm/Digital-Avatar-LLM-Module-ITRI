Ministry of Economics leads innovation achievements shine internationally. The Institute of Industrial Research won the top 100 innovation institutions award for its patent uniqueness 6 degrees
Share to Facebook

Date: 2022/02/24
Under the active investment and leadership of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research has won the "Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award" for the 6th for five consecutive years. It is the research institution with the most awards from the Asia-Pacific region, and the number of awards is also the first among Taiwan institutions.
The Ministry of Economics has been recognized internationally for its excellent intelligent finance operation performance in promoting forward-looking technology research and development and international layout of key patents!Clarivate today released the 2022 "Top 100 Global Innovative Institutions" report. Under the leadership of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research has won the sixth award for five consecutive years. It not only shows that the patents of the Institute of Industrial Research have performed well in the five major selection criteria: technological uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity, becoming the research institution with the most awards in the Asia-Pacific region. It also ranks first among Taiwan institutions. It is also as famous as internationally renowned institutions 3M, Intel, Qualcomm, Samsung, as well as the French Alternative Energy and Atomic Energy Commission (CEA), the French National Center for Scientific Research (CNRS), etc., showing that the Ministry of Economic Affairs spares no effort to bet on R&D resources, supports legal person R&D institutions to deepen cross-field forward-looking technology success, deepen global patent layout, implement all-round intelligent operation strategies, and ensure Taiwan's international competitiveness and its position as a major center for the global high-tech industry.
Technological innovation is the main driving force for driving industrial and economic growth.As the promoter of Taiwan's industrial innovation and transformation, in response to the post-epidemic era, the world attaches importance to the balance between sustainable environment and economic development, the demand for green supply chain transformation, 5G implementation and digital accelerated development, and actively uses science and technology projects and through legal person R&D institutions to assist the industry in improving patent intelligence power. With the strategic thinking of "focusing on quality but not chasing quantity", the legal person science and technology project has applied for a total of 1,299 domestic and foreign patents, and the number of foreign patents obtained accounts for nearly twice the number of domestic patents. Taking the Institute of Industrial Research as an example, about 80% of the patents are R&D results supported by the Ministry of Economic Affairs’ science and technology projects.Among the top 100 patent institutions in my country, the Institute of Industrial Research has firmly ranked among the highest number of patent inventions in research institutions, demonstrating the outstanding achievements of the Ministry of Economic Affairs in promoting legal persons to create the value of intelligent property rights.
Clarivate Security Ball’s top 100 innovative institutions report long-term evaluation of institutions’ innovation energy through patent data analysis, commending top institutions’ persistence in innovation and their excellent creativity.Ed White, Vice President of Intelligent Property and Innovation Research at Clarivian, said: "This year, only three research institutions in the world were selected on the list of top 100 innovation institutions. The INDU is one of them, and it has been selected for five consecutive years and a total of six times. The analysis shows its breadth of global layout and patent rights in many major economies, actively investing in global scientific and technological development, and creating excellent innovation results. We look forward to the INDU to continue to invest in high-quality innovation, so that the technological R&D results can exert greater economic effects and value."
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research, said that he has won the Top 100 Innovation Institution Award for the World for the 6th for five consecutive years, showing that the Intelligent Power Management Direction and Strategy of the Institute of Industrial Research.In terms of cross-field innovation research releases, this award report shows that the Industrial Research Institute has international influence.Overall, the patents of the Institute of Technology have three major characteristics: 1. Highly innovative, with the proportion of invention patents as high as 98%; 2. International layout, the number of foreign patent applications is nearly twice the number of domestic applications; 3. The quality of patents is high, and the number of citations is a common international standard for measuring patent quality. Compared with other international R&D institutions, such as the Fraunhofer, the Stanford Research Center (SRI), the total number of high citation patents in the past five years ranks first, indicating that the patents of the Institute of Technology have a high international influence in their fields.
Liu Wenxiong further pointed out that smart property rights are the key to Taiwan's technology industry's global competition.The Institute of Technology has deepened its layout from three aspects: "blue ocean strategy, capital market linking technology market, and patent banking"; the Institute of Technology focuses on blue ocean strategy, explores the real market demand, strives to develop technologies that can create value for the market, and redefines the future business model with new technologies. At the same time, the process of technology towards industrialization requires capital investment.The Institute of Technology actively promotes the link between the capital market and the technology market, links the credit guarantee fund for small and medium-sized enterprises and 27 important financial banks in Taiwan, and creates a "Technology Added Financing Guarantee Project". It has assisted nearly 50 new entrepreneurial and small and medium-sized enterprises to strengthen technology energy and apply for intangible asset financing, and the accumulated financing amount exceeds 100 million yuan. In the future, it will continue to assist in connecting the abundant capital water of Taiwan's capital market and the potential scientific research results, so that new entrepreneurial companies and small and medium-sized enterprises can use intangible assets to raise funds and start businesses, and enhance the value of innovation.In addition, the Institute of Technology Research has actively promoted the national patent bank (IP Bank) model to assist enterprises in properly responding to the increasingly frequent threats of international patent litigation. Based on patent resources, systems, databases, talents, etc., and combining Taiwan's production, academic, and research intelligence and financial energy, the Institute of Technology has established a national patent pool (Patent Pool), and has linked the 5G industry innovation and development alliance to build a domestic industry, academia, research 5G patent cooperation platform and expert consulting group to create a patent protection umbrella for Taiwanese enterprises, strengthen Taiwan's intellectual power energy, and enhance Taiwan's international industry negotiation status.
The Institute of Industrial Research has been deeply engaged in smart property for a long time, using creative full-life cycle thinking, considering the operation of smart property rights and patent development, and creating a sound patent ecosystem. While thinking about how to protect creativity, it also considers the commercialization and application model of patents, pays attention to patent layout analysis and strategy formulation during R&D planning, and tracks and implements patent applications to accurately layout key international markets, becoming an important backing for Taiwan's industry to enter the world.
The Institute of Technology won the "Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award" for 6 times, indicating that the Institute of Technology's patents performed well in five major selection criteria: technical uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity.
