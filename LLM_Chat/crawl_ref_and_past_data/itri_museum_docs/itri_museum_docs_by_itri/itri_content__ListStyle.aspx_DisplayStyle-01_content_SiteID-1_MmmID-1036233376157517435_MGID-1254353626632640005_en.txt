Technology introduction
Intelligent cognitive detection solutions.
Today's elderly society has increased the incidence of dementia, and cognitive decline affects individuals' living ability and increases the overall national medical care expenditure.The detection of cognitive function requires training professionals to perform and takes time.However, symptoms have occurred 10 to 15 years before the diagnosis of dementia, which is difficult for the public to detect, resulting in missed opportunities for early intervention.
Smart cognitive testing can provide long-term accumulated test results and objective information that cannot be obtained by traditional scales (such as correctness interpretation, answer content and reaction speed, etc.), which helps doctors confirm the situation more quickly, and can obtain continuous cognitive function test results, tracking and testing data to assist in early disease diagnosis.
Features and innovation
This technology has the dual functions of cognitive testing and empowerment. Intelligent cognitive testing and hospital-cooperated verification are highly correlated with traditional clinical testing scales.
Through AI, the analysis can be carried out accurately record the answer rate, response time and accuracy of the subject's test, making personal cognitive health easier to master.
Application and benefits
Create a new insurance operation model, cooperate with Cathay Life Insurance, and provide online smart cognitive testing and empowerment services for more than 1.05 million general users and specific policy customers. By June 2013, it had reached one million people.
