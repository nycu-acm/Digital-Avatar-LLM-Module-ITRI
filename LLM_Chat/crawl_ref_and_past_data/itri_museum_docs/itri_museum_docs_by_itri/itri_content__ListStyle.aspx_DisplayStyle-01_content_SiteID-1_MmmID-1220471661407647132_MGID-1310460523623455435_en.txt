Technology introduction
Multi-degree of freedom hose robot
Chronic Obstructive Pulmonary Disease (COPD) is the third largest cause of death in the world, but the existing treatment methods are insufficient. Inhalation drugs are limited in effect. Traditional percutaneous puncture surgery has the risk of bleeding and a long recovery period. It is urgently needed to have safer and more efficient alternatives.In order to break through the existing therapeutic restrictions, the Industrial Research Institute is committed to developing and innovative natural cavity robot surgical technology and developing a "multi-degree-of-freedom hose robot", which can penetrate more than 90% of the lung area and perform precise treatment without surgery, greatly reducing the risk of complications.
Features and innovation
The hose formed by this technology is only 2.9mm in diameter, with 210° omnidirectional swing ability, flexible adaptation to complex lung structures, and achieve "zero dead corner" treatment.At the same time, AI image-assisted guidance and preoperative planning can be integrated to help doctors accurately navigate to the lesions and achieve automatic in vivo navigation and cooperation between medical machines.
Application and benefits
Preclinical animal verification has confirmed its effectiveness, which not only provides better treatment options for COPD patients, reduces surgical risks and recovery periods, but also promotes the domestic medical robot industry to establish international competitiveness and has won the 2024 Top 100 Global Science and Technology R&D Awards.
