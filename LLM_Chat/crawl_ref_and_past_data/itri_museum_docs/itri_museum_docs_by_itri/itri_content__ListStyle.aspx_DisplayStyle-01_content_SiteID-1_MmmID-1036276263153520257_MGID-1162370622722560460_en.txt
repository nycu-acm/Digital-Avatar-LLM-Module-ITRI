Date: 2019/10/23
iRoadSafe Smart Road Safety Alert System, ITS World Conference Industry Achievement Award.
Taiwan’s smart transportation shines internationally!The largest annual event in the world's smart transportation industry - the 26th World Congress 2019, held an award ceremony in Singapore today (23rd).With the support of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economics, the Institute of Technology won the "Asia-Pacific Industry Awards" with the outstanding achievements of iRoadSafe's Internet of Vehicles Technology Connection Industry Cooperation, which not only defeated the host country this year's Singapore Technology Company (ST Engineering) and Genvict, the mainland China Technology Company (Genvict), and also praised the conference with the outstanding achievements in the construction of Taiwan's smart transportation system and the vision of the smart transportation island, and wrote a new milestone for Taiwan's smart transportation.
The Smart Transportation System World Congress is one of the world's largest transportation technology professional conferences. It attracts nearly 100,000 people from more than 100 countries, including government units, information and communications industries, and transportation industries, and more than 300 manufacturers to participate in the exhibition.It is a major event in the field of transportation technology. The conference also issued the Industry Awards, Hall of Fame, and Local Government Awards. It is an important award that countries in the Asia-Pacific, Europe and the Americas compete for each year. The Industry Achievement Awards mainly commend the implementation of the application of transportation technology industry. The winners in previous years include West Japan Railway Co., Ltd., Taiwan High-speed Railway, and Yuantong Power Supply.
When Li Shiguang, chairman of the Institute of Technology Research Institute, went on stage to receive the award, said that European and American countries have long actively implemented policies to drive the development of smart transportation, hoping that the Internet of Vehicles can integrate multiple applications such as information, communications, automotive electronics, digital content technology, etc. to create a smart city;the Ministry of Transport and the Ministry of Economic Affairs of Taiwan have also worked together to develop and formulate major transportation policies, and smart transportation is one of them. Applications such as self-driving cars and workshop communications in the Institute of Technology Research Institute have also gradually implemented, such as self-driving cars have been tried in Taichung and Hsinchu.The V2X (Vehicle-to-Everything) vehicle networking technology that won this award has also been industrialized and applied in eight counties and cities in Taiwan. In the future, Taiwan will continue to gather production, official and research energy to promote Taiwan's smart transportation industry internationally.
Que Zhike, director of the Information and Communications Research Institute of Industrial Research Institute, pointed out that the Internet of Vehicles mainly obtains road and driving information through positioning systems, and then uses V2X communication technology and the assistance of sensing devices to obtain road conditions information, so as to achieve safety warnings for driving and pedestrians.The focus is to provide drivers with advance warnings about possible dangerous situations, so that drivers can take early response measures to avoid traffic accidents.Taiwan is a powerful country in the field of communications. Through the combination of communications and transportation, it hopes to further prevent traffic accidents by analyzing traffic flow and building a smart city.
Que Zhike believes that the main reason why he was able to win from Singapore and mainland China this time was that the Asia-Pacific region's industrial achievement award is that the traffic environment in the Asia-Pacific region is generally complicated, and there are multiple transportation modes such as vehicles, motorcycles, bicycles, etc. on the road, which leads to traffic safety problems at mixed intersections at complex traffic signal intersections.With the support of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the smart road safety warning system (iRoadSafe V2X Safety Solution) developed by the Institute of Technology is a smart road safety solution for complex traffic environments in the Asia-Pacific region.
Que Zhike pointed out that the iRoadSafe intelligent road safety warning system integrates communication, sensors, traffic signs, facilities and other information, calculates the distance between people, vehicles and objects through anti-collision algorithms, and further predicts the trajectory. Through the roadside unit (RSU) communication and broadcast equipment, it sends a warning to all passers-by equipped with vehicle-mounted aircraft or electronic boards (CMS).
At present, this technology has been deployed at 26 accident-prone intersections in 8 counties and cities, including Keelung, Shuangbei, Hsinchu, Taichung, Nantou, Tainan and Kaohsiung.According to statistics, the traffic accident rate can be reduced by about 50% and the safety of intersections can be improved.In addition, this technology has also been transferred to domestic vehicle-based communication manufacturers, such as Dinghan International and Fuhua Electronics, hoping to assist manufacturers in moving forward to Southeast Asian countries and export Taiwan's domestic smart transportation system.
2019 ITS World Congress 10/21-10/25 was held at Singapore Suntec International Convention and Exhibition Centre.The theme of the conference is Smart Mobility, Empowering Cities. This year's World Conference invited international system integration operators, car manufacturers, automotive electronics, electronic map applications, 5G communications and smart transportation management agencies of various countries, and international manufacturers, and more than 10,000 representatives from more than 100 countries/regions in the world attended the conference.The conference activities included research and discussion forums, exhibitions, displays and technical visits.The Ministry of Transportation of Taiwan also led a delegation to participate in the grand event, presenting the achievements of Taiwan's ITS construction, striving for international business opportunities, and showing the vision of establishing Taiwan's smart transportation island.
The Institute of Technology was awarded the ITS World Conference Asia-Pacific Industrial Achievement Award in Singapore today (23rd), as famous as Panasonic North America and Connected Places Catapult in Europe.
The largest annual event in the world's smart transportation industry - the 26th World Congress 2019, was held at the Singapore Suntat City International Convention and Exhibition Center from the 21st to the 25th.From the left picture, Wang Muheng, director of the Department of the Ministry of Transportation, Zheng Shengqing, deputy director of the Information and Communications Research Institute of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, Li Shiguang, chairman of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, and Jian Renjizheng of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economics.
