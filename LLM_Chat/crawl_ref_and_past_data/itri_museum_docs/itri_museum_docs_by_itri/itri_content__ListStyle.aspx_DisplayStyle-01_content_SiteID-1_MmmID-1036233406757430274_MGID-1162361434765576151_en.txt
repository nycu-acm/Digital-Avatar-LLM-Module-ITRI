The Institute of Technology and Research and Research cooperated with Oxford Instruments in the UK to sign the Memorandum of Cooperation for the Joint Laboratory of Prospective Semiconductor Measuring Technology (MOU). The upper row in the picture starts from left: British representative of the Taiwan Office Deng Yuanhan, Vice President of the Institute of Technology Peng Yumin, and Christian, Director of Products of Oxford Instruments, UK.The lower row in the picture starts from left: Su Mengzong, director of the Institute of Industrial Technology International Strategy Development of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, Lin Zengyao, CEO of the Institute of Measuring Center of Industrial Research Institute, and Jonathan, Vice President of Business and Services of the Oxford Instrument Nano Research Group.
As semiconductor manufacturers rush into the 2nm process, it has become extremely challenging to measure the accuracy of key components such as transistor structure, size, and film thickness.The Ministry of Economic Affairs has established cutting-edge measurement needs derived from the industry in automotive electronics and new generation semiconductors, and jointly promoted the cooperation between the Institute of Technology and Research Institute and Oxford Instruments in the United Kingdom on October 28 to sign the Memorandum of Cooperation for the Joint Laboratory of Prospective Semiconductor Measuring Technology (MOU) to plan to integrate the joint research and development energy of the Institute of Technology and Research Institute and Oxford Instruments and lay out the next generation of semiconductor detection capabilities.
The Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs stated that Taiwan is an important global semiconductor hub, with a complete industrial chain, and is also leading the world in dinano process technology.According to Mordor Intelligence market survey statistics, the market size of semiconductor metrological testing equipment in 2020 was US$4.1647 billion, and is expected to reach US$5.3734 billion by 2026, with great development potential. This cooperation case is a milestone in advanced semiconductor measurement.Oxford Instruments is a world-renowned multinational group for instrument and equipment manufacturing, with advanced products and services all over the world.The Industrial Research Institute has the world's leading advanced semiconductor measurement technology. The cooperation between the two parties can help accelerate the application of forward-looking semiconductor technology in China and develop a more complete semiconductor ecosystem.
Deng Yuanhan, the British representative of the Taiwan Office, pointed out that semiconductors have a huge impact on global industrial development, and they play an important role in both automotive and electrical, 3C and other fields.The UK has several world-leading semiconductor companies such as Oxford, focusing on ultra-low power IC design to composite semiconductor design and production, providing high-tech equipment and services to the world's leading industrial manufacturing companies and scientific research groups.The Institute of Technology is an important driving force for promoting the development of Taiwan’s semiconductor industry. We look forward to the cooperation between Oxford Instruments and the Institute of Technology to create more innovation, collaboration and partnerships.
Peng Yumin, deputy director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that the Institute of Technology has abundant semiconductor and optoelectronics research and development energy and talents, and its structural surface spans the upper, middle and downstream industrial chains. It has now developed forward-looking technical testing capabilities for the micro-shrink to two- to three-nanometer chip processes.The Industrial Research Institute and Oxford Instruments first started with the cooperation in semiconductor optoelectronic components development and equipment technology in 2017. The two sides have a good understanding of cooperation. Once again, they will provide forward-looking measurement technology for advanced processes and establish a key tool for industrial mass production yield monitoring. We hope that international cooperation between Taiwan and the UK can accelerate the technological progress of both parties and industries and lead the semiconductor industry to a new development.
