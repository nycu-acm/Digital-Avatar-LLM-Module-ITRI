Microstructure lateral solar light collector turns windows into solar panels
Tao Xiaoman
This technology integrates light-guided solar light-collection panels (films) and trace solar cells to develop a roof-type solar system and a window-type building integrated solar energy (BIPV) system, which can save a lot of silicon crystal cell costs and maintain the transparency of the window itself.
"In the past, solar panels could only be placed tightly in place, which not only occupied space, but also did not generate electricity efficiency. Although later, a window-type solar system was developed, consumers complained that the windows turned brown and were in a bad mood after watching them for a long time." Lin Huixiong, manager of the Advanced Manufacturing Core Technology Group of the Institute of Machinery, pointed out that the Institute of Technology and Research has pioneered high-transparency and high-power generation microstructure diaphragm technology, which can be simply applied to transparent glass plates to generate electricity, saving a lot of the use of silicon crystal cell cells, and accelerating the implementation of the parity grid (Grid Parity).
This technology can be widely used in building windows, building materials, roofs or solar power plants, and has been mass-produced and developed with Huiguang Rising and Qiling Technology.In the future, in addition to green building roofs, they can also be used in plant factories, windows and skylights, waiting sheds or charging stations of mass transportation systems, allowing the integration of green power generation and smart power grid to ferment simultaneously.
