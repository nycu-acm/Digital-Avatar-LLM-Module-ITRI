Industrial Research Institute and Mitsubishi Electric Carbon Capture, Cross-border Empirical Launch
Compiled/Lai Wanjing
Net Zero Technology Enters Japan
The Institute of Technology and Mitsubishi Electric held the installation ceremony of the "Solid-state amine adsorbent carbon capture module" at the Hyogo Prefecture Institute of Technology, symbolizing that Taiwan and Japan jointly plan the Asian carbon neutral technology market.
With the global commitment to reaching net zero emissions in 2050, carbon capture has become one of the key technologies to achieve industrial carbon reduction. The Institute of Technology and Mitsubishi Electric held the "Solid-state amine adsorbent carbon capture module" installation ceremony at the Hyogo Prefecture, Japan, and immediately launched the evidence of recycling carbon dioxide from waste gas, symbolizing Taiwan and Japan working together to layout the Asian carbon neutral technology market.
Hu Zhusheng, deputy director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that in the face of global net zero emissions and climate change challenges, scientific and technological innovation is the key to solving the problem of sustainability.The Institute of Technology and Research is actively investing in the research and development of new technologies, including carbon capture, utilization and storage (CCUS), and is looking forward to promoting the implementation and value creation of technology through industrial cooperation. This is not only the first cross-sea verification of Taiwan's carbon capture technology, but also has the indicator significance of Taiwan and Japan working together to promote carbon reduction technology.
Aki Nishizawa, director of the Mitsubishi Electric Institute of Advanced Technology, said that Mitsubishi Electric attaches great importance to the potential of low-concentration carbon dioxide capture. This time, it introduced the carbon capture module developed by the Industrial Research Institute, and combined with Mitsubishi Electric's years of control technology and energy management technology accumulated in air conditioning systems and industrial systems, it accelerates the creation of a consistent carbon capture system that is recycled and reused.In the future, Mitsubishi Electric's "Energy & Facility (E&F)" solution will also be included in Mitsubishi Electric's "Energy & Facility (E&F)" solution, committed to reducing carbon emissions in factories and other places, and jointly achieving carbon neutrality.
The key technology of this cooperation, "solid amine adsorbent carbon capture module", is developed by the Institute of Technology and Research, and has the advantages of low-temperature desorption and energy saving. Like a "breathing sponge", it can capture carbon dioxide from thin gas, and then release it at a low temperature of about 80°C and recycle it.In addition, solid amine technology also has the characteristics of small equipment size, long equipment life and good thermal stability. It is not as energy-consuming as traditional liquid absorption methods. It is particularly suitable for small but dispersed carbon emission sources such as natural gas boilers, fuel cells, and semiconductors.It is more highly scalable and helps to establish a carbon management chain in Asia.
This cooperation will demonstrate the advantages of Taiwan-Japan's complementary technology through the innovative carbon capture technology of the Institute of Technology and Technology and other strengths, combining with Mitsubishi Electric's electromechanical control, and help strengthen the establishment of the carbon capture industry chain in Asia.Faced with the increasingly strict international carbon emission standards, the two sides look forward to cooperating to develop commercial carbon capture systems, opening up new carbon capture markets, assisting enterprises to strengthen their carbon reduction capabilities, and jointly move towards a net zero future."
