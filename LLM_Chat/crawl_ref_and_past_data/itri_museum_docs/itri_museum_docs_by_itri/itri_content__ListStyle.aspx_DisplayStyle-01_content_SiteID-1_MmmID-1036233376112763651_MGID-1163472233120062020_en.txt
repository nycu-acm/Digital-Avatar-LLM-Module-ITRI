Technology introduction
Taiwan's first self-driving logistics system is trial operation.
The home delivery industry has long had problems such as insufficient driver labor and long working hours.In addition, under the zero-contact economy in the post-epidemic era, unmanned self-driving logistics has become an international trend. The "self-driving logistics system" independently developed by the Institute of Technology and Research independently designed for the special road environment of Taiwan and Asia. It has overcome domestic environments such as mixed transportation of automobiles and locomotives and long rainy seasons, and cooperated with domestic logistics service industry to become the first case in Taiwan to conduct self-driving logistics experimental testing on open roads.
Features and innovation
The experimental path of this trial run is about 1.9 kilometers long, and it carries out point-to-point self-driving delivery from two business offices, and travels in the city of Hsinchu with the busiest mixed traffic of automobiles and locomotives in Hsinchu. The efficient calculation system handles the complexity of information such as people, vehicles, roads, etc., and is completely operated in Taiwan's unique automobiles and locomotives complex traffic environment.
Application and benefits
The Institute of Technology continues to develop self-driving logistics vehicle technology, drives Taiwanese logistics operators to undergo digital transformation of smart logistics, creates new logistics operation service models, and establishes an important milestone in the commercialization of domestic smart logistics.
