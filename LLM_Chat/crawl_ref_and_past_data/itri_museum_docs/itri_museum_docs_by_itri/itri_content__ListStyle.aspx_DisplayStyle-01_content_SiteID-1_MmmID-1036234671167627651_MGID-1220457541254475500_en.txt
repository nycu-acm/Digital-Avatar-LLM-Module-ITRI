Wu Mingxian Academician
Academician Wu Mingxian is the director of National Taiwan University Hospital. He has been deeply engaged in the medical field for more than 30 years. He is also good at clinical and basic medical research, and has worked tirelessly to benefit more patients.Academician Wu has more than 500 academic papers and 4 monographs. His works have been found in internationally renowned journals such as Lancet, Journal of Clinical Oncology, Gastroenterology and Gut. He has also won many academic awards, including the Ministry of Science and Technology Outstanding Research Award and the recognition of the new leader of the Asia-Pacific Digestive Society. He is also an editorial committee member of internationally renowned journals such as J Formos Med Assoc, Hepatogastroenterology, Advance in Digestive Medicine, Gut, and Gastric Cancer.
After graduating from the Department of Medicine of Taiwan University, Academician Wu received sub-specialty training in internal medicine and gastrointestinal dysfunction at NTU Hospital. He was promoted to attending physician in 1995 and obtained a doctorate from the Institute of Clinical Medicine in 1998. His fields of expertise are general internal medicine, gastroenterology, molecular biology, medical education, and epidemiology, with special emphasis on Helicobacter pylori and gastrointestinal cancer.Academician Wu actively participated in the prevention and treatment of gastric cancer, established a cooperative alliance for clinical trials of gastrointestinal diseases and Helicobacter bacteria in Taiwan, and led the team to Mazu, whose gastric cancer incidence was 3 to 5 times higher than that of Taiwan's island at that time, and screened and treated Helicobacter pylori. After 15 years of research, the rate of Helicobacter pylori carrier among Mazu residents has dropped from 70% to 10%, significantly reducing the incidence and mortality rate of gastric cancer.The gastric cancer prevention and treatment model proposed by the "Mazu Experience" has been recognized internationally as an international standard for gastric cancer prevention and treatment, and has been published on the cover of the world's top gastrointestinal journal Gut. It is the light of Taiwan's medicine, and it has also lowered gastric cancer from the third place in the top ten cancer deaths in Taiwan to the eighth place, making great contributions to saving medical expenses and reducing the pain of patients.
Academician Wu took over as the president of NTU Hospital in 2020, and set three important development directions for NTU Hospital: "smart medical care", "cutting medical care" and "precision health". He strengthened cooperation with schools, industries and research units such as China Research Institute, Institute of Industrial Research, and National Health Center to lay out the leadership position of next-generation medical care for NTU Hospitals.In addition, the "NTU Hospital 2.0" upgrade plan has also been launched, integrating the resources of Taiwan University Hospital in various campuses of Hsinchu and Yunlin Branches, moving towards the medical center, and continuously serving the "urgent, severe, difficult and rare" patients, and working for the health of the people, relying on "medical".
