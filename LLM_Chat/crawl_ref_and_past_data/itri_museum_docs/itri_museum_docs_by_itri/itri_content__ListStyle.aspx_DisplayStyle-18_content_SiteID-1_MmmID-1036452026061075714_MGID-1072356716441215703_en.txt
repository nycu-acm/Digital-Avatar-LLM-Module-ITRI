Hsinchu Logistics creates a new smart future
Oral statement/Li Zhengyi Compilation/Chen Yiru
Hsinchu Logistics Operations Director Li Zhengyi.
"Stores can have no storefronts, but they cannot have logistics," said Li Zhengyi, the head of logistics operations at Hsinchu, the largest local logistics industry, to reveal the key role of logistics in the digital society.With the arrival of the new crown epidemic in 2020, the home economy has leaped into the mainstream of consumption, from masks, people's livelihood supplies, to the daily needs of people in isolation and quarantine, all rely on logistics to connect the lifeblood of life.The value of logistics has become increasingly prominent after the epidemic. Li Zhengyi wants to use new technologies to grow in sync with the world!
In Hsinchu logistics distribution business, to B (to face enterprises) and to C (to face consumers) each account for half of the revenue, which is very special for logistics companies.Hsinchu Logistics, which has a history of 82 years, started as a traditional freight company and was the main customer. By the rise of e-commerce in 2000, we realized that the times were about to change. Faced with the arrival of digital economy, we boldly introduced Japan's Sagawa-Hitai technical team to assist in enterprise process reengineering (BPR). This became a very important turning point in Hsinchu Logistics - transforming from transportation to service industry.
It took nearly 8 years before and after this transformation to put the concept of the service industry into the minds of colleagues. Everyone clearly understood that what I sent was not the shipment, but the expectations and trust of customers. Only then did you know that you should cherish the goods and hand over the goods to customers politely.Without that transformation, Hsinchu Logistics would not be able to continue to compete in the e-commerce era. In 2011, Hsinchu Freight was officially renamed "Hsinchu Logistics" to provide more solutions other than delivery.
From logistics to commercial flow and gold flow, propose a complete solution
30 years ago, Hsinchu Logistics began to do logistics management in P&G, and is the first third-party partner in logistics warehousing in Taiwan.In the past, it was thought that it would be good to have an iron house in the warehouse. In fact, logistics storage includes many specifications, whether it is picking accuracy, order completion, inventory accuracy, 5S and the hygiene and safety of the factory, even ants have to be taken care of.We have never understood logistics to now become a logistics center that meets international standards.In addition to warehousing, we also do fulfillment distribution, helping customers accept orders, customer service, distribution, and even handling accounts receivable.Now, Hsinchu logistics is not just logistics, but also crosses business flow and gold flow, providing a complete solution.
Last year, Hsinchu Logistics' turnover reached 17.5 billion yuan, an annual growth of 12%, and the payment for goods received reached 27 billion yuan in the first year.Although Hsinchu Logistics mainly uses domestic delivery, we are also a designated delivery provider for many cross-border e-commerce companies in Taiwan. Currently, shipments from cross-border e-commerce can reach nearly one million pieces in a month.
Among the total 60,000 customers, it covers more than 20 industries. It is precisely because the industry is wider. After the outbreak of the epidemic, some customers were affected and some customers rose against the trend. They continued to show positive growth.In the early stages of the outbreak, we also used logistics power to transport masks.At 11 pm on the third day of the Chinese New Year, I received a call from the Commercial Department and immediately started dispatching. At around 2 o'clock in the middle of the night, the convoy arrived at the mask factory and started delivering goods at 4 o'clock. In this way, the first batch of 18 million masks that were started to work were delivered to major routes for three consecutive days.
The home economy has leaped into the mainstream of consumption, from masks, people's livelihood supplies, to the daily needs of people in isolation and quarantine, all rely on logistics to connect the lifeblood of life.
Four directions and two trends, and plan the future of logistics
The epidemic will not only not hinder logistics, but will also stimulate the development of the logistics industry.I think the logistics industry has four development directions.First of all, cross-border e-commerce, not only shipments from mainland China, we have joined forces with Sagawa to establish the "Xin Ruisakawa", first focusing on Japan's cross-border e-commerce, and then Southeast Asia. Regardless of delivery or collection of goods, we are responsible for logistics on the Taiwan side to strengthen the development of cross-border e-commerce in Taiwan.
The second is the retail channel to the store.For example, consumers place orders with Quanlian or family, and may be delivered to your home in 1 or 2 hours, because their store is a warehouse. If our troops are stronger in the future, they can enter this market.The third is medical flow. In order to ensure the safety of drug use, the warehousing and distribution of medicines have strict GDP standards. After five years of preparation, we obtained the GDP warehousing certification that complies with international standards in 2017. Now the market for medical flows is also continuing to grow.
The fourth is cold chain logistics.Cold chain logistics between enterprises has existed for a long time. In recent years, consumers' demand for low-temperature home delivery has risen, and they have grown by more than double-digits every year.However, cold chain home delivery is more difficult. In order to ensure that even if it is delivered to a remote village, we can maintain low temperatures, just for the refrigeration equipment of vehicles and stations, we invested 1 billion yuan in the first stage, and one-tenth of the existing 3,500 vehicles have refrigeration equipment.
Furthermore, we observe two trends that may affect the logistics industry.First of all, the gig economy. Taiwan’s e-commerce market is booming. After every holiday or shopping festival, the volume of goods will increase by 20% to 30% the next day. It must be distributed as scheduled, and the pressure is not small. If flexible human resources join, the peak of delivery can be alleviated.Just like the concept of Uber Eats, in the future, we may be able to build a platform for everyone to take orders, and these flexible manpower are our resources available.
The second is contactless logistics.Due to the impact of the epidemic, distribution has also developed towards "contactless". Hsinchu Logistics previously cooperated with the Industrial Research Institute to launch "smart withdrawal cabinets", and now it is also investing in the research and development of unmanned delivery vehicles. We foresee that it may face the impact of less births in the future, and it is more difficult to recruit drivers. Contactless delivery is expected to reduce labor costs.
Introducing automation technology creates a precedent for logistics industry
As logistics operators, we have been studying how to make logistics more efficient.Returning to the essence, the biggest feature of the logistics industry is the "large quantity". Whether it is shipments, customers or materials, it is difficult to bear if you rely solely on traditional labor. Therefore, it is necessary to invest in information technology, even the Internet of Things (IoT) or AI artificial intelligence.
As early as 20 years ago, we started to cooperate with the Industrial Research Institute. We raised the front-line problems and helped solve them. If we talk about the technical and equipment aspects, the Industrial Research Institute is the national team and has introduced a number of technologies.For example, when installing a "small items automatic cargo distributor", among our about 500,000 home delivery volumes per day, small items below A4 paper account for 20%.Since Hsinchu Logistics has 60 strongholds in Taiwan, small items have to be packaged to 60 barrels. Manual classification is not only time-consuming and labor-intensive, but sometimes the address can be misread.After importing the automatic cargo distribution machine for small items, the machine will judge that the shipment barcode will be automatically classified into 60 points, which not only speeds up, but also improves the accuracy rate.
In the past, when measuring material volume was measured by customers themselves or the driver's eyes, it was easy to cause errors. In addition, material volume was related to freight, which also affected the accuracy of freight calculation.Later, we introduced the "Material Automatic Identification System" on the conveyor belt. When the box passes, the size is judged by the time difference of the laser light reflection, and the accuracy can be controlled within positive and negative 3 cm. At the same time, taking photos to reduce controversy. The conveyor belt runs 80 meters to 100 meters in one minute.I think this technology of measuring the material volume of items while moving is currently the best in the international industry.
Containers and cage trucks are important fixed assets for logistics stations. In order to facilitate dispatch, we have to manually inventory the locations of 1,200 containers and 5,6,000 cage trucks every week. Each station takes 30 minutes to inventory each time, but the number is too large and it cannot be guaranteed to be accurate.Later, the Industrial Research Institute was asked to assist in introducing RFID technology, which could sense a range of 30 to 50 meters nearby. As long as the colleague stood in the center of the station platform, held a terminal in his hand, and turned a circle, he would almost complete the whole process.Now he can take stock twice a day, which is 50 times faster.
In addition, there is the "SD automatic navigation system", which requires up to 100 points for delivery in one day. You must know where each address is, how to sort it smoothly, and how to rearrange temporary changes. This is a great challenge for novice drivers. With the automatic navigation APP, the system will automatically arrange orders and navigation, which is a good helper for novice drivers.
Last year, we set another peak in smart logistics with the Industrial Research Institute, and worked together to build the first logistics center in Taiwan to introduce AI artificial intelligence decision-making for a large e-commerce platform in Taiwan. It is also the Shuttle Rack with the highest reserves in the industry.This is not only an automation device, nor is it just AI artificial intelligence, but also an integration of the order system of the e-commerce platform and the warehouse system of Hsinchu logistics, and all the inbound and outbound operation processes on site to create a complete AI e-commerce automation.
Since the e-commerce platform has as many as 100,000 items, and a small number of them are diverse, it is quite time-consuming to pick goods by relying on manpower.With Shuttle Rack, the system can calculate the best storage position based on the hot sales level of the product, design the shortest and best picking schedule, and drive back and forth through a high-speed shuttle bus, and move the storage basket to the picker. The warehousing efficiency is greatly improved by using goods to satisfy people.Not only does the commodity shipment time be reduced by 60%, but the production capacity can reach 10 times the original capacity during the peak order period of Double 11.
Hsinchu Logistics introduces an "automatic identification system for material volume" on the conveyor belt, and the accuracy can be controlled within plus or minus 3 cm, and can also take photos at the same time to reduce controversy.
Be brave to invest and experiment and treat failure as tuition
Regarding new technologies, Hsinchu Logistics has always maintained a very open mind and is willing to continue to try.I often say that the Institute of Technology can hold a "Industrial Research Institute Experimental Field" in our Hsinchu Institute because we are willing to invest in experiments, and the team of the Institute of Technology often arrives on-site for testing at 2 or 3 o'clock in the middle of the night. This kind of experimental spirit is very important.If successful, it can be copied to many places. Technology research and development is originally intended to create competitiveness for the country, and we cannot lock the knowledge-how of the research and development in the drawer.
"Innovation" is the DNA of Hsinchu Logistics. We prepare hundreds of millions of budgets every year and introduce innovative information and communication technology. At the same time, we also allow employees to continue to absorb new knowledge outside and bring them back to change management, processes and customer service. I think this is the key to Hsinchu Logistics' continuous creation of competitive advantages over the past 10 years.
The Institute of Technology and Research assists Hsinchu Logistics to introduce RFID technology. The colleagues only need to turn a circle with the terminal in the center of the station platform, and the position of the cage car will be invisible.
