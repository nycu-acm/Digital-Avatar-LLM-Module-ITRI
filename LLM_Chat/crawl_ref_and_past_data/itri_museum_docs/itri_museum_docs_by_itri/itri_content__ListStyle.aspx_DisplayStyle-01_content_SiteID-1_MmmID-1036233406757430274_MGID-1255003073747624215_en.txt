In order to promote technical cooperation and talent exchange in Taiwan and Japan, 2024/8/1 Japan Sunsuoden Co., Ltd. came to Taiwan to sign the Master Collaboration Agreement and MCA cooperation agreement with the Institute of Technology Research. The contracted representatives of the two parties were Lin Zhaoxian, deputy general manager of the Institute of Technology Research and Director of the Institute of Obstetrics and International, and Masaichiro Hirakikuro, executive officer and director of the Institute of Development of Japan. Su Mengzong, senior vice president and assistant director of the Institute of Technology Research and Technology, as witnesses. The main purpose of this cooperation agreement is to carry out organizational cooperation with the Institute of Technology Research and Technology. In the future, it will target diverse cooperation such as cross-field technical cooperation, talent exchange, and open experimental field co-creation.At the same time, the Sunlight Institute and the Institute of Obstetrics, Electrical and Optical Institute, Machinery Institute, and Material Chemical Institute were also arranged to conduct exchange meetings on consulting services, 3D packaging, built-in capacitors, micropore processing and other technologies, and the cooperation between the two parties is expected to be carried out.
