Lin Jiannan Academician
Academician Lin Jiannan is currently the chairman and general manager of Taiwan Plastics Company. He has contributed more than 50 years to petrochemical-related industries. Under the trend of diversification and internationalization, he has a forward-looking mindset, adheres to the concept of altruism and ends at the highest good, and is coupled with "sincerity", "reputation" and "harmony" as the business philosophy.
After graduating from the Department of Chemistry at Zhongyuan University, Chairman Lin entered the Qianzhen Factory of Formosa Plastics Kaohsiung in 1967. He started from the grassroots level, accumulated rich practical and management experience, and broke through all difficulties with extraordinary perseverance. He actively assisted Formosa Plastics enterprises in the global layout and built complete up, mid- and downstream production plants for petrochemical raw materials. In addition to being sent to Texas in the United States to create Formosa Plastics Texas in the United States in 1979, he established a petrochemical industrial park in Ningbo City, Zhejiang Province in 2001 to give full play to the advantages of vertical integration and the benefits of industrial cluster aggregation, so that the global development of Formosa Plastics enterprises has entered a new page.
In addition, Chairman Lin has implemented corporate social responsibility, vigorously promoted "one family in the factory", assisted local governments in their spare time, and followed the trend of the times, developed circular economy and low-carbon green energy, actively developed the carbon fiber market, and laid out four major renewable energy fields, including solar energy, wind power generation, sensitive batteries and electrolytes.Under his leadership, Formosa Plastics promoted Renwu Factory's green energy transformation plan, and cooperated with the Industrial Research Institute to build a mass production factory for dye-sensitive batteries in Tainan Shalun Green Energy Science City in 2018, setting a new example for Taiwan's petrochemical industry to invest in the development of new energy technology.
Furthermore, because of long-term concern about the future development of Taiwan’s petrochemical industry, as the chairman of the Plastic Raw Materials Association, he made suggestions to the government on behalf of the petrochemical industry and made efforts to focus on my country’s petrochemical industry and promote the sustainable development of Taiwan’s economy.
In recent years, Chairman Lin has led Formosa Plastics to comprehensively promote Industry 4.0 and introduce artificial intelligence technology to accelerate the realization of smart upgrades. At the same time, he donated to participate in the establishment of the "Taiwan Artificial Intelligence School", hoping to cultivate AI talents for Taiwan and lay the foundation for Taiwan's AI development and enhance its competitiveness.
