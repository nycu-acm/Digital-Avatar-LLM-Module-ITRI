Technology introduction
Plant pretreatment wall breaking technology and polysaccharide low-thermal extraction technology
Our experimental team has a complete extraction process in response to plant cell wall destruction technology, combining Fenton Reaction and Ultrasound-assisted extraction (UAE) in a specific way to achieve rapid extraction and increase the extraction rate of plant-based ingredients.
Features and innovation
The traditional extraction method is mostly heated acid-base extraction. Although it can quickly degrade the cell wall, it is easy to degrade the extraction target at the same time and has chemical solvent residue problems.This technology combines oxidation pre-treatment with non-heated ultrasonic extraction of algae polysaccharides, which can effectively improve the extraction efficiency and obtain functional extracts.
Application and benefits
Taking health food-grade polysaccharide raw materials as an example, the domestic annual demand reaches 30 tons.Domestic agricultural residues, etc. are used as extraction targets, and negative value is converted into high-value products, with their extended output value exceeding 6 billion.
