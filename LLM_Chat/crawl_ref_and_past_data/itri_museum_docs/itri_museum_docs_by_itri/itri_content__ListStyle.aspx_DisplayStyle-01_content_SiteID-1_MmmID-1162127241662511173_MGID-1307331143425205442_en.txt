Technology introduction
H-shaped steel structure laser welding technology.
Develop innovative low-carbon smart laser welding technology, break through the traditional manufacturing methods that have not changed for 50 years, and provide advanced manufacturing methods that are innovative in the industry and take into account energy conservation and carbon reduction, filling the insufficient production capacity of domestic building steel structures.
Application scope
The rapid welding quality prediction technology greatly shortens the processing and line change decision-making schedule from 20 days to 3 days.
Multi-sensing welding robot integrated control (instant bead tracking, molten pool monitoring, welding power/speed monitoring, etc.).
Increase production capacity by 5 times and reduce carbon emissions by 80%.
Carbon reduction benefits
The technical achievements have been transferred to Taiwan’s photocopy and H-shaped steel structures will be officially put into production in 2024.
Passed the certification of SGS and the National Earthquake Center, and promoted the Taiwan Welding Association to formulate welding procedures and inspection standards (CNS 15876-11, CNS 15877-4, CNS 16246-1).
