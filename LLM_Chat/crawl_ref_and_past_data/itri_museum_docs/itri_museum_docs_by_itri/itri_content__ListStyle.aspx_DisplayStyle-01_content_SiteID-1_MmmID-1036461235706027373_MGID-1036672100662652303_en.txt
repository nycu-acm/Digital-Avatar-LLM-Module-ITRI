Introduction
Thanks to the support of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Smart Manufacturing Networked Data Value-added Alliance (hereinafter referred to as: this Alliance) was initiated by the Institute of Industrial Technology and was established in cooperation with legal entities such as the Metal Industry Research and Development Center, Information Industry Policy Association and other legal entities.This alliance aims to promote the common networking standards of smart manufacturing, jointly break through the problems of upstream and downstream networking integration of the industrial chain, combine various manufacturing data value-added services, and link international industrial standards, with the purpose of promoting the development of the smart manufacturing industry.
