Technology introduction
Biochar micro-coated ornamental fish water purification materials.
This water purification material is the first ornamental fish water purification product in China that has both environmental protection and carbon reduction benefits.This material uses biochar made of agricultural residues as the main raw material, and then is molded and granulated. It also has the physical filtration of activated carbon and the biological filtration of ceramic rings, which can improve the existing problems of poor bacteria-carrying capacity of activated carbon and poor adsorption effect of ceramic ring holes too small.The application methods of this material are simple and diverse, which can meet the needs of different consumer groups, and can extend the frequency of water exchange for ornamental fish tanks from one week to one month.
Application scope
This material can be directly placed in a general filter box and integrated into the existing fish tank filtration system for use; it can also be placed directly in the fish tank to meet consumer groups that cannot be plugged in and are inconvenient to install filter systems, such as ecological bottles, landscaping tanks, and office groups.
Carbon reduction benefits
This water purification material uses agricultural residues as the main raw material to store carbon dioxide in biochar.Each kilogram of biochar can store about 3.7 kilograms of carbon dioxide.
