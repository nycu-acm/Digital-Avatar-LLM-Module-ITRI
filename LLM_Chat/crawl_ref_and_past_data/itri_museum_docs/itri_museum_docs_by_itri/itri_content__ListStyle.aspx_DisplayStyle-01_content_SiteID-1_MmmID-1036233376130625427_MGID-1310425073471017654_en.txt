Technology introduction
The Industrial Research Institute provides one-stop exosome separation and analysis services for TFF columns and SEC columns developed in cooperation with the industry.From isolation to analysis and identification, high recovery and high purity exosomes were obtained perfectly.
Exosome isolation and purification technology:
Combined with the advantages of tangential flow filtration (TFF) and size exclusion chromatography (SEC), most of the proteins were removed with TFF initial screen and the sample was concentrated, and then the residual protein was further separated using the SEC column.This two-step purification method can process large-scale samples at once, produce exosomes on a large scale and have the advantages of low protein residues.Special surface treatment reagents are developed for the characteristics of TFF and SEC columns, which have the advantages of higher exosome recovery and shorter separation and purification time compared with commercial competitors.
Exosome analysis and detection services:
Exosomes are vesicles rich in diverse biological molecules, which makes it more difficult to clarify their mechanism of action and implement quality control.Our services combine comprehensive exosome characteristic analysis and multisomatic testing to ensure the safety and effectiveness of therapeutic exosomes, which helps accelerate the development of new drugs and improve product quality.
Features and innovation
Technical Features
Exosome isolation and purification technology:
Special surface treatment reagents are developed for two different columns of TFF and SEC, which can improve the affinity of the sample for filter membranes or colloids, shorten the separation time, and reduce the extracellular vesicle/exosome sticky column to improve its recovery rate.
It is suitable for a variety of sample sources, including cell culture medium, plasma, etc. from different sources, and has a wide range of use.
It does not destroy the structural activity of extracellular vesicles/exosomes and is not biotoxic. It can be directly applied to pharmaceutical or medical beauty related products in the future.
Exosome analysis and detection services:
Multisomatic technology was used to analyze the exosome composition and conduct a differential comparison to identify inter-batch variation and key quality attributes.
Establish a complete detection platform in accordance with regulatory requirements, strengthen functional analysis, and be easy to integrate with exosome CMC.
It has cross-species compatibility and is suitable for exosome identification and analysis of most species.
