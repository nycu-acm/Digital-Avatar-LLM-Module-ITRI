The Ministry of Economic Science and Technology R&D Theme Pavilion officially debuted on March 27 at the Taiwan International Machine Tool Exhibition "TMTS 2024". The Institute of Technology and Research has exhibited 20 key technologies for advanced tool machines, including the "world's most sensitive vibration sensing chip" that surpasses the Swiss sensor manufacturers' minimum vibration measurement technology twice, has successfully entered the advanced aerospace industry, and the "AI high-precision five-axis tool machine" that can significantly shorten the measurement time ten times and has been introduced to domestic machine tool manufacturers and terminal manufacturers. It has successfully assisted 25 Yongying aircraft to complete national defense autonomous tasks.
The Industrial Research Institute said that the launch of the first domestic "AI high-precision five-axis tool machine" uses AI technology to improve accuracy, shortening the time before departure from the past two weeks to within six hours, so that the domestic tool machine accuracy doubles to the same level as the Japanese and German joint venture tool machine factory DMG.In addition, it independently developed the "world's most sensitive vibration sensing chip" to innovate composite vibration sensing, and can measure the tiny vibration volume twice that of the European chip manufacturer Swiss sensor manufacturer, and has more than 2,000 sets of domestic diffusion applications, assisting five-axis tool machine operator Baide to introduce composite sensors, improve positioning accuracy and stability, and expand high-end aerospace supply chains such as General Electric Company (GE) and Rolls-Royce, with an expected output value of 2.5 billion.
Qiu Qiuhui, Director of the Industrial Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, said that domestic tool manufacturers performed well in the export market.However, with the rapid changes in the global production and manufacturing supply chain, in order to assist the manufacturing industry in accelerating the transformation and upgrading and developing low-carbon manufacturing, the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs has invested nearly 10 billion yuan in resource subsidies for legal person research and development in the field of machinery in the past four years. In the field of tool machines, we will assist tool manufacturers in strengthening their high-level manufacturing strength with four core axes such as AI smart manufacturing, digital transformation, green energy conservation, and efficient composite.In the past four years, the Technology Department has obtained 219 domestic and foreign patents in the field of machinery, has cooperated with nearly 700 manufacturers, and has promoted the derivative production value of more than 15 billion yuan. It has cooperated with the domestic tool machine manufacturer Xiehong and applied it to the processing of key parts of the Yongying fuselage and wing. It has successfully completed 25 production and manufacturing, which can drive the price of five-axis tool machines to increase by 20%, and the output value doubles to more than 8 billion yuan.
The Industrial Research Institute published a number of key technical achievements this time as follows:
1. AI high-precision five-axis tool machine: one-click production of five-axis machine measurement and compensation
The nationally produced space accuracy measurement and compensation tool replaces the complex operation of traditional gauge and laser interferometer. Through innovative modules, the volume space accuracy error measurement and compensation of the five-axis machine is completed in one-click.With the support of the Science and Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology has pioneered a complete solution for integrated rapid accuracy detection and error source analysis and an internationally universal cross-controller platform. Compared with traditional gauge and laser for about 2 weeks, the five-axis volume space accuracy detection and compensation technology can control the measurement time within 6 hours, and the error after compensation is ≦30μm (original 60μm).In addition, it can assist in the formulation of compensation strategies in less than 20 minutes, and strategic compensation can be improved by improving the machine's accuracy.Taiwan's three-axis tool machine manufacturer has become a five-axis tool machine solution provider, which is expected to drive the output value of 8 billion high-end tool machine.It has reached 10 domestic five-axis machine tool in-depth analysis strategy partners.
2. The world's most sensitive vibration sensing chip: the electrocardiogram probe of the tool machine, accurately analyze the operating status
The Institute of Technology and Research independently developed micro-electromechanical vibration sensing chips to innovate fully differential sensing components and built-in temperature compensation and anti-electromagnetic interference circuits. It is currently the world's lowest vibration sensing chip (noise reaches 50μg/rtHz, surpassing international manufacturers ~80μg/rtHz).It has the characteristics of accurately measuring micro vibrations, overcomes the equipment's low-frequency vibration error, and improves abnormal recognition rate (80% → ≥95%). It can be widely used in tool machine equipment and key components, like the electrocardiogram probe of the tool machine, which analyzes the operating status of the tool machine at any time, provides equipment health information, and assists in maintaining processing accuracy and stability.At present, more than 50 domestic operators have been introduced, such as Baide Machinery's advanced five-axis tool machine, which introduces intelligent vibration detection solutions to improve equipment accuracy and stability, expand the high-end aerospace supply chain (GE, Rolls-Royce), with an expected output value of 2.5 billion.
3. AI audio non-destructive detection technology: intelligent listening and recognition, improving the detection efficiency of metal products
The Institute of Technology has developed "AI Audio Non-Destruction Detection Technology" using artificial intelligence algorithms to effectively identify the existence of cracks inside metal products and improve detection accuracy. Through the knocking and radio of metal products, combined with artificial intelligence and machine learning technology, it realizes automatic identification of sound signal characteristics and classification of good/bad products, and creates digital, automated, and standardized internal defect non-destruction detection technology for metal products. The detection accuracy is about 13% higher than that of previous manual identification, and saves 50% time and labor costs.The inspection of Xuhong Metal, the largest local powder metallurgy plant, has been introduced on electric vehicle brake components, successfully assisting the accuracy rate from 85.12% to 98.44%. Due to the reduction of post-processing and product scrapping losses, the annual carbon reduction of about 433 tons of CO2e was promoted, creating an output value of more than NT$50 million.
4. Digital transformation service platform-net zero sustainable production service suite
Provide the carbon management services required by the factory, use the global sustainable production data system, and can obtain information from order reception, production, storage, energy and waste discharge in a digital manner. Provide multi-level carbon emission management at the factory, products and suppliers. According to different sustainable management (carbon, water, waste) needs, the relevant performance and output management reports can be calculated instantly, and the optimal scheduling can be carried out according to the management goals.It has been imported into many fields and is used for real-time monitoring of manufacturing equipment/production line status, systematic production history tracking, systematic production carbon inspection and carbon footprint tracking.
5. Energy-saving cutting navigation and energy consumption management module of tool machine: a powerful tool to establish the best energy-saving solution
The Institute of Technology Research Institute's "Power Machine Energy-saving Cutting Navigation and Energy Consumption Management Module" provides low-cost carbon management solutions, which have high accuracy, high horizontal diffusion, and low cost in product carbon history tracking. It provides elastic applications across machines and can be applied to the same product processes in the entire factory and the entire line without the need for a meter, reducing the installation of expensive sensing equipment.In addition, the processing path is optimized to reduce energy consumption, establish a nonlinear relationship between machine network information and total power consumption status (Function Mapping). The accuracy of energy model training and test data has reached more than 95%, assisting enterprises in achieving low-carbon manufacturing.It is mainly used in the field of metal processing and can also be expanded to other industries to assist operators in the automatic production of precise carbon history required in response to the EU CBAM specification.
The Institute of Technology has planned the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" in the resilient social field. In response to the market demands such as energy conservation and carbon reduction, remote operations, and simulated operations, it has promoted the development of smart manufacturing-related soft and hard integration technologies to enhance international competitiveness.
