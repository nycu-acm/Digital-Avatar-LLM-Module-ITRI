Academician Su Zifeng
Academician Su Zifeng is the chairman and CEO of AMD. He has been deeply engaged in the semiconductor field for more than 30 years. With abundant professional knowledge and spirit of continuous innovation, he has broken through countless technical problems; based on technology, compatible with excellent management capabilities, and has led the R&D team and enterprises to the industrial peak many times. He is a key driving force for the development of advanced semiconductors and high-efficiency computing technology in the world.
Academician Su graduated from the Department of Electrical Engineering at the Massachusetts Institute of Technology (MIT) and internships at Analog Devices, stimulating his interest in semiconductor research and development through the University Research Opportunity Project (UROP) and an intern at Analog Devices.After obtaining his bachelor's degree, he continued to study for a master's and doctoral degree at MIT, and was also the first research member in the world to study "insulating overlaid silicon (SOI)" at that time.Later, he took up an important position at IBM and developed semiconductor chips with copper process technology through SOI technology to solve the problem of copper impurity pollution during the production process, increase the chip's operating speed by 20%, and establish new standards in the semiconductor industry.After 2000, IBM will continue to develop new technologies, including microprocessors that greatly improve the battery life of handheld devices.In 2008, he transferred to Freescale Semiconductor (Freescale) to develop new business types for it. The then president praised Academician Su as a key figure in promoting Freescale into a new generation.
Academician Su joined AMD as senior vice president and general manager of the global business group in 2012, and took over as president and CEO in 2014. He is committed to expanding the market outside of personal computers through technology investment, streamlining product lines and continuous diversified development.Under its leadership, AMD rose rapidly in the central processor market, successfully reversed, turning losses into profits, rising from a minimum of US$1.61 per share, and now it has exceeded US$100 and its market value has exceeded US$200 billion. It is not only rated as one of the 50 most influential women in the world by Fortune, but also known as the "Queen of Semiconductors."She regards Taiwan as an indispensable part of the global semiconductor ecosystem, and closely cooperates with Taiwan's electronics industry to promote the development of Taiwan's semiconductor industry. As the demand for AI servers rises, in the future, it will set up Asia's first AI R&D center in Taiwan, deepen cooperation with the IC design industry, implement AI servers from Taiwan factories, actively cultivate high-level R&D talents, improve the industrial supply chain, and strengthen Taiwan's semiconductor ecosystem.
