Technology introduction
Hydrogen storage bottle for vehicles.
Carbon fiber composite hydrogen storage cylinder with high hydrogen storage density, lightweight, high safety, and avoid hydrogen embrittlement.
Application scope
Developed to accurately control the resin content of winding prepregs and low-temperature hardening and improve rigidity to solve the uneven distribution of wet winding resins in traditional gas cylinders, resulting in a decrease in rigidity.
Compared with traditional steel cylinders, it can reduce weight by more than 50%.The design has a pressure resistance of more than 2.5 times, and is highly safe.
Composite materials have corrosion resistance properties suitable for high salt environments in Taiwan.
Carbon reduction benefits
According to the hydrogen energy planning blueprint of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, Taiwan's hydrogen demand will reach 4.35 million tons/year in 2050, and it is estimated that it can save 1.276 million tons/year of domestic storage and transportation carbon emissions, reducing transportation carbon emissions by 72.8%. Calculated at the EU carbon rights price of 94 euros/ton, the carbon reduction benefits exceed NT$4 billion/year.
