Cover Story Features
LEDs are both "light" and "bright" and "bright" to replace incandescent light bulbs in tradition
LED, an optical technology that saves electricity and energy, is gradually becoming popular in the lives of ordinary people.Now, with the improved bulb lights of the Institute of Technology Research, LEDs will completely eliminate traditional incandescent bulbs, and people will also enjoy more economical and convenient lighting.
As LED (light emitting diode) technology becomes more and more mature, its application scope is becoming more and more widespread. From TV screens, traffic numbers to outdoor electronic boards, you can already see the traces of LEDs.However, in the eyes of the team of the Institute of Electrical and Optical Research Institute, LEDs can play far more than this--their goal is to completely replace traditional incandescent bulbs with LEDs. And, they have succeeded.
The optoelectronic components and system application group of the Institute of Electrical and Optical Research Institute of Industrial Research is the ever-winning army of R&D100.In 2008, they won the recognition of R&D100 by the "chip-type alternating current light emitting diode lighting technology" that can directly drive LED chips to emit light with alternating current to reduce power consumption losses; four years later, they won another goal with "light bright LED bulb lamp technology", and their technical strength has long been widely recognized internationally.
As the name suggests, the main feature of "light bright bulb lamp" is that it is lighter and has higher brightness; however, why do weight and brightness become the key to technological breakthroughs?In terms of market applications, what is the significance of a lighter and brighter LED?
One-way transmission maximum limit on LED application
Looking back at the LED technology, although it is quite popular at the application level, it cannot replace traditional high-energy-consuming incandescent bulbs because light can only be transmitted in one direction, so it cannot be used more generally on lighting equipment.Therefore, whether it can break through the limitations of unidirectional transmission of LED light sources has also become the key to strengthening the scope of LED application.
Xu Zhenpeng, the special project manager of the Institute of Electric Opticals, analyzed that if you just want to increase the luminous angle, the easiest way is to install a reflector around the LED to allow the light to refract outward."But doing this will lose brightness and will consume more energy, which is not necessarily feasible," he stressed that while solving the problem of luminous angle, we must also consider production cost, efficiency, product weight and other conditions to truly provide the operator's solution.
The bulb lamp designed by electro-optical is a product that takes into account many conditions and limitations.From the appearance, the bulb lamp is still similar to the traditional round incandescent bulb and has a round shell; but the difference with the incandescent bulb is that the LED light emitting elements are not placed in the bulb, but are embedded in the dents on the surface.In addition, the material used in this model is not the glass and metal shells that were familiar to the past, but the thermal plastic with various colors.
The biggest advantage of this new design is that it breaks through the limitations of LED unidirectional light sources in the past.Xu Zhenpeng explained that in order to simulate the luminous angle of an incandescent light bulb, the R&D team designed dents on the surface of the bulb that can be embedded in LED elements, thereby allowing the light to emit evenly around to make up for the shortcomings of LED unidirectional transmission, and also achieve a wider luminous range than incandescent light bulbs."Generally, the luminous angle of incandescent bulbs is only 300 degrees, and we can already achieve 330 degrees, and the luminous range is even larger," said Xu Zhenpeng.
However, although such a design solves the problem of luminous angle, it also presents a new challenge: heat dissipation.Since hundreds of LED light-emitting elements may be placed on a bulb lamp, the heat is also very concentrated.Therefore, in order to allow heat energy to be discharged smoothly, the team of the Electric and Optical Institute has designed many small heat dissipation holes inside and outside the bulb lamp, and has also changed the material of the bulb lamp to heat-conducting heat-dissipating plastic to solve the problem of heat energy emission.
Use thermal plastic, lighter and lower cost
When using thermal plastic to make bulb lamps, the electro-optical institute also solved the two major problems of production cost and weight.In the past, the materials of incandescent bulbs were mostly made of metal and were relatively expensive; this time, in order to create a special appearance, the electro-optics industry tried to use plastic with more plastic for the first time."Because we use plastic to make it, as long as the mold is made, it is easy to produce in large quantities. This is an advantage that metal cannot do," Xu Zhenpeng analyzed. Although the cost of thermal plastic is higher than that of ordinary plastic, compared with existing metals, the production cost is less than one-third, which is quite price-competitive.
In addition, another advantage of using plastic material is that it greatly reduces the weight of the bulb lamp.Xu Zhenpeng estimated that traditional metal bulb lamps weigh about 180 grams, but plastic bulb lamps weigh only 90 to 100 grams, which is about 50%."The weight has become lighter and the application range is wider," he gave an example. Because the LED bulb made of plastic has become lighter, it can be hung out of thin air or adjusted angles. Unlike traditional metal bulbs that need to be fixed, new applications such as concert projectors will appear one after another in the future.
Looking back at the entire process of developing light-bright LED bulb lights, the most difficult part is not the research and development of new technologies, but how to use existing technologies to create new value.As for bulb lamps, it is actually not difficult to use thermal plastic or design appearance, but how many LED light-emitting elements should be embedded in a bulb lamp to balance the luminous efficiency and production cost.
Xu Zhenpeng pointed out that the first generation of 60-watt light-bright LED bulb lamp has a total of 16 dents that can be embedded in LED components on the outside. "This is the result of our attempts many times. If there are too few dents, the bulb will not be bright enough, but if there are too many dents, there will be too many LEDs on it, and there is no need to."
LEDs originally had the advantages of saving electricity and energy. Now, with the blessing of brightness, weight and production costs, it is no longer an out-of-reach dream to obtain traditional high-energy-consuming incandescent bulbs; especially after obtaining the recognition of R&D100, the "light bright LED bulb light" technology will inevitably attract global attention.This will also make another contribution to the Institute of Electric and Optical's long-term contribution in LED technology.
