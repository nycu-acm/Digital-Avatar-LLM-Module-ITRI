Technology introduction
Markless 3D live action capture technology.
Just walk into the simple studio without wearing devices.After the video is recorded, you can capture 3D action postures to create virtual characters and virtual scenes.
Features and innovation
Using AI image analysis and 3D spatial positioning, markless motion capture technology is developed, and 3D motion information can be captured and generated by human body without changing clothes, and applied to virtual characters.
Using a general 2D camera and combining the independent development of 3D space node positioning and tracking fusion algorithm, it can quickly deploy and capture the 3D skeleton of the human body and apply it to virtual scenes.
Application and benefits
Break through the problems of wearing motion capture suits in the current industry and not being able to move studios quickly.
Suitable for forward-looking content manufacturers such as 3D virtual character action generation and virtual reality integration interactive applications.
