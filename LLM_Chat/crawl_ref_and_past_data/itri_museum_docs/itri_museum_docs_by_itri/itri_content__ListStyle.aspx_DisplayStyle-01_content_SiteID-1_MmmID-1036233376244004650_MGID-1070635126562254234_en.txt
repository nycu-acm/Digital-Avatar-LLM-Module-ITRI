Technology introduction
Atmospheric plasma egg sterilization technology.
Eggs and shells contaminated by chicken manure are the infection routes of salmonella, and incomplete sterilization will cause food safety crisis.The atmospheric plasma technology of the Institute of Technology is used to sterilize eggs, which can eliminate 99.9999% of bacteria on the surface of the eggs. The sterilization of eggs is safe and environmentally friendly!
Features and innovation
Atmospheric pressure plasmon surface process technology provides a green and environmentally friendly process, using plasma for activation, metal oxide reduction, organic etching and coating processes. Compared with wet chemical processes, there is no waste liquid pollution problem and reduces environmental pollution.There is no need for vacuum cavity or complex instruments, and the general environment can generate plasma, saving process equipment costs.The Institute of Machinery has unique high-density plasma source design and manufacturing capabilities, which can handle conductive materials, as well as plastic substrates with low reaction temperature and not heat-resistant (process temperature <120℃).
