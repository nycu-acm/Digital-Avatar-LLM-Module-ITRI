North American company of Industrial Research Institute
The North American Company of Industrial Research Institute is located in San Jose, California. It is a subsidiary of the Taiwan-based Industrial Technology Research Institute. It is mainly engaged in entrepreneurship and development cooperation, commissioned research, technology transfer, intellectual power business, talent recruitment and advanced training. The purpose of the North American Company of Industrial Research Institute is to continuously build bridges through the industrial cooperation platform established by long-term operations to promote cooperation and connection between Taiwan and the American science and technology and industry, and extend it to other regions of the world when necessary, and promote win-win cross-regional technology and commercial cooperation through different regional advantages and local innovation settlements to create and expand value beyond regional boundaries.
The North American company of the Industrial Research Institute is located in Silicon Valley, a global innovation, entrepreneurship, investment and high-tech industrial cluster. In addition to closely cooperating with the Industrial Research Institute and the customers/partners of the Industrial Research Institute and the industry in Taiwan, it has established excellent network and cooperative relationships with well-known universities, research institutions, startups, enterprises, venture capital, technology development and industry associations in North America. Through the Industrial Research Institute North American company, the North American customers/partners can make full use of the Multi-tech R&D energy of the Industrial Research Institute, their own technical capabilities and Taiwan’s huge and high-quality supply network of various industrial clusters to accelerate the value creation process.
Service content
Entrepreneurship accelerates
Since 2006, North American companies in IRI have trained more than 30 Silicon Valley startups through physical or virtual methods, and some have collaborated with the Industrial Technology Institute to develop their technology, successfully launch products on the market and continue to grow.At the appropriate time, North American companies of the Industrial Research Institute will introduce these cooperative startups to venture capital companies under the Industrial Technology Research Institute - Industrial Research Institute Innovation Company or other venture capital companies.As for the further development and commercialization of products, it can be conveniently arranged in the open laboratory of the Industrial Technology Research Institute in Taiwan to take advantage of the equipment, technology and R&D advantages of the Industrial Technology Research Institute, and to get close to grasping the dynamics of new applications in the Asian market.
R&D cooperation
The basic research strength in universities and research institutions can be practiced and strengthened through the application R&D energy of the Institute of Industrial Technology.The North American company of the Industrial Research Institute has promoted research and development cooperation between the Industrial Technology Research Institute and North American partners. All parties involved in the cooperation also included researchers, including long-term investigations, investment of resources in important and common interests in cooperation projects, and sharing of smart property rights for cooperative output.The North American company of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has also participated in multiple international industrial R&D alliances, and introduced international industrial partners to participate in the Ad-STAC and OLED lighting commercialization alliance led by the Institute of Industrial Research and Research and Research and Research.
Commissioned research
The North American company of the Industrial Research Institute promotes commissioned research cooperation between many major and small companies in the Silicon Valley and other regions, and assists customers to make full use of the R&D capabilities and energy of the Industrial Technology Research Institute in electronics, optoelectronics, information and communications, displays, materials and chemicals, steaks, advanced manufacturing, medical equipment, water treatment and energy efficiency, in order to shorten its R&D schedule, reduce resource investment, and improve product development efficiency.
Technology transfer and authorization
The North American company of the Industrial Research Institute works with the Industrial Technology Institute to license and transfer many outstanding technologies.
Talent recruitment and advanced training
For many people, whether they are fresh graduates or semi-retired in different careers, they have gained a lot of experience in working at the Institute of Industrial Technology. The North American Company of the Institute of Industrial Technology assists the Institute of Industrial Technology to recruit talents throughout the year; for the employees and industrial partners of the Institute of Industrial Technology, the North American Company of the Institute of Industrial Technology designed and implemented the "elite project" and brought outstanding Taiwanese researchers to SRI International to train value creation and introduce the famous innovation, entrepreneurship and managers of Silicon Valley.Since 2011, the North American Company of the Institute of Industrial Technology has provided a series of training courses to selected senior management and medical experts, and has comprehensively linked and cooperated with the value chain of the medical equipment industry in Silicon Valley.
