Zhu Zhiyang Academician
Academician Zhu Zhiyang is the founder of Youjia Group. He is known for his "accurate vision and extraordinary courage". He has deeply cultivated the tool machine industry, actively integrated and developed advanced industries, and has made Youjia Group a solid enterprise with a territory of 10 major machinery manufacturing countries including Asia, Europe, America, and 96 companies around the world. He is known as the "master of the tool machine industry merger and acquisition king".
President Zhu started from scratch as a sailor in his early years, and was built at the top ten construction projects. He was optimistic about the takeoff of domestic demand. He resolutely entered the machine machine industry and founded Youjia Company in 1979, and initially represented Japan's Kobe Steel Construction Machinery.In recent years, many tool machine manufacturers in Taiwan have faced fierce competition in the international market. President Zhu is brave enough to break bottlenecks and actively promote mergers and acquisitions or joint ventures around the world to quickly acquire world-class technology and talents and build industrial competitiveness.
It is precisely because of President Zhu's foresight that Youjia acquired more than 40 overseas tool manufacturers and jointly ventured with 17 well-known companies (Joint Venture) to diversify the main business groups such as tool machines, industrial equipment, and green energy to create huge growth and profits.In 2016, the Group's tool machine business group grew into the world's third largest tool machine group. It has multiple production bases and marketing bases around the world, covering 16 countries, including Boeing, Mercedes-Benx, BMW, Ferrari, Bentley and other famous car manufacturers, and are all customers of Youjia Group.
President Zhu has served in the tool machine industry for more than 30 years and witnessed the rise and fall of Taiwan's machinery industry. He attaches importance to talents. In order to implement the concept of "R&D innovation, marketing services, rationalization and value creation", Youjia invests 4% of its turnover every year in R&D funds, and has more than 22% of its R&D manpower, moving towards the goal of "product diversification, Taiwan's R&D, connecting Asia, and deploying the world".In 2016, President Zhu won the Ernst & Young World Entrepreneur Award in the name of "Entrepreneur Olympics" to recognize his outstanding business management skills.
