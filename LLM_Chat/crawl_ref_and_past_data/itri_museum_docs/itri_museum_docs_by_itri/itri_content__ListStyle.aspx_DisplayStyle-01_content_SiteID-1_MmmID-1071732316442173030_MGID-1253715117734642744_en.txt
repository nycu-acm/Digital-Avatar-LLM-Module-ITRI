Technology introduction
LEO low-orbit satellite ground communication radio frequency system
ITRI ​​has built the basic energy of ground communication equipment and key components in space industry and has laid out the development of LEO satellite ground station RF front-end modules; it can improve process yield and realize design with a splicable antenna module architecture, integrates transmit and receive RF front-end modules, including Tx/Rx array antenna modules, upscaling modules, downscaling modules, digital circuit control modules and power supply modules, etc. The most critical of these is the phased array antenna Tx/Rx module, which is implemented in the form of Antenna in Package (AiP), combined with industrial energy, using LTCC or Organic substrates; it can reduce the size of the antenna array, shorten the RF signal transmission distance, and reduce losses.
Features and innovation
Domestic independent development of satellite communication system ground station advanced phase array antenna and RF front-end module design.
Innovatively develop elastic millimeter wave RF antenna packaging (AiP) architecture design, and high-integration improved parallel series peripheral interface (SPI) to achieve 128 antenna unit phase array antenna RF circuit integration and beam control.
Establish an array antenna and RF front-end module technology between the Ka frequency band transmitting end and the Ku/K frequency band receiving end, supporting RF antenna subsystems with inclination angle +30°~+90° and azimuth angle 0°~360°, which can assist domestic manufacturers in improving phased array antenna radio frequency technology.
The 128 array antenna transceiver UT radio frequency system completes 32APSK and 62.5MHz Symbol Rate transmission verification respectively.
Application and benefits
Low-orbit satellite communication ground equipment industry, NTN communication ground equipment industry.
