Academician Shi Chongtang
Chairman Shi has long been committed to innovative technology research and development, industrial design and brand marketing. He has excellent results in promoting innovative application of personal computers and brand internationalization. He has also won many recognitions such as the Outstanding Science and Technology Contribution Award, the Outstanding Entrepreneur Award and the "Science and Technology Management Award" of the Society of Science and Technology Management of the Republic of China.Chairman Shi has also actively promoted green environmental protection, social welfare, humanistic care and internationalization, led the Taiwan information industry to improve its visibility around the world, promote Taiwan's overall economic growth and high-tech industry development, and contributed outstandingly.
