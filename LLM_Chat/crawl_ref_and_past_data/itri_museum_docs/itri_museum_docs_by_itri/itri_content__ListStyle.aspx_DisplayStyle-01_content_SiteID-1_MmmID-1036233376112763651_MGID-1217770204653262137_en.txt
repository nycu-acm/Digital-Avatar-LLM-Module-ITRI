Technology introduction
Vehicle controller and battery management system testing equipment technology.
With the increasing number of automotive-specific microcontrollers (ECUs), the corresponding demand for automotive specifications is becoming more and more demanding.However, due to the complex development process and verification methods of related soft firmware, a large amount of capital is required, so my country has long relied on the supply of foreign businesses.
Features and innovation
In order to get rid of the constraints of foreign manufacturers, the Industrial Research Institute pioneered the development of "Car Control/Line Transmission Two-in-One Controller" and "AutoSAR Software" that meets automotive safety functions and environmental reliability certification.
Application and benefits
Six vehicle manufacturers have been successfully introduced, cooperated to carry commercially available vehicles, officially entering the mass production stage, assisting Taiwan's small and medium-sized enterprises in software development, transformation and upgrading.
