Technology introduction
The construction and industrialization of CUI corrosion-proof coating system test platform are promoted.
Under-insulation corrosion (CUI) is a difficult problem in petrochemical pipelines worldwide.In the past, domestic lacked independent CUI corrosion-proof coating research and development and testing and verification energy, making it difficult for industry operators to develop new CUI corrosion-proof coatings and evaluate the use of coating products suitable for field environments.
Features and innovation
Developed a low VOCs CUI corrosion-proof coating system that complies with ISO 12944 CX grade, with a 50% reduction in solvent content compared to commercially available products and a three to four times increase in corrosion resistance.
Simulate the CUI trigger mechanism, develop the first CUI corrosion environment test and verification platform in China, and import the CUI electrochemical monitoring module, which can instantly monitor the deterioration characteristics of CUI anti-corrosion coatings and quantitatively evaluate the performance advantages and disadvantages.
Application and benefits
Tutoring local paint manufacturers and petrochemical companies to invest in the development of independent anti-CUI corrosion-proof coating technology. Compared with general anti-corrosion coating products, the unit price has increased by two to four times, and the added value rate has increased by 30% to 50%.
Tutoring the domestic petrochemical group to use this verification platform to evaluate the performance advantages and disadvantages of many CUI corrosion-proof coatings and their applicable field environment.As a reference guide for the selection/maintenance of coating systems of pipeline maintenance units, the annual coating maintenance expenditure of plasticization was reduced by about 300 million yuan (original annual expenditure was about 2 billion yuan).
