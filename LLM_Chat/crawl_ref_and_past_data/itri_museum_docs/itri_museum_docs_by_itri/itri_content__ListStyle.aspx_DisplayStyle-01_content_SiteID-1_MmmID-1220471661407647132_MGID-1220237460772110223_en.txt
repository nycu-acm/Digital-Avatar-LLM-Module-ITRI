Technology introduction
Micro LED technology.
Micro LED has the characteristics of high brightness, high efficiency and fast reaction time, and has self-luminous display characteristics and no backlight is required.Therefore, its products have the advantages of energy saving, simple organization, small size and thinness. The Institute of Technology can provide customized services and solutions such as material/equipment verification, module trial production, etc. according to different specifications and needs.
The characteristics and advantages of Micro LED make it particularly suitable for unique markets that require high transparency, high brightness, high resolution, and low energy consumption in small pitch displays, transparent displays, amplified reality (AR/MR) displays, wearable devices, and on-board displays.The Institute of Technology has successfully developed key technologies for the above products, integrated Micro LEDs into glass/soft substrate/Si/PCB, and verified its feasibility to meet commercial needs.
Features and innovation
Through its own technological research and development energy, the Institute of Technology actively connects upstream and downstream manufacturers in the industry to jointly promote Micro LED application products. So far, it has integrated the Micro LED ultra-small pitch display industry chain, and has also invested in augmented reality (AR/MR), vehicle-mounted display application technologies, and at the same time established wafer-level optical technology to create a highly integrated micro-optical display system platform, targeting the mega-level cosmic business opportunities to accelerate the productization process of Micro LED, thereby promoting industrial development.In order to further promote industrial value-added, the Industrial Research Institute also deploys heterogeneous integration technology combining display and sensing functions to lay the foundation for differentiation of technology and products.
