Technology introduction
Galvanized metal non-toxic anti-rust technology.
Most solar power plants in Taiwan are located in coastal areas.The brackets are mostly made of traditional galvanized steel (ISO 9223 C5 or above), and they are severely corroded after 3 to 5 years of use. The rusted coating releases zinc oxidation fog that may harm the environment and marine organisms. The corrosion-proof coatings coated on the brackets contain heavy metals and bisphenol A, and in order to improve their adhesion capacity, sandblasting is required, which is time-consuming and labor-intensive.
Features and innovation
The Industrial Research Institute has developed "Galvanized Metal Non-toxic Anti-rust Technology". The coating does not contain eight heavy metal components such as zinc, chromium, and lead, and is free of bisphenol A. It is sandblasted and can be directly applied to the galvanized surface.It extends the consumption time of zinc layer by more than 3 times, greatly improving the performance and safety of the coating.
Application and benefits
After the rusted carbon steel is coated, it passes ASTM B117 continuous salt spray for 500 hours without red rust and the coating film has excellent corrosion resistance.The coating was tested for sunshine resistance for ASTM G154 UVB-3131,000 hours, and the gloss retention rate of the coating reached more than 95%.It has been used in Taiwan's 90MW coastal photoelectric and fishery symbiosis case.
