Technology introduction
Energy Asset Performance Management Program (POXA).
The global energy storage market size is estimated to reach 44.7 billion by 2025. POXA Energy independently develops an energy management system, uses AI to comprehensively quantify assets, maximizes energy storage benefits, and increases energy utilization benefits, becoming the best assistant for manufacturers' energy traders.
Features and innovation
1 minute 3 steps to fully master energy assets: SaaS service elastic architecture, can set up personalized energy asset dashboard, select instant information of power field equipment, and automatically generate analysis charts.
Optimize battery performance and reduce the number of uses: POXA Energy can optimize battery performance, extend battery life by 2 to 3 years, and monitor the site asset performance, and provide data analysis to improve asset performance.
Application and benefits
Solve problems such as high-cost maintenance of electricity users, industrial and commercial energy storage, and balance of production line feed rate.
