Rut-resistant asphalt concrete extends the life of the road by 8 times, 30% lower cost, 75% reduction in carbon
Rut-resistant asphalt concrete technology.
Trucks weighing 100 tons come in and out and go 24 hours a day.Many industries and factory roads in Taiwan are often trapped in the infinite cycle of "repair and repair, and replenish again". "Road level" can often only last for a while. The vehicle rolling, rainwater, and high temperatures all bring many challenges to maintaining the smoothness of the road.
More than four years ago, when Formosa Plastics Renwu Factory conducted an internal improvement case review, the road section of the factory not only had heavy vehicles frequently entered and exited, but also a "V-shaped large slewing place". The degree of truck rolling was particularly serious, and we actively sought improvement methods.At the end of 2019, the Institute of Technology was invited to participate in the Formosa Plastics Innovation Seminar, introducing innovative technologies for road material removal and reuse, and opening up the road of cooperation between the two sides.
The Institute of Technology and Research has actively developed resource recycling technology, replacing two recycled raw materials, 50% converter stone and 30% asphalt removal, replaced 80% natural sand and gravel, and combined with the asphalt regeneration agent developed by the Institute of Technology and Research, it is integrated into "rut-resistant asphalt concrete" and applied to the heavy vehicle entry and exit roads of Formosa Renwu Factory. The paving has been intact so far, extending the service life of the road by more than eight times.
In addition, "rut-resistant asphalt concrete" is also of substantial benefit to energy conservation and carbon reduction.Taking asphalt removal, which is one of the components of "rut-resistant asphalt concrete", as an example, as long as there is a pavement renovation project, such waste will be generated. Since it is difficult to dispose of and cannot be discarded at will, many asphalt factories have stacked more than 100,000 tons of asphalt removal, and the estimated total annual volume of Taiwan is 13 million tons. Using such raw materials not only reduces the cost of mining natural sand and gravel, but also reduces carbon by more than 75%, which is 30% lower than traditional construction costs, making it a model case of recycling and re-use of inorganic materials.
