Find the optimal parameters and process personnel quickly become experts
Written by/Chen Yufeng Photography/Xie Muyu
To make delicious bread, you must pay attention to everything from dough recipes, fermentation temperature and humidity to baking temperatures. The same is true for the production of chips in the epitaxy factory, except that the parameters that need to be set include temperature, pressure, flow rate, speed... up to hundreds of sets. How to find the "optimal recipe"?The "visual epitaxial process optimization system" of the Institute of Technology can allow process personnel to "see" the effect of parameter adjustment, quickly find the best process parameters, and shorten the product's time to market.
The research team of the Institute of Technology has developed the "Epigrade Process Parameter Optimization System", which can quickly find the optimized process parameters and perfectly solve the troubles of epigrade operators.
When designing epitaxy processes experimentally, you must find the optimal process parameters.Generally speaking, the experience of epitaxy process personnel can only rely on the experience of epitaxy process personnel. That is to say, the amount of experience of the person in charge will affect the length of the machine adjustment time and even affect the yield. This is a common problem faced by LED epitaxy operators. "In order to improve the competitiveness of LED epitaxy processes, we must start from this part. Through cross-field cooperation, the Institute of Technology Research has finally found a solution," said Fu Yigeng, manager of the Epitaxy Components Technical Department of the Institute of Electronics and Optoelectronics Systems of the Institute of Technology Research.
The solution is "visual epitaxial process optimization system".Unlike artificially necessary to repeatedly adjust parameters and test errors, the system imports the process parameter experimental database and the optimization results of simulation analysis into a huge database, and tailor-made to find the best parameter combination according to the finished product requirements.After importing the "Visual Epilogue Process Optimization System", the time for the epilogue factory to find all the optimized process parameters can be shortened from one week to two hours, and the parameter analysis accuracy has been increased from 92% to 95%, and the new product launch time can also be shortened from three months to one month.
Fu Yigeng gave a footnote to this system, "Using this system, epitaxial process operators can immediately transform into experienced experts."
Soft and hard integration and visualization to improve accuracy
It is worth mentioning that in addition to finding the optimized process parameters, this system can also "see" the changes under parameter adjustment."The visual design verification stage of the LED process is like the nuclear magnetic resonance imaging (MRI) in a hospital, allowing us to "see" the changes in the thermal and airflow layers in the MOCVD cavity," Wang Qingjun, deputy research and development director of the Institute of Mechanical and Electromechanical Systems of the Institute of Technology Research Institute, further explained. "This stage can simulate the environment of the epitaxial process, allowing staff to directly observe the dynamic flow field of high temperatures, improve the accuracy of numerical simulation and analysis technology, and thus make the analysis of epitaxial process and equipment parameter optimization software more accurate."
Take bread as an analogy. As for baking temperature alone, if the impact of the oven baking temperature setting on the dough can be simulated and the heat conduction and heat convection in the oven affect the dough expansion, the baker can adjust the heat and water content of the oven and the dough to bake the ideal bread.
"Soft and Hard Integration" is another feature of this system.Through parameter optimization, the integration of software and gas spray heads can further optimize the "organometal Chemical Vapor Deposition (MOCVD)" process and improve yield.Wang Qingjun pointed out that "MOCVD epitaxial process is the most difficult step in the LED production process. Because there are many variables, we reduce the difficulty through the two ways of "soft" and "hard". The former refers to the introduction of a parameter optimization system, and the latter is a gas spray head module combining the machine and the process." In this way, LED epitaxial process operators can not only quickly find the optimized parameters, but also improve the accuracy of the MOCVD process; equipment operators can greatly shorten the design and development time of new gas spray head modules, and can improve the machine production capacity and yield.
Surface reaction path decision-making technology: simplify the traditional
In addition, in the face of the difficulties brought about by the multiple physical and chemical coupling phenomena of MOCVD epitaxial process, the team of the Industrial Research Institute has innovatively developed the "Epilocifer Chemical Surface Reaction Path Decision Technology" to solve it.MOCVD epitaxial process has been developing for 50 years. There are only two companies in the world, Veeco and AIXTRON. It is because epitaxial process is a multiple physical and chemical coupling phenomenon, including three or five gases, high-temperature composite materials up to 1,000 degrees Celsius, fluid power, heat transfer and chemical reaction power, etc., which is too complex and has a very high entry threshold.
Wang Qingjun said that there are too many chemical reactions to consider in the MOCVD epitaxial process. The "Epilocid Chemical Surface Reaction Path Decision Technology" of the Institute of Technology can analyze and screen 100 complex chemical reaction formulas into more than 20 reaction formulas that truly dominate the reaction at different temperatures, which helps to quickly optimize the key epitaxial surface process.

The "Visualized Epilacera Process Optimization System" is suitable for two types of users. One is LED operators who want to develop new epitaxial processes or improve existing process parameters through this system; the other is equipment developers who want to improve equipment performance or use new spray heads to obtain higher production capacity.At present, this set of advanced software system technology has been used by domestic and foreign epitaxial process and equipment operators, and is negotiating cooperation with international MOCVD equipment manufacturers.
Wang Qingjun emphasized that this system is not limited to application in the field of LED. "This is a highly original digital decision-making technology. In addition to assisting LED operators in technology upgrades, it can also be applied to epitaxial processes in other fields in the future." These fields include epitaxial manufacturing of semiconductors, next-generation displays, solar photovoltaics, wireless communications, and high-efficiency power transistors and other products.
Looking ahead, based on the existing "visual epitaxial process optimization system", the Industrial Research Institute will also launch a new wave of cross-field cooperation to further deepen the application of AI in advanced manufacturing fields of intelligent analysis and optimization of thin film process, providing a stronger backing for Taiwan's manufacturing industry to move towards Industry 4.0.
