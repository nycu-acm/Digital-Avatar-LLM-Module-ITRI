The light of Taiwan!5G small base station smart network management technology of the Institute of Technology won the 2018 Small Base Station Forum Award

Date: 2018/06/15
The 5G small base station smart network management technology jointly developed by the Institute of Technology and Yuanchuan and Mingtai Technology recently won the JUDGES’CHOICE of the SCF Awards in the UK. The representative of Liu Jialong, manager of the Institute of Technology and Research Institute of Technology, received the award in the UK.
The 5G innovative technology of the Institute of Industrial Research has been recognized internationally!In response to the government's 5+2 industrial innovation plan, the Institute of Technology continues to develop key 5G technologies and applications.With the support of the Technology Project of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the "Self-Organizing Network (SON) Technology" jointly cooperated with ITRI, Yuanshi Telecom and Mingtai Technology, recently held the Global Telecom Industry Technology Technology Exhibition (SCWS World 2018) in London, UK, and won the JUDGES’CHOICE of the SCF Awards (JUDGES’CHOICE of the SCF Awards) awarded the JUDGES’CHOICE of the SCF Awards, and other international manufacturers including Nokia and SoftBank.
Small Cell Forum (SCF) is an important occasion to promote the exchange of standardized technology in the global telecommunications industry. Every year, the Small Cell Forum Awards are selected to make innovative breakthroughs or industry contributors to small base technology, and the Small Cell Forum Awards are awarded.Internationally renowned communication manufacturers such as Nokia, SoftBank, Cisco, Intel, Qualcomm, T-Mobile, Vodafone, Commscope and other units have won awards. ITRI's self-organizing network management technology participated in the Small Cell Forum Small Base Station Network Technology Competition (SCF Awards 2018) this year and won the jury special award, highlighting ITRI's technical achievements in the management of small base station network operations.
Que Zhike, director of the Information and Communications Research Institute of the Institute of Industrial Research, said that Zitong Institute continues to deepen the intelligent networking and maintenance automation technology of small base stations, and completes the cumbersome procedures for setting system parameters when starting the base station with the self-Configuration function (Self-Configuration), achieving the characteristics of small base stations with Plug & Play, greatly reducing the burden of manual setting of base station parameters.In the optimization of small-base station networks, wireless channel positioning technology is combined to quickly sense user distribution status, and optimize network coverage areas accordingly, so that small-base station networks have the ability to independently adjust and greatly improve network operation efficiency.
The Institute of Technology Research and Research emphasizes the dynamic optimization technology of the base station network, mainly users, and overcomes the problems of inaccurate positioning in basements or large shopping malls in traditional life due to poor GPS signals or no reception of messages.It is suitable for any mobile phone and supports various operating systems and versions, including iOS, Android or Windows phones; users do not need to install any additional apps on their phones, and do not need to deliberately change their mobile phone usage habits. As long as the mobile phone is connected to the Internet, the location of the mobile phone can be positioned through the wireless channel of a base station, and the indoor positioning accuracy can reach a centimeter level.
The Industrial Research Institute also collaborated with manufacturers Mingtai Technology and Yuanshi Telecom to conduct a technology display at SCWS World 2018, the annual event of the global Small Cell ecosystem. Through the on-site display of the integration of ITRI's self-organizing network management technology and positioning technology, attracting international manufacturers to pay attention to Taiwan's small-base platform network technology energy and application results, which will help expand international cooperation opportunities and business opportunities.
Li Zhongwang, chairman of Mingtai Technology, said that he is honored to work with the Institute of Technology and China Telecom to win the recognition of international reviews.Mingtai began to develop small base stations since the 3G era, and deepened its efforts in cloud access network (Cloud-RAN) in the 5G era. Cloud access network is an edge computing system composed of small base stations, switches and servers.Small base stations are not only access points for many connected devices, but also have the functions of basic edge computing. Whether they are equipped with telecommunications companies to strengthen signals to improve coverage or tailor-made networking for enterprises, they play a key role.
Rao Zhonghua, executive vice president of the China Telecom Network Technology Department, also pointed out that he is honored to participate in this R&D project. Small cell plays a very important role in the construction of 5G networks, and Small cell with SON technology can better highlight its advantages in vertical industrial applications. The project team has planned to introduce this R&D results into the "China Telecom National Team" project and implement it in the Internet of Vehicles application.
The cooperation between Industrial Research Institute, Mingtai Technology and Yuanchuan Telecom will apply the Industrial Research Institute's self-organized network management technology to the medical field.In large medical centers, medical equipment, patient medical records, inspection objects, etc. will change their placement due to medical procedures, and medical personnel and patients are busy moving in the hospital area, causing the location to be uncertain and difficult to find.The self-organizing network (SON) system combines wireless channel positioning technology to provide accurate indoor positioning functions, assist in positioning and mastering the location of medical objects and equipment, and instantly track the location of medical personnel or patients coming to the hospital, as well as control the medical campus for restricted area management and control to improve medical efficiency and quality.
