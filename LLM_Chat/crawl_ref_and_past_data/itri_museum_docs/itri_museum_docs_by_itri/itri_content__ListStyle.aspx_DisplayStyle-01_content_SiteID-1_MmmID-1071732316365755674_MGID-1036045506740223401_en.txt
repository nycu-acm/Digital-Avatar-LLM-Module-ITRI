Technology introduction
Next-generation semiconductor detection technology.
The advanced semiconductor process has reached less than 7 nanometers, and the process film thickness and key size are getting smaller and smaller, and the existing detection technology is facing detection limits.
Features and innovation
The Institute of Technology has developed X-ray metering technology, and through the X-ray sub-nanometer resolution capability, it has developed long-wavelength X-ray reflection technology to measure the ultra-thin sub-nanometer film thickness of micro-zone (~50 μm x 50 μm), with the minimum film thickness up to 1 nm; at the same time, it has developed penetrating small-angle X-ray scattering technology and signal enhancement modules to measure the key dimensions of the 7-nanometer, 5-nanometer and 3-nanometer processes online.
Application and benefits
The size of the key defective particles in the process has reached below 20 nm, and the quantity concentration is less than 103 particles/mL. However, there is a capability fault in the nanoparticle measurement technology in the solution.The Institute of Technology has developed a sample introduction system and signal enhancement module, combined with the measurement and analysis technology of trace metal particles in solution - single-particle inductively coupled plasma mass spectrometry (spICP-MS), which successfully breaks through the measurement technology fault, and pushes the international spICP-MS size detection limit to 4nm and the quantity concentration detection limit to 40 pixels/mL, which can meet the next generation of semiconductor detection needs.
