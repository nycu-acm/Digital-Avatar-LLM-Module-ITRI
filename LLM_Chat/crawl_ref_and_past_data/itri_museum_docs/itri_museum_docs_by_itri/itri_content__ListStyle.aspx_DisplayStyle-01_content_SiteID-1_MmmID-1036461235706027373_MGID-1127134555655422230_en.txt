It is the first and most indicative industry-university super alliance in the semiconductor field in my country. It has jointly formed an innovative industry-university cooperation mechanism with the industry, academia and the government, and has promoted: to assist the education through industry, education to promote production through education, and to cultivate high-quality R&D talents in my country's leading semiconductor technology as its top priority.
1. A non-profit association legal person composed of a group member of semiconductor companies and research units.
2. Promote industry-university cooperation in the semiconductor field and engage in research and development of pre-market competition in advance technology.
3. Emphasize the mechanism for cultivating supply and demand and industry-university cooperation with advanced R&D talents required by the semiconductor industry.
4. Build a super alliance with active interaction and deepening exchanges in the semiconductor field of my country.
5. Attach importance to important issues in my country's semiconductor industry and academic circles, and make suggestions to the government in a timely manner.
6. With advanced deployment strategies, my country's semiconductor technology will continue to be a powerful global partner.
