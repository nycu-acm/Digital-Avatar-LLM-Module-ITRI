Technology introduction
Methane conversion catalyst.
Methane is an important energy storage chemical that can be synthesized from carbon dioxide and hydrogen, and is an indispensable key technology for achieving the net zero carbon emission target.By establishing a high-performance carbon dioxide catalyst hydrogenation technology, the captured carbon dioxide is reacted with hydrogen and converted into methane, which can simultaneously meet the needs of zero emissions of carbon dioxide and convert energy storage chemicals in the process.
Features and innovation
We develop high-activity, high efficiency and low-temperature nickel-based methanating catalysts on our own, and use program system engineering (PSE) methods to establish methanating processes.
Application and benefits
Carbon dioxide converts methane into one of the important technologies for electric to gas (P2G) and gas to electricity (G2P).my country imports about 16.8 million tons of natural gas in a year, part of which is used for gas-fired power generation, and the main component of natural gas is methane. The methane produced by this technology can be used for domestic natural gas and partially replace imported natural gas.
