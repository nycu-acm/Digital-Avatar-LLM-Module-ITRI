Waste panel glass operators can recycle and reuse efficiently and develop output value of more than 120 million yuan
Recycled and reused by discarded LCD glass.
Taiwan is a world-renowned major development center for water hardware, such as door handles, faucets, nuts, and car parts. Water hardware products are widely used in daily life. In order to make these products shine with bright gloss, the process of "electroplating" is required. By soaking in metal ions in sequence and powering up, a metal film is produced on the surface of the plating parts, and the effects of antioxidant and anti-wear are achieved.
In order to assist the operator in recycling wastewater in the process, the Industrial Research Institute will abandon the waste LCD glass and perform nano-porous treatment, transform it into a heavy metal adsorbent, apply it to the water treatment system, and successfully introduce it to Jixiang Electroplating Company in Zhangbin Industrial Zone.When the process wastewater enters the system, the porous adsorbent can adsorb metals and impurities such as copper, nickel, chromium in the water, thereby obtaining clean water like tap water, which can be returned to the electroplating process and reused. The porous adsorbent can be recycled after adsorption and saturation.It is estimated that more than 300,000 metric tons of electroplating process wastewater can be recycled every year, reduce the production of one-third of the metal sludge, and develop a circular economy value of more than 120 million yuan. It is the best example of science and technology being implemented in the industry, taking into account both the economy and the environment.
Compared with the unintroduced electroplating plants, this technology saves water to 30 metric tons, which is equivalent to reducing the water consumption by 30% to 50%, and no chemical agents are added and no metal sludge is produced.If the two factories in the first and second phases of Zhangbin Metal Surface Zone are combined, the output value of more than 120 million yuan can be developed in the future.This is an important milestone for Taiwan to create a circular economy. It not only recycles precious water resources and also achieves high-value resource utilization, fully reflecting the spirit of the circular economy.
In the past, the treatment fee of 1 kilogram of metal sludge was nearly 25 yuan, and traditional sewage treatment equipment was often 10 million yuan. The relevant sensors and feeders must be repaired or replaced regularly to replace traditional sewage treatment with innovative recycling materials. By converting various costs, it can save about 70% to 80% of the cost. The most important thing is that the electroplating industry has solved the most headache-inducing process wastewater and metal sludge problems.
