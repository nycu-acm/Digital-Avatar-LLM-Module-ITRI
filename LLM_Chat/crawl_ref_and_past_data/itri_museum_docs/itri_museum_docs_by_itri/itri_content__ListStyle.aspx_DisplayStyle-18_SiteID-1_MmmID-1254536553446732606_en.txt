The "Sustainable Report" of the Institute of Industrial Technology is an annual publication, with Chinese and English versions, which mainly reveals the important achievements and performance of the hospital's sustainable field in the previous year, including: the basic introduction of this report, the management of sustainable major themes and the negotiation of interested parties, integrity governance, scientific and technological research and development, a happy workplace, green campus, social impact and appendix.
In view of the global sustainable development trend and the international community's eager demand for the disclosure of sustainable information, our court independently compiles the sustainable report in accordance with the following preparation principles to benefit reference by all walks of life and interested parties.
GRI Sustainability Reporting Standards 2021 Edition
The United Nations Global Compact (UNGC)
SASB Sustainability Accounting Standards Board Standard (SASB standard) Professional and Commercial Services Industry Standards
Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD)
