Academician Zheng Chonghua
Delta Electronics, founded by Chairman Zheng, is the world leader in switching power supply and ranks world-class leadership in many product fields. Chairman Zheng has won many awards such as "Most Revered Chinese Entrepreneur" and "Entrepreneur Most Admired Entrepreneur".Chairman Zheng has long cared about the development of Taiwan's ecological environment and social welfare, promoted "green buildings", advocated environmental protection and energy conservation, and while developing science and technology, it also took into account the sustainable development of the environment;established scientific and technological lectures and scholarships in many universities to cultivate outstanding R&D talents, and encouraged aspiring young people to dare to try new things, which is a model.
