Group photo of the 8th Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (108 years)
Time: October 15, 2018
Location: Conference Room 604, Hall 51, No. 195, Section 4, Zhongxing Road, Zhudong Town, Hsinchu County (Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research)

The 108th annual academician meeting of the Institute of Technology Research was held in the Zhongxing Academy of Technology Research. It was chaired by Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of the Zhirong Cultural and Education Foundation of the Consortium. Minister of Economics Shen Rongjin attended the meeting in person. The academicians attended the meeting include General Manager of Wanghong Electronics/founder and chairman of Xinquan Technology, Chen Xingshi, founder of Ronggang Group, Lin Benjian, Distinguished Professor of Tsinghua University, Yu Huanian, Academician of the Institute of Technology Research, Lin Genghua, Chairman of Mengli Automation, Wang Kanglong, Thor Session Professor of the University of California, Miao Fengqiang, Chairman of Lianhua Shentong Group, Yang Yumin, Chairman of AbGenomics New Drug Company in the United States, Zhang Anping, CEO of the Taiwan Clay Enterprise Group, Jiao Youjun, Chairman of Huabang Electronics Company, Yang Zhentong, President of Yali Electric Group, and Lin Jiannan, Chairman of Formosa Plastics Company.Lin Desheng, deputy director of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, Li Shiguang, chairman of the Industrial Research Institute, and Liu Wenxiong, president of the meeting also attended the meeting.
1. Suggestions for the 2030 Technical Blueprint and Institute of Technology
(I) Suggestions for the 2030 technical blueprint
The three major application areas, including smart life, healthy and happy life, and sustainable environment, are very important, but we must also pay attention to the development of common basic technologies and not neglect them.The common basic technology belongs to Hard core technology and requires long-term government support and takes into account both the technology in the application field.
Brain computing technologies such as the field of smart life should be closely integrated with education to cultivate better talents;the government should also educate the people with correct concepts, such as: cherishing the use of health care and medical resources, and the promotion of electric vehicles.
The field of health care can be combined with Taiwan’s good health insurance and medical systems to establish a world-class medical and health big data system as the basis for smart medical and regenerative medical care.
The setting of the development goals of air purification technology in a sustainable environment should be more actively set based on how well the earth needs to do, rather than just setting based on the improvement of the current situation.
The 2030 technical blueprint of the Institute of Technology Research and Research should be planned more in-depth and clearer in the resource investment and industrial profit models of each project;it should also consider making some project execution more concrete and setting milestones so that some profit benefits can be seen before 2030.
(II) Suggestions for the Institute of Technology Research
It is a very correct approach for the Industrial Research Institute to carry out the 2030 technical strategy and blueprint.It should continue to promote implementation to lead the development of Taiwan’s new industrial ecosystem (Ecosystem). The formation of ecosystems and the accumulation of domain knowledge are very important.Data collection and analysis energy should be strengthened to assist industrial development.
The Institute of Technology Research and Research has focused on hardware research and development in the past.In recent years, it has strengthened investment in serving science and technology. Talents must also simultaneously cultivate and change their thinking to change the idea of ​​mass production and reduce costs. This requires long-term investment in order to gradually achieve changes in organizational culture, create a new Institute of Technology Research and Research, and then assist the government in changing the industrial ecological environment.
The transformation of the Institute of Technology and Research also needs to think about the concerns of the funding sources. For the services needed by the government at present, we can think about helping Taiwan change the ecosystem needed by energy (production, transportation, consumption, technology and legal issues), environmental protection (circular economy ecology), and the aging society.
The European Environmental Protection Act has changed the industrial structure, such as banning fuel vehicles, banning disposable plastics and using recycled materials, etc. The Institute of Industrial Research can invest more to assist in the needs of related industries.
At present, a large number of renewable energy equipment (solar panels, fans) and energy storage equipment will soon face the problem of large-scale replacement. It is recommended that the Industrial Research Institute help establish a circular energy service industry and strive for international market opportunities.
The biomedical process needs to be fully automated, and the technology of the Institute of Technology can be invested in helping the development of the industry.
The competitiveness of many traditional small and medium-sized enterprises in Taiwan is mostly maintained by masters (such as molds, punching tools, knives, etc.). The Institute of Technology can assist in AI in order to preserve and inherit industrial technology.
2. Suggestions for government and industry development
Taiwan’s development of circular economy and smart long-term care requires a decentralized strategic thinking, starting from the on-site development of scientific and technological solutions, and then replicating it to the whole world; in the face of an elderly society, government policies and research institutes should establish several models that meet the real needs of society.
The government should establish waste treatment policies and recycling models, and the Institute of Technology can provide assistance from a strategic and technical perspective.
For Taiwan's industry to develop for a long time, it must be internationalized and use more international resources and talents; the practices of the Netherlands and Israel are examples of Taiwan's internationalization.
Artificial intelligence involves diversified applications and complexity, and is the key to launching various intelligent services. It can drive the development of a number of industries. The government needs to invest resources to stabilize the research and development of this project. Similar to our previous investment in semiconductor technology, the industry will become an important national economic foundation after it develops.
Internationalization of IP is an important model for Taiwan's economic development. This IP is not only a technology/patent, but also a business secret. Taiwan can use other countries to produce or manufacture critical necessary components to overcome tariff barriers.
The Institute of Technology Research and Research is an important R&D institution in Taiwan.In order to assist the development of Taiwan's service industry, all government departments have the responsibility to jointly support it.
