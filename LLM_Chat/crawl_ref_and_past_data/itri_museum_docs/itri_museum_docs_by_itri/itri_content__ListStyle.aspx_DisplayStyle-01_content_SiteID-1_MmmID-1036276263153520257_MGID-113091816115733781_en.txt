The Institute of Technology won the 2024 World Permanent Citizen Award and Talent Permanent Award double champion. Innovative technology promotes industry sustainability and people-oriented work to cultivate cross-domain talents

Date: 2024/09/18
The Institute of Technology has stood out from more than 200 Taiwan’s most indicative enterprises this year, and won the “World Sustainable Citizen Award—No. 1 in the Nonprofit Government Group” and the “World Talent Sustainable Award—No. 1 in the Nonprofit Government Group” for the first time. The award was presented by Minister of Labor He Peishan (left), and Liu Wenxiong (right), the Director of the Institute of Technology, received the award on behalf of the Institute of Technology.
On today (18th), the Institute of Industrial Research won the 2024 "Tianxia Permanent Citizen Award - No. 1 in the Government Group" and "Tianxia Talent Sustainable Award - No. 1 in the Government Group", which stood out from more than 200 of Taiwan's most indicative enterprises and institutions, and was also the first double champion of the award's participation in non-profit institutions. The World Sustainable Citizen Award is one of Taiwan’s largest and most indicative ESG awards.This award not only recognizes the efforts of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research in promoting corporate social responsibility and sustainable development, but also a high recognition of the Institute of Industrial Research in talent cultivation and social contributions.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that as the largest industrial R&D institution in Taiwan, the Institute of Technology Research, has made forward-looking R&D as its core value, and has drawn up the "2035 Technical Strategy and Blueprint" and actively created with the industry, so that the world can see Taiwan's R&D energy. At the same time, it focuses on organizational sustainable development. It builds a "sustainable development strategy blueprint" from six major aspects, including honest governance, scientific and technological R&D, happy workplace, green campus, social impact, and industrial development. Through the continuous compilation and release of sustainable reports every year, it continues to discuss with internal and external stakeholders, and work together with the value chain to exert its sustainable influence.
The "World Sustainable Citizen Award" has become an important indicator of corporate social responsibility and sustainable development for 18 years. The selected enterprises are all industrial models in terms of environment, society and governance (ESG). All enterprises participated in the past years.This year, the non-profit organization group was established. The Institute of Technology participated in the competition with three major projects: "social participation", "environmental sustainability" and "enterprise commitment". Among them, the selection criteria of the "World Sustainable Citizen Award-Non-Profit-Government Group" focused on the all-round actions of the above three aspects. The Institute of Technology was highly recognized by the jury for its outstanding results.
The Institute of Technology is not only committed to developing forward-looking technologies and integrating solutions with cross-fields, but also deepens technology across Taiwan to drive industrial upgrading.In the "social participation" part, for example, the introduction of "intelligent grape control light source system" and "agricultural surplus recycling technology" for Changhua winery to realize the agricultural circular economy and reduce carbon emissions for the industry.Promote scientific and technological public welfare, and promote warm application of technology with three major aspects, namely "technology application and service", "technology education promotion" and "enterprise volunteer", such as the development of "portable UVC LED mobile water module" to check the drinking water quality of residents in earthquake-stricken areas in Turkey.
In the "Environmental Sustainability" part, in addition to internally committed to energy conservation and carbon reduction, and using smart energy-saving technologies and efficient facilities, we will gradually achieve the goal of a net zero sustainable demonstration campus.Starting from 2021, the "Net Zero Sustainable Strategy Office" will be established to instigate the "Industry Net Zero Sustainable Transformation Plan", promote the all-round upgrade of sustainable technology within the hospital, and invest in green industry transformation from the inside to the outside; it will also actively exert its influence externally, and jointly develop innovative technologies to promote environmental sustainability and drive the industrial circular economy with enterprises; establish a net zero sustainable school to cultivate sustainable talents for the industry.
The "Talent Sustainable Award" is one of the important awards in the field of talent in China. It uses "Company Commitment" as the selection policy and aims to commend institutions with outstanding performance in the workplace, such as diversity and inclusiveness, salary incentives and benefits, talent investment and cultivation, physical and mental health promotion, and friendly home care and childbirth.The Institute of Technology and Research continues to optimize the organizational diversity, equality and inclusiveness, and provides a working environment that all ethnic groups and generations yearn for; design a complete salary and welfare system so that employees can also get full living security while focusing on their work; the long-term investment and cultivation of talents brings abundant innovative energy to various industries in Taiwan, and has also entered the international stage with dazzling achievements and won many international major awards; in order to balance the balance of physical and mental development of employees, it also plans long-distance hybrid flexible work and fully equipped action offices across Taiwan, providing flexible locations and working hours and psychological friendly services for colleagues to improve work and life balance, and has won many employer brand awards such as LinkedIn, Universum, and HR Asia. In addition to taking care of employees, it also benefits families of more than 6,000 employees, from getting married to settling down, from raising to nurturing, helping employees to set foot and live and work in peace and contentment.
As the largest industrial R&D institution in Taiwan, the Industrial Research Institute won the "World Sustainable Citizen Award - No. 1 in the Nonprofit Government Group" and the "World Talent Sustainable Award - No. 1 in the Nonprofit Government Group" for the first time this year.From the left, there are Wu Yingchun, chairman of Tianxia Magazine, Yan Bowen, executive director of the Tzu Chi Charity Foundation of Buddhism, He Peishan, Minister of Labor, Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Engineering Research, Wang Ruihui, legal person chairman of Chang Geng Medical Group, and Wu Wanyu, co-executive director of Tianxia Magazine.
