There is water and electricity. Fire sight light provides fire scene lighting
Written by/Chen Yufeng Photography by/Qiu Ruren
The "Fluid-driven Emergency Lighting" technology invested by the Institute of Technology and Research is to use the positional energy difference of the fluid to convert it into electrical energy. It is used in emergency lighting, and fire protection and disaster relief are the best application areas.Due to the strong water pressure of the fire water column, it can provide power for the fire sight lights, allowing firefighters to have sufficient lighting in the fire scene, which not only improves disaster relief efficiency, but also further ensures the safety of firefighters during disaster relief.
Affected by the movie scene, most people believe that the fire scene is not illuminated due to firelight. However, the actual fire scene has no electricity and is filled with black fires covered by thick black smoke. Therefore, without sufficient additional lighting, the fire fighting and disaster relief will increase greatly, which may even endanger the lives of firefighters.
"I have friends around me who are engaged in fire protection and disaster relief work. I see their hard work and have always wanted to do something for these heroes who have worked hard and made great contributions." This is the original intention of Liao Ronghuang, manager of the corporate service promotion office of the Green Energy and Environment Research Institute of Industrial Research Institute, to invest in the "fluid-driven emergency lighting" technology.
Symbiosis design with turbine power generation
Traditional fire lighting mostly uses headlights or chest lamps. These equipment are light but not bright enough. If a powerful flashlight is used, one person will be wasted, which will affect the allocation of firefighters on the disaster relief site. Therefore, the current fire relief lighting is not ideal for firefighters."I want to find a solution that can balance lightness, brightness and endurance." Liao Ronghuang further explained that the reason for lightness is that the firefighters had as much as 30 kilograms of equipment when entering the fire, and the new equipment could not add too much burden.
Under various considerations, Liao Ronghuang applied the turbine rotor power generation technology he had previously encountered to fire-field lighting solutions."We add a turbine generator to the fire sight, and use the fire water flow to rotate the turbine, which can power the LED lights attached to the fire sight. Through this symbiotic design, there is water and electricity. In addition, we use small lithium batteries to store electricity, so that we can still provide continuous lighting without the need for water jets."
Takes into account lightness, endurance and brightness
The fire sight light developed by the "Fluid Driven Emergency Lighting" of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute can have a brightness of more than 25,000 candlelight, which is 10 times that of general fire headlights and more than five times that of chest lamps. After continuous improvement, the weight of the sight light has dropped from the first generation of metal-made products and weighing three kilograms to the fourth generation sight light that is currently repurposed with heat-resistant plastic materials, with only 800 grams.It is also worth mentioning that the fire sight light developed by the Institute of Technology uses a quick-connect joint design and can be assembled in just 30 seconds. The joint can rotate and change the direction of the light at any time. It is also suitable for left-handed firefighters to hold it.
Liao Ronghuang also mentioned, "To promote it to firefighting units, cost is a very important consideration. Our fire sight light can be directly installed on existing fire sights without changing equipment, so it can increase the acceptance of users." At present, fire brigades in Tainan, Taipei, Keelung and other places have begun to adopt this product. The Institute of Technology and Research has actively made patent layouts. It is necessary to promote such good products to the world to provide sufficient lighting for high-tension fire scenes, so that firefighters can save more lives while also making themselves safer.
