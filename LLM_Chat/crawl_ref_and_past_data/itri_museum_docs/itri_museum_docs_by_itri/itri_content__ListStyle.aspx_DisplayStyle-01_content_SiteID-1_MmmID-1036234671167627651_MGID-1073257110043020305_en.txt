Academician profile
Academician Kong Xiangzhong
Academician Kong Xiangzhong is a professor of Bill Gates at Harvard University and president of Taiwan’s Artificial Intelligence School. He specializes in information science and engineering. The Systolic Array theory proposed in 1978 has had a revolutionary impact on computer science.Academician Kong assisted Taiwan’s various forward-looking plans for information and communications, bringing international scientific and technological trends back to Taiwan and promoting development. In recent years, he founded a Taiwan Artificial Intelligence School to cultivate more than 6,500 AI-related talents into Taiwan’s industry and injecting strong AI momentum into the industry.
With his curiosity and perseverance towards science and technology, Academician Kong has invested in the fields of information science and engineering for a long time, and his research fields include parallel algorithms, large-scale integrated circuit architecture design, mobile computing and computer networks.After obtaining a Ph.D. from Carnegie Melon University in 1974, he taught in the Department of Computer Science at Carnegie Melon University. In 1992, he transferred to Harvard University as a lecturer.In 1990 and 1993, he successively won awards such as Academician of the Central Academy of Engineering and Academician of the National Academy of Engineering.
Academician Kong, who has been living in the United States for a long time, is very concerned about the development of Taiwan's academic and technology, and has given direction suggestions on Taiwan's scientific and technological research and development. He frequently travels to and from Taiwan to the United States, guiding a number of scientific and technological projects for Taiwan, including digital Taiwan, mobile Taiwan, telecommunications national projects, and network communication national projects.In 2003, he served as a science and technology consultant of the Executive Yuan, promoting Taiwan's information industry. During his tenure, he led all the research and development projects in Taiwan's largest info and communication industry.
In 2017, as emerging technologies began to enter industrial applications in large quantities, Academician Kong felt that Taiwan's industry needed the driving force for innovation and transformation, so he formed a team with the academic and research community to work with the industry to create the "Taiwan Artificial Intelligence School" to assist Taiwan in cultivating AI technology talents, allowing Taiwan's industry to break through the old framework and create more value in flip-up and upgrade.
Academician Kong compared AI to fire and electricity, which is an important technology to lead the development of civilization. He used the "Taiwan Artificial Intelligence School" as a platform to continue to cultivate AI talents in the fields of industry, academia and research, encourage young people to develop the habit of deep thinking, and use AI to drive industrial transformation, create opportunities for the development of AI applications, and open up new ways for Taiwan's industry.
