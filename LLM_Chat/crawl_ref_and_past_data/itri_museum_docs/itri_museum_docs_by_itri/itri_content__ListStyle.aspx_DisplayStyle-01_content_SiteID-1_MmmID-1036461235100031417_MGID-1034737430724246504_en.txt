Silicon Photonic Integral Photoelectric System Measurement Laboratory.
The Silicon Photonic Integral Photoelectric System Measurement Laboratory provides an advanced integrated photoelectric component and system measurement platform, with industry-leading testing capabilities and is committed to promoting the development of next-generation high-speed optical communication technology, meeting the research and development needs of 1.6 Tbps and even ≧ 12.8 Tbps optical transceiver modules and components, and helping forward-looking photoelectric technology innovation breakthroughs.
This laboratory has a single-channel 224 Gbps PAM4 signal and a 67 GHz optical network analyzer, which can support high-frequency and high-speed optoelectronic module testing.In addition, it is equipped with 8-inch and 12-inch wafer-level probe holders and automated testing programs, which can quickly locate coupling light and conduct high-precision measurements to achieve full-wafer-level photoelectric component detection.
