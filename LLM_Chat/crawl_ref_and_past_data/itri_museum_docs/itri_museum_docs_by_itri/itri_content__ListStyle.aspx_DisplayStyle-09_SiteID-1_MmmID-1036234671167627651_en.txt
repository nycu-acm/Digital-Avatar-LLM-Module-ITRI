Academician profile
In order to commend individuals who have made outstanding contributions to industrial development and the improvement of people's living well-being in industrial technology, and to promote my country's scientific and technological development, a "ITRI Laureate" (hereinafter referred to as "Academician")" is specially set up. They must have experience in industrial management or scientific and technological research and development practices, have outstanding achievements in technological innovation and industrialization, and have made outstanding contributions to the national industrial development and the improvement of people's living well-being. Each elected academician will announce and award certificates for public praise in that year, and the academician of the Institute of Technology Research will be given a lifetime honorary title.
