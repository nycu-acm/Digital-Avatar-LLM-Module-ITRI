Early warning of equipment failure and reduce downtime losses
Written by/Lai Wanjing Photography/Huang Dingxiang
The semiconductor production line needs to operate continuously 24 hours a day. How to maintain the normal operation of the machine and the smooth production line is the key to ensuring yield.The Institute of Technology has innovatively developed semiconductor machine fault prediagnosis software, which can predict the health status of production line equipment in advance and reduce losses caused by no warning abnormalities.
The "machine fault prediagnosis software" used in semiconductor production lines injects new competitive energy into my country's semiconductor industry, which already has competitive advantages.
If a person only goes to a doctor when he is sick, it will not only have an impact on his physical health, but also cause a waste of medical resources. Therefore, preventive medicine emerged.Just like human health maintenance, mechanical equipment that must be continuously operated 24 hours a day. If a large amount of data can be analyzed, the industry's competitiveness can be improved. This is a very important part of the industry's move towards smart manufacturing.
In recent years, global manufacturing has been fierce competition.Under the challenges of shortage of professional human resources, large changes in market supply and demand, and customized customer demand, the global manufacturing industry is actively introducing huge amounts of data analysis technology to diagnose production line equipment and reduce the chance of sudden equipment failure. The "semiconductor machine fault prediagnosis software" developed by the Institute of Technology was born.
Machine fault prediagnosis effectively improves productivity
Lin Qunwei, manager of Juliang Information Technology Center of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, pointed out that according to IBM's research report, 83% of the information managers believe that equipment maintenance and overall asset analysis optimization are the main ways to enhance the competitiveness of enterprises. The introduction of machine fault prediagnosis technology benefits include: reaching 10 times the return on investment (ROI), reducing maintenance costs by 20%-25%, eliminating 70%-75% unanticipated crashes, and increasing productivity by 20%-25%. It can be seen that the stability and normality of factory production line equipment deeply affects the competitiveness of the manufacturing industry.
In view of this, the Industrial Research Institute began to invest in the research and development of "semiconductor machine fault prediagnosis software" since 2013. By collecting and analyzing machine data to monitor and evaluate the health status of equipment and its parts, predict the time point when the machine needs to be repaired early, and reduce the risk that the production line must stop due to sudden machine failure. At present, the "mechanical fault prediagnosis system" applied to semiconductor production lines has been developed, injecting new competitive energy into my country's semiconductor industry, which already has competitive advantages.
Lin Qunwei explained that "Semiconductor Machine Fault Prediagnosis Software" is a system platform based on artificial intelligence (AI) and machine learning. It analyzes the process data generated by the machine, conducts instant monitoring, prediction and presents it with visual data, so that production line managers can grasp the health status of the equipment.
Taking the epitaxial process machine on the production line of the optoelectronic factory as an example, this system can predict the failure probability and residual service life of the Mass Flow Controller (MFC) and Particle Filter (PF) of important parts, thereby determining the time period for parts replacement and maintenance.Lin Qunwei pointed out that by predicting that abnormal events are about to occur, it can be eliminated in advance, reducing the pressure and risks of repair time caused by emergencies, improving the work efficiency of production line engineers, allowing engineers to concentrate on innovative research and development, and creating more added value for the company.
Three key points to build a global machine fault prediagnosis system
"Semiconductor Machine Fault Prediagnosis Software" has been transferred to many domestic optoelectronic semiconductor manufacturers, and has made a big step forward for the domestic industry to move towards the era of smart manufacturing in Industry 4.0. This innovative R&D has also won international recognition and was shortlisted for the 2017 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards, and has three keys to success:
Key 1》Collection of the right data and extract key parameters from it
Since the data parameters of semiconductor production lines are as many as 400 or 500, it is the first level to develop this system to find parameters that truly affect the health of the machine.To this end, the Institute of Technology integrates the R&D team in the fields of optical, mechanical, electrical and information software, and after repeated verification and testing, it successfully finds key parameters that can reflect the health of the machine equipment.
Key 2》 Establishing a holistic learning prediction technology
After finding the key parameters that can reflect the health of the machine equipment, a data analysis model will be established.The research team broke away from the past using a single "hero" analysis algorithm, integrated dozens of advanced machine learning algorithms to establish a "holistic learning prediction and analysis model" to improve the accuracy of machine prediction and analysis.If the accuracy of machine prediction analysis is not high enough, and issuing false alarms will cause trouble to production line engineers and affect production line production efficiency. At present, many smart manufacturing prediction analysis technologies have such problems.The overall learning prediction and analysis technology developed by the Institute of Technology has an accuracy rate of more than 95%, reducing the occurrence of false alarms.
Key 3》 Establish data analysis criteria
It is not enough to improve the accuracy of the "semiconductor machine fault prediagnosis software". In order to import this system into more production lines in the manufacturing field, the research team of the Institute of Technology further established a set of data analysis guidelines so that when this system is actually imported and applied to other production lines, it can directly hit the core issues of the production line production link and quickly establish an analysis model.
"Semiconductor Machine Fault Prediagnosis Software" is based on artificial intelligence and machine learning, which can accurately predict device abnormalities and grasp device health status.
