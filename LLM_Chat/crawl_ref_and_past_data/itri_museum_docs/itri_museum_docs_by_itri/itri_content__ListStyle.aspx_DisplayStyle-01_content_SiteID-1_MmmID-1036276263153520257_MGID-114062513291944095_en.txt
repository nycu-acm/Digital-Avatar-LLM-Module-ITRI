Today (25th), the Institute of Technology announced the Elite Award of Industrial Research, known as the "Oscar Award of Industrial Research Institute", which includes two "Industrialization Contribution Awards" and four "Outstanding Research Awards" gold award technologies, spanning diverse fields such as advanced materials, smart manufacturing, building steel welding, infrastructure monitoring, AI chips and retail areas integration in people's livelihood. These technical achievements show that the Institute of Technology, driven by AI, echoes key trends such as global sustainable development and digital transformation, and is also actively Assist the industry towards sustainable high-value and intelligent upgrading, and comprehensively promote the transformation of Taiwan's industrial layout and enhance competitiveness. Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research, said that the two "Industrial Contribution Awards" that won the gold medal this year include "Smart Innovation in Business Services" and "Omena Diagnostic Technology and Application"; the four "Outstanding Research Awards" cover "ultra-low-power edge recognition technology", "Smart pipeline leakage auscultation system", "H-shaped steel structure laser welding technology" and "Ethylene polymerization high-value technology", which can be mainly divided into two major features: "AI diversified application" and "Environmental sustainable contribution". In AI diversified response In terms of application, the industrial R&D results cover the fields of semiconductor chips, smart manufacturing, infrastructure and smart retail; in terms of sustainable environmental contribution, there are specific results that assist the high-value transformation of the semiconductor industry, steel industry and petrochemical industry, echoing the development trend of global industries to accelerate digital transformation and supply chain upgrading, showing the market-oriented R&D of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, and in line with the planning direction of our institute's "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint". Industrialization contribution bonus medals: 1. "Driving commercial service innovation with smart technology: creating efficient and sustainable new retail services" The Circulation Smart Management Model Technology integrates AI, IoT, automation and computer vision to create a one-stop smart retail solution across operation planning, commodity circulation, marketing and distribution. With the "AI Solution Hub" as the core, it develops technologies such as AI site selection, automatic configuration of store layouts, digital marketing content generation, smart inventory and logistics forward warehouses, and assists more than 50 businessmen including the whole family and Meilianshe to improve efficiency and experience. For the Last Mile Logistics, it has launched cross-platform delivery integration, driving digital assistant and low-carbon circulation packaging system to promote the sustainable transformation of circulation. The technology has achieved revenue of more than 300 million yuan and has won many international recognitions such as the 2021 Edison Gold Medal. 2. "Omena Diagnostic Technology and Application Services" Production line AI prediagnosis expert This solution is the first domestic prediction and maintenance solution that combines AI algorithms with 30 years of mechanical data. It has three core capabilities: real-time monitoring, equipment life prediction (Error <10%), and fault diagnosis (accuracy rate >95%). It is like an all-weather AI monitoring engineer, assisting enterprises in early warning of abnormalities, reducing maintenance costs and shutdown risks. The technology has been applied to six major industries such as semiconductors and metal processing, shipped more than 1,500 sets and served more than 200 companies. The performance includes assisting vacuum equipment manufacturers to build smart early warning systems, successfully preventing wafer scrapping and revenue increase by more than 20%. It has also authorized domestic sensor industry operators to develop integrated solutions, enter the semiconductor and optoelectronic supply chain, obtain thousands of orders, and demonstrate the potential of AI pre-maintenance market. Outstanding research prize gold medals: 1. "Smart Pipe Network Leakage Auscultation System" The urban pipeline safety guardian smart pipeline leakage auscultation system can instantly analyze underground sound and vibration signals, quickly identify and accurately locate water leakage, with an accuracy rate of up to 98%, effectively reducing maintenance costs. The technology has cooperated with Taiwan Water Company to successfully locate 834 water leakage points, saving more than 10.94 million tons of water each year. The system has three major features: it can quickly identify, use AI acoustic analysis to instant detection; high accuracy rate, combined with deep learning and signal processing, with an accuracy rate of 98%; it is diverse in applications, and is also suitable for oil and gas pipeline monitoring in addition to water pipes. This technology has verified the effectiveness of oil and gas leakage positioning in Taiwan, and will conduct wide-area proof of concept tests to accelerate smart detection solutions for global water shortage and energy The application of the facility was implemented and won the 2025 Edison Award Gold Medal. 2. "Ultra-low-power edge recognition for the integration of soft and hard computing architectures" The new edge AI computing team has developed the world's most energy-efficient memory integrated computing (Computing-In-Memory; CIM) architecture AI chip "Ultra-low-power edge recognition system" to break through the bottleneck of AI computing energy consumption, and can operate stably at extremely low voltages of 0.3V, saving power by more than ten times. CIM technology can directly complete computing in memory, reduce data handling energy consumption, and support high parallel processing. The team has cooperated with ICs, sensors and semiconductor manufacturers to promote AI microcontrollers (Micro Control Unit; MCU), smart microphone and memory IP applications show high expansion and industrial potential. This chip is especially suitable for drones, robots and other fields that require immediate response and are limited in power. It is highly forward-looking and is expected to become a key core technology in the edge AI era. 3. "Ethylene precision polymerization high-value PE/POE technology" Strengthen the competitiveness of high-value ethylene materials in China, plastic made of ethylene is mostly used for people's livelihood purposes, and key applications such as green cables, robot parts and semiconductors still rely on imports. In order to improve the autonomy of ethylene-derived materials, the Industrial Research Institute has developed three groups of high-efficiency polymeric catalysts: 1. High-three-dimensional barrier chromium catalyst combined with chlorine-free assisted catalysts to increase the conversion rate of 1-hexene and 1-octene and reduce corrosion risks; 2. High-solubility metallocene and sulfonium metal auxiliary catalyst design can adjust POE density and elasticity and reduce metal residues; 3. Single point titanium It is a catalyst that can prepare low-winding, high molecular weight polyethylene, suitable for high-strength fiber development. The technology has cooperated with domestic petrochemical companies to accelerate local mass production and strengthen the supply chain and downstream competitiveness of ethylene high-value materials. 4. "Laser Welding Virtual and Real Integration Technology for Low-carbon Manufacturing of H-shaped Steel Structures" The steel structure is the core of factory and building construction, but traditional high-thermal welding relies on manpower and time-consuming and energy-consuming. The Industrial and Research Institute and Taiwan Lightmask jointly develop "H-shaped Steel Structure Low-carbon Laser Welding Virtual and Real Integration Technology", using Lei Replaces traditional welding, combining digital twins and intelligent sensing, to achieve fully automated, high-speed and low-carbon processes. Compared with traditional methods, production speed has increased by five times, cost has been reduced by at least one third, and carbon emissions have been reduced by 88%. The technology has mass-produced more than 2,000 metric tons of steel structures, and has won the silver medal of the Edison Award, which demonstrates the strong potential to promote the intelligent carbon reduction and low-carbon building materials transformation in the construction industry. This year, it won the silver medal of the 2025 Edison Award. "Driving commercial service innovation with smart technology: creating efficient and sustainable new retail services ”Won the Industrialization Contribution Award, and in terms of operation planning, inventory management and marketing attracting customers, AI has been introduced to improve efficiency. In the past four years, it has assisted domestic employees such as FamilyMart and Meilian Society.
