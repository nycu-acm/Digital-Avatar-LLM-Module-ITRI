Turn good technology into real value and make the industry shine
Written by Xu Shupei
Faced with the dual wave of global industrial transformation and net zero challenges, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research continues to drive industrial transformation through innovation and research and development.The Elite Award of Industrial Research, known as the "Oscar of the Institute of Industrial Research", has selected 2 industrialization contribution awards and 4 outstanding research awards this year, spanning the fields of materials, manufacturing, AI, petrochemicals and retail, injecting impetus into Taiwan's industry's future towards high-value and intelligence.This issue specially planned the special topic of the Engineering Research Elite Award, and deeply analyzed the efforts of the winning technology and R&D team.
The "Smart Innovation Technology for Business Services" which won the Gold Award for Industrialization Contribution is a one-stop AI smart retail solution. From store location selection, inventory scheduling, promotion to logistics scheduling, etc., it can assist operators in optimizing, allowing retail and logistics channels to improve operational efficiency and reduce scrapping and carbon emissions; another technology that won the gold award is "Obsolute Diagnostic Technology to Realize Prediction and Maintenance Technology and Application". Through AI combined with vibration signal analysis, the research team established three modules of health evaluation, life prediction and fault diagnosis. It has introduced the manufacturing industry with slalom equipment intensive, successfully improving equipment productivity and reducing maintenance costs. It can be regarded as a powerful engine to assist traditional factories in crossing digitalization.
In addition, the four technologies awarded the Outstanding Research Award include "H-shaped steel laser welding innovative technology", creating a 10,000-watt laser welding technology used in large steel structures, integrating three major features, including digital twin simulation, smart bead tracking and low-oxygen atmosphere control. It not only reduces process energy consumption by one-third and reduces carbon emissions by 88%, but also sets the world's first laser welding standard for steel structure manufacturing; "AI chip with soft and hard integration and power saving of more than 10 times" uses the "memory in-body computing" (CIM) architecture, and is equipped with extremely low voltage and offset compensation design to successfully develop a world-record energy-efficient AI chip, which is significantly surpassing similar technologies.
In the field of materials, "ethylene precision polymerization technology" reverses the fate of Taiwan's high-end polyolefin materials that have been relied on imported for many years. Through single-catalyst technology, it can produce clean UPE, elastic and adjustable POE, and high-value polymers that can be applied to cables and solar materials to meet the industry's high-performance and low-carbon needs; the "Smart Pipe Network Leakage Auscultation System" successfully transforms the experience of the older generation of listening masters into AI models through 210,000 audio data and actual test verification, and applies it to various pipeline leak detection and fault warnings such as tap water and industrial gases, and is successfully exported to Southeast Asia and stand on the international stage.
Behind these technologies is a group of developers who make impossible possible.They proved with their actual results that innovation is not a slogan, but implementation and industrialization, turning good technology into real value, and truly letting Taiwan's industry shine in the world.
