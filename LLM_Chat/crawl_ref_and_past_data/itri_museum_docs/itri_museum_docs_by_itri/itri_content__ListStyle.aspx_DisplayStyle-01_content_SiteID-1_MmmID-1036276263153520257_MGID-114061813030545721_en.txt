Industrial Research Institute innovative display technology helps Daqing seize new business opportunities, promote the implementation of AI virtual and real integration technology, and expand the upgrading of qile, retail and transportation display

Date: 2025/06/18
With the support of the Industrial Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics, the Industrial Research Institute announced that it would work with Daqing, a subsidiary of AUO Optoelectronics, to develop AI virtual and real integration technology and emerging display products.From the left picture, Ke Furen, CEO and General Manager of AUO Optoelectronics, Guo Zhaozhong, Director of the Industrial Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics, and Zhang Shijie, Deputy General Manager of the Institute of Electronics and Optoelectronics Systems Research.
In order to promote the digital transformation and high-value application of the display industry, the Industrial Research Institute announced that it will work with Daqing, a subsidiary of the leading supplier of domestic display solutions, to use the AI ​​virtual and real integration technology subsidized by the Industrial Research Institute of the Ministry of Economics to develop AI virtual and real technology to develop emerging display products including: smart entertainment, smart retail and smart mobile commercial application situations, create a differentiated market, and create a new value experience beyond virtual reality VR.
The Industrial Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs stated that AI virtual and real integration technology is accelerating the Taiwan display industry toward high value and intelligence, especially in smart retail, smart transportation and immersive entertainment and other fields, combining AI's transparent and interactive display applications with rapid growth. Taiwan has a complete display supply chain.The Industrial Technology Department has assisted the Industrial Research Institute in developing integrated technologies such as virtual and real integration and smart display through special science and technology projects, and has promoted the transformation of the industry from manufacturing-oriented to system integration and application service orientation. This time, by integrating advanced display technology and software and hardware application services, it has enhanced the added value of products and created new momentum for the upgrading of the display industry.
Zhang Shijie, director of the Institute of Electronics and Optoelectronics Systems of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that in order to assist the industry in developing smart display solutions and expanding application fields, the Institute of Industrial Research Institute has long invested in scientific and technological research and development and cross-domain innovation, successfully integrating smart display, AI identification and human-computer interaction, and developing AI virtual and real integration technology.In terms of application results, the Industrial Research Institute has launched the "IV AI Fish Tank", equipped with the world's first transparent display virtual and real fusion system. Combined with cameras and AI line of sight recognition technology, it can accurately detect the direction of tourists' line of sight and the location of aquatic organisms, and present corresponding information on transparent displays in real time. It has also won the "CES 2023 Innovation Award" in the United States and was applied in the National Museum of Marine Science and Technology.In addition, it has also cooperated with AUO Optoelectronics to develop the "AI Smart Window Screen Guide System", imported the Kaohsiung Yawan Smart Ferry, converting traditional boat windows into transparent Micro LED displays, combining GPS positioning and passenger sight tracking, providing instant guidance on attractions along Kaohsiung Port.AUO Optoelectronics is a world-leading display solution supplier with deep panel R&D and manufacturing capabilities. This time, it will work with its subsidiary Daqing to strengthen the technological and market advantages of both parties, give full play to the benefits of 1+1 than 2, and create new display business opportunities.
Ke Furen, CEO and General Manager of AUO Optoelectronics, said that Daqing focuses on next-generation display and smart integration applications, has the capabilities of system integration, optoelectronic design and precision manufacturing, and is committed to creating high-value-added display solutions.As display applications continue to develop towards intelligence, immersiveness and field-oriented development, the development of "AI virtual and real integration technology" through the Industrial Research Institute will help accelerate the commercialization of technology, develop more innovative application situations, and thus enhance corporate competitiveness and revenue growth.Daqing actively builds smart display solutions based on deep display technology, expands to smart retail, enterprises and education, medical care and other industries, promotes a new blue ocean of smart field application, strengthens interactive experiences in diverse scenarios and actively expands overseas markets.
"AI fusion technology of virtual and real" has three major features: 1. Image recognition and sensing technology: Capture environmental images through the lens, and the AI ​​system can instantly identify people, objects and field information, and perform intelligent analysis and interactive applications.2. High-definition transparent projection module: The application of a transparent projection display solution with high penetration ensures that the background is clear and visible, allowing the virtual images to naturally blend into the real scene, and enhance the visual immersion.3. Interactive system for virtual and real integration: combining AI and image sensing to achieve the integration of virtual and real on transparent display, identify user actions and respond instantly, and create a new human-computer interactive experience.
Faced with the challenges and opportunities of the new era, the Institute of Technology will continue to be market demand-oriented, and based on the 2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint, focusing on four major application areas: "smart life", "healthy and happy life", "sustainable environment" and "resilient society", and promote industrial transformation and innovation through "smart enabling technology".In the field of "smart life", the Institute of Technology will work with industry partners to jointly develop and apply technology according to the actual needs of society and the market, hoping to bring new breakthroughs to Taiwan's science and technology industry, improving human life well-being, and creating a better future.
Through AI virtual and real integration technology, the Institute of Technology and Research has joined hands with AUO Optoelectronics to create the "AI Smart Environment Guide System" and is applied to the black bear area of ​​Kaohsiung Shoushan Zoo. Through the transparent screen that is rotating and lifting, tourists can observe the black bear and its living area.When tourists point the screen to the black bear or its environment, the system will automatically display the knowledge introduction and life highlights of the black bear in AR mode, allowing every tourist to observe the black bear's behavior up close.
