Academician Jiang Shangyi
Dr. Jiang Shangyi is the chairman of Taiwan Integrated Circuit Manufacturing Co., Ltd., attends the board of directors and holds consulting work.In 1997, Consul Jiang joined TSMC as Vice President of R&D, established a world-class semiconductor R&D team, and led the team to successfully develop and complete advanced processes in various generations such as 0.25, 0.18, 0.15, 0.13 microns, 90, 40 nanometers, and other advanced processes. The results were remarkable and made outstanding contributions to TSMC's becoming the world's largest wafer foundry manufacturer.In 2001, Consultant Jiang was awarded the "Asian Star" for the 50 most important innovators selected by American Business Weekly magazine; in 2003, he won the "Outstanding Science and Technology Talent Award" of the Executive Yuan; in 2013, he was awarded the "ERSO Award" of the Pan Wenyuan Cultural Education Foundation; in 2014, he won the Ernst Weber Managerial Leadership Award from the International Society of Electrical and Electronic Engineers (IEEE), demonstrating his excellent leadership skills.
