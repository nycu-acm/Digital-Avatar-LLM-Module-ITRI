Technology introduction
Fashion AI - Recommended technology for personalized style dressing in fashion e-commerce.
The epidemic has greatly affected people's consumption behavior, including clothing and accessories products that must be tried on and purchased in physical stores in the past. The proportion of consumers who switch to online purchases is also increasing. "Fashion AI Fashion E-commerce Personalized Style Dressing Recommendation Technology" combines artificial intelligence, based on global fashion clothing styles, clothing feature marking technology and customer browsing preference records, consumers can get personalized dressing suggestions like store clerks through online interfaces, improve customer purchasing experience, and expand sales of fashion e-commerce.
Features and innovation
Common recommendation methods for fashion e-commerce platforms include "User-based Recommendation" based on consumer procurement history based on similar conditions and "Content-based Recommendation" based on product description keywords, both of which have insufficient standardization and poor recommendation results.The breakthrough of "Fashion AI fashion e-commerce personalized style dressing recommendation technology" is to simulate the shopping guide logic of clothing store clerks by "through AI deep learning of clothing image features" to achieve humanized recommendation effect:
Similar recommendations:
According to the similarity of clothing products' appearance attributes, we provide similar product recommendation services to solve the problem of difficult screening of clothing products.
Recommended outfits:
Customers choose corresponding body templates and create digital clones to experience products such as clothing that emphasize vision and matching.
Agile recommendations:
Analyze large e-commerce platforms, use the product recommendation results established by huge amounts of products and people, and explore product recommendation rules and combinations.
Market Analysis:
Search and climb the products and sales information of domestic and foreign comprehensive and clothing e-commerce platforms, establish a clothing product library to analyze the composition and matching combination of various regions/platform products, and provide trend information.
Application and benefits
"Fashion AI Fashion E-commerce Personalized Style Dressing Recommendation Technology" is suitable for fashion clothing e-commerce, which can predict consumer preferences and accurately recommend products, achieving more accurate and intelligent clothing shopping guides.It can be easier for consumers to find the products they want and improve customer experience; for e-commerce companies, in addition to increasing turnover, customer statement rate, and repurchase rate, it also helps supply chain management and cost control.
Wear recommendation technology can also be applied to physical clothing stores, combining with an immersive environment to optimize the fitting experience, thereby improving customers' purchase rate.
