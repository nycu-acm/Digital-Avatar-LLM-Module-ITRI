About the Production Service Center
The industrial service center is located in Taiwan. Its center is positioned as an ITRI Outreach Arm, an industrial policy driver, and an ITRI Business Force, providing a full-service solution to the government and enterprises.
Vision and business axis
With the competitiveness of "technology management and market development" in Taiwan's industrial innovation, entrepreneurship and transformation and upgrading, it has the vision to enhance the value of corporate business operations, and focuses on the three main business axes: the operation of emerging industries, the operation of professional fields, and the operation of industry-creation ecosystem.Assist customers to grasp the needs and opportunities with economic driving force and become a solid business partner for customers to create value together.
Emerging industry operations
The Industry Service Center is committed to cooperating with national industrial policies and promoting the development of emerging industries such as net zero perpetual, digital value-added, and space economy.Provide an overall net zero emission solution with innovative technology and services, realize the ESG vision and successful low-carbon transformation.In addition to assisting enterprises in reducing carbon emissions and strengthening competitiveness, we will also jointly plan the blueprint for the development of green gold business with enterprises, conduct new green gold businesses such as green gold investment evaluation, trial production and factory building application system integration.Use cross-domain innovation and value-added technology to develop cross-industry transformation service platforms.Assist Taiwan’s ICT industry to lay out the next generation satellite communication supply system, form a national team of low-orbit satellites, and promote international satellite operators to set up strongholds in Taiwan, and connect with the international supply chain to achieve space industrialization.
Professional field management
The Production and Service Center has 15 professional fields with industrial themes in Taiwan.We will accelerate the investment and development of diversified enterprises in diversified business models with magnetic absorption effect and improve operational value.
Production and creation ecological management
Build an ecosystem of industry-creation cooperation between enterprises and new innovations, support the new innovation team to connect domestic and foreign resources and the technical energy of our institute to develop towards the global cause, and assist enterprises in the exploration of innovative solutions at home and abroad, and implement value co-creation.Assist small and medium-sized enterprises in linking resources such as production, education and research, promote cross-domain innovative cooperation to develop new business models and markets, and create new technological value again.
