The Trusted AI Seminar of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research will be held in 2022. Gathering heavyweight speakers at home and abroad to discuss AI governance

Date: 2022/07/20
The Institute of Technology will hold the first "2022 Trusted AI International Symposium" online on August 2, and will invite heavyweight VIPs at home and abroad to share the development trends of global trusted AI, discuss domestic and foreign experiences, and jointly discuss the development blueprint for Taiwan's integration with the global trusted AI ecosystem.
Have you ever used web pages to watch news, and you are always recommended by AI algorithms to similar news?AI artificial intelligence is gradually permeating people's lives.How to ensure the trustworthiness of AI or the possible security risks, etc., "AI governance" has become a topic that global democratic countries and international organizations attach great importance to.Under the guidance of the Ministry of Economics and Industry Bureau, the Institute of Technology will hold the first "2022 Trusted AI International Symposium" online on August 2, inviting heavyweight guests at home and abroad, including experts and scholars from the National Institute of Standards and Technology (NIST), Columbia University, Taiwan AI Laboratory, ASCHEU, etc. to share the development trends of trusted AI, discuss domestic and foreign experiences, and jointly discuss the development blueprint of Taiwan's integration with the global trusted AI ecosystem.
Faced with the gradual maturity and widespread application of AI technology, countries are gradually paying attention to the governance of AI.In order to achieve the long-term goal of trustworthy AI, global experts in the field of AI have begun to formulate AI-related specifications and systematically evaluate the effectiveness and risks of AI, and must break the black box of AI algorithms brought by deep learning and machine learning.In order to keep Taiwan's technology industry in line with international standards, the "2022 Trusted AI International Symposium" is the latest AI governance information sharing platform, which will have 5 master-level speeches, including AI risk management, creating AI systems that can be trusted by both identification and interpretation, joint learning technology that breaks data barriers and takes into account privacy, feature analysis for AI ethics, the current status of artificial intelligence development and future prospects in Taiwan.
This invitation covers key areas of government units, software innovation, technology manufacturing, chip design, and academic and research institutions at home and abroad. For example, Elham Tabassi, chief of staff of the Information Technology Laboratory of the National Institute of Standards and Technology, will share how to manage the risks of individuals, organizations and society related to AI; Jeannette M, Vice President of Columbia University in the United States. Wing will explore how AI can help humans while avoiding the risks it brings. China also invites experts such as Tang Feng, Executive Yuan Political Commissar, Lu Zhenghua, Director of the Ministry of Economics and Industry Bureau, Li Yujie, Deputy Executive Secretary of the Office of the Science and Technology Association of the Executive Yuan, Xu Yongzhen, Professor of Taiwan University, Du Yijin, founder of Taiwan AI Laboratory, Li Weibin, CEO of Hon Hai Research Institute, Zhou Shangwei, Director of the National Weights and Standards Laboratory and CEO of the Measuring Center of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, and Hua Kailong, Deputy Director of the Institute of Technology and Technology, to share the development trends of trustworthy AI, and discuss domestic and foreign experiences, and jointly discuss the development blueprint for Taiwan's integration into the global trustworthy AI ecosystem.All walks of life are welcome to sign up, registration website:
