Academician Liu Kezhen
Academician Liu Kezhen is the chairman of Advantech Co., Ltd., and has been deeply engaged in the industrial computer field for more than 40 years. Through continuous development of innovation and application, Advantech has led Advantech to become the world's largest industrial computer leader. He has also been awarded the well-known brand appraisal company Interbrand and has been selected as the top five in Taiwan's top 20 international brands.In recent years, in view of the vigorous development of the global Internet of Things, Advantech has led Advantech to fully embrace the new era of the Internet of Things, committed to promoting all-round integrated solutions of smart cities and Industry 4.0, and deepening the application of related vertical industries, making Advantech the most critical and influential global enterprise in the Internet of Things industry.
Academician Liu graduated from the Department of Telecommunications Engineering of National Jiaotong University and worked as a business engineer in the Instrument Division of the Taiwan Branch of the American Business Network.In the era when personal computers were just emerging, seeing the market demand for industrial automation, 29-year-old Academician Liu and two colleagues jointly founded Advantech Company and invested in the field of industrial computers to provide integrated industrial computer platforms and customized products and services.Practicing the hedgehog principle, Academician Liu proposed a unique three circles of hedgehog for Advantech, including vision, growth model and focus on goals, that is, embracing the vision of "the promoter of the intelligent earth", starting from the spirit of altruism, focusing on decent management, taking the pursuit of top-notch as a strategy, committed to replicating the successful experience of altruistic concepts, developing industrial clusters and gathering benefits, and aiming to establish a virtuous causal cycle with society, focusing on becoming an outstanding industrial leader, and sharing good fortune with society.
Adhering to the concept of taking it from society, giving back to society, and implementing the altruistic concept, Academician Liu established the Advantech Foundation to create better learning experiences and benefits for students; through promoting various projects such as "Innovation and R&D Internet of Things", "Sustainable Innovation Education", "Humanistic Aesthetic Literacy" and "Particular Country Charity Care", we will cultivate future talents; among them, the "TiC100" innovation competition held since 1998 attracts hundreds of teams and thousands of people to participate every year, which not only inspires the innovation and entrepreneurship atmosphere of Taiwanese students, but also becomes an important platform for industry-university cooperation and creates value for society and enterprises.
