106 Annual "Academician of the Institute of Technology" Conference
Group photo of the 6th Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (106 years)
Time: November 1, 2016
Location: Conference Room 604, Hall 51, No. 195, Section 4, Zhongxing Road, Zhudong Town, Hsinchu County (Zhongxing Institute of Technology Research)
Chairman: Academician Shi Zhenrong
The 106th Annual Academicians Conference of the Institute of Industrial Research was held in the Zhongxing Academy of Industrial Research, and was chaired by Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of Zhirong Group. The academicians attended include Asus Chairman Shi Chongtang, Founder and Honorary Chairman Zheng Chonghua, Chairman and General Manager of Changchun Petrochemical, Cai Mingjie, Chairman of MediaTek, Li Bingjie, Chairman of Jingdian, Lin Baili, Chairman of Guangda, General Manager of Wanghong Electronics/Xinquan Technology, Founder and Chairman of Chairman of Ronggang Materials Technology Company, Chen Xing Shi, former deputy general manager of TSMC R&D and academician of China Research Institute Lin Benjian, vice president of Changchun Group General Management Office Chen Xianzhang, honorary chairman of the Foresight Technical Steering Committee of the Institute of Engineering Research Institute Yu Huanian, academician of China Research Institute Lin Genghua, former consultant of TSMC Chairman Jiang Shangyi, TSMC Outstanding Professor Hu Zhengming, Chairman of the University of California TSMC Sun Hong, Chairman of Mengli Automation Shi Qintai, professor of National Tsinghua University, founder of Changxing Materials Industry Gao Yingshi, chairman of China-US Silicon Crystal Lu Mingguang and other nineteen others.In addition, the attendees included Li Shiguang, Chairman of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, Liu Zhongming, Vice Presidents Liu Junting and Zhang Peiren, and two VIPs, Vice Chairman of Changxing Materials Industry, Xiao Cifei, Technical Director and Deputy General Manager Zhang Jiayuan.
Meeting conclusion focus
The Industrial Revolution established the extension of human physical strength, and the rise of artificial intelligence confirmed the extension of human intelligence. Due to the breakthroughs in probability and statistical models, coupled with big data and powerful computer/quantum computing capabilities, deep learning has made great progress in the fields of clear application; the Institute of Technology should develop from Taiwan's core strength (hardware), combine artificial intelligence technology with appropriate industrial application professional fields to innovate, and can prioritize smart health, medical care and smart manufacturing.
Smart health and medical care should improve the efficiency of the medical system, reduce medical expenses, and make the quality of life of the whole people better; it should be promoted by national policies, loosened laws and regulations, and corporate investment to assist industrial development.
When Taiwan's industry transforms into smart manufacturing, it is necessary to first solve the data output on the machine and equipment side and analyze big data platforms. The Industrial Research Institute can assist in the development of the industry more.
The Institute of Technology Research can select several major demand projects for digital transformation of traditional industries, leverage the energy of legal person research institutions in specific fields, and jointly develop together, such as the experience of the Digital Service Innovation Research Institute of Zi Ce Society and the Service Science Center of the Institute of Technology Research Institute.
Industry 4.0 should be dominated and invested by the industry, and jointly promoted through the industry, official, academic and research platform.The government supports the promotion of the Industry 4.0 service team to establish an ecosystem for related service industries.
It is recommended that the government amend the laws so that foreign talents (students) can stay in the country for a long time to assist in the development of the industry.
The Ministry of Science and Technology and the Ministry of Education should avoid excessive concentration of artificial intelligence and big data in resources, resulting in insufficient cultivation of talents in other industries and imbalance in supply and demand.
The government must have encouragement policy investments to promote digital transformation of the industry.
Industrial development should aim at the international market and take the Taiwan market as the experimental field.
