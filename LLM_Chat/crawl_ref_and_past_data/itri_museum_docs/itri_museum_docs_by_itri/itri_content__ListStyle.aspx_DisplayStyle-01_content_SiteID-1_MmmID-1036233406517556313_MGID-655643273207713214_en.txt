About Smart Sensing and Systems Technology Center
The Intelligent Sensing and Systems Science and Technology Center of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute was established in January 2016, and was promoted to the Director of the New Establishment Center by Dr. Zhu Junxun, Deputy Executive Director of the South Branch of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute.The Microsystems Center has accumulated many years of micro-electromechanical technology research and development results and energy, such as micro-electromechanical process integration, component and module design, and technical integration of smart/biological micro-systems, and is used in fields such as Smart Sensor, Micro-Systems and Machine Intelligent.
In response to the global trend of smart sensing Internet of Things and smart manufacturing, the Micro System Center will take smart micro systems applied to "smart manufacturing" and "sensing Internet of Things" as the R&D target, quickly combine the R&D resources of the science and technology center, legal persons and academic circles, link the industry and international R&D energy, and develop key technologies for integrating smart sensing and micro systems into the application of key technologies.The central task in the future will take the goal of localizing key components, self-owned key technologies, and establishing industrial settlements and ecosystems as the goal, and play the role of the promoter of Taiwan's smart microsystem industry, and then become a world-class smart microsystem technology research and development center.
The research and development of smart microsystem technology applied to "smart manufacturing" and "perceptual Internet of Things". The research and development direction and main services of the Smart Microsystem Technology Center are:
Machine Intelligence, integrated application of key industrial sensing components/modules and systems.
Smart life/smart care and perception Internet of things applications (IoT Service), MEMS smart sensor, multi-sensor fusion and system integration applications.
The open rapid trial production platform for smart sensing modules (MEMS Fab.), providing mass production services for microelectromechanical components, CMOS/MEMS manufacturing and sealing.
