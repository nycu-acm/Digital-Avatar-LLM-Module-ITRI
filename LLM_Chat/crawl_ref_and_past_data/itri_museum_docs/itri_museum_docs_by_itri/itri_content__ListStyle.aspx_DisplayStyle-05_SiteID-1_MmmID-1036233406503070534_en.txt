The Industrial Technology Research Institute is an internationally-level applied research institution with 6,000 R&D pioneers. Its mission is to drive industrial development, create economic value, and enhance social welfare.
The Industrial Technology Research Institute is an internationally-level applied research institution with 6,500 pioneers. Its mission is to drive industrial development, create economic value, and enhance social welfare.Since its establishment in 1973, it has taken the lead in investing in the research and development of integrated circuits and fostering emerging technology industries;it has accumulated more than 30,000 patents and has been newly created and cultivated, including listed cabinet companies such as TSMC, UMC, Taiwan Optoelectronics, Jingyuan Optoelectronics, Mengli Automation, and Taiwan Shengmai, driving waves of industrial development.
2035 Technical Strategy and Blueprint
When digital technology changes the industrial structure and lifestyle, the population structure affects the demand for productivity and elderly care, climate change brings opportunities and challenges for net zero emissions in 2050, and industrial and social resilience has become key trends in the future national and economic development, the Institute of Technology and Research will integrate cross-domain solutions to accelerate the momentum of the industry forward, draw up the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint", focus on the research and development directions of four major application areas: "smart life", "healthy and happy life", "sustainable environment", and "resilient society", and develop "smart enabling technology" to promote application fields.Use technological innovation to transform life, take market demand as the orientation, develop solutions, and create new markets to seek the welfare of human society and lead the industrial society toward a better future.
Smart life
Create a happy new life with smart technology, and create a more efficient and profound life experience for users through the connection, customization, creation and interaction of space.The Institute of Technology has developed three major sub-fields, including "human-computer interaction and service", "independent mobile system", and "smart consumption and operation services", and has deepened the development of key projects such as virtual and real interactive systems, cockpit interactive systems, sensing and prediction, decision-making control, independent mobile platforms, intelligent consumption services, and intelligent operation services, to meet the needs of people's lives and the daily operation of enterprises, and at the same time, it has become a key role connecting Taiwan and international scientific and technological development and create new opportunities for business development.
Healthy and happy
We will make life better with advanced medical care, combine medical, ICT, big data and artificial intelligence and other technologies, and actively promote health and innovative care models to extend healthy lifespan, and develop preventive whole-person care for precise health, precise medical care and precise care.The Institute of Industrial Research and Research has linked the advantages of Taiwan's ICT industry and medical system, developed two major fields: "smart medical care" and "health care", and deepened the key projects such as smart medical and electrical, regenerative medicine, pharmaceutical research and development, sports and leisure, care assisted decision-making systems, and intelligent participation promotion, and jointly created a new industrial ecology of smart medical and health care across the domain, entering the international bio-medical market value chain, and improving people's health and well-being.
Sustainable environment
With scientific and technological innovation, the Institute of Technology has developed three major sub-fields, including "circular economy", "low-carbon manufacturing", and "green energy system and environmental technology", and has deepened the key projects such as sustainable transformation of the chemical materials industry, sustainable technology of the electronics and technology industry, sustainable manufacturing of the biotech industry, low-carbon product design technology, energy conservation and low-carbon production technology, digital manufacturing technology, agricultural low-carbon technology, low-carbon energy, energy efficiency, smart grid and energy storage, and advanced environmental technology. It can supply energy demands at the production, consumption, recycling and reuse ends of sustainable renewable energy, and minimize the impact of energy resource consumption and environmental emissions, and establish an ecological system for net zero sustainable society and industrial development to prosper.
Resilient society
Use technology to respond to risks and stabilize social growth. When shocks and crises come, prevent risks, reduce losses, and respond quickly, and then take advantage of the situation to turn the crisis into a business opportunity.The Institute of Technology has developed three major sub-fields, including "infrastructure resilience", "resource and energy resilience", and "productivity resilience", and has deepened the resilience of key projects such as transportation facilities, information and communication networks, power grid facilities, energy and resource transmission, storage and maintenance, key resource supply, manufacturing labor, production equipment and components. Through mature technology combination and emerging technology research and development, from the perspective of intelligence, diversification, decentralization and alternative, it has built a technical application program that can strengthen society's early warning, response, backup and recovery capabilities for emergencies and long-term stress factors, and develop a resilient society.
Intelligent enabling technology
To analyze the key common needs of the four major application areas of smart life, healthy and happy life, sustainable environment, and resilient society, the Institute of Technology has developed four intelligent enabling technologies, including "Artificial Intelligence and Security", "Semiconductor chip", "Communication", and "Smart Sensing", so as to effectively support the development of the four application fields, and demonstrate the value of innovative systems and application services with related application needs. It also forms a technology-driven industry, promotes more application possibilities, and strengthens my country's industrial strength.
Cross-domain office
In order to promote cross-field work, the Institute of Industrial Research has successively established five major offices, namely "Grid Management and Modernization Strategy", "Artificial Intelligence Application Strategy", "Southern Industrial Innovation Strategy", "Net Zero Sustainable Strategy", and "Smart Green Energy Vehicle Promotion Strategy Office" from 2018 to 2022, integrating the functions of the platform and gathering relevant scientific research energy of our institute to maximize resources.
Grid Management and Modernization Strategy Office
It was established in 2018, with the purpose of establishing it to actively promote power grid management and modern technology solutions to improve the resilience and reliability of domestic power systems.In order to convey correct concepts of power knowledge and act as a bridge between government policy promotion and industrial development, our office integrates professional energy and resources of the Institute of Technology and Research internally, and gathers experts from the academic and industrial circles to promote the following three major work axes:
First, play the role of the grid think tank: bringing together domestic industry, academia and research power experts to form the "Grid Advisory Committee" to put forward suggestions and views on power development, as the basis for promoting the annual topic, and submit it to government units for policy reference at the same time.
Second, promote a sustainable consensus atmosphere: use media, seminars, lectures and other forms to expand the public's understanding of electricity and build consensus on electricity issues and concepts.
Third, promote talent cultivation: The office calls on industry, academia and research to establish the "Grid School and Talent Development Alliance" and award the "Grid Talent Development Alliance Scholarship" every year to cultivate cross-field talents in the power industry.
Artificial Intelligence Application Strategy Office
It was established in 2018. The main purpose is to take AI innovative technologies and applications, and take the 2035 strategic blueprint of the Institute of Technology and Research as its vision, integrate the AI ​​technology energy of the entire institute, adhere to people-oriented x cross-domain innovation, digital resilience x net zero perpetuality, and lead the cross-domain cooperation and value improvement of domestic and foreign industries.At the end of 2022, with the rise of large-scale generative AI models, we also call for the integration of AI-related technologies and applications exchanges in various cross-field units in the hospital, and the development of cross-domain innovative AI application fields such as smart commerce, smart manufacturing, smart medical care, unmanned vehicles, smart terminal chips, net zero sustainability, etc., and invited top experts from various professional fields in China to serve as AI consulting committee members, and put forward suggestions from four aspects: industrial impact and opportunity, industrial technology, social impact and legal system, and talent cultivation, to assist industrial upgrading and transformation, cultivate talents, and then promote innovation and influence, and promote industry integration with the international level.
Two major goals:
1. "People-oriented" x "cross-domain innovation": Let AI develop understanding of human beings, assist human life and work, and enhance human value.At the same time, combined with the AI-driven energy of cross-domain innovation, the special comprehensive effect of Taiwan's industry is formed with "industrial advantages x AI innovation".
2. "Digital Resilience" x "Net Zero Sustainability": Use AI to help establish a strong Taiwan industrial ecosystem and become a trusted and trustworthy key partner in the world.At the same time, AI integrates various technologies to assist Taiwan's industry in sustainable development and connect with international standards.
Southern Industrial Innovation Strategy Office
Established in 2020, in order to meet the vision and goals of the Southern Development Strategy of the Academy: "Use our technical research and development and service energy to assist the industrial upgrading and transformation of the Southern region" and "strengthen the brand image of our institute, show added value and high influence to South Taiwan", integrate southern project resources, promote in-depth cooperation planning and implementation across units, actively promote industrial innovation in the southern region with intelligent technology and digital technology, and assist in the transformation and upgrading of the southern region to respond to the operating risks brought by various rapid changes such as climate change, geopolitical conflicts, etc.
The Institute of Technology actively integrates the relevant technical energy of materials, energy, environment, information and communications, manufacturing, healthy life and other related technologies in the institute, and starts from the local area, relying on close contact with local governments and exploring local industrial development needs;and deepens industrial exchanges with the public association, and collaborates with key operators to create a thematic innovation model to assist the digital transformation of the southern industry and respond to the trends and requirements of global net zero emissions. Continue to focus on the issues of net zero sustainability and smart transformation, deepen research and investigation of the South Taiwan industry with significant scale and influence, interview the industry to understand the current situation, collect and listen to local voices;in response to the long-term development direction and industrial chain nature of the southern part, experts will be gathered for consultation and analysis, and develop emerging industry strategies and policy suggestions.
Net Zero Perpetual Strategy Office
It was established in 2021, and its main tasks focus on six major research directions, including: supply, demand, manufacturing, environment, economy and industry.Under the guidance of supervisors and experts from various units, we work together to promote the innovative development of the industry, while creating added value for the industry.At the same time, it also plays a key platform role in promoting cooperation between industry, officials, academia and research, assists the government in achieving Taiwan's net zero emission reduction target in 2050 and planning a blueprint for Taiwan's industrial and technological development, and is committed to creating a greener and more innovative future for Taiwan, while assisting Taiwan's industry in maintaining a leading position in international competition.
Five major driving directions:
1. Integrate the cross-unit technical energy and resources in the courtyard, and use the four major aspects of net zero technology as the main axis strategy to create emerging technological development with my country's advantages.
2. Master the business models and opportunities of emerging industries such as renewable energy, circular economy, green transportation, and environmental protection, and guide resources to focus on innovative development.
3. Participate in the operation of net zero emissions between the Executive Yuan and various ministries and assist in policy promotion, and assist the government in improving Taiwan's net zero policy layout.
4. Market the concept of net zero emissions inside and outside the institute, handle social communication matters, and assist the industry in grasping new knowledge of net zero development and policy trends.
5. Draw the 2050 net zero emission path and industrial technology development blueprint, and guide the net zero policy goals and R&D resources to achieve comprehensive results.
Smart Green Energy Vehicle Drive Strategy Office
It was established in 2022. The mission is to establish a cross-domain strategic development communication platform for smart green energy vehicle technology research and development. It currently covers seven key research main axes including: drones, self-driving vehicles, rail transit, green energy vehicles, vehicle electronics, autonomous mobile robots and communication services.
Three medium and long-term tasks:
1. Promote international links, technological innovation and verification.
2. Assist the government in developing industrial policies and standards, and put forward long-term development strategies and suggestions.
3. Promote the common development of industrial formation alliances.
Industrial Services
In response to the trend of the industrial environment, the Institute of Technology has not only continued to deepen the integration of technological forward-looking and cross-field technology, but also provided all-round R&D cooperation and business consulting services, including commissioned development of new technologies and new products, small-scale trial production, process improvement, inspection and calibration measurement, as well as technology transfer, intelligent value-added services, etc., and set up open laboratories and entrepreneurship cultivation centers to actively promote and cultivate new startup companies, accelerate industrial technology development and nurture emerging high-tech industries.
In recent years, the Institute of Technology has actively applied science and technology to care about social welfare, and has built a science and technology public welfare platform with three strategic directions, including science and technology application and service, science and technology education promotion, and corporate volunteers, gathered the strength of scientific and technological people, transformed R&D energy into a warm current of public welfare, and strived to become the pioneer of industrial technology, and worked with the industry to innovate technology and lead the future!
