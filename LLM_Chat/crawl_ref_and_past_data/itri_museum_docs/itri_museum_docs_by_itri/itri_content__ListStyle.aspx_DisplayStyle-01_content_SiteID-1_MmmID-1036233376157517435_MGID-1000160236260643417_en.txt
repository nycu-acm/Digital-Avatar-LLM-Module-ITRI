Technology introduction
Micro gas sensing technology.
Develop a MEMS microheater that resists thermal stress and is high uniform temperature, loads thin-film metal oxide sensing materials, and integrates programmable temperature control and sensing circuit chips in the same package, with a concentration detection limit of ppb, and has the advantages of miniaturization, digitization, ultra-low power consumption and high stability.
Features and innovation
The MEMS mini heater loads film sensors, combined with innovative wide-domain high-precision sensing circuits, reaches the micro-concentration detection limit.
Technical Features
Resistant thermal stress/high temperature uniform microheater structure.
Patented programmable temperature control circuit.
Patented wide-domain high-precision sensing circuit.
Thin film sensing materials and loading technology.
Technical Specifications
TVOC Range: 0~3000 ppb.
Ethanol Range: 0.5~500 ppm.
CO Range: 0.1~400ppm.
DC Power Consumption: < 50mW.
Application and benefits
It can be applied in fields such as home care, industrial and commercial industries, public environmental security, health management, medical security, and Internet of Things environmental sensing.
