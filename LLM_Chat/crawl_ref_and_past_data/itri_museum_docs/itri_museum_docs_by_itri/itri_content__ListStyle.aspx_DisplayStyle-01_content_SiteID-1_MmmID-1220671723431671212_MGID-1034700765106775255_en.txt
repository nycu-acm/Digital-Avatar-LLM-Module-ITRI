Technology introduction
Laser metal layering manufacturing technology - Laser metal deposition (LMD).
Use laser energy to melt the metal powder/wire material sprayed at the same time and cover it on the base material, and form a metal protective layer (resistant to corrosion, anti-wear, anti-rust, etc.) on the base material, or melt and sinter the metal powder/wire and directly form a special molded body, so as to achieve the purpose of 3D printing and forming the shape of the structure.This technology can be produced or recycled and repaired for large and complex working components, solving the problems of time-consuming or difficult traditional processing, and reducing mold opening costs and time course.

Processed powder materials: metal powders such as iron, cobalt-based/nickel-based alloys.
Processed wires: iron, alloy and other metal wires.
