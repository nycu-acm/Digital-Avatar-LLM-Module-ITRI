Technology introduction
Plastic chemical recycling technology.
The plastic chemical recycling technology developed by the Institute of Technology has a fluidized bed cracking system with a back-end oil purification unit.It does not require special process design and can be applied to the existing domestic plastic recycling system, and energy resource recycling is carried out according to different recycling characteristics.
Features and innovation
The fluidized bed has high heat mass transfer efficiency, can be continuously operated and amplified, and cracked gas circulation reduces energy consumption, breaking through the existing mechanical recycling technology, and simple material source demand and bottlenecks of the decline in the properties of recycled products. The specifications of recycled monomers of special plastics (acrylic, polystyrene, nylon) are comparable to those of new fossil material monomers, and can be directly returned to the original production process to recycle.
Application and benefits
It can process existing recycling plastics and agricultural waste plastics, build a domestic plastic chemical recycling and recycling application model, and reduce fossil source mining.In addition, cracked oil products have carbon reduction benefits, and recycling can help domestic carbon emission majors reduce their process carbon emissions and product carbon footprints, and assist domestic to achieve the policy goal of net zero of 2050.
