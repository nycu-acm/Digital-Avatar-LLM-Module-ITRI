Easy to disassemble solar modules and open up new business opportunities for solar energy circulation
Traditional abandoned PV modules with low energy consumption and high value reuse technology
Solar photoelectric is one of the world's mainstream renewable energy sources. In order to assist in the sustainable recycling of large amounts of waste solar panels in the future, the Institute of Technology has thought about recycling from the source of product design and developed easy-to-disassemble solar modules. Unlike most of the methods of breaking and lowering the order after being eliminated, the biggest technical key is to replace traditional EVA packaging glue through innovative easy-to-disassemble composite film materials. As long as the module is recycled by temperature regulation, the solar module can be completely separated and the product recycling value can be improved.
The disassembled complete cell can obtain high-definition regenerated silicon wafers and high-purity silver to produce new solar cells in cycles; the glass panels can also be completely retained and can be reused to produce solar modules, or converted to building materials and furniture.After reuse of these materials, they can reduce their dependence on imported raw materials and can also drive a reduction in carbon emissions by more than 50%.The R&D team estimates that the recycling value of solar modules after the cancellation will be significantly increased from NT$600 million per 1 million (GW) to NT$2.4 billion, creating a new circular economy model for the solar energy industry.
At present, we have cooperated with United Renewable Energy and Sanfang Chemical to promote the industrialization of easy-disassembly modules. In addition to being recognized by the German TÜV Rheinland certification body, we have issued Taiwan's first International Electrotechnical Commission (IEC) international certificate. At the same time, easy-disassembly solar modules have also obtained the TÜV SÜD certification. The significance of this dual certification lies in providing the most suitable easy-disassembly modules for different field conditions, which are closer to the needs of the case environment, and drive my country's solar energy industry to the international market. Easy-disassembly modules and simultaneously conduct strict environmental testing and verification in Keelung Island, Penghu and southern France on Taiwan's outer islands, strengthening the competitiveness of my country's solar energy industry in net zero games.
