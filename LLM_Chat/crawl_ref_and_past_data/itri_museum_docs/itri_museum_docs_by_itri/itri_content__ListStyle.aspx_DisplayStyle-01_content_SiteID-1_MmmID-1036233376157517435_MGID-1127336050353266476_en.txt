Smart care for chronic wounds.
Technology introduction
There are as many as 2.3 million diabetic patients in Taiwan, of which about 15% of them will have chronic wounds. In addition to other severe and long-term bedridden patients, there is also a need for chronic wound monitoring and proper disposal, otherwise it will cause inflammation, infection, and even systemic sepsis.The "Smart Care for Chronic Wounds" system developed by the Institute of Technology assists medical staff to quickly obtain the color, heat and 3D images of wounds with lightweight devices without contact, and can be obtained immediately. With just one click, you can obtain the necessary information about wound care.
Features and innovation
This device integrates multiple sensing instruments to capture wound information, and uses AI artificial intelligence deep learning to identify wound size, depth and organizational situations of chronic skin injuries, and assists medical staff in quickly recording and evaluation. It not only shortens the working time of medical staff, but also uploads the medical system instantly, allowing remote doctors to immediately grasp patient information, save medical and disease communication time, and reduces wound healing time by more than twice under immediate treatment, achieving high-quality and efficient innovative remote care services.
Application and benefits
This technology won the 2020 Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Awards (R&D 100 Awards).
