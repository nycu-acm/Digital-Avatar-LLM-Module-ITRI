Technology introduction

Application scope
The pure oxygen combustion and flue gas return design energy of cyclone kilns and multi-stage cyclone towers has been established and rich operating experience has been accumulated.

The process is improved using pure oxygen combustion flue gas reflux technology, so that the carbon dioxide concentration in the flue gas can be concentrated to 90%, and can be reused or directly liquefied and sealed to achieve carbon reduction benefits.
