Technology introduction
Carbon nanofluid multi-loop pulsating heat pipe.
This heat transfer element has the advantages of high thermal conductivity, low thermal resistance, high heat transfer volume and low cost.It can start dual-phase flow under room temperature environment, supports the application of kilowatt-level heat dissipation system, greatly improving the heat dissipation area and efficiency.
Application scope
Strengthen the boiling heat transfer efficiency and extract graphene nanofluid to increase the number of nucleation sites by more than 3 times and increase the fluid speed by twice, effectively enhancing the heat dissipation ability.
The thermal resistance is greatly reduced. Compared with traditional heat pipes, the thermal resistance value can be reduced by more than 50%, and the thermal conductivity coefficient is increased twice (up to 35,000W/mK).The maximum heat transfer capacity can be increased by more than 3 times, and it has high stability.
Carbon reduction benefits
Through exclusive patented multi-loop pipelines, passive heat transfer benefits can be achieved and can be applied to the heat dissipation module industry, such as solar panels, large power supply, substation box and energy storage battery thermal management technology.
