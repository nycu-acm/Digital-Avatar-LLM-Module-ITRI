With the support of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology and the largest financial group, Mitsubishi UFG, signed a memorandum of cooperation with the largest financial group Mitsubishi UFG, Japan, and representatives of Masato Ito (lower right), the director of the Institute of Obstetrics and International Affairs of the Institute of Technology and UFG, and representatives of Masato Ito (lower right), the governor of the Taiwan General Office of Mitsubishi UFB, Japan. Qiu Qiuhui (top left), the Director of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, and Zhang Peiren (top right), the Vice President of the Institute of Technology and Research, served as witnesses.According to MOU, the "Taiwan-Japan Innovation R&D and Industrial Exchange Platform" will be established to build a cooperation bridge for Taiwan-Japan research, accelerate R&D cooperation in the semiconductor field and industries such as electronics, energy, machinery, chemical materials and biomedicine to stimulate technological innovation, and at the same time assist in business opportunities discovering and media docking, thereby promoting bilateral corporate cooperation and economic development.
The restructuring of global supply chains has become a trend in the post-epidemic era, and enterprises need to actively seek more opportunities for international cooperation; on the other hand, the epidemic has led to the rise of long-distance work and home economy consumption models, and 5G services have begun to enter a business transition period, driving the increase in demand for global semiconductor industries.With the support of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology and Research and Development and Industrial Exchange with Japan's Mitsubishi UFG Bank (MUFG) today (10th), and will jointly establish the "Taiwan-Japan Innovation R&D and Industrial Exchange Platform". Combined with the innovative R&D energy of Japan's largest financial group and the Institute of Technology, it will lead domestic legal persons to exchange with indicator-related companies in Taiwan and Japan, and deepen bilateral cooperation, promote a new wave of cooperation business opportunities in semiconductor application technology in Taiwan and Japan, and create a win-win situation.
The Institute of Technology and Mitsubishi UFE Bank jointly held the online "Taiwan-Japan Innovation Technology Cooperation Online Forum". The main axis of the forum focuses on issues such as the development of Taiwan's innovation system industry, Taiwan-Japan semiconductors, electric vehicle technology and industry development, and provides information on investment and technical cooperation in Taiwan.The forum was unveiled by Qiu Qiuhui, Director of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics, and Hikari Hoshino, Chief Deputy Representative of the Japan Taiwan Exchange Association, a legal person in the public welfare consortium, and delivered an online opening speech.
This year's forum also facilitated the signing of a memorandum of cooperation between the Institute of Technology and Research and Research and Research Bank of Japan's largest financial group Mitsubishi UFAN Bank. Based on long-term good interactive relationships, the two sides will deepen innovative technology cooperation in multiple fields in the future. At the same time, they will establish the "Taiwan-Japan Innovation and R&D and Industrial Exchange Platform". On this platform, the two sides will strengthen international cooperation in science and technology, hold international conferences and other activities regularly, exchange market technical information, assist in business opportunities discovery and media docking, in order to expand support for Taiwan-Japan enterprise cooperation and promote bilateral enterprise cooperation.
