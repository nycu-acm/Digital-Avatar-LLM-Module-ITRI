Technology introduction
Solar photoelectric product testing and verification technology.
The best way to ensure the quality of solar photovoltaic modules is to perform verification tests by a certified testing laboratory.To perform the test, the solar photovoltaic module must be packaged and shipped to the testing laboratory.In this case, in addition to the heavy logistics problems, the battery cell may also cause hidden cracks during the transport of the module due to transportation vibration.
Through the development of solar photoelectric module swimming testing technology of solar photoelectric testing laboratory with the same reliability as conventional certification laboratories, various tests can be performed directly on site at the solar photoelectric power station.The main advantages can shorten the delay in the construction period caused by module transportation to and from conventional laboratories; minimize the power generation loss caused by module disconnection; and eliminate the risk of vibration and rupture of battery cells due to module transportation to conventional laboratories.
The Operation Solar Photoelectric Testing Laboratory is designed to perform the following surveys of the solar photoelectric power station case: the maximum power test of solar photoelectric module STC, insulation impedance test, thermal imaging test and electroluminescence imaging test.The design of the action test lab ensures that the results of performing module game tests have the same reliability as conventional labs.
Features and innovation
The advanced design of the extendable mechanism and hydraulic stretching platform enables the tunnel-type solar simulator for module testing to be loaded in small trucks less than 3.5 metric tons and has high stability optical performance.Parameters such as spectral compliance, optical radiation inhomogeneity and instability can be maintained at the Class A+A+A+, and environmental conditions can be controlled during the module test.
Application and benefits
This technology can be applied in the fields of solar photoelectric detection and operation and maintenance;advanced vehicle box extension mechanism and hydraulic expansion platform have obtained patents from the Republic of China and the United States; on-site solar photoelectric module inspection and inspection can be carried out on-site solar photoelectric module inspection and inspection in the solar photoelectric power station, and solutions for on-site inspection and inspection of solar photoelectric modules are provided.The cumulative performance of system sampling in imported modules before installation and domestic module operation has exceeded 100 MW.
