Mitsui Real Estate launches RISE-A platform, Industrial Research Institute becomes an international partner, jointly builds the Taiwan-Japan semiconductor innovation chain

Date: 2025/07/22
The Institute of Technology and Research has officially signed a cooperation agreement with the RISE-A Innovation Community Platform, a subsidiary of Japan's Mitsui Real Estate Group. The two parties will focus on the cultivation and technical guidance of semiconductor startups, and accelerate the international cooperation between Taiwan and Japan in the semiconductor and innovation industries.From the left picture, there are Ueda Toshima, President of Mitsui Real Estate Group, Kazuna Yamashita, Executive Director of Mitsui Real Estate Group, Fu Rubin, Director of the Industrial Development Department of the Industrial Research Institute, and Hu Zhusheng, Vice President of the Industrial Research Institute.
In order to strengthen the link between Taiwan and Japan's semiconductor and innovation industry, the Industrial Research Institute signed a cooperation agreement with the Innovation Community Platform of RISE-A (Revolutionary Innovation by Semiconductor Ecosystem for All industries), a subsidiary of Japan's Mitsui Real Estate Group, and is ranked as the founding international partners of the platform with Belgian semiconductor research institution imec and Japan's Institute of Integrated Industrial Technology (AIST), highlighting Taiwan's key position in the global semiconductor ecosystem. This cooperation will assist Taiwan’s new start-ups and scientific research teams to expand the Japanese market and promote the application of semiconductor technology in diverse fields such as AI, materials, and automotive electronics.The two parties will focus on the cultivation and technical guidance of semiconductor new start-ups, work together to create a cross-domain innovation ecosystem covering suppliers, application ends and support institutions, promote technology docking, B2B media and international exchanges, deepen bilateral industry and research cooperation, and accelerate the application and implementation of diversified technologies.
Shun Ueda, president of Mitsui Real Estate Group, said that Mitsui Real Estate has been positioned as a "industrial developer" for many years and promotes industrial innovation and social development through urban development and the establishment of cross-domain platforms.Following the life science platform "LINK-J" and the space industry platform "CROSS-U", the establishment of RISE-A marks the group's official entry into the field of semiconductor innovation.The platform not only connects suppliers and users, but also combines various roles such as government and research institutions.By providing co-creation "fields" and "opportunities" to promote innovation and being able to cooperate with the Industrial Research Institute with rich technical strength and semiconductor ecosystem network will become an important support for promoting the development of RISE-A.
Hu Zhusheng, deputy director of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, said that the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has been deeply engaged in the semiconductor field for a long time and has complete R&D, new innovation guidance and international linkage capabilities.Through the RISE-A platform, we will deepen cooperation with global innovation and research institutions, promote the implementation of semiconductor technology and application, and the advanced packaging and heterogeneous integration trial production line constructed by the Industrial Research Institute covers complete capabilities such as material verification, process integration and module packaging. In the future, we can also hope to use this platform to cooperate with Japanese materials and equipment operators.He stressed that the Institute of Technology will continue to play the role of the hub between industry, academia and research, promote the industrialization of academic and research results, and become an important bridge for Taiwan to connect with the international community.
The RISE-A Innovation Community Platform was initiated by Mitsui Real Estate Group in Japan, continuing its experience in the development of life sciences and space industries and entering the semiconductor field as a key foundation for digital society.The platform is centered on "field" and "opportunities". It is expected to set up a co-creation base in Nihonbashi, Tokyo in October 2025, providing space for cross-domain collaboration between enterprises and research institutions.It will launch global member recruitment from July 16 to promote the implementation and application of innovative technologies.
