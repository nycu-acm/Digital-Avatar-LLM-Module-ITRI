The Institute of Technology and Research and Development signed a memorandum of cooperation with the Kyushu Semiconductor Digital Innovation Association (SIIQ), the largest official, industry-university-research association in Kyushu, Japan, to deepen technological research and development and information exchanges in the fields of semiconductors, electronics and digital.
With the development of AI, data centers, 5G and autonomous driving technologies, the semiconductor market is expected to continue to grow.In order to consolidate Taiwan's competitive advantage in this field, the Institute of Technology and Research and Development signed a memorandum of cooperation (MOU) with the Kyushu Semiconductor Digital Innovation Association (SIIQ) in Japan to strengthen research and development and exchanges in semiconductor, electronics and digital technologies.The cooperation aims to accelerate technology research and development, and promote cooperation between Taiwan's downstream manufacturers in Japan, enhance international competitiveness and strengthen the resilience of Taiwan's semiconductor industry chain.
On the other hand, at the "Taiwan-Japan Semiconductor Technology International Symposium", leading manufacturers from Taiwan and Japan, such as Sony, Yixin Technology and Industrial Research Institute, jointly discussed the future technological needs and trends of automotive semiconductors, attracting nearly 200 representatives from industry, officials, academic and research.Hu Zhusheng, deputy director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that this cooperation will deepen the strategic partnership between Taiwan and Japan and lay out global business opportunities.The Institute of Technology has long invested in the research and development of advanced semiconductor technology and proposed the 2035 technical strategy and blueprint. In the future, through cooperation with SIIQ, it will promote more Taiwan-Japan technology exchanges and cooperation, promote Taiwan's technology to enter the Japanese supply chain, and create new international business opportunities.
