Technology introduction
Aluminum microflower heat exchanger module.
The aluminum microflower heat exchanger is made of all aluminum, with flat tubes, current collector tubes and blinds fins being formed in a brazed type, with a hydraulic diameter Dh<1.5mm.It has the advantages of small thermal resistance, large heat dissipation area, small size and light weight.Key technologies include: (1) aluminum heat exchanger performance calculation software, geometric dimensions, refrigerant flow distribution, loop design, etc.; (2) mold development; (3) domestic production of key components.
Features and innovation
(1) Aluminum microflower heat exchanger design software: the only domestic independent design software and a variety of refrigerant databases can perform heat exchange capacity and pressure loss calculations, and the actual measured capacity comparison error is <5%.
(2) Key technology mold development: custom mold for blind fins and flat tube microflower production.The air side fins are wrinkled shutters.The refrigerant side is water, R-134a, R-407C, R-410A, R32, HFO-1234yf, and R468A (other types of refrigerants can be added as appropriate).
(3) Domestic production of key components: The first commercial air conditioner in China uses an aluminum microflower heat exchanger, integrating a domestic high-power saving EC high-static pressure fan module, with energy efficiency improvement of >10%, reduced refrigerant volume by 20%, and reduced weight and volume by 20%.
Application and benefits
It is the first time in China to establish key mold specifications for aluminum microflower heat exchanger module products, and is used in equipment such as frequency conversion multi-connection (VRF), inverter air conditioners, cold chain logistics vehicles and electric buses.Technology and service key component and system brand factories, as well as assisting key component factories to build trial production lines.
