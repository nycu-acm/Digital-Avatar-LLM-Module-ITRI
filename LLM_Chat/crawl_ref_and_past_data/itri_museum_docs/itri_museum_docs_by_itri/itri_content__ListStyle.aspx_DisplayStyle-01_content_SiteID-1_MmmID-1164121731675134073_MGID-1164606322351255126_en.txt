Smart monitoring system for injection molding helps plastic products reduce waste by 30%
Injection molding intelligent monitoring system.
During the injection molding of plastic products, waste and bad products will be produced.According to statistics, domestic public institutions produce about 240,000 metric tons of plastic waste each year, only about 28% of which are recycled, and the rest are incinerated, causing environmental burden. Moreover, the application of plastic recycling and reuse technology has become increasingly diversified, and it is still difficult to achieve full recycling.Therefore, it is necessary to cooperate with the production line to reduce waste at the source and take a two-pronged approach.The Institute of Technology has developed the "Injection Molding Intelligent Monitoring System" to help the injection molding processing production line reduce the bad plastic waste by 30%, and increase the proportion of resource utilization by reducing waste from the source to achieve a sustainable cycle and move towards net zero emissions.
The parameters of injection molding equipment affect yield and the generation of plastic waste. In the past, it was necessary to continuously "trial and error" by adjusting by manual experience. In addition, manual sampling of finished products consumed a lot of time and manpower, and also produced a lot of bad plastic waste.In the process of plastic melting and pressing molding, the injection molding intelligent monitoring system can instantly sense the pressure and temperature changes of the equipment and molds, and then perform digital online operations such as production history management, process energy consumption monitoring, visual machine adjustment, quality virtual inspection, etc. through AI artificial intelligence auxiliary computing data. Equipment operators can therefore upgrade the stand-alone function of the injection molding machine to a smart molding solution to enhance product value. Application manufacturers can establish smart molding production lines, implement online inspection of finished injection products, improve product quality, and reduce waste production.
The intelligent monitoring system for injection molding has been combined with injection molding equipment produced by Taichung precision machine, and has been inquired by many manufacturers. This technology has also been successfully introduced to many injection molding factories in central and southern China through actual testing. After actual testing, it can effectively improve the product yield by 3%, reduce bad waste by 30%. In addition, it can also introduce subsequent plastic waste recycling production lines to improve recycling efficiency and quality.
