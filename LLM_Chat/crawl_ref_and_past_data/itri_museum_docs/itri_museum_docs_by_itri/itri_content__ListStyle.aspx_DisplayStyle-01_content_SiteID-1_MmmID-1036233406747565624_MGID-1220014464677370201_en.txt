The Institute of Technology and the University of Southern California announced the formal cooperation, hoping to promote the next generation of semiconductor trial production and chip design through the advantages of the Institute of Technology's cross-field R&D platform and the ability to connect the R&D and production energy of the University of Southern California foresight chip semiconductors.
The Institute of Technology and Research and Technology and the top international academic institution, University of Southern California (USC), announced the formal cooperation. In the future, it will combine the two parties' cross-field integration of R&D technology advantages and forward-looking chip semiconductor production capacity to further conduct forward-looking research on advanced design and processes such as "heterogeneous integration", "next-generation computing technology" and "next-generation memory technology", allowing Taiwan to accelerate the industry to become the leading group of "next-generation computing technology" in the global semiconductor industry chain.
