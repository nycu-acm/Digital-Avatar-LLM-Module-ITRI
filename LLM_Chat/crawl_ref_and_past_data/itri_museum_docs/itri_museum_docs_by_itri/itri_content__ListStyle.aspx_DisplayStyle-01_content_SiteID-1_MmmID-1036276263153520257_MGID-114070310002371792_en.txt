Industrial Research Institute and France Saft cooperate with lithium manganese iron phosphate battery technology, deeply integrating materials and battery technology

Date: 2025/07/03
The Industrial Research Institute and France's Saft Company signed a cooperation agreement, jointly promoting the technical verification and commercialization of LMFP batteries, and creating a new model of international cooperation.From the left picture, Chen Hongli, chairman of Taiwan Hongchen Materials (HCM), Dr. Nechev, technical director of France's Saft Company, Qiu Guozhan, director of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering, and Mr. Viriot, performance director of France's Saft Company, Liao Shijie, group leader of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering, Liao
The Industrial Research Institute and global battery technology leaders have joined forces to create a new model of international cooperation!Dr. Nechev, technical director of French Saft, visited the Industrial Research Institute a few days ago, and under the witness of Chen Hongli, chairman of Taiwan Hongchen Materials (HCM), the two sides officially signed a cooperation agreement.The Industrial Research Institute will use the high-performance lithium manganese iron phosphate powder positive electrode material developed by Hongchen Materials Company, and work with France Saft to develop a new generation of lithium manganese iron phosphate (LMFP) battery technology to seize the market of electric vehicles and energy storage systems, and also demonstrate Taiwan's R&D strength and industrial value in the field of high-end battery materials.
Lithium manganese iron phosphate batteries are an upgraded version of traditional lithium iron phosphate (LFP) batteries. In addition to continuing their low cost, long life and high safety characteristics, the voltage and energy density are increased by about 15 to 20%, becoming a key technology for future electric vehicles and energy storage systems.This cooperation between Taiwan and France will promote the deep integration of materials and battery technology and bring more competitive solutions to global new energy applications.
Qiu Guozhan, director of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that this cooperation with Saft is an important milestone for Taiwan's battery material technology to enter the international market.We will combine the advantages of both parties to jointly promote the technical verification and commercialization of LMFP batteries and create a new model of international cooperation.
Saft Technology Director Dr. Nechev also highly affirmed Taiwan's performance in battery material innovation and looked forward to strengthening Saft's global layout in the new generation of battery technology through this cooperation.
This cooperation will not only expand international partnerships, but will also drive the overall upgrading and market expansion of Taiwan's battery industry chain, injecting new impetus into the goal of net zero emissions and sustainable development.
