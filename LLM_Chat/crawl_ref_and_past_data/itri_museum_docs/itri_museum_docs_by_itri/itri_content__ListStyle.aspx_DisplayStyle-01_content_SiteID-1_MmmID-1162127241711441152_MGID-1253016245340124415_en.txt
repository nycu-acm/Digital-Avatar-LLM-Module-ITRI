Technology introduction
CCU high-active CO2 methanation catalyst technology.

Application scope
Traditional polycarbonate PCs have large usage but a carbon footprint of more than six, or the process uses toxic phosgene as raw materials, which is difficult to meet the goal of net zero sustainable development. The industry needs innovative production technologies that are environmentally friendly and low-carbon emissions.Qimei Technology has independently produced polycarbonate, invested in carbon capture technology, and used domestic innovative catalysts and processes to use carbon dioxide as raw materials to develop a new generation of global carbon-solid PC manufacturing technology and field evidence, so that the carbon displacement of the process will be reduced by another 17% compared with the current petrochemical industry PC technology, and will reduce carbon emissions by 178,500 tons each year in the future.

The CO2 reuse process effectively reduces PC carbon emissions by 17%.
