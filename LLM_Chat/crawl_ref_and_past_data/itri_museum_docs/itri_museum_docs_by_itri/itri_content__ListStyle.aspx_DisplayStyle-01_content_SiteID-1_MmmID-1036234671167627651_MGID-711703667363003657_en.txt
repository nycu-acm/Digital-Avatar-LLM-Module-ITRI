Academician Huang Minqi
Chairman Huang Minqi is the first Chinese chairman of the Global Semiconductor Equipment Organization (SEMI).Academician Huang Minqi founded Hanmin Technology in 1977, starting with equipment agency and system integration, and later turned to semiconductor equipment research and development and manufacturing.From 4 inches to 12 inches, it assisted about 164 factory construction projects in the Asia-Pacific region and installed more than 10,000 semiconductor equipment, opening up the technical service model of Taiwan's equipment industry agents.Chairman Huang invested in research and development and successfully developed the first "electronic beam detection machine" and "ion implantation machine" of Chinese people. He created two major brands, Hanchen Technology and Hanmin Micro Testing, breaking the situation where semiconductor equipment is monopolized by foreign investors.
