Features and innovation
Highly active methanol production catalysts have the characteristics of high productivity (up to 1,000 grams of methanol/kg of catalyst • hours) and low-temperature operation, which can save energy by 20% and reduce production costs compared with the existing technology.
Application and benefits
Methanol can directly or indirectly convert basic raw materials of the chemical industry such as CO, acetic acid, olefins, aromatic hydrocarbons.The carbon dioxide captured by flue gas from industrial processes (covering high-carbon emission industries such as power generation, steel, chemicals, and cement) will be converted and reused at high value.
The domestic imports 1.5 million tons of methanol every year. The methanol generated by this technology can supply the domestic low-carbon methanol emission demand and replace part of the import.The output value exceeds NT$15.2 billion, and the production process can reduce carbon dioxide emissions by 2.1 million tons.
