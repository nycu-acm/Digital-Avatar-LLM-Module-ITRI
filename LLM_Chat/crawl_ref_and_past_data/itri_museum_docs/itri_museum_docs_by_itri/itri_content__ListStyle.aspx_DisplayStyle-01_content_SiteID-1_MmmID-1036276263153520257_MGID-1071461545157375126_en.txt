Taiwan Technology has brought back good news in international award competition!With the support of the Science and Technology Project of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology won the "Gold Awards" of the innovation community with the "formaldehyde-free environmental protection agent" today (9th), leading the United States in materials science and engineering technology.This is the first time that the Institute of Technology has won two consecutive Edison Awards for health and safety technology. After the bionic polyprotic magnetic bead preparation technology (iKNOBEADS) for the treatment of cancer, it has stood out from nearly 400 competitive technologies around the world with a healthy and non-toxic conjunction technology that avoids the harm of formaldehyde. It has won the Edison Award together with internationally renowned institutions such as MIT, EPSON, SRI, MasterCard, and LEGO Education, allowing Taiwan's innovative health materials technology to exert its influence internationally.
Frank, executive director of the Edison Award.Frank Bonafilia said: "The winning technology of the 2020 Edison Awards allows us to understand how important the progress of R&D and design is to reshape the ever-changing future imagination. These technologies have set the world's indicators of how positive innovation can change the world." Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that innovation is an important driving force for promoting industrial development. The Institute of Technology Research is not only brave in innovation, but also focuses on market-oriented, bringing R&D to market demand, but also draws 2030 technical strategies and blueprints with global future life trends, and actively finds new developments and applications for Taiwan's innovative technology.This year is the first time that the Institute of Technology Research and Research won two Edison Awards. The two technologies of "bionic polypropylene magnetic bead preparation technology" and "formaldehyde-free environmentally friendly adhesion agent" that have won are very meaningful. They not only use innovative technologies to provide more precise medical treatment for human health or avoid formaldehyde poisoning, but more importantly, find consumer pain points and integrate technology into life.The Institute of Technology Research and Research is not for winning awards, but for the sake of industry and humans to solve problems!
The harm of formaldehyde to health is no longer news, but most wooden furniture boards still need to be bonded with formaldehyde-containing adhesives.In 2018, there were about 37 million tons of wood adhesives worldwide, of which up to 90% of wood boards are made of urea formaldehyde resin adhesives. The award-winning "Formaldehyde-free Environmental Protection Adhesive" of the Institute of Technology has broken through the traditional method of adhesion containing formaldehyde by the traditional board, and used the most and easy-to-get cellulose in nature as the base, and developed an environmental protection adhesion containing formaldehyde.It not only has good adhesion and water resistance, but does not volatile toxic gases, and is easy to integrate in the current process to produce. It is suitable for use in various wooden furniture, solid wood floors and bamboo boards as adhesion.
"Formaldehyde-free environmental adsorbent" has obtained patents from Taiwan, Japan and the United States, complying with the world's most stringent NAF standards and the latest EPA standard TSCA-6 of the US Environmental Protection Agency. It has successfully entered the supply chain of internationally renowned furniture chain manufacturers and completed product certification.It is expected to gradually use this formaldehyde-free wood adhesive by 2022. The Industrial Research Institute has also established a new startup company for production, which will assist traditional industries such as construction and decoration to transform and upgrade in the direction of non-toxicity, providing the people with a healthier and non-toxic living environment.
Since 2017, the Institute of Technology and Research has won the Edison Awards for four consecutive times with high-speed rechargeable aluminum batteries, fluid-driven emergency lighting, and iRoadSafe smart road safety warning system. This year, it has stood out from nearly 400 products/services/solutions around the world with bionic multi-protrusion magnetic bead preparation technology and formaldehyde-free environmental adsorbents. It has won the 2020 Edison Awards with international manufacturers such as Dow Chemical, Adobe, P&G, NVIDIA, Bose, GE Healthcare, Thermo Fisher Scientific, Lenovo, Osram, etc., allowing Taiwan's innovative biomedical and material technology to exert its influence internationally.

【R&D Story】
Targeting consumer pain points, setting safe and non-toxic targets, formaldehyde-free environmentally friendly agents attack international supply chain
As the public becomes more alert to formaldehyde, the United States stipulated in early 2018 that the formaldehyde emission of all imported American boards must be less than 0.05ppm; Germany is more interested in setting a lower testing standard than the United States in 2020, and the 0.05ppm standard is almost equivalent to the formaldehyde content of natural wood itself.
Considering that the product demands are safe and non-toxic, it is necessary to avoid petrochemical materials as much as possible. It must be taken naturally and avoid grain competition. After several thoughts, Lin Xianguang, head of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering of the Institute of Technology Research Institute, said that the R&D team selected the most suitable substrate - cellulose, "not only natural, but also inexhaustible, and it is the most material in nature." Soon, the first bottle of "formaldehyde-free environmentally friendly adhesion" was made in the laboratory, and two pieces of wood were found, which were sticking to each other by 1 cm.The adhesion force was quite strong, and they succeeded!
However, Lin Xianguang's R&D team is not satisfied. The viscosity alone is not enough.If you soak in water, it will dissolve and you still can't enter the kitchen and bathroom. So after another year of continuous preparation and research and development, I finally passed the water resistance test and threw it into boiling water for 4 hours and would not melt. Lin Xianguang said happily: "This is a very strict technology, and even the formaldehyde-free wood adhesive in the United States cannot do it."
After the experimental verification, the next step is to go out of the laboratory and conduct field verification to ensure that this research and development is indeed feasible for market applications.The first field verification was on a small stage in the Institute of Technology Research and Development. The R&D team laid about 20 square meters of flooring, and the results confirmed that both flatness and durability were successful.The first step is stable, which also gives them more confidence to promote it to outsiders. Next, they not only cooperate with operators who specialize in mountain wooden houses, but also take on the big case of a total of 360 square meters of indoor space in the Kaohsiung Finance and Taxation Building.
Inspiring news continues to come, formaldehyde-free environmentally friendly adhesion agent has been favored by international chain furniture manufacturers.Among them, well-known European chain furniture manufacturers are expected to gradually use this formaldehyde-free environmentally friendly adhesion agent before 2022. At the beginning of the contact, the other party did not believe that the team could meet their requirements, especially the bonding and production of some furniture, and even the adhesive produced by international manufacturers might not be able to cope with it. "A particular furniture designed by the manufacturer uses curved and curved wood at the corners. Only two pieces of curved wood are bamboo skin, and there are 5 layers of birch wood in the middle. It is easy to explode the board at the curved point. The adhesion technology is quite difficult. Even if urea formaldehyde resin adhesive is used, only one Malaysian manufacturer can pass it," Lin Xianguang said with a smile: "And the Industrial Research Institute is the second one, and it is formaldehyde-free!"
The research has not stopped yet. In the future, the team hopes to increase the flame resistance of the product, so that it can be applied to more furniture or building materials, and the goal of free aldehydes can be locked on yellow glue, white glue, waterproof glue and other related products, creating a "non-toxic and aldehyde-free" living environment, which is the ultimate goal.
【News Dictionary】
Edison Awards
Established in 1987, it enjoys the reputation of the "Oscars in Innovation" in the United States. It commends global innovative products and corporate leaders every year, commemorates the spirit of inventor Edison's pursuit of creativity and excellence. The winners' representatives provide products and services, innovative excellence and leadership to create new projects. The selection criteria are concepts, value, communication and influence. The awards are divided into 15 categories.The winners of the past include deceased Apple founder Jabers, Tesla founder Elon Musk, and Levono President Yang Yuanqing.
