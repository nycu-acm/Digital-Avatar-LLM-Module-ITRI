110th Annual "Academician of the Institute of Technology" Conference
The 10th Academician Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research and Research Institute, photo of academicians attending (110 years)
Time: November 16, 2010
Location: Conference Room 1003, Zhang Rongfa International Conference Center (No. 11, Zhongshan South Road, Taipei City)
Chairman: Academician Shi Zhenrong
Topic: Challenges and Opportunities of Taiwan’s Industry under Net Zero Carbon
The 110th Annual Academician Conference of the Institute of Industrial Research was held at the Zhang Rongfa International Conference Center in Taipei, chaired by Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of the Zhirong Foundation. Through the combination of physical and online methods, a total of 18 academicians of the Institute of Industrial Research were invited to discuss how the industry overcomes challenges and grasps business opportunities under the trend of net zero carbon emissions.The academicians attending include Chen Xingshi, founder of Ronggang Group, Shi Qintai, honorary professor of Tsinghua University, Zhu Zhiyang, president of Youjia Industrial Group, Miao Fengqiang, chairman of Lianhua Shentong Group, Yang Zhentong, president of Yali Electric Group, Du Shuwu, president and CEO of Lianqiang International Group, Li Yuande, honorary professor of the School of Medicine of Taiwan University, Lin Xianming, chairman of Wisconsin Zitong, Cai Lixing, vice chairman and CEO of MediaTek Technology, and Onward Therapeutics Chairman and CEO Ye Changjing, convener of the forward-looking steering committee of the Institute of Technology Research and Research Wu Jincheng, etc.; online attendance will include 6 academicians including Cai Mingjie, Chairman of MediaTek Technology, Academician of the China Research Institute, Wang Kanglong, Thor's lecture professor of Electrical Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles, Zhang Anping, Chairman of Taiwan Clay Group, Lin Jiannan, Chairman and General Manager of Taiwan Plastics Company, and Kong Xiangzhong, Professor of Bill Gates, Professor of Bill Gates at Harvard University.Executive Yuan Government Affairs Committee Member Guo Yaohuang, Director of the Ministry of Economics and Industrial Bureau Lv Zhenghua, Chairman of the Institute of Industrial Research, and President Li Shiguang and President Liu Wenxiong also attended the meeting.
Meeting conclusion focus:
The global carbon reduction target of 2030 can be achieved with existing technologies, but to achieve the goal of net zero in 2050, we can consider future emerging technologies such as hydrogen energy.
A clear net zero blueprint for the country in the medium and long term is required. Not only are there 2050 goals, but there are also inspection points at each stage that require government and enterprises to provide guidance.
The government and large enterprises need to drive upstream and downstream ecosystems to conduct carbon footprint inspections first and establish a credible carbon emission data database to meet the international carbon emission inspection needs.
We need to make good use of the resources of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, combine carbon reduction and carbon capture technology and green electricity, introduce blockchain technology to commodify carbon rights transactions, use green financial systems and market forces to promote it, and combine advanced technology to assist carbon reduction and carbon capture and utilization (CCUS).
Accelerate the revision of the temperature pipe law, introduce carbon pricing and carbon trading systems to promote carbon reduction; the government reviews basic regulations and makes appropriate adjustments to promote net zero emissions and recycling; and open up new markets and new industries under the use of new technologies and new life forms in the future.
Establish national carbon reduction and green energy education, use science and technology to visualize carbon emissions, and establish cross-generational education to establish people's awareness of carbon reduction costs and change their lifestyles to reduce resistance; enterprises can deploy personnel work transformation in advance.
The use of liquefied natural gas (LNG) has become an existing energy policy in Taiwan. We can think about using a large amount of vaporized cooling energy and storing it twice.
It is possible to consider establishing a new hydrogen ecosystem from hydrogen production-related technologies, hydrogen refueling stations and hydrogen-energy vehicles.
The demand for AI machine learning, data computing, and Internet access will grow exponentially in the future, and energy consumption will be increased, and energy efficiency and DataCenter (IDC) energy saving are important; at the same time, the application of AI and the Internet will assist in the development of net zero technology and smart grids.
The government needs a clear green energy policy.Although the cost of green energy such as PV comes from economic scale, technological breakthroughs are still needed in the end, and the Industrial Research Institute and the industry need to cooperate in investment.
For emerging industries such as: Biotech OEM (CDMO), Taiwan can use experience in mirror semiconductor industry to use more production-efficient equipment to meet the trend of net zero carbon emissions.
