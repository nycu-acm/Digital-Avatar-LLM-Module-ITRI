Technology introduction
Laser metal lamination manufacturing technology - Powder bed melt forming (PBF) equipment.
Metal laser accumulation layer manufacturing technology is an innovative component manufacturing thinking.The mechanism of action is to cut the processed objects into two-dimensional patterns, lay a layer of metal powder and then use laser to melt the pattern, lay one layer of burning and stack it into a stack, which can create complex morphology, internal runners and structures, and create high-value products.
The research and development of process equipment and special materials and trial production workshops assist the industry innovating and promoting two major directions.It has the advantages of custom metal laser accumulation manufacturing platform development and functional materials research and development, and can assist the industry in establishing relevant manufacturing energy, developing high-efficiency products, product trial production and functional effectiveness.
