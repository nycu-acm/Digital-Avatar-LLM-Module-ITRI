Technology introduction
The core technology of developing high carrier mobility transistor (HEMT) structure epitaxial and field-effect transistor (FET) components with gallium nitride-based (GaN-based) provides
Epictal verification of RF Power Amplifiers and Power Switches in new substrate materials
Process verification of ampere-grade FET components and voltage resistance characteristics of enhanced and empty epitaxial chips
Features and innovation
GaN-based HEMT structure epitaxial technology, including
Development of new substrates and buffer layers
Stress and warpage management of epitaxial layer
Normally-Off ultra-thin gate epitaxial design
GaN-based HEMT FET components process technology, including
GaN/AlGaN High Selective Etching
Metal structure design with low contact resistance
Epilox voltage test
Ampere-grade component verification
Application and benefits
In the industry, gallium nitride components are suitable for high-frequency, high-power, radiation-resistant and high-temperature environments, such as 5G base amplifiers, military radars, rechargeable batteries and automotive energy management systems.
We can provide integrated services from substrate, epitaxial to module design, and tailor-made one-stop solutions for customers, which not only meets market demand, but also strengthens Taiwan's innovation capabilities and technological autonomy in the energy-transforming system and 5G industry.
