Technology introduction
Combining technologies such as augmented reality (AR), virtual reality (VR) and hybrid reality (MR) are used for occupational safety simulation training.
The physical combination is combined with a dynamic platform and a surrounding landscape display, somatosensory feedback is generated through the operation of the students, and highly realistic operation training is carried out.
Provide customized smart industrial safety and disaster simulation training system to strengthen the safety of the workplace.
Application and benefits
Applied to simulation training for public institutions, labor, etc.
