The winning technologies of the Industrial Research Institute are as follows: dye-sensitized batteries are applied to smart home technology - low-light inventive energy Nova dye-sensitized batteries are energy-inventive technology for low-light power generation. With the support of the Energy Bureau of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the dye-sensitized batteries developed by the Industrial Research Institute have a low power generation threshold and light weight , low cost and 100% mastery of technology. Unlike traditional solar cells that require strong light power generation, power can be generated with just about 3 candles. At the same time, the Institute of Technology and Research and Development has cooperated with Formosa Plastics to create the world's first trial production line with an annual production capacity of 100,000 pieces in the Shalun Green Energy Technology Demonstration Field in Tainan. It has automated production of the whole line. It has obtained a number of process and equipment patents, and the equipment is 100% domestically produced. The award-winning "Dye-sensitized Battery Applied to Smart Home Technology" has a module conversion efficiency of 17%, leading the world; its partner Formosa Plastics has been striving to integrate applications for many years, and has also successfully introduced dye-sensitized batteries into electric curtains, sensors, electronic paper and other commodity applications, showing specific results .High energy and high safety resin solid-state batteries - New development of the electric energy storage industry lithium batteries are widely used in mobile phones, tablets, laptops, and even electric vehicles. In order to solve the safety problems of high energy lithium batteries, the Industrial Research Institute has developed "high energy and high safety resin solid-state batteries", which mainly replaces flammable electrolyte with high ion conductive resin (NAEPE) materials. It does not require heating or UV light. NAEPE can cure at room temperature, maintaining the original electrolyte's high ion conductivity and improving high voltage stability. After curing, NAEPE flash ignition point temperature is increased, which has the characteristics of flame retardancy and good high temperature cycle life. The solid lithium battery using NAEPE can be connected in series like a building block (Cell To Module), breaking through the single structure limitation of lithium batteries, reaching a high volt voltage of 12V for one battery. The process is easy to operate and can quickly import the existing battery process. It has cooperated with several domestic battery operators, including Taiwan CNPC, Formosa, Yiliang, and Gus, to drive the future development of Taiwan's electric vehicle and energy storage system industries. Chronic wound smart care - AI supports chronic wound care. Faced with the challenges of medical staff shortage and urban-rural medical gap, in order to optimize the quality of long-distance medical care, the "Chronic Wound Smart Care" system developed by the Institute of Technology uses three-in-one devices such as color, heat sense and 3D sensing to identify slowness with AI artificial intelligence deep learning. The wound size, depth and tissue situation of sexual skin injuries are uploaded immediately to allow remote doctors to immediately grasp patient information and save medical and disease communication time. Integrate a variety of wound sensing elements to present analysis functions, the judgment accuracy of this system has reached 85%. Through the color, heat and depth of wounds, it helps medical staff to quickly record and evaluate, which not only shortens the working time of medical staff, but also reduces wound healing time by more than twice under immediate treatment, and achieves high-quality and efficient innovative services for distance care.
