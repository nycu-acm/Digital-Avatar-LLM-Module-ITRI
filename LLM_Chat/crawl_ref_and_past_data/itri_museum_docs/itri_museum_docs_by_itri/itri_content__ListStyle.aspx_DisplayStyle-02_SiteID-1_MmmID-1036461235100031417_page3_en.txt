Digital Manufacturing and Verification Laboratory
The virtual cutting process analysis and optimization technology of this laboratory is synchronized with the world's advanced cutting laboratories. Nano Electronics Laboratory
With the goal of promoting the upgrading of the semiconductor industry, we will establish a stable and flexible process capability, able to meet industrial needs and technological development trends, and provide a core R&D, process and verification environment for next-generation system chips in all walks of life and industry, academia and research.
Three-dimensional integrated circuit research and development laboratory
It is the first R&D laboratory in Asia that has a complete 12-inch 3DIC core process - silicon substrate perforation (TSV) and three-dimensional wafer stacking integration.
Optoelectronic semiconductor process laboratory

Human Lighting Laboratory
Combining human-causing lighting and intelligent control systems, adjustable light variable parameters are applied to various lighting situations, allowing LED lighting to satisfy people's physiological, psychological and preferences as the main goal, while achieving energy-saving and environmentally friendly results.
Photoelectric semiconductor measurement laboratory
Founded in 1994, it is the first LED measurement laboratory in China to pass the Laboratory Certification System (CNLA) in the Republic of China, dedicated to the analysis, research and testing of photoelectric semiconductor materials and component characteristics.
