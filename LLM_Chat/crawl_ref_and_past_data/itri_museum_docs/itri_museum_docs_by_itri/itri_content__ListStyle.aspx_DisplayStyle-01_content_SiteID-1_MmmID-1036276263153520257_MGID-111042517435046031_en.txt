Ministry of Economics helps industries grasp business opportunities in advanced medical markets, with "precision health" as the main axis, and the Institute of Technology won the Edison Award

Date: 2022/04/25
The silver medal award-winning "Promoting Organization Integration of Bionic 3D Printing Technology" uses laminate manufacturing (3D Printing) to create "bionic hollow bone nails", which can produce bone nails of different specifications, porous bone nails, intervertebral fusion devices and other implanted bone materials through simulation, design and process control to provide support for smart medical care.
Taiwan’s scientific and technological research and development strength has been recognized internationally!The Edison Awards, known as the "Oscar Award in Innovation", announced this year's award list.The Institute of Technology has won awards for six consecutive years. Under the guidance of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, this year, it once again took "precision health" as the main axis, "Promoting the organization's integration of bionic 3D printing technology (BioMS-Ti)" and "Portable Edge AI-DR" two innovative technologies. With the participation of more than 400 technologies and products around the world, it won the highest honor in science and medicine in the "Edison Award", one silver and one copper in science and medicine, making Taiwan's health and medical technology shine on the international stage.
International manufacturers such as 3M, Abbott, Dow Chemical Company, and IBM have also won the Edison Award this year. Frank, executive director of the Edison Award.Frank Bonafilia said that the Edison Award is a supreme honor for innovators who subvert traditions and develop new products and services to make life better. The Institute of Technology continues to work hard to innovate in different fields, far ahead of many competitors.The award-winning "Ophthalmic Imaging Assisted Diagnosis Technology" combines artificial intelligence technology for analysis and interpretation of diabetic retinopathy, which is helpful to both junior and non-specialist doctors;"Promote the integration of bionic 3D printing technology in tissues" is made of bionic bone material through 3D printing, and its excellent design has been highly praised and recognized.
Qiu Qiuhui, Director of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, said that the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs has long been strong in my country's scientific and technological research and development capabilities with scientific and technological projects.Taiwan's ICT-industry communication technology strength and biotechnology innovation and R&D energy are ahead of the world. It is optimistic that the global population will continue to drive the rise in medical care demand. The combination of science and technology and medical care will inevitably be the future market trend. We welcome the two award-winning technologies of the Institute of Industrial Research, demonstrating Taiwan's innovative and outstanding R&D energy in science and technology and medical care across fields.
In recent years, the cross-domain trend of digital medical care has emerged, and the world is raging with the COVID-19 epidemic, many countries have accelerated the development of important projects such as vaccine development, telecare, artificial intelligence (AI)-assisted diagnosis required for smart medical care.Under the trend of global epidemic prevention technology, hardware integration innovation will become the next breakthrough for Taiwan's digital precision health.The Institute of Industrial Research and Research aims at industrial needs and actively draws out the "2030 Technical Strategy and Blueprint", uses technology to build a "healthy and happy" application field, deepens its key technologies such as smart medical and electrical, regenerative medicine, and pharmaceutical research and development, and creates a new industrial ecosystem of precision diagnosis and treatment and health care across the domain.
Lin Qiwan, director of the Institute of Biomedical and Medical Materials Research Institute, pointed out that "promoting the organization's integration of bionic 3D printing technology" creates "bionic hollow bone nails" through laminate manufacturing (3D printing). Through simulation, design and process control, it can produce implanted bone nails with different specifications, porous bone nails, intervertebral fusion devices and other implanted bone materials to provide support for smart medical care, stand out among many innovative technologies and win silver medals.Another technology of the Institute of Technology Research and Research that won the bronze medal is "Ophthalmic Imaging Assisted Diagnosis Technology". Ding Bang'an, director of the Institute of Information and Communications of the Institute of Technology Research and Research, said that "Ophthalmic Imaging Assisted Diagnosis Technology" is to use AI artificial intelligence to assist diagnosis to effectively assist doctors in diagnosing subtle lesions and identify the severity of lesions. Working with Jinhong Technology, we will jointly create the world's first handheld fundus camera with AI artificial intelligence analysis, and use edge computing devices to assist diagnosis to accurately interpret diabetic fundus lesions to avoid missing the golden treatment period. At the same time, we also cooperate with domestic medical institutions to provide advanced service experience in rural areas.We look forward to more industry, officials, academic and research in the future, and the added value will be added, to establish high-level medical materials production and production capacity, and create new opportunities for the industry.
