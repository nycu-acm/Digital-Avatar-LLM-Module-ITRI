Technology introduction
The integrated application of RQMS of the smart air sampling robot of the Institute of Technology Research Institute.
Smart air sampling robots are widely used in biopharmaceutical manufacturing plants, medical equipment manufacturing, food processing and medical care fields, and adopt modular design to integrate field temperature and humidity monitoring, suspended particle detection and suspended microbial sampling functions.It has the remote one-click task dispatch function, can independently navigate and perform sampling and detection operations, and flexibly respond to changes in the manufacturing field environment through obstacle avoidance path planning.Its customized remote management system can be applied to various clean-level fields, supports instant data transmission, recording and traceability, and meets the regulatory requirements of the field of advanced biomanufacturing.
Features and innovation
Technical Features
Integrated functional module design to avoid dirty accumulation
Can expand detection and sampling functions, such as temperature and humidity, microparticles, microorganisms, etc.

Benefits and Applications
The first domestic case to introduce a smart automation environmental quality supervision system in the biopharmaceutical process field will establish standard standards and provide a strong reference for the adjustment of laws and regulations.
