The Consumer Electronics Exhibition (CES), which will be held in January next year, is a trend trend in the global consumer electronics market, especially the Innovation Award of the Consumer Electronics Exhibition (CES 2021 Innovation Awards) is more indicative!The Institute of Technology and Research also published a CES exhibition theme website today (21st), announcing that it will participate in the 2021 CES digital exhibition with 12 innovative technologies including AI, Robotics and e-Health.Today, the iDarlingWeaR infant smart care device, which has just won the Innovation Award for Consumer Electronics Exhibition, stood out among many international manufacturers with contactless micro-physiological sensing and warm protection of infants and young children, and won the CES Wearable Technologies Wearable Technologies Wearable Technologies Wearable Technologies Innovation Award. It has become the only award-winning R&D institution in Taiwan. It has also won awards with internationally renowned brands such as AMD, Philips, HP, Panasonic and LG Electronics.
Zhang Peiren, deputy director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that contactless business opportunities are a new industrial opportunity in the global epidemic, among which home care technology will be the mainstream of development.The iDarlingWeaR infant smart care device, which won the Consumer Electronics Exhibition Innovation Award, is a physiological sensing product combining home care and contactless technology to provide warm care for infants and young children. Healthy and Loho is one of the key areas of the development of the Institute of Technology Research and Technology in 2030. In order to effectively integrate forward-looking research with the needs of terminal caregivers, the development of system services is based on human needs and focuses on market orientation.It has won the CES Innovation Award for four consecutive years, proving that the achievements in this direction have been recognized. It is also the best example of the Institute of Technology Research and Technology extending the advantages of innovative ICT to the application of smart care services and developing seamless and full-person care and cross-field soft and hard integration systems.
Zheng Renjie, CEO of the Science and Technology Center of the Service System of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that sudden neonatal death has made many parents feel terrified!The study pointed out that health analysis and management of infants and young children can effectively reduce the risk of possible accidents in infants and young children under one year old.According to Grand View Research, the global infant and toddler care products market size is expected to reach US$1.678 billion in 2025, and the average annual compound growth rate will reach 5% from 2018 to 2025. The iDarlingWeaR infant smart care device, which won the 2021 Consumer Electronics Exhibition Innovation Award, is a contactless micro-infant physiological sensing product, with the characteristics of simplicity of operation, immediate warning, remote monitoring and diverse applications.iDarlingWeaR infant smart care device uses a low-power physiological detection radar technology that is completely harmless to the human body. It is light-weight and can be placed on the chest of infants with a wrap. Without contacting the baby's delicate skin, it can detect and record the baby's heartbeat, breathing and mobility 24 hours a day. The average heartbeat error rate per minute is less than 5%.
iDarlingWeaR infant smart care device uses blue bud technology to transmit the collected infant physiological information to the parents or caregiver's mobile device, and instantly understand the baby's sleep and awakening state through the app. In case of abnormal heartbeat and breathing or sudden death, parents can also receive a warning notification immediately. This device is easy to operate and easy to carry.It can be used especially in a light-free environment. It can grasp the physiological status of infants and young children anytime and anywhere, becoming a good helper for taking care of babies at home, and can also be used in large infant and young children care institutions such as confinement centers and baby care centers. At the same time, it can be extended to medical hospitals, regional clinics and other fields, and integrated and applied in remote smart health and care services, effectively reducing the human burden of caregivers and improving the quality of care.
The theme website of the 2021 CES Digital Exhibition of the Institute of Technology, with AI Robotics and e-Health as the theme, exhibits the first 64-channel intelligent handheld ultrasonic wave in Taiwan, which can be transmitted to the handheld device instantly through Wi-Fi, and can be matched with different ultrasonic probes according to the situation; it has a bionic palm that grabs irregular and soft objects, holds books, business cards, mobile phones, or Batte bottles, and even plays the electronic piano; the self-taught battery management system uses original AI algorithms to instantly correct the battery's safety and service life; Wi-Fi not only connects to the Internet, but also can sense sleep quality and provide sleep stage detection technology required for sleep learning, and these innovative and exciting next-generation technologies will be available on the CES theme website of the Institute of Technology of Technology!The CES theme website of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research will accept business opportunities and cooperation with domestic and foreign manufacturers from now on.
