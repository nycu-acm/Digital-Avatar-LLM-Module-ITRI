In response to the global corporate social responsibility development trend, the Board of Directors of this institute approved it in June 2011 to use the technology of our institute to promote social welfare and implement the task of "using scientific and technological research and development, driving industrial development, creating economic value, and enhancing social welfare"; and establishing a social welfare committee to promote social welfare affairs of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research with three major aspects, namely "Technology Application and Service", "Science and Technology Education Promotion" and "Corporate Volunteer".
In addition to extending the research and planning results to social welfare, we also connect the research resources of our institute's units to hold science and technology education promotion activities such as children's science and technology camps, science and technology education seed teachers seminars, and club "volunteer training and lectures".Provide learning opportunities, expand the life horizons of children in rural areas, and get access to diverse and rich scientific and technological information.Call on volunteers from enterprises and integrate various associations and units in the courtyard to participate in social welfare activities.It also extends its tentacles to various places for inquiry, strengthens cooperative relations with various outside units, and connects appropriate partners to deepen local public welfare services.
The "Technology Public Welfare Experience Exchange Seminar" is held every year, and indicator companies investing in social welfare (TSMC, Microsoft, Acer Zhitong, Realtek Semiconductor and Zhongqiang Optoelectronics... etc.) are invited to share the promotion results with the public welfare technology research team of our institute, thereby expanding the public welfare promotion energy of technological influence.
The Industrial Research Institute's determination to protect Taiwan with scientific and technological research and development has been consistent since its establishment in 1973. We adhere to the spirit of "using love for charity and making Taiwan more beautiful", and hope to use this social welfare service platform to link social welfare institutions, corporate partners and government institutions to create a win-win situation and allow all participating groups and individuals to gain rich harvests.The research results of the Institute of Technology can be extended to social welfare and drive all walks of life to work together to enhance social welfare.We also hope to create a people-oriented culture through public welfare services that care about people and the environment, and to develop practical technologies and products that are in line with human nature.
We need to do our best to do social welfare and we need to work together!I hope to spread this warm current of love through various channels, so that our society can be warmer and more positive forces will be brought!
