Zhang Qingju Academician
Dr. Zhang Qingju was once the chairman of the World Advanced Integrated Circuit Co., Ltd. and has made outstanding contributions to the development of my country's IC semiconductor industry.In 1976, Dr. Zhang joined the integrated circuit development program of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute and became a seed member sent by the government to receive technology transfer to Radio Corporation of America (RCA). He is the first generation engineer in the semiconductor industry in Taiwan. He has a far-reaching impact on domestic semiconductor technology innovation, industrialization, and cultivating semiconductor talents.Dr. Zhang has served as director of the Electronics Institute of Industrial Research Institute, deputy general manager of Jianbang Venture Capital, general manager and vice chairman of Huabang Electric.During his tenure at Huabang Electronics, he successfully established Huabang Electronics' leadership in coded flash memory (NOR Flash).In 2009, he took over as the world's advanced Dong Zuo and led the world's advanced to occupy a place in the global special wafer foundry market.
