Taiwan's innovative research and development is a powerful international, and the Industrial Research Institute wins the Edison gold and silver medals

Date: 2021/04/26
The Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs held a press conference on the Edison Awards today (26th) to share glory with the award-winning manufacturers and cooperative units.The VIPs in the picture are from left to right: Ding Weineng, general manager of Shangzhiqiuyi, Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology, Qiu Qiuhui, director of the Ministry of Economics, and Lin Qiufeng, CEO of the Metal Center.
The Edison Awards, known as the "Oscar of Innovation", announced this year's winners, and Taiwan's innovative achievements shine!With more than 400 technologies/products participating in the world, two research institutions and two manufacturers won one gold, one silver and three coppers, ranking second in the world in terms of awards, four of which came from the achievements of the Ministry of Economic Science and Technology Project.Qiu Qiuhui, Director of the Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, said that the "AI three-dimensional intelligent warehousing system" that won the gold medal in the Institute of Technology and Research has not been easy to stand out from many international companies in the AI ​​application category of innovative services. Unlike the United States, which has a vast hinterland that can develop flat warehousing for international e-commerce platforms, in response to the urbanization of Chinese people, three-dimensional space warehousing can reach up to 14-layer shelves, and then matched with shuttle automatic warehousing, the storage and transportation space will be increased by more than twice. At present, it has cooperated with Yahoo, Hsinchu Logistics and Hanlu Technology, and introduced Yahoo's first AI three-dimensional automated logistics center in Taiwan.It is currently the logistics center with the highest reserve shuttle automatic warehousing in Taiwan.
The Edison Award is set up to commemorate the inventor Edison's pursuit of creativity and excellence. Most of the winning units are large companies and large enterprises. It is an indicative international innovation award. This year, international major manufacturers such as Corning, Merck, Abbott, GoPro, and Medtronic have won the gold medal.
Taiwan’s scientific and technological research and development strength has been recognized internationally, and technological research and development has also locked in global scientific and technological trends.Qiu Qiuhui said that Taiwan’s small and medium-sized enterprises have many unique technologies, which have good potential and competitiveness, and can demonstrate innovative strength without a huge R&D scale or organization. This 2021 Edison Award reflects this spirit. In addition, Taiwan’s hardware competitiveness is well-known all over the world. This time, there are two award-winning technologies related to AI artificial intelligence software, showing that Taiwan has the strength to use software added value to hardware and software integration to create higher added value, which is very valuable.The silver medal award of the Institute of Technology Research and Research "Microbial Dye Development and Fiber Application" is used to transform it with commonly used safe microorganisms. It uses fermentation and separation and purification processes to produce several natural dyes that can be used for cotton and nylon. In the past, Taiwan did not produce chemical dyes. With the rise of environmental awareness in the future, the green process of dyeing and finishing has great potential. It is not only more environmentally friendly than chemical dyes, but also has better color saturation and color firmness after washing.At present, it has conducted preliminary verification cooperation with the textile manufacturer Ruhong and Fuying Technology, a leading brand of special chemicals. The metal center won the bronze medal technology "Continuous Micro Component Heat Treatment System Equipment" is the world's first. The pinion produced has sufficient hardness and very accurate accuracy.It has been used in Tesla's electric door handles and has been certified by the supply chain of BOSCH major manufacturers. It is now cooperating with Xiangyi, Xingguang Industry and other companies. In addition, the "Mobile Medical Imaging Server" of Shangzhiqi Company, which won the bronze medal this time, was transformed from Zicehui Technology and technology.Through AI artificial intelligence, the breast cancer screening process is greatly accelerated. The inspection report can be shortened from more than two weeks to within 7-10 days. Innovative R&D Aiding Machinery Yiyue International.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, pointed out that the most important spirit of the Edison Award is to focus on the implementation of innovative R&D results in product innovation and services.This award shows that the 2030 technical strategy and blueprint direction promoted by our institute are in line with international trends and market-oriented promotion directions, and the agile strategy of the R&D team has been recognized. In order to master the home economy in the post-epidemic era, the "AI Three-dimensional Intelligent Warehousing System", which won the gold medal, uses AI artificial intelligence development demand forecasting and warehousing decision-making technology to vertically develop warehousing design, up to 14 layers of shelves, and matched with shuttle automatic warehousing, the storage space is immediately increased by more than twice, achieving "use things to people" operation, and the warehouse delivery time is reduced by 60%. It has increased 10 times the volume of goods during the peak promotion period.It has been successfully applied in the AI ​​three-dimensional automation logistics center, allowing the system to be implemented in the industry faster, creating win-win business opportunities and opportunities for consumers and industries. The silver medal award-winning "Microbial Dye Development and Fiber Application" uses organic carbon sources as nutrients and transforms it with microbial strains, which is safe for human health, improves dye production efficiency, and is non-toxic and low wastewater. It has been verified and cooperated with domestic textile factories in advance.Faced with the new global net zero carbon emission trend, it deepens the green process, conforms to the trend of environmental protection and sustainable development, and assists the industry to enter the international green supply chain.
"Continuous Micro Component Heat Treatment System Equipment" won the bronze medal, Lin Qiufeng, CEO of the Metal Center, said that as 3C products become thinner and shorter, related components also emphasize high precision and miniaturization. For components with high complex structures or diameters below 2mm, the team integrates functions such as automatic feeding, real-time monitoring and instant regulation of main heating furnaces, innovative oil tanks and material collection devices, intelligent services, etc., which can reduce the dependence and exploration of human experience to improve the quality of components. The overall material collection rate reaches 100%, the yield rate reaches 99%, and significantly reduce the delivery time and cost. It has now been introduced into the supply chain of international manufacturers such as Tesla and BOSCH, and cooperate with Xiangyi and Xingguang Industry.In the future, it can also be used in medical machinery, robotic arms, electric vehicles, optoelectronic semiconductors, aerospace, etc., to enhance manufacturing competitiveness for the domestic industry.
【News Dictionary】
Edison Awards: Founded in 1987, it enjoys the reputation of the "Oscar for Innovation" in the United States. It recognizes global innovative products and corporate leaders every year, commemorates the inventor Edison's spirit of pursuing creativity and excellence.The winners represent innovation excellence and leadership, and provide products and services that create new ground. Apple founder Jabers and Tesla founder Elon Musk are both winners of the "Eddie Achievement Award", which recognizes the overall industry contribution of the award.Another category is the products and services that praise innovativeness, including: Industrial Research Institute won seven awards since 2017-2021, Apple's iPad, 3M™ molecular detection system, and Boeing 787 Dreamliner.
The selection criteria are concept, value, communication and influence.The awards are divided into 14 categories. This year's winners include internationally renowned manufacturers such as Corning, Merck, Abbott, GoPro, Medtronic, Google Maps, IBM, Dow Chemical (DOW).
