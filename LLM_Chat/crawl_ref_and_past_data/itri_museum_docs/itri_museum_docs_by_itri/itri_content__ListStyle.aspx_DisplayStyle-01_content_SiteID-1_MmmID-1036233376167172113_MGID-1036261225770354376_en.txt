Technology introduction
Use MRT technology to produce creams fully automatic commercial models.
my country's existing chemical industry mostly uses traditional batch production technology.In addition to being unable to produce accurately, it also has problems such as energy consumption, industrial safety and environmental protection.Microreactor technology (MRT) has the advantages of precisely controlling temperature, pressure and elastic production, thereby improving the efficiency of chemical reactions, reducing the generation of by-products, improving product quality, improving the production efficiency of chemical and pharmaceutical industries, and reducing production costs and pollution, which is conducive to the trial production of small molecule drugs.MRT can lead my country's chemical and pharmaceutical technology innovation and breakthroughs.
Features and innovation
This technology uses the principle of computational fluid mechanics to perform micro reactor channel calculations, with the purpose of designing a novel micro-runch mixer.After design verification, the commission has been professionally processed and produced, using acid and alkali-resistant titanium metal materials to produce three different test and verification machines, including laboratory-type micro-reactor modules, small-scale reactor modules, and fully automatic commercial cream production models using MRT technology.
Application and benefits
The preliminary test has been completed with the self-developed machine of the Industrial Research Institute and can be applied to the synthesis of drug intermediates, and has achieved benefits such as shortening reaction time, improving reaction selection rate, and reducing by-product yield.Among them, Tadalafil can shorten the reaction time by 5 to 60 times, and the incidence of side reactions is 1/3 lower than that of patented technology; the yield of P-toluenesulfonamide (PTSA) reaches 97.5%, which is about 10 to 15% higher than that of traditional batch production methods.The above micro-reaction mixer hardware applications have applied for patents from Taiwan and the United States respectively, and have obtained patents from the Republic of China.
