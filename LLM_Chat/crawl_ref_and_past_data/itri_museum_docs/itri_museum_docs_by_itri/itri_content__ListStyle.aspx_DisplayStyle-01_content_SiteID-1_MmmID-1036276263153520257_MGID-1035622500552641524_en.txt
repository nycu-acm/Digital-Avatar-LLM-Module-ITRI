Date: 2019/06/24
The Elite Award of the Institute of Technology Research Institute was revealed today (24th)!Four outstanding gold medal technologies show the outstanding achievements of the Institute of Technology under the "5+2 Industrial Innovation Program" of "Green Energy Technology", "Circular Economy" and "Digital National Innovation Economy"!The photo shows that President Liu Wenxiong (center) of the Institute of Technology Research Institute and representatives of the four awards are Lin Yuren (first from left), Lin Xianguang (second from left), Chen Huijuan (second from right), and Wan Haopeng (first from right) of the Institute of Green Energy Institute of Technology Research Institute (first from right) together at the Elite Press Conference of Engineering Research Institute.
The Engineering Research Elite Award, known as the "Oscar" of the Institute of Technology Research, was revealed today (24th)!Faced with the challenges and critical moments of Taiwan's industrial transformation, the Institute of Technology Research has four gold awards comparable to the world's largest manufacturers, showing the outstanding achievements of the Institute of Technology Research in "Green Energy Technology", "Circular Economy" and "Digital National Innovation Economy" under the "5+2 Industrial Innovation Program". It has won the "Golden Research Award for Outstanding Research" with excellent adhesion, water resistance and no carcinogenic formaldehyde. It has completed product certification with international chain furniture manufacturers to create a non-toxic home!"Microal Algae Carbon Reduction Energy and Biotechnology Health", which won the "Gold Medal of Industrialization Contribution", uses the carbon dioxide emitted by industrial plants to cultivate microalgae, and extracts biomass energy or high-value commodities from it, reducing carbon and adding value!"Assist the business service industry in grasping key consumption intentions" uses multiple algorithms to accurately predict consumer demand and import e-commerce momo; "Logistics Industry Value-added Application" successfully doubles the output value of the logistics service industry through digital transformation, driving a brand new economy!
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that technological innovation is an important driving force for national economic growth. The US-China trade war has continued to heat up recently, and technology has become the key to the struggle between major powers. It can be seen that innovative technology is not only a driving force, but also a strategic role in the international competition and cooperation situation.The Institute of Industrial Research will innovate R&D energy and truly implement it in the industry. The "Gold Medal of Outstanding Research" and "Gold Medal of Industrialization Contribution" selected by the Institute of Industrial Research this time echo the government's "5+2 Industrial Innovation Plan" covering from digital economy, smart logistics to environmental sustainability, and are both forward-looking R&D, which helps the continuous leap in industrial value.The importance of scientific and technological research and development is undoubtedly high. However, there is an insurmountable "Missing Link" between innovative technology and industrialization. It requires close cooperation between developers and industries to successfully reach the other side of industrialization. We hope that colleagues will continue to closely link innovative technology and industrial transformation and upgrading to create a new blue ocean for the industry.
This year's Elite Gold Medal:
"Nontoxic environmentally friendly glue and flex wood application" Non-toxic home is so fresh and fresh, and won the "Gold Medal of Outstanding Research Award"
In the past, urea formaldehyde resin was used as an adhesive for the sheets. As time and temperature change, formaldehyde that is harmful to the human body would be released.The non-toxic environmentally friendly glue developed by the Institute of Technology has no formaldehyde, is adhesion-resistant and has good water resistance. It has obtained patents from Taiwan, Japan and the United States. It can comply with the world's strictest NAF standards and the latest EPA standard TSCA-6 of the US Environmental Protection Agency, and has completed product certification with international chain furniture manufacturers. At the same time, this technology has also established a new startup company to assist in the transformation and upgrading of traditional industries such as construction and decoration, and further to a non-toxic home.
"
Promote the application of added value in the logistics industry” Efficient storage and transportation won the "Gold Medal of Industrialization Contribution"
This is the first dynamic weight measurement and identification system developed by Chinese people. It can quickly measure the volume and weight of tens of thousands of packages every day, automate purchase billing, and accelerate shipment through AI picking decisions.In addition, the Institute of Technology has developed my country's first set of logistics service robots (iAGVs), which uses the world's first ceiling positioning and navigation technology to provide labor-saving handling and mobile services for picking and picking goods.In the vehicle scheduling, efficient AI distribution scheduling (iRouting) and IOT capacity integrate service technology, introduce artificial intelligence into the path planning links and promote vehicle sharing, greatly improving distribution efficiency.Now, leading logistics operators such as Hsinchu Logistics, Full-Daily Logistics and Jingyang International have been introduced, as well as cooperation with super businessman Lairfu.
"Assisting the business service industry in grasping key consumption intentions" understands your heart best and wins the "Gold Medal for Industrialization Contribution"
This technology is assisting e-commerce, audio and video platforms and other business service providers to analyze website viewers' click history and transaction information with more than 10 multiple algorithms, predict consumers' ideas, and improve the accuracy of personal recommendations.Taking Momo, a leading e-commerce industry leader in Taiwan, as an example, after introducing this technology, it will drive a leap in revenue through system recommendations and at the same time increase customer adhesion.In addition, telecommunications operators, Taiwan’s big brother, use this technology to recommend products or services.
"Microalgae reduces carbon energy and biotechnology health" carbon circular economy - waste turns into gold and won the "Gold Medal for Industrialization Contribution"
After capturing carbon dioxide from the high-carbon emission industry, it is extended to be used for microalgae breeding. Depending on the algae species, high-carbon-solid algae for energy or microalgae containing high-monovalent substances can be produced, converting carbon dioxide into energy and high-value products.With the biotechnology platform of the Institute of Technology, we have successfully developed various key technologies and systems for carbon sequestration, breeding, and harvesting and extraction of microalgae, and have successfully developed thalasthenon medical beauty health care products and special health foods to achieve high-value product applications, with both carbon circulation, energy and economic benefits.In addition to cooperating with Taiwan Mill Company to build the world's first microalgae breeding demonstration farm combining calcium circuit carbon capture system and the first domestic erythrocytic aquaculture farm, this technology has also been linked to domestic upstream, midstream and downstream industries of microalgae. Through technology and patent authorization, it assists the industry in the whole factory output mode to enter the world and implement the sustainable environmental goals of the carbon cycle.
The Institute of Industrial Research Institute's "Non-toxic Environmental Protection Glue and Curved Wood Application" won the "Gold Medal of Outstanding Research Award" without formaldehyde, has good adhesion and good water resistance, and has assisted the transformation and upgrading of traditional industries such as construction and decoration, and takes a step further to a non-toxic home.
The Institute of Industrial Research Institute won the "Gold Medal for Industrialization Contribution" of "Promotion of Value-added Applications" by the Logistics Industry. The first dynamic weight measurement and identification system developed by Chinese people can quickly measure the volume and weight of tens of thousands of packages every day, automate purchase billing, and accelerate shipment through AI storage and picking decisions, efficient storage and transportation.
The Institute of Industrial Research and Research won the "Gold Medal for Industrialization Contribution" "Assisting the Business Service Industry to Master Key Consumption" and its technology mainly uses multiple algorithms to assist e-commerce, audio and video platforms and other business service operators to predict consumers' ideas and improve the accuracy of personal recommendations.
The Institute of Industrial Research Institute's "Microal Algae Carbon Reduction and Energy Creation and Biotechnology Health" won the "Gold Medal for Industrialization Contribution". It uses bioenergy and carbon sequestration technology to capture the carbon dioxide emitted by industrial emissions, and then use it to cultivate microalgae or microorganisms, and extract biomass energy or high-value products from it, develop medical beauty and health products and special health foods, and complete the application of high-value products.
