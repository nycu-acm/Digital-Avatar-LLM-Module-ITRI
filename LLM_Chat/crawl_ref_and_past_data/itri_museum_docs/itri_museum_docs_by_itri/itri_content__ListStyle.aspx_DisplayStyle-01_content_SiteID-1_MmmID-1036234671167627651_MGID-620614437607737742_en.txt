Academician profile
Academician Cai Mingjie
Chairman Cai led MediaTek to become the top ten IC design manufacturers in the world. He also actively invested in cultivating academic and scientific talents, continuously supported science and technology education activities and provided scholarships, and worked with Taiwan University, Jiaotong University, Tsinghua University and other schools to maintain an uninterrupted communication bridge with the academic community. He also won honorary doctoral degrees from Tsinghua University and Jiaotong University, as well as the "Science and Technology Management Award" of the Society of Science and Technology Management of the Republic of China and other honors.Continue to use innovative technologies and business models to grasp market opportunities, establish strong competitiveness, and create a situation where Taiwan’s IC design industry goes from followers to leaders, which has a great impact and contribution to industrial development.
