The Institute of Industrial Research shows the advantages of biomedical, net zero sustainability, and AI innovative technology, highlighting R&D strength and innovation momentum: "Breakthrough Multi-Effective Temperature Hydroglue" that won the gold medal in the field of biomedical medicine It is an innovative biomedical material with high biocompatibility and can be absorbed by the human body. It is liquid in low temperature and gels rapidly after entering the body, bonding to damaged tissues and forming a protective film. It can stay for up to 30 days, and its effectiveness is far superior to traditional water glue. Its temperature-sensitive properties are suitable for minimally invasive injection and are expected to replace surgical treatment. In addition, polymer carriers can be equipped with a variety of drugs and have been used in clinical trials such as intervertebral disc herniation and degenerative arthritis. It has been patented in Taiwan, the United States and Europe, and has been transformed into the new "Renhui International", entering the global dental and surgical market, and promoting Taiwan's biomedical technology to the international level. In the field of net zero perpetual, The "NaPoGlass" technology, which won the gold medal, uses patented nanoporous technology to solve the problems of metal wastewater treatment and resource recycling. It can recycle metals, no sludge, and allow water resources to be recycled, reducing the water consumption by 50 to 90%. Compared with the chemical coagulation and sedimentation method, every 10,000 tons of water can be processed to reduce carbon emissions by about 10,000 tons. It has been used in the semiconductor and electroplating industries to help enterprises transform greenly. In addition, NaPoGlass, rich in metals, can make moisture-regulating, antibacterial, anti-mold and deodorizing products to expand people's livelihood applications. The global shortage of water resources is intensified, and traditional pipeline inspection is time-consuming and susceptible to the environment, making it difficult to detect water leakage in time and has high maintenance costs. The "Water Leakage Intelligence" won the gold medal Smart monitoring technology can quickly identify and locate water leakage, with an accuracy rate of 98%, reducing maintenance costs. This technology has cooperated with Taiwan Water Company to locate 834 water leakage points, saving 10.94 million tons of water per year, and is expected to expand to global water-scarce areas. It is especially suitable for historical cities in Europe, accurately detect leakage under lossless monuments, and optimize water resource management. The silver medal award "H-shaped steel structure low-carbon automatic production and manufacturing technology" solves the shortage of H-shaped steel supply, avoids delays in semiconductor factory construction and soaring costs, and breaks through the limitations of traditional welding relying on technician experience. The technology combines high-energy laser welding, digital twinning, intelligent weld bead tracking, smart steel structure positioning and automated loading and unloading/transmission system The technology has eliminated the steps of grooved filler, slag-free waste and deformation correction, and the manufacturing cost has been reduced to one-third of the traditional working method, and the production speed has increased by five times. This technology has broken through the 50-year arc/iron sand filler welding production model. It has passed the trial production verification of Taiwan's photomasks and passed the SGS and the National Earthquake Center certification, and has mass-produced more than 2,000 metric tons.
