Date: 2025/03/12
The Institute of Industrial Research has won the "Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award" for the ninth time for eight consecutive years, setting a record for the most awards from R&D institutions in the Asia-Pacific region.
Clarivate announced the 2025 "Top 100 Global Innovation Institutions" report today (12th).With the subsidy of the Industrial Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics, the Institute of Industrial Research has continued to produce abundant R&D results and high-quality patents, leading the Institute of Industrial Research for 8 consecutive years and won the 9th Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award.The report pointed out that the Institute of Technology has demonstrated outstanding R&D strength in five major patent indicators, including technical uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity.This year, only three research institutions in the world were selected. The Industrial Research Institute is not only one of them, but also continues to set a record of the most award-winning R&D institutions in the Asia-Pacific region. The innovative achievements are as famous as internationally renowned institutions such as Mitsubishi Electric, Toshiba, Qualcomm, Dow, and Philips.The award has been selected for internationally renowned institutions such as Amazon, Apple, and Microsoft.
Clarivate Security Ball’s top 100 innovative institutions report has been actively analyzing the innovation energy of global enterprises or institutions through patent data for a long time, and selecting them based on scientific patent indicators to commend top institutions’ persistence in innovation and their excellent creativity.Gordon Samson, President of Clarivate Intelligent Property Rights Group, said: "Congratulations to the ICITIC Research Institute for its ninth and eight consecutive years to be selected as the top 100 innovative institutions in the world! The number of global inventions has grown 80 times since 2000. Today, with the rapid development of innovation, being selected as one of the top 100 innovative institutions in the world is an outstanding achievement. Today, market competition is more international than before. The technological development of a region can often have a profound impact on global industries. We look forward to and believe that the ICITIC Research Institute can continue to promote industrial upgrading, make good use of its rich R&D results, and create greater economic value for Taiwan and the world."
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that innovation is the cornerstone of national competitiveness. As geopolitical risks intensify, countries are committed to mastering key independent technologies, and continuous innovation has become an important issue for national development.The Institute of Industrial Research has been deeply engaged in cross-field innovative research and development, and has accumulated more than 30,000 patents, which has led to the ninth consecutive year of winning the Top 100 Innovation Institution Award in the World for the ninth time, indicating that the Institute of Industrial Research has reached the world's first-class level and has global influence.The Institute of Industrial Research and Research is committed to promoting innovative technology to assist industrial development, actively cultivating new ventures and building monetization of intangible assets. It has established Intellectual Property Innovation Corporation (IPIC), gathering high-quality patents for industry, academia and research, and building Intellectual Finance Bank (IP Bank), deepening industrial competitive advantages, providing patents needed by manufacturers, strengthening international competitiveness, and assisting manufacturers to activate intangible assets, promote sustainable industrial development, and innovating the value of Taiwan's technology research and development.
Looking forward to the future international trends, the Industrial Research Institute has advanced to plan forward-looking technology research and development strategies, and has drawn up the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint". With four major application areas such as smart life, healthy and happy life, sustainable environment, and resilient society, as well as intelligent enabling technologies that promote the development of application fields, we use innovative technology and services to propose solutions based on the development trends of semiconductors, AI artificial intelligence, robots, drones, etc., and at the same time expand high-quality patent pools and activate intangible assets, help the industry improve international competitiveness, and establish irreplaceable market advantages.
The Institute of Technology has won the "Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award" for nine times, showing that the Institute of Technology has performed outstandingly in five major indicators, including technological uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity.
