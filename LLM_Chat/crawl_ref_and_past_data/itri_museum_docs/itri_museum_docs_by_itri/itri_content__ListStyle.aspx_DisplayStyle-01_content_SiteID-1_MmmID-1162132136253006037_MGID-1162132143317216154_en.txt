Service Introduction
Net Zero Human Resources Training.
In order to assist enterprises in responding to the global net zero carbon emission target and talent needs of 2050, the Industrial Research Institute has developed a training theme with carbon reduction practice and technology-oriented training, and cultivate talents through workshops and digital mixed learning methods to accelerate the promotion of net zero carbon reduction in enterprises.
Scope of service
Social education: Use digital micro-course learning shortcuts to accelerate the penetration of green knowledge in enterprises and promote net zero sustainable lifestyle.
Professional training: In combination with the Institute of Industrial Research, we will open professional technical courses for carbon reduction and cultivate key talents.
