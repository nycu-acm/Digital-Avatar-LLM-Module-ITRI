Industry Focus Spotlights
Attack global giant energy storage business opportunities
Written by Chen Yiru
Welcome to the first year of energy storage after the table
The Institute of Technology and Research and Development worked with the Taiwan Electric Power and Energy Engineering Association to hold the "Symposium on the Application of Science and Technology Energy Storage and System Resilience", inviting experts from all walks of life to share the prospects and actual successful cases of the energy storage industry. It hopes to encourage more cross-domain industries to jointly invest, build a complete energy storage industry chain, and work together to seize the international energy storage market.
Energy storage technology is on the center of the energy stage!As electricity becomes the lifeblood of the economy, Taiwan actively promotes the application of post-meter energy storage and smart applications.The Institute of Technology and Research has joined hands with industry, officials, academic and research to explore business opportunities, held the "Symposium on the Application of Science and Technology Energy Storage and System Resilience", inviting experts from all parties to share the prospects of the energy storage industry, inject new momentum into net zero and energy transformation, and seize the business opportunities of the international energy storage market.
The demand for smart life gains electricity, sufficient and stable electricity is closely related to a country's economic development and the national living standards. It stores excess electricity and releases it when needed.It not only concerns the stability and resilience of the power grid, but also the future business opportunities. According to statistics from the International Energy Agency (IEA), the global overall energy storage capacity will increase by 6 times the current level by 2030. US research firm Research Nester also estimates that the market size will exceed US$660 billion in 2037, with unlimited business opportunities.
The Institute of Technology and Research and Development joined hands with the Taiwan Electric Power and Energy Engineering Association to hold the "Symposium on the Application of Science and Technology Energy Storage and System Resilience", inviting experts from the Ministry of Economics Energy Administration, Taiwan Power, Yongfeng Yu subsidiary "Yongyu Intelligence", Anselway and other experts to share the prospects and actual successful cases of the energy storage industry. It is hoped to encourage more cross-domain industries to jointly invest and build a complete energy storage industry chain, not only strengthen the resilience of the power grid, but also work together to compete for the international energy storage market.
The supply and demand ends work together to help the power grid stabilize
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research and Technology and chairman of the Taiwan Electric Power and Energy Engineering Association, said that taking into account stable power supply and carbon reduction is the key to Taiwan's energy transformation. Only by working together with supply and demand and establishing a resilient energy strategy can we respond to future challenges.Taiwan can use Mirror International to introduce integrated resource planning analysis, and after comprehensively considering the resources of both power supply and demand, find the power usage combination with the lowest cost and best efficiency to avoid resource waste and excessive investment.
On the demand side, starting from improving energy efficiency and smart electricity consumption can also contribute to the stability of power supply; on the supply side, in addition to building renewable energy facilities, energy storage systems are also indispensable, because renewable energy and energy storage complement each other, which can effectively solve the intermittent characteristics of renewable energy, balance the peaks of electricity consumption, and help the grid stability and energy sustainability.
Wang Yaoting, general manager of Taiwan Electric Power Grid, said that the power grid is Taiwan’s most important common asset and will continue to invest and strengthen its resilience. Faced with the expectation of power stability in industries and people’s livelihood, Taiwan Electric Power Company actively implements the secondary energy transformation policy and promotes strategies such as supply based on demand, diversified green energy, deep energy conservation, and advanced energy storage systems.
At present, TaiPhone has built large grid-level energy storage systems in Taoyuan, Tainan, Kaohsiung and other places. At the same time, through the power trading platform that will be launched in 2021, private energy storage systems can participate in diversified power commodities, improving grid resilience and creating new business opportunities.In addition, in line with the current government-promoted technology energy storage policies, we encourage users to set up energy storage, and Taiwan Power redesigns time electricity price rates to raise the price difference from peak to peak, providing enough incentives for operators to set up incentives, hoping to work with the industry to help Taiwan's energy transformation and upgrading.
Promote technology energy storage and encourage corporate users to store energy after importing tables
Wu Zhiwei, deputy director of the Energy Administration of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, pointed out that at this stage, my country's power generation and grid energy storage have developed steadily, and the grid energy storage has also reached the 2025 1,000MW target; the Ministry of Economic Affairs will use "technical energy storage" as the focus of user-side energy storage in the future. Science and technology energy storage is to encourage industrial users to import post-meter energy storage systems to transfer power during peak peaks of the power system. At the same time, operators can also manage power consumption through post-meter energy storage to improve power quality.
Wu Jinzhong, deputy general manager of Taiwan Electric Power, said that Taiwan Electric Power has a complete plan to strengthen the resilience and stability of the power grid. In terms of capacity reserve, Taiwan Electric Power cooperates with the Ministry of Economic Affairs to adjust the power supply light rating system to improve information transparency and provide specific power saving suggestions. In response to the increase in renewable energy, Taiwan Electric Power and the Institute of Technology jointly study the technical conditions for providing virtual inertia inertia in energy storage, verify that energy storage can act according to the design specifications, so that energy storage can perform frequency change rate (ROCOF) measurements, and conduct actual reaction output power, providing system virtual inertia and frequency modulation.
Chen Yiping, general manager of Yongyu Intelligent, said that the domestic energy storage system has achieved many application results in the industrial field. Taking Yongfengyu as an example, the first gas-electric symbiosis combined with meter-meter energy storage system in Taiwan has been built to integrate energy creation, energy storage, energy consumption and energy regulation to optimize the energy use efficiency of the plant; and an industrial microgrid energy storage system is built in Yongfengyu's own papermaking factory, combining renewable energy and diversified generator sets in the factory to optimize energy dispatch and application, effectively reducing operating costs.
Anserway CEO Zheng Zhiwen believes that in addition to being able to sell "post-meter energy storage" in Taiwan through Taiwan's power trading platform, the "trade to the market can also enter the factory with more than one's energy trading platform, and the future is promising. For enterprises, energy storage is established after the investment table to ensure stability and safety of electricity use.
