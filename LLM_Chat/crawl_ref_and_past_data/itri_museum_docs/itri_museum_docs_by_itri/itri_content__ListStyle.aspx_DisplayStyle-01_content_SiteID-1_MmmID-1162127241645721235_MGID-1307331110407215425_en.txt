Technology introduction
Create dry and clean air.
The new low-temperature renewable adsorbent grabs water and recombines it with a high-efficiency condensation and dehumidification system, which can be condensed and dehumidified at any humidity.
Application scope
Patented MOF (Metal Organic Framework; MOF) adsorbent, regeneration temperature below 80℃, and can continuously grasp the water in the air at a relative humidity below 10%.
It can be combined with traditional high-efficiency condensation and dehumidification systems, and regenerates the compressor waste heat.According to different environmental humidity, the energy efficiency can be increased by 10~30% compared with traditional dehumidification and drying systems.
Different MOF adsorbent elements and system styles can be made for different drying systems such as high-pressure air suction dryers, plastic dryers, household dehumidifiers or air water makers.
Carbon reduction benefits
The new metal organic framework solid dehumidifier can be prepared by molding technology into particles and beech drying elements.Compared with traditional adsorbents, the adsorption capacity is increased by more than 30%, the regeneration temperature is reduced from 180℃ to below 80℃, the energy-saving benefits can reach more than 30%, and the minimum dew point can reach -40℃. It can be used in both people's livelihood and industry, with a total market >6.8 billion/year.
