Technology introduction
Semi-open thermal processing temperature control and energy-saving technology.
Thermal processing friction welding and thermal image monitoring and feedback control can instantly detect heat treatment temperature, dynamically adjust the friction rotation speed, pressure and time and other hot processing parameters to improve the stability and quality of the thermal welding process. It can be widely used in heterogeneous metal welding and high-precision manufacturing applications.
Application scope
Developed thermal processing friction welding and thermal image monitoring and feedback control to solve the bottleneck of poor products due to the difficulty of precise control of traditional thermal processing welding temperature and difficulty in bonding different metals, and achieve the improvement of welding quality, reduce waste rate and improve energy-saving and carbon reduction benefits through precise temperature control.
Accurate temperature monitoring of thermal images, instant temperature monitoring of thermal processing, accurate control of heating temperature, and significantly shorten the machine's thermal regulation time.
Intelligent feedback parameter regulation, adaptive thermal processing friction welding parameter adjustment, improve quality stability and yield, and effectively reduce the amount of waste.
Carbon reduction benefits
For the thermal processing and welding process, accurate temperature monitoring of thermal images can save 40% of electricity, introduce intelligent feedback parameter regulation, improve yield and effectively reduce carbon by 50%.It can be used in metal thermal processing heterogeneous metal welding processes, such as Aerospace (high-strength turbo nickel-titanium structure), Automobile (lightweight transmission shaft steel aluminum welding), Minsheng Industry (electric chassis aluminum steel alloy).
