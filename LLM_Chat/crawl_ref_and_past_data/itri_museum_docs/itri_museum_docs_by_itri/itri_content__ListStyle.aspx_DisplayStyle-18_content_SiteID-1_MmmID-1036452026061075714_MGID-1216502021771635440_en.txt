The Institute of Technology won the top 100 innovation institutions award in the world for seven times
Writing/Editorial Department
Under the leadership of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research has won the "Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award" for the seventh time for the seventh time, and has continued to be the most awarded R&D institutions in the Asia-Pacific region, and has also ranked first among Taiwanese institutions.
The evolution of science and technology and mastering the core technologies of the next generation are the decisive key to industrial competition.The Patent Energy of the Institute of Technology has performed outstandingly in five major indicators, including technical uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity. For six consecutive years, it has won the Clarivate Safety Ball Top 100 Innovation Institution Award for seven consecutive years.
Today, when technology is rapidly innovating, only by mastering the core of technology can we obtain the key to success under the fierce global industrial competition.In view of this, the Ministry of Economic Affairs has used its scientific and technological project plan to promote my country's scientific research and innovation, integrate the cross-field energy of legal person R&D institutions, implement all-round intelligent power operation strategies, and deepen Taiwan's industrial intelligent power strength.85% of the patents of the Institute of Technology Research comes from the support of the Science and Technology Research Institute, and the number of patents and inventions in the Institute of Technology Research ranks first among R&D institutions.
Clarivate Security Ball’s top 100 innovative institutions report long-term evaluation of institutions’ innovation energy through patent data analysis, commending top institutions’ persistence in innovation and excellent creativity.Gordon Samson, head of Clarivate Products, said that only three R&D units in the world were selected on the list of top 100 innovation institutions this year. Taiwan Institute of Technology and Research not only ranked among them, but also ranked seventh and was selected for six consecutive years.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that the Institute of Technology Research has won awards for many years, which not only shows the uniqueness and influence of the patent layout, but also shows that the overall direction and strategy of intelligent power management have international competitive advantages.Scientific and technological research and development and industrial innovation are important cornerstones for promoting economic growth. The Institute of Technology continues to deepen its layout with two major aspects: "blue ocean strategy and capital market linking the technology market".
In the Blue Ocean Strategy, the Industrial Research Institute uses three major levels: existing technological breakthroughs, redefining industrial pain points, and finding new business opportunities to create a market to assist the industry in building value innovation business models.At the same time, the Institute of Technology actively links the capital and technology markets, connects the credit guarantee fund for small and medium-sized enterprises and 26 important financial banks in Taiwan, and creates an intangible asset financing service mechanism. It has assisted 25 new and small and medium-sized enterprises to accumulate more than NT$300 million in funding through patent and technology added financing, promoting the accelerated growth of small and medium-sized enterprises and new enterprises, becoming the key driving force for Taiwan's industrial transformation and upgrading.
In addition to leveraging its R&D energy, the Institute of Industrial Research has also joined hands with all sectors of industry, academia and research in Taiwan to form IP Bank. It has now collected thousands of high-quality patents for global popular technologies from top domestic enterprises and legal entities, and promoted patent pools in the fields of network communication, chip semiconductor design and manufacturing, computer systems, net zero emissions, multimedia audio/video processing and comprehensive applications, building a solid shield for the industry to enter the international market.
