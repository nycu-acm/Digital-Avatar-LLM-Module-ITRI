Service Introduction
Greenhouse gas inspection.
In response to the international net zero carbon emission trend, the Greenhouse Gas Inspection Agency of the Institute of Technology provides inspection services for the greenhouse gas inspection report of the applicant unit based on ISO 14065 and TAF "Certification and Verification Agency Certification Specifications".
The greenhouse gases tested include carbon dioxide (CO2), nitrous oxide (N2O), methane (CH4), hydrofluorocarbons (HFCs), perfluorocarbons (PFCs), sulfur hexafluoride (SF6) and nitrogen trifluoride (NF3).
Scope of service
The scope of inspection services of this institution includes:
•ISO 14064-1 (CNS 14064-1) Organizational level greenhouse gas inspection (voluntary plan, Ministry of Environment plan)
•ISO 14067 (CNS 14067) Carbon Footprint Greenhouse Gas Inspection (Voluntary Program, Ministry of Environment Program)
This institution will conduct a written review of the inspection report and send personnel to the site to check the relevant supporting materials and equipment used.
The acceptance units cover all domestic organizations (government departments, legal entities, public and private institutions and manufacturers, etc.).
This institution publicly commits to understand the importance of impartiality in performing verification activities and to deal with conflicts of interest to maintain objectivity.
