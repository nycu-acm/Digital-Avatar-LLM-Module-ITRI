The Institute of Technology won the "CES 2023 Innovation Awards" with the "I View AI Fish Tank" in more than 2,000 exhibition works, allowing Taiwan's innovative R&D capabilities to shine internationally.
The Ministry of Economic Affairs innovates and develops technology to let the world see Taiwan! The US Consumer Electronics Exhibition (CES), which is the global consumer electronics market trend, will debut in January.With the active investment and leadership of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology has stood out from more than 2,000 exhibition works and won the "CES 2023 Innovation Awards" with the "I See AI Fish Tank".This is also the 10th award accumulated by the Institute of Technology since its participation in the exhibition, bringing more future innovative imaginations to smart education and allowing Taiwan's innovative R&D strength to shine internationally.
The Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs stated that the Swiss Institute of Management (IMD) of Lausanne (IMD) released the 2022 World Digital Competitiveness Ranking 2022; DCR. Taiwan's R&D-related evaluation is among the best in the world. my country ranks 11th among the 63 major countries and economies in the world, indicating that my country's R&D capabilities are among the world's leading companies.Not only that, the Ministry of Economic Affairs' science and technology project has long supported legal person research and development and innovative technologies and has also won many international awards, including the Top 100 Global Innovators, the Top 100 Global Innovators, the R&D 100 Awards, and the Innovation Awards of Consumer Electronics Exhibition (CES 2022 Innovation Awards). More than 90% of the technology has been transferred to international major manufacturers or domestic manufacturers, demonstrating the benefits and applications of real industrial cooperation.In the future, the Ministry of Economic Affairs will continue to assist the industry in research and development, innovation and technology, and transformation and upgrading, becoming the core driving force for Taiwan's industrial transformation leaps and bringing innovative water to the industry.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research, said that the Institute of Industrial Research has been deeply engaged in scientific and technological research and development and cross-field innovation technologies, exploring industrial needs and solving industrial problems.The 2023 CES Innovation Award is one of the most eye-catching awards in science and technology in the world.This year, the number of products applied for more than 2,100 to compete for 28 categories, and the application volume hits a new high. The Institute of Industrial Research won the award for "I see AI fish tank". "I see AI fish tank" integrates smart display, artificial intelligence recognition, and human-computer interaction technology.It has been applied to the National Museum of Marine Science and Technology in Taiwan, strengthening the achievements of people's ocean exploration and intelligent cultivation, providing more interactive options than traditional aquariums, and can be applied in medical surgery simulation or related exhibition fields in the future, bringing exhibition methods such as virtual and real interaction and immersive experience in the post-epidemic period.In the future, the Institute of Technology will continue to be guided by user needs, focus on the integration of market applications and scientific and technological research and development, take "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" as the key technical direction, and create innovative and valuable technologies through cross-field cooperation, transform from scientific and technological innovation to value innovation, bring changes to life with scientific and technological power, and create the true application value of science and technology.
"IV AI Fish Tank" is the world's first fusion technology to use virtual and real technology, which can show the information of relevant marine creatures based on tourists' sight. The accuracy rate is as high as 98%. It can also be used for gesture control to achieve interactive needs through contactless methods.This year, the eight innovations of the Industrial Research Institute in the fields of sports and fitness, artificial intelligence, machinery and information communication will be exhibited at CES 2023, Booth 9855, LVCC North Hall, Las Vegas from January 5 to 8.
