Taiwan Science and Technology Shining International Institute of Technology won the CES 2019 Innovation Award

Date: 2018/11/09
The most anticipated innovative technologies in CES 2019 are released! The 2019 CES 2019 Innovation Awards announced today.With the support of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology stood out among the technical products of many exhibitors around the world with its "high load, high range commercial drones", and won the CES 2019 Innovation Award in Robotics and Drones. At the same time, the new startup company of the Industrial Research Institute "Chuangjing Technology" won the Home Appliances Innovation Award for "e Lijing Disinfection Spray Manufacturing Machine".These two award-winning products will be exhibited in the CES Innovation Awards exhibition area from January 8 to 11, 2019.
The CES Innovation Awards is mainly established to commend the outstanding technology and design of innovative products.The Consumer Technology Association of the United States (CTA) will be selected based on the standards of product engineering quality, art design, consumer demands, social or industrial influence, originality, product market competitiveness, and will be awarded innovation awards and best innovation awards respectively.This is an excellent honor for the exposure of new products from major manufacturers in global brands, and it is also a must-fight for major manufacturers over the years. In addition to the Institute of Technology Research, the winners of the CES 2019 Innovation Awards also include well-known companies such as NVIDIA, Honeywell and Sony.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that the Institute of Technology won the CES Innovation Award for the second consecutive year;this international recognition oriented towards consumer products proves that the Institute of Technology's focus on market-oriented R&D results can stand out in the global innovation stage, and also demonstrates the Institute of Technology's commitment to create economic value with its outstanding innovation capabilities.The award-winning "High Load and High-Range Commercial Drone" has the characteristics of high load and long-term flight, providing applications such as industrial operations, air patrols, emergency rescue, etc., opening up a new imagination of traffic carrying.In recent years, the Institute of Industrial Research has strengthened its cultivation and accelerated the development of new innovation, allowing the Institute of Industrial Research to enter the market with a new innovation.Chuangjing Technology adopts patented technology development of the Institute of Industrial Research to convert tap water into disinfectant water through electrochemical methods, meets the needs of the disinfection market and solves the problem of sanitation and disinfection for the public. We will continue to cultivate new startups like Chuangjing Technology, and work closely with the industry and academia to play the role of the bridge between Taiwan and the international community, and lead the industry into a new blue ocean with innovative technologies.
Manniu-level "high load, high range commercial drone" is loaded heavier and flew longer
"High load and high-end commercial drone" uses a high-power density integrated generator and composite double-splier propulsion module developed by the Industrial and Research Institute, and a composite power system developed by the lightweight aviation engine to create an eight-axis multi-rotor multi-function drone.It has the advantages of high load and long battery life. With a load of 30 kg, the endurance can reach 45 minutes.If the load of 20 kg, the navigation time can be up to 1 hour. The hovering and low-altitude and high-load operation functions of this drone can provide diverse applications in various industries.For example, the inspection and hindrance cleaning of medium and high-voltage tower transmission and distribution systems of the power supply and transmission system;in agriculture, it can be used for special plant protection and large-scale plant protection applications following mountain terrain; in terms of disaster prevention and relief issues, in addition to air shooting inspections and emergency relief materials delivery, etc., it can also play the role of temporary communication relay station for disaster relief.
The magician of the water "e Lijing Disinfection Spray Manufacturer" converts water into disinfection water
"e Lijing Disinfection Spray Manufacturing Machine" is a product developed by the Industrial Research Institute's new startup company - "ELECLEAN Chuangjing Technology" using patented technology of the Industrial Research Institute. With brand-new electrochemical technology, high-reactive oxygen disinfectant can be produced after adding tap water to power on. Unlike traditional disinfection products, chemical additives such as preservatives, stabilizing agents, antibacterial agents or fragrances are required.eLijing has four major advantages: safety, strength, economy and convenience. It is the only high-efficiency disinfection manufacturing machine in the world that can destroy bacteria and virus cells by adding water and power. It has launched the "Personal ELijing Disinfection Spray Manufacturing Machine", which has been well received by all walks of life. It not only successfully cooperates with many companies to provide consumers with a safe disinfection line, but also won the recognition of this year's Foreign Trade Association Taiwan Premium Award and will be exhibited at the 2019 CES US Consumer Electronics Exhibition.
The Industrial Research Institute's "High Load, High-Range Commercial Drone" won the CES 2019 Innovation Award, with a load capacity of 30 kilograms and a battery life of 45 minutes, which is suitable for material transportation and other high-load industries.
"e Lijing Disinfection Spray Manufacturing Machine" is a product developed by the Industrial Research Institute's new startup company Chuangjing Technology applied patented technology of the Industrial Research Institute. After adding tap water to power on, it can produce high-reactive oxygen disinfectant water.This time it won the CES 2019 Innovation Award.
