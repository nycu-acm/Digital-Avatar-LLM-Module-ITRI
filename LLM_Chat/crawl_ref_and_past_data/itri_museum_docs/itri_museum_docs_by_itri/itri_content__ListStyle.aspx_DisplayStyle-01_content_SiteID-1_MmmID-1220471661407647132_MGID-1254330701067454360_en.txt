Technology introduction
Soft touch AMOLED panel technology.
In response to the soft display screen needs for smart life, the Industrial Research Institute has complete technical solutions from panel design, structural stress simulation, soft substrate, multi-functional upper plate, soft gas barrier packaging and TFT back plate production, and has successfully completed the development of AMOLED panel technology that is wear-resistant, scratch-resistant, impact-resistant and bendable.
Application and benefits
Soft displays have product characteristics such as bendable and foldable, not easy to break, and can break through the application limitations of existing displays, change product design and bring new applications.Application fields include smart handheld devices, laptop computers, smart homes, smart mobile and other fields.
