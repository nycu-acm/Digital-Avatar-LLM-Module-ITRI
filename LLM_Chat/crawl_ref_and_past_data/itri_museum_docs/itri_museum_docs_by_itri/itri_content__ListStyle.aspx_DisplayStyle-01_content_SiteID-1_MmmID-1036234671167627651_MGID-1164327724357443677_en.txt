Xiao Cifei Academician
Academician Xiao Cifei is a senior consultant of Changxing Materials Industry Co., Ltd. and has been with Changxing Materials for nearly 40 years. He has served as executive vice president, general manager, vice chairman and strategy director. He participated in the company's development of key materials in the semiconductor and optoelectronics industries, breaking the monopoly of some special chemicals, and establishing my country's independent supply chain.Currently, he has been transferred to a senior consultant, committed to inheriting valuable industrial experience and business practices, promoting the transformation of state-owned businesses, and setting an example for Taiwan's industry to take root.
Academician Xiao graduated from the Department of Chemical Engineering of National Taiwan University and obtained a master's degree in corporate management from the National Political University. Later, he joined Changxing Materials (then called Changxing Chemical) for services, and assisted the company in the development and expansion of circuit board UV monomer LCD and IC materials.At that time, Taiwan's information industry and semiconductor industry were booming, and many electronic materials were in the hands of foreign investors. Academician Xiao actively participated in the promotion of Changxing Chemical's factory expansion and transformation. Based on the research of the Changxing Institute of Luzhu Changxing Research Institute in Kaohsiung, he participated in the establishment and operation of Changxing Semiconductor Materials (CMP) subsidiary in Luzhu Science Park. He was also awarded the ERSO Award of Pan Wenyuan Cultural Education Foundation, which indirectly successfully promoted the reduction of the cost of the relevant materials of the Taiwan circuit board industry material UV light curing industry LCD and IC industry, assisted in improving the competitiveness of Taiwan's IC and semiconductor industry, and was an important participant in Taiwan's science and technology materials industry.
Using his international connections, industrial R&D and business experience accumulated over the years in Changxing Materials, Academician Xiao has actively assisted the government in the transformation and guidance of state-owned enterprises in recent years, assisted young engineers in state-owned enterprises such as Taiwan Sugar and CNPC to form an elite R&D team, find niche and market demand projects, stimulate the potential energy of state-owned enterprises, and contribute to promoting the transformation of state-owned enterprises and establishing a traditional industrial transformation platform in Taiwan.
