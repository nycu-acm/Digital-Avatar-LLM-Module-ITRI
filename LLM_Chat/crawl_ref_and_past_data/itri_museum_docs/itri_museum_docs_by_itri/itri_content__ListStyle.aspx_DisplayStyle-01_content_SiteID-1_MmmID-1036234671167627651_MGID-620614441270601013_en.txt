Academician profile
Academician Lu Zhiyuan
Chairman Lu Zhiyuan is the founder of Xinquan Technology Co., Ltd. and is also the general manager of Wanghong Electronics Co., Ltd. Chairman Lu spared no effort to promote the development of the semiconductor industry, and was committed to improving Wanghong's R&D technology and manufacturing capabilities of innovative non-volatile memory, leading Wanghong to obtain more than 5,000 high-quality international patents. In 2011, the US Patent Committee evaluated 240 semiconductor leading manufacturers around the world. Wanghong Electronics' patent value is the first in Taiwan and ranked 11th in the world in patent strength, making Wanghong Electronics a world-class leading manufacturer.At the same time, Xinquan Technology's focused wafer testing and IC finished product testing business has become an indispensable part of the cutting-edge semiconductor production process.During his tenure as deputy director of the Institute of Electronics of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, Chairman Lu Zhiyuan presided over the Micron Project of the Ministry of Economics, led Taiwan Semiconductor to make a lot of contributions to the world stage, and was recognized by the Executive Yuan Outstanding Science and Technology Talent Contribution Award.
Chairman Lu's contribution to the semiconductor industry has won awards and recognitions, including Pan Wenyuan's Outstanding Research Award, Academician of the Society of Science and Technology Management of the Republic of China, APS Fellow of the American Physics Society, IEEE Fellow, and IEEE Fellow, and awarded the IEEE Frederik Philips Award, known as the Oscar of the Science and Technology R&D Engineer Industry.In addition, Chairman Lu has served in industrial groups such as the Taiwan Semiconductor Industry Association, the Science Park Association, and the Global Semiconductor Association (GSA) for many years, actively promoted the practical development of semiconductors, and has excellent performance and influence in the academic and industrial circles, which has had a great influence and contribution to the development of Taiwan's semiconductor industry and talent cultivation.
