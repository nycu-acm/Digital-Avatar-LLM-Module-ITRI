Application scope
Hydrogen energy is an important energy for Taiwan's future active development. The IEA predicts that global hydrogen demand will reach 420 million tons in 2050. In 2019, the Institute of Technology has planned the "Taiwan's 2050 hydrogen application development technology blueprint". The short-term development strategy is mainly based on product demonstration and verification, establish independent brands in the medium and long term, capture the international hydrogen energy market, and lay the foundation for domestic hydrogen energy development.
Establish a local hydrogen energy supply chain, connect substantive cooperation between upstream, midstream and downstream industries, promote the development of local electrolytics and fuel cell industries, and accelerate the development of the hydrogen energy industry.
