Ethylene precision polymerization technology is for Taiwan Petrochemical Innovation Bureau
Compiled/Lai Wanjing
From general use to high value
The Institute of Technology has developed "Ethylene Precision Polymerization High Value PE/POE Technology", breaking through the past technical bottlenecks in high-end polymerization catalysts and processes, and opening the door to Taiwan to enter the high-value materials market.
For a long time, Taiwan has been subject to technical thresholds and import dependence in the field of high-end polyolefin materials.In order to break the dilemma, the Institute of Technology has developed the "Ethylene Precision Polymerization High Value PE/POE Technology", which not only breaks through the bottleneck of polymer performance and process, but also has the potential to be applied to high-value applications such as high-strength fibers and green energy cables. It has a new direction for the petrochemical industry and won the Gold Medal for Outstanding Research Award.
High-order polyolefin materials are mainly used in films, pipes, wires and cables, automotive parts, medical and sanitary products and other fields.However, domestic high-end polyolefin materials mainly rely on imports and lack related material technology. For this reason, the Industrial Research Institute has developed "Ethylene Precision Polymerization High-value PE/POE Technology", which not only breaks through the past technical bottlenecks in high-end polymerization catalysts and processes in China, but also opens the door to Taiwan to enter the high-value materials market in terms of functionality and application breadth.
"Our goal is to turn Taiwan from a country that can only produce universal plastics to a technology supplier who can make and even export high-end polyolefin materials," said Shi Guocheng, technical director of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering, the Institute of Industrial Research Institute.
The so-called ethylene precision polymerization technology refers to precisely regulating the molecular weight, distribution and structure of the polymer by controlling the catalyst design and process conditions of the polymerization reaction, and then creating materials with specific physical and chemical properties.The key to achieving all this is "single-point catalyst"."Traditional Ziegler-Natta catalysts are multi-active sites. Although they are suitable for mass production, they are difficult to accurately control the molecular structure and have limited functions," Shi Guocheng explained. "We designed catalysts such as metallocene and postmetallocene at single reaction sites, which can effectively control the chain segment structure and give the material higher mechanical strength, elasticity and environmental resistance."
Low-winding, high-cleaning and meet sensitive application needs
For example, the high-three-dimensional barrier ligand chromium catalyst designed by the team can effectively improve the production selectivity of 1-hexene and 1-octene. It not only has high reaction efficiency, but also has stable quality, and reduces the risk of equipment corrosion caused by chlorine-containing assisted catalysts in the past process.Another highly solubility metallocene catalyst can not only flexibly regulate density and elasticity when producing polyolefin elastomers (POEs), but also greatly reduce metal residues and environmental burdens.
In the field of ultra-high molecular weight polyethylene (UPE), the team has also made forward-looking breakthroughs.UPE is suitable for making high-strength fibers such as body armor and is also widely used in industrial parts, but in the past, relevant catalysts and polymerization technologies were lacking in China."The UPE made from single point metallocene catalyst not only has low winding characteristics, but also has significantly improved processing efficiency. It is suitable for mass production of high-strength fibers, but also has high cleanliness and low impurities, meeting the needs of sensitive applications such as medical care, electronics and humanoid robots." Shi Guocheng gave an example.The team has currently conducted trial production of high-strength fibers and negotiated technology transformation and mass production with leading domestic petrochemical manufacturers.
Get out of technical blockade and use strength to build an independent route
In the past, high-end material catalysts were long in the hands of major international manufacturers. Taiwan had to rely on imported materials for the high-value application of olefins. Not only was the cost high and the specifications were limited, which also squeezed downstream industrial development space.Shi Guocheng said that the team reconstructed the entire system with very little information. "We must read a lot of international patent documents, repeatedly ponder the structural design, simultaneously establish a water-absorbing and oxygen-absorbing polymerization environment technology, and repeatedly experiment with the performance and polymerization reaction of catalysts, so that we can do it."
In addition to the technological innovation of catalysts and processes, the team is paying attention to market application potential simultaneously.Taking POE as an example, the Industrial Research Institute has developed products for cable covering and insulating materials, and has completed the test and verification of the insulation layer for solar cables, which complies with the international standard of IEC 62930. These materials have low metal residue and single solvent process design, which is easy to recover and are suitable for high-end green energy field applications such as solar packaging, and meet the industry's needs in energy transformation and low-carbon sustainability.
Find a new positioning and fight against the Red Sea with high value
In recent years, China has successively built seven petrochemical zones, resulting in overcapacity of basic raw materials such as ethylene and propylene, and has also compressed the living space of Taiwan's traditional petrochemical industry.How to break through this red ocean?"Only by developing towards high-value and differentiated application products can we break out," Shi Guocheng emphasized.Taking the high-value ethylene materials developed by the Industrial Research Institute as an example, whether it is a substitute for imported POE or a UPE applied to high-strength fibers, it proves that Taiwan has the strength and potential of self-made high-end materials.
"We hope that Taiwan will not only be the role of downstream OEM, but can become a supply partner with strategic technology and brand value in material technology." Shi Guocheng said that the Institute of Technology has cooperated with large domestic petrochemical companies to plan to introduce this set of ethylene precision polymerization technology, and is expected to carry out the construction of a trial production line to further promote the commercialization of materials.
Under the global trend of promoting green and high-value materials, mastering core catalysts and basic polymerization technologies has become the key to industrial upgrading.In the future, the Industrial Research Institute will also expand its technology to more diverse polymer systems, such as high-function ethylene and propylene derivative materials, and its application areas cover construction, energy, high-voltage cables, electronic components, etc., providing technical support for transformation for the local petrochemical industry.
