Technology introduction
Cloud-based smart air compressor energy-saving system.
Combined with embedded computing core modules, composite multi-channel sampling, wavelet calculation, expert intelligence and wireless transmission are used to create an efficient cloud-based energy-saving management system to help optimize industrial energy.
Application scope
Industrial IoT integrated control and control technology, develop industrial IoT integrated control technology with an instant energy service system platform, to accurately grasp the energy flow of public systems, optimize equipment energy efficiency evaluation and management, and strengthen energy use efficiency.
Optimize energy management efficiency, improve the accuracy of energy consumption assessment by 22%, increase the energy saving potential of equipment by 29%, and significantly improve the evaluation timeliness, providing high-value service applications for the energy industry.
Smart energy-saving regulation, with functions such as On-line air compressor grading, system optimization and energy declaration, can reduce energy consumption by 17% to 54.74%, significantly improving the efficiency of industrial equipment.
Carbon reduction benefits
Save 17.6% of industrial power consumption, the total annual electricity savings reach 24 billion kWh, and reduce electricity bills by 72 billion yuan.
It is suitable for manufacturers' new purchase market and existing product market, and is expected to be imported into tens of millions of equipment systems, with a potential business opportunity of about 43.6 billion yuan.
It has been used in 111 working places including Xinfu Textile and Heyou Textile.
