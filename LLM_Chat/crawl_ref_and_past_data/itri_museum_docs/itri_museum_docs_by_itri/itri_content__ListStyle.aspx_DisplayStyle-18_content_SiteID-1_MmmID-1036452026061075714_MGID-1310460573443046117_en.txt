The UK Office of the Institute of Technology Opens
Compiled/Lai Wanjing
Taiwan-English Strategic Hub Launched
The Institute of Technology has expanded its European layout and established a new office in London, UK at the end of May. It has officially signed a letter of intent for strategic cooperation with Catapult Network, a UK national innovation engine, to deepen cooperation between the two sides.
Faced with challenges such as changes in the global industrial and economic situation and supply chain restructuring, the Institute of Technology has expanded its European layout and held the "English Office of the Institute of Technology and Research in the UK and Taiwan-English Forum" in London, UK at the end of May, which is its fifth overseas base after the United States, Japan, Europe and Southeast Asia.At the same time, the Institute of Technology and Research has also officially signed a letter of intent for strategic cooperation with the UK's national innovation engine Catapult Network to deepen cooperation between the two sides.In the future, the UK Office of the Institute of Industrial Research will continue to strengthen bilateral cooperation, connect Taiwan-English scientific research and industrialization resources, promote the substantive implementation of key technologies, and help the Taiwan-English industry work together to develop the global market.
Economic Minister Guo Zhihui congratulated him on cross-sea video recording and said that Taiwan has deep strength in the field of semiconductors and information communications, and combined with the UK's leading advantages in scientific research and innovation, it will bring great cooperation opportunities to both sides.With the "Taiwan-UK Innovation and R&D Cooperation Plan" jointly supported by the British Innovation Agency under the Ministry of Economic Affairs and the British Innovation and Technology Department, coupled with the support of the British Office of the Institute of Technology Research, Taiwan and the UK will continue to deepen cooperation in the three major aspects of technology, industry and market, opening up more win-win situations, and also symbolize the cooperation between the two countries in the fields of science and technology and innovation to a deeper partnership.
Wu Zhengzhong, chairman of the Institute of Technology Research, said that Taiwan and the UK have established a solid foundation for cooperation in the fields of science and technology, scientific research and industrial innovation for many years, and have continued to deepen partnerships through bilateral R&D plans and talent exchanges.The UK has long been in the leading position in science and innovation, while Taiwan has become a key promoter in the global value chain with its strong technical strength and R&D energy.Therefore, the UK Office of the Institute of Technology will be a substantial platform, as an important base for knowledge exchange, talent cultivation and joint innovation, connecting the advantages of both Taiwan and Britain, accelerating the commercialization of innovative achievements, and creating value for global industries.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Technology Research, said that in the past, the Institute of Technology Research has had close exchanges with Catapult in the UK and has promoted cooperation with CSA Catapult and ORE Catapult respectively in the application of compound semiconductors and offshore wind power. This time, the two parties will formally sign a letter of intent for strategic cooperation.The two parties will give full play to their advantages in strategic fields such as 6G communications, quantum technology, net zero technology, biotechnology and medical care, artificial intelligence and smart manufacturing, and deepen the link between Taiwan and the UK through cross-border talent exchange, field verification and advanced joint scientific research projects, and promote long-term cooperation between Taiwan and the UK in the field of innovation. It is expected to help Taiwan's industry improve its competitiveness and help the industry to be high-value and globalized.
