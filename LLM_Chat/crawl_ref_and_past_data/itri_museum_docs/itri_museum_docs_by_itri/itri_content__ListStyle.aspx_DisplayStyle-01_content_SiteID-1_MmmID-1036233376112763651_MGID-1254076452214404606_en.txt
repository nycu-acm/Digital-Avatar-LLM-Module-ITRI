Technology introduction
Automotive AI-ADAS technology and application development.
AI-ADAS technology integrates components such as light, radar, camera, etc., and has key core technologies such as deep learning image recognition, multiple perception fusion, and real-time event reasoning.It is equipped with a networked self-driving bus and is used for Hsinchu County high-speed rail self-driving shuttle service.
Since 2010, the Institute of Technology and Research has jointly promoted the Hsinchu County Government, Science and Technology Star, Chewang Electronics and Quanding Technology to promote the experimental plan for self-driving and shuttle operation of Hsinchu County high-speed rail, and conducted verification and testing on unmanned vehicle technology (Proof of Concept; POC) and service (Proof of Service; POS). This project is tested on busy downtown roads with mixed traffic between automobiles and locomotives, and is equipped with Cellular Vehicle-to-Everything; C-V2X technology developed by the Institute of Technology, integrating roadside communications, sensors and traffic signs, reducing blind spots for self-driving detection and improving self-driving operation safety. This technology has also won the 2019 ITS World Congress Industry Achievement Award and the Edison Awards Silver Award.
This project has a total of four self-driving buses to be put into verification and testing.Each self-driving bus is equipped with eight cameras and three optical radar sensors, which are respectively configured on the front, side and rear of the vehicle body. In order to provide the functions of the autonomous driving control system to stabilize the steering, obstacle orbit and stop on the road, the Industrial Research Institute has developed a peripheral sensing subsystem, integrating sensing and communication components such as light transmission, radar, camera, satellite positioning, inertial navigation, C-V2X, etc., and has key core technologies such as deep learning image recognition, multiple perception fusion, and real-time event reasoning. It is designed for the special road environments of Taiwan and Asia (such as complex Hazhi intersection, mixed flow of automobiles and locomotives, heavy rain), effectively detecting road objects, and greatly expanding the vehicle peripheral sensing area.
Features and innovation
Real-time image deep learning object detection technology
Provide two-dimensional coordinates for effective positioning objects, accurately identify information about objects on the road, such as pedestrians, cars, trucks, buses, locomotives and bicycles, and also adjust the detection model structure and expand the detection of triangular cone objects, so that self-driving vehicles can safely drive when they encounter road construction.At the same time, this technology further combines object tracking technology to optimize computing efficiency, reduce system load, provide continuous and stable object sensing information, and effectively improve driving safety of sensing and fusion technology and vehicle control technology.
Guangda deep learning object detection technology
Guangda deep learning object detection technology uses point cloud data from multiple optical radars as input, and combines object detection technology of real-time image and optical radar. In addition to giving full play to the advantages of real-time image object detection, it can also use optical radar to make the selection of three-dimensional object frames more fit and positioned more accurately, improving the reliability of autonomous driving vehicles.
Perception Fusion Technology
The networked self-driving bus is equipped with multiple cameras and optical radar sensors. The Late-fusion method is used to integrate multi-sensor object detection information to provide obstacle sensing information required for functions such as stable steering, obstacle orbiting and stopping on the road. The main method is to obtain optical radar and real-time image output at synchronous time, and project the optical radar information to the camera coordinate system through the projection conversion matrix of the real-time image and optical radar coordinate system. The corresponding object is found through the Intersection over Union (IoU), and the final target object frame information is filtered out based on the fusion decision mechanism.This technology can effectively detect road objects, provide accurate identification of pedestrian and vehicle objects, as well as object size and coordinate positioning information, and greatly expand the sensing area around the vehicle to improve the reliability of autonomous driving vehicles.
Event reasoning technology
Event reasoning technology is mainly based on the results of perceptual fusion of hardware and software and deep learning image recognition, and conducts inference of real-time prediction of dynamic road events of objects. By predicting whether pedestrians’ intention to cross the road, they can react early before the pedestrian’s movement behavior changes, resulting in slower and slower behaviors that are closer to general driving.The main method is to generate the probability of whether the adjacent vehicle enters the self-driving lane or pedestrians crossing the intention of crossing the road, and cooperate with the position and speed information provided by three-dimensional object detection, and at the same time, it introduces the driving direction, the distance between the body and the lane center line/dividing line, greatly improves the prediction probability when the object changes lane, and then early warning of the self-driving time point to optimize braking control.
The number of times and promotions
Effective use of the time-based phase can improve traffic accidents. Recently, stories of pedestrians' threading incidents have frequently spread, causing foreigners to have bad impression of the hell of pedestrians in Taiwan. The Ministry of Transportation comprehensively promoted the "peedy-based phase" and "peedy-based phase" in May 2023. This practice is the most effective way to avoid human-vehicle conflicts in the short term."Peering-only time-to-peering" means that all pedestrians are finished and the vehicle is driven again. When the crossroads are set up, the green lights of pedestrians in all directions will light up at the same time, and the driving number signs are all red lights, which will reach the time-to-way division of people and vehicles. As for the "Peering-only time-to-way division of pedestrians", the green lights of pedestrians are lighted up a few seconds earlier (usually 5 to 10 seconds), and the driving number signs are turned on again, so as to make it possible to clearly see pedestrians walking on the pedestrian crossing line when turning, and avoid accidents caused by blind spots in sight.In addition, the broadcasting uses wireless communication technology to wirelessly transmit information on the driving computer. When the driver's vision in front is blocked by a large car or the distance is too far, he can still clearly know the intersection ahead as a reference for driving acceleration, deceleration and braking.
