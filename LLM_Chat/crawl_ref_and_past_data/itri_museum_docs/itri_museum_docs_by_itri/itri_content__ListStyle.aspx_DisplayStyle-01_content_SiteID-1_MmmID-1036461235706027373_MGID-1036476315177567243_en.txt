Introduction
Taiwan Display and Application Industry Association (formerly known as Taiwan Thin Film Crystal Liquid Crystal Display Industry Association of the Republic of China; TTLA) is mainly composed of domestic display panel-related and innovative and intelligent application industry technology development manufacturers. Currently, the members include more than 60 companies including Qunchuang Optoelectronics, AUO Optoelectronics, Hanyu Caijing, Yuantai Technology, Jiashida Technology, Ruisheng Optoelectronics, Qunfengjun Technology, Fucai, Fuchuang Display, Yi Optoelectronics and Industrial Research Institute.Actively participate in international organizations (such as IEC, SID, etc.), promote the formulation of standard platforms (such as IEC TC 110, ICDM, etc.) and the formulation of standardization of international LCD environmental organizations (such as WDICC, World Display Industry Cooperation Committee), and co-organize major international events such as important global display exhibitions (such as Touch Taiwan) and DIT (Display International Technology Conference).The association upholds its established purpose and tasks, gathers consensus on industrial development, promotes cooperation and international cooperation among members, and strengthens service to the industry.
