Surface energy measurement
Measuring the surface characteristics of powders, particles, fibers, and semi-solids, and described by surface energy, is a solid-phase surface characteristic analysis instrument. It is like the principle of gas-phase layer suction. The probe solvent is vaporized into the pipe column and passes through the surface of the sample to be tested. It is affected by the intermolecular forces of solid and gas and has different retention.
