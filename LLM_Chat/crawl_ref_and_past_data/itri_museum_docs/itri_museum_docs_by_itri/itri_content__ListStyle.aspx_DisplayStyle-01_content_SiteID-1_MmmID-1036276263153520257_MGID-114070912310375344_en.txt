The Institute of Industrial Research and Research went to Europe to start 6G international cooperation and actively deployed the next-generation communication ecosystem

Date: 2025/07/09
The Institute of Industrial Research and Research has joined hands with industry, officials, academic and research to participate in the EU's largest and most indicative next-generation communications R&D event "2025 EuCNC & 6G Summit", and also announced the "FORMOSA-6G" project for the first time, including the R&D of 6G new chips, non-terrestrial network (NTN) and other technologies, and the construction of end-to-end verification platforms, to build a complete industrial chain, and signed three important 6G industry-research cooperation.
In order to promote Taiwan’s next-generation communication technology and international cooperation in industry and research, under the leadership of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has joined hands with industry, officials, academic and research to participate in the EU’s largest and most indicative next-generation communication research and development event “2025 EuCNC&6G Summit”, and signed three important 6G industry and research cooperation.At the same time, the Taiwan-English 6G seminar jointly held by the University of Cambridge and the United Kingdom (UK Federated Telecoms Hubs; UK FTH) for the first time brings together Taiwan and the British Telecommunications Industry and Research Institute to jointly promote the innovation and international cooperation opportunities of 6G next-generation communication technology, and hopes to build a global innovation ecosystem.
This Taiwan 6G technology expansion team includes Taiwan Zitong Industry Standards Association, MediaTek, Renbao Computer, BenQ Materials, Lingyan Technology, Yaodeng Technology, Yichuan Technology, Tayya Technology, Entong Technology, Industrial Research Institute, the 6G Project Office of the National Science and Technology Association, Chung Tsinghua University, and other industry-official and academic research institutions. In addition to cooperation with the EU 6G project, it has expanded bilateral exchanges and cooperation opportunities with the UK and Germany. It has attracted about 300 people from research institutions, schools and large factories in 15 countries, and cooperated with more than 100 European research institutions, 6G-related organizations, universities, etc. In the future, Taiwan's independent technology will be jointly verified in international 6G fields such as the UK, Germany, the Netherlands and Spain, which will help Taiwan's next-generation communications industry grasp the development direction and system specifications of the future 6G and lay out international market business opportunities in advance.
Zhang Mingfang, a special member of the Industrial Technology Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, said that Taiwan plays an important role in the global ICT supply chain. In the past, the Ministry of Economic Affairs has also made international layouts for 6G's advanced technology research and development, leading industry, academia and research to the EU.The 6G era is a critical moment in international cooperation, and the future development of the network requires cross-border and cross-field cooperation.The Ministry of Economic Affairs will continue to work hard to promote industrial innovation, actively connect Taiwan and international R&D energy, promote R&D and international cooperation in 6G forward-looking technology, and promote Taiwan's industry to enter the European 6G R&D ecosystem.
The Institute of Technology and Research has published the "FORMOSA-6G" project for the first time in "2025 EuCNC&6G Summit", which includes the research and development of new 6G chips, non-terrestrial networks (NTNs) and the construction of end-to-end verification platforms to create a complete industrial chain.This time, the Industrial Research Institute signed three 6G international industry and research cooperation, jointly with the EU 6G-SENSES project and the Netherlands Institute of Applied Sciences (TNO) to deepen sensing and communication integration (ISAC) technology and promote the verification of reconstructible intelligent surface (RIS) technology in the 6G experimental field.At the same time, the Institute of Technology and Technology has cooperated with the German Fraunhofer Institute of Production Technology (Fraunhofer IPT) to jointly develop smart production line meta-universe solutions.
In the bilateral exchange of 6G technology in Taiwan and Britain, the Taiwan-English 6G joint seminar brings together Taiwan and the UK's official research and research, such as the UK Department of Science and Technology (DSIT), UK Telecom Innovation Network (UKTIN), UK Telecom (BT), UK Digital Technology Center (Digital Catapult), Semiconductor Application Technology Center (CSA Catapult), University of Cambridge, University of Bristol, etc., focusing on five key 6G technologies, such as non-terrestrial network (NTN), open wireless access architecture (Open RAN), AI native network, sensing and communication integration (ISAC), reconstructible intelligent surface (RIS), etc., hoping to promote next-generation communication technology innovation to build a global ecosystem.
Ding Bang'an, deputy president of the Institute of Information and Communications, said that "opening" has been an important core value of Taiwan since the 5G era.The Institute of Technology and Research has joined hands with Taiwan's industry, academia and research institutions to actively invest in Open RAN research, laying a solid foundation for the development of 6G. In order to accelerate the verification of 6G technology, the Industrial Research Institute has launched a 6G experimental network construction plan.This year, it will set up a 6G experimental network in the Zhongxing Campus in Hsinchu. As a hub for the industry to connect with international cooperation, international partners are sincerely invited to Taiwan to participate in joint testing.
Catherine Page, deputy director of the UK Department of Science Innovation and Technology (DSIT), said that the UK is committed to promoting the development of new generation digital technology, and has made significant progress in 5G and network coverage, and is actively planning to develop 6G at this stage.The three major goals of the future network development of the UK include reducing the digital gap by improving coverage and inclusion, ensuring the resilience and reliability of future networks, and achieving sustainable digital development.She affirms international cooperation with Taiwan in the fields of semiconductors, open wireless access networks (Open RAN), and is highly looking forward to continuing to deepen cooperation between Britain and Taiwan, and hopes that the two sides will work together to create more new opportunities for cooperation.
Professor Harald Haas of Cambridge University said that innovative technologies need to accelerate on-site verification to promote the industrialization process. The UK's leading advantage in innovative technology research and development is highly complementary to Taiwan's information and communication supply chain, and the cooperation potential between the two sides is unlimited.At the same time, he is also full of confidence in the long-lasting and fruitful cooperation between the UK and Taiwan, and looks forward to opening a new page for the UK and Taiwan bilateral cooperation.
The Institute of Technology has formulated the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" to support four major application areas based on intelligent enabling technology.We hope to accelerate cross-border cooperation through intelligent enabling technology communication technology.
