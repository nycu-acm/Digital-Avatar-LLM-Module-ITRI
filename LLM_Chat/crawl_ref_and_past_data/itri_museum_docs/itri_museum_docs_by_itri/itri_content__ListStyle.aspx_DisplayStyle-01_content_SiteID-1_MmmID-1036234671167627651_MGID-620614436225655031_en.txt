Academician profile
Academician Shi Zhenrong
Chairman Shi created Taiwan's personal computer industry, committed to developing innovative scientific and technological applications, implementing the concepts of humanities and technology islands and world citizens, and spared no effort to promote Taiwan's industrial upgrading and internationalization.Chairman Shi's many innovative business philosophy has enabled domestic and foreign enterprises and management academic circles to have a deeper understanding of the conditions for high-tech development and play a guiding role.He has won the "Science and Technology Management Award" of the Society of Science and Technology Management of the Republic of China, the Special Contribution Award for Promoting Taiwan International Brands, and the National Second-Class Scenic Star Medal.Chairman Shi has long assisted the government in planning the high-tech industrial environment at home and abroad, and has made specific contributions to the country's overall economic growth and investment in high-tech industrial development.
