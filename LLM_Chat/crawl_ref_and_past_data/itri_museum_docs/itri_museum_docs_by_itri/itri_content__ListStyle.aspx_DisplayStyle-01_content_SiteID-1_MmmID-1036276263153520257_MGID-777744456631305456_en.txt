The Institute of Technology won the Top 100 Innovation Institution Award, Honor International with innovative technology energy and patent influence

Date: 2018/01/25
Clarivate Analytics (formerly Thomson Reuters Zhiquan and Science Division) today won the "2017 Global Top 100 Innovation Institutions Award" and is the only award-winning R&D institution in Taiwan.
The Institute of Technology has sent another good news and won international recognition for its innovation! Clarivate Analytics (formerly Thomson Reuters Zhiquan and Science Division) today announced that the Institute of Technology and Research won the "2017 Global Top 100 Innovation Institutions Award" is the only award-winning R&D institution in Taiwan, and Taiwan only won the award with Hon Hai.This is the second time that the Institute of Technology Research has won this honor in 2014, and it recognizes the Institute of Technology Research and Technology's innovative scientific and technological research and development energy and international influence in patent layout.
Qiu Mingjun, consultant at Clarivate Intelligent Solutions, said that he is honored to announce that the Industrial Research Institute won the top 100 innovative institutions in the world in 2017. Qiu Mingjun pointed out that the Institute of Technology has continued to invest in innovative research and development in the past five years, and the patents applied for have been frequently cited by the third party.Therefore, it performed outstandingly in the Influence Index (Influence) of this selection, which also reflects the influence of the Institute of Technology's research and development energy on subsequent technological development. In addition, the Institute of Technology has also made good progress in the Globalization Index (Globalization), and its patent layout has a more international perspective and has been able to claim patent rights in major global markets.
Liu Zhongming, director of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, said that today's award not only shows the international influence of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research in patent layout, but also affirms the Institute of Industrial Research and Research and Research and Development.Taiwan is facing challenges in industrial upgrading, but the era of fighting alone has passed. Now it depends on a platform of cross-field co-creation to continue to upgrade. The Institute of Technology and Research has built an innovation park without walls, combining the nearby Qinghai University, Jiaotong University and Science Parks to connect the domestic and foreign industry and academic circles, and cook a pot of innovative stone soup to accelerate industrial transformation and upgrading. We look forward to gradually forming Taiwan's scientific and technological settlements and let the whole world feel the power of Taiwan's innovation.
In addition, Clarivate pointed out in its report on the 2017 Global Top 100 Innovation Institution Awards that the selection criteria for the Global Top 100 Innovation Institution Awards include the total number of patents in the database, the success rate of patent applications, the degree of internationalization of the patent portfolio, and the patent influence determined by the number of citations of documents.The Industrial Research Institute not only has attracted international attention in its performance in scientific and technological innovation, but also has an international influence in joint industrial patent layout.
Faced with the global technological innovation trend, high-quality patents have become a necessary condition for manufacturers to enter the international market and maintain industrial competitiveness.The Institute of Technology has obtained a total of 28,598 patents worldwide so far.Among them, invention patents account for more than 98%, maintaining a high level of innovative inventions.In terms of industrial applications, the number of technology transfers and revenue of the Institute of Industrial Research has hit new highs in recent years; the authorization amount of multiple patent portfolios has been 10 million. In recent years, we have actively promoted the complete patent analysis layout of cross-field and cross-legal cooperation, significantly improving the value of smart property in R&D results including smart machinery, biotechnology and medical materials, material chemistry, electronic and optoelectronics and information communications;not only avoiding the waste of R&D resources, but also creating a high-quality patent portfolio (Patent Portfolio), this cross-technical field combination of R&D and patents is a key advantage compared to other international industry-university-research institutions.
