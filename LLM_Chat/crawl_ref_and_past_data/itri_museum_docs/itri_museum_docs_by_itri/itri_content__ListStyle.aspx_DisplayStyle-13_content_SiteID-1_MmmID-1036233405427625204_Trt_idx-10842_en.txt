Technical name:
Multi-purpose processing technology of bamboo
Technology introduction
This technology combines innovative technologies in the fields of biotechnology, building materials and agricultural biotechnology, and is committed to developing diversified bamboo processing products.At present, seven innovative bamboo products have been developed, including new air purification products, high-quality bamboo charcoal cat litter, diverse home-based ant-driving products, natural fruit and vegetable preservation materials, green biological agents, bamboo wire health boards and bamboo fiber filter materials and other products. These products use bamboo resources and combine the concept of home and leisure, which not only improves the quality of life, but also introduces new concepts of health and wellness. In addition, through the second and third-level processing technologies of bamboo, the sales and management of first-level bamboo are driven, and the sustainable development of the bamboo industry and the improvement of market value are promoted.
Abstract
It mainly combines technologies from the fields of biotechnology, building materials and agricultural biotechnology. It has developed a total of 7 innovative basketball processing products, including new air purification products, high-quality basketballo charcoal cat litter, various home ant repelllent products, natural vegetable and fruit preservation materials, green biological agents, bamboo silk health boards and basketball fiber filter products, etc. It combines forest products with home living to improve the overall quality of life and achieve a new concept of health and wellness. It drives the sales and management of primary basketball materials through the secondary/tertiary processed products of the basketball industry.
Technical Specifications
Nano technology + biodegradable plastic → Prepare bamboo activated carbon composite filter, remove more than 80% formaldehyde/VOCs, 20% CO/biogas glue, and develop a desktop air purifier.
Deodorization + antibacterial → The deodorization rate is increased to 80%, the replacement cycle is extended, and the antibacterial rate is above 90%.
Natural ant removal formula → Plant extraction, avoiding termites with more than 80%, non-toxic, safer than commercial products.
Bamboo vinegar liquid controls pests and diseases → The prevention and control rate of powder scale insects, aphids, weevils and various diseases is 80%.
Bamboo charcoal preservation → Extend the shelf life of fruits by more than twice.
Bamboo wire fiberboard → Comply with Japanese regulations, humidity regulating and stable, with an antibacterial rate of 95%.
Bamboo fiber heavy metal adsorption → After surface modification, the adsorption rate is above 80%.
