Academician Miao Fengqiang
Miao Fengqiang, chairman of Lianhua Shentong Group, is a pioneer in Taiwan's computer information industry. He has been committed to the group's reform for more than 40 years, leading the group to successfully transform into an international information group and develop into more than 40 related enterprises in the world, distributed in IT R&D and manufacturing, 3C product channels, system integration, software R&D, consumer electronic brands and other industries. Its subsidiary Synnex Corp. is the world's third largest commercial IT product channel provider, Concentrix is ​​the world's top five BTO/CRM companies, and Lianqiang International is the largest 3C product channel provider in the Asia-Pacific region, and is known as the "Mr. Joint Venture and Acquisition".
Chairman Miao worked at Intel in the United States in his early years and participated in the design and development team of the well-known 8080 microprocessor. Feeling that Taiwan lacks computer manufacturing technology, he has returned from the United States to join Taiwan's first computer company: Shentong Computer, introduced Intel microprocessors, and actively participated in the promotion of the Hsinchu Science Industrial Park project that year, laying the foundation for the development of Taiwan's personal computer industry.
Chairman Miao has a precise vision in R&D and international layout, and has mastered the trends and is more advanced in the industry. It proposes an innovative operation model of global production management (Global Logistics) and achieves comprehensive e-e-in-Shentong Group and suppliers by improving the production, sales and logistics management structure. He is good at negotiating and breaking through joint ventures and strategic alliances with relevant industry leaders. Under the leadership of Chairman Miao, Lianhua Shentong has established a strong and diverse corporate territory, covering computers, information, IT channels, system integration, software R&D, petrochemicals, industrial gases, food and other fields.
In addition to corporate operations, Chairman Miao has been concerned about Taiwan's scientific and technological development for a long time and has been deeply committed to the infrastructure of information and communication industries. He has served as the convener of the National Information Infrastructure Group (NII) Private Advisory Committee and the Executive Yuan Industrial Automation and Electronics Private Advisory Group (iAeb), and the convener of the National Information and Communication Development Promotion Group (NICI) Private Advisory Committee.He encourages the development of the national information technology industry, advocates the implementation of the knowledge economy and knowledge industry, and is the best promoter of the development of my country's information and communication industry.
