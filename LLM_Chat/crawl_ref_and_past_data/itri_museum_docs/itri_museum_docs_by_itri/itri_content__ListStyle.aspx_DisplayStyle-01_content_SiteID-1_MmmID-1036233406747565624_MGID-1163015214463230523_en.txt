The Institute of Technology and the University of California, Los Angeles will jointly develop the next generation of magnetic memory in the future, hoping to combine the technical foundation and international combination of the Institute of Technology and Research.
5G and AI artificial intelligence have driven the growth trend of the semiconductor industry. As the chip size becomes smaller and smaller, high-speed and high-efficiency magnetic memory (Magnetic Random Access Memory; MRAM) technology has become the mainstream.With the support of the Technical Department of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the magnetic memory developed by the Institute of Technology has made the latest breakthrough.With the support of the Defense Advanced Research Projects Agency (DARPA), the Institute of Technology and the University of California, Los Angeles (UCLA) announced the signing of the VC-MRAM cooperative development project, and will jointly develop the next generation of magnetic memory in the future. It is expected to use the technical foundation of the Institute of Technology and Research, which is comparable to the international level and combined with the innovative concept of UCLA, to apply material components to the memory chip for computing and storage. In the future, it is expected to reduce energy consumption by nearly 100 times, increase the speed by more than ten times, and set a new milestone in innovative memory.
The Ministry of Economic Affairs stated that the Ministry of Economic Affairs has long invested resources to assist in the technological research and development of domestic semiconductor industry, and also urged the Institute of Industry and Technology to invest in the next-generation spin Hall magnetic memory (SOT-MRAM) technology to develop faster, more stable and amnesia-free new generation memory technology. It was published in 2019 at the International Electron Devices Meeting MRAM Poster Session (IEDM Poster). Its technical achievements have gradually been implemented, paving a smooth road for Taiwanese factories to enter the new generation of memory.Today, a deep R&D foundation attracts UCLA, a top academic institution with forward-looking R&D technology R&D energy to cooperate with the Industrial Research Institute to develop voltage controlled magnetic memory (Voltage Control Magnetic RAM; VC-MRAM).Compared with SOT MRAM, VC-MRAM has the characteristics of faster write speed (50% shorter) and lower read and write energy consumption (75% reduced), which is very suitable for the application needs of AIoT and automotive chips.In this cooperation, the two sides joined forces to further invest in the process of innovative memory technology development and industrialization, and strengthen the relationship between important American partners, successfully assisting domestic and foreign manufacturers in improving their international competitiveness and seizing the initiative in the next generation of advanced processes.
