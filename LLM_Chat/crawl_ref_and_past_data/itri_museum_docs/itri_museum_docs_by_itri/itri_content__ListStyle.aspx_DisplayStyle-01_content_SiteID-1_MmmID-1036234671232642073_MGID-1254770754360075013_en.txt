113 Annual "Academician of the Institute of Technology" Conference
Group photo of the 13th Academicians' Meeting of the Institute of Technology Research (113 years).
Time: September 6, 2013 (Friday)
Location: Conference Room 604, Building 51, Zhongxing Academy, Industrial Research Institute (No. 195, Section 4, Zhongxing Road, Zhudong Town, Hsinchu County)
Chairman: Academician Shi Zhenrong
Topic: Taiwan's industrial generational AI development initiative
AI artificial intelligence is an important key to the competitiveness of the new generation of industries. The Institute of Technology held the 113th annual academician meeting on September 6, focusing on the vision, goals, opportunities and promotion of Taiwan's generative AI development, as well as the construction of my country's AI center for all industries and industries, and proposed the "Taiwan Industrial Generative AI Development Initiative". The meeting was chaired by Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of the Zhirong Cultural and Education Foundation.Participating in the Academicians of the Institute of Technology Research include: Shi Zhenrong, Chairman of the Zhirong Cultural and Educational Foundation, Yu Huanian, Honorary Chairman and Senior Advisor of the Foresight Technical Steering Committee of the Institute of Technology Research, Sun Hong, Chairman and President of Mengli Group, Shi Qintai, Chairman of Pan Wenyuan, Chen Xingshi, Founder of Ronggang Group, Wang Kanglong, Thor Lecture Professor of Electrical Engineering at the University of California, Los Angeles, Yang Zhentong, Founder of Yali Electric Group, Xiao Ci, Senior Advisor of Changxing Materials Fei, General Manager of Wanghong Electronics, Zhang Youde, Chairman and General Manager of Yi'an Health Medical Section, Jiao Youjun, Chairman and Executive Director of Huabang Electronics, Yang Yumin, Chairman and Co-founder of Yushi Expo Biotechnology, Lin Xianming, Chairman and Strategy Director of Wischuang Zitong Co., Ltd., Yang Panchi, Professor of Internal Medicine at the School of Medicine of Taiwan University, Liu Kezhen, Chairman of Advantech Co., Ltd., Chen Qihong, Chairman of BenQ Jiashida Group, and Chen Shi'an, Director of Taichung Veteran General Hospital.Wu Zhengzhong, Chairman of the Institute of Technology Research and Liu Wenxiong, President, also attended the meeting.
Meeting conclusion focus:
1. Vision and objectives of Taiwan's generative AI development
Vision: Integrate humanistic thinking and technological innovation, promote industrial transformation with AI and enhance my country's overall competitiveness
Objective: Build an AI-based ecosystem of all industries and industries in my country, and build Taiwan into a global AI value co-creation partner
2. Opportunities and ways to promote the development of generative AI in Taiwan
Through five major strategies: the characteristics of the leverage industry, accelerate the research and development and application of AI technology, formulate legal norms for AI governance, improve AI data and basic environment, cultivate cross-domain AI talents, promote international partners' cooperation and co-creation, build AI centers of hundreds of industries, and accelerate the AI ​​transformation of small and medium-sized enterprises.
3. ITRI role expectations
Assist the localization of the AI ​​industry:
ITRI ​​assists AI localization (taking roots, finding industrial niches), and guides companies to find profit models, such as: promoting cooperation between North, Central and South East Expeditions and Industrial Research Institute.
ITRI ​​established an AI service company to use the 10 billion AI fund of the National Development Fund, and it will be helpful to small and medium-sized enterprises.
Talent support: ITRI talent pool assists the industry (such as: promoting talent circulation, industry research and co-creation cooperation model); linking international factories and talents.
New creation incubation: Make ITRI the cradle of Taiwan’s new AI innovation.
Organization: Strengthen the Centre for Key Cross-Domain Integration (Borrowing the expertise of academicians to participate in different groups).
