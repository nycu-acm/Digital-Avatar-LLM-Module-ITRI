Technology introduction
Cold chain energy-saving magician.
This system integrates artificial intelligence (AI) and Internet of Things (IoT) technology, dynamically adjusts the operating parameters of store power-consumers (freezers, air conditioners, lighting, etc.) to achieve optimal energy-saving control, and can support automated demand response (ADR) to assist the power grid to reduce load.
Application scope
Floating temperature control method, the refrigerator dynamically changes the temperature control point according to the load change, saving energy consumption of the refrigeration system.
Intelligent defrost uses AI technology to identify frosting features and defrosting only when frosting features are detected. The number of defrosting times per day can be reduced to 1 to 2 times, saving energy consumption of the refrigeration system.
Coordinated operation control, and based on dynamic environmental changes, the machine learning model can find the best operation combination of the most energy-saving air conditioner and refrigerator to reduce overall energy consumption.
Automated demand response, using AI algorithms to predict demand response events and automated demand responses can achieve a low-volume suppression of 5% user load baseline.
Carbon reduction benefits
The system can save 5-10% of electricity bills per year on average, and the contract capacity has reached a 5% reduction in the contract capacity. More than 3,000 stores have been introduced in Taiwan, making great contributions to energy conservation and carbon reduction.
