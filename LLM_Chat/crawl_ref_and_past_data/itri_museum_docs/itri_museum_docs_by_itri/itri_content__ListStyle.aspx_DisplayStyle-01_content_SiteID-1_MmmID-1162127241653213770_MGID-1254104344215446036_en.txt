Technology introduction
E-Cube mobile solar energy storage equipment.
E-Cube mobile solar energy storage equipment integrates photoelectric power generation, energy storage and low-carbon backup power, with modular and scalable characteristics.It can support off-grid power supply and rapid deployment of green power, helping to achieve net zero carbon emission target.
Application scope
Rapid deployment of low-carbon microgrids: a decentralized green energy power supply system can be quickly built, suitable for markets, temporary event venues, disaster relief and emergency power, and other scenarios.
Modular design, flexible expansion: energy storage capacity and solar power generation modules can be adjusted according to demand, adapting to application fields of different scales.
Smart energy management to improve energy stability: Built-in smart energy management system can realize load regulation, optimize energy storage and power generation strategies, ensuring stable power supply.
Support off-grid and remote areas power supply: suitable for remote areas where the power grid is difficult to reach, providing independent green energy power supply solutions to improve energy accessibility.
Respond to the instability of the power grid and emergency power supply demand: Even in the event of power outages or unstable power supply, it can continue to operate through energy storage and green energy technologies to ensure that the power supply of key equipment is not interrupted and promote the net zero emission target.
Carbon reduction benefits
Mobile solar light storage devices can maintain stable power supply through smart green power management and energy storage technology when power is unstable.Modular design allows the equipment to be expanded according to needs and adapt to different application scenarios, such as remote areas, temporary power demand in cities, markets or disaster relief sites, to achieve the concept of sustainable net zero.
