Technology introduction
Optical transmission-silicon photonic integration technology.
Driven by the metaverse, 5G, AI and other applications, the backend data center will bear a huge amount of data, the electrical signal transmission of traditional cables is no longer sufficient, and the penetration rate of optical transmission products will increase significantly. The Institute of Technology and Research actively develops high-speed and low-cost silicon photonic integration technology, and manufactures various optical transmission components on silicon photonic platforms. Their product features are large volume, high output value and low cost.
Features and innovation
The Institute of Technology has developed key technologies for silicon photon transmission chips, including component design, process integration, optical packaging and photoelectric testing technology, and is combined with the technical advantages of domestic industry and academia in various active and passive components, with the goal of a single-channel multi-channel optical transceiver module of 200Gb/s.
Application and benefits
It is hoped that by using Taiwan’s optoelectronics industry in precision process and assembly, combined with the advantages of the IC semiconductor industry in design, manufacturing and packaging and testing, we will establish a next-generation high-speed and low-cost light transmission silicon photonic assembly technology and industrial chain to develop industrial innovation and competitiveness.
