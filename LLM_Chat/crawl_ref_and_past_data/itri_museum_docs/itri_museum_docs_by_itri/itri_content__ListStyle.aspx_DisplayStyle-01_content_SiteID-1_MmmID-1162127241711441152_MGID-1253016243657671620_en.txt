Technology introduction
Deep decolorization technology for polyester fabrics.
Effectively improve the reusability of self-textile recycling polyester and realize the Fiber To Fiber cycle.
Application scope
Waste textiles are mostly treated in incineration. In addition to causing resource consumption, they also produce excess carbon emissions due to combustion. Polyester fabric deep decolorization technology can not only reduce the carbon emissions of the original incineration, but also obtain low-carbon polyester raw materials, achieving a win-win situation of carbon reduction and effective reuse of resources.
This technology uses environmentally friendly water-based decolorization accelerator to achieve deep decolorization of polyester fabrics to recover high-quality colorless polyester raw materials, solves the problem that domestic polyester fiber decolorization rate is poor, residual dyes are easy to crack and cannot be spinned, and recycling can only be made into dark textiles or downgraded for use.
Carbon reduction benefits
If polyester is recovered from waste textiles using the deep decolorization technology of polyester fabrics, it will not only reduce the carbon emissions of 2kg CO2e/kg that was originally incinerated, but also reduce the carbon emissions of 1.5 kg CO2e/kg, compared with the native polyester from petrochemical sources, which is 2.7 kg CO2e/kg, reducing carbon emissions by 45%.
