150,000 patented protective shields!The Institute of Technology Research, Industry and Technology Federation, and Electric Power Association promote the establishment of LOT industry alliance

Date: 2025/06/24
The Institute of Industry and Research, the National Industry Federation, the Electrical and Electronics Industry Association and the Chuangzhizhiquan Company (IP Bank) officially established the "LOT Industry Alliance", inviting nearly 20 heavyweight semiconductor, communication and PCB companies to support it.
In recent years, global industries have faced a surge in the risk of litigation of non-Practicing Entity (NPE), commonly known as "patent cockroach". According to IP Bank research, between 2022 and 2024, up to 70% of overseas patent infringement lawsuits by Taiwanese companies were filed actively by NPE.In order to help the industry jointly defend against the NPE threat, the Industrial Research Institute announced today (24th) that it will join hands with the National Industry Federation, the Electrical and Electronics Industry Association and the Chuangzhi Zhiquan Company (IP Bank) to officially establish the "LOT (License on Transfer) Industry Alliance", inviting nearly 20 heavyweight semiconductor, communication and PCB companies, including TSMC, Sun and Moonlight, Licheng, Silicon, World Advanced, Gigabyte, Zhiyi, MSI, Xinying Materials, Xinxing and other technology giants with a total annual revenue of more than NT$4.4 trillion, and the first wave is expected to gather more than 150,000 valid patents, and Taiwanese manufacturers and scientific research institutions jointly build a cross-industry "Smart Property Protection Network".
NPE does not produce, develop, or provide goods or services. Its business model is to acquire patents and charge manufacturers high premiums.The cost of initiating a lawsuit is extremely low, but for the defendant company, the operating threat and litigation cost burden are huge.The LOT Alliance is an effective defense strategy for international defense against NPE.It is adopted by major technology manufacturers such as Google, Dropbox, Canon, SAP, and Red Hat. The Industrial Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics uses science and technology projects to support the research and development and patent layout of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, and also actively strengthens the protection of intellectual and financial industries in Taiwan. The LOT Industry Alliance jointly established by the Institute of Industrial Research and Research and Enterprises has built a smart property joint defense network in my country through a complete patent defense mechanism, actively responding to NPE risks and improving global competitive resilience, showing the government's achievements in the value of intellectual and financial development and forward-looking technology protection.
Wang Pengyu, the legal director of the Institute of Technology Transfer and Law, said that the mechanism of the LOT industry alliance has three major characteristics: First, it does not interfere with commercial competition between enterprises; second, it only initiates the authorization when the patent is transferred to NPE, and will not weaken the value of patent independent application; third, this authorization does not involve technology disclosure or commercial secrets.Through the LOT industry alliance, the manufacturers' dispersed patent combat power will be upgraded to an industrial joint defense system. Enterprises no longer have to fight alone. They will also simultaneously protect the upstream and downstream of the supply chain and strengthen Taiwan's voice in the global technology competition.In the future, we will further establish cooperative relations with similar international organizations and large manufacturers, strive for greater protection for Taiwanese companies and create business opportunities for diversified patent use.
Xu Jieli, executive director of the All-China Federation of Industry and vice chairman of the Taiwan Electrical and Electronics Industry Association, said that the "LOT Industry Alliance" has built the first cross-industry "Smart Property Protection Network" for Taiwan, so that enterprises can effectively reduce operating risks and costs, so that corporate resources can focus on technological development market development, and strengthen the overall competitiveness of enterprises.
Liao Chengwei, Director of Intelligent Property of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, said that in recent years, Taiwan’s institutional and enterprises have won the top 100 innovative institutions awards in the world, and in 2024 it is the third highest in the world, which shows that Taiwan’s intelligence power and innovation energy cannot be underestimated.The establishment of the LOT Alliance is the best example of proving Taiwan’s scientific and technological research and development capabilities and showing the world that domestic businesses attach importance to innovation in protecting smart property rights.
The LOT Industry Alliance focuses on the key niches of Taiwan's technology industry in the global value chain, covering patented technologies in the fields of semiconductor processes, IC design, action and broadband communication, computer hardware architecture, smart electronic products and their peripheral applications.In order to take into account technical characteristics and market flexibility, the alliance adopts diversion management of two major technical fields: semiconductor and information communication. Members must choose one or participate at the same time according to their own R&D and patent layout.After joining the alliance, companies can instantly obtain NPE litigation defense, share cross-industry patent resources, save high litigation and settlement costs, and use the alliance platform to master patent information and risk warnings to strengthen the overall resilience of the supply chain.The Institute of Technology Research, Industry and Technology Corporation, Electric Power Association and the initiator sincerely invite more domestic and foreign companies to participate to jointly expand the protection scope of the "Smart Property Protection Network" and build a solid shield of wisdom and power for the industry.
