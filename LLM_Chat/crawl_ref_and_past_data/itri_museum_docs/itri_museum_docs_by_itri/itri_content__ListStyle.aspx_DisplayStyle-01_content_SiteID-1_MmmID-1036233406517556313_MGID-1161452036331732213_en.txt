About the Institute of Information and Communications
The Information and Communications Research Institute of Industrial Research Institute was established in 1990, focusing on the research on the topics required for the development of the ICT industry, the development of key technologies, and the improvement and industrialization of the added value of its R&D results.Over the past 20 years, from early personal computers (including laptops), wired communications and networks, to wireless and mobile communications, emerging smart terminals and cloud application services, Zitongshe is not only a pioneer in ICT technology, but also cultivates quite abundant and profound technical energy and outstanding talents, laying a solid foundation for domestic industrial development.In addition, the Institute of Finance and Communications has also cooperated with government policies to assist in integrating domestic industry, officials, academic and research sectors from scratch to participate in the organization activities and standard formulation of international ICT-related standards.Through the accumulation of technological innovation and intellectual finance, my country's visibility and influence on the global competition stage will be enhanced.
In recent years, the Institute of Zitong has broken through the original hardware-focused thinking framework of domestic industry, transforming into software centric, service orientation and system integration as the main axis. In addition to investing in the research and development fields of 5G wireless communication system and network technology, cloud system technology, advanced chip and system design technology, mobile device systems and audio-visual technology, Internet of Things and innovative applications, it has further expanded to emerging themes and applications such as financial technology, autonomous driving and unmanned vehicles, information security, and artificial intelligence deep neural network machine learning models.
Looking ahead, the Institute of Zitong will continue to strengthen its ability to integrate soft and hard integration and innovation, strengthen cross-field and domestic and foreign industry-university-research cooperation, and be brave to explore and expand new application fields and new business opportunities. In order to contribute in the deep development of technology, industrial benefit value, and social welfare promotion, give full play to the endless key momentum and show fruitful results!
