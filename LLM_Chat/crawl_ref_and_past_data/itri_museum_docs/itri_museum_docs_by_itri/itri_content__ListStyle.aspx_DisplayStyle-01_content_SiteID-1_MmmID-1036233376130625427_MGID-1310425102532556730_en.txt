Technology introduction
The patented eye drop product of the Institute of Technology provides a non-invasive, self-administered treatment option for middle- and late-stage elderly patients with dry macular lesions.
Using patented coordination supramolecular composite carrier technology and active ingredients with therapeutic potential, we develop eye drop products that can treat dry macular lesions to avoid the continuous deterioration of patients' vision.The eye drop formula can increase the exposure dose of eye target tissues, and reduce the endoplasmic reticulum pressure and the accumulation of retinal pigment epithelial cells to maintain the functions of photoreceptor cells and retinal pigment epithelial cells to achieve optic nerve protection effects.This technology can be extended to the development of therapeutic products for other posterior chamber diseases.
Features and innovation
The patented formula uses excipients approved by the U.S. Food and Drug Administration (FDA) to effectively deliver the drug to the lesions of the posterior chamber of the eye.
The drug administration by eye drops is better than the efficacy of the competitor drug Risuteganib (intravitreal injection, completing phase II clinical trials) in protecting the integrity of photoreceptor cells and reducing sub-epithelial cells of retinal pigment.
Eye drops showed good eye tolerance and safety in rabbit eyes for three consecutive months.
Application and benefits
Intervention in middle and late dry macular lesions through non-invasive treatment can improve treatment convenience and patient compliance.
Combining patented eye drop technology, expand new market opportunities for the treatment of post-eye atrial diseases.
