Technology introduction
Lithium ferromanganese battery manufacturing technology.
Transportation is one of the main sources of greenhouse gas emissions, and the conversion of transportation vehicles from oil to electricity is an effective solution to improve greenhouse gas emissions. At present, the power source of electric vehicles in the world is mainly based on lithium-ion batteries with high energy density.The safety, energy density, power density, life and cost of lithium batteries are the key to whether the electric vehicle industry can be accepted by consumers and flourish. The voltage of traditional lithium iron phosphate (LFP) cathode materials is low, which corresponds to a slightly insufficient output power;while the energy density of lithium manganese phosphate (LMP) is 20% higher than that of lithium iron phosphate, and has advantages such as safety, cost and life. It is regarded as the most promising cathode material in the next generation.
