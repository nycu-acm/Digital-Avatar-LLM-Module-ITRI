About the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering
Integrate the energy of chemical and material research and development, play the role of a forerunner of material technology research and development and a creator of smart assets, create industrial competitiveness, and cooperate with the development of emerging industries and the improvement of traditional industries. We are committed to the development of key technologies such as new generation electronic materials, green energy materials and components, high-value chemical materials and people's livelihood and welfare-related materials, and use value to demonstrate substantial industrial benefits. Through forward-looking pioneering R&D and holistic technical services, we will accelerate the development of industrial materials and chemical technology and enhance the international competitiveness of Taiwan's industry.
Vision
With the thinking of circular and sustainable ecological chain, we will build four-dimensional integrated technologies for new high-value/high-circulation materials, processes, software/hardware and application fields, and guide Taiwan's materials and chemical industry to transform into a low-carbon cycle sustainable high-value industry.
Development and business strategy
The Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering and various science and technology centers and base research institutes within the Institute of Technology Research and Technology, through the technological development blueprint and the innovation platform for special materialization technology, is a cross-field and cross-unit approach, and a special materialization technology innovation research and development platform for international leaders to lead the technological output.
Materials and Chemical Research Institute has the key industry demand and industrial value chain as the core, combined with the energy of domestic and foreign academic circles, strengthen cooperation with the industry, concentrate resources on the research and development of special chemicals and materials needed by the industry, choose a suitable business model, and establish a new company with product technology that enables material chemical technology to form an international-level industrial innovation structure and enterprise system for special chemicals/materials.
