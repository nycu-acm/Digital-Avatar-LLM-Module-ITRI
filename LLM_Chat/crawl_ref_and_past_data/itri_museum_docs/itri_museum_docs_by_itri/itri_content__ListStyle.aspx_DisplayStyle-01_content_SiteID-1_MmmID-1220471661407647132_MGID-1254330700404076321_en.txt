Technology introduction
Low diffraction transparent display technology.
In response to the future application of smart life field products, transparent display development will be regarded as a key project, but traditional transparent display devices have obvious blurred background images. Therefore, the Industrial Research Institute has developed low diffraction transparent display technology, which can greatly reduce the diffraction phenomenon of background light sources, improve the clarity of the background image of the display, and expand the application of smart transparent display related fields.
Application and benefits
Low-draining transparent display technology can be matched with the existing processes of panel factories, with high visibility and high transparency technology advantages. In the future, it can also drive display-related industries and connect emerging fields such as smart mobile, medical, retail and yacht.
