iSmartweaR Smart Sensing Clothes Successful Integration of Cross-Domain Technology
Written by/Li Xingyi Photography by/Huang Dingxiang
With the goal of the world's first mass-producible and washable smart clothes, the "iSmartweaR smart sensing clothes" dedicated to innovative research and development has successfully created a market niche and branding opportunity for Taiwan's textile industry.
Smart clothing has always been a market trend that all parties are optimistic about, but product prices, style design, and comfort in wearing are all difficult issues.The Institute of Technology starts from materials and processes, replacing silver particles and silver sheets with nano silver wires to provide the same touch and better conductive effects as fabrics. In addition, the use of contactless radar sensing technology provides basic functions such as measuring breathing, heartbeat and calculating calories. It has innovatively developed the "iSmartweaR Smart Sensing Clothes", which not only solves the usage restrictions that must be worn intimately, but also expands the scope of wearable applications, extending from sports and leisure and care to smart homes, as well as professional fields such as military, police, firefighting and medical care.
Breakthrough Innovation Opening from Nano Silver Wire
Sun Wenxian, manager of the Institute of Materials and Chemical Engineering, Industrial Research Institute, said that wearable devices such as watches may not be worn 24 hours a day. In contrast, clothes are still the most accepted form of use.Although many manufacturers claim to launch smart clothes, if you look deeper, you will find that there are almost no mass-produced products, or the unit price is unattainable. The problem lies in water washing. According to the experience of cooperative manufacturers, functional clothes must be washed at least 250 times (50 times for professional experiments), so that consumers feel that it is cost-effective and willing to buy them.
However, textile itself is a very complex technology. Different fabric types, weaving methods and structural combinations have an impact on the washing tolerance of clothes. The corresponding material development must adopt different reactions and formulas. These are new areas that no one has done in the past and have also become the best entry point for cumulative Know How.Sun Wenxian invested in the research and development of nano silver wires in the early days, allowing nano silver wires to have functions such as conductivity, thermal conductivity and antibacteriality. Later, he studied the structure of clothes in depth and found the answer to the problem of washing.
In fact, the current conductive method is to use spherical silver particles or sheet-like silver sheets, and the nano silver wire method itself is a big breakthrough.The second technological breakthrough is to apply the Roll to Roll production technology that has developed in the electronics industry to the conductive glue for printing clothes, and successfully produce the world's first and only washing smart clothes.
The third breakthrough in technology is that in the past, smart clothes had to be used as close-fitting clothes to have measurement functions. The radar sensing technology of the Institute of Technology can measure heartbeat and breathing at a distance of more than 20 cm, becoming the best solution. The style of smart clothes is no longer limited. Whether it is short-sleeved or long-sleeved, T-shirt or jacket, it can meet the wearable needs of different ethnic groups and different situations.
Lin Hongdun, director of the Center for Measuring and Technology Development of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, pointed out that one of the passive elements of radar sensing is an antenna, and its hard structure is difficult to integrate into clothes. Through the research and development of nano silver wires and polymer materials, the circuit diagram of the antenna is transferred to clothes, allowing the sensing elements and weaving to be smoothly combined.In the minified part of the antenna, it can be close to the pocket size, the antenna is completely invisible from the surface of the clothes, and there will be no discomfort when worn, and it can also achieve accurate, efficient and high-sensitivity measurement integration.
Going towards refined and professional development
The reason why smart clothes are complex is that they cannot be completed by a single industry. First, they must follow the specifications of the textile industry, without affecting the touch, feel and comfort, and must comply with relevant regulations such as washing fastness, sweat fastness, skin irritation and sensitivity tests; on the other hand, they must also follow the regulations of the electronics industry.The smart clothes of the Institute of Technology not only completely pass the above specifications, but also meet the AATCC professional washing standard of 50 times, which can be converted into household washing up to 250 times.
Another problem that all wearable devices must overcome is that if they want to be used for a long time, the battery will be large. The Institute of Technology has adjusted from firmware to achieve the most power-saving mode and can meet the monitoring of lifestyles. It can currently be used for 12 hours continuously.
Sun Wenxian said that since the Institute of Technology masters core technologies such as materials and processes, the R&D team can launch at least one to two new products per year based on nano silver wire.Continuous improvement of the prior art is another focus. In addition to the original screen printing, the selection of transfer paper has been added. As long as it is hot pressed on the clothes, it can be presented in the form of trademark patterns or famous brands, and is completely free from the restrictions of cloth.It is beautifully printed, flat, functional, and can also be resistant to pulling, rubbing and washing, which will be the direction of continuous improvement.
It is worth mentioning that smart fabrics are not just smart clothes, but based on this, extending smart fabrics to the research and development and application of smart homes. Professional markets such as military and police firefighting or disaster relief cannot be ignored. For example, police smart clothes can obtain police physiological information from the remote end to understand whether they are suitable for continuing on duty or in danger.
Lin Hongdun added that any innovative product has risks, and manufacturers are willing to cooperate with the Industrial Research Institute to invest, so that everyone can create new product value from mutual learning, so that the Industrial Research Institute's technology and materials can be diversified. It is also expected that through the integration of materials and sensing, the benefits of one plus one greater than two can be exerted, and the Taiwan industry will be able to change from OEM manufacturing to develop its own brand and technical value.
The Industrial Research Institute has developed the world's first washable smart clothes, bringing breakthrough innovation to Taiwan's textile industry.
