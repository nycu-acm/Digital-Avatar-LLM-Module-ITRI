Technology introduction
Lignoxy™ is an epoxy resin developed using the biomass lignin. It can solve the health and environmental problems caused by bisphenol A raw materials in epoxy resins. It has a very good protective effect when applied to metal materials.
Lignoxy™ is an epoxy resin developed using the biomass lignin.It can solve the health and environmental problems caused by bisphenol A raw materials in epoxy resins. It has a very good protective effect when applied to metal materials.
Features and innovation
Biomass epoxy resin has passed the testing standard for food utensils, containers, and packaging sanitary metal cans, and has reached the testing standards for bisphenol A, no residues of heavy metal hazardous substances, excellent adhesion with tinplate sheets, sufficient hardness, chemical resistance, acid and alkali resistance, and high temperature boiling resistance.
Application and benefits
In the future, biomass material derivative technology will be applied to food containers, construction and textile materials in combination with midstream and downstream manufacturers.
