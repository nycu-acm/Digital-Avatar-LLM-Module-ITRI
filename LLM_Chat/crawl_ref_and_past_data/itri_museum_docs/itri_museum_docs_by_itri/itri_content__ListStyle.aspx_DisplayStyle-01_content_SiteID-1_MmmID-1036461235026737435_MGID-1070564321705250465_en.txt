JEOL JSM-6500F
Field emission scanning electron microscope (FE-SEM) JEOL JSM-6500F
Equipment functions
Multifunctional field emission scanning electron microscope additional:
Energy dispersion X-Spectrometer (SDD EDS, Oxford X-Max 80 mm).
Cathode luminescence analyzer (CL, Gatan monoCL3).
Electron backscatter diffraction diffraction meter (EBSD, Oxford INCA Crystal 300).

Equipment Application

CL is used for luminescence spectral measurement, image analysis, trace impurities and defect analysis.

LEO 1530
Field emission scanning electron microscope (FE-SEM) LEO 1530
Equipment Service
Topography analysis: The surface features of an object or "how it looks", its texture; detectable features limited to a few manometers.
Morphology analysis: The shape, size and arrangement of the particles making up the object that are lying on the surface of the sample or have been exposed by grinding or chemical etching; detectable features limited to a few manometers .
Composition analysis: The elements and compound the sample is composed of and their relative ratios, in areas ~ 1 micrometer in diameter .
Equipment functions
Energy dispersion X-Spectrometer (SDD EDS, Bruker Quantax™ 10mm).
Equipment Specifications
Field emission (schottky type) electronic gun.

Operating voltage 0.5~30kV, Probe current: Max.>5 nA.
Detectors: QBSD, SE2, InLens.
Measurement function: X, Y direction distance, any two points distance, and angle can all be measured in multiple groups at the same time.
Output device: USB.
Equipment Application
EDS system (Bruker Quantax 400).

JEOL JEM-2100F
Field emission penetrating electron microscope (FE-TEM) model: JEOL JEM-2100F
Field emission penetrating electron microscope, which simultaneously analyzes material images, microstructures, components, bonding and other information, includes high resolution image (High Resolution Lattice Image), scanning penetrating image (STEM image) and high angle annular dark field image (HAADF), EDS component analysis and Mapping, EELS electron energy loss spectrum and Mapping, etc.
Equipment functions
The main functions include high-resolution imaging, scan penetrating imaging and high-angle annular dark field image (HAADF), EDS component analysis and Mapping, EELS electron energy loss spectrum and Mapping, and three-dimensional structure analysis.
Equipment Specifications
electron gun: ZrO/W (100), Schottky type (field emission).
accelerating voltage: max.200 KV.
magnification: 50 X～1,500,000 X.
title angle: ±25 degree.
Probe Size:
TEM Mode: 2 ~ 5 nm (diameter).
EDS Mode: 0.5 ~ 2.4 nm (diameter).
NBD Mode: 0.5 ~ 2.4 nm (diameter).
CBD Mode: 0.5 ~ 2.4 nm (diameter).
Equipment Application
The detection application includes general semiconductors, metals, ceramics, and polymer materials, and can analyze information such as images, microstructures, components, bonding, etc.
