Leave cable aside to make VR interaction more intuitive and smooth
Written by/Zhang Weijun Photography/Xu Yukai
Virtual reality (VR) is no longer a new technology thing. Cool Hand Technology uses innovative technology to lead users into a more realistic and accurate 3D gesture sensing experience.In addition to combining VR games, muscle activity signal gesture recognition somatosensory technology can benefit patients with long-distance rehabilitation and help athletes do training.
After winning the "OpenStack Application Hacking Matsu" competition championship in 2016 and the National Invention and Creation Award of the Intellectual Finance Bureau of the Ministry of Economics, the "Muscle Activity Signal Gesture Recognition Somatosensory Technology" developed by the Institute of Technology recently announced another success and was shortlisted for the 2017 Top 100 Science and Technology Research and Development Awards in the 2017 World.

The muscle activity signal gesture recognition somatosensory technology developed by the Institute of Technology has detected the user's muscle contraction signal and combined with the application of virtual reality.When players wear virtual reality helmets, as long as they identify somatosensory technology through muscle activity signal gestures, they can directly control the game with gestures. In the future, it can also be used in smart homes, such as clapping hands, and you can convert the full screen or control the briefing to play.The muscle activity signal gesture recognition technology uses unique technology and cost advantages to make VR interactive applications more popular and more intuitive.
Input devices for the new generation of human-computer interface
It is equipped with a sensing device that is like a magic bracelet, and can fire a flame gun with a fist;it can pull the trigger by raising your palm and bent your fingers, and can also control the protagonists in video games to jump, shoot and other actions, replacing the remote-controlled handle or rocker to become the input device for the new generation of human-computer interface.
Wu Jigang, the founder of Kushou Technology, a spin-off company of the Industrial Research Institute, developed the prototype of the product concept as early as six years ago during his work in the Industrial Research Institute, but was unable to determine the market potential of the technology. Until 2014, MYO, a similar concept product appeared on foreign mass fundraising platforms, and received investment from Intel and Amazon, raising 120 million US dollars in one fell swoop. Wu Jigang's long-planned muscle activity measurement technology finally had the opportunity to come to the fore.
The "Muscle Activity Signal Gesture Recognition Somatosensory Technology" developed by the Institute of Technology is not affected by human sweating, causing errors in signal identification, and is comfortable to wear and has a cost advantage.
The most successful product for gesture recognition is Microsoft's Kinect, which uses optical imaging to perform 3D gesture sensing, while the aforementioned MYO arm ring uses the principle of using electrical myoelectric signal (EMG).Unlike Kinect or MYO, Wu Jigang uses muscle activity signal (MMG) gesture recognition somatosensory technology to perform gesture recognition.When the muscle contracts, the muscle fibers rub against each other and the serous membrane will also rub against the body, and the muscle volume will change, etc. These will generate vibration signals that are lower than the sound, and then the measurement can be performed through the inertial element.
Compared with products that detect gestures through Immunoelectric signals, muscle activity signals are not affected by human sweating, resulting in errors in signal judgment. Collecting Immunoelectric signals usually requires sticking electrode patches to the surface of the body or even coating conductive glue. These hard material patches will cause discomfort in the human body.What’s important is that cool hands have more cost advantages.As for products detected by optical image, they are easily affected by light and are less suitable for outdoor use. They may cause masking when used by multiple people. If fingers are squeezed or wrapped, the optical detection product may fail, and these are not a problem for muscle activity signal measurement technology.
Exercise, rehabilitation and identity recognition can be applied
Despite having excellent competitive advantages in the market, Wu Jigang founded Coolshou Technology with the support of the Institute of Technology Research in early 2017. The product is positioned in modules and technology output, and does not involve the manufacturing of terminal products.This muscle activity signal gesture recognition somatosensory technology is selected and combined with virtual reality games as interactive control. In the future, motion analysis can be performed and applied to exercise and rehabilitation.
Taking exercise as an example, this muscle activity signal gesture recognition somatosensory technology can be combined with exercise electrocardiogram, and combined with sports accessories to measure the order and intensity of muscle contraction. It is suitable for long-distance running, bicycle and even swimming training. It can determine the athlete's gait stage and see whether the muscles are used reasonably to confirm whether the athlete is exercising correctly in an effort-saving posture.Taking long-distance running as an example, often after several hours of running, the athlete's running posture will slightly deviate or sway it is not as correct as it was at the start. At this time, the more you run, the more you run, the more you are, the more you are running, the more you are.Through this technique, the muscle activity signal can be measured for several hours, and the muscle application situation can be determined in just over ten seconds.
It can also be used in small-scale functional rehabilitation, especially at present, long-distance rehabilitation can only be transmitted back to the therapist by image. However, the human body has a compensation mechanism, which will extend alternative actions to replace the original target actions. Therefore, using image methods cannot determine whether the patient has correctly trained the right muscles.Through MMG technology, muscle activity signal data can be sent back, and more accurate judgments can be made than correct rehabilitation posture signal diagrams.
Not only that, muscle activity signal gesture recognition somatosensory technology can also be used in identity recognition.Compared with the identity recognition technology that uses mobile phones to sign in the air, MMG can not only record the location of the signature, but also add the signer's muscle activity size to identify it.Even if the signature can be imitated, the order and intensity of muscle contractions in each person can be said to be unique.
At present, this technology mainly recognizes the grab and release movements of the hand. It can match the angles of the wrist and arm. It can recognize 30 sets of movements and be used for command control. The next step will be more refined to identify the grab and release of each finger of the hand.This way, physical movements that are closer to the virtual reality can also be applied to prosthetics or robot controls.
In addition, in the next stage, Cool Hand will use MMG to further integrate the spatial positioning technology of VR manufacturers, such as HTC Vive's laser spatial positioning technology (Lighthouse) and Sony PlayStation VR's photosphere technology.When applied outdoors, it is to use inertial elements to make translational motion recognition and position determination of postures, etc., which will be the focus of next research and development.In addition, the system architecture will continue to be modified and optimized to reduce power consumption and lower cost.
In the past year, the amount of investment in virtual/amplified reality has repeatedly hit new highs, and peripheral input devices are also varied. Although Kushou Technology is not the first to enter the market, it can make VR interactive applications more popular and more intuitive and smooth with its unique technology and cost advantages.
