Xu Xiulan Academician
Academician Xu Xiulan is the chairman and CEO of Sino-US Silicon Crystal and Global Wafers. He has been deeply engaged in the fields of renewable energy and semiconductors for more than 25 years. With his abundant professional knowledge, professional M&A strategies, and clear and accurate decision-making capabilities, he has led the company's team to continuously expand the market, and his revenue and profits have been hitting peaks.At the same time, the precise global localization strategy also enables the company to minimize the impact and create a good physique with resilience when facing various unprecedented black swans.
Academician Xu obtained a master's degree in computer science from the University of Illinois in the United States. After returning to Taiwan, he invested in the electronics industry. In 1998, he was recruited to serve as an associate business manager of Sino-US Silicon Crystal and promoted to executive vice president within six years. He was then transferred to General Manager of Lingguang Technology. In 2008, he returned to Sino-US Silicon Crystal and served as general manager.In 2011, Sino-US Silicon Crystal made its semiconductor business unit independent and established Global Wafers, with Academician Xu as chairman. Over the past decade, Academician Xu has obtained 8-inch and 12-inch wafer products through several major mergers and acquisitions, such as the world's sixth largest Japanese silicon wafer factory Covalent Material Silicon (CVS), in 2012; in 2016, Danish semiconductor factory Topsil and SunEdison Semiconductor Limited (SEMI), the world's fourth largest semiconductor wafer manufacturer and supplier, obtained 12-inch epitaxial, SOI technology and production capacity.Through mergers and acquisitions, we have achieved technology or markets that we do not have, and quickly exert comprehensive results, making global wafers grow and grow, becoming the world's third largest semiconductor wafer manufacturer.
In 2020, Academician Xu officially took over the position of Chairman of Sino-US Silicon Crystal. Under his leadership, Sino-US Silicon Crystal and Global Wafers both achieved new highs in 2023. Academician Xu successfully expanded his global layout with an excellent merger and acquisition strategy. In recent years, he has turned to an organic growth strategy and expanded production globally.Through the three major strategies of local production, diversified procurement, and automated production, it has achieved risk diversification and strengthened the resilience of the supply chain. As an outstanding female leader, Academician Xu also emphasizes the care of female employees.In 2021, the Utsunomiya Factory (MJL) of Global Wafer Japan was awarded the highest-level female active promotion owner "Eruboshi" and other certification, which affirms the Utsunomiya Factory's contribution to improving women's careers.
