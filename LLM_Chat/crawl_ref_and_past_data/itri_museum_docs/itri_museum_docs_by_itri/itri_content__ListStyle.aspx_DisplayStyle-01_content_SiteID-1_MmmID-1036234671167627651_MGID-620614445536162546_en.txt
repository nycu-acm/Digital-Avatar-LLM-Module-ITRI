Academician Chen Xianzhang
Mr. Chen Xianzhang is currently the vice president of the General Management Office of Changchun Relations Enterprises. Since 1979, he has been the general manager of Dalian Chemical. During this period, he has successfully developed many advantageous process technologies. Many products have jumped to the world's leading position: such as vinyl acetate catalyst improvement, EVA latex and VAE Powder, acrylic alcohol and 1,4-butanediol and PTMEG process efficiency improvement, etc., and accumulated as many as 79 patents. The company's turnover has also increased from NT$2.7 billion in 1994 to NT$55 billion in 2013, a growth of nearly 20 times. It has made outstanding contributions to the national economic development and has also promoted Changchun Group to become a world-class supplier of downstream products such as PBT, PVA, PVB, EVOH and epoxy resin. Vice President Chen has been appointed as a concurrent professor at Taipei Medical University and served as a member of the oral examination of the master's degree at Taiwan University for 16 years.He has made a lot of contributions to the field of industry, official and academic studies, and is truly a giant in the chemical industry in Taiwan.
