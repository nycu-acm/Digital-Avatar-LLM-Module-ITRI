The Ministry of Economic Affairs has achieved great achievements in winning glory for the country. The Institute of Technology won the top 100 innovation institutions award for its patent influence and uniqueness seven times

Date: 2023/02/17
Under the leadership of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research has won the "Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award" for the seventh time for the seventh time, and has been awarded the most award-winning R&D institutions in the Asia-Pacific region, and has also ranked first among Taiwanese institutions.
Clarivate released the 2023 "Top 100 Global Innovation Institutions" report today.Under the leadership of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the patent energy of the Institute of Industrial Research has performed outstandingly in five major indicators, including technological uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity. It has won the seventh award for six consecutive years, and has continued to win the most awards in the Asia-Pacific region, and has also ranked first among Taiwanese institutions.It is also in line with the internationally renowned company ASML, the R&D organization France's Alternative Energy and Atomic Energy Commission (CEA) and the French National Center for Scientific Research (CNRS), which shows that the Ministry of Economic Affairs actively uses scientific and technological projects to encourage legal research institutions to invest in cross-domain forward-looking technology research and development, continuously optimize the global intellectual and financial layout, and enhance Taiwan's international competitiveness with outstanding results.
The evolution of science and technology and mastering the core technologies of the next generation are the decisive key to industrial competition. The Ministry of Economic Affairs grasps the latest global trends, uses science and technology project plans to promote my country's scientific research and innovation, integrates the cross-field energy of legal person R&D institutions, lays out prescient technologies for industrial transformation and upgrading, implements all-round intelligent power operation strategies, and deepens Taiwan's industrial intelligent power strength with the "focus on quality but not quantity" thinking.In 2022, the legal person science and technology patents were certified 1,035, technology transfers 1,146, and accumulated valid patents of 21,532. Taking the Institute of Technology as an example, about 85% of the patents are R&D results supported by the Ministry of Economic Science and Technology Project. Among the top 100 patent enterprises and institutions in my country, the number of patent inventions of the Institute of Technology continues to rank first among R&D institutions, showing the important achievements of the Ministry of Economic Science and Technology to promote the creation of intellectual wealth value by legal person research and development institutions.
Clarivate Security Ball’s top 100 innovative institutions report long-term evaluation of institutions’ innovation energy through patent data analysis, commending top institutions’ persistence in innovation and their excellent creativity.Gordon Samson, head of Clarivate Products, said that this year, only three R&D units in the world were selected on the list of top 100 innovation institutions. Taiwan Institute of Technology not only ranked among them, but also ranked seventh and was selected for six consecutive years.The Institute of Industrial Research has always maintained high-quality innovation, and has strengthened its global layout and performed well in recent years. We hope that the Institute of Industrial Research will continue to drive the development of the industry and economy with innovative concepts and solutions.
Liu Wenxiong, director of the Institute of Industrial Research, said that winning the Top 100 Innovation Institution Award for the World for the seventh time in a row, not only demonstrates the uniqueness and influence of the Institute of Industrial Research, but also shows that the overall direction and strategy of intelligent power management have international competitive advantages.Scientific and technological research and development and industrial innovation are important cornerstones for promoting economic growth. The Institute of Technology continues to deepen its layout with two major aspects: "blue ocean strategy and capital market linking the technology market".In the Blue Ocean Strategy section, the Industrial Research Institute uses three levels of existing technological breakthroughs, redefining industrial pain points, and finding new business opportunities to create a market to assist the industry in building value innovation business models.At the same time, the Institute of Technology actively links the capital and technology markets, connects the credit guarantee fund for small and medium-sized enterprises and 26 important financial banks in Taiwan, and creates an intangible asset financing service mechanism. It has assisted 25 new and small and medium-sized enterprises to accumulate more than NT$300 million in funding through patent and technology added financing, promoting the accelerated growth of small and medium-sized enterprises and new enterprises, becoming the key driving force for Taiwan's industrial transformation and upgrading.
Liu Wenxiong further pointed out that with the support of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, joined hands with the IP Bank, a joint group of industry, academia and research in Taiwan, has collected thousands of high-quality patents for global popular technologies from top domestic enterprises and legal entities, and promoted patent pools in the fields of network communication, chip semiconductor design and manufacturing, computer systems, net zero emissions, multimedia audio/video processing and comprehensive applications, building a solid shield for the industry to enter the international market.
The Institute of Technology promotes the "2030 Technology Strategy and Blueprint", deploys scientific and technological research and development strategies ahead of schedule, and introduces the technical life cycle (Technical Cycle Time; TCT), strengthens the scope of patent examination, formulates cross-domain intellectual finance strategies with full life cycle management, and combines rolling adjustments of technological development and market changes, implements intelligent power management results and tracking, and truly layouts in key areas to assist Taiwan's industry in moving towards a new blue ocean market.
The Institute of Technology won the "Top 100 Global Innovation Institution Award" for seven times, indicating that the Institute of Technology's patents performed well in five major selection criteria: technical uniqueness, influence, globalization, successful footprint and quantity.
