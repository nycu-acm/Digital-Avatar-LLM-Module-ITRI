Energy-saving home appliances must not be missed!Innovative network smart audit system of the Institute of Technology and Research wins the International Energy Award

Date: 2021/11/08
The Global Energy Awards hosted by the Austrian Energy Global Foundation, the Institute of Technology won the Taiwan Champion, and was recently awarded by representatives of the Austrian Business Office.From the left picture, Peng Shumei, a member of the Energy Bureau, Hermann Ortner, representative of the Austrian Business Office, Wang Renqian, director of the Green Energy and Environment Research Institute of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, and Luo Xinheng, business director of the Green Energy and Environment Research Institute of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute.
Taiwan’s green energy innovation and integrated management technology have been recognized internationally again! The Energy Globe Award, the world's largest and known as the Oscars in the environmental protection industry, recently announced the list of winners of the 2021 National Energy Globe Award.With the support of the Energy Bureau of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Institute of Technology won the favor of the jury with its "Internet Smart Inspection System" and won the Taiwan championship of the Global Energy National Award.This time, the Industrial Research Institute won the award as the four best solutions in the world in the "Air" category of the Global Energy Award in 2020, showing that the Industrial Research Institute has continuously launched innovative energy in the field of energy management, and has leaped onto the international stage and become the light of Taiwan.
Wang Renqian, director of the Green Energy and Environment Research Institute of the Institute of Industrial Research, said that when many consumers purchase home appliances, in order to save electricity bills, most of them will give priority to purchasing energy-saving marks or energy-efficiency products. However, with the change in consumption patterns and the booming development of e-commerce shopping in the post-epidemic era, it is even more difficult for the government to inspect energy-saving home appliances.The "Internet Smart Audit System" innovatively developed by the Institute of Technology integrates the Internet crawl information collection technology and AI artificial intelligence comparison, which can check whether the energy efficiency marking information is correct for home appliances sold online, and provide consumers with more guarantees when choosing energy-saving home appliances in online stores.
Wang Renqian further pointed out that this network smart audit system has three innovative features, including: 1. Accurate auditing and identification: The network smart audit system first selects the address of the e-commerce website target, which can automatically capture and classify product information content in large quantities, and uses AI artificial intelligence to identify and compare the government energy-saving marks and energy efficiency grading database to find false label information; 2. Massive information comparison: Compared with traditional manual comparison at most 40,000 data per year, this system can inspect and verify the graphic content of more than 1.21 million home appliance-related product web pages every year, and the error rate is greatly reduced; 3. Innovative energy-saving benefits: demonstrating the implementation and integrity of the government's promotion of energy-saving equipment, while ensuring the correctness of consumers' choice of energy-saving equipment is an indispensable innovative integrated management technology, and it has also successfully attracted international teams to actively inquire.
The Energy Globe Award was established in 1999 and held its first in Austria in 2000. It was then hosted by the Austrian Energy Global Foundation every year. Its partners include the United Nations Industrial Development Organization (UNIDO), the Global Chamber Platform, etc. In recent years, more than 180 countries have participated in the competition every year, with a total of more than 25,000 projects. It is the largest environmental award in the world and can be called the Oscar of the environmental protection industry.This award encourages countries to propose innovative projects to solve environmental and energy problems, and also calls on more companies to invest in green commodity research and development. This year, the shortlisted include the electric thermal energy storage (ETES) system for renewable energy from Siemens Gamesa Renewable Energy, Germany, and the seedling packaging bags that can naturally decompose in soil by the Austrian non-profit organization Naku.
With the support of the Energy Bureau of the Ministry of Economics, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has developed the "Internet Smart Inspection System" and won the Taiwan Champion of the Global Energy National Award, and the innovative energy of energy management has jumped onto the international stage.
