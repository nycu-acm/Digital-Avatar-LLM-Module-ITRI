Industrial Research Institute promotes the application of electric vehicle and shows green transportation results with the industry

Date: 2024/11/29
The Institute of Technology and Research and Development worked with the industry to hold a demonstration and promotion of electric vehicle demonstration results, hoping to accelerate the introduction and popularization of electric vehicles in the logistics industry by establishing a benchmark demonstration.Lin Anhong, deputy general manager of Green Energy, Zhang Nianci, head of the Mechanical Research Institute of Technology, Zhang Conghai, general manager of Hsinchu Logistics Group, Liu Kunyi, associate director of China Automobile, Rao Daren, senior technical expert of the Mechanical Research Institute of Technology, Jian Renji Tong Jianqiang, deputy general manager of Xiangyi Freight, Zheng Yanglong, assistant manager of Kerry Darong, Hu Mingchang, deputy general manager of Weigang Technology, Jiang Changxian, and Su Weibin, assistant manager of Guorui Automobile.
As the world moves towards net zero emissions, electric vehicle traffic has become a key solution for low-carbon transportation.With the support of the Industrial Development Agency of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, the Industrial Research Institute joined hands with many industrial partners on November 29, including China Automobile, Weigang Technology, Hsinchu Logistics, Kerry Darong Logistics, Xiangyi Freight, Guorui Automobile, and Qierxing Green Energy, demonstrating the effectiveness of diverse field applications including 3.5-ton electric small trucks, electric locomotives and charging stations.The Institute of Technology emphasized that it hopes to accelerate the introduction and popularization of electric vehicles in the logistics industry by establishing a benchmark demonstration, and assist Taiwan in achieving the goal of net zero emissions and green transportation in 2050.
Jian Renji, Industrial Development Agency of the Ministry of Economic Affairs, said that with the increase in the demand for supply chain carbon reduction in the international market, electric vehicle is not only a solution to the low-carbon transportation, but also a key link in realizing smart logistics.The UNDP has long promoted various innovative and intelligent low-carbon transportation solutions. Currently, the electrified vehicle solutions such as electric locomotives, passenger cars, and buses have been launched in China. In the field of electric commercial vehicles, large-scale mass production in international areas is still not popular.The Ministry of Economic Development and Development Agency provided subsidies for demonstration operation of electric commercial vehicles in 112, supporting on-site testing of vehicle manufacturing and logistics operators to verify the quality and safety of electric commercial vehicles.In the future, UNDP and Development will continue to work closely with the industry to accelerate the digital transformation of the logistics industry and work together to create a cleaner and more sustainable transportation environment.
Rao Daren, a senior technical expert at the Institute of Mechanical and Mechanical Systems of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, said that the Institute of Industrial Research Institute assisted the Development of the DRC to promote the demonstration operation of electric vehicle traffic, and achieved remarkable results, including participating in the preliminary technology development of electric trucks of China Automobile and Guorui Automobile, and transforming the technology into "electric vehicle chassis system design and development" and "power system integration technology" to China Automobile and "130kW integrated power motor and drive controller technology" to Shilin Motor, and assisted Guorui Automobile in establishing the integration of electric vehicle systems and strengthening the design foundation of domestic electric vehicles.Looking ahead, the Industrial Research Institute will continue to assist the industry in developing 5-ton electric trucks and lightweight cold chain electric vehicles, strengthen the load-load, endurance and cold chain application capabilities, and continue to lead Taiwan's logistics industry to move towards low-carbonization and intelligence.
During the exhibition, China Automobile showed its 3.5-ton domestic electric small truck, showing its excellent carrying efficiency. It is expected to cooperate with Hsinchu Logistics next year to invest 30 electric car traffic, which will be used for B2B and B2C logistics transportation, and replace traditional oil trucks year by year.Weigang Technology cooperates with Kerry Darong Logistics and Xiangyi Freight respectively to invest in Guorui Automobile's 3.5-ton electric small truck and Weisuden electric tricycle. It also combines its own low-carbon logistics platform to calculate the carbon emissions of logistics and optimize the distribution paths of AI to effectively manage logistics vehicles, further reduce vehicle carbon emissions, and strive to establish a low-carbon logistics fleet and promote the "last mile" green transportation service.
The Institute of Technology continues to focus on new market values ​​and plans the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" as the research and development direction. In the application field of "smart life", it will develop key components and vehicle systems of smart electric vehicles, and combine the cross-field R&D advantages such as machinery and electronics to assist in the development of my country's electric vehicle logistics vehicles, and jointly move towards the future of a green economy and a sustainable environment.
