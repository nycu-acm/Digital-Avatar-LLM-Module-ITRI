Technology introduction
Blockchain health record authorization management system.
Blockchain PHR (Personal Health Record) gateway connection technology can shorten the time for people to get health examination reports after health examinations, and can provide the insurance industry, medical industry, and health promotion industry to authorize and exchange cross-industry health examination data, and assist in quick claims settlement and insurance actuariality.The industrial problems that can be solved include: taking spillover insurance products as an example, health promotion services involve multiple parties to handle the confidentiality of the insured households.Each insured household's insurance period and authorization object are different, and the authorization complexity is high.
