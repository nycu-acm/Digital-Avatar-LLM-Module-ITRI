Technology introduction
AI digital energy-saving solution.
In view of the process characteristics of high-energy-consuming industries, we will quickly develop digital carbon reduction technology solutions that do not require high costs to update equipment.
Optimize energy saving for continuous processes (such as petrochemicals, glass, etc.), and produce products of the same quality with lower energy consumption.
Optimize energy saving for the production schedule of single-station/full-production lines (such as steel, metal heat treatment, etc.).
Smart scheduling (APS) value-added enterprise resource management system (ERP) software, strengthen carbon information management, and improve the industry net zero ecosystem.
Application scope
Parameter optimization application of chemical, glass and other industries in continuous process.
Scheduling application of single-station/full-production line for metal thermal process.
Smart scheduling value-added enterprise resource management system software.
Carbon reduction benefits
Process parameters optimization and energy saving
Introduce world-class petrochemical plants to break through the bottleneck of the reaction station yield, increase the production yield by 2.5% on average, which is equivalent to saving 3.01% raw material and energy consumption, and also saves steam consumption by 3% at Santa Purification Station, reducing carbon by about 6,500 tons/year (energy consumption + raw materials)
Diffusion and application to the glass industry can improve quality by 3%, reduce energy consumption by 2%, reduce carbon emissions by about 1,500 tons/year (direct carbon emissions) compared with before using the system (manual parameter adjustment).
Production scheduling optimization and energy saving
Introduced into large steel factories, reduce energy consumption (natural gas) at heating stations by 8.7%, reduce the standby idle power of forging machines by 5.97%, and reduce the carbon emissions of about 4,000 tons per year (accounting for 4.07% of the overall direct carbon emissions)
Diffusion is applied to the heat treatment industry, improving the problem of excessive frequency of cooling and wire replacement, reducing power consumption by 5.05%, and evaluating a single manufacturer to reduce indirect carbon emissions by about 500 metric tons per year
