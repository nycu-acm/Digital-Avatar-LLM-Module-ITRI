Technology introduction
Soft tubular medical surgical robot system.
The "Soft Tubular Medical Surgical Robot System" developed by the Institute of Technology has established a 3D bronchial model using preoperative CT, combining endoscopic navigation, tactile feedback and omnidirectional manipulation to improve the safety and efficiency of bronchoscopy.Equipped with an extremely fine bronchoscope (outer diameter 2.9mm), it can reach the end, expand the sampling range and shorten the process time.The soft tubular medical surgical robot system has passed many tests and international certifications and won the 2024 Top 100 Global Science and Technology Research and Development Awards, promoting the localization of technology and strengthening international competitiveness.
Features and innovation
Technical Features
Minimized endoscopic system to observe thinner lung tube diameters
Multi-angle hose robot, flexible operation without dead angles
Technical Advantages
Preoperative 3D imaging modeling is conducive to doctors' preoperative simulation
Intraoperative image navigation to accurately reach the lesion location
Intraoperative omnidirectional control system
Benefits and Applications
Solving the current clinical use difficulties of bronchoscopy, including operating bottlenecks, easy to get lost, and time-consuming and labor-intensive searching for lesions.In addition, this technology helps doctors to accurately, quickly and reduce the operation time of radiation environment, quickly and accurately achieve lesions, and improve the success rate of surgery.
