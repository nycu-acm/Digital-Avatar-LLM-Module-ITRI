Academician Zhang Youde
Academician Zhang Youde is the chairman of Yian Health Medical Co., Ltd. and has served as partner of Vertical Group, a large-scale American medical equipment venture capital, founder and CEO of many international medical materials companies such as Integrated Vascular System, Ensure Medical, etc. He leads and participates in the authorization or acquisition of many successful advanced medical materials companies in the American medical materials venture capital industry, and has also invested in the global research and development of key medical materials, intravascular ultrasonic and groin hemostasis devices for cardiac catheter stent surgery.He is known as the "godfather of medical materials".
Chairman Zhang has all-round industrialization experience in the development of advanced medical materials. At the invitation of Shengde Group, he returned to Taiwan in 2012 to cooperate with Shengde Pharmaceutical Factory and Shangzhi Biotechnology Venture Capital, a subsidiary of Yongfeng Yu Group, to establish Yian Biotechnology.It is the first innovative medical material research and development company in China, focusing on micro-invasive high-end medical materials, in order to give birth to Taiwan’s innovative medical materials industry and keep pace with the international medical materials industry development trend.
Under the helm of Chairman Zhang, Yian Health Medical focuses on R&D and manufacturing, and bears fruit in just 5 years of its establishment.Two laparoscopic surgical products and one orthopedic equipment have obtained the marketing authorization of the US Food and Drug Administration (FDA). In 2018, it successfully authorized Terumo, the world's top five cardiovascular medical materials manufacturer, with its own developed products, setting a precedent for domestic innovative medical materials authorization international manufacturers.
Chairman Zhang has been paying more attention to the development of biotechnology and medical materials in Taiwan for a long time. He serves as an advisor to the Institute of Biotechnology and Medical Materials Research Institute and a member of the forward-looking guidance meeting, as well as a member of the Biotechnology Industry Strategy Advisory Committee of the Executive Yuan (BTC).He has repeatedly made suggestions on government investment policies, biomedical park planning, industrial development strategies and talent cultivation strategies. He and former head of the National Science and Technology Association in San Francisco, Yang Qihang, jointly gave birth to the "Taiwan-Stanver Medical Equipment Product Design Talent Training Program" (STB Program). To date, more than 10 new startups have been established, and have made outstanding contributions to promoting the improvement of Taiwan's biomedical industry and talent cultivation.
