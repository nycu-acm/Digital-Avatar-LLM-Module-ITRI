About the Institute of Industry
Talent is an important capital of the country and the driving force of corporate competitiveness. The Industrial College of the Institute of Technology focuses on accelerating the quality and quantity of talents needed for training knowledge economy, and deepens its efforts in the two major areas of talent policy research and talent development and cultivation.The School of Industry has a solid professional team to assist the government in formulating national talent development strategies and establishing a complete talent development system.At the same time, we will connect talent research, develop key talent cultivation plans for the industry, and nurture cross-field talents of enterprises.
In recent years, scientific and technological development has driven rapid changes in the industrial environment.In order to assist my country's industrial transformation and upgrading, the Institute of Industry will organize talent training at accelerating the development of emerging science and technology and industrial industries, and cultivate my country's industrial talents' strength. The main tasks include: 1. Connecting global industrial development trends, introducing international advanced courses, training seed teachers and talents needed in the industry; and 2. Assisting the government in developing and promoting industrial talent policies and becoming a talent development strategy partner for industrial upgrading.
Under the above tasks, the Institute of Industry refers to the benchmark cases and learning models for new types of talent cultivation at home and abroad, and integrates the advantages and experience of the Institute of Technology and Research to cultivate cross-field innovative talents.On the one hand, based on the functional needs of industrial talents, in line with the development of global industrial trends and innovative learning models, and based on the deep R&D energy accumulated by the Institute of Technology over the past 40 years, it gathers various resources such as the R&D results of the Institute of Technology, field experts, advanced teaching equipment and environment, and develops a diversified learning design foundation and handles various scientific and technological professional courses to establish core or accelerate the diffusion of seed teachers for industrial upgrading and enterprise transformation.On the other hand, the Institute of Industry continues to study the talent development strategies of advanced countries, not only assisting the government in formulating talent policies and training plans for emerging industries, but also helping to promote my country's functional benchmarks, talent certification and development mechanisms, hoping to establish a solid foundation and advantages for Taiwan's talent competitiveness.
For many years, the Institute of Industry has provided integrated value-added corporate talent services in line with the purpose of serving customers, so as to assist customers in responding to environmental changes and market competition. In addition to playing the strategic partner of technical units within the Institute of Industry and Research, it also actively plays the role of consulting and assisting the talent topics of government think tanks and enterprises in the industry, and continues to work towards creating innovative talents for the science and technology industry, rich human resources and thus enhancing industrial competitiveness.
