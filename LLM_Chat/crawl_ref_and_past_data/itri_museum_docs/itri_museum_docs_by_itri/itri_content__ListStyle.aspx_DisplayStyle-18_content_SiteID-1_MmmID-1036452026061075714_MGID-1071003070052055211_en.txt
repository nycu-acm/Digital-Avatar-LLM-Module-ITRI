Research Institute of Technology Links Resources Light up South Taiwan
Written by Tu Xinyi
The Liujia Campus of the South Branch of the Institute of Technology Research and Research will become a demonstration community for smart life and ecological circulation.
As the world economy is in fluctuation, the Institute of Technology has gone south many years ago to work together with the local industry, pioneering innovative technology, providing various services such as industrial transformation and upgrading, and injecting the technological energy of the South Taiwan industry to leaps up.
The southern industry is diverse in style, from energy, IC, medical equipment, shipbuilding, metal, automobile and locomotive components, textiles, plastics, to agriculture, fishery and animal husbandry, it includes everything. However, each industry has its own needs for industrial transformation and technological upgrading. How to meet each industry has become the biggest challenge of the Institute of Technology Research.
In order to provide strong support for the southern industry, the Institute of Technology has successively established four major campuses in the south, including the Lokka Campus, the Shalun Campus, the Nanchuang Campus (i.e. the South Taiwan Innovation Park of the Ministry of Economics) and the Kaohsiung Service Zone (i.e. the South Taiwan Industrial Cross-Domain Innovation Center), and have stayed in these four campuses for a long time. With the needs of the transformation and upgrading of the local industry in South Taiwan as the focus goal, it provides immediate and effective on-site services.
Four major courtyard areas link resources to form the South Taiwan Science and Technology Network
Zhuang Bainian, deputy executive of the South Branch of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute, led the team to promote the development of the southern region with action plans such as focusing on goals, combining links, local connections, and long-term in-depth action plans.
Zhuang Bainian, deputy executive of the South Branch of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research, said that in order to give full play to the local advantages of each campus, different campuses have different technical R&D teams, which are linked to each other to form a tightly intertwined South Taiwan industrial service network.Among them, the Liujia Campus has teams such as laser and 3D printing technology, green energy energy creation and energy storage technology, plant medicine source and healthy seedling technology, and provide options for creating new value in South Taiwan's industry and agriculture.
The Green Energy Technology Team of Shalun Campus shoulders the important responsibility of building Taiwan’s green silicon valley. They promote the demonstration field of R&D of South Taiwan’s green energy technology, focusing on the research and development, testing, verification and industrialization of green energy technologies such as energy creation, energy conservation, energy storage and system integration, in order to complete the last mile of the development of the South Taiwan’s green energy industry chain.
The Nanchuang Campus technical team located in the South Taiwan Innovation Park of the Ministry of Economic Affairs focuses on the research and development and industrialization of smart sensing system technology, assisting the South Taiwan factory in the establishment of IoT and smart manufacturing communication networks, and assisting the South Taiwan industrial entrepreneurship and development.The Kaohsiung Service Zone mainly focuses on transformation and upgrading talent training, promoting circular economy, patent (IP) legal counseling and agricultural and fishery value regeneration.
The four southern campuses of the Institute of Technology Research and Research have nearly 700 professional engineers. They use their mature engineering technology and management experience to form a network to become a science and technology network that defends the competitiveness of South Taiwan’s industrial industry.
Link to southern industry and official schools to show results and plan the future
"The Institute of Technology Research and Research is focusing on the industrial needs of South Taiwan, linking the energy of industry, and assisting the transformation and upgrading of South Taiwan's industries and moving towards a new blue ocean market. In order to implement the above core strategies, the technical teams of the southern campuses of the Institute of Technology will implement the core strategies with action plans such as focusing on goals, linkage, local connections, and long-term in-depth." Zhuang Bainian clearly analyzed the southern development strategy of the Institute of Technology.
The southern campuses of the Institute of Technology Research and Research have guided the South Taiwan industry with the above core strategies and action plans, and have shown many guidance performances. For example, first, the Institute of Technology Research and Research and Research and Research and Research and Research have cooperated with the Southern Science Park Administration to specially link 18 manufacturers to establish a 3D printing industry cluster to promote the emerging industry supply chain of 3D printing medical equipment, and jointly enter the international market.
Second, in order to assist southern small and medium-sized enterprises in transformation and upgrading, the Institute of Technology and Research actively cooperates with southern colleges and universities to establish a small and medium-sized enterprise service team to provide the upgrading services of small and medium-sized enterprises in South Taiwan through academic and research cooperation. The southern universities currently affiliated with the Institute of Technology include Kunshan University of Science and Technology, Jianan Pharmacology University, Nantai University of Science and Technology, Nanrong University of Science and Technology, Gaoyuan University of Science and Technology, etc., providing solutions urgently needed by southern small and medium-sized enterprises from green energy, finance, business management, marketing, pollution prevention and control.
Third, the Institute of Technology and Chenggong University signed a memorandum of cooperation on academic and research in July 2019, and conducted a preliminary study on cell therapy and other issues.In the future, it will also conduct cooperative research on artificial intelligence AI or sports technology, which will be effectively implemented in the industry and society in South Taiwan.The Institute of Technology Research and Research is also planning to carry out relevant academic and research cooperation with Zhongzheng University, Sun Yat-sen University and Kaohsiung University of Science and Technology in the fields of smart agriculture, smart long-term care, smart manufacturing, marine engineering, etc.
Fourth, the South Branch of the Industrial Research Institute plans to propose the vision of transforming into a smart city and a happy society to the southern county and municipal governments with three aspects: smart life, healthy and happy life and sustainable environment. It has received positive responses from the Tainan Municipal Government;the Industrial Research Institute will continue to cooperate with the southern county and municipal governments, southern industries, schools and related legal persons to strive for relevant central policies and budget support, and complete the vision of developing the southern county and municipal governments into a smart city and a happy society year by year.
In order to plan the future development of the southern campus, the South Branch of the Industrial Research Institute takes the Liujia Campus as the first goal and plans to build the Liujia Campus in the future to become a smart life and ecological circulation demonstration community. The vision of this community will include smart vegetable planting greenhouses, rainwater recycling systems, biorefining factories, solar self-powered power generation systems, unmanned self-driving cars, smart community safety image monitoring systems, home health care smart management systems, 24-hour unmanned convenience stores, 5G smart life demonstration and application, baseball sports technology experimental field demonstration and other forward-looking technical elements, such as smart vegetable planting elements, which can be reflected here in the future.Entering the Liujia Campus is like entering the new world of the future!
