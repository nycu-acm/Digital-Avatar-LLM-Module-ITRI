Technology introduction
Full digital magnetic levitation bearing control.
The fully digital magnetic levitation bearing controller developed by the Institute of Technology and Research, locks the application of high-speed magnetic levitation motor products such as centrifugal refrigerant compressors, blowers, vacuum molecular pumps, liquid pumps, turbo expanders, flywheel energy storage, etc., and establishes a patent layout, design, manufacturing and application chain of the overall technology.
Features and innovation
Hardware technology includes magnetolev bearing driving unit, magnetolev bearing control unit, sensor unit, and software technology includes sensor correction, system identification, magnetolev control, signal processing and human-machine monitoring, etc., so that the magnetolev performance of high-speed magnetolev motors complies with the international standards and specifications for vibration trajectory and sensitivity of ISO-14839-2,-3.
Application and benefits
This technology won the 5th National Industrial Innovation Award of the Ministry of Economics, the Ruineng Award of the Ministry of Economics, the Outstanding Research Award of the Institute of Industrial Research and the Industrial Research Institute's Industrialization Contribution Award.The Institute of Technology actively links domestic machinery, electricity and magnetism, and lays out various high-speed magneto-levitation motor applications, creates high-efficiency oil-free products, and enhances international competitiveness.
