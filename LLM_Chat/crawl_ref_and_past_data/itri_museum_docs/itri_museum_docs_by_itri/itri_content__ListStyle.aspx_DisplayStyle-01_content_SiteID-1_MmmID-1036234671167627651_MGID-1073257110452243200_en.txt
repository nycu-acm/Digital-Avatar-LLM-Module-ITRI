Li Yuande Academician
Academician Li Yuande is a world-renowned cardiac medicine expert, specializing in cardiovascular diseases and cardiac emergencies. He is currently an honorary professor at the School of Medicine of National Taiwan University, chairman of the Medical Education Foundation of the Consortium, and founder of the Medical Clinic. He was the director of the National Taiwan University Hospital and was awarded the honor of Academician of the American College of Cardiology and the European Society of Cardiology.Professor Li was the first to introduce coronary balloon dilation surgery in 1983, and was a pioneer in various interventional cardiac catheter treatments in Taiwan.
Professor Li graduated from the Department of Medicine of National Taiwan University and later received a Ph.D.in Medicine from Tokyo Medical University, Japan. With his persistence in professionalism, he actively participated in the treatment of coronary heart disease and valve heart disease. He first introduced ultrasonic cardiography and exercise electrocardiogram, and trained hundreds of medical talents to teach the backward to establish correct medical values ​​with a "patient-centered" attitude. Professor Li made many achievements in clinical research, with more than 400 original papers.During his tenure as the president of NTU Hospital, he led NTU Hospital to be the first in the country in the hospital evaluation in 2000 and 2004. The hospital and Professor Li have also won the National Quality Awards.
In 2003, during the Severe Acute Respiratory Syndrome (SARS) crisis, Professor Li was then the director of the National Taiwan University Hospital. He led the National Taiwan University Hospital to first launch the fight against evil and fight the epidemic, decisively closed the emergency room to prevent the epidemic from expanding, and established the basic code of care for SARS patients in NTU. It became an important key to preventing the first wave of SARS epidemic in China. It was awarded the "Anti-Serious" medical team awarded by the Executive Yuan.His experience in successfully leading the SARS medical team has set a good example for our country in responding to the new crown pneumonia epidemic.
In order to promote the development of Taiwan's biomedical industry, Professor Li proposed to the Executive Yuan in early 2002 to establish a national "Hsinchu Biomedical Park" development plan, that is, to plan the construction of the Zhubei Biomedical Park with the Institute of Technology to create a good environment for "biotechnology" and "medical industry" to grasp the world's pulse and national competitiveness. Professor Li actively promoted the rural medical care and spared no effort.During his tenure as the president of National Taiwan University Hospital, he promoted the transformation of the National Taiwan University medical system to support the establishment of the Department of Hospitals. He accepted the establishment of the Mansion Campus of the National Taiwan Army, took over the establishment of the National Taiwan University Hospital, and established the National Taiwan University Hospital Yunlin Branch, and merged the establishment of the Taipei Nursing College Hospital Beihu Branch. In 2019, he won the "Special Award for the Preservation Medical Dedication Award", which has contributed a lot to the popularization of medical services in Taiwan.
