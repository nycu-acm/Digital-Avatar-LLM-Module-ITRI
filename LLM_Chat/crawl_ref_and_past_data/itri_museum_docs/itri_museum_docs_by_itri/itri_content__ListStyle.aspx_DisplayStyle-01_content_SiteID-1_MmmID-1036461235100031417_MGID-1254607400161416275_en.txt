Quantum Computer Laboratory.
Quantum Computer System Laboratory can provide testing services, technology transfer, and assist in industrial planning and construction of professional cryogenic measurement laboratories. In addition, this laboratory can also cooperate to develop innovative products for verification.
Laboratory construction:
(1) The wafer circuit probe measurement system for 4K low-temperature environment and the closed-loop low-temperature environment microwave controller for 4K circuit module measurement.It can provide product characteristics verification of low-temperature electronic circuits.
(2) Quantum signal generation and measurement equipment, this system includes software that can write signals and hardware that generates various writing and analyzing various signals to form a complete system.Including control signal generation and analysis of superconducting quantum applications below 7GHz.
