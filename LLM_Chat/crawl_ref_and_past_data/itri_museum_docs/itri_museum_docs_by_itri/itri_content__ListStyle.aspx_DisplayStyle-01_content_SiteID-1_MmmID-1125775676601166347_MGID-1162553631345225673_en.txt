Clarification Note
The Institute of Technology uses technology transfer and talent diffusion to inject fresh water into the industry, provides flexible cross-domain careers, and strengthens the competitiveness of talent acquisition

Date: 2022/05/04
According to the latest survey data released by 104 Human Resources Bank in February, benefiting from the chip shortage, the average monthly work in the semiconductor industry reached 34,000 in the fourth quarter of last year, and the talent gap hit a seven-year high.Faced with the desire for talent in the industry, the Industrial Research Institute actively invested in forward-looking and innovative research and development in response to the needs of the industry through the diffusion of talents.
The Institute of Technology said that as an international R&D legal person with the establishment purpose of promoting Taiwan's scientific and technological innovation, in the past 50 years, the Institute of Technology has been spreading to the industry with talents and technology, which not only promotes the vigorous development of Taiwan's semiconductor industry, but also injects domestic industrial water resources; allowing Taiwanese manufacturers to continue to maintain their competitive advantages in the international market.Taking the cooperation between the Industrial Research Institute and Delta Research Institute as an example, the Industrial Research Institute will cooperate comprehensively with technology plus talents through technology transfer and talent diffusion.This is a corner of the fact that the Institute of Technology Research and Technology is carrying out many cooperation plans with Taiwan's industry, education and research in addition to new innovations. We actively drive the development of technological industrialization from multiple directions.
The Institute of Technology Research and Research is an industry partner. It not only creates many forward-looking and critical technologies for Taiwan, but also transfers into the industry through normal talent flow. At present, more than 20,000 people have become CEOs or senior management talents, and has also guided new ventures and cultivated more than 330. It is a treasure house for the breeding of industrial talents.Faced with the strong pull of industrial talents, the Institute of Technology hopes to strive for a more flexible talent reward mechanism, strengthen enterprise-oriented changing salary, such as performance bonuses, so as to facilitate more flexible use of management, increase the retention of outstanding talents and the competitiveness of external talents.
Talent cultivation lies in continuous innovation, cultivating an international outlook and taking into account the balance of work and life. Faced with the post-epidemic era, long-distance work has become one of the options recognized by enterprises. The Institute of Industrial Research and Development actively establishes a friendly working environment and a flexible long-distance working mechanism, including long-distance hybrid and flexible working, setting up satellite offices across Taiwan, attracting talents from all over Taiwan and even global talents to join the Institute of Industrial Research and Development, and achieving a more flexible and broader career for talents.Not only epidemic prevention, but also work efficiency and life, and achieving a win-win situation. At the same time, the Institute of Technology has also designed a plan that is better than the law stipulated by the law based on the needs of employees at all stages of their careers; set up baby care centers, kindergartens and attached parent-teacher classes to assist busy employees in taking care of their children; combined with a health center and consulting room, as well as a warm-hearted dedicated line for employees to take care of their physical, mental and spiritual health;in addition, employee restaurants with health concepts and the creation of a multi-functional sports and fitness venue "Yuhuo Hall" are friendly measures to take care of employees.We hope to meet the career needs of all generations with an innovative and flexible working environment.
