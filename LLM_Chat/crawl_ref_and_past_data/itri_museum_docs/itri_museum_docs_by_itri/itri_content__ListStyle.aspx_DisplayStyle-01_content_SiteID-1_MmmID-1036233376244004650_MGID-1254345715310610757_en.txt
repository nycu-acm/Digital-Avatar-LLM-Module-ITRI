Technology introduction
One-stop management platform for net retail office buildings
Provides a standardized process for building net zero improvement PDCA, integrates our key technologies, from "energy identification", "energy conservation and improvement", and even "recognition of benefits" one-stop service to help building owners meet net zero carbon reduction needs.
Features and innovation
Local building energy simulation and evaluation platform: domestic meteorological/building materials/power consumption equipment database, rapid and accurate assessment of the measures and benefits required for level 1+ energy efficiency, and formulate the best net zero path (shortest ROI) for the owner.
Low-operation carbon equipment solution: Level 1 energy-efficient ice machine, significantly reducing power consumption/carbon emissions of key hot spots (air conditioners)... etc.
Application and benefits
Rapidly evaluate the net zero path to planning domestic commercial and office buildings, and cross-domain integration to assist commercial and office buildings in completing in-depth energy-saving transformation (to level 1 + building energy efficiency) and renewable energy assessment to achieve net zero goals.
