About the Development Center of Measuring Technology
The Center for Measuring Technology Development originated from the Central Standards Bureau (now the Bureau of Standards Inspection) signed a contract with the Institute of Technology Research on behalf of the Ministry of Economic Affairs and commissioned the Institute of Technology to implement the "Specification and Measuring Instrument Inspection Project". The Institute of Technology therefore established the "Type of Weights and Measuring Instrument Inspection Center", which was later renamed the "Type of Measuring Technology Development Center".
The Measurement Technology Development Center supports the construction and fair transactions of science, industry, people's livelihood foundation with the technical research and development and industrial services of measurement standards, testing, verification, and measurement instruments, and achieves "precise and good service, standard and good life", so that product quality, people's lives, government public power and environmental protection and medical care can be accurately guaranteed.
The main tasks of the Measuring Technology Development Center are to build and be responsible for the highest national measurement standards, develop advanced metrology science and technology, implement the industrialization of metrology technology, build the world's leading semiconductor metrology technology, and lead the independent supply chain of high-level metrology instruments and equipment.Develop standards, metrology, detection and verification technologies in the fields of solar photoelectricity, renewable energy, green chemistry, etc., and establish industrial and social resilience.The Center for Measuring Technology Development has actively cooperated with domestic and foreign organizations to deeply explore the next-generation metrology technology, including forward-looking fields such as net zero carbon emissions and sustainable development, semiconductor detection and metrology, and digital transformation.
