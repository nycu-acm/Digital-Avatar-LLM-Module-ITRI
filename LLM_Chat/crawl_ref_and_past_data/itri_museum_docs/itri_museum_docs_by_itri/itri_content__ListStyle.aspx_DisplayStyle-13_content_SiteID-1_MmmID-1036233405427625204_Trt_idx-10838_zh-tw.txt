技術名稱：
複合生物炭技術
技術簡介
建立並優化了複合生物炭製作流程與參數，確保製備過程的可操作性，並通過CO2吸附試驗評估，測得複合生物炭的CO2吸附量，驗證複合生物炭在CO2吸附方面的性能。
Abstract
It established and optimized the production process and parameters for composite biochar, ensuring the operability of the preparation process. Through CO2 adsorption experiments, the CO2 adsorption capacity of the composite biochar was measured to meet the project targets, verifying its performance in CO2 adsorption.
技術規格
複合生物炭的CO2吸附量2-5mmole/g
Technical Specification
The CO₂ adsorption capacity of composite biochar is 2–5 mmol/g.
