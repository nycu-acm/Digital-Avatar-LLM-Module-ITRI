Academician Yang Yumin
Academician Yang Yumin is the chairman and co-founder of Yushi Expo Biotechnology, the chairman of Archigen Biotechnology, and the chairman of the new drug company AbGenomics in the United States. With his outstanding professional ability, he is active in the global pharmaceutical industry. He is also a director of Taishan Investment Management Company and serves as a special expert of the Executive Yuan Biotechnology Advisory Committee. He is an important driving force in promoting the integration of Taiwan's biomedical industry into the international market.
After graduating from the Department of Electronic Engineering at Jiaotong University, Chairman Yang went to the United States to obtain a Ph.D.in Electrical Engineering from Ohio State University. In his early years, he worked at Qiyi Motor. Due to his excellent performance in the control engineering, he was recruited by Merck Group and successfully entered the pharmaceutical industry. He has served as Vice President of Merck's Operations Supply Chain, Executive Vice President of Gene Technology, and President of Roche Global Technology.He is still the highest manager of Asians in international pharmaceutical factories. Based on the biotechnology industry's many years of production and manufacturing experience, Chairman Yang proposed the "Yang's Law" equivalent to the "Moore's Law" in the semiconductor industry.It is internationally renowned, and the production of antibody per liter per liter will increase by about 100% every 4 years.
In recent years, Chairman Yang has been concerned about the development of the next generation of biotechnology industry and believes that "Taiwan cannot submit a blank paper in the biotechnology industry." In 2006, he was invited to return to Taiwan and served as a member of the Zhubei Biomedical Steering Committee of the National Science and Technology Council, setting development goals and strategies for the Zhubei Biomedical Park.Later, he served as a member of the Executive Yuan Biotechnology Industry Strategy Advisory Committee and a director of the Taishan Investment Management Consulting Company, serving as the promoter of the government's investment fund to promote the development of the biotechnology industry, assisting the government in formulating policies and promoting industrial development strategies.
In order to accelerate the integration of domestic biomedical industry with advanced biotechnology trends, Chairman Yang and U.S.-based biotechnology expert Xiao Shijia co-founded Yushi Expo Biotechnology in Taiwan in 2016, specializing in the development of immune cell antibody bonding technology and new drugs to improve the shortcomings of existing cell immunotherapy, which not only cultivates talents and technologies for the biotechnology medical industry, but also brings new opportunities to the precision medical industry.
