=== Full Compatibility Two-Way Intelligent Charger ===

The Full Compatibility Two-Way Intelligent Charger is compatible with multiple international electric vehicle charging interfaces, and provide quick charging services within 10 minutes. This intelligent charger is combined with 6 proprietary dynamic load management and bi-directional charging technologies to complete smart grid modulation. With cloud-based vehicle and charger diagnostics, a new energy service platform is established for users, utility operators, vehicle manufacturers, and vendors. This platform has highly compatibility with international standards and can be exported directly to Southeast Asia, Europe, and the United States without changing any system designs.

CelluadTM utilizes cellulose derivatives as raw materials, and possesses excellent adhesion and water resistance. === The Muddle of Micro-LED Signage ===

ITRI developed a fine pitch passive matrix μLED display module with the resolution of 80 x 80 by successfully integrating the μLED array with the PCB substrate. Fine pitch μLED display panel breaks the bottleneck of the conventional LED display which has been suffered by the limitation of reaching less than 1mm pitch. === iPetWeaR ===

iPetWeaR is an innovative wearable device using ITRI's low-power radar sensing technology to detect multiple physiological parameters for non-contact and long-term pet health monitoring. This innovation is capable of tracking a pet’s health data such as heart rate, respiration, and activity. It can alert the owner in events of abnormalities via a mobile app and is usable both indoors and outdoors.

=== An Optical Particulate Matter (PM) 2.5 Sensor ===

ITRI’s Optical PM 2.5 Sensor is developed based on optical scattering, combining with a dual flow particle filtering channel and particle signal feature recognition to estimate PM 2.5 concentration in ambient air. The Environmental Protection Agency (EPA) field tests showed that the sensor yielded a precision-and-bias error rate in concentrations ≦50% and a high correlation with R2>0.7 compared to reference instruments. The sensor can collaborate with other devices as a reference. This cost–effective technology can be adopted for personal use or indoor/outdoor air quality monitoring for IoT applications.

圖片: https://vrmaker.istaging.com/3e5c2a66-9f4a-4174-aff1-3d8d0e7449b9/markers/VIuY84aN5z

=== Soybean Dregs Discarding and Value-Adding Technology ===

ITRI provides two different methods for industries to dispose of soybean dregs: (1) using large quantities of soybean dregs as swine feed, and (2) processing soybean dregs into value-added products such as cat litter and vermicompost. Starting from the pet and feed markets, ITRI collects the feedback from export manufacturers and consumers and then markets the technology to food and fertilizer indsutries. Gradually, a  collaborative ecosystem for soy products is built up, driving futher industrial development.

=== PECOLA ===

ITRI's PECOLA employs ambient intelligence technology in caring for its elderly companions, making sure they are eating and sleeping well and are in good health and spirits. The robot also compiles information and sends it to the children of these individuals to help spark topics of discussion and bolster communication between them. PECOLA uses image recognition to record food portion change and carry out diet analysis to understand the individual's food intake. PECOLA also uses a WiFi signal in detecting one’s breathing rate during sleep. What's more, it utilizes deep learning technology to detect falls. Once a fall is detected, the robot immediately dials the individual's children and initiates a two-way video session, helping to improve safety in the home. A daily analysis report that includes the above physiological information will be generated and sent to family members’ mobile phones so that they can learn the seniors’ emotion, activity, diet, and sleep conditions. PECOLA continues to integrate third-party services, such as entertainment and social activities for the elderly, as well as functions to foster convenience, smart living, and healthcare.

=== Building Energy Simulation Technology with Artificial Intelligence ===

BESTAI (Building Energy Simulation Technology with Artificial Intelligence) is an energy-saving expert system with a user-friendly interface that allows non-specialists to easily perform energy diagnosis for commercial buildings, and residential buildings. It provides energy-saving strategies, ROI analysis, and real-time integration of geographical information systems (GIS) to analyze power consumption of buildings across multiple geographic zones. With BESTAI, users are able to conduct precise and low-cost energy-saving diagnosis for a large number of buildings in a short period of time.

=== Portable UVC LED Water Sterilizer System ===

ITRI’s Portable UVC LED Water Sterilizer System uses its self-made UV LED as the light source and reduces the sterilizer volume by 1/3, compared to a mercury lamp sterilizer. The UV LED is activated only when water flows through the sterilizer, which is energy-efficient. Also, the battery-powered design allows for easy carrying. This technology can provide immediate water sterilization to ensure drinking-water safety, making it an ideal solution for emergency situations such as in humanitarian assistance/disaster relief or in areas with poor sanitation.
