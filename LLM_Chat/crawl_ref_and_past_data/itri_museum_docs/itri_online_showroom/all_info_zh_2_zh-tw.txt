=== Reclaimed Asphalt Pavement Regeneration Technology ===

ITRI’s asphalt rejuvenating agent transforms reclaimed asphalt pavement (RAP) into new pavement material and thus solves its disposal problem. In general, recycled asphalt concrete with 40% RAP complies with the international standard of rutting resistance according to the Hamburg Wheel Tracking (HWT) Test. This novel technology creates a new paradigm for circular economy in turning waste into materials.

Key Features:
1.	High Efficiency: The technology rejuvenates aged asphalt binder and reduces the viscosity to improve compatibility between new and old asphalt.
2.	High Quality: The ductility of the recycled asphalt binder and the rutting resistance of the recycled asphalt concrete are both improved.
3.	High Recycle Rate: RAP can be 100% used in asphalt mixtures while meeting domestic requirements.
4.	Environmental Sustainability: The technology minimizes the use of natural resources, greatly improving the environment.

=== E-scooter Motor Drive ===

“世界各國已經制定了促進車輛電氣化的政策，以達到全球碳排放量的目標。儘管踏板車的便利性和機動性，但傳統的汽油踏板車仍會增加空氣污染並對環境構成嚴重威脅。
這款電子駕駛室電動機驅動器結合了世界上第一個具有超過8kW功率的高功率密度E型PIM。由ITRI電力電子研究小組開發的PIM，結合了三相逆變器和直流轉換器，當前電感，保護開關和溫度傳感。高密度包裝（SIP）的利用進一步增加了功率密度的三倍，而離散模塊的功率密度則增加了三倍。此外，使用絕緣金屬底物使電源模塊更加可靠和更安全。靈活的模塊與電動機驅動器集成在一起，並由於其易於變化的特性可以大大降低電動機故障頻率，因此輕鬆地穿透了AM市場。”
