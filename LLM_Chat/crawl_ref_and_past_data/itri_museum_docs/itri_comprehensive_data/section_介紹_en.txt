# introduce

The Institute of Technology is Taiwan’s largest industrial technology research and development institution and is also the pioneer in creating Taiwan’s semiconductor industry [1].
At the end of 1969, Sun Yun-hyun, who had just taken over as Minister of Economic Affairs, was invited to visit South Korea. When visiting the Institute of Science and Technology in Seoul, South Korea, he was impressed by the Korean government's approach to giving preferential treatment and flexible regulations to scientific and technological talents to develop the science and technology industry. After returning to the country, he immediately began to promote similar institutions.[2] In 1973, then Minister of Economic Affairs Sun Yun-chi promoted the merger of the United Industrial Research Institute, the United Mining Research Institute and the Metal Industry Research Institute to establish the Industrial Technology Research Institute, and gave the Industrial Research Institute the goal of promoting Taiwan's industrial development and economic takeoff with advanced industrial technology.In 1975, RCA was selected as a partner of Taiwan's integrated circuit cooperation project. In 1976, the first batch of engineers trained in the United States were ready to go. In 1977, the Institute of Technology established Taiwan's first integrated circuit demonstration factory with 4-inch wafers. Since 1980, it has successively derived semiconductor manufacturers including UMC, TSMC, Taiwan's photomonitor, and the world's advanced, laying the foundation for the takeoff of Taiwan's IC industry; in 1983, the Institute of Technology developed a personal computer compatible with IBM and transferred technology to domestic operators, driving the vigorous development of surrounding industries and laying the foundation for the personal computer information industry.
The Institute of Technology has used the uniqueness of the R&D environment and through the dense and solid industrial network relationship, connecting the R&D energy of the region or industry, and actively assisting Taiwan's industrial development and upgrading.Since 2005, in order to cooperate with Taiwan's overall regional industrial development policy and strengthen the connection between science and technology and local industries, the "South Branch" in Luojia, Tainan and the "South Branch" in Nantou have been established to deepen the research and development zone for Taiwan's industrial innovation.
