# Controversy

In April 2025, during the implementation of the Ministry of Health and Welfare’s “Next Generation Digital Medical Platform (page archive backup, saved in the Internet Archives)” project, the Institute of Industrial Technology (Industry Research Institute) issued a notice of widespread concern and controversy among the open source community because it did not mark the authorization content of the open source project in accordance with regulations and explicitly prohibited specific individuals and companies from using the project in the authorization clause of another national standard project “TW Core IG”, which aroused widespread attention and controversy among the open source community.[3] On April 16, 2025, an engineer (hereinafter referred to as the "Engineer in question") added a new issue discussion to the GitHub project ITRI-BDL-D/CQL-Project-template (page archive backup, stored in the Internet Archives) [4], accusing the Institute of Technology of failure to comply with the Apache-2.0 authorization terms (page archive backup, stored in the Internet Archives) and copied the code of the US AHRQ open source project CQL-Testing-Framework (page archive backup, stored in the Internet Archives) and uploaded it to the project managed by it, but did not attach the original authorization statement [5][6]. On the same day, the Institute of Technology Research and Research added authorized files, but still did not mark the necessary information such as the original author, which did not meet the requirements of the authorization terms [7].On April 19, 2025, the Institute of Technology updated the authorization clause in the TW Core IG project, expressly prohibiting the engineers and their companies from using the content of the project, which caused the outside world to question whether government resources should not be restricted for specific individuals [8].On April 19, 2025, the Institute of Technology updated the authorization terms and lifted the above restrictions, stating that "we know that the engineers involved have learned that the CC BY-NC-SA 4.0 license in their special project is particularly prohibited from using the Institute of Technology, so we have lifted the restrictions on our special project license." [9].On April 19, 2025, the engineer involved responded, pointing out that the reason why the restricted clauses of his special project was because the Institute of Technology "has it incorporated its special project into the medical capital platform without consent or notice?" and called on the Institute of Technology to apologize publicly [10].On April 19, 2025, the Information Department of the Ministry of Health and Welfare issued a statement thanking the public for reminding the open source authorization sign. The program could have been publicly obtained from the US website. In order to avoid unnecessary misunderstandings, this department has already required the tools to provide links to the original website; the digital tools and resources provided by the medical information platform will never set limits or block everyone's applications under the premise of reasonable and legitimate use.On April 20, 2025, the Institute of Technology and Research Public apologized for the incomplete authorization notice, saying that it will complete a comprehensive review of the technical structure and document guidance within one week, and emphasized that it will strengthen cooperation with open source communities in the future and implement the spirit of knowledge sharing.However, no specific explanation is provided for restricting the use of government public resources by specific persons [13][14].
