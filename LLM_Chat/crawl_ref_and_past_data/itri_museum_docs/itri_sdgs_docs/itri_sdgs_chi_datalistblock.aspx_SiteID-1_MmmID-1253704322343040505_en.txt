External Independence Warranty Statement
The Institute of Technology has independently issued a sustainable report in 2022, not only following the GRI standard (GRI standard) that global reports must comply with, but also further referring to the opinions of domestic and foreign benchmark companies, scientific research organizations and internal and external expert consultants, integrates the international climate and human rights disclosure framework, and focuses on 16 SDGs United Nations sustainable development goals, presenting our institute's sustainable performance in a more transparent, consistent and verifiable way, leading the value chain to jointly exert its sustainable influence.
In 2023, our court further introduced the "TCFD climate-related financial information disclosure" structure to enhance our court's ability to face climate risks and opportunities and respond to global climate change.
In 2024, our hospital extended its TCFD structure and added a new "TNFD Natural Related Financial Revealing" structure;and in accordance with the ISO 26000 Social Responsibility Guidelines and the IFRS S1 and S2 International Financial Reporting Standards-Sustainable Revealing Standards, we will continue to deepen the sustainable practice of our hospital organization.
