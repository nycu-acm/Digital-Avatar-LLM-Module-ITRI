Management Structure and System The occupational safety and health management structure of this institute is centered on the "Integrated Management System Committee" composed of integrated management representatives, professional members and employee representatives of each unit. It also sets up a "Biosafety Association" and "Experimental Animal Care and Use Committee" in accordance with the law. It is responsible for deliberating and coordinating related management matters such as safety, sanitation, environmental protection, fire protection, radiation protection, biosafety management in the workplace. It is set up a "Environmental Safety Working Group" under the "Integrated Management System Committee". Its members are the environmental safety and health supervisors and employee representatives of each unit. It is responsible for the development and development of relevant management plans, submitted to the "Integrated Management System Committee" for review and approval and implementation and promotion within the unit. Through the comprehensive management structure and consulting of employee representatives , participate and communicate to ensure that the entire hospital meets the requirements of occupational safety and health management. The promotion structure of the integrated management system of the safety and health products of the hospital. In order to make the occupational safety and health management of the hospital have efficiency and quality, the hospital continues to build and improve the environmental safety and health information system. The "On-site Operation Management and Control" information system is used during on-site operation and management to improve management efficiency and save management resources; the "Obnormal Control" information system summarizes abnormal events of each unit and tracks and manages; the "Risk Control" information system is identified, evaluated and controlled through a systematic method. In recent years, it has continued to move towards system intelligence and data integration and application, and the system is continuously improved and optimized. Safety and Health Promotion In order to enhance the safety and health awareness of colleagues in the hospital, 2023 Promote multiple safety and health activities in the year, including safety and health training for new colleagues, new supervisors, in-service employees and professionals, and hold safety and health model case selection, safety and health poster selection, theme safety publicity design and publicity, visitor safety video shooting, SDGs board game activities and supervisor special speeches. These activities aim to promote participation of all employees, improve safety and health attention and practice ability, create a good atmosphere, and establish an excellent safety and health culture. To ensure the safety of workers and workplaces, all units plan automatic inspection plans every year, perform independent inspections, and complete ISO 45001 internal audit, cross-audit audits of all units in the hospital, and management system verification and audits, etc.; 2023 In response to the "safety of chemical substance operation", a special inspection of the entire hospital was planned and special inspections were conducted. Experts outside the hospital were invited to serve as inspectors in some of the auditors. They could not only assist in discovering and analyzing the situation of non-compliance and propose solutions, but also provide the opportunity for safety and health personnel to study and observe, and continuously improve the capacity of the safety and health professionals of the hospital. Each hospital area received external inspections such as safety and health management, environmental protection management, fire protection, and energy of the competent authorities. All the proposed improvements will be improved within the deadline. Accidents and occupational injury. This court has formulated a complete accident notification, investigation, tracking and improvement process, and counted and compiled the story and improvement status every month. After the general accident is closed, it was reported at the meeting of the Environmental Safety Working Group, and major accidents were reported at the Integrated Management System Committee. In 2023, a total of 24 colleagues' injuries occurred, including falls, harmful substance contact, electricity induction, etc., and all units have completed the notification, investigation and improvement, and carried out risk reassessment and prevention measures.
