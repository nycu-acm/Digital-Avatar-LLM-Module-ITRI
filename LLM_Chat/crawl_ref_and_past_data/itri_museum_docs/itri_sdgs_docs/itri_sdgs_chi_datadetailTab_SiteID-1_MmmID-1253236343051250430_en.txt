Together to create value
Since the Industrial Revolution, human beings have excessive use of natural resources, resulting in global warming and climate change, and have brought many irreversible impacts to social and environmental destruction. COP28 was launched for the first time after it was launched in 2023, assessing and reviewing the progress of carbon reduction in countries since the signing of the Paris Agreement in 2015. In the future, countries must regularly report on the actions taken and related progress taken to mitigate climate change. As a national research institution, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research conducted a "self-inventory" for the first time in 2022, leading domestic scientific research organizations to release a sustainable report.
In 2023, our hospital will continue to take stock of the development of scientific and technological research and development, industrial development, social welfare, integrity governance, happy workplace and green campus based on the six key points of the organization's sustainable development strategy blueprint.In terms of integrity governance, a sound integrity governance system is built through legal compliance, risk control and security management.In terms of a happy workplace, we actively create a safe, healthy and friendly workplace to promote the integrated development of diverse talents.In terms of green campuses, energy-saving strategies are formulated, combined with solar photoelectric renewable energy equipment construction and power health inspection, and continuous introduction of self-developed energy-saving technologies, saving nearly 10 million kilowatt-hours of electricity per year;expand the scope of greenhouse gas inspection to the six major campuses of our hospital, and analyze them based on the results of the inspection to set carbon reduction paths and various action plans; and form a TCFD climate governance team for the whole hospital to evaluate the impact of climate risks on our hospital and develop response measures.In addition, using the solid R&D energy of our institute to invest in industrial development and social welfare, 54 charity activities were held annually, with nearly 1,326 volunteers participating, and cultivating more than 2,000 industrial carbon management talents. The SROI social impact assessment results of "Net Zero Sustainable School" were released for the first time. For every NTD 1 invested, a social value of NTD 7.45 can be generated, symbolizing that our institute continues to promote the sustainable operation of the organization with steady steps, coordinates the industry and society to exert social influence, and move towards sustainable prosperity together.
In order to actively implement and promote the sustainable development of our institute, the first "Sustainable Development Model Award" will be held in 2023, encouraging all units and colleagues to innovate technology research and development or services, and specific practices of sustainable and related applications will be applied in organizational operations; for various sustainable development goals, the six sustainable working groups will set short, medium and long-term management indicators, and will be reviewed and improved year by year, and will correspond to various sustainable norms and guidelines issued by major organizations around the world: such as the latest preparation guidelines (GRI 2021), climate-related financial disclosure (TCFD), and SASB industry standards index. Through the verification of the implementation reports of third-party independent institutions, the risk management mechanism of sustainable issues and the transparency of the disclosure of sustainable reports will be strengthened year by year.
As a bridge between the country and industry, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has conducted a self-examination with rigorous sustainable strategies and target management in the face of national sustainable policies and various emerging sustainable issues. It also upholds the spirit of the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals "Leave no one behind", continues to use the energy of our institute's scientific and technological innovation and research and development, assists the industry in establishing a circular economy thinking, and through the joint efforts of stakeholders, it will expand its sustainability to the supply chain, industry and client, establish a sustainable value chain with ITRI as the core, and move towards the 2050 net zero emission target.
