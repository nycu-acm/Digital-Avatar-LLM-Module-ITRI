In 2015, the United Nations proposed the "2030 Sustainable Development Goals" and clearly provided 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs), which prompted governments and organizations to systematically include their impacts on the environment, population and society in the assessment in addition to developing economic activities. The National Development Conference also proposed and released the "Taiwan Sustainable Development Goals" in 2016-2022 with reference to the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals SDGs. As a national think tank, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research insists on moving towards the sustainable development goal with integrity and steady pace. It not only continues to promote integrity governance, innovative R&D and technology transfer, but also contributes to the improvement of Taiwan's international competitiveness;it is also committed to improving social welfare, applying various scientific and technological R&D achievements to invest in public welfare activities and industrial applications, and actively participating in social responsibility; in terms of environmental protection, our institute has built green buildings and vigorously promoted the layout of renewable energy and energy-saving and carbon reduction measures.In the future, our institute will continue to use scientific and technological innovation to reverse the industrial chain and national life, and will be guided by market demand, develop solutions, and create a new market to seek the welfare of human society and lead the industrial society to a better future.
