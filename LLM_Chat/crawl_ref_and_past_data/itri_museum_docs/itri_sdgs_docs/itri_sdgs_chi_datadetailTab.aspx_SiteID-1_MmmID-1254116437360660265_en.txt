Waste treatment
Release date 2024-07-15
Our hospital attaches importance to waste treatment and air pollution prevention and control, and is committed to reducing environmental impact. This institute has formed a professional team to develop waste reduction strategies, optimize resource recycling processes, expand recyclable species, and communicate with suppliers and users to improve waste conversion and recycling efficiency.At the same time, as a national R&D unit, our hospital ensures that it meets the requirements of the laws and regulations based on the diverse and complex waste generated by the laboratory, and focuses on experimental waste management and air pollution prevention based on the research and development direction and planning schedule.
Waste and waste management
General waste disposal
In order to improve resource utilization and extend the life cycle of items, our hospital has expanded the types of recyclable species, promoted paper reproduction of documents, and held waste reduction and carbon reduction activities, and cooperated with suppliers, resource recycling manufacturers and social welfare groups.The total amount of general waste in 2023 was 724.95 metric tons, an increase of 19% over the previous year, mainly due to the increase in manufacturers in Zhongchuang Park and Shalun Green Energy Technology Demonstration Sites.In the future, our hospital will increase the types and proportions of recycling to continuously reduce the impact of waste on the environment.
General waste statistics
General waste statistics in each campus
Experimental waste treatment
The experimental waste in this hospital mainly comes from chemical laboratories and experimental factories. There are many types of raw materials and chemicals used in the research experiments, and the R&D properties are different from the project schedule. There are a small amount of experimental waste and the output is uncertain.The experimental waste treatment of this hospital shall be applied for cleaning and transportation through the waste cleaning and transportation management system of this hospital.The management department will assist and send it to the waste storage sites in each hospital for classified management.
In the past three years, since 2021, the proportion of hazardous business waste has increased from 28.89% to 35.25% and 50.01% year by year; among them, C-02 corrosive and C-03 flammable waste is the bulk, and taking 2023 as an example, it accounts for 55.44% and 43.42% of hazardous business waste.In order to legally and effectively deal with various experimental wastes, our hospital has signed annual contracts with 24 qualified waste removal and reuse institutions, covering various methods such as incineration and heat treatment, and preferential reuse manufacturers.Over the years, our hospital has also continued to promote and promote the purification and reuse of waste liquids in the process, screening waste liquids as wastewater treatment nutrient bases to practice resource recycling and achieve the goal of reducing waste.

Note: The statistical scope of experimental waste covers the Zhongxing Campus, Guangfu Campus, Liujia Campus, Zhongchuang Park, Nanchuang Park and Shalun Green Energy Technology Demonstration Fields.
