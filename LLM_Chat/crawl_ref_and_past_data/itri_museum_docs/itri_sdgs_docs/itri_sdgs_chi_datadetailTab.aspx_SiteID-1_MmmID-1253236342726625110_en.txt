Identification of stakeholders
The Institute of Technology attaches importance to and listens to the voices of all interested parties. After discussion and review by members of the "Organizing the Sustainable Development Committee", the Office of the Sustainable Development and the Working Group, the internal and external groups that have an impact on the operational activities of the Institute of Technology and Research and causes certain impacts, the five principles of the AA1000 Stakeholder Engagement Standard (SES) are based on the five principles of the AA1000 Stakeholder Engagement Standard (SES): dependency, responsibility, influence, tension / attention, and diverse perspectives. The 40 questionnaires were distributed through the sustainable key management level to conduct stakeholder identification surveys, and after statistical scores, seven major stakeholder interested parties were identified (sorted by weight): government units/competent agencies, employees, enterprises/customers, public associations/industry alliances, academic and research partners, suppliers, communities/ Social welfare groups, subsequent major thematic analysis will also refer to the weight scores of stakeholders for identification.
Seven categories of major stakeholders
Interested parties
The Institute of Technology attaches great importance to and listens to the voices of stakeholders from all parties. Through regular and irregular communication channels, we can understand the actual and potential impacts of our hospital's operating activities on relevant groups, and serve as the basis for relevant prevention and mitigation actions in the future.In the future, we will collect relevant feedback from interested parties through physical interviews and regular collections, and conduct identification and investigations based on needs to ensure the comprehensiveness of interested parties and establish a diversified and two-way communication pipeline with them to implement the sustainable industrial value of our hospital.
