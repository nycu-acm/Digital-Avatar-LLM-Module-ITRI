Organizational culture
Since its establishment in 1973, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has taken "innovation, integrity and sharing" as its core value and attaches great importance to the promotion and implementation of the organization's integrity culture. In order to deepen the culture of integrity within the organization, our court has formulated the "Code of Integrity Business", "Measures for the Setting of Integrity Business Committees" and "Key Points for Handling Integrity Business", and implemented the concept of honest business of all colleagues in their work, becoming an effective bridge connecting industries and research, assisting industrial transformation and upgrading, and continuing to make important contributions to Taiwan's economic development.
"Innovation" is the unchanging core value of the Institute of Technology Research.In order to improve the resilience of organizational response to changes in the environment, Institute of Technology integrates the three major cultural DNAs of "innovative thinking", "open communication" and "agile management", and has designed an employee-centered learning experience, subtly embedded the cultural DNA of Institute of Technology in the hearts of every colleague, leading the institute from an active follower of science and technology to an important innovator, and working with stakeholders to move towards a more open, agile and innovative future.
Direction of organizational cultural transformation
