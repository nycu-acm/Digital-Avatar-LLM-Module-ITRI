Value chain of the Institute of Technology
The Institute of Industrial Research has been deeply rooted in Taiwan for 50 years, and has continued to focus on Taiwan's industry as the core, connecting the global innovative technology ecological network, connecting with the international community, building a platform for scientific and technological research and development and cross-domain cooperation. Through diversified partners, advanced technology research and development and application, and international industry-university exchanges, it assists the industry in developing innovative technologies, cultivating key talents, and driving the overall industrial transformation and upgrading of the country.
International participation
The Institute of Industrial Research is a bridge for exchanges between Taiwan's industrial science and technology and international institutions.It connects the global innovative technology ecological network with deep potential forecast industrial technology development and scientific research capabilities, and integrates and layouts forward-looking technology market applications across fields.
In addition to establishing overseas strongholds such as North American companies (Silicon Valley in the United States), Japan (Tokyo), Europe (Berlin in Germany), and Europe (Berlin in Germany), the Southeast Asia Office (Bangkok, Thailand) has also been added in response to the cooperation type. Through overseas strongholds, it continues to deepen its links, connects international strategic partners and resources with bilateral/multilateral cooperation niche and long-term partnerships, continuously expands international cooperation, establishes a regional exchange and cooperation platform, strengthens technological R&D energy and spreads R&D results, and plays an important role as an international bridge. So far, international partners have covered the industry, official, academic and research circles, covering 4 continents, 29 countries, and more than 191 organizations.

Partners
As an important bridge to assist in the transformation and upgrading of the industry, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research continues to focus on important issues such as scientific and technological innovation, integrity governance, and environmental sustainability through continuous participation in different industrial associations, scientific and technological R&D societies, and alliances and non-profit organizations related to public policy, through policy discussions, academic seminars, consulting consultants, etc., to contribute to the overall industry, our institute's strong scientific and technological innovation application and R&D capabilities, and to promote Taiwan's overall industrial exchanges and scientific and technological development.
As of the end of 2023, the number of domestic public associations participating in this institute has reached 557, including various industrial public associations, societies, alliances and research groups. Among them, 183 organizations are held by senior supervisors, supervisors and colleagues of the Institute of Technology and Research.

Industrial Services
The Institute of Technology is committed to deepening the integration of technological forward-looking and cross-field technology.Through diversified technical guidance, patent advantages and the foundation of open laboratories and cultivation centers, it provides all-round R&D cooperation and business consulting services to various industries. The service scope includes new technologies and new products commissioned development, small-scale mass production, process improvement, inspection and measurement, as well as technology transfer, intelligent value-added services.In addition, our hospital actively promotes and cultivates new startup companies, accelerates industrial technology development and nurtures emerging high-tech industries, and provides the support and resources needed by entrepreneurs through open laboratories and entrepreneurial cultivation centers to help realize their entrepreneurial dreams.
supply chain management
The main operating bases of this institute are all located in Taiwan.By 2023, the total number of suppliers has been exchanged. The largest number is labor service procurement.The others include hardware and components, machinery and production equipment, repair engineering, instruments and equipment and accessories, etc. In 2023, there were about 9,830 suppliers that had purchased transactions with our institute.
Regarding the actions of our court in terms of integrity governance on supplier management, the "Supplier Code of Conduct" of this court will be declared on the homepage of the "Procurement Bidding Announcement";starting from August 1, 2023, bidders participating in this court's bidding will provide the manufacturer's ESG statement as a qualification document based on the bidding instructions of this court, with a total of 403 cases.
