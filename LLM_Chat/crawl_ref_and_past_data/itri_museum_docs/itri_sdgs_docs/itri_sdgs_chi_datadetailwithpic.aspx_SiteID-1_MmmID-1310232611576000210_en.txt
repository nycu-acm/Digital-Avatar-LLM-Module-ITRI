The Institute of Technology adheres to the core values ​​of innovation, integrity and sharing, continues to deepen the culture of organizational integrity, strengthen the governance structure of our institute, and ensures that all scientific research activities are carried out in accordance with the principle of integrity through the implementation of legal education and internal supervision mechanisms. At the same time, our hospital has established a comprehensive and complete risk management system to prevent potential risks and promote the continuous improvement and innovation of the organization.The Institute of Technology and Research has also continuously strengthened international participation, linked partners, strengthened industrial services and improved supply chain management, and continuously enhanced the value of the overall industrial chain, which not only strengthened its competitiveness in the global science and technology stage, but also contributed important strength to Taiwan's industrial development.
