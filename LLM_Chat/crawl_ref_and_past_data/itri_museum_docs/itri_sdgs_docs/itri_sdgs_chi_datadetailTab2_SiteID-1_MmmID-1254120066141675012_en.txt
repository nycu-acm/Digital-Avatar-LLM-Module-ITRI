Release date 2024-07-22
The Institute of Technology and Research actively recruits outstanding professionals from around the world, committed to Taiwan's industrial development and organization of sustainable operations. Talent recruitment and cultivation goals are set every year to ensure that sufficient elites lead innovation, drive industrial development, and connect with national policies and the United Nations SDGs. In order to expand the source of talents, promote long-distance work, jointly recruit professors, deepen chemical research cooperation and encourage cross-field collaboration, and attract global elites to join.
Operate employer brand
Our institute continues to operate professional community platforms (such as LinkedIn, etc.) to attract professional talents in various fields.The 2023 cross-border cooperation to recruit talents and retain talents will be specially planned for the "Research Universe" of the dual-spindle "Research Universe" will be a wonderful story for unboxing and researching people, and will increase the outside world's understanding of the Institute of Technology.
Recruitment platform and pipeline
In 2023, 804 full-time personnel were recruited, including 78 overseas talents, with a new entry rate of 13.07% (up 2.62% from the previous year). In order to solve the gap between learning and application, our college also actively hires school interns to provide young students with opportunities to explore various technology applications and industrial learning.Interns with excellent performance can obtain priority employment qualifications, creating a win-win situation for young people's employment and our college's innovative R&D goals.A total of 1,161 regular employees are hired annually, accounting for 91.13% of them under the age of 30. In order to help new employees quickly adapt to and integrate into the new environment, the "100-day companionship plan for newcomers" will be promoted in 2023. The overall favorability of newcomers is 4.5 (five-point scale), of which the tutoring and assistance to supervisors and mentors will be praised by 4.6.
Deepen chemical research cooperation
We have signed a cooperation agreement on academic research with 16 universities in China, making good use of diversified cooperation in academic research to attract talents and cultivate talents by 1,658 people, the number of professors jointly hired has grown by 22%, and the number of students has grown by 15%.In addition, we have signed a talent exchange agreement with the National Research Council Canada (NRC) to carry out talent exchange, etc.
Note: Liu Wenxiong, Director of the Institute of Technology Research (left), signed a talent exchange agreement with Iain Stewart, President of the Canadian NRC (right), as RIN (RTOs
International Network; RIN) Demonstration of the first talent exchange agreement.
Hold various talent recruitment activities
Various talent recruitment activities and global talent online recruitment meetings are held every year. There will be a total of 25 events in 2023, with the supervisors and colleagues of our institute serving as recruitment ambassadors. The participation rate of foreigners and land is growing year by year.
Develop diversified internship project
Every year, through the intermediary internship project, the research and development field of our institute is displayed to guide students to experience collaborative experience.The training-style recruitment held in 2023 - Jingying Project, establishing a pre-employment training model for young people's employment, with the first admission rate reaching 33%.
Layout the next generation of talents
Actively recruit the former Minsheng Campus Ambassador, promote the former Minsheng and high school students to visit and hold discussions in the college, so that young students can get to know the Institute of Engineering Research.
