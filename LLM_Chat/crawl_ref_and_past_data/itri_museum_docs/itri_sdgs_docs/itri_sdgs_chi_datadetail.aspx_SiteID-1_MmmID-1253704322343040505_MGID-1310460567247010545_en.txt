For details on the permanent financial performance of our court in 2024, please refer to the chapter "About this report - Financial Performance". The direction of independent disclosure of our court follows my country's policy guidelines and is based on the "Blueprint of my country's compliance with IFRS's Perpetual Disclosure Guidelines", although IFRS The Perpetual Revealing Guidelines cover all sustainable themes, but all sustainable themes can be exempted from being exposed in the first year, and only climate-related themes are revealed. This institute uses the TCFD working group composed of each unit to identify climate-related risks and opportunities, and defines significant risks and opportunities based on their impact intensity and possibility of organizational operations. Therefore, the "climate-related risks and opportunities" of our institute are used as the evaluation target. According to IFRS S1 S2, reasonable expectations are recognized and will affect our institute's sustainability and climate-related risks and opportunities. The following major information is disclosed for the main users of general purpose financial reports. Summary of Sustainable Financial Performance of the Institute of Industrial Research in 2024 Resilience is committed to converting technical assets into industrial benefits. In 2024, enterprise revenue accounts for 30% of the total revenue of our institute (NT$8.8 billion), an average growth of 5 over the past three years. % to make financial health more sound.
