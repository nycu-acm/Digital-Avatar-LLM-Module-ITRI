As one of Taiwan’s largest scientific and technological research and development institutions, the Institute of Technology continues to deepen technological forward-looking and cross-field integration, provides diversified R&D cooperation and consulting services, and strives to promote Taiwan’s industrial upgrading and transformation. At the same time, the Institute of Industrial Research has long assisted the high-tech industry in cultivating key strategic talents. Through market-oriented talent training, professional technical courses and excellent talent resources in the institute, it actively cultivates elites in various fields and comprehensively enhances the competitiveness of the industry and the country.
At the same time, our college also extends the benefits of scientific and technological development to the social level, combining science and technology education promotion, corporate volunteer participation and public welfare services, solving social problems through innovative technology, shortening urban-rural gaps, and improving social welfare.Taking "scientific and technological innovation" as the core force in promoting social progress, we are committed to creating positive influence for Taiwan's industry and society, and work with all walks of life to create a sustainable future.
