Release date 2024-08-09
All laboratories of this hospital apply for fixed pollution source installation and operation permits and set up special personnel in accordance with the provisions of the law to implement air pollution prevention and treatment measures to reduce the emission of air pollutants such as volatile organic compounds (VOCs), nitrogen oxides (NOx), sulfur oxides (SOx) and particulate pollutants (PM). The air pollution prevention equipment of this hospital is suitable for different pollutants. For specific toxicity, flammability and perfluoride process exhaust gases, air pollution prevention and treatment equipment is set up, and special treatment technologies such as adsorption and combustion cracking are used to send the treated waste gas to the central exhaust scrubber for water washing and neutralization and discharge.
This court commissioned a testing agency approved by the Ministry of Environment to conduct air pollutant emission concentration testing. In 2023, the volatile organic emissions reported by our court were 1.74 metric tons, 0.70 metric tons of nitrogen oxides, 0.04 metric tons of sulfur oxides and 0.01 metric tons of granular pollutants.The test results of each emission pipeline are lower than the emission standards stipulated by the Ministry of Environment. This not only presents the rigorous management of our court, but also demonstrates its commitment to the development of a sustainable environment.
Annual air pollutant emissions
Note:
1. The air pollutant emissions of this hospital shall be declared in accordance with the laws and regulations.
2. The scope covers the electro-optical system office of ZTE campus, the machinery office and the material and chemical office of the Liujia campus.
