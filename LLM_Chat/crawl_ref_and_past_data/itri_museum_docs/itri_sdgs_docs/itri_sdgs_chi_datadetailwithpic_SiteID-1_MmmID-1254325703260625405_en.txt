It is committed to using science and technology to drive Taiwan's industrial upgrading, and has long assisted the high-tech industry in cultivating key strategic talents, in order to strengthen market-oriented talent training, professional technology and excellent talent pool in the institute, assist in cultivating elites in various fields and enhancing the competitiveness of the industry and the country.At the same time, our institute also applies scientific and technological development strategies to the social orientation, and through science and technology education, volunteer groups and public welfare services, we will make good use of science and technology to exert social influence, use scientific and technological innovation as a solid backing for social development, create positive benefits for Taiwan's industries and society, and move towards a better future together.
