Net Zero Transformation and Promoting Industrial Net Zero
Release date: 2024-08-12

Net Zero Sustainability Key Technology and Service Types
48
item
This court has formulated the "2050 Net Zero Sustainable Strategic Goal", using its net zero-related R&D and application experience in renewable energy, energy conservation and carbon reduction over the years, and internally use the four major aspects of "low-carbon living, construction, transportation and parks" as a demonstration field for promoting net zero transformation; externally, it continues to join hands with governments and enterprises to participate in international environmental contracts, and promotes the fields of renewable energy, carbon emission reuse and storage (CO2Capture, Utilization, Storage, CCUS) and water resource recycling to assist in the establishment of a green industrial chain and connect with the 2050 national net zero carbon emission target.
Our hospital's net zero transformation
Low-carbon life
Low carbon construction
Low-carbon transportation
Low carbon park
• Promote the "Green Office" environmental protection campaign in 2000
• Gradually improve work from 2008
In order to achieve environmental energy consumption, implement five major energy-saving measures for the replacement of old motors, use of solar water-heating systems in dormitories, and planting roofs to reduce heat energy transmission, office and laboratory lighting improvement, and carbon footprint employee restaurants.
• In 2013, it was certified by the Ministry of Environment Green Restaurant
• In 2010, our hospital's first diamond-grade green building was built. So far, 7 green buildings have been built, and a total of 7 green building marks and 2 smart building mark certifications have been obtained.
• The first regional energy center was built in 2013, which reduced the energy consumption of buildings with a 13-year-old age (10th hall of ZTE campus) by 43% compared with the original one. In 2015, it was awarded the "Diamond Grade Green Building Mark"
• From 2008 to the present, we have promoted low-carbon transportation through energy-saving measures such as promoting the "home sharing platform", setting up official bicycles and energy-saving bus shelters in the U-bike campus, using official electric vehicles in full, and providing electric shuttle services.
• It has been put into industrial wastewater treatment since 1985. In recent years, it has developed from wastewater treatment to wastewater recycling and reuse, and is actually used in industrial wastewater treatment systems and water purification in disaster areas.
• In 2021, the world's first technology can use air to create clean water sources to solve water resources problems

• Since the 1990s, the Institute of Technology and Research has organized a delegation to participate in the "United Nations Convention on Outline of Climate Change" and the "Montello Protocol" as observers of non-governmental organizations (NGOs) to participate in the Conference of the Parties to the "United Nations Convention on Outline of Climate Change" and the "Montello Protocol" through concrete actions to demonstrate our country's emphasis on global environmental issues and its determination to actively participate in international environmental protection conventions.
• In the 1980s, the technology development of renewable energy was invested in the development of wind, solar, geothermal, and biomass power generation related technologies
• In 2022, the "Easy Disassembled Solar Optoelectronic Module" was successfully developed to solve the environmental protection problems derived from retired solar panels.In recent years, technology in renewable energy and hydrogen energy production, storage and application continues to be developed
• In 2011, we began to develop carbon capture technology, invest in solving the disadvantages of excessive carbon dioxide capture costs, and develop carbon sequestration technology
• Promote "temperature co-production" in 2022, using carbon dioxide captured by steel mills to produce related chemicals in the petrochemical industry, creating economic benefits
• It has been put into industrial wastewater treatment since 1985. In recent years, it has developed from wastewater treatment to wastewater recycling and reuse, and is actually used in industrial wastewater treatment systems and water purification in disaster areas.
• In 2021, the world's first technology can use air to create clean water sources to solve water resources problems
