2035 Technical Strategy and Blueprint
Smart life
Healthy and happy
Sustainable environment
Resilient society
Intelligent intelligent technology
Through innovative strategies, under the mission of "using scientific and technological research and development, driving industrial development, creating economic value, and enhancing social welfare", the Institute of Industrial Research has drawn up the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint", using it as a guide for technological research and development, and observed the rapid evolution of international and industrial industries, such as the change in industrial structure and lifestyle of digital technology and economy, the impact of population structure changes on productivity and elderly care, the impact of net zero carbon emissions on society and industries in 2050, and the resilience of industry and social resilience becomes the key to future national and economic development, etc., and the rolling revision of the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" to strengthen the four major application areas of smart life (Smart Living), Quality Health, Sustainable Environment, and Resilient Society (Resilient). Key R&D fields such as Society keep pace with the times, and use Intelligentization Enabling Technology as the application support to strengthen the intelligence of the four major application areas, hoping to continue to use scientific and technological power to drive industrial transformation, improve the overall quality of life of Chinese people, and take into account the sustainable development balance of the environment. Technical strategies and blueprints are the R&D compass of our hospital and guide the R&D direction.In order to establish a long-term mechanism, relevant operational guidelines are formulated, and the scope of 2035 Technical strategies and blueprints and their roles are clarified, laying a solid foundation for our hospital and ensuring that the technical strategies and blueprints can develop more stably.Promote common progress in industries and society through comprehensive strategic planning and action.
2035 Technical Strategy Blueprint 4+1 Domain Architecture
