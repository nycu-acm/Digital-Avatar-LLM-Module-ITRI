Release date 2025-06-19
The Institute of Technology and Research has established the "Sustainable Development Steering Committee", with the president as chairman. With the frequency of meetings held once every six months, the directors' office appointed by the president jointly formulate decisions, implement the vision, strategies and operating policies of the sustainable development of the institute, and supervise the achievement of various projects of sustainable development and the direction of improvement. Two meetings will be held in 2024. To promote and implement the sustainable tasks of our court, the "Organization Sustainable Development Committee" is established. The director of the president's office appointed by the president serves as the chairman. Progress meetings are held regularly every quarter.Four meetings have been held in 2024. Its main responsibilities are to identify the theme of sustainable development, formulate sustainable development goals, supervise cross-team communication and resource integration and coordination, promote action plans and improvement plans, and track the implementation results of sustainable operations. The "Organization and Sustainable Development Committee" has six promotional sub-items, each promotional sub-items are responsible for the implementation of the sustainable affairs promotion of each sub-item of the "Sustainable Strategic Blueprint" of our institute, and has expert consultants for the sustainable industry."Organizing the Sustainable Development Promotion Group"
(Note)
It is mainly responsible for continuously promoting the sustainable development and operation of our hospital and integrating the implementation of the tasks of our hospital's sustainable affairs.In order to enhance the connection between the Steering Committee of Sustainable Development and the Board of Directors, our institute regularly reports on the achievement of sustainable development, annual results of sustainable development goals (SDGs) and external communication information to the Board of Directors to ensure that the sustainable development commitment of the Institute of Technology and Research can be implemented.
Note: The original name of "Organization Sustainable Development Office" was established. In order to demonstrate the characteristics of the unit's cross-departmental cooperation and flexible adjustment of strategies, and to highlight the determination of the Institute of Technology to actively promote sustainable development, the "Organization Sustainable Development Promotion Group" was renamed, and to work with the entire institute and various units to promote the overall sustainable development of the organization.
