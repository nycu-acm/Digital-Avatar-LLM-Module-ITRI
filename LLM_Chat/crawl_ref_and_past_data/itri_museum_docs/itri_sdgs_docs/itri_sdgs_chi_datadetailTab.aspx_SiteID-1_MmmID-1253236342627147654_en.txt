Permanent commitment to protect the future
The 28th UN Climate Conference (COP28), which ended at the end of 2023, gathered representatives, experts and non-governmental organizations from 156 governments to discuss global warming measures. The number of participants hit a record high, indicating that the perpetual sustainability of the earth has become an issue of concern to the world. Taiwan is a part of the earth, and no one is an outsider. Sustainability will be the common goal pursued by the Taiwan government, industry, society and the whole nation.
In 2023, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research proposed the "Sustainable Strategic Blueprint", focusing on six sustainable development aspects: integrity governance, scientific and technological research and development, industrial development, social welfare, happy workplace, and green campus, and comprehensively reviewed the achievements of the Institute of Industrial Research and Research for the first time.In 2023, we will examine the results of all business-oriented projects based on this, and all indicators have achieved significant growth.At the same time, in order to align with the international sustainable evaluation indicators, the "Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures" (TCFD) framework was introduced. Through the four core elements of "governance", "strategy", "risk management" and "indicators and goals", the impact of climate change on the operations, strategies and financial planning of our institute is evaluated to provide reference and measurement by interested parties, and implement the management mechanism and disclosure status of climate change-related risks, so as to respond to the needs of interested parties from all aspects, and then move towards sustainable organizational development.This report is not just a document, but also our commitment to the future and our responsibility to the next generation.
Faced with the treacherous international situation, mastering independent innovation technology is the task of the Industrial Research Institute to assist the industry in sustaining its industry.Insight into the global international situation and changes in overall economic trends, outline future market demand, integrate cross-field R&D energy, draw the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint", focus on four major application areas such as smart life, healthy and happy life, sustainable environment, and resilient society, and use intelligent enabling technology to support the development of the application field, work with the industry to plan the future, and jointly promote the 2050 net zero sustainable development goal.
Moving towards the road of sustainability, the Institute of Technology has built a green campus, towards low-carbon development, and established a "carbon reduction fund", linking carbon reduction investment and unit performance, and putting it into use as related low-carbon equipment, so as to encourage departments to independently reduce carbon and form a positive cycle; build renewable energy power generation equipment in the campus to supply some electricity consumption in the campus, converting from power users to Prosumer (producer consumers), changing the traditional power supply concept that the power supplier meets the needs of the electricity users, and contributing to Taiwan's energy transformation.Net zero emissions are the largest carbon reduction project in human history. However, "a journey of a thousand miles begins with a single step". On the road of sustainable development, every effort and change is a big step towards a sustainable future. From now on, let us work together to overcome the challenges of climate change and jointly protect the bright future of the next generation.
