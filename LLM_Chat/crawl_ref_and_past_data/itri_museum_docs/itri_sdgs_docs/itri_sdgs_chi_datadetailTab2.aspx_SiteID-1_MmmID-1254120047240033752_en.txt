Social Investment Remuneration SROI Analysis｜Net Zero Sustainable School
Release date 2024-07-16
The Institute of Industrial Research and Research has driven industrial development through scientific and technological research and development. At the same time, it has been committed to assisting the high-tech industry in cultivating key strategic talents, actively linking global industrial development trends, and introducing international advanced academic processes. It has successively won the most representative awards in my country's human resources field "2022 National Talent Development Award - Organization (Institution) Group Award" and "2023 TTQS Training Institution Edition - Gold Medal". It has been highly recognized in talent development and training quality, and is a good partner for the cultivation of talents in the government and industry.
Promote strategy
This court has long assisted the government in promoting key talent measures to cope with the talent dilemma of digital and sustainable transformation in the industry. In 2023, the courses will be offered mainly in six major fields and cultivate four categories: professional technology (UPSKill), science and technology management (TM), advanced talents (CXO) and career development (Reskill).A total of 4,237 enterprises were served and 13,663 industrial specialists were trained. Among them, the advanced class focuses on the operational needs of the high-tech industry.Through the establishment of the CXO club membership system, it promotes regular exchanges among senior corporate supervisors, and also provides customized training on important issues of the company or industry.It has 106 annual corporate project services, covering Cathay Shihua, Delta Telecom, Hon Hai, Zhonghua Telecom, etc.

ATD Global Talent Innovation Award
The Institute of Industrial Research Institute has achieved outstanding results in 2023 and was awarded the Talent Development Innovation Award from the American Talent Development Association (ATD).Our college uses the results of the Grid School and Net Zero Sustainable School, and uses digital innovative approaches to integrate virtual and real, and jointly promote talent cultivation strategies with the talent alliance. Its innovative actions and results have been recognized internationally.
To cope with the net zero emission challenges of the circuit board industry, the Industrial Research Institute and the Taiwan Circuit Board Association jointly established the "PCB Industry Net Zero Sustainable Professional Talent Certification Class".The first course will be held from October to November 2023, attracting more than 30 professionals to participate. In addition, a "Net Zero Sustainable Talent Alliance" has been formed to expand and deepen talent training and promote the transformation and upgrading of the circuit board industry.In addition, the Industrial Research Institute and the Bicycle Sustainable Alliance have jointly opened the world's only carbon reduction technology course designed for the bicycle industry, teaching key decisions and manufacturing supply of carbon reduction technologies.
PCB industry net zero sustainable professional promotion talent certification class and ATD talent development innovation award
Social Investment Remuneration SROI Analysis｜Net Zero Sustainable School
In order to assist the industry in promoting net zero transformation, our institute cooperated with eight major industrial associations to establish the "Net Zero Sustainable Talent Alliance" to solve the challenges of industrial talent cultivation.At the same time, the "Net Zero Sustainable School" was established to systematically plan and offer more than 50 professional courses, including five core technical fields such as sustainable carbon management, energy systems, low-carbon introduction, recycling and negative carbon value-added, integrate net zero leaders in the industry, academia and research circles, integrate the most advanced net zero-related scientific and technological knowledge of our college, cultivate Taiwan's green professional talents, and enhance my country's overall competitiveness in the green industry.
In 2023, our college introduced the calculation method of Social Return on Investment (SROI) to conduct data analysis on the achievements of important stakeholders of Net Zero Sustainable Schools, monetize social value and influence, so as to visualize the intangible value of talent cultivation and continuously improve the results of curriculum design and training. For every NTD 1 invested by a net zero perpetual school in 2023, it can generate a social value of NTD 7.45.
