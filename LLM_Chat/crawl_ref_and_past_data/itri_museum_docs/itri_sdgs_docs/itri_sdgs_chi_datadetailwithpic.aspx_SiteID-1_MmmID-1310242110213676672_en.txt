The Institute of Industrial Research and Research actively responds to climate change mitigation and adjustment, sets a net zero emission target for the whole hospital in 2050, promotes short, medium and long-term action plan, and follows the Task Force on Climate-Related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) and nature-related financial disclosures (TCFD).
