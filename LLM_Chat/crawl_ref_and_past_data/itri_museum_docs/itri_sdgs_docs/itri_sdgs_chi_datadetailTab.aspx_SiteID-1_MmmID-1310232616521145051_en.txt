Firm belief and move forward forever
At the 2024 COP29 meeting, the article 6 of the Paris Agreement was passed, which improved the global carbon market mechanism and injected new momentum into achieving net zero emissions, which also demonstrated the urgency of climate action. Faced with the test of international political and economic situation and climate change, the Institute of Technology adheres to the vision of scientific and technological innovation and achieves sustainable development, continues to promote social progress and practice its own sustainable mission.
The Institute of Technology is an international R&D institution, demonstrating its sustainable commitment with practical actions and has independently issued sustainable reports for three consecutive years.In order to improve the quality of the information disclosed, in addition to adopting the dual major principles of the EU Permanent Reporting Standard (ESRS), it continues to pass third-party institutions to verify it;establish a sustainable website for organizations to provide transparent and convenient information review services for stakeholders and promote cooperation. All actions have also been recognized by the outside world, and won four gold awards from the World Magazine and Taiwan Enterprise Permanent Award in 2024.
Integrity governance is the cornerstone of the sustainable operation of the Institute of Industrial Research.We uphold the cultural DNA of "innovative thinking, open communication, and agile management" and deeply cultivate the culture of organizational integrity.Internally, by introducing the ISO 20400 sustainable procurement guide, we will strengthen supply chain management and use Gen AI technology to improve process efficiency, and significantly improve operational efficiency for a variety of businesses covering technology research and development, marketing promotion, accounting audits and other businesses; externally, we will continue to organize discussions with 653 domestic public associations as a sustainable leader, and connect with 29 countries and 211 international partners to carry out diversified and two-way cooperation to create sustainable influence.
In addition to deepening the technical blueprint, our institute is also actively developing the application field of "sustainable environment" to strengthen resilience through research and development and implement energy conservation and carbon reduction in the hospital.In 2024, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research will follow the climate-related financial disclosure (TCFD) and nature-related financial disclosure (TNFD) architecture to inventory and identify the environmental impacts caused by operating bases and behaviors, and integrate internal resources to promote ISO 50001 energy management, carbon inspection, renewable energy application, waste sewage management and renewable water application.Driven by years of construction, 2024 will save 12.48 million kWh of electricity (5,919.50 metric tons of CO) compared with the benchmark year (2020) in 2024 (5,919.50 metric tons of CO)

"People-oriented, giving back to the society" is an important core concept of our institute. The Institute of Technology and Research actively creates a human-centered working environment and promotes lifelong learning and physical and mental health support for employees.At the same time, deepen the forward-looking and cross-field integration of technology, assist Taiwan’s industrial upgrading and transformation, and solve social problems through innovative technology. In 2024, the number of industrial services reached 19,620 (7.4% year-on-year), of which approximately 60% of small and medium-sized enterprises’ services accounted for; 19,959 industrial training specialists were assisted.Eight public welfare science and technology application achievements were completed, and science and technology education and volunteer activities were promoted using the two major public welfare platforms of central and southern China and Huadong to shorten the urban-rural gap. They connected 56 organizations to work together, becoming the core force in promoting social progress.
Looking to the future, no one can stay out of the road to sustainability. The Institute of Technology will pay attention to and echo international sustainability initiatives and trends, continue to work with partners in the sustainable value chain, firmly believe in moving forward steadily, and create a better future together.
