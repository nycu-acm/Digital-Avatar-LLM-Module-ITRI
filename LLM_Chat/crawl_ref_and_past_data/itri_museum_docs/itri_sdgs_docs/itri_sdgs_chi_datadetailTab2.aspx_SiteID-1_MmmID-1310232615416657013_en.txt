The external audit coverage rate has reached 100% in the past three years, ensuring the effective implementation of occupational safety and health management. The Occupational Safety and Health Management Structure and Systems Institute of Technology is centered on the "Integrated Management System Committee" and is composed of integrated management representatives, professional committee members and employee representatives of each unit. Meetings are held once a quarter to review, consult and communicate about workplace safety and health, environmental protection, fire protection, radiation protection and biosafety management. The "Environmental Safety and Health Working Group" is set up under the "Integrated Management System Committee", which is composed of environmental safety and health supervisors and employee representatives of each unit. Responsible for the development and planning of management plans. The relevant topics are submitted to the "Integrated Management System Committee" for review and approval and are implemented in the hospital. Through the comprehensive management structure and employee representative consultation, participation and communication, we ensure that the entire hospital meets the requirements of occupational safety and health management. In order to improve employees' safety and health awareness and ability, and to achieve the goal of "safety and health for all employees", various safety and health training, publicity, selection and selection will be planned and promoted in 2024 to improve employees' attention and practice of safety and health, and to establish a high-quality occupational safety and health culture. The specific roles include: implementing the compulsory safety and health training of all types of employees, Professional safety and health certificate training, holding safety and environmental example case selection, safety and health publicity poster selection, safety and health awareness theme publicity, shooting emergency response and fire prevention publicity micro-films, etc. All staff participate in the integrated management activities, and formulate "Safety and Health Education and Training Operation Instructions" and "Occupational Safety and Health Training Plan". The training categories cover three categories: general homework, special homework and legal certificates. The number of trainees completed in 2024 will reach 13,453, and 9 publicity posters will be produced to enhance the safety awareness of all employees. Each unit formulates automatic inspection plans every year and performs independent inspections to complete ISO 45001 Unit internal audit, cross-audit and management system verification audit. In 2024, the whole hospital special inspection was carried out on the "Public Hazardous Materials and Combustible Gas Operation Management". The coverage rate of independent inspections and high-risk places special inspections reached 100%, and 33 matters to be improved were all improved. Each hospital area received 25 external audits of the competent authorities such as occupational safety, environmental protection and fire protection, and the 4 improvements listed were also completed as scheduled. The Institute of Accidents and Occupational Injury Industrial Research formulated the "Environmental Safety and Sanitation Accident/Incident Notification Investigation and Tracking Operation Procedures" to ensure that the incident stories can be reported, investigated and improved immediately, and the incident stories and tracked improvements of each unit every month, including risk re-evaluation and root cause analysis. After the general accident is closed, it will be held at the Environmental Safety and Sanitation Working Group meeting. A special project report was conducted, and major accidents were reported by the Integrated Management System Committee. In 2024, a total of 28 incidents and stories occurred, including 11 falls, 4 cuts, 2 roll injuries, 2 harmful objects, 1 object falling and smashing, 1 impact injury and 1 food poisoning, and 20 false alarm events. In response to the most common fall accidents, the cause analysis has been completed and prevention publicity has been strengthened, and a comprehensive investigation and improvement has been carried out for falls caused by environmental facilities. In order to strengthen the safety management of contractors, contractors have formulated "Guidelines for safety and health management of personnel outside the hospital to work in this hospital" and "Contractor Safety and Health Code" and other methods. It is required that contractors who work in designated places in this hospital sign the "Contractor Safety and Health Commitment Letter" and must complete the "Contractor Safety and Health Meeting" before operation.
