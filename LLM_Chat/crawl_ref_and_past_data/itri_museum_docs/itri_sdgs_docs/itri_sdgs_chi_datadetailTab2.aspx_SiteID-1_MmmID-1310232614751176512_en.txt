Organizational governance structure
The Board of Directors is the highest governance unit of the Institute of Technology Research. In accordance with relevant laws and regulations and the powers granted by the competent authority, it is responsible for the resolutions and supervision of important matters in the institute; in order to improve the supervision function and strengthen the management function, it is set up the Supervisory Board in accordance with the establishment regulations and donation charter of this court, which is responsible for the verification of the annual final accounts of this court and the supervision of important affairs of the court; consider the direction of organizational operations and laws and policies,
The board of directors has three functional committees, including a compensation committee, an integrity management committee and an audit committee to ensure the improvement of the effectiveness and quality of organizational governance.
. In addition, in accordance with Article 8 of the Establishment Regulations of this Court, in order to implement and improve governance, the President of the Executive Yuan will select one president and one to two vice presidents, adhere to the resolutions and principles of the board of directors, and comprehensively manage the affairs of the court.
A total of 6 meetings of directors and supervisors were held in 2024 to supervise key major events, including proposals such as integrity and legal compliance of the whole court, environmental improvement, energy-saving measures, human compensation, innovative investment and sustainable reports.

Board of Directors and Supervisors
A sound, diverse, professional and efficient board of directors and supervisory boards are the basis for organizational integrity governance.The Board of Directors of this court is responsible for formulating the organization and reporting to the competent authority for approval, formulating work guidelines, reviewing budget and final accounts and other important decisions, and supervising the important business of this court;the Supervisory Board is responsible for the verification of the annual final accounts of this court and the supervision of important affairs of this court.
Members of the Board of Directors and Supervisors are selected by the President of the Executive Yuan. The term of office is three years.They must be renewed once after the expiration of the term. The number of renewals shall not exceed one-half of the total number of original members. Their employment is based on long-term business strategies and background diversity, and is served by industry experts and scholars in various fields such as information and communications, business management, semiconductors, and finance.Regarding this term of directors and supervisors,

In addition to holding joint meetings of directors and supervisors regularly every quarter, if there are major issues or emergency affairs, the hospital may also hold an extraordinary meeting at any time.
