Research and development will continue to work together
Promote Taiwan's industrial upgrading and transformation and cultivate new businesses
Release date: 2024-04-18
Adhering to the original intention of founding, the Institute of Technology and Research continues to play a key role in promoting Taiwan's industrial upgrading and transformation, and takes cultivating new ventures as one of the key strategies. Its strategies cover four major aspects: cultural shaping, tutoring and cultivation, investment attraction and post-investment management. Combining professional technology and intellectual financial resources, the Industrial Research Institute provides abundant support to the new team and helps them grow.Most of the new startups cultivated by the Industrial Research Institute are high-tech industries and have market uniqueness, such as TSMC, Lianhua Electronics and other companies.So far, 165 technology-oriented startups have been successfully cultivated.Looking ahead, the Institute of Technology will continue to deepen its cultivation of new innovation culture, integrate guidance resources, strengthen the competitiveness of new innovation teams, and promote the sustainable development of the industry.
Highlight Project-Wanyi Energy
Taiwan’s strategic development policies and energy transformation goals have a high demand for energy storage systems and related technologies, further promoting the demand for lithium batteries.The Institute of Technology has been committed to the research and development of lithium battery negative electrode materials technology for many years, with important achievements and technical energy, and has decided to start with fast-charging lithium battery negative electrode materials to carry out industrialization and technological improvement.In 2023, the Industrial Research Institute and Dariguang cooperated to establish "Wanyi Energy Company" to develop titanium lithium niobate battery negative electrode material products suitable for fast charging buses, energy storage systems, hybrid vehicles and other markets.This product has the characteristics of fast charging, high safety and long life, bringing new business opportunities to enterprises and enhancing Taiwan's competitiveness in the global lithium battery industry.
Business Opportunities & Market Assessment
Talent & Technology Introduction
Industry & Funding Media
With the increase in demand for lithium batteries, the global lithium battery market output value is estimated to reach US$450 billion in 2030. Advanced manufacturers and research institutions are committed to developing innovative battery materials, which will make the future of lithium batteries extremely capable of growing potential and invest in this emerging market as soon as possible, cooperate with domestic battery supply chain manufacturers, and seize the market opportunities.
The Institute of Technology will have rich experience in the research and development, production and manufacturing of nano materials and lithium battery materials, battery design and process verification of industrial transfer, assist in technological development, and continuously improve material efficiency.
Energy storage industry and electric buses are the main application areas of battery cell factories that Wanyi Energy initially supply.In the future, in addition to establishing a trial production line for titanium niobate materials, it will continue to carry out the research and development of new generation energy storage materials with the Industrial Research Institute.
The Industrial Technology Department of the Ministry of Economics has invested 1 billion yuan in scientific research funds in the past five years to support the research and development of fast charging batteries, solid-state batteries, green and low-carbon processes, etc., and establish Taiwan's competitive advantage.Attracted Daliguang to invest 450 million yuan across the border, seize the initiative in the global electric vehicle and energy storage market, and accelerate the energy transformation process.Cooperation with domestic and foreign battery material factories, the long-term goal is to become a professional energy material company.
