In addition to actively participating in international organizations, we can instantly grasp international innovation policies and scientific and technological development directions, and promote my country's scientific research technology and industrialization strength to the global market, building a scientific and technological cooperation platform for key countries or regions around the world; and take industrial demand as a niche to promote cooperation with international industry research institutions that complement each other's technological advantages, lay out scientific and technological business opportunities in emerging industries, and continuously inject innovation momentum into the industry. Our institute's international cooperation is laid out through six major strategic tasks such as technology introduction, technical cooperation, industrial innovation, market development, brand flag insertion and talent recruitment, and at the same time, it leverages international energy and resources, and cooperates with strategic partners to create a win-win situation, creating a sustainable future together. Six major strategic tasks layout technology introduction technology cooperation industry innovation market development brand flag insertion talent recruitment targets forward-looking technology, and conducts resources (talents) Link, Benchmark International Advanced RTO, master the key technologies that focus on emerging fields of various needs of our institute, focus on the international ecosystem network industry, academia and research (international organizations) to build and focus on the integrated application technology of emerging industries, assist in improving domestic industrial innovation capabilities and competitiveness, focus on the opportunities of technology outlets and overseas tempering, grasp market demand to enhance the international influence of the brand, strengthen local customer links and international strongholds, local networks, and recruit outstanding international talents in two-way recruitment and training highlights: breakthroughs in the implementation of industrial technology research and development and the leap forward in global popularity in 2024. In 2024, the number of international partners of our institute reached 211. Under the main axis of technology introduction, technical cooperation, industrial innovation, and market development, the key semiconductor technology will be successfully integrated in 2024 (12” Low-temperature hybrid bonding technology for wafer bonding process) was launched in Taiwan, and the long-distance smart medical solutions developed by the Institute of Technology will be brought overseas to achieve the goal of cross-border medical treatment without borders. Whether it is the introduction of foreign technology or domestic technology output, it has a brilliant achievement. In addition, it has strengthened the strategic thinking of brand flag insertion and talent recruitment. In particular, the High Level Forum summit was moved to Taiwan for the first time, and was hosted by the Institute of Technology, and has become the focus of various countries. The forum attracted a total of 20 countries at home and abroad to participate in this event, including: France, the United States, Germany, the United Kingdom, Finland, Japan, the Netherlands, Spain, Canada and other countries. The number of experts attending the conference exceeded 100. People, discuss the themes of how ecological systems in various countries build resilient supply chains and social benefits of innovative social research. Six major strategies and tasks Important achievements of technology introduction, technology cooperation, industry innovation market development, brand, talent recruitment of talents has been successfully attracted three international benchmark companies to set up R&D centers at the Institute of Technology Research and Development and launched more than ten years of cooperation plans, completing 12" wafer bonding process development and conducting trial verification, assisting semiconductor low-temperature hybrid bonding technology to introduce into Taiwan, and promoting technology implementation. Join hands with Japan Tokuyama and domestic Tsukuba Technology to establish a laboratory to introduce Tokuyama powder process equipment, combine independent powder detection platform, connect the industrial chain to accelerate the implementation of technology, strengthen domestic compound semiconductor materials independent development capabilities, and promote innovative applications. The Institute of Technology Research and Development cooperates with the Netherlands' Taiwan offices to connect with the Taiwan Optoelectronics and Compound Semiconductor Industry Association TOSIA to connect with the Netherlands, European organizations and associations to promote Taiwan Factory Trade Union and the Netherlands Phix Expand cooperation in the heterogeneous integrated packaging technology of photonic chips in the Next World, build a bridge for domestic industries and internationally, and seize the opportunity of silicon photonics. Our institute has developed an iMAS smart medical information platform, integrates portable testing equipment, and realizes the Homespital medical model. It has transformed into a subsidiary of Hon Hai and works with Thailand's largest telecommunications company True Digital and system integration operator TKC to promote the development of multinational long-distance medical care. The 2024 Global Innovation Summit High Level Forum Summit (HLF) is hosted by the Institute of Technology and Research in Taiwan and co-organized by the French CEA. The theme is "Innovation Ecosystems for Resilient Society", bringing together the elites of the innovation ecosystem of 20 countries around the world, promoting cross-border cooperation, and demonstrating Taiwan's scientific and technological strength. The results of the event are shown in HLF Official website, to give full play to the benefits of marketing in Taiwan. Link foreign bases, strengthen diverse and two-way international talent cooperation, and participate in key industry-university-research activities and talent recruitment. In addition to conducting internship cooperation with many overseas universities to attract outstanding students to intern in the college, the elites of our college are also selected overseas for 3-12 months of training and cultivation, actively shaping the international workplace with the advantages of distance and elasticity.
