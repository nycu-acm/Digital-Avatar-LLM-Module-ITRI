General waste and business waste treatment process General waste This institute's general waste area is mainly entrusted to the Hsinchu Municipal Environmental Protection Bureau or other counties and cities for incineration treatment. Since the Hsinchu County incineration plant is still under construction, when the incineration plant is undergoing equipment maintenance or shutdown, waste treatment may face delays. Therefore, temporary storage measures need to be properly managed to reduce odor and environmental impact. This institute continues to optimize waste management strategies and give priority to treatment plants with nearer transportation distances to reduce carbon emissions. At the same time, it is committed to improving resource recovery rates and reducing general waste volume. Through cooperation with environmental protection technology manufacturers, we will increase the recycled items and resource reuse mechanisms, reduce environmental impact, and practice environmental sustainability goals. Resource Recycling and Reuse Our Institute promotes the expansion of recycling items and improving circulation efficiency. To recycle and reuse resources, reduce the impact of waste on the environment. Promote the recycling of toilet paper and furniture circulation standards, build a recycling market platform, promote resource sharing, and handle second-hand clothing fundraising activities to promote resource reuse.
