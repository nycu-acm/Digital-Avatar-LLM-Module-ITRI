In 2024, a number of emerging technology courses will be launched. Taking the focus on healthy life, future energy, resilient environment and local care as the face, cultivate key talents with sustainable thinking and technical professions, and promote enterprises to create new opportunities. The Institute of Industrial Research responds to industrial needs immediately and starts industrial talent training in 2024. The theme focuses on six major fields: smart information communication, net zero sustainability, smart grid and energy, biotechnology and medicine, smart machinery, and technology management. The training targets cover: professional and technical engineers, middle and senior managers, career development, industrial supply chain partners, through diverse training models such as physical courses, digital self-study, mixed learning, etc., a total of 6,552 services will be provided throughout the year. 19,959 industry specialists were trained. Among them, due to net zero emissions and digital transformation, the industry's demand for cross-field talents has driven up. At the same time, supply chain partner management is the key to enterprises achieving net zero goals and stable operations. Therefore, it is more focused on cross-field talent development and supplier collaboration training to provide customized training for enterprises. There are 110 annual enterprise training projects, serving enterprises including: TSMC, Qunguang Electronics, Huacheng Motors, Sanyang Industry, etc., providing enterprise training solutions. With the large leading to small-industry chain learning model, the Industrial Research Institute promotes sustainable transformation and technological upgrades of enterprises through the large leading to small industrial chain learning model, assisting customers in achieving net zero emissions and improving market competitiveness. In combination with the technical energy and professional tutoring experience of the Industrial Research Institute, courses are tailored to meet the needs of different industries, and promoting the joint learning and growth of upstream and downstream partners. Sanyang Group-Supplier Energy Storage Theme Training will assist Sanyang Industry 50 since 2022 All supply chain manufacturers above are conducting net zero perpetual training. Combining the technology and guidance energy of our institute, the course covers the themes of corporate carbon management / carbon inspection, product carbon footprint, carbon rights, energy efficiency management / energy saving measures, green electricity, energy storage and other topics. The demand for green-leaders has tripled in the past eight years. Talent cultivation is a key force in achieving net zero transformation. We will promote related work through diversified strategies and lay the foundation for the industry to move towards sustainable development. A total of 7,159 people were trained in 2024. (1) Establish a national sustainable appraisal system. The Institute of Technology and Research assists the Ministry of Economic Affairs to establish a national sustainable appraisal examination, promoting the standardization of net zero skills through a two-pronged approach of "training" and "checking". Starting from the public version of the textbook, the number of views of the textbook has exceeded 200,000 since it was launched, and more than 750 seed teachers have been successfully cultivated to stabilize the national training base. The iPAS "Net Zero Carbon Planning Manager" capability appraisal was held for the first time in 2024, attracting nearly 1.8 Ten thousand people apply for the exam, of which more than eight have become current workers. This certificate has been recognized by more than 1,000 companies, and showing that the popularity of net zero skills has become the top priority of industrial talents. (2) Cultivate the green strength of corporate talents. In order to deepen the skills of industrial specialists, the Institute of Technology and the Public Research Institute jointly established a net zero perpetual talent alliance to explore industrial needs and open a net zero perpetual school course, including an international workshop for sustainable senior executives (CSOs), inviting domestic and foreign experts to analyze the practice of international climate agreements in Taiwan and the application strategies of net zero technology. For small and medium-sized enterprises, technical solution courses are designed, such as opening a "Green Machinery Research and Development Engineer Training Course" for the machine tool industry combined with the smart manufacturing field; providing a "Taiwan Building Energy Efficiency Evaluation Practical Promotion Class" for the construction and construction industry, introducing a "Building Energy Consumption Simulation and Analysis" platform to strengthen practical applications. The "Green Collar Carbon Reduction Promotion Talent Development Course" cultivates carbon reduction technology majors from the basic courses, and can be invested in the field of sustainable management after training. In addition, the Institute of Technology and Research Institute cooperates with Qunguang Group, such as custom planning training plans for Qunguang Group to assist employees in participating. iPAS "Net Zero Carbon Planning Manager" capability assessment combines theory and practice to comprehensively improve professional qualities and practical capabilities. Our institute is firmly committed to the cultivation of green-leader talents. In the future, we will continue to deepen the training of professional and sustainable development of professional technology, actively expand the scope of influence, and provide core support for industrial transformation. Customer affirmation + Carbon management, energy efficiency management and energy theme training tailored for Sanyang's suppliers, invites many experienced expert lecturers to deeply analyze the practical challenges that enterprises often encounter in promoting carbon reduction, and share successful response measures and practical case solutions, providing valuable practical guidance and inspiration to enterprises, which is of great help to the implementation of carbon reduction work. Student feedback + "Green Collar Carbon Reduction Promotion Talent Development Class" is quite fulfilling.
