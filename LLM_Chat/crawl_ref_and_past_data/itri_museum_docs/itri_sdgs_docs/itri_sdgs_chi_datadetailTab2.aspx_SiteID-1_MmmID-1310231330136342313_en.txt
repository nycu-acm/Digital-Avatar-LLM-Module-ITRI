Environment (E) Social (S) Economy (E) Saves process consumption costs and accurately controls steel production time to reduce 1% energy consumption reaches the experience inheritance of the operator, reduces the occurrence of industrial safety accidents and reduces unnecessary energy losses, accurately controls the steel production process, and improves the quality of steelmaking solutions. Work with China Steel to establish a digital clone of the steel production process. Combined with AI technology and dynamic simulation of the steel fluid through 6 million particles, plus prediction and identification technology and steel flow pattern estimation model to obtain simulation information as a control reference. The process simulation results correspond to China Steel’s actual data accuracy of more than 90%. This system has won the "2024 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards (R&D100 Awards)". Technology Application + Steel Flow Particle Simulation: Dynamic Simulation of Steel Flow through a large number of particles to reproduce the physical characteristics of the steel flow. + Five AI models, real-time analysis and feedback process patterns: • Fluid Physical Simulation Model: Simulates the physical patterns and behaviors of the steel flow. • Steel stock estimation model: accurately measure and estimate the remaining steel volume in the converter. • Steel flow information identification model: analyze key information such as the width of the steel flow and the slag drop. • Steel flow point prediction model: instantly predict the position of the steel flow point to improve accuracy. • Converter wear experience model: dynamically adjust the converter wall thickness parameters based on the furnace body wear information. Results display + Work with Sinosteel to establish a digital clone for the steel production process and won the "2024 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards (R&D100 Awards)". + This project is the first case of digital twin system being used in the steel industry development, and is combined with visual virtual AI Model calculation, physics engine simulation and million-particle technology accurately restore the actual steel production situation and further estimate key information that cannot be measured directly in the real world, thereby establishing a reliable experience curve, laying a solid foundation for the realization of intelligent and automated steel production. Using the "Nine Grid of Science and Technology Baseball", "Striker Training Analysis" and "Training Load Algorithm" combined with AI algorithms to conduct personalized data monitoring and training planning for players. It also uses technology to import the Red Leaf Primary School Baseball Team to verify and improve its training results, and gradually advance to professional baseball fields and sports fields to form an industrial application model to effectively meet the needs of baseball training at different levels. Technology application + Image data conversion technology: Deep learning is used to identify the sphere trajectory and player joint position, and capture the sphere and player field movement data. + 3D Nine Grid System: Provide the pitching position through computer vision technology, the coordinate error is within 2 cm, and the ball speed error is only plus or minus 2.9 km/h. + Data visual analysis application: Through data integration and visualization, help the team data analysis efficiency be improved by more than 2 Time, predict pitching behaviors in different situations, assist coaches in improving training effects. Results display + Training system strengthening: Establish a new foundation for baseball data analysis, assist coaches in improving guidance efficiency, and achieve scientific training of players. + Improve data analysis quality: Ensure data consistency and accuracy, and ensure analysis quality, it has been actually applied to the 2024 China Professional Baseball Championship and the 12-World China-Czech Exchange Competition. + Talent cultivation and community impact: Through the introduction of the science and technology baseball system, the Hongye Primary School Baseball Team won the Taitung County Primary School Baseball League Championship in 2024, improving local baseball strength.-- Section --- The converter steel production process digital twin system combines AI and digital twin technology, optimizes the steel production process through virtual simulation, improves quality, saves energy and reduces consumption, and realizes digital transformation.
