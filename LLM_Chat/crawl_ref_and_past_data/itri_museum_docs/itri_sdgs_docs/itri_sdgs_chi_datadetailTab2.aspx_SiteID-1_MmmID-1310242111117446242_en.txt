Water Resources Use
The main water use of this hospital includes domestic water, process water, and plant irrigation. Domestic water accounts for 68.93%. The source of water for water withdrawal is Taiwan Water Company. According to the World Resources Research Institute's "Water Resource Risk Assessment Tool" (WRI Aqueduct), the water resource pressure in the area where this hospital is located is "Low - Medium (1-2)" and the water shortage is "Low (0-1)". The main operating bases are not located in water resource pressure areas. The water intake has not had a significant impact on the local water sources; the flood risk of rivers is "Low - Medium Risk (1-2)", which does not currently affect the operation of each campus.In addition, our hospital continues to use "smart water network management and AI-assisted leak measurement and positioning technology" to accurately control the water leakage rate to reduce to 5%, improve the water utilization rate and flexibly dispatch water resources.

Waste (sewage) water treatment
In response to the improvement of global environmental awareness, our hospital continues to strengthen waste (sewage) water management and revise the "Waste (sewage) water discharge management procedure" in 2024 to clarify the rights and responsibilities of each unit and enhance employees' environmental awareness.At the same time, it is standardized that the experimental wastewater must meet the entry limit, avoid high concentration of waste liquid impacting the treatment system, and reduce environmental risks. Each campus has water quality monitoring equipment to instantly grasp water quality data and adjust management strategies to ensure that all water discharged complies with laws and regulations.No exceeding the standard occurred in 2024, demonstrating our court’s effective control and sustainable commitment.

Recycling and reuse of sewage in the dormitory of Zhongxing Campus
Make good use of every drop of water resources and strive to improve water use efficiency.Our court has established a special task force for source diversion and recycling and reuse, and plans short, medium and long-term waste (sewage) water reduction goals in ZTE District, promotes waste (sewage) water emission reduction planning and implementation projects. At the same time, it introduces digital management technology for waste (sewage) water emissions, applies recycled water to system cooling water and other needs, and implements water resource recycling.
