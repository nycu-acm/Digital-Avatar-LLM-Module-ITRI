AI Sign Language Virtual Anchor Service AI Sign Language Virtual Anchor Service uses generative AI and large language models to automatically convert spoken words into smooth and natural sign language action images, breaking through the bottleneck of sign language translation inadequate power. Technology has been applied in fields such as meteorological information and disaster warning to improve the accessibility of information for hearing impaired people, promote the inheritance of information equality and sign language culture. Social (S) Governance (G) develops "inclusive technology", promotes information equality, inherits Taiwan sign language cultural assets to use AI digital services, alleviate the problem of insufficient human resources for sign language translation, assists enterprises to strengthen care for hearing impaired people, shape a socially responsible brand image, and win customer affirmation solutions AI The purpose of sign language meteorological anchors is to automatically convert spoken language into sign language actions to achieve seamless language communication. First, the system translates spoken language into sign language through a large language model. Then, the model estimates the skeleton movements of human hands and body, ensuring the smoothness and accuracy of sign language movements, and presents natural and grammatical sign language expressions. Finally, the generative AI technology is used to convert the skeleton movements into sign language images of virtual humanoids, so that hearing impaired people can instantly see vivid sign language demonstrations, greatly improving the convenience of meteorological information transmission. Technology application + Meteorological broadcasting: AI sign language virtual anchors can be used in meteorological channels to provide instant weather information. Hearing impaired people can understand daily weather changes through sign language. + Emergency disaster warning: AI sign language virtual anchors provide instant disaster information and instructions, allowing hearing impaired people to instantly obtain dangerous situations and countermeasures to ensure their safety.+ The delivery of public service information: Government agencies can use AI virtual sign language anchors to transmit public service information such as social welfare policies, health and safety guidance, and ensure equality of information for hearing-impaired people.
