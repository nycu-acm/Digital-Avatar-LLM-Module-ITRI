Major topic identification process
The Institute of Technology and Research will refer to the major theme decision process and reporting principles of GRI 2021 edition. Based on the organizational operation of this institute, the following major identification process will be constructed as a reference for promoting sustainable issues and resource allocation.The formulation and implementation results of major theme-related goals shall be submitted to the Sustainable Development Steering Committee regularly by the Organization's Office of Sustainable Development, and the Dean is authorized by the Board of Directors to review and improve relevant promotion directions and practices to improve the effectiveness of the organization's sustainable development.

Major theme management
According to the identification process of major themes of the Institute of Industrial Research, our institute has the following six major themes in 2023.
(Note)
:
Technological innovation and research and development
Technology application and promotion
Customer Relations (Customized Major Topics)
Partnerships
Information security management
Talent cultivation and development (customized major themes)
After analyzing these six major themes, our hospital will explain the management results in "Importance and Impact", "Policies/Commitments and Action Notes", "2023 Goals and Important Achievements", "2024 Goals", "Value Chain Impact" and "Complied Chapters" explaining the detailed contents of each major theme, and present them in our hospital's 2023 Perpetual Report.
