Polyester Fabric Decolorization Technology Polyester Fabric Decolorization Technology promotes the reuse of fiber products, achieves the circular economy goal of textiles, and reduces carbon emissions by 45%. This technology can regenerate new fibers after decolorization of waste textiles, improves textile recycling and reuse rate, and has won the Edison Awards silver medal, known as the "Oscar of the Innovation World". In the future, green textile technology will continue to be promoted to achieve the circular economy and sustainable development goals. Environmental (E) Social (S) Governance (G) 45% reduction compared with petrochemical-derived polyester Carbon emissions solve the problem of textile waste, turn waste into treasure, realize the application of sustainable resource recycling and construction of textile recycling system, and was selected as 2023 The 100 Global Science and Technology R&D Award Solution Through the deep decolorization technology of polyester fabrics, high-quality colorless recycling polyester raw materials can be obtained after removing dyes from polyester fabrics, and then remade into recycled polyester fibers to realize fiber to fiber (fiber to fiber) circulation, and construct innovative closed zero-waste recycling system for textiles to reduce resource consumption and carbon emissions caused by textile production. Technology Application◆ Deeply remove dyes for dyeing polyester fabrics, high-quality colorless polyester raw materials can be recycled, solving the disadvantages of dark appearance and deterioration of the recycled products due to dye residues, and improving the value of textile waste recycling◆ Use environmentally friendly water-based decolorization accelerator to decolorize the fabric, which has the characteristics of high decolorization rate and low physical loss, which is conducive to the display of high-value application results of polyester recycling◆ This hospital has carried out advanced technical cooperation with domestic index textile operators (New Synthetic Fiber, Tainan Textile) to establish corresponding regeneration technology for textile recycling and reproduction production system, which is conducive to the subsequent realization of zero-waste recycling and regeneration of textiles◆ Re-made textiles using textiles as recycling materials is a future trend. This technology will lead domestic operators to follow this trend, simultaneously solve the problems of insufficient textile recycling materials and waste, and fully realize the low-carbon zero-waste sustainable recycling production of textiles IPM comprehensive management service platform technology IPM comprehensive management service platform technology to respond to climate change by reducing the use of fertilizers and pesticides, increase agricultural product safety and consumer confidence.
