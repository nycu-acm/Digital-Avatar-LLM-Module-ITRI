Starting from 2023, in accordance with the "Recommendations of the Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosure (FSB) proposed by the Financial Stability Board (FSB), TCFD" framework to assess the potential impact of climate change on research projects and cooperative industries, including policy, market changes and natural disaster risks, and identify related opportunities. From 2024, the "Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD)" framework is introduced, using the LEAP methodology (Locate, Evaluate, Assess, Prepare), analyzes the biosensitive location and natural impact and dependence of the base and supply chain. The Institute of Climate-Related Financial Exposure Governance and Engineering has established the "Sustainable Development Steering Committee", chaired by the President, responsible for managing the strategies and goals of sustainability and climate change, holds meetings every six months, and regularly reports the implementation situation to the board of directors. It has a "Organization Sustainable Development Committee" to supervise major climate issues, and is coordinated by the Organization Sustainable Development Promotion Group. Promote the working group and various units to identify climate risks and opportunities, take corresponding measures, and comprehensively promote the climate and sustainable action of our institute. Strategic Research Institute will include climate change risks and opportunities into operation and R&D strategies, mitigate greenhouse gas emissions from the entire value chain (internal and upstream and downstream), strengthen organizational climate resilience and reduce environmental impacts. In 2024, according to government regulations, hospital policies and technical directions, we will re-identify the sources of risks and opportunities, and identify 25 climate risks and 23 climate opportunities, including 10 medium- and high climate risks and 5 medium- and high climate opportunities, and have been put into management practices. Indicators and Targets Institute of Industrial Research continues to set and promote internal and external climate change goals, and set short, medium and long-term goals through indicators such as greenhouse gas emissions, water saving rate, environmental management and energy management system promotion, and regularly review and disclose implementation performance. The path to carbon reduction. As an important technical staff member of the government, our institute's R&D activities and policy development are interdependent. In 2021, carbon emissions have briefly increased due to R&D demand. Apart from such external impacts, our institute continues to reduce energy use and carbon emissions through zero-carbon electricity, low-carbon processes and equipment optimization, and track results with indicators such as greenhouse gas emissions, water saving rate, environment and energy management. At the same time, we promote carbon reduction funds and low-carbon life, and plan to reduce carbon by 35% in 2027 and 50% in 2030, which is consistent with the national target of 32±2% in 2032 and 38±2% in 2035. In the future, we will accelerate the R&D and demonstration application of low-carbon technologies and move towards the net zero goal of 2050. Natural-related financial disclosures Industrial Research Institute introduced natural-related financial disclosures in 2024 (Taskforce) The on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD) framework uses the LEAP method (Locate, Evaluate, Assess, Prepare) to evaluate the ecological risks of the base, strengthen natural capital management and decision-making resilience, covering key base positioning, operational impact assessment, risk and opportunity analysis and response strategy formulation. The LEAP methodological assessment echoes the Kunming-Montreal Global Biodiversity Framework, GBF) Global Goal 15, 2030, encourages enterprises to expose biodiversity risks, dependence and impacts, and enhance information transparency. The Institute of Technology and Research has introduced TNFD to construct a mechanism for identifying, evaluating, managing and exposing natural risks, gradually grasping the dependence, impact and risk opportunities related to nature. In the future, it will deepen its assessment methods, strengthen the quality of exposure, and promote ecological integration and sustainable development.
