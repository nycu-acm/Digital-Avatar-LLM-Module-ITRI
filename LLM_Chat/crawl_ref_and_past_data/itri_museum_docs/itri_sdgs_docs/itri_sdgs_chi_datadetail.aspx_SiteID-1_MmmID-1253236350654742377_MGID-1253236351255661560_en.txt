Research and development will continue to work together
Through intangible assets as financing collateral, link to "Green Finance 3.0"
Release date: 2024-04-18
Faced with the challenge of net zero emissions in 2050, the Industrial Research Institute provides technology and funds to assist Taiwanese manufacturers in transformation and upgrading. In terms of technology, the Industrial Research Institute established the "Chuangzhizhiquan Management Consulting Company", integrating patented technology, creating a national net zero patent pool, and authorizing the technology required by manufacturers to help achieve the SDGs goal. In terms of funds, the Industrial Research Institute cooperates with small and medium-sized enterprise credit guarantee funds and financial institutions to promote the "intangible asset financing" project, use intangible assets such as patents as financing collateral, connect to "Green Finance 3.0" and improve investment confidence of financial institutions.Up to now, nine companies have been assisted to obtain hundreds of millions of yuan in financing to promote the successful transformation of the industry.
Highlight project-ChiLu, creating international competitiveness for environmentally friendly footwear industry
In order to get rid of the fate of OEM for Taiwanese shoe factories, "ChiLu International Co., Ltd."was founded in 2012, focusing on environmentally friendly footwear production, using waste silicon wafers, Baote bottles, agricultural and fishery waste, etc. to make casual shoes, and accumulated 73 patents.The Institute of Technology and Research assisted it to obtain tens of millions of bank financing with invention patents as financing targets to enhance the value of intangible assets.
Chilv International
It was selected as one of the top 30 fast-growing companies that deserve attention by the US Global Business Leaders Magazine, and became the first shoe company certified by Type B enterprise in Asia.
Carbon reduction benefits and circular economy, linking national carbon reduction goals
Chilü International mainly aims to recycle waste and regenerate various functional environmentally friendly shoes, with the goal of improving resource utilization and reducing net zero carbon emissions of waste.
On the road to net zero carbon emissions in 2050 in participating countries, ChiLu International has sold 500,000 pairs of environmentally friendly recycled shoes in 2023, a decrease of about 5,000
Metric tons of greenhouse gases are net carbon reduction
Provide advanced industrial deployment and work together to assist the industry in upgrading its low-carbon smart
With the assistance of the Industrial Research Institute, Chilü International has obtained tens of millions of financial resources through many years of research and development of innovative shoemaking technology and its recycled environmentally friendly shoe products, and has invested in the research and development energy of carbon-reducing shoes to facilitate its rapid entry into the market. Due to its technological gold plating, it has gained favor and attention from all walks of life, including the investment industry.
Linking to technology and capital markets, intangible asset financing increases the fund gap
In terms of technical links, our institute assists Chilü International in its technology and product research and development capabilities to be more competitive in the market.In terms of capital links, it assisted in obtaining support from small and medium-sized credit insurance funds and banks, providing financial loans for Chilü International, allowing it to realize its product research and development and market expansion plans.
