Release date 2024-08-16
Through innovative strategies, the Institute of Technology has formulated the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" under the mission of "Science and Technology R&D, drives industrial development, creates economic value, and enhances social welfare" to plan the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" to use it as a guide for technological R&D, and observes the rapid evolution of international and industrial industries, such as the rapid development of Gen AI, the attractiveness of remote work to global talents, the extension of healthy lifespan brought about by successful aging, the multiple commitments and declarations caused by COP28, and the new challenges faced by national and social public security, etc., and the rolling revision of the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint" to strengthen the four major application areas of smart life (Smart Living), Quality Health, Sustainable Environment, and Resilient Society (Resilient). Key R&D fields such as Society keep pace with the times, and with Intelligentization Enabling Technology as the application support, we will strengthen the intelligence of the four major application areas, and hope to continue to use scientific and technological power to drive industrial transformation, improve the overall quality of life of Chinese people, and take into account the sustainable development balance of the environment. Through comprehensive strategic planning and actions, we will promote the common progress of industries and society.
