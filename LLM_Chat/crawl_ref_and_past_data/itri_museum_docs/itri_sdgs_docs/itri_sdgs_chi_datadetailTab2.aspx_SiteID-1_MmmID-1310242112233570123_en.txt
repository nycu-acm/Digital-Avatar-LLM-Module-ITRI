Innovation protection umbrella helps industrial layout
Release date 2025-06-27
Faced with the rapid changes in the global industry, the Institute of Technology plays a key role of "leverage" and "bridge". In response to the trend of the industrial environment, it provides diversified R&D cooperation and consulting services, including commissioned development of new technologies and new products, small-scale mass production, process optimization, inspection and calibration measurement, measurement standards, as well as technology transfer, intelligent value-added services, etc. Combining domestic and foreign resources, diversified cooperation models will help enterprises break through technical bottlenecks and enhance their competitiveness.The Institute of Technology and Research has also set up an open innovation platform and cultivation mechanism, actively promotes innovation, research and development and cultivation of new businesses, accelerates the implementation and application of technology, and is committed to building an industrial ecosystem that is both flexible and resilient to respond to the uncertainty and changeable needs of the global market, promotes Taiwan's industry toward sustainable development, and realizes the future vision of science and technology driving social progress.
Through the transfer and application of technical achievements, the Institute of Industrial Research has successfully transformed R&D energy into industrial value and promoted the upgrading and transformation of domestic enterprises.Based on the "2035 Technology Strategy and Blueprint", we will create key technologies to help Taiwan achieve its net zero emission target.At the same time, the Industrial Research Institute adopts a joint R&D model, cooperates closely with domestic and foreign enterprises and research institutions to promote industrial innovation;supports new innovation teams, promotes technology commercialization, and expands the impact of R&D results.Through corporate consulting and talent training services, the Industrial Research Institute deepens technical application and strengthens industrial resilience.These achievements not only drive industrial upgrading and social development, but also enhance Taiwan's global competitiveness and become an important force driving industrial innovation.
Innovation protection umbrella helps industrial layout
Our hospital's "Smart Power Week" has been going on for 30 years.We continue to promote the concept of smart power, improve the cultural literacy of smart power in the entire hospital, and also assist Taiwan's industry in improving its competitiveness in smart wealth. Under the global wave of technological innovation, the "Smart Power and New Creation Week" will be launched in 2024. The theme is [New Creation and Intelligence, Sustainable Future].It invites 28 heavyweight experts from the field of industry, officials, academic and research to share practical experience in the application of smart property rights and innovative sustainable development, assist enterprises and new teams in advance to lay out smart property rights, make good use of resources, and seize business opportunities!The event attracted a total of 2,125 participants, and the industry students covered nearly 50 institutions in various fields such as smart sensing, optoelectronics, automotive electronics, biotechnology, and smart power. Through the "Smart Innovation Week", the Institute of Technology continues to inject lively water into Taiwan's sustainable innovation ecosystem.
