Employee health promotion
Release date 2024-07-25
The Institute of Industrial Research attaches importance to the health and well-being of employees. Through the annual employee health check-up, the provision of health check-up items that are better than laws and regulations, the establishment of a health insurance clinic and a pharmacy in the hospital, the establishment of the "Engine Research People Good Health" health information management system, and the handling of a variety of health promotion activities and lectures every year, it plans comprehensive health management and care services for employees. Each campus also has on-site services for professional medical specialists in occupational medicine, providing services such as health examination results consultation, matching/resumption of work and maternal protection, and abnormal workload assessment.
In 2023, a total of 708 employee health promotion activities were held, and 201,735 participants were participated.In addition, our hospital has a psychological counseling room, which provides professional psychological counseling services and diverse physical, mental and spiritual growth courses, so that employees can be fully supported and cared for in their body, mind and spirit.
Systematize health inspection information and improve health promotion plans
Health examinations for employees are handled every year and can be taken regardless of age.Through the "Kungsen Ren Hao Health" management system developed by our hospital, the abnormal health examination items of employees are analyzed.After the health examination, the doctor will be arranged to explain the examination results individually, and a customized health promotion plan is designed based on the employee health examination results.
Healthy catering-The Gongyan Canteen of Sustainable Green Food Catering
The Gongyan Canteen has about 17 counters, 80 special dishes, and both meat and vegetables. Special nutritionists are hired to monitor the nutrition level with three-color trays (eat more green, occasionally yellow, and less red), and launches healthy meals with more vegetables, more comprehensive and less fried to protect the health of employees.
Improve luck and rest services and physical and mental health lectures
Build a first-class leisure center - Youhuo Hall, which provides heated swimming pool, gym, badminton hall, billiards, billiards, pressure relief massage and KTV and other facilities, and subsidies for welfare payments are encouraged to use by colleagues.Every year, various sports courses, various physical fitness competitions, and physical and mental health lectures are held, covering various aspects such as disease prevention, emotional education, career planning and spiritual exploration, and encourage employees to learn and maintain a healthy life in body and mind.
Light up the integrated experience of body and mind
Our psychological counseling room provides a comfortable and privacy-oriented interview space. Through professional psychological support, we help employees settle their body and mind, improve their quality of work and life, and then combine their personal career with the development of our hospital's organization to move towards a win-win situation.Each employee provides up to 15 consultations a year. Within this scope, the employees' immediate relatives and spouses can also use the services, and the fees are borne by our hospital.
