Faced with the rapid development of global AI technology, it actively plays a bridge role, and combines industry, academia and research resources to inquire about industry, and promote the application of artificial intelligence technology in the industry. In 2018, the Institute of Industrial Research took the lead in establishing the cross-unit "Artificial Intelligence Application Strategy Office", integrating the AI ​​technology energy of the entire institute, and under the dual main axes of "people-oriented x cross innovation" and "digital resilience x net zero perpetual", it will develop AI innovation technologies and applications, lock in six major development areas including smart manufacturing, smart commerce, smart medical care, unmanned vehicles, smart terminal chips and net zero perpetuality, and established the "Gen AI Promotion Group" and the "Artificial Intelligence Promotion Group" , further promote AI application, and also echo the government's "five trust industries - artificial intelligence" and "Taiwan AI Action Plan 2.0" policies. ITRI has established a model for comprehensively driving AI application and industrial transformation with three major aspects. Create AI application in the whole hospital. On May 3, 2023, the "Guidelines for the Use of Generative Artificial Intelligence" was formulated to guide colleagues in the hospital to use various Gen AI tools under the basis of legal compliance. In 2024, it will hold 35 promotional exchanges and situational practice trainings to comprehensively improve colleagues' cognition and application capabilities of Gen AI. The Information Department of the Institute integrates existing technical resources in the hospital, builds its own Gen AI service and tool platform, and establishes a high-speed GPU computing platform for sharing and use. Examples are as follows: Recruitment: The recruitment process is fully introduced to GenAI for resume analysis, abstract writing and interview suggestions, and handles more than 10,000 cases each year. Accounting Review: More than 160,000 documents are inspected every year through Gen AI, which greatly improves efficiency and accuracy, and meets audit requirements. Meeting minutes: More than 200 meetings per month use Gen AI for records, saving about 50% on average record human labor hours implementation transformation. Improve the AI ​​knowledge of public departments in 2024, and plans a "civil servant AI training map", providing systematic learning guidance, strengthening the digital soft power of the public department, and promoting more efficient public services and policy innovations in the national think tank.
