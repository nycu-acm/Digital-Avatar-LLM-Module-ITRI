Organizational governance structure
The Board of Directors is the highest governance unit of the Institute of Industrial Research. In accordance with relevant laws and regulations and the powers granted by the competent authorities, it is responsible for resolutions and supervision of important matters within the institute. Considering the direction of organizational operations and laws and policies, the board of directors has set up three functional committees under its jurisdiction, including a compensation committee, an integrity management committee and an audit committee to ensure the improvement of the effectiveness and quality of organizational governance.In addition, in order to implement and improve governance, the President of the Executive Yuan has selected one president and one to two vice presidents, adhered to the resolutions and principles of the board of directors, and comprehensively carried out the affairs of the Council.
The annual operating performance, budget planning and final accounts report of the Institute of Industrial Research are submitted to the board of directors regularly, and the president will publicly explain the organizational positioning, business strategies and R&D strategies to internal stakeholders, clarify the organizational goals and future prospects of the entire institute, and convey the annual strategy direction planning of employees at all levels of the institute, link the performance goals of the entire institute and the unit to jointly achieve organizational performance goals.The overall operating performance is reported in detail through the annual publicly published annual report, including important information such as the organization and human resources, intelligence and services, industrial trends, international awards, industrial promotion, international partners, etc., and is announced on the official website every year to facilitate the review of stakeholders from all walks of life.

Board of Directors and Supervisors
A sound, diverse, professional and efficient board of directors and supervisory boards are the basis for organizational integrity governance.The Board of Directors of this court is responsible for formulating the organization and reporting to the competent authority for approval, formulating work guidelines, reviewing budget and final accounts and other important decisions, and supervising the important business of this court;the Supervisory Board is responsible for the verification of the annual final accounts of this court and the supervision of important affairs of this court.
Members of the Board of Directors and Supervisors are selected by the President of the Executive Yuan. The term of office is three years. Renewal must be held once after the expiration of the term. The number of renewals shall not exceed one-half of the total number of original members.Their employment shall be considered for long-term business strategies and background diversity, and shall be held by experts and scholars in industry in various fields such as information and communications electronics, business management, smart property, mechanical engineering, aerospace technology, etc., including the supervisors of the relevant department committees of the Executive Yuan, experts and scholars, and representatives of private enterprises.
