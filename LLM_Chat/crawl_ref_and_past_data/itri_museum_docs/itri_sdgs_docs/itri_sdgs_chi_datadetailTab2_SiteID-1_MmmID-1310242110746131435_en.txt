Greenhouse gas investigation
This court is committed to practicing the concept of sustainable business. It will independently implement the organizational inspection of greenhouse gas emissions at the level from 2022. According to the standard process of ISO 14064-1:2018, adopt the "Operation Control Law" as a benchmark, and systematically count the greenhouse gas emissions generated by various operating activities to ensure that the inspection results meet international standards. In response to the carbon reduction path planning, the organizational scope consistent with this report was established in 2024, and the base year was retrospectively 2020, and the same calculation method was used to complete the annual inspection.
The 2024 inspection covers the ZTE Campus, Guangfu Campus, Liujia Campus, Zhongchuang Park, Nanchuang Park, Shalun Green Energy Technology Demonstration Site and Taipei Office.After verification by the BSI British Standards Association in accordance with ISO 14064-1:2018 guidelines, the total greenhouse gas emissions of this hospital are 68,886.311 metric tons of CO₂e, and the proportion of purchased electricity accounts for 68.04%, which is the main source of emissions.In order to identify high-carbon emission hotspots, our hospital has established a greenhouse gas inspection system for the whole hospital, and combined with data analysis, accurately planned carbon reduction.

Greening of the campus
itree tree database
Through the greening and environmental management measures of the campus, the Institute of Technology is committed to maintaining ecological diversity and a friendly environment. The "Itree Tree Database" will be built from 2023, and tree risk assessment, update and set up famous tree brands will be carried out regularly, so that maintenance units and colleagues can scan the signs to grasp their health status immediately.In 2024, further promote "tree health examination", construct standard procedures for tree assessment and diseased tree removal, ensure proper care and reduce the risk of lodging, and strengthen long-term results in greening and carbon reduction.
Greening of the campus
Energy-saving building
Through the transformation of existing buildings and the construction of green buildings, our hospital demonstrates its determination to practice low-carbon operations.As of the publication of the report, the self-built green building of this institute has obtained a total of 8 green building marks and 2 smart building marks, including one existing building being transformed into a diamond-grade green building. In 2024, Zhongchuang Park has obtained nearly zero carbon building certification, showing the results of continuous implementation of "building energy conservation" and setting a model of organizational sustainability.
China-Taiwan Innovation Park obtains Level 1+ certification for energy efficiency mark
Energy-saving results
Compared with the benchmark year (2020), more than 116,300 kWh of electricity is saved, which is equivalent to 13.21% of the overall electricity consumption.
Renewable energy
Water Resources Management
The park has built a solar photoelectric system with a total of 150,000 watts, with the power generation reaching 201,100 kW in 2023, meeting 22.85% of the electricity consumption needs of public areas of the park; in 2024, the renewable energy generation reaches 211,500 kW.
A rainwater recycling system was built, and the rainwater recycling and use volume in 2023 reached 9,375 degrees, accounting for 49.14% of the total annual water use; the rainwater recycling and use volume in 2024 reached 5,194 degrees, accounting for 41.56% of the total annual water use
