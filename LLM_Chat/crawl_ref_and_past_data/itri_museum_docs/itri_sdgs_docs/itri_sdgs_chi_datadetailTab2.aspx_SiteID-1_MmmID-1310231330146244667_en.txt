Tactile Perception Navigation Endoscope Robot
Tactile Perception Navigation Endoscope Robot
Develop tactile navigation endoscopic robots to realize precise surgery, automated medical care, and promote the upgrading of the smart medical industry.
Environment (E)
Society (S)
Economy (E)
Quick change module can provide patients with
Special endoscope for humans to reduce medical treatment
Treat waste and reduce carbon emissions
Solve the problem of shortage of medical staff
Promote the domestic medical equipment industry
Upgrade and enhance domestic medical materials production
Product enters the international market competitiveness
Solution
Our hospital combines integrated forming and omnidirectional swing micro hose robot, AI air hole recognition assisted guidance technology and lung biopsy system, remotely manipulate natural cavity surgery, overcomes direct trek in the body, solves postoperative complications, and provides efficient medical robot solutions.
Technology application
+ Integrated bend segments of multi-degree-of-freedom robot hose and omnidirectional swing control (210°; minimum size up to OD2.9mm, ID1.2mm), flexible operation without dead angles.
+ New natural cavity robot surgical technology to avoid bleeding and perforation caused by external puncture, it can penetrate into section 6 of the human lungs and cover more than 90% of the lung area.
+ In vivo automatic navigation system combines preoperative planning path assistance and AI air hole identification assist guidance to achieve accurate operational guidance.
Results Display
+ Link to the domestic related component supply chain to complete the feasibility verification of the application of tactile perception navigation endoscopic robots in endoscopic surgery, and realize non-invasive natural cavity interventional treatment.
+ Cooperate with Hsinchu National Taiwan University Branch and Institute of Dynamics of the Academy of Agricultural Sciences to complete preclinical animal trials. Director Yang Shunmao, director of Hsinchu National Taiwan University Branch Hospital, operates tactile perception navigation endoscopic robots to achieve telemedicine collaboration, providing clinical operation feedback and optimal treatment effects.
+ Win the "2024 Global Top 100 Science and Technology R&D Awards (R&D100 Awards)" and the "21st National New Innovation Award".
Regenerative Medicine Allogeneic Cell Therapy-Key of Cell Function
Regenerative Medicine Allogeneic Cell Therapy-Key of Cell Function
Allogeneic cell screening platform accurately matches treatment needs, reduces costs, improves treatment success rate and promotes the development of regenerative medicine.
Environment (E)
Society (S)
Economy (E)
Focus on precise treatment, reduce excessive medical waste and improve the utilization rate of medical resources
The cost is about one-tenth of current cell therapy products, providing patients with affordable, safe and effective cell therapy, greatly improving patient survival rate and quality of life
Use innovative technologies to break through traditional restrictions, realize the pharmacization of cell therapy, promote the development of innovative industries, create higher economic benefits through cost advantages, and become the future economic driving force of the biotech industry
Solution
Key Functional Cell Identity (KFCI)" is an accurate and standardized cell screening platform for allogeneic cell therapy. It can quickly screen out high-quality cells and find the best corresponding diseases, breaking through the traditional dilemma of time-consuming and labor-intensive and delaying golden treatment time in finding suitable donors, and further improve the success rate of surgery and accelerate the development of cell therapy products.
Technology application
Features of the "Key of Cell Function" Cell Screening Platform:
+ Identification is smarter: Using precise single-molecule identification technology, cells with poor quality can be filtered and 40 indications can be used to find out the most suitable therapeutic use for each cell.
+ More cost-effective: Through systematic identification and screening, the cost estimate is only one-tenth of the current cell therapy products.
+ More elastic amplification: It can be commercially produced in mass production. One donor cell can produce 100,000 to 500,000 preparations. If patients with severe acute illness, they can immediately obtain safe and effective cells, which greatly improves the patient's survival rate and quality of life.
Results Display
+ Winner of the Edison Awards Silver Award in 2024. The selected cell products have passed 2 TFDA clinical trial applications and are used for the treatment of acute myocardial infarction and diabetic foot ulcer disease.
