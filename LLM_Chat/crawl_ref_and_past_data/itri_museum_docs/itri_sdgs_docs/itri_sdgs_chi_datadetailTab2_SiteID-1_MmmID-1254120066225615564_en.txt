The Institute of Welfare and Care Industry and Technology regards employees as important partners. In order to improve the physical and mental health and well-being of employees, the Welfare Committee is responsible for planning and promoting employee welfare matters, and provides a number of better welfare and employee care measures than laws, taking into account the balanced development of employees' work, life and health. In 2023, 39 arts, cultural, parent-child and sports activities, 6 public welfare projects (including charity donations), etc., and the total employee welfare expenditure in 2023 is NT$1.34 billion. The Welfare Committee holds various sports, parent-child, art and culture activities every year to promote the physical, mental and spiritual health of employees. Encourage family members to participate and enjoy together to enhance organizational vitality and cohesion. In order to continuously implement human care and encourage social welfare investment, family care plans are provided for different ethnic groups, such as special leave, volunteer leave, female colleagues' pregnancy and parental leave, and male colleagues' parental leave. In 2023, a total of 519 people apply for child care and family care leave. Various benefits not only take care of employees, but also support their responsibilities and dedication to the family. Career development is in response to the competitive trend of the talent market in the science and technology industry. In order to actively attract and retain outstanding talents and continuously improve organizational competitiveness, our institute has designed reasonable rewards and promotion plans to reduce talent loss. Resignation meetings will be held for each resignation employee as a reference for talent retention and system optimization. At the same time, through teaching supervisors, we will assist employees in career development and promote cross-domain rotation of talents within the organization. The internal rotation of talents is 13.77%, totaling 862 people.
