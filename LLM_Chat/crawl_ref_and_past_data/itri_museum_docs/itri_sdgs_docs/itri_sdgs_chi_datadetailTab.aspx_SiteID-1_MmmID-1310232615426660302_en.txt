Employee health promotion
Release date 2025-07-01
The Institute of Industrial Research attaches importance to the health and well-being of employees, provides health examination projects, health insurance clinics and pharmacies that are better than laws and regulations, and has established the "Engine Research and Human Good Health" system and promotes diversified health promotion activities and lectures to create comprehensive health management and care services. Each campus has on-site services for specialized doctors in occupational medicine, providing services such as health consultation, adaptive matching/resumption of work, maternal health protection and occupational disease prevention. A total of 731 health promotion activities were held in 2024, with 196,375 participants.A psychological counseling room is also set up to provide professional psychological counseling and diverse physical and mental courses to fully support employees' psychological and health needs.
Create a sustainable and healthy workplace
Sustainable practice has expanded from "environment" to "employee health care and promotion".The Institute of Technology and Research actively integrates the diverse aspects of "knowledge (health awareness), movement (healthy exercise), food (healthy diet), and auxiliary (health management)" to promote physical and mental health care for employees and achieve a balance between work, life and health. In 2024, he won the CHR Health Corporate Citizens' Non-Profit Gold Award.
Our hospital independently developed the "Work Research and Human Good Health" system to track five major indicators of metabolic syndrome, assist employees in grasping the data trends of previous years, systematically manage health risks, and implementing "prevention is more important than treatment".In terms of health promotion, we promote activities such as walking, fat loss, and YOUHUO courses, and use the "Posefit muscle energy detection mirror" to analyze posture and muscle group status, and combine coach suggestions to improve sports awareness and quality of life.In addition, we will promote the "National Health Insurance Family Physician Integrated Care Plan", build a two-way referral mechanism between clinics and hospitals, and strengthen the care for chronic diseases and the third high.
In terms of psychological care, there is a psychological counseling room and a physical and mental clinic to provide professional support to enhance the sense of physical and mental happiness in the workplace.In addition to providing nutritious, diverse and low-burden meals, Gongyan canteen also regularly performs independent food safety testing. Employees can check the daily menu, calories, food sources and inspection reports, taking into account healthy diet.
Improve luck and rest services and physical and mental health lectures
Build a first-class leisure center - Youhuo Hall, which provides heated swimming pool, gym, badminton hall, billiards, billiards, pressure relief massage and KTV and other facilities, and subsidies for welfare payments are encouraged to use by colleagues.Every year, various sports courses, various physical fitness competitions, and physical and mental health lectures are held, covering various aspects such as disease prevention, emotional education, career planning and spiritual exploration, and encourage employees to learn and maintain a healthy life in body and mind.
