Net Zero Transformation and Promoting Industrial Net Zero
2024/08/12
This court has formulated the "2050 Net Zero Sustainable Strategic Goal", using its net zero-related R&D and application experience in renewable energy, energy conservation and carbon reduction over the years, and internally use the four major aspects of "low-carbon living, construction, transportation and parks" as a demonstration field for promoting net zero transformation; externally, it continues to join hands with governments and enterprises to participate in international environmental contracts, and promotes the fields of renewable energy, carbon emission reuse and storage (CO2Capture, Utilization, Storage, CCUS) and water resource recycling to assist in the establishment of a green industrial chain and connect with the 2050 national net zero carbon emission target.
Use technology to prevent epidemics and protect the health of the people
2024/08/12
COVID -19 The COVID-19 epidemic is spreading around the world. Our hospital applies the accumulated R&D technology energy in the past to meet the needs of the government and the public, conduct horizontal connection and cross-domain integration, and quickly set up an "epidemic prevention and response team" to create a comprehensive science and technology epidemic prevention network through strategies such as scientific and technological epidemic prevention, agile response and digital management, and actively protect the health and safety of all Chinese people and our employees.At the same time, through the epidemic review, we will gradually strengthen the organization's digital transformation and risk management policies, turn crises into opportunities, strengthen the overall resilience of the organization and improve rapid response capabilities.
Promote Taiwan's industrial upgrading and transformation and cultivate new businesses
2024/04/18
Adhering to the original intention of founding, the Institute of Technology and Research continues to play a key role in promoting Taiwan's industrial upgrading and transformation, and takes cultivating new ventures as one of the key strategies. Its strategies cover four major aspects: cultural shaping, tutoring and cultivation, investment attraction and post-investment management.Combining professional technology and intellectual financial resources, the Industrial Research Institute provides abundant support to the new team and helps them grow.Most of the new startups cultivated by the Industrial Research Institute are high-tech industries and have market uniqueness, such as TSMC, Lianhua Electronics and other companies.So far, 165 technology-oriented startups have been successfully cultivated.Looking ahead, the Institute of Technology will continue to deepen its cultivation of new innovation culture, integrate guidance resources, strengthen the competitiveness of new innovation teams, and promote the sustainable development of the industry.
Through intangible assets as financing collateral, link to "Green Finance 3.0"
2024/04/18
Faced with the challenge of net zero emissions in 2050, the Industrial Research Institute provides technology and funds to assist Taiwanese manufacturers in transformation and upgrading.In terms of technology, the Industrial Research Institute established the "Chuangzhizhiquan Management Consulting Company", integrating patented technology, creating a national net zero patent pool, and authorizing the technology required by manufacturers to help achieve the SDGs goal.In terms of funds, the Industrial Research Institute cooperates with small and medium-sized enterprise credit guarantee funds and financial institutions to promote the "intangible asset financing" project, use intangible assets such as patents as financing collateral, connect to "Green Finance 3.0" and improve investment confidence of financial institutions.Up to now, nine companies have been assisted to obtain hundreds of millions of yuan in financing to promote the successful transformation of the industry.
