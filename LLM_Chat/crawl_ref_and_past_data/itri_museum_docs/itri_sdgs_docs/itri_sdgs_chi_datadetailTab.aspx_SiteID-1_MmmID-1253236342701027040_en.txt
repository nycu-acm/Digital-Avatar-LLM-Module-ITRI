Sustainable Development Goals
In order to assist Taiwan's industry and its own sustainable operations, our institute referred to the 17 Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) and 169 detailed goals issued by the United Nations in 2015, and formulated the "Sustainable Development Goals" based on the "Taiwan Sustainable Development Goals" formulated by the National Sustainable Development Committee of the Executive Yuan in 2016. Based on the principles of "Innovative Thinking", "Open Communication" and "Agile Management" of the Institute of Technology's core culture, we will discuss six major promotional key points, including "Integrity Governance", "Technology and Technology Research and Development", "Industrial Development", "Social Welfare", "Happy Workplace" and "Green Campus", cover three aspects of environment, society and governance, and formulates quantitative goals for short, medium and long-term sustainable performance for six major promotion priorities, to regularly review the achievement of the sustainable development goals, continue to make the greatest contribution to Taiwan's sustainable development path, and respond to global sustainable development actions.
