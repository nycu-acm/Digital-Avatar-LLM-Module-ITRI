By promoting the transformation of organizational culture and establishing a talent development mechanism, the three major cultural DNAs of "innovative thinking", "open communication" and "agile management" are implemented at all levels of human resources management. The talent development system of the Institute of Technology and Research Institute includes "formal training", "performance development", "independent learning" and "knowledge sharing" The four major structures take into account the short, medium and long-term development needs of organizational operations, and also ensure that the talent management system keeps pace with the times. By introducing diverse learning resources and advocating cross-domain learning culture, we promote full cooperation among cross-department to form a special project team to jointly assist the development of talents within the institute and improve organizational efficiency. Our institute also actively encourages independent learning and shape learning organizations. The average number of training hours in 2024 is 53.10 hours, and the total number of training hours increased by 8.59% compared with the previous year. The average training fund investment has grown by 3.67%. There are 128 people who are in-service doctoral and master's degrees at home and abroad, including 106 doctoral degrees and 22 master's degrees. In order to meet organizational development and cultural transformation, our hospital actively creates a diversified learning blueprint for colleagues through various cultivation projects and advocates cross-domain learning culture. Dual-axis leadership cultivation: cultivate management leadership and business strength at all levels, and build sustainable talent infrastructure and leadership (People Skill and Business Skill Learning Module Design "New Supervisor Leadership Development" Manual, empowering novice leaders' management literacy and personal development to hold more than 50 physical training sessions, more than 6,600 people participated, and more than 60,000 online independent learning sessions Generative artificial intelligence (Gen AI) training: Cultivate employees on a large scale to use Gen AI to promote business efficiency improvement and promotion Follow Gen AI usage standards and ethical context-based propt application practice training, such as: operation staff, marketing business, technology research and development, project management, etc., to organize diversified learning activities, self-group learning community and knowledge sharing, business application competitions, etc., cultivate 4,796 people (excluding related activities), the average course practicality is 4.8 points (out of 5 points), tracking the learning results of two months after class, 95% of the completed colleagues have applied Gen AI Auxiliary business international talent bridge project/Honghu project: Cultivate talents with forward-thinking and able to bridge foreign research energy, improve technical level and international perspective, recommend talents to go to internationally renowned universities or top research institutions for 3 months to 1 year of training, learn forward-looking technology and applications, improve the international vision and technical level of talents, select outstanding potential elites to go to overseas bases for training, establish a hub with international institutions, and promote more links and cooperation opportunities 2024 A total of 6 elites were selected to go to international training or train new employees in the chain. The training of new employees in the Institute of Technology can effectively help new people understand the core values, culture, systems and resources of the organization, understand the diversity of career development, expand cross-field connections, and allow new people to accelerate integration and enhance their sense of belonging. In addition to inviting senior supervisors to talk to new people, let new people feel the attention of the organization, they also teach new people precise questions and team communication skills through interactive lectures on communication and questioning; also carefully arrange team tasks and treasure hunting activities to familiarize new people with the environment and resources of the campus, and promote interaction with partners across fields and across campuses. The Institute of Institute of Sustainable Environmental Education will handle sustainable environmental education training in 2024, and the courses cover environmental protection, ecological conservation, net zero emissions, and ISO 14001 and organizational sustainable innovation cases and other topics, the accumulated learning hours reached 48,540 hours, the annual compulsory course completion rate reached 100%, deepening employees' awareness of sustainability and implementing them in practice. ITRI Co-learning Circle In order to encourage employees' independent learning motivation and actively shape learning organizations, it designed learning activities for the whole institute for the fourth consecutive year in 2024. With the "Co-learning Circle" as the main axis, in addition to all employees' learning, employees self-organize 224 teams to participate, and promote team learning and cross-domain knowledge sharing through the delivery of theme task packages, and encourage good growth together.
