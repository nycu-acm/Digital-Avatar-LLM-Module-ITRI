In order to assist Taiwan's industry and its own sustainable operations, our institute has approved the "Blueprint for the sustainable development of the Institute of Industrial Research Institute" (see the figure below), and discussed six major promotional key points, including "integrity governance", "technology research and development", "industrial development", and "social welfare"
(Note 1)
, "Happy Workplace" and "Green Campus", covering the environment, society and governance aspects, and formulates quantitative goals for short, medium and long-term sustainable performance in response to the six major promotion priorities. The achievement of the sustainable development goals will be reviewed regularly in the Organization and Sustainable Development Committee every quarter.
(Note 2)
, continue to make the greatest contribution to Taiwan’s sustainable development path and respond to global sustainable development actions.
Note:
1. The original name of "Social Welfare" is "Social Welfare". After considering the organizational characteristics of our institute, in addition to applying scientific research to the public welfare field, it can also give full play to the characteristics of legal person organizational negotiations and extensions to local industries and communities, and jointly achieve wider social benefits with the government.Therefore, after discussion by the senior supervisor group of our institute, the resolution was officially renamed.
2. The spirit of the blueprint for sustainable development of the Institute of Technology lies in pursuing balanced development of various indicators, not neglecting secondary themes, and expecting that the organization should attach equal importance to talent cultivation, human rights management and vulnerable groups in society in terms of development environment and governance plans.
