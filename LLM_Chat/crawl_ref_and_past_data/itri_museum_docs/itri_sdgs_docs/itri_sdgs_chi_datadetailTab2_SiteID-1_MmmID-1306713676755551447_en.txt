Governance
Faced with the challenges of global climate change, the Industrial Research Institute provides the industry with a foundation for responding to climate risks through continuous scientific and technological innovation and forward-looking research, assists in opening up new business opportunities, and enhances society's ability to adapt to future uncertainties. In 2023, our court will assess the impact of climate change on research projects and cooperative industries, including policy changes, market demand changes and natural disaster risks, based on the "Climate-Related Financial Disclosure Recommendations (TCFD)" framework proposed by the International Financial Stability Committee (FSB).At the same time, we actively identify green technology development and sustainable industry solutions, sort and analyze the identified climate risks and opportunities, and systematically integrate them into our hospital's operation and research strategies.Ensure that while responding to climate change, our institute continues to promote scientific and technological innovation and industrial progress, and contribute to the sustainable development of the industry, Taiwan and the world.
The "Environmental Policy of the Whole Academy" signed by the Dean of the Institute of Technology demonstrates its commitment to environmental sustainability and climate change.Through the "Sustainable Development Steering Committee" and senior management strategies for managing sustainability and climate change, our court regularly reports to the board of directors.The Organizational Sustainable Development Committee is responsible for overseeing climate risk issues and is led by the Organizational Sustainable Development Office and Quality and Risk Management Office to promote actions.All R&D, operation and business units will collaborate to identify and respond to climate risks to ensure the effective implementation of climate and sustainable actions in the entire hospital.
Strategy
Our hospital's 2035 Technical Strategy and Blueprint 4+1 fields are closely related to climate change issues.Climate change issues will pose risks and opportunities to our institute's business and finance due to changes in R&D direction and competitive opportunities.In view of this, the Office of Sustainable Development was organized to cooperate with various units to identify and discuss climate risks and opportunities, evaluate the impact of climate change on the short-term, medium-term and long-term operations of our institute, formulate climate mitigation and adaptation management measures, and actively find opportunities derived from climate change, and explore new cooperation opportunities and business areas.
The goal and plan of the net zero carbon reduction path of the Institute of Industrial Research
Risk Management
Organize the Office of Sustainable Development to identify and evaluate climate change risks based on the risk management process structure of this institute, and formulate mitigation and adaptation measures to continuously monitor changes in climate change risks.With the TCFD structure, in the risk identification stage, we analyze the entity risks, transformation risks and potential development opportunities caused by climate change, and evaluate their impact on the finance and operations of our institute from the perspective of the value chain, and conduct preliminary quantitative analysis.Based on the evaluation results and the energy-saving and carbon reduction goals revealed by the President in 2021, our hospital’s sustainable development goals will be calibrated and the mitigation and adaptation action plan will be launched.Through the organization of the Sustainable Development Committee and various units, we conduct verification and risk control, and use smart energy monitoring systems and smart water network management for continuous monitoring, and use annual stakeholders and major issues to identify risk performance and continuously improve.
Indicators and goals
After identifying climate risks and opportunities, in addition to financial indicators, it is confirmed that the indicators used by our court to evaluate and manage climate-related issues include electricity saving rate, water saving rate, greenhouse gas emissions, and environmental-related management systems.The short, medium and long-term goals of each indicator and the performance of 2023 will be fully disclosed through this sustainable report. This court introduces the TCFD climate change management mechanism to integrate and calibrate various management plans and measures. With the continuous integration and adjustment of the existing management system of this court, it can be foreseen that this court will gradually implement the vision and goals of climate change response and sustainable development, and play a greater influence and contribution to the operation and sustainable development of our court.
