Major topic identification process
The Institute of Technology and Research will refer to the major theme decision process and reporting principles of GRI 2021 edition. Based on the organizational operation of this institute, the following major identification process will be constructed as a reference for promoting sustainable issues and resource allocation. The formulation and implementation results of major theme-related goals shall be submitted to the Sustainable Development Steering Committee regularly by the Organization's Office of Sustainable Development, and the Dean is authorized by the Board of Directors to review and improve relevant promotion directions and practices to improve the effectiveness of the organization's sustainable development.
Major analysis process of the Institute of Technology
Major Theme Risk Matrix
According to the identification process of major themes of the Institute of Industrial Research, the 2024 major theme matrix and identification results of our institute are shown in the figure below:
Majority matrix of the Institute of Technology Research
Note:
1. The major themes identified by our hospital for 2024 are shown in bold.Compared with 2023, there are 7 major themes in 2024: greenhouse gases and renewable energy, with a total of 7 major themes.
2. After the identification process of major themes, our hospital re-invents the resource allocation and sustainability goals in the hospital. After confirmation by experts and senior supervisors in the Institute of Technology Research, the actual actions of the original theme "Climate Change Mitigation and Adjustment" will be implemented on various environmental themes to meet the main principles and directions of the climate theme;and merge "Greenhouse Gas Emission Reduction" and "Renewable Energy" into a single theme of "Greenhouse Gas and Renewable Energy", which is defined as a major theme of 2024.
Major theme management
According to the identification process of major themes of the Institute of Industrial Research, our institute has the following seven major themes in 2024.
(Note)
:
Technological innovation and research and development
Technology application and promotion
Customer Relations
Partnerships
Information security management
Talent cultivation and development
Greenhouse gases and renewable energy
After analyzing these seven major topics, our hospital will explain the management results in "Importance and Impact", "Policies/Commitments and Action Notes", "2024 Goals and Important Achievements", "2025 Goals", "Value Chain Impact" and "Complied Chapters" explaining the detailed contents of each major topic, and will be presented in our hospital's 2024 Sustainable Report.Please click here for details for details.

Note: The major theme differences between 2024 and the previous edition of the report: Compared with 2023, the original theme "Greenhouse Gas Emission Reduction" and "Renewable Energy" were merged into "Greenhouse Gas and Renewable Energy", and this theme has been elevated to a major theme of our hospital.
