Sustainable management organization
In 2022, the Institute of Technology and Research established the "Sustainable Development Steering Committee" with the president as chairman. With the frequency of meetings held once every six months, it will make decisions with the directors' office directors appointed by the president to implement the vision, strategies and operating policies of the sustainable development of the institute, and supervise the achievement of various projects of sustainable development and the direction of improvement.
To promote and implement the sustainable tasks of our court, the "Organization Sustainable Development Committee" is established. The director of the president's office appointed by the president serves as the chairman. Progress meetings are held regularly every quarter.The main responsibilities are to identify sustainable development issues, formulate sustainable development goals, supervise cross-team communication and resource integration and coordination, promote action plans and improvement plans, and track the implementation results of sustainable operations. Six promotion working groups are divided into two groups, and expert consultants for the sustainable industry. Established the "Office of Sustainable Development of Organizations"
(Note)
, to continuously promote the sustainable development and operation of our hospital organization.
In order to enhance the connection between the Steering Committee of Sustainable Development and the Board of Directors, our institute regularly reports on the achievement of sustainable development, annual results of sustainable development goals (SDGs) and external communication information to the Board of Directors to ensure that the sustainable development commitment of the Institute of Technology and Research can be implemented.
Note:
1. The different colors of the sustainable theme are the differences from the management sustainable theme last year. Our hospital will update the relevant sustainable themes in a rolling manner year by year according to the results of the major themes of the year, and refer to the suggestions of external expert consultants, the sustainable topics of international scientific research organizations and the benchmark industry, to respond to the sustainable trend of development.
2. In response to the overall sustainability of the organization, the former "Office of Organizational Sustainable Development" was renamed as the "Office of Organizational Sustainable Development Promotion Group" in June 2024. The original six promotion working groups were adjusted to six major work sub-items, and the relevant content will be adjusted in the announcement of the content of the sub-version of the perpetual report.
