Social welfare blueprint
Since 2011, the Institute of Technology Research and Research has established the "Social Public Welfare Committee" with the vision of "using technology to do charity and making Taiwan more beautiful", and is committed to improving social welfare. Through the application of science and technology, promotion of science and technology education, corporate volunteers and technology going to the countryside, we actively integrate core science and technology and volunteer forces, invest surplus in social welfare year by year, and solve problems such as emergency rescue, public safety and vulnerable care. Through the public welfare technology platform, we will transform research results into practical applications, focus on youth cultivation, rural education, local creator and environmental conservation, connect social welfare institutions, corporate partners and government agencies, and promote the comprehensive improvement of social welfare.
Technology application
The Institute of Technology and Research will lead a bright future with innovative technology, continue to contribute to society, play the role of a public welfare platform, and solve urgent assistance problems through scientific and technological media, and promote social and environmental protection.We integrate R&D technology, pay attention to the assistance and medical care of vulnerable groups, and plan to follow-up business transfers, link corporate resources, extend research results to social welfare, and enhance social welfare.Recently, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research has assisted Turkey's earthquake victims to donate water purification systems and provided support to vulnerable groups in China.It has launched "active ultraviolet surface sterilization technology", developed such as "AI sign language translator" to reduce communication barriers, and introduced "brain wave pulling and pressure relief device" at Kenna Manor.Faced with the extremely elderly society that is about to enter, we cooperate with hospitals to promote the concept of preventing diseases, and at the same time use technology to assist in the development of local economies by aboriginal tribes and local villages, and light up the light of hope for society.
Science and Technology Education
In Taiwan’s 2050 net zero emission path, “life transformation” is one of the important strategies to achieve net zero perpetuality.The Institute of Technology is committed to innovative technology research and development and net zero tutoring, bringing new opportunities for industrial transformation and basic science education.We use technology to empower scientific innovation experiments and popular science education to expose the entire people to the latest technology and improve scientific literacy, especially to cultivate environmental and social sustainability awareness among children in the countryside and the general public.Our college cooperates with industry-university-research institutions and non-profit organizations to transform technical resources into educational materials, promote environmental education through public welfare lectures, teacher training and science popularization, and promote scientific and technological education in rural areas to help children establish a sustainable awareness, thereby affecting their families and communities and achieving public welfare influence.
Corporate volunteers
In order to help bring science and technology applications and public welfare resources into the local area, the Institute of Technology and Research has called on all colleagues to voluntarily serve as public welfare volunteers, integrate clubs and NGO groups in the institute, and jointly promote the "Society and You Yiqiu charity" project, with the main service targets for children and elderly people of disadvantaged ethnic groups, rural areas and indigenous people.In 2023, the Institute of Industrial Research and Research will link more social welfare groups and enterprises to give scrapped computers new students, donate them to tribes in the villages, improve children's digital capabilities, and launch the Sichuan Cleaning Action to protect water sources.In addition, the community activities in the hospital are booming, and colleagues use their spare time to actively participate in charity, such as volunteer teams and badminton charity activities, promoting the cycle of love and expanding the influence of charity.
Technology goes to the countryside
The Institute of Technology uses the advantages of operating bases in the central and southern regions and Huadong regions to serve the surrounding neighborhood communities, combines local technical units resources and community volunteers, and uses public welfare visits, technology to go to the countryside, and tribal community entrepreneurship as the main axis to care for and serve local vulnerable groups for a long time.Our hospital promotes materials, agriculture, industry and processing technologies, such as combining local characteristics through 3D printing, scientific and technological experience and agricultural application technologies, and advances to the local and indigenous tribes, take scientific and technological education and provide local and rural public welfare services.It is beneficial to the application value of community tribal industries. The trajectory of public welfare is spread across the outlying islands and mountains. It mainly focuses on local youths. It provides guidance and assistance to economic creation and improve life, implements local recognition, and continues the value of public welfare without interruption.
