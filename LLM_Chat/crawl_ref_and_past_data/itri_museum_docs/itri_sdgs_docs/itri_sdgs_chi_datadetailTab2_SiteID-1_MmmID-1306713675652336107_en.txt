According to the standard process of ISO 14064-1 greenhouse gas inspection and quantification, the "Operation Control Method" is used as the judgment principle to check the greenhouse gas emissions generated by various equipment and operational activities. In 2023, the boundary of the organizational inspection will be expanded to ZTE Campus, Guangfu Campus, Liujia Campus, Zhongchuang Park, Nanchuang Park and Shalun Green Energy Technology Demonstration Field. In response to the changes in the boundary structure of the organizational inspection, the base year of greenhouse gas emissions of this hospital has changed from 2022 to 2023. The overall inspection and data results are passed ISO 14064-1:2018 Third-party verification of greenhouse gas investigation. The total greenhouse gas emissions in 2023 are 98,967.949 metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent, direct (category 1) emissions are 7,240.77 metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent, indirect (category 2) emissions are 50,301.655 metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent, and other indirect (category 3 and 4) emissions are 41,425.524 metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent; the main source of greenhouse gas emissions is purchased electricity, accounting for 51% of the overall carbon emissions. In the future, this court will continue to conduct greenhouse gas investigations and external verifications, examine the emission hot spots and energy consumption within the organization, formulate corresponding emission reduction plans, and thus achieve the goal of net zero emissions. 2023 The total annual greenhouse gas emission category direct emissions (category 1) indirect emissions - input energy (category 2) indirect emissions - transportation and organizational use (category 3, 4) Total emissions of the hospital Greenhouse gas emissions (metric tons of CO2e/year) 7,240.7750,301.65541,425.52498,967.949 Greenhouse gas emission density (metric tons of CO2e/million) 0.271.891.553.71 Growth category proportion (left picture) and greenhouse gas emission distribution in 2023 (right picture) 2023 Category 1 Greenhouse gas emissions Green Buildings Our hospital demonstrates its determination to practice low-carbon operations through the transformation of existing buildings and the construction of green buildings. In 2011, Green Campus was launched It is planned to conduct inspections and energy consumption improvements of existing buildings to promote the transformation of existing buildings into green buildings. As of the time of publication and issuance of the report, the green buildings built by our court have obtained 8 green building mark certifications and 2 smart building mark certifications, including the successful transformation of one existing building as a diamond-grade green building, showing the court's efforts to continue to implement "building energy conservation" and set a sustainable architectural model for legal person organizations. Note: 1. Zhongchuang Park once again verified the green building and smart building marks in 2020; Lujia Campus Huying Pavilion and Zhongxing Campus 10 and 90 Pavilions (single dormitories) The above three museums have also continued to maintain and operate in accordance with the green building energy conservation standards and are included in the scope of this disclosure. 2. Zhongchuang and Shalun Park entrusted the court to manage the agency for the disclosure and included in the scope of zero-carbon buildings OMEGA ZONE Industrial Research Institute established the Omega Zone Entrepreneurship Guidance Center in Hualien, committed to promoting the development of the eastern industry, focusing on new energy, circular applications, long-distance medical care, science and technology agriculture, industry and youth return home. Through the building energy simulation system, the construction of solar photoelectric equipment is optimized, and low-carbon energy-saving ceiling fans, energy-saving insulation coatings and energy management are used to build self-generating renewable energy and energy-saving equipment. The related equipment has been established in 2023 The annual construction is completed, and the goal is to obtain the "nearly zero carbon building 1+" level certification of the Taiwan Building Energy Efficiency Evaluation System of the Ministry of the Interior to create a demonstration base for young entrepreneurs in rural areas, local entrepreneurship and zero carbon building. The imported asphalt regenerator for recycled asphalt is expensive and of different quality, while most of the waste asphalt for industrial use are buried out of office, and its recycling rate is relatively low, which not only wastes resources, but may also cause environmental pollution. The biomass asphalt regenerator developed by our hospital uses lignin to improve the properties of aging and scrapping materials, improve the compatibility of new and old asphalt, and increase the reuse rate to 40%, and the cost is only one-third of the imported products. By 2023, the utilization rate of recycled asphalt on the hospital has reached 48%, reducing the emission of about 535 metric tons of carbon dioxide equivalent. In the future, ultra-micrometer technology will be introduced to improve the comfort and safety of the road , and extend the service life of the road and reduce the generation of waste materials. Greening of the campus covers a vast area and is rich in ecology. The hospital has a diverse greening measures and the provision of biological habitats to reduce the impact of operations on the community and the ecological environment, and promote biodiversity and sustainable development. In 2023, a tree database has been established in ZTE, Guangfu Institute and Guangming New Village, and a health risk assessment will be conducted. For medium and high-risk personnel, further medical examinations will be conducted. The remaining campuses will complete the database construction and evaluation in 2024. In addition to recording the health status of trees and inspecting them regularly, plant experts are also invited to treat the diseased trees. For trees that cannot be treated, manufacturers will be entrusted to make office furniture to realize resource recycling. As of 2023, the six major courtyard areas (including Guangming New Village) have included 11,003 trees, with an average green coverage rate of 206.04%. The greening effect of the campus area is ecological conservation and diverse species. The hospital covers a vast area. In addition to planting diverse trees to maintain carbon conservation, it also provides an ideal environment for biodiversity. Not only are there incubation of offspring of conservation animals such as horned owls, but also various birds, turtles, squirrels, pangolins, beetles, bees, butterflies and other activities. Through planting, tree maintenance and owl nest boxes, a healthy ecological cycle is created. Guangming New Village has a beautiful green life. Guangming New Village retains a large area of ​​forest since the Japanese era. In addition to the original forests of the community, there are also fruit trees and new trees planted by employees and hospitalists of all generations. The lake of Chenggong is sparkling and the grass in the Central Park is green. Guangming New Village provides a recreational place with rich natural ecology in the Hsinchu urban area for employees, residents and surrounding residents.
