COVID -19 The COVID-19 epidemic is spreading around the world.Our hospital applies the accumulated R&D technology energy in the past to meet the needs of the government and the public, conduct horizontal connection and cross-domain integration, and quickly set up an "epidemic prevention and response team" to create a comprehensive science and technology epidemic prevention network through strategies such as scientific and technological epidemic prevention, agile response and digital management, and actively protect the health and safety of all Chinese people and our employees. At the same time, through the epidemic review, we will gradually strengthen the organization's digital transformation and risk management policies, turn crises into opportunities, strengthen the overall resilience of the organization and improve rapid response capabilities.

Design of pressure inspection booths, and the safety inspection environment is deployed in hospitals across Taiwan
AI access control combined with rapid detection, convenient front-line screening
Agile team quickly expands mask production capacity and protects comprehensively
The "positive pressure inspection booth" was developed to provide a safe inspection environment for frontline medical personnel.Within two months of the epidemic warming up, nearly 200 of the facilities were built across Taiwan to provide immediate and effective scientific and technological prevention capabilities for medical care.
The "thermal image temperature abnormality detection technology" that combines AI artificial intelligence, can perform body temperature screening in places where a large number of people enter and exit outdoors, and the "iPMx molecular rapid inspection system" that can quickly complete the nucleic acid test of the new coronavirus, assist in epidemic prevention detection and improve the efficiency and accuracy of procurement and inspection.
In 2020, our court responded to the government's call to join the national mask team, and produced 92 equipment in 40 days with high efficiency, solved the domestic problem of insufficient equipment at this stage, and stabilized the production capacity of medical masks.Not only did Taiwan's mask production jump to the second highest in the world, but the daily mask production has also increased to 16 million pieces accordingly, protecting the health of the people with practical actions.
In order to prepare for the demand for respirators in the post-epidemic era, our hospital has developed a home-based "medical-grade respirator" to assist confirmed patients in rehabilitation and life.
Assist in building a GMP nucleic acid manufacturing plant, provide vaccine adjuvants to assist in the localization of vaccines
Research and develop zero-contact and zero-wear technology applications such as long-distance monitoring and digital certification, and protect the health of the people from multiple aspects such as prevention, diagnosis, treatment, monitoring and management.
Take care of the health and safety of employees with agility
In 2020, Covid-19 experienced a cross-border spread. Our hospital immediately established a "epidemic prevention and response team" (disassembled on May 20, 2023), adopting the principle of "rolling review and adapting to local conditions" to conduct comprehensive epidemic prevention and effective management, and inspect and determine the intensity of epidemic prevention based on the concept of "concentric circles", and quickly establish a notification system and epidemic prevention network to effectively take care of the health and safety of all employees.
Digital management to strengthen organizational resilience
In order to implement digital transformation, our hospital has gradually promoted long-distance work before the epidemic. The epidemic has accelerated the process of digital transformation, prompting our hospital to continuously optimize work processes with emerging communication technology. Now long-distance work has become the new normal of work.Our hospital has also transformed its management in response to the epidemic, shifting from focusing on management "procedures" to management "results" performance. The epidemic has also made the organization pay more attention to risk management. In the future, it will also plan to include risk management and organizational resilience in the major themes of sustainable development to ensure the organization's continuous operation and strengthen overall competitiveness.
