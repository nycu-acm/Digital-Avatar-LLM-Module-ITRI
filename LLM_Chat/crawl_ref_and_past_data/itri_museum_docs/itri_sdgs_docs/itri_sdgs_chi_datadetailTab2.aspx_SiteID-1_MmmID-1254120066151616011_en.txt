In order to promote the development of human resources, this institute formulates the "Human Development Measures" and the "Performance Evaluation and Annual Assessment Measures" The management regulations are committed to cultivating scientific and technological expertise and professional management talents. By promoting organizational culture transformation and establishing a talent development mechanism, the three cultural DNAs of "innovative thinking", "open communication" and "agile management" are implemented at various talent cultivation and human resources management levels. The talent development system of the Institute of Technology and Research includes four major aspects: "formal training", "performance development", "independent learning" and "knowledge sharing", taking into account the short, medium and long-term development needs of organizational operations, and also ensuring that the talent management system keeps pace with the times. This institute actively encourages independent learning and shapes the learning-oriented organization culture. 2023 The average training hours of employees in the year are 48.90 hours, the total training hours increased by 13.75% compared with the previous year, and the average training funding investment increased by 14.32%; 60 domestic and foreign public-funded in-service doctoral degree, 70 self-funded doctoral degree. Complete and diversified training programs and independent learning resources cultivation and development projects. In response to organizational management and cultural transformation, our institute actively creates a diversified learning blueprint for colleagues through various cultivation plans, lectures, training, overseas training and learning communities, and advocates cross-domain learning culture. Supervisory leadership cultivation - Light and Shadow Project: Individually inherited and matched by senior supervisors 1 to 1 mentor tutoring system. New supervisor X-Gathering Salon Xiaoju: Operate in a community model, encouraging novice supervisors to communicate, share and build connections. More than 58 physical leadership training sessions have been held, more than 6,000 people participated, and more than 12,000 online learning. The 100-day companionship program for newcomers promotes the "100-day companionship program for newcomers". From before to after joining the job, help newcomers feel the attention and care of the organization, and combine learning resources and exclusive Mentors to accelerate the development of newcomers' talents. The teaching supervisor talks with newcomers' careers and discusses learning and development plans for personal design. A newcomer vitality camp is held, with a training rate of 100%. International talent training bridge project/Honghu Project: Cultivate talents with forward-looking and able to bridge foreign research energy, improve technical level and international vision. Select outstanding and potential elites to go to overseas bases for training, establish a hub with international institutions, and promote more links and cooperation opportunities. In 2023, a total of 12 elites were selected for international training or chain. Permanent environmental education colleagues are compulsory for the annual compulsory 4 Hourly "Environmental Education" courses, with a completion rate of 100%, promoting colleagues' sustainability awareness and then implementing them in work and life. The sustainable and environmental education training held in 2023 includes 50 physical courses, 164 hours, 19 digital courses, 46 hours, a total of 41,655 learners, including introduction to ISO environmental management system, net zero carbon management, enterprise carbon reduction cases, natural ecological conservation, SDGs United Nations sustainable development goals, etc. Sustainable promotion activities 2023 In 2019, the first "Sustainable Development Model Cases" selection and "Innovation Green Ideas" competition were held. With "Carbon Reduction and Sustainability" as the main axis, it proposed a creative solution to help the hospital create a low-carbon and green environment, and it will be applied to the hospital's field. At the same time, the "Net Zero Hero List" regularly reveals the energy-saving results of each unit, and uses it to ranking to increase the attention and efforts of colleagues in each unit to improve the energy conservation and carbon reduction of energy conservation and carbon reduction, and actively respond to and practice the sustainable goals of carbon reduction for all employees. Independent Learning Culture Our Institute provides diverse and abundant online and physical learning resources, allowing employees to make good use of their time to independently select the learning content they need, improve overall work performance, and improve employees' self-worth. The entire hospital-wide learning competition is held every year, such as the "Innovation and Entrepreneurship Learning Award" in 2022 and the "Super Reading in 2023" 50-Learning Stars, etc., encourage all employees of the institute to learn and receive incentives.
