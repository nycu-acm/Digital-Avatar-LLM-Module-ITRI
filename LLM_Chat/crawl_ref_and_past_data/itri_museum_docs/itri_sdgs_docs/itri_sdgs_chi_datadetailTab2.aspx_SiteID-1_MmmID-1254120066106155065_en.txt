Career story of the colleagues
Release date 2024-07-16
The Institute of Technology is committed to forward-looking innovative technology research and development, taking "innovation, integrity, and sharing" as its values, building a collective consciousness of "altruism" and "sharing" and creating a career stage for elites in various fields. Adhering to the spirit of "people-oriented", following core values ​​and organizational culture, we will focus on the career cycle of our college, attach importance to personal needs, and start from the leadership and mechanism aspects to help every employee get a good work experience and develop appropriately.
All-round management talent career experience
Employee profile
In order to create a diverse and innovative workplace environment, our college continues to recruit capable talents from all generations, domestic and foreign academic and research interns, international talents, indigenous people and employees with physical and mental disabilities, etc. to improve the diversity of talent composition.The hospital also set up a smooth communication and appeal pipeline to promote two-way equal communication between labor and capital, and encourage internal flow of talents, so that every colleague has equal employment and development opportunities.
There are 6,259 full-time employees and 596 regular employees in this hospital, of which 38.21% are women; 26.29% are women in the research/engineering category;30.42% are female supervisors; female colleagues have equal employment and promotion opportunities in this hospital, and they have high achievements.

Employee communication
The Institute of Technology and Research actively listens to and attaches importance to the opinions of employees.The President provides a report on the institute to all employees every six months to publicize the key development directions and business performance results of our institute. In addition, through regular labor-management communication meetings with the trade union and employee representative meetings, we maintain good two-way communication and friendly relations between the institute and employees. In 2023, employees communicated and discussed 22 times.
In addition, our institute has a "Heart Warm Consulting Service" to provide convenient channels for instant communication with employees; for any specific, major or timely issues, the institute will immediately hold an extraordinary meeting with employee representatives to communicate and discuss to ensure that the voice of employees is smoothly transmitted.In addition, service satisfaction or employee opinions surveys are conducted every three years to fully listen to employee feedback.

Human Rights Protection
The Institute of Technology supports the international human rights convention and eliminates any violations and violations of human rights. The supervisors of this institute and all colleagues do not discriminate against or treat any employees unfairly because of race, color, social origin, class, language, thoughts, religion, party, origin, place of birth, gender, sexual orientation, age, marriage, appearance, facial features, physical and mental disorders, zodiac signs, blood type or other factors.
Formulate the "Prevention Plan for Illegal Infringement of Positions" to establish a workplace culture of safety, dignity, non-discrimination, equality, mutual respect and inclusiveness, implement policies, strengthen employees' awareness of respecting human rights, and abide by the laws and regulations of various operating bases around the world, and strive to create a workplace environment of gender equality and diversity and inclusiveness.
Our hospital conducts relevant education and training on human rights protection and diversified integration every year, with a total training hours of more than 45,000 hours, and a total of 17,105 people were trained.There will be no discrimination, child labor, hindering freedom of association, forced labor, or infringement of the rights and interests of indigenous people in 2023. In the future, we will continue to work hard to strengthen the publicity of human rights issues to specifically improve management efficiency and employee satisfaction.
