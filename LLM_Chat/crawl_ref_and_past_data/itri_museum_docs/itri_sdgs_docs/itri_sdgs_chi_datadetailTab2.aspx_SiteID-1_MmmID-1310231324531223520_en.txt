Scientific Research Steering Committee
In order to strengthen the forward-looking and industrialized achievements of scientific and technological research and development, the Institute of Industrial Research has selected international experts to form the "Scientific Research Steering Committee" to serve as the highest guiding unit for scientific research and innovative development of the entire institute. The committee is divided into the Strategy Advisory Committee (SAC), the Advanced Research Advisory Committee (ARAC), the Commercialization Advisory Committee (CAC), and the Technical Advisory Committee (TAC) at the Technical Advisory Committee (TAC).Through clear division of labor, it provides comprehensive planning and guidance from macro strategies to technical details, helping the Institute of Technology to improve its resilience in the face of future scientific and technological and industrial challenges, promote local value from a global perspective, and achieve innovation and sustainable development through scientific research and professionalism. In order to improve the establishment of technical strategies and blueprints and discuss the future trend of the industry, the Strategy Steering Committee (SAC) has guided internationally renowned experts and scholars in various fields including biomedicine and medical materials, machinery and systems, green energy and environment, materials and chemical industry and nano, electronics and optoelectronics, as well as information and communications, to more accurately grasp the pulse of science and technology and innovation, and assist the Industrial Research Institute injecting innovative thinking that conforms to industrial trends.
Organizational structure of the Institute of Technology Research and Research Scientific Research Steering Committee
Highlight Project - Creating Blue Ocean Challenge
The Institute of Technology and Research encourages cross-field and cross-generational cooperation with the "Creating Blue Ocean Challenge" to stimulate innovative thinking and new possibilities for technology application.A total of 25 teams participated in the competition in 2024. Among them, the "New Generation AI chip cooling technology development" team combined the industrial insights of senior generations and the creativity of young generations to create natural water-immersive cooling technology, successfully allowing the chip to run stably below 30 degrees C, and facilitated cooperation with Japanese businesses.Through cross-generational and cross-field collaboration, the Institute of Technology continues to promote innovation and create a future blue ocean market.
