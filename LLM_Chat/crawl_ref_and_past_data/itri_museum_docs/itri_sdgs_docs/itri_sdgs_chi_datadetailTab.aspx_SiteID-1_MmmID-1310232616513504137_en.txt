The future is unavoidable
Nearly 40 years ago, the indicator book "We Only One Earth", which awakens environmental awareness, was published. The idea of ​​environmental sustainability began to sprout, and now it has become the goal and consensus of most people. However, there is still a difficult road to slow down the heating of the earth, and everyone is ready to move forward together. Last year, floods in Spain, heat waves in California, and wildfires were raging frequently. The climate disasters we thought were far away were approaching the door and began to quietly affect daily life, such as strawberries that were not harvested and out of stock, fruit production declined, coffee prices soared, and climate change was no longer a major proposition that had nothing to do with people's livelihood.The perseverance of the earth is not an option, but an inevitable condition for survival. Out of love and responsibility for generations of descendants, sustainable development is our unshirkable mission.
Faced with a sustainable vision, no one is an outsider, and it is the goal pursued by the government, industry, society and the whole people.The challenge of climate change is the greatest project in human history, and it requires gathering advantages in various fields and taking action together.The Institute of Industrial Research and Research will integrate the United Nations Sustainable Development Goals (SDGs) into R&D work, develop carbon reduction technologies with innovation as the core, and will hold the ITRI Net Zero Day Forum and the South Taiwan Net Zero Forum and Special Exhibition from 2022. It will assist enterprises in net zero layout from the perspective of net zero technology solutions, green patents, net zero talents, carbon footprint inspection, net zero strategy consultants, etc., to assist enterprises in net zero layout, promote green innovation and promote industrial transformation, and in 2024, it has coached large and small and medium-sized enterprises to inject fresh water into emerging green industries.
At the same time, the Institute of Technology Research and Research has continued to respond to the changes of the times and deepened the cornerstone of organizational sustainability, and built a "sustainable development strategy blueprint" from six major aspects, including integrity governance, scientific and technological research and development, a happy workplace, green campus, social welfare, and industrial development, and gradually implemented short, medium and long-term goals, leading the organization to move towards a sustainable direction.So far, the greenhouse gas investigation in the entire hospital has been completed, and high-efficiency energy-saving equipment has been continuously invested. In combination with the relevant technologies developed by the Institute of Industrial Research, it has effectively saved electricity and water, including through throttling control and open source regeneration.
As the perpetual report enters its third year, more international inspection standards have been added. In addition to following the existing "Task Force on Climate-related Financial Disclosures (TCFD) framework, due to the mutual influence between the natural environment and human economic activities, this year, our court has also aligned with international standards and added "Taskforce on Nature-related Financial Disclosures (TNFD) content to accurately grasp the impact of operations on the biological environment, and then formulate financial information related to nature, so that stakeholders can better understand the risks and opportunities posed by the enterprise value chain when facing major climate risks, as well as decisions and actions on the economy, the environment and human life.
After years of hard work, we welcomed the recognition of the China Research Institute's Sustainable Action for the China Research Institute's Sustainable Action, and stood out from more than 200 of the most indicative enterprises in Taiwan, and won the 2024 "World Sustainable Citizen Award - No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No. 1 in the No.

It hopes to use a little bit of light to light up Taiwan and the corners of the world, and show the common sustainable value.
