=== DeepLook, a Cloud-Based Video Analytical Platform ===

DeepLook is the world's first scalable video analytics PaaS (Platform as a Service) technology that is designed to facilitate the development of domain-specific video surveillance applications. The technology scales to a large number of surveillance cameras in a cost-effective way. Its open structure and unique plug and play nature allow AI to enhance recognitions upon humans, objects and cars. DeepLook provides video summarization, license plate recognition and object analysis, which currently offers excellent service for the police to save their time and resources in investigation.

=== Connected Autonomous Driving Vehicle Solution ===

ITRI’s Autonomous Driving Vehicle (ADV) is Taiwan’s first autonomous driving mid-size shuttle bus carrying up to 17 passengers and sponsored by the Ministry of Economic Affairs (MOEA). ITRI’s ADV is empowered by Surrounding Sensing Subsystem (S3). It is ideal for Asia’s urban signalized intersections and mixed traffic. The ADV won second prize in the start-ups category of 2019 Dubai Challenge for Self-driving Transport. In 2021, ITRI ‘s ADV operates and offers shuttle service between Hsinchu station of Taiwan High Speed Rail (THSR) and Sheraton Hsinchu Hotel in Zhubei, Hsinchu.

=== RAIBA—Reconfigurable and Regulatable Battery Array System ===

RAIBA is a battery system which can sense, think and reconfigure. These capabilities result in two unique features: the adaptability for heterogeneous batteries and the zero-redundant fail-operational function. RAIBA integrates online reconfigurable battery arrays with a real-time reconfiguration system. It is able to analyze the composition of a battery array, simulate possible configurations, choose and implement the best configuration to suit load requirements, bypass depleted or failing batteries, and ensure optimal array operation and charge/discharge cycles.

=== Networked Amide Epoxy Polymer Electrolyte for Solid State Lithium-Ion Batteries, NAEPE ===

ITRI’s NAEPE is a nonflammable, low-cost solid-state electrolyte with high ionic conductivity and voltage stability. It is a key material for the next generation solid-state Li-batteries, offering high energy density cells with better safety and cycle life. NAEPE is suitable for applications that operate at high voltages and temperatures.

=== Reclaimed Asphalt Pavement Regeneration Technology ===

ITRI’s asphalt rejuvenating agent transforms reclaimed asphalt pavement (RAP) into new pavement material and thus solves its disposal problem. In general, recycled asphalt concrete with 40% RAP complies with the international standard of rutting resistance according to the Hamburg Wheel Tracking (HWT) Test. This novel technology creates a new paradigm for circular economy in turning waste into materials.

Key Features:
1.	High Efficiency: The technology rejuvenates aged asphalt binder and reduces the viscosity to improve compatibility between new and old asphalt.
2.	High Quality: The ductility of the recycled asphalt binder and the rutting resistance of the recycled asphalt concrete are both improved.
3.	High Recycle Rate: RAP can be 100% used in asphalt mixtures while meeting domestic requirements.
4.	Environmental Sustainability: The technology minimizes the use of natural resources, greatly improving the environment.

圖片: https://vrmaker.istaging.com/ad5e74e3-fe9f-422b-af82-79253068248f/markers/HKcohKvCl0

=== RAIBA 可動態重組與自我調節之電池陣列系統 ===

「RAIBA可動態重組與自我調節之電池陣列系統」結合線上恆電流開關模組和電池陣列重組演算軟體，是一款具備感知、思考、分析、重組等四大能力的電池系統。此系統具有「可相容異質電池」和「零備援的容錯特性」兩大特點，能於電池系統運轉前和運轉期間即時分析電池陣列，模擬所有可能組合，並選擇最佳配置並快速切換，智慧調控不同電池模組，同時達到滿足負載需求和延長系統運轉時間。

=== E-scooter Motor Drive ===

"Countries in the world have established policies to promote vehicle electrification, in order to reach global carbon emission reduction target. Despite scooters’ convenience and mobility, conventional gas-powered scooters increases air pollution and pose a serious threat to the environment .
This e-scooter motor drive incorporates the world’s first high power density e-scooter PIM with more than 8kW power. The PIM developed by ITRI’s power electronics research group, combines three-phase inverter and DC converter, current inductance, protection switching, and temperature sensing. The utilization of high-density system-in-package (SiP) further increases the power density by three times than that of the discrete module. Furthermore, the use of insulated metal substrate makes the power module more reliable and safer. The flexible module integrates with the motor drive and penetrates the AM market easily due to its easy-to-change characteristic that can significantly reduce the motor malfunction frequency."
