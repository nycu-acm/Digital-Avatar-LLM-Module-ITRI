Major Milestones
Semiconductors
ITRI delegates signed an IC technology transfer and licensing contract with RCA in 1976, successfully introducing the 3-inch wafer to Taiwan.
ICT
No. 2 Chinese-Language Computer was developed by ITRI in 1979.
Materials, Chemicals, and Nanotechnology
ITRI is dedicated to fostering Taiwan’s lithium battery technologies and industry.
Vehicles
ITRI launched the mid-size self-driving bus shuttle service between the HSR station and northern Hsinchu City in 2021.
Metrology
ITRI was certified by the U.S. NVLAP to conduct LED lumen maintenance (LM-80) testing and Energy Star testing in 2011.
Textiles
An environmentally friendly, moisture-wicking polyester composite yarn fabric was jointly created by ITRI and Taroko Textile in 1996.
PCB
Flexible PCBs were developed by ITRI in 1985.
Machinery
A seven-axis integrated smart joint robot arm was created by ITRI in 2020.
Biomedicine
In 2006, ITRI brought together several companies to advance gene chip technologies, encompassing the manufacturing, automated hybridization instruments, and scanner technology.
Green Energy
Carbon capture and storage technologies were tested and verified at the pilot plant for steel and chemicals co-production.
AI and Big Data
ITRI’s big data algorithms were iterated to improve click-through rates and analyze consumer behavior for the MOMO online shopping platform and Taiwan Mobile in 2015.
Others
The pioneering AI-Based High-Density Shuttle Rack Service System co-developed by ITRI and Yahoo! Kimo Asia helps increase storage space efficiency by integrating HCT Logistics and iAmech.
