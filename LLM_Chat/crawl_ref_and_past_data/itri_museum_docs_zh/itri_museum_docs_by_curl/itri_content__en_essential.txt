Tech Insight
The Birthday Wishing Tree is a testament to ITRI's technological collaborative power. The project requires the expertise of software and hardware developers, designers, metal components, electronics, optoelectronics, and more. The resulting exhibit features a stunning 3-meter-tall, wide, and long tree-like structure made with curved mini-LED displays.
In addition to the interactive experience, the Birthday Wishing Tree also utilizes advanced AI technology to recognize human movement, creating a 3D visual experience without the need for 3D goggles. By manipulating light and shadow, the exhibit offers a dynamic and engaging interactive experience for visitors.
